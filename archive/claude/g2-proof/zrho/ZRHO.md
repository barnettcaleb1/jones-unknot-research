# Kasahara's Problem 6.2 and the Jones unknot conjecture: what the Z_rho pass established

3 November 2026. Synthesis of five lanes and one referee pass; lane files are under ~/jones-lanes/g2-proof/zrho/.

**Setup.** I_2 is free on separating twists (Mess). Let d^ : I_2 -> Z be the exponent sum in Mess's basis, so d^(T_c) = 1 for every separating twist c. It is M_2-invariant, spans the invariant homomorphisms, and its kernel is [M_2, I_2]. Write N = Ker rho ∩ I_2, which holds every kernel element up to iota (Kasahara 2008, Thm 5.1). Then Z_rho = Z/d^(N). Problem 6.2 (Kasahara 2001) asks for the order of this group. As far as we could read, it is open; Morita 2006 asks the same question for all genera.

## What was established

**1. TRUSTED. The genus-two Casson formula.** On I_2, lambda(M_phi) = -d^(phi)/10 + q(tau_2 phi), where q is linear. The constant 1/10 is 1/(2(2g+1)) at g = 2. It was derived twice independently (from Faes-Massuyeau's closed core, and from the Seifert form by invariant theory) and confirmed by the referee's exact script. In Kasahara's notation this means d_1 = (12/5)d^. His §6 claim that q_f factors through tau_2 needs exactly this normalisation, which he does not state. We did not find the constant stated in a source; Morita-Sakasai-Suzuki (arXiv:1711.07855) is the likely place.

**2. TRUSTED. The identity.** For phi in I_2, M_phi is the double branched cover of a 3-bridge knot K_phi (Birman-Hilden). Mullins's formula then gives 4d^(phi) + 5 sigma(K_phi) = G(rho(phi)), where G is built from V_{K_phi}'(-1)/V(-1) and tau_2. Both are functions of rho(phi), the first since V is a matrix coefficient of rho, the second by Kasahara's Cor 1.2. A check on T(3,6n+1) passed for n = -3..3 (exact arithmetic, seconds). It confirms the conventions and cannot test the constant. The L/12 coefficient in Mullins was pinned only on L(3,1), which is harmless on N, where L = 0.

**3. DAMAGED, repaired. The brief's form "d = 3 sigma + F".** It holds only for the non-integral d = -(12/5)d^. With the integral d^ the relation is sigma(K_kappa) = -(4/5)d^(kappa) on N. The brief also said the Meyer function vanishes on I_2. The Meyer cocycle does vanish on I_2 x I_2, but the Meyer function restricts to -(4/5)d^ there.

**4. TRUSTED. The signature reformulation of Problem 6.2.** Z_rho is finite if and only if some kappa in N gives a Jones-trivial 3-bridge knot K_kappa with nonzero signature. The order is then (5/4) min |sigma|. If the order is 10, there is a knot with V = 1, sigma = -8 and lambda(Sigma_2) = -1. In particular T implies Z_rho = Z by a direct argument that needs neither Ito's theorem nor the normal-intersection lemma. Repair from the referee: the Casson version (lambda(Sigma_2 K_kappa) = -d^(kappa)/10) needs only Morita, Cor 1.2 and Birman-Hilden. Mullins contributes only the signature value. Kasahara already says in §6 that rho carries lambda if and only if it carries d_1, so the Casson form of the equivalence is his. The signature form and the direct implication from T were not found in our searches; they are short consequences of classical results and may be folklore. Commutator kernel elements have d^ = 0 and give sigma = 0, so the reformulation says nothing about them. It cannot decide T in either direction.

**5. TRUSTED, modulo two recalled normalisations. Residual methods cannot decide the question.** Theorem A: for every finite quotient of M_2, the image of I_2 modulo the image of [M_2, I_2] has order dividing 10. Consequently every Kasahara truncation gives exactly Z/10, for every k. This reproves his computer results by hand. The reason: M_2/[M_2, I_2] is a central Z-extension of Sp(4,Z) whose class is 5 lambda_1 up to sign and torsion, and Deligne's theorem (confirmed for g >= 2 in Funar-Pitsch) kills twice the generator in every finite quotient of the universal extension. Lemma B extends the bound to every specialisation whose image of M_2 contains the image of I_2 with finite index. That includes every root of unity other than q = -1. If lambda_1 were twice Deligne's class the bound would become 20; boundedness survives either way. Theorem A is a fact about M_2 alone: the identity map has infinite Z, yet every finite quotient sees at most Z/10. So it gives no evidence for either answer. It also means Kasahara's truncation data cannot support his remark that order exactly 10 "remains possible".

**6. TRUSTED. Where the 10 comes from.** The 5 is the image of I_2 in H_1(M_2) = Z/10, which shows up at q = 1, where every separating twist acts as a scalar. The addendum records this image. The 2 is a parity effect: d^ mod 2 is the sum of the ten even Birman-Craggs homomorphisms. Together the 10 is the Casson denominator 2(2g+1). It carries no information about N.

**7. TRUSTED, elementary. The scalar normal form.** rho(T_c) = t^6 R_c, with R_c a pseudo-reflection. Z_rho = Z/m exactly when the scalars in the group generated by the R_c are generated by t^{-6m} I. No two distinct R_c commute or satisfy a braid relation, so a scalar can only come from a long global word: at least 10 letters, whose root lines span the whole space.

**8. DEAD.**
- The finite lane's leads (d^ mod 25 at t = zeta_25, a 5-adic tower): Lemma B decides them in advance.
- Its verdict that "every reason points to Z_rho = Z", which rested on T and on pairwise relations only.
- Its claim that finite Z_rho would be "far stronger than not-T": one Jones-trivial knot with sigma != 0 gives unbounded signature by connected sum.
- The identity lane's §9 lead, which vanishes identically on N.
- The infinite lane's named crux (a Hodge winding along kappa) is a restatement of d^|_N = 0 with no mechanism. Its Toledo-blindness argument and the claim that sigma(K_.) is a homomorphism on I_2 remain unchecked sketches.

## The sharpest open statement

**Conjecture (Problem 6.2, infinite side).** Every kappa in Ker rho ∩ I_2 has d^(kappa) = 0. Equivalent forms:
- every Jones-trivial knot K_kappa from the kernel has signature 0;
- the pseudo-reflections R_c generate a group containing no scalar other than I;
- the Casson invariant of M_phi is a function of rho(phi).

Strength: open. It follows from T, and from faithfulness of the [2,2,1] representation of B_5. It does not imply T, because commutator kernel elements are untouched.

Why it should hold: the reasons are modest. The finite alternative would produce a 3-bridge knot with trivial Jones polynomial whose nontriviality is certified by its signature, -8 or larger in absolute value. That is exactly the kind of counterexample the computer verifications of T (up to 24 crossings, Tuzun-Sikora) would most likely have met. Bharathram-Birman-Brendle's 2026 proof that Burau_4 is faithful would close the one known route to a kernel (Kasahara 2008, Rem 5.6). Correction added after this synthesis: the genus-two lane found a gap in that proof (../BBB-GAP.md, under adversarial check), so this route is open again and this reason no longer counts. We found no structural reason. By Theorem A, any proof must be non-residual: it cannot pass through finite quotients, truncations or roots of unity.

## Next step

Test the referee's corollary against Wright 1994: in genus 2, no Birman-Craggs homomorphism is induced from a finite quotient of M_2. If both hold, the remaining theory route is global: decide whether lambda_1 pulls back to zero in H^2(rho(M_2); Q).
