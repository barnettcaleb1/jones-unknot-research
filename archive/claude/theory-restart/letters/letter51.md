CORRECTIONS TO LETTER 50.
1. Withdrawn: "a single root of unity detects the unknot among two-bridge knots". The cosh formula stands (no published source found; the triangle-group statement itself is McMullen 2013 Sec. 5, Funar-Kohno Prop. 3.3-3.5, Miyamoto arXiv:2609.33703), but the vertex of a rational tangle only records its slope modulo the normal closure of the n-th power of the twist, so the unknot value is attained at all n neighbours: V_{T(2,2m-1)}(t) = 1 whenever t^m = 1, t != +-1 (Przytycki's t_k moves).
2. BN~ of a quotient tangle is not "a(k) plus linear compact curves". The linear classification is a theorem for Kh~ only (arXiv:2202.01460 over F_2; arXiv:2602.23442, Feb 2026, over any field, now the reference); compact components of BN~ can wrap non-special punctures and carry local systems, and torsion also comes from the arc. The trefoil's BN~ is one immersed arc with no compact component. So the objects are the special components of Kh~.
3. The structure is a theorem: if T has an unknot closure then, in Seifert framing, Kh~(T) = r_1(k) + special components of slope infinity, k even, with at least one special iff T is not rational (Kotelskiy-Lidman-Moore-Watson-Zibrowius, proof of Thm 1.2; specials are exactly the curves disjoint from a(infinity)).
4. The identity V~ = q^s + (1 - q^-2) chi_q(Tors BN~) is correct and was not found in print (one line from Lewark-Zibrowius Thm 3.16); max torsion order = lambda(K;F) <= u_q(K) over every field is Lewark-Marino-Zibrowius arXiv:2409.05743 Prop 3.33 and Thm 1.2.

THE REDUCTION (F_2 throughout; kht++ labels: a special s4(infinity) printed with q^c delta_^d has offset c and level d).
Data: the published table of all 138 strong inversions on knots up to nine crossings (arXiv:2104.13592 Thm 3.1, recomputed), plus my runs today: strong inversions of two-bridge knots with denominators up to 45 (in progress) and the doubled-strand tangles T_K of Lewark-Zibrowius (the quotient of K # K by the swap; W^+-(K) = T_K(+-1/2)) for twenty two-bridge K, up to 84 specials and 11 levels.
 (a) In every case Kh~(T) = r_1(k) + n copies of s4(infinity) (KWZ's s_2), 4 | k.
 (b) Decategorification (verified against direct bracket computation on the trefoil): with j = lk(tau(0))/4 read from the grading of r_1(k),
       [T] = q^{24j} [Q_0] + x ([Q_infinity] - (q + 1/q)[Q_0]),
       x = (1 - q^2)(1 + q^4) ( -q^-3 P(q) + q^{24j+1} [k/4]_{q^8} ),   P(q) = sum over specials of (-1)^{c/2 - d} q^c,
     [m]_{q^8} = (1 - q^{8m})/(1 - q^8). So T is Jones-flat iff P = q^{24j+4} [k/4]_{q^8}: the rational component behaves as a string of |k|/4 "virtual specials" at offsets 24j+4+8i, and flatness says the actual specials cancel it. Classical evaluations give (1 - t^4)(1 - t + t^2) | x and x/((1-t^4)(1-t+t^2)) = -j at t = 1 (all 138); the trefoil and both figure-eight inversions are the only cases where that quotient is a monomial.
 (c) Each special pairs delta-thinly with an arc; within a level signs alternate with c/2. Sign coherence is FALSE: 19 of the 138 and almost all doubles have pairs of specials with equal offset in adjacent levels, cancelling in P; I checked 9_2 (second inversion) over F_3, F_5 and Q: same curves, so this is not 2-torsion.
 (d) TILT (no exception in 138 + 83 + 20 tangles, several hundred adjacent level pairs): the occupied levels are consecutive, and passing from level d to d+1 strictly lowers both the minimum and the maximum offset (by 6 in every two-bridge quotient outside the table so far; by 2, 4, 6 or 8 in general). Mirror-invariant.
 (e) OVERHANG (no exception): if k > 0 the largest offset of an actual special exceeds the top of the virtual string, 24j + 2k - 4, by at least 6 (values seen: 6, 8, 10, 12, 18, ..., 66); if k < 0 the smallest offset lies at least 6 below the bottom of the virtual string.
 Consequence. TILT puts all specials of extreme offset in one level, hence with one sign; OVERHANG says the virtual string does not reach that offset. So the extreme coefficient of P - q^{24j+4}[k/4]_{q^8} is nonzero and x != 0:
       (a) + TILT + OVERHANG  =>  Lemma Q,   and for k = 0, (a) + TILT alone.
 This also certifies V != 1 for all twisted Whitehead doubles of the twenty companions, beyond the range of the crossing-number tables.
