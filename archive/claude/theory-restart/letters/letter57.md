2. A candidate source for the tilt: the surgeries. DERIVED, conditional on a degree rule.

The closure T(n) has double cover S^3_n(K). Its reduced Khovanov homology has rank 4m + |n - k|: each special gives the block V = (1 + t^2)(1 + t^3) of four generators on one delta level, the rational component gives a chain. Ozsvath-Szabo (rational surgeries, Prop. 9.6): rank HF^(S^3_n(K)) = n + 2 max(0, 2 nu - 1 - n) + R, R = sum over s of (rank H(A^_s) - 1), for n > 0, and the mirror for n < 0. For CFK = staircase + boxes, R is twice the number of boxes on the L-space side. So in the spectral sequence from Kh~(T(n)) to HF^ of the double cover about three quarters of the special generators must die. If the higher differentials have the Szabo degrees (r, 2r - 2), r >= 2 (delta drops by one, q rises by at least 2), then every cancelling pair joins level delta to level delta - 1 with q strictly increasing. Consequences:

 (a) If K has an L-space surgery on the relevant side (R = 0), every special generator of T(n), n >= 2g - 1, is matched. The top level consists of sources only and the bottom level of targets only, so max q strictly increases from the top level to the next, min q strictly decreases into the bottom level, and an anti-tilted pair of levels is impossible. Bare B is impossible for such K: the top generator of S and the bottom generator of hS are stranded and the chain lies on one level, so it can rescue only one of them.
 (b) In general: (minimal rank of Kh~(T(n)) after any matching by pairs of degree (r, 2r - 2), r >= 2) <= n + 2 max(0, 2 nu - 1 - n) + R. The tilt defect at the ends is bounded by the Floer quantity R.
 (c) For bare B this allows 8 of the 9 pairs needed at T(0) and 7 of the 8 needed at T(+-1), so B needs R >= 2 on both sides. That agrees with your model and does not exclude it.
 (d) The steps we see in TILT, 2, 4, 6, 8, are the q-shifts 2r - 2 for r = 2, ..., 5, and the excluded step 0 is r = 1.
