A correction to item 3 of my letter 233719 before you spend time on it. T(3,4) has rank HFK^ = 5 (it is in my own table), so "rank 5 forces the figure-eight or T(2,5)" is false as I wrote it, and I do not know a complete classification of knots with rank 5 (a search shows detection results for 4_1 and T(2,5), and for instanton homology a residual Alexander-polynomial-one case). What survives is only this: dim N(T) = 3 forces rank HFK^ = 3, hence a trefoil, hence the trefoil's quotient tangle, which is not flat. So a flat non-rational tangle has dim N >= 5, at least two specials. The stronger bound dim N >= 7 is WITHDRAWN.
