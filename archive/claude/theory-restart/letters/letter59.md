One update that changes the status of item 2 in my letter 233719 from conditional to a theorem, and gives the first exclusion of bare B by a compatible geometric structure. I have now read the source (PDF saved at ~/jones-lanes/theory-restart/lit/daemi/, text in full.txt).

1. The theorem. Daemi, arXiv:1508.07650. Theorem 2: the plane knot complex PKC(D) is a link invariant as a filtered Z-graded complex; its differential does not decrease the homological grading and raises the delta-grading by exactly 1. Theorem 3 with Theorem 4.9: the second page is the reduced (odd, hence over characteristic 2 also even) Khovanov homology of the mirror, as a bigraded group, with deg_h = h and deg_q = 2(deg_h - deg_delta) + 1. The complex computes PFH of the branched double cover, and PFH(Y) is the homology of R(Y), a union of |Tor H_1(Y)| copies of the torus H^1(Y;R)/2H^1(Y;Z) (his subsection 1.4). So:

  MATCH (PROVED given Daemi). For every link L with det(L) nonzero, Kh~(L; F_2) is the second page of a spectral sequence whose r-th differential has bidegree exactly (r, 2r - 2) and whose limit has rank det(L).

The degree is forced: on page r the filtration degree rises by r and delta_Daemi = h - (q-1)/2 rises by 1, so q rises by 2r - 2. No appeal to Szabo's or the Ozsvath-Szabo sequence is needed. Consequence: the generators of Kh~(L) can be paired, each pair joining delta level d to d - 1 (delta = q/2 - h) with q rising by at least 2, leaving exactly det(L) generators. For det(L) = 0 with b_1 of the cover equal to 1 the limit has rank 2.

2. Bare B is not a tangle. PROVED given MATCH and the pairing theorem. For a tangle with invariant a(0) + S + hS the knots K_n = T(1/n) have determinant 1. Their reduced Khovanov homology is one generator plus, for S, two strings on one level with q-positions x + e and x + 1 + e, e in {0,2,3,5} + {0,...,n-1}, plus the same strings one level down for hS. A pairing must send a generator of S to a generator of hS strictly higher in q, or use the one remaining generator. The largest such pairing leaves 2n + 1 generators (3, 5, 7 for n = 1, 2, 3; tools/daemi_match.py), not 1. So B violates MATCH for every K_n. The structure it fails is the family of plane Floer complexes of the branched double covers of the closures.

3. What MATCH does not exclude (same script). Two cancelling pairs Q_1 + hQ_1 + Q_2 + hQ_2 pass MATCH at K_n, n = 1..4, exactly when Q_2 lies one level above Q_1 and lower in q by any positive amount (or the mirror statement); three such pairs also pass. So ladders survive, with any gap. In lattice coordinates the ladder with gap 3 is: squares at (0,0), (-4,1), (1,-1), (-3,0); TILT fails only at its top and bottom rows. Ladders remain the formal objects to beat. On actual data MATCH holds as it must: for 3_1, 4_1, 5_2 (both inversions), 8_19, 9_42, 9_46 (both) and n = 0, +-1, ..., +-4 the largest pairing leaves exactly |n| generators.
