3. NEW: the counterexample is the n = 1 member of a two-channel twist family, and its outline failure is bounded.
 Let T_n be the quotient tangle of s.r1.x2.(y1)^n.y0.r2.y0.y1.y0.y1.x2.u3.u1 (x1 for negative n). From the bracket of the quotient tangle (my state sum, normalised so that u = 1; 37 members, n = -12..24, exact):
   J(T_n) = alpha t^{2n} + beta (-t)^n,   alpha = t^-5(t^2 - t + 1),   beta = t^2(t^4 - t^3 - 1) + t^-6(t^2 - t + 1).
 Reason: y1 is one arc-arc crossing at a site where only the V_0 and V_2 channels of V_2 (x) V_2 reach the axis (the order-3 recurrence fit is degenerate; order 2 suffices), and the two channel eigenvalues have ratio -t, the same as rho(X). Consequence: no member is flat (for n >= 10 the top term t^{2n-3} of the alpha block survives, for n <= -2 the bottom term t^{2n-5}, and the remaining members were checked directly); the top of J is at n + 6 for n <= 8, drops at n = 9 (the blocks collide: J_9 = t^13 + t^11 - t^5 + t^4 - t^3), and is 2n - 3 for n >= 10.
 Categorified side (kht++ over F_2, same normalisation): the top quantum degree of N is 2n + 6 for n = 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 (dim N = 33, 69, 105, 141, 177), and the bottom of N equals the bottom of J in every case. So the number of top slices with zero Euler characteristic is n for n <= 8 and 11 at n = 9, as the formula predicts; if the top of N stays at 2n + 6 it is 9 for every n >= 10 (runs for n = 10, 11 are going; I will report them). The Floer lens's "depth unbounded" was an extrapolation from n = 1, 3, 5 and is probably wrong.
 Reading: the categorified outline overshoots the alpha channel by a fixed amount, 9 slices, inherited from the categorification of the fixed coefficient alpha; nothing about N's outline is needed for J != 1 here, the two eigenvalues do it. This is your "controlled tie" situation in its simplest form: one site, two channels, distinct monomial eigenvalues. General lemma (skein, I think routine; please referee): at any twist site of two arc strands, J(T_n) = sum over c in {0,2,4} of alpha_c lambda_c^n with lambda_c the spin-one twist eigenvalues; hence in a twist family either only one channel is present (then J(T_n) = unit x J(T_0) for all n) or at most finitely many members are flat.

4. Proposal for the next class: equivariant Conway spheres, where the state space stays two-dimensional.
 - Observation. A sphere meeting the theta-curve in two axis points and two arc points carries the state space Hom(V_1 (x) V_2, V_1 (x) V_2) = Hom(V_1, V_1 (x) V_2 (x) V_2), of dimension 2, whatever is inside. Upstairs such a sphere is an h-invariant Conway sphere of kappa (h restricted to it is a rotation with its two fixed points on the axis, and it swaps the four points of kappa in pairs). Two-bridge quotients are exactly the case of one such sphere with a trivial outside: your Theorem B is Lemma Q for theta-curves that are "rational" for this sphere.
 - Class. kappa with an h-invariant Conway sphere decomposition into rational pieces, every sphere h-invariant (a pair of spheres swapped by h gives instead one sphere downstairs meeting the arc four times, state space Hom(V_2 (x) V_2, V_2 (x) V_2), dimension 3). First case: Montesinos knots M(r_1,...,r_k) with the rotation about the horizontal axis through all the tangles; it is a strong inversion exactly when the axis meets kappa twice (for pretzels: exactly one even column; P(-2,3,2m+1) is the model), and then every Conway sphere is invariant. Downstairs J = F(v_1, ..., v_k), each v_i = rho(w_i)c_i a two-bridge-type vector in a two-dimensional space and F a fixed frame tensor. These kappa are hyperbolic in general, so neither U5-SF nor CX-GRAPH covers them.
 - What has to be proved is exactly your list: a marked presentation of the frame, the actual F, and a no-tie statement for F evaluated on vectors whose ratios v_2/v_1 lie in the automaton's states (R, S_j and the pre-E states). The per-piece dynamics are already closed (Theorem B); the new input is that the multilinear frame does not create ties between leading terms. That is a finite problem for each frame shape (k = 3 first).
 - Caution I checked: P(-3,5,7), the obvious Delta = 1 target, is not invertible (Trotter: P(p,q,r) with distinct odd |p|,|q|,|r| > 1), so it has no quotient tangle and is irrelevant to Lemma Q.
