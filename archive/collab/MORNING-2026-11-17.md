## How the night went

## What is proved now
- Theorems N, O, V, Q-MONT, and V3 (vertical closures of rational bodies, including the pretzel case).
- Non-flatness theorems for big classes of nested boxes, all by exact certificates that were independently re-verified:
  - 46,771 single boxes;
  - all pairs from the 300 commonest box types;
  - M300: any number of boxes from those 300 types;
  - D36 and D36*;

- Consequence for knots: for these strongly invertible knots, every branch knot has nontrivial Jones polynomial for even filling parameter. For odd parameter it holds when a_2 = 0.
- Literature check: the a_2 = 0 part is not in any published result. Example family E6 consists of hyperbolic knots. Both extreme Jones coefficients have size 2, so they are not semi-adequate, and the crossing number goes past 24. So known theorems do not cover them.
- Width two, Astra's lane:

  - a finite-window theorem for two-twist families;
  - the seven-sector ranks proved.
  - Our search grammar is certified empty up to word length 14.
