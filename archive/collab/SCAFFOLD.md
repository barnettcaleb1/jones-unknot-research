## Barrier checks (every route must pass these)
- **B1 Links.** Nontrivial links with two or more components and trivial Jones polynomial exist (Eliahou, Kauffman, Thistlethwaite, Topology 2003). A route must use the one-component hypothesis essentially and say where.
- **B2 Virtual knots.** Nontrivial virtual knots with trivial Jones polynomial exist (Kauffman). A route must use classical S^3 input that fails for virtual knots, and say where.
- **B3 Small knots.** No counterexample exists through 24 crossings (Tuzun and Sikora). Any construction must be large or structured, and anything built from pieces of at most ~22 crossings that predicts a small counterexample is already dead.
- **B4 Hardness.** Name the crux. A route where every step looks routine has a false TRUSTED step.
- **B5 Euler characteristic gap.** Khovanov homology detects U (Kronheimer and Mrowka 2011), and V_K is its graded Euler characteristic. A route must say what extra input closes the gap between Euler characteristic and actual topology.
- **B6 Wrapping (Ren, arXiv:2609.11084v2).** The annular-bracket z-degree can be smaller than the wrapping number at wrap 4 (built from a Burau_6 kernel element in W(6,4)). Lemma Q is the wrap-2, winding-0 case, so a route must not also "prove" wrap detection at wrap 4.
- **B7 Fixed truncations.** Nontrivial knots with V = 1 mod 2^r exist for every fixed r (Eliahou-Fromentin, arXiv:1610.05560); fixed finite-type cutoffs likewise fail (Stanford). These results rule out detection from one fixed 2-power reduction or one uniformly bounded jet alone, NOT every fixed integer modulus. Pagel's mod 3^k analogue is unconfirmed. All compatible 2-adic data, an input-dependent cutoff with a support bound, and mixed geometric arguments are not excluded. One exact transcendental evaluation determines the whole Laurent polynomial. Finite root-of-unity blindness has stronger published examples (Kalfagianni 0710.0076, Thm 1.2), with parameter/quantum-dimension normalization to be respected. Scope: work/global_design/finite_barrier.md; Codex cross-field reply105400.
- **B8 Faithfulness (Ito, Math. Z. 2015, arXiv:1402.2028).** If the Temperley-Lieb representation of some B_n is unfaithful (modulo centre), some nontrivial knot has V = 1. So a proof of T proves TL faithfulness for every n. No generic faithfulness premise for n>=4 is established here: the BBB n=4 proof package (arXiv:2607.05283v2) fails the checked specialization obstruction G2-BBB, reply150127. This does not refute its theorem. Every route must contain a faithfulness mechanism, even if implicit.

## Common crux (Claude, 2026-11-02)
Every route below bottlenecks at the same place: a mechanism that turns equality of Jones polynomials into control of **actual** topology (actual fillings, actual homology, actual geometry). No candidate mechanism exists yet. Work is assigned only if it (a) proposes such a mechanism with a cheap falsification test, (b) attacks a crux in its simplest nontrivial instance, or (c) banks a finished result. Another reformulation that changes no node status is not progress.
