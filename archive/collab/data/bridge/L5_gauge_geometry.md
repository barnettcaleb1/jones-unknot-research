## 0. Orientation (first pass, before lit check)
Working hypotheses to test:
H1. Every gauge theory with only a cyclic (Z/2, Z/4) grading decategorifies to t = -1 data (det, signature, Casson-Lin counts). At t = -1 the quotient tangle T of ANY (K,h) looks exactly like a rational tangle (E_K has the homology of a solid torus), so Lemma Q is vacuous there: J(-1) = 1, J'(-1) = 0 always. => the classical/SU(2) shadow of Lemma Q is empty; all content is in the q-deformation.
H2. Fixed-colour Jones (V, J) is "ultra-quantum": the r -> infinity limit at fixed colour only sees the trivial flat connection (finite type), while the traceless (KM) regime is colour ~ r/2. So a bridge to SU(2) representation spaces must pass through (a) categorification + spectral sequence (Kh -> I^natural), or (b) roots of unity where the bracket's puncture loop delta = -A^2-A^-2 vanishes (A^4 = -1, i.e. t = -1): there the Bonahon-Wong Chebyshev-Frobenius makes the skein algebra see the TRACELESS SL(2,C) character variety (Cayley cubic = pillowcase complexified).
H3. Orbifold dictionary: SO(3) reps of the bifold (S^3, theta(K,h); all edges order 2) = h-invariant traceless reps of K. So Kronheimer-Mrowka web homology J^sharp(theta) is the gauge-theoretic home of (K,h); its quantum shadow would be an SO(3) (colour-2) evaluation of theta, while our J is the (1,1,2) evaluation.
H4. SL(2,C) complex Lagrangians intersect positively: the traceless SL(2,C) Casson-Lin count of branch knots T(1/n) is a positive count, detects K != unknot through the A-polynomial (Dunfield-Garoufalidis, Boyer-Zhang). Contrast with SU(2) signed counts.

## 2. MAIN DEVELOPMENT: what Lemma Q is on the pillowcase

### 2.1 Two-closure dictionary (PROVED, elementary; numerically re-verified)
Conway closures of a 4-ended tangle: D joins NW-SW and NE-SE; N joins NW-NE and SW-SE. b = [0] (two horizontal arcs, neutral for tangle addition), c = [inf]. D(b) = 1 circle, D(c) = 2 circles, N(b) = 2 circles, N(c) = 1 circle. Quotient tangles of strongly invertible knots are EXACTLY the tangles T with D(T) = unknot (Montesinos trick: Sigma_2(D(T)) = S^3, the lift of the D-arc core is K, the deck involution is h). They have b-connectivity. In the pillowcase: D = pairing with the slope-inf arc (lifts to mu), N = pairing with the slope-0 arc (lifts to an integral slope m; preferred framing m = 0 = Seifert longitude, so Sigma_2(N(T)) = S^3_0(K), det N(T) = 0).

Write [T] = J b + x c. D(T) unknot => x = (u - J)/delta (u = framing unit (-A^3)^w). Hence
   <N(T)> = (delta^2 - 1) J + u.                                   (*)
(checked: scratchpad closure_check2.py, plus [2]: D = unknot, N = Hopf.)

PROPOSITION 1 (band criterion). Let L be a 2-component link obtained from the unknot U by ONE coherent band move, oriented compatibly with the band. Let T be the tangle outside a ball around the band (so D(T) = U, N(T) = L). Then
  (a) V(L) = V(O u O)  <=>  [T] = (-A^3)^w [0]  <=>  J(T) = 1 (preferred normalisation).
  (b) If V(L) = V(O u O) and L != O u O, then T is non-rational, K := lift is a nontrivial knot, and for every n != 0 the knot L_n = T(1/n) (rational replacement inside the band ball) is a NONTRIVIAL knot with V(L_n) = 1.
Hence: Lemma Q <=> "the Jones polynomial detects the 2-component unlink among links at coherent-band distance one from the unknot", and JC => no nontrivial 2-component link with trivial Jones polynomial is one coherent band away from the unknot.
Proof. (a) Coherent orientation: in D(T) the strands are traversed W->E and E->W; N(T) with that orientation has the same crossing signs and the closure arcs add none, so w(N) = w(D) = w and both framing units are u = (-A^3)^w. V(D) = 1 gives J + delta x = u; V(N) = V(OuO) gives delta J + x = u delta. Subtracting delta*(first) from ... gives (1 - delta^2) x = 0, so x = 0, J = u. Conversely x = 0, J = u gives <N> = u delta^2. (b) If T were rational, D(T) = unknot forces T = [m] integral, N([m]) = T(2,m), V = V(OuO) forces det m = 0, so L = OuO. So T is non-rational; a 2-string tangle with solid-torus double branched cover is rational (equivariant Dehn lemma), so E_K is not a solid torus, K nontrivial. [T] = unit*[0] makes every closure of T bracket-equal (up to the unit) to the same closure of [0]; T(1/n) and [0](1/n) = unknot are knots, so V(L_n) = unit with V(1) = 1 and V'(1) = 0, i.e. V(L_n) = 1. Sigma_2(L_n) = S^3_{1/n}(K) != S^3 by Gordon-Luecke, so L_n is knotted. QED
Status: a RESTATEMENT of Lemma Q, but it imports (i) the known infinite families of 2-component links with trivial Jones polynomial (Thistlethwaite 2001, EKT 2003) as concrete test objects, and (ii) the band-surgery literature (Kanenobu et al.; Lidman-Moore-Vazquez distance-one fillings). Cheap test: for each EKT 2-component link, search for a coherent band to the unknot (band sum of the two components equal to the unknot). Any hit = explicit JC counterexamples L_n. Expected (since JC is believed): EKT tangles must fail D(T) = unknot; finding out WHY (which band-distance obstruction) is itself information. Note that an EKT tangle with [T] proportional to [0], b-connectivity and D(T) = unknot would already disprove JC, so EKT's bracket-trivial tangles cannot be of that form.

### 2.2 The classical shadow is empty (PROVED modulo cited theorems)
PROPOSITION 2. For every strongly invertible (K,h):
 (i) [T] at t = -1 (A = e^{i pi/4}, delta = 0) equals [0] up to sign: J(-1) = 1, and J - 1 vanishes to order 2 at t = -1 (project arithmetic: (t^4-1)(t^3+1) | J - 1). Pillowcase meaning: the homology class of the pillowcase Lagrangian of T (equivalently of the KWZ curve, lifted to the torus) is the class of the rational arc, because H_*(E_K) = H_*(S^1 x D^2) with rational longitude the Seifert slope.
 (ii) Every SU(2)-gauge Euler characteristic of T-closures is classical: chi(I^natural) = sigma/2 (Daemi-Scaduto), Lin's count = sigma/2, equivariant Casson = sigma/8 (Collin-Saveliev), Casson of S^3_{1/n}(K) = n a_2(K) (Casson surgery formula; with Mullins this is the first non-vacuous Taylor coefficient at t = -1, = the project's "a_2 != 0 settles Lemma Q").
 (iii) KM's Kh => I^natural spectral sequence cannot transport the q-graded Euler characteristic: I^natural carries q only as a filtration (KM 2014), and generically a cancelling pair at bidegrees (i,j), (i+r, j+2r-2) contributes (-1)^i q^j (1 + (-1)^r q^{2r-2}); vanishing for all r >= 2 forces q^2 = -1, i.e. t = -1. (Algebra is exact; that d_r has this bidegree is the Ozsvath-Szabo/Baldwin form, quoted from memory, NOT re-checked.)
Consequence: Lemma Q has NO content at the classical (t = -1) point and only the a_2 layer at first order; everything else is in the q-deformation of the pillowcase class. Any gauge-theoretic route must manufacture a q-grading.

### 2.3 Colour-regime obstruction (heuristic, but sharp)
Fundamental colour at level r: boundary holonomy angle ~ pi/r, the pillowcase of S^2_4 with those holonomies has symplectic area O(1/r) and dim V_r(S^2_4;1,1,1,1) = 2 for all r. So [T] is the quantization of the tangle Lagrangian in a pillowcase that SHRINKS to a point; Witten asymptotics at fixed colour see only the trivial connection (= the project's FT-DEEP finite-type layer). The traceless (Kronheimer-Mrowka, HHK) pillowcase is the colour ~ r/2 regime (dim ~ r/2, Lagrangian visible by stationary phase). V and J sit at the opposite end. => No direct bridge V <-> R(T); the only bridges are categorical (Kh curve ~ pillowcase Lagrangian, HHHK/KWZ) where the q-grading has no gauge counterpart.

## 3. Equivariant Kronheimer-Mrowka for quotient tangles (PROVED from KM + Gordon-Luecke)
PROPOSITION 3. Let (K,h) be strongly invertible, K nontrivial, T its quotient tangle, L_n = T(1/n) (Sigma_2(L_n) = S^3_{1/n}(K)). For every n != 0:
 (a) L_n has an irreducible traceless SU(2) representation, and none of its irreducible traceless reps is binary dihedral;
 (b) hence S^3_{1/n}(K) has a non-abelian SO(3) representation invariant (up to conjugacy) under the lift of h;
 (c) in the pillowcase P, the traceless character variety R(T) meets the slope-1/n arc at a point NOT on the arc L_ab of reps whose restriction to E_K is abelian (L_ab = the rational arc of the unknot's quotient tangle).
Proof. det L_n = |H_1(S^3_{1/n}(K))| = 1, so L_n is a knot; it is knotted since S^3_{1/n}(K) != S^3 (Gordon-Luecke). KM (IHES 113 (2011)) give an irreducible traceless rho. Non-abelian binary dihedral traceless reps number (det-1)/2 = 0 (Klassen), so rho is not binary dihedral. ad(rho) kills mu^2, so factors through the orbifold group, whose index-2 subgroup is pi_1(Sigma_2(L_n)). If ad(rho) restricted there were abelian, im(ad rho) would have an abelian normal subgroup of index <= 2, i.e. be cyclic/dihedral/O(2)-type, making rho abelian or binary dihedral: contradiction. Restrict further to pi_1(E_K) (surjects onto pi_1 of the filling) for (b), and read off the restriction to T for (c). QED
Value: gives the pillowcase picture of "T is not rational" at the GAUGE level for the exact family of fillings used by Lemma Q; it is the instanton-level shadow of Lemma Q. Novelty: an immediate corollary of KM + GL; probably folklore, not found stated.

## 4. SL(2,C) positivity: a COUNTING version of Lemma Q that is TRUE (PROVED for hyperbolic K; sketch for torus K)
Setting: traceless SL(2,C) characters of S^2_4 = Cayley cubic x^2+y^2+z^2+xyz = 4 = X(T^2) = (C*)^2/inversion (complexified pillowcase; 4 nodes = corners). Tangles give complex curves in it; complex curves in a complex surface intersect POSITIVELY.
LEMMA 4.1. If K is hyperbolic and h a strong inversion, h* acts as the identity on the canonical component X_0 of X(E_K), and every irreducible character on X_0 extends to a traceless SL(2,C) character of the quotient tangle T (meridians of T go to traceless elements).
Proof. For all but finitely many slopes p/q, S^3_{p/q}(K) is hyperbolic (Thurston) and h extends to it (h preserves every unoriented slope); by Mostow-Prasad the holonomy character of the filling, which lies on X_0, is h*-fixed. An algebraic involution of the irreducible curve X_0 with infinitely many fixed points is the identity. For irreducible rho with rho o h_* = Ad_g rho, g is unique up to sign and g^2 = +-I; g = +-I and tr g = 0 are disjoint closed conditions on the connected set of irreducible characters of X_0; at the holonomy, h is a rotation by pi about its axis, so g is traceless throughout. The meridians of T are conjugates of h. Lifting PSL -> SL is unobstructed: H^2(B^3 - T; Z/2) = H_1(B^3 - T, boundary) = 0. QED
PROPOSITION 4. For hyperbolic K, the number of irreducible traceless SL(2,C) characters of the branch knot L_n = T(1/n) is >= c_K |n| - c'_K with c_K > 0; for K = unknot it is 0 for every n.
Proof. Characters on X_0 with rho(mu lambda^n) = +-I are zeros of f_{mu+n lambda} = tr^2 - 4 away from the finitely many characters with parabolic peripheral image or reducible; the zero count is the Culler-Shalen norm ||mu + n lambda||, a NORM on H_1(dE_K;R) for hyperbolic K (CGLS), so it grows linearly in |n|. Each such character descends (Lemma 4.1) to a traceless irreducible character of L_n (finite-to-one). QED
Torus knots: h* is trivial on X(E_K) (traces of x^{-1}, y^{-1}, (yx)^{-1} equal those of x, y, xy), and the same count grows linearly except along the fibre slope. Satellites: open here (need an h-invariant non-abelian curve; Boyer-Zhang/Dunfield-Garoufalidis give a curve but not invariance).
MORAL (the actual bridge). Three decategorifications of "T is not rational":
  SU(2) signed count  -> sigma/2, Casson n a_2       : cancels, classical, does NOT detect.
  SL(2,C) positive count -> >= c|n| (Prop 4)         : no cancellation (complex positivity), DETECTS.
  Jones / bracket (fixed colour)                     : signed, q-graded, open (= Lemma Q).
Lemma Q needs a positivity mechanism. The project's own mechanism (additive end invariants, "adequacy-like") is a combinatorial positivity at the extreme q-degrees; the gauge picture says this is the right kind of mechanism and that no classical (q = 1) positivity is available for the fundamental colour. The quantum objects that DO see the SL(2,C) curve are skein MODULES (Gunningham-Jordan-Safronov, Detcherry-Kalfagianni-Sikora: dim Sk(S^3_{1/n}(K)) = #characters under tameness), not the Jones polynomial; Sk(S^3_{1/n}(K)) involves all coloured Jones of K (cabling), consistent with 2.3.

## 5. Bridge list (status, leverage, first step)
B1 [main, PROVED, restatement] Band criterion (Prop 1): Lemma Q <=> Jones detects O u O among links one coherent band from the unknot. Leverage: imports Thistlethwaite/EKT trivial-Jones links as concrete test objects and band-surgery obstructions. First step: take EKT's 2-component links, search (small, diagrammatic) for a coherent band whose result is the unknot; record which obstruction (det/Arf/V(omega) band constraints of Kanenobu type, or genus/Scharlemann-type) blocks it.
B2 [PROVED obstruction] Classical vacuity + spectral-sequence decategorification (Prop 2) + colour regime (2.3): every SU(2)/instanton Euler characteristic of T-closures is sigma/det/Casson data; at t = -1 every quotient tangle is rational-looking; the only gauge-visible layer is a_2. Tells us: do not look for a gauge-theoretic Euler characteristic proof of Lemma Q.
B3 [PROVED, likely folklore] Equivariant KM (Prop 3): R(T) leaves the rational arc on every 1/n line. This is Lemma Q at the instanton level for exactly the fillings Lemma Q uses.
B4 [PROVED for hyperbolic/torus K; NEW as stated?] SL(2,C) positivity (Lemma 4.1 + Prop 4): the traceless SL(2,C) character count of L_n grows linearly iff K nontrivial. A decategorified, positive invariant that does detect; the contrast isolates "positivity" as the missing ingredient for Lemma Q.
B5 [UNCLEAR, undeveloped] Fibonacci orbifold: SU(2)_3 = Fib x semion, so colour 1 = tau x s and <Theta;1,1,2> at the golden point is the all-tau Fibonacci evaluation of theta(K,h); the project's identity J(t) = V_K(t^2) mod Phi_10 reads "Fib(theta; tau,tau,tau) = Fib(K; tau)", an orbifold identity for the double branched cover along A. Possible bridge to Tutte's golden identity / flow polynomials (graph theory) and to universality (complexity lane).

## 6. Verdict for this lens
- The gauge/character-variety lens does not supply a proof route for Lemma Q, and it now says precisely why: V and J sit at fixed colour, where representation spaces are invisible (2.3), and every SU(2) Euler characteristic is classical (Prop 2). The detection theorems in gauge theory (KM, A-polynomial) are categorified or positive (Props 3, 4).
- The useful outputs are (1) the band reformulation with an explicit falsification test against known trivial-Jones links, (2) the clean separation signed/positive, and (3) the instanton- and SL(2,C)-level versions of Lemma Q, which hold.
- Recommendation: any proof of Lemma Q must be a q-graded positivity statement (the project's adequacy-type end invariants are the right shape). The most promising gauge-adjacent import is the KWZ multicurve classification (arXiv:2602.23442) as the categorified home of [T]: Lemma Q <=> no q-graded cancellation between the one component meeting the slope-inf curve and the slope-inf-parallel components of Kh~(T) (since D(T) is the unknot, the KWZ pairing with the slope-inf curve has reduced rank 1, so all components but one have zero minimal intersection with it, i.e. are parallel to slope inf). That is the categorification lane's question, not this one.
