**Barrier accounting.**

- **B1:** A1 uses connected sums of knots staying in the knot fiber; A5 is the rank-one **knot** detector. The reduced unlink rank for multiple components is different, and no analogous implication from an unlink Jones polynomial is assumed. In the standard normalization, EKT's “trivial” link polynomials mean the corresponding multi-component unlink polynomials, not literally the knot polynomial 1.
- **B2:** A3 is explicitly about actual classical knot complexes, and A5 uses the classical (S^3) instanton/Floer endpoint. It is not a theorem for arbitrary virtual diagrams or abstract complexes.
- **B5:** A3 is exactly the extra information beyond Euler characteristic: a bound on the dimension of actual homology, not on polynomial coefficients or a chosen chain presentation. Nothing in the graded Euler identity gives it.

**Existing checks and stop rule.** Fixed Watson templates have a rank bound (MID-13), so the old proposal to extract rank divergence by iterating one such template is superseded. Raw spanning-tree counts are unbounded even after minimizing diagrams on some whole Jones fibers (MID-19); formal universal Frobenius complexes retain arbitrarily much invisible homology (LATE-50). A new assignment here should specify a geometric restriction on actual knot cubes that survives those tests. Another equivalent bound without such a restriction changes no node.

Existence of *some* bounded nonempty fiber is equivalent to detection: if detection holds, choose the singleton unit fiber. A bound for a particular nonunit fiber is not known to follow from detection. The conditional implication is clean; the existential reformulation is not an independently easier theorem.

## Route C — bound exterior JSJ volume on one whole Jones fiber

Input: (K\subset S^3), (V_K=1). Define

\[
v(K)=\sum_{H\text{ hyperbolic JSJ piece of }E(K)}\operatorname{Vol}(H).
\]

This is the total hyperbolic volume of the **knot exterior**, including composite and satellite knots. It is not the volume of the branched cover, and not just the volume of knots that happen to be hyperbolic.

**Barrier accounting.**

- **B1:** The positivity lemma C3 is about **graph knots**, generated by knot cablings and knot connected sums. It is not a classification/positivity theorem for arbitrary graph links, and the knot (a_2) argument does not detect multi-component unlink-valued examples.
- **B2:** C2–C3 are classical (S^3) exterior and graph-knot topology. A virtual knot does not automatically have such an exterior or cable decomposition.
- **B5:** C5 asks for extra geometric control of actual exteriors. No Euler-to-rank implication is used. A Turaev–Viro explanation would require higher colors and an additional reverse volume comparison; neither is a free consequence of ordinary Jones and neither is assumed here.

**Existing checks and stop rule.** Fixed-template Watson/Kanenobu constructions do not refute this bound merely by having unbounded crossing number or fixed-color span.
