## 4. Finished family: all integer powers of the Bigelow seed

Use [a,b]=aba^-1b^-1 and one-based generators. Define

    psi1 = sigma4 sigma5^-1 sigma2^-1 sigma1,
    psi2 = sigma4^-1 sigma5^2 sigma2 sigma1^-2,
    beta = [psi1^-1 sigma3 psi1, psi2^-1 sigma3 psi2].

Let C have caps (2,5),(3,4), with free endpoints 1,6. Let D have caps (1,2),(5,6), with free endpoints 3,4.

**TRUSTED theorem.** For every integer n, both cap_C beta^n cup_D and cap_D beta^n cup_C are the trivial split two-string tangle, up to unframed boundary-relative isotopy. In the specified diagram framing their skein class is exactly e.

The proof has a finite algebraic certificate and a uniform geometric mechanism:

1. The id coefficient is zero for every n. Nine initial zeros suffice by Cayley–Hamilton on W(6,2), including negative n because the matrix is invertible. The numerator bracket is delta^2 for every n: apply the same argument to W(6,0) direct-sum the scalar identity, dimension six. Hence the entire framed class is exactly e. These are all-integer deductions, not extrapolation from a scan.
2. **Actual braid identity:** [beta,sigma3]=1. Its faithful Artin action on F6 has reduced images x1,...,x6. Consequently beta preserves the isotopy class of the central arc (3,4).
3. Slide that cap through any power of beta to the exposed endpoints of D. This splits off a boundary-parallel arc. Reflection treats the exchanged configuration. This is the structural reason for the zeros in this family.
4. After forgetting the central pair, the remaining local knot closes as a four-plat, hence is two-bridge. The bracket identity gives determinant one, which forces this local knot to be the unknot. No general Jones detection theorem is used.

As an additional calibration, n=2,3,4 in both configurations have direct split certificates and actual closures U_2 and U. Those six additional cases are separate from the 222-word batch.

## 5. EKT regluing follow-up

**Standard Jones-preserving EKT regluing: no nontrivial torsion-free member. Arbitrary regluing: torsion-free is possible, but the explicit example loses the Jones condition.** These quantifiers matter.

The archive already contains a full-family obstruction in `work/band_obstructions.md`, §7. Let S have actual unknot numerator, use the EKT seed H(S,infinity+2), and apply opposite omega powers. Normalize the boundary data of S as (1,d). Write

    Omega^j = [[a,8b],[b,a]],  a>0, a^2-8b^2=1.
    H1(Sigma2 L_j) = Z + Z/|D|,
    D = 1+12b(b+ad).

The torsion is zero only at j=0, the actual unlink seed. Indeed D is 1 mod12; D=1 forces b=0, using gcd(a,b)=1 and the Pell equation. Thus every nonzero iterate has |D|>1. This is **reused archived work**, not a new discovery or a claim covering every outside tangle. It applies to arbitrary S with actual unknot numerator, including nonrational S. The opposite-omega construction itself is [EKT's](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/ekt.pdf); the homology formula is our archived deduction.

Ordinary Conway mutation cannot repair this torsion because it preserves the branched double cover. The separate archived result `work/research41/pairing.txt` handles universally invariant outside wirings made from boundary braids: their opposite omega actions cancel as actual homeomorphisms, so an unlink anchor stays the unlink. Essential outside tangles are not covered.

For the broader literal question, independent boundary regluings of the Hopf-pattern pieces **can** give torsion-free homology. Take the rational inputs T=3/1 and U=3/2. Their presentation is

    H1(Sigma2 H(T,U)) = Z + coker [[3,-2],[-4,3]] = Z.

A full 14-crossing diagram independently confirms two components and H1=Z. Both component knots have trefoil numerators, so this is nontrivial. But its exact Jones polynomial, in x=t^(1/2), is

    x^7 - x^5 + x^3 - 2x - x^-1 - x^-5 + x^-7 - x^-9 + x^-11.

Its breadth is 18, whereas the two-component unlink has breadth 2. Thus it fails the necessary Jones condition. Homology alone does not rule out arbitrary regluing, but this example supplies no counterexample to the band-Jones statement and no band to U.

For reference, the same presentation calculation gives H1=Z for H(3,(4r+3)/(3r+2)), r>=0; no Jones-preservation assertion is attached. The remaining question is a **new Jones-preserving regluing outside the excluded orbit**, with actual band-to-U geometry. Nothing in this pass settles its existence.
