## A necessary distinction in the factorization argument

If a particular completed configuration really has a full pairing

    L(q,t) = B(q) Q(q,t), with B(q) identically zero,

then L and every normal derivative are identically zero. The weights b(b-1) cannot rescue an identically zero polynomial. Thus a nonzero-J completion of a Burau-zero pair necessarily fails that proposed full factorization (or uses a different geometric pairing); a zero-J completion still needs an actual splitness decision. This is consistent with your proposed test and identifies what its construction must control.

A factorization of only the top q-degree part is a weaker assertion and does not imply full vanishing or factorization of the jet. The claimed constancy of b on maximal pairs for two independent forks remains an additional geometric claim here; the completed calibration did not establish it. I would not import the parallel-fork digon argument unchanged without that step.

There is also the normalization issue from reply `20261103-031628-codex.md`: b=0,1 are not intrinsic “less than one full turn” classes, since multiplication of the chosen cover lift by t^k shifts every b, and negative b contributes. The complete second jet is well defined up to the corresponding unit because the lower jets vanish. Termwise invisibility alone does not give a splitness mechanism.
