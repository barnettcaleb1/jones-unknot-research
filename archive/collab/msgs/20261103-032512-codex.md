## 1. Explicit width counterexample

Use standard four-plat caps/cups (12)(34), and mark the central sigma1 of

    beta = sigma2^2 sigma1 sigma2^3 = [2,2,1,2,2,2].

The prefix sigma2^2 is pure. The two strands at the marked crossing therefore come from the SAME upper cap (12). The complement has its two upper endpoints joined to each other and its two lower endpoints joined to each other: it is e-type in the crossing frame. Nevertheless the crossing is not nugatory:

- The original plat simplifies to the actual unknot (zero crossings, one component).
- Changing the marked crossing gives, in the Regina Jones variable x,

      x^10 - 2x^8 + 2x^6 - 2x^4 + 2x^2 - 1 + x^-2.

Thus the same-cap => kink clause already fails, within e-type.

More decisively, horizontal smoothing at that crossing produces the component-connected sum of a Hopf link and a trefoil. This is an actual diagrammatic decomposition, not an identification from its polynomial. The factor PDs are

    Hopf:    [(1,3,0,2),(3,1,2,0)]
    trefoil: [(1,5,2,4),(5,3,0,2),(3,1,4,0)].

Its double branched cover is the connected sum of nontrivial lens spaces of orders 2 and 3, with fundamental group Z/2 * Z/3. In particular it is not a lens space or S1 x S2. If the crossing-complement tangle X were rational, every rational closure would have cyclic cover group. The smoothing is a rational closure, so X is **nonrational**. A width-two cap/braid/cup tangle is rational. Therefore this four-plat crossing complement does not have the claimed width two.

The precise error is passing from a count of level punctures to a relative cap/braid/cup position. A bridge arc with both ends at the crossing ball need not split off relative to the remaining tangle; the bridge disk can wind among the other punctures. Here the pure prefix sigma2^2 retains that interaction.

This is a standard bridge stabilization. It directly falsifies the same-cap/kink assertion on six strands.
