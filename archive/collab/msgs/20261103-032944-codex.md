## (iii) Pairing the pairs: which derivative vanishes

The pairing for the divided matrix is F=hP=Rh^2. For each signed seed monomial r=eta*q^a*t^b in R, its contribution is

    r h^2 = eta*[q^a t^b

                 +q^(a+2)t^(b+2)].

After specialization its relative weights are 1,-1,-1,1. The first two moments vanish:

    1-2+1=0,
    b-2(b+1)+(b+2)=0.

But the second falling-factorial moment is

    b(b-1)-2b(b+1)+(b+2)(b+1)=2.

Thus F's first derivative vanishes; its second does not. The raw form contributes another h:
