STRUCTURE LEMMA (width 6, e-type, two arcs, no closed components). Each arc of X alternates caps and cups. An e-type arc from a top-free point to a top-free point has one more cup than cap, so it is (1 cup, 0 caps) or (2 cups, 1 cap); the other arc is then (1 cap, 0 cups) or (2 caps, 1 cup). So EVERY such tangle has a short arc s: a single cup joining the two top-free punctures, or a single cap joining the two bottom-free punctures. Your tight counterexample is exactly s = (56) straight.

SPLIT CRITERION. X is split iff s can be isotoped, rel punctures and modulo slides over the other arc of its own system, to miss the two figure-eights of the opposite system. One direction: if s misses both loops it lies outside the region of the opposite caps/cups, hence is boundary-parallel, hence X is split. The other: a split X has its short arc unwindable. (If you see a case where the short arc is boundary-parallel in 3D but cannot be unwound in the disk modulo slides, say so; that would be the subtle point.)

MATCHING COMPLEXITY. c''(C,C') := minimum over representatives of the number of configuration pairs, |c_1 ∩ γ'_1||c_2 ∩ γ'_2| + |c_1 ∩ γ'_2||c_2 ∩ γ'_1| in your square-to-torus model. Split => c'' = 0 by the lemma. Conjecture N: nonsplit => c'' > 0 (the short arc's loop must meet the opposite arcs in every representative, and the complementary factor is a four-puncture essential intersection). KL(6,2) is then: c'' > 0 implies the collapsed signed sum over the pairs is nonzero. This is matching-sensitive, as you asked, and it separates the topological statement (Conjecture N) from the cancellation statement.

What this buys for the instrument: in width-6 e-type tangles one target loop is the figure-eight around the short arc and the other is around a cup on the remaining four punctures. The complete matchings therefore always pair "short-arc loop against a cap" with "four-puncture loop against the other cap". That is a much more constrained geometry than a general pair of tori, and it may be why the PL enumerator can be calibrated here where it failed on the 14-letter word: the only long object is the short arc's winding.
