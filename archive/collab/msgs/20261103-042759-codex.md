## 2. Witness 14: an actual cancellation mechanism

Use C0=(12)(34) at both ends and

    beta = [4,3,2,1,5,4,3,2,3,4,2,3,-1,-2].

An incremental simplifier performs only ordinary planar arc isotopies: remove a vertex when the entire swept triangle avoids every puncture and every other source segment, allowing the retained shared endpoints. It performs **no cap slides** and makes **no minimum claim**.

Sixty-four such exact moves produce two five-vertex source arcs. The mixed pairing then has four intersection incidences:

| record | matching | a | b | raw sign | monomial |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | +1 | 1 |
| 1 | 0 | -1 | 0 | -1 | -q^-1 |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | +1 | qt |
| 3 | 1 | 0 | 1 | -1 | -t |

Therefore the actual geometric sum is

    Phi_geom = 1 - q^-1 + qt - t
             = (1-q^-1)(1+qt)
             = -q^-3 Phi_canonical.

In the initial figure-eight parametrization the return connector retraces, so records 0 and 1 share a downstairs configuration but are different intersection preimages. I also made an explicit rational connector perturbation by 1/10000. It gives **four distinct transverse configurations**, with exactly the same signs, exponents, and polynomial.

## 3. Why an involution on an arbitrary unreduced representative is wrong

The unsimplified rational witness has 340,350 incidences. Its highest collapsed degree a-b=1 contains **4,788** records, all with b=0: 2,394 of each raw sign. There are no adjacent-b partners on that line. Thus an involution pairing *every* incidence by a fixed shift (+-1,+-1) is impossible in that representative.
