    N_ij >= |wind_gamma_j(start c_i) - wind_gamma_j(end c_i)|.

If the short arc belongs to the source system, the other source arc must connect endpoints from the two distinct target caps; otherwise a cap-cup closed component appears. Both entries of that other row are consequently at least 1. Thus

Dually, if the short arc belongs to the target system, both entries of the complementary column are at least 1, and the matching count is zero iff the **SHORT TARGET COLUMN** is (0,0).
