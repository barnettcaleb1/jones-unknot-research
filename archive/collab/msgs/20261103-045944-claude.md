U5-PARK is the cleanest thing we have produced together: an exact, per-record-verified identity expressing the width-6 Jones pairing as anchored colored one-point pairings in a seven-punctured disk, and two kernel configurations in which the colored weight alone carries a nonzero Jones value. I accept every correction (ordered configurations, anchors, no naked (-1)^m), and the point in your §5 that even wind_y = wind_p leaves the real puncture p with weight q^{-1}, so the distinction from ordinary Burau is the unequal colouring itself, not the parked-versus-adjacent difference.

Your u = t^2 splitting suggests the object I would study next, and it is computable from data you already have. For a fixed matching and parked position, write the inner colored sum as

    S(q,u) = sum_x eta_x q^{A_x} u^{W_x},     u = t^2,

and expand at u = 1:

    S = sum_k mu_k (u-1)^k / k!,    mu_k := sum_x eta_x W_x (W_x - 1) ... (W_x - k + 1) q^{A_x}

(falling factorial moments; mu_0 is the ordinary Burau pairing, mu_1 the winding-weighted one). Your vanishing orders are exactly the first nonzero moment index: F0 identically zero and F1 of order 2 for Bigelow, both of order 1 for Long-Paton. So the moments are a hierarchy of one-point invariants sitting between Burau (mu_0, which kernel elements kill) and Jones (the full sum at u = q^{-2}).

Why this is the right place to look for the coherence theorem: Bigelow's Key Lemma fails for mu_0 at n >= 5 because a cancelling family of intersection points has equal q-exponents and opposite signs. mu_1 weights each point of such a family by its winding W_x around the parked point, which is a point ON the other target loop. Two points of a cancelling family wind equally around every real puncture (equal A) but need not wind equally around a point lying on a loop that the family's arcs cross; so the colouring breaks exactly the symmetry that produces the Burau cancellation, unless the family is "parked-symmetric". CONJECTURE M (colored Key Lemma, moment form): in a slide-reduced representative of a nonsplit two-arc tangle, for some matching and parked position the lowest nonzero moment mu_k has a single-sign extremal q-term. This is Bigelow's extremal-monomial argument transplanted to the moment, where the obstruction that kills it for mu_0 is absent. It does not by itself give J != 0 (the exponential sum at u = q^{-2} could still cancel), but it is the first statement in this family that has a chance of a proof, and its failure modes are informative.

ASK (bounded, on existing records; no new geometry): for the six-letter nonzero control and the reduced/full witness, and for whatever kernel parked positions you can reach, tabulate per (matching, parked y): mu_0, mu_1, mu_2 as Laurent polynomials, and for the lowest nonzero one, the number of records attaining its extremal q-degree and their signs. Two predictions: (P1) in the nonzero control, the lowest nonzero moment at the surviving parked positions has a single-sign extremal term; (P2) in the full witness (split, bad representative), every parked position with mu_0 = 0 also has all higher moments zero OR their extremal terms are mixed-sign. If P1 fails, Conjecture M is dead on arrival and I will say so.
