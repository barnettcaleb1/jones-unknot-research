**Matching orders are not parked orders.** Write p_m for the reference exchange parity, kappa_m=(b_m-p_m)/2, c_y=kappa_m+w0_m(y), and r_m for its raw reference sign. After extracting t^p_m the matching aggregate is

\[

\]

Hence

\[
F_m^{(k)}(q,1)=r_mq^{a_m}\sum_y\eta_yq^{A_y}
\sum_{j=0}^k {k\choose j}(c_y)_{k-j}\mu_{j,m,y}(q).
\]

Anchors mix moments and different y terms can cancel. The archived Bigelow aggregate order two and Long–Paton aggregate order one do not determine their individual parked orders. No completed kernel parked-position records exist in the archive, so no such table is claimed here. Computing one would require the new geometry excluded by this assignment.

**Equal A means equal total puncture winding**, not equal winding around each of the six punctures. The inference about equal winding around every real puncture in the proposal needs to be removed.

**The first nonzero moment polynomial is stable up to a unit under a lift shift; its record-level sign coherence is not.** If S is multiplied by epsilon*q^r*u^c, its first nonzero mu_k is multiplied by epsilon*q^r, but the individual weights become eta_x*(W_x+c)_k. For example, S=u-1 has mu_1=1 with one nonzero positive contribution; u*S=u^2-u has the same mu_1 as +2-1. Our closed-loop anchors fix a convention, so this is a normalization warning rather than a geometric counterexample. A future conjecture must specify allowed anchors and representatives.

For negative W the Taylor expansion at u=1 is formal and may be infinite. We evaluated finite Laurent S directly; substituting u=q^-2 into an infinite formal series is not automatic. With d distinct W values, the first d moments determine S by the falling-factorial Vandermonde matrix, so exact order is decidable from finite saved support.

Finally, even a coherent first moment does not prevent cancellation at u=q^-2, and even nonzero specialized inners can cancel in the outer sum. These remain separate gaps. The moment hierarchy does not yet provide a noncancellation mechanism for either one.
