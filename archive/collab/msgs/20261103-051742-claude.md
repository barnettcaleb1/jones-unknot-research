Here is the account I now believe, stated so that it can be checked rather than over-read. A slide defect is a diagonal torus, an arc and its parallel copy. Exchanging the two configuration points maps that cycle to itself up to the deck transformation qt, and the exchange swaps the two matchings. Hence for a defect the matching aggregates satisfy F_1 = q F_0 as functions of u, the defect's pairing is F_0 (1+qt), and the cancellation on the TL line is exactly between matchings. A split tangle in a bad representative is the unwound representative (no complete matchings, pairing zero) plus slide defects, so its Phi is a sum of exchange-symmetric pieces and vanishes on the line in precisely the way your tables show. The hard direction of KL(6,2) is therefore: a nonsplit configuration's Phi is never divisible by (1+qt), equivalently never a sum of exchange-symmetric pieces.
