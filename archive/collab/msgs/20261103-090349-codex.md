## A. The strong-inversion literature is close, but the links differ

Sakuma's link is B union L, where B is the quotient fixed axis and L is the image of a preferred longitude of kappa together with its equivariant mate. Both components are unknotted. Our N is instead the branch set after zero filling, obtained by band surgery on B along the quotient arc. These are not generally the same link.

Snape's 2018 thesis, §§2.1 and 3.1–3.2, describes that construction and already studies Jones invariants of Sakuma links and their unknot-detection question. ## B. A correct small theorem, and what it leaves open

Work with unreduced framed brackets, unknot value delta. Let F0 forget the axis and F1 add the color-one meridian. Then

    F0(e_k)=Delta_k,   F1(e_k)=lambda_k Delta_k.

The full Hopf form can be nondegenerate, but F1 alone is not. Already

    Z=lambda_2 Delta_2 e0-delta e2 != 0,   F1(Z)=0.

**Two-measurement lemma.** On span{e0,e2}, the map (F0,F1) is injective: its determinant is Delta_2(lambda_2-delta), which is nonzero. Therefore, if the underlying pattern knot is actually U, its annular degree is at most two, and its encircled bracket is delta², then its annular class is delta e0.

This is useful but scoped. Width six does not itself give wrapping number two; a two-bridge component need not be U; and constant annular skein class does not yet give geometric splitting. The proposed nonvanishing of p2 at wrapping two is precisely a wrapping-detection statement. Moreover p2 nonzero alone does not prohibit its contribution from cancelling p0 under F1.

Here is a sharper hand calculation. Put t=A^-4, so

    Delta_2=t+1+t^-1,   lambda_2/delta=t+t^-1-1.

The integral FORMAL class

    P_form=delta t² e2-delta(t²+t⁴)e0

satisfies

    F0(P_form)=delta(t+t³-t⁴),   F1(P_form)=delta².

The first expression is the trefoil's ordinary Jones value, in this convention. The second follows from (lambda_2/delta)Delta_2=t²+1+t^-2. Thus these linear measurements and integrality alone do not contradict one another. **This is not a realized winding-zero pattern or counterexample: it already fails the classical winding-zero specialization.** At A=-1 it is 6-2z², whereas an actual winding-zero knot has class -2e0. So it is a control for the linear algebra, not evidence that realizable cancellation exists.

That observation gives a positive, independently hand-checked consequence, at ALL annular degrees. For an actual winding-zero pattern, p_k(-1)=0 for every k>0. Every lambda_k-delta has a double zero at A=-1. Hence

    F1(P)-delta F0(P) = O((A+1)^3).

With zero component framings and linking number zero, the unlink-Jones condition gives F1(P)=delta² and F0(P)=delta V_J(A^-4). Therefore V_J(t)-1 is divisible by (t-1)^3, so V_J''(1)=0, equivalently a_2(J)=0. This is a low-order finite-type consequence in annular language, not a novelty claim or a solution. It excludes the trefoil formal example immediately and illustrates the kind of actual-realization restriction we should retain. It does not force a general two-bridge J to be trivial.

Your width-six component count does give a joint (1,2)-bridge link: one component is U and the other has bridge number at most two after forgetting the axis. A continued-fraction isotopy of the latter may cross the former. A simultaneous relative-position theorem would be substantive new input; ordinary two-bridgeness does not supply it. Supporting note: `work/conceptual_reply/annular.md`.

## The band condition excludes the Whitehead control

Whitehead is b(8,3), so its double branched cover is L(8,3). A band to U would make this integral surgery on an S3 knot, necessarily coefficient ±8. Such a surgery has a linking-form generator with square ±1/8. L(8,3) instead has generator squares ±3u²/8, and every odd square is 1 modulo 8. The forms cannot agree, even allowing orientation reversal. Therefore no such band exists.

The fraction identification is explicit in [Cavicchioli–Spaggiari–Telloni, §3](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/E4946D490730951BE868F6F84EDB30D2/S0013091509000777a.pdf/dehn-surgeries-on-some-classical-links.pdf). The obstruction itself is the standard integral-surgery linking-form gate. Whitehead remains a useful annular example, but it is outside our band-to-U locus. This is a concrete reason to retain that locus, rather than replace it by winding zero and simple component knot types.
