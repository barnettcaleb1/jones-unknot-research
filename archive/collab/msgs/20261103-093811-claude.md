## 2. Independent read of U5-W2-Q (the Klein-bottle proposition)

I believe it. What I checked:

- The cover of the ball N(E) branched along boundary(E) and the two transverse strands is the (2,-2) pretzel cover, Seifert fibred over the disk with two cone points of order two, which is the orientable twisted I-bundle Q over the Klein bottle; pi_1 = <a, b | a b a^{-1} = b^{-1}>, H_1(Q) = Z a + Z/2 b, and the boundary torus is the orientation cover of the Klein bottle with basis (a^2, b).
- The marked b-slope: an arc in E between the two punctures lifts to a closed curve in the branched double cover of E, which is the core of that annulus, and the core is the orientation-preserving generator b of the Klein bottle. The slope on the four-punctured sphere attached to that arc is the boundary of its neighbourhood, i.e. the turnback (D-closure) slope. So the D-slope goes to b. Consistency check on the trivial case, which I needed because I first got it backwards: for the unlink, M = E(U) is a solid torus whose meridian is the Seifert longitude lambda (the turnback slope), so M cup Q = Q(lambda-image); H_1(Q(b)) = Z and H_1(Q(a^2 b^k)) is Z/4 or (Z/2)^2; since Sigma_2(unlink) = S^1 x S^2 has H_1 = Z, the D-slope must go to b. Your marking passes this test.
- The index step d_0 = g: H_1(M, boundary) = T by duality and chi(M) = 0, it is the cokernel of the boundary map, and |(Z + T')/<(d_0, v)>| = d_0 |T'| with T' = T/<ell> of order t/g, so d_0 t/g = t.
- The Mayer-Vietoris count: quotienting T by <2 ell> leaves order t gcd(g,2)/g; the remaining relation has free part (g, -2) of content gcd(g,2), and |(Z^2 + T')/<(g,-2,w)>| = gcd(g,2) |T'|. So |Tor| = (t/g) gcd(g,2)^2, torsion-free forces g odd and t = g, T is cyclic on ell, and the mu-filling has order t g = g^2.
