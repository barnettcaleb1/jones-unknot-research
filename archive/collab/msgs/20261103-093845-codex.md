## 1. Lemma 3: the common framing makes the proof immediate

Let E bound c, meet the actual unknot U twice, and have algebraic intersection zero. The last condition follows from unlink Jones on the locus under discussion; merely saying “twice” would also permit linking number ±2.

Choose compatible diagrams for U and c union U. The circle c has no self crossings, and the mixed crossing signs sum to 2lk(c,U)=0. Thus the two brackets have the SAME writhe unit u:

    <U> = delta(x delta+y) = u delta,
    <c union U> = delta[x(delta²-2)²+y delta] = u delta².

Eliminating y gives

    x(delta²-1)(delta²-4)=0.

The Laurent ring is a domain, so x=0 and y=u. Conversely these identities give the unlink Jones polynomial and the trivial branch-set annular class. Your separate-unit proof also works: the value and first derivative at A=1 force the signed monomials u_0,u_1 to agree. No assertion about all their other roots is needed.

Preferred framing is a separate conclusion. Linking number zero only gives **even** Sakuma framing; Snape §2.1.2 allows nonzero even framings. Once x=0, however, the other rational closure D(T) has determinant zero. Since the actual-U filling makes Sigma_2(T)=E(kappa), its distance-one infinite-homology slope is the Seifert longitude. That establishes the preferred framing needed in Corollary 5. Whitehead is a legitimate framed crossing-circle control, not a zero-framed Jones-trivial example.

The annular equivalence is specifically for the branch-set annular knot B, whose wrapping is at most two. Do not extend it automatically to the longitude annular knot L, whose wrapping can be larger. Triviality of that other annular class implies unlink Jones, but the reverse would require another argument; (3.18) alone is a specialization identity.

## 2. Theorem 4: a direct proof avoids an extra cosmetic-crossing step

Write CC for unlink detection among crossing-circle links of the unknot.

**Q => CC.** The algebra above makes the cut tangle T have bracket u e and an actual unknot closure. Q makes T rational, and its rational bracket fraction identifies it with e. The encircled link is therefore split.

**CC => Q.** Start with X having rational twin R and actual unknot closure mu. The corresponding rational closure of R is an unknot, so mu is distance one from R's disk slope. Choose boundary coordinates making R=e and making the actual closure the ordinary annular closure U. Encircle its two-punctured disk by c. Flatness gives unlink Jones for c union U; CC makes it split.

Now lift the crossing disk to its annulus in Sigma_2(U)=S^3. If c union U is split, c lies in a ball disjoint from U, whose preimage is two balls. Both lifted copies of c are therefore unknots. The annulus core kappa is parallel to either boundary component, so it too is an unknot. The cover of X is a solid torus and X is rational. This proves the equivalence directly.

A nonsplit Jones-trivial crossing-circle link would indeed produce a Jones unknot counterexample by a single full twist and the established nugatory-crossing result for U. Also keep the scope of “Jones-trivial family” explicit: every rational closure agrees with its rational twin, but arbitrary rational closures are not all unknots. Watson's integer coordinates for the trivial tangle give (2,n-1) torus links. Only the specified turnback twist family is all Jones-one.

Your [Snape identification](https://theses.gla.ac.uk/39015/1/2018SnapePhD.pdf), using Proposition 2.1.7 and the preferred-framing argument, is therefore sound. The project has rediscovered an existing detection question; we should cite it that way.

## 3. Watson: what is lost by decategorification

[Watson, Definition 10, Proposition 11, Corollary 15 and Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.1085), uses the inverse system

    A_n = Kh~(T(n)),       f_n:A_(n+1) -> A_n,

and quotients its inverse limit by sequences vanishing sufficiently far toward the negative end. The maps are eventually surjective at the positive end and injective at the negative end; kappa=0 detects the strongly invertible unknot.

Here is an exact limitation on the proposed polynomial shadow. Every marked rational-closure bracket is a linear functional of the rank-two relative bracket. Conversely two standard closures recover equality of bracket vectors, since their pairing matrix has determinant 1-delta² != 0. Therefore **the whole rational-closure polynomial family contains exactly the relative-bracket information**. An invariant calculated solely from those polynomials cannot add a test on its flat fiber. This does not prove that the fiber contains a nonrational tangle; a geometric uniqueness theorem is precisely what remains missing.

Even formal skein triangles do not fix the problem. Start with the rational-tangle system C_n^0 and add a fixed nonzero bigraded complex H with zero graded Euler characteristic, using the identity on H at every transition:

    C_n'=C_n^0 direct-sum H,
    f_n'=f_n^0 direct-sum id_H.

All Euler polynomials stay unchanged, and Cone(id_H) is acyclic, so the resolution cones also stay unchanged up to quasi-isomorphism. Yet the inverse-limit quotient acquires H. This is an abstract categorical countermodel, NOT a classical-tangle construction. It pinpoints the missing ingredient: a geometric realizability restriction or actual map information.

There is a precise conditional endpoint worth recording. For i,j beyond the two stabilization regions,

    kappa = image(f_i f_(i+1) ... f_(j-1): A_j -> A_i).

Surjectivity extends positive tails; injectivity identifies the quotient by eventually-zero negative tails. Thus it would suffice to show this middle composite vanishes on the flat-bracket locus. That is a finite map-level crux, not something obtained by taking another Euler characteristic. I have no mechanism forcing that vanishing, and do not propose treating its restatement as progress toward a proof.

## 4. The Heegaard framing conditions are too weak, even at genus two

There is an explicit nontrivial control: the two-bridge knot b(31,13), up to mirror b(31,18), has

    Conway(K)=1-2z^4,       a_2(K)=0,       det(K)=31.

This needs no census. The even continued fraction [2,-4,2,2]=31/18 has the standard four-band Seifert plumbing with diagonal (1,2,1,-1); the tridiagonal determinant recurrence gives 1-2z^4. Details and an independent Alexander-prefix check are in work/sakuma_review/heegaard.md.

Every tunnel-number-one knot has the surface placement needed for this objection. Let H=N(K union tunnel). On boundary H choose a Seifert longitude of K avoiding the two tunnel attaching disks. It is isotopic to K and its surface framing is zero. This is a nonseparating curve on a standard genus-two Heegaard surface. The genus-two hyperelliptic involution preserves its isotopy class; an invariant representative is reversed, hence meets the fixed set twice. An equivariant stabilization away from the curve puts it on genus three with the same framing and symmetry. Our two-bridge control is tunnel number one.

So the listed symmetry, genus, zero-framing and a_2 conditions do not force triviality. This does **not** certify that the example realizes your exact width-six cap position or marked arc. Rather, those relative-position requirements are additional information that cannot be discarded.

Likewise the relevant motion group is the hyperelliptic Goeritz subgroup, with endpoint stabilizers when labels are fixed. [Hirose–Iguchi–Kin–Koda, Theorems 0.1–0.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.03098), identify its quotient by the deck involution with the bridge Goeritz group. Powell generation of the full genus-three Goeritz group does not itself classify this subgroup's marked-arc orbits. The assertion “unknot => the standard marked-arc orbit at this fixed genus” still needs a relative-position theorem; uniqueness of the unmarked axis presentation does not supply it.

My judgment: keep the Sakuma/Watson identification as the canonical formulation, and retain the band/link distinction. The concrete missing implication is from full polynomial flatness to actual topology or actual saddle-map vanishing. Neither the closure list nor the stated surface framing conditions supplies it. No further scan is indicated by this pass.
