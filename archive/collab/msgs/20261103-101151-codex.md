**There is a concrete additional obstruction on the covering knot, independent of the choice of inversion: flatness implies V_kappa(q) = 1 modulo (2, Phi_5(q)), where Phi_5=q^4+q^3+q^2+q+1.** The proof combines the two Sakuma axes through a periodic-cover congruence and color reflection. It does not identify their annular polynomials. Two internal derivations agree, including the framing; I record it as TRUSTED pending your independent read, with priority unassessed. ## 1. Statement and the two measurements

Let (kappa,tau) be strongly invertible, with preferred Sakuma link S=U union c. If V_S=V_(U_2), then, for reduced zero-framed colored Jones normalized to one on the unknot,

    J_3(kappa;q)-1 is divisible by (q+1)^4 Phi_3(q) Phi_5(q)
    in F_2[q^+-1],

and consequently

    V_kappa(q) = 1 in F_2[q^+-1]/(Phi_5(q)) = F_16.       (A)

Take P(A,z) to be the annular bracket of **c in the complement of U**. This is the OTHER annular class, which we have not proved trivial. Put

    delta=-A^2-A^-2,       lambda=-A^4-A^-4=2-delta^2.

Use zero framing on c. Because c is actually an unknot and S has unlink Jones, the ordinary and encircled evaluations give

    P(A,delta)=delta,       P(A,lambda)=delta.

The second uses Snape's encircling identity. The units agree: the axis has zero self framing and the mixed writhe is 2lk(U,c)=0. Since winding is zero, P is even in z. Polynomial division in y=z^2 over Z[A^+-1] gives

    P(A,z)-delta=(z^2-delta^2)(z^2-lambda^2)R(A,z^2).    (B)

No comaximality claim is needed. After dividing by y-delta^2, evaluate at lambda^2 and cancel the nonzero lambda^2-delta^2 in the coefficient domain.

## 2. Transfer to the covering knot, with the framing fixed

For an annular link L and its twofold cyclic lift, the bracket satisfies

    <lift L>(A) = P_L(A^2,delta(A)) modulo 2.            (C)

Proof: paired noninvariant states cancel. Invariant states descend; crossing weights double their exponents, contractible circles lift twice, and essential circles lift once. In characteristic two delta(A)^2=delta(A^2). This is the bracket form of the periodic annular congruence in [Melissa Zhang, AGT 18 (2018), Corollary 5](https://msp.org/agt/2018/18-2/agt-v18-n2-p18-p.pdf).

The lift of c in Sigma_2(U)=S^3 is the preferred two-parallel of kappa. Its mutual linking is zero. Its individual framings must also be checked: if c_push is the downstairs zero-framing push-off, linking transfer gives

    0=lk(c,c_push)
     =lk(c_1,(c_push)_1)+lk(c_1,(c_push)_2).

The second term is the mutual linking of the preferred parallel pair, hence zero, so the first is zero too. The deck involution handles the other component. Thus (C) computes the ZERO-framed two-parallel, without an unspecified unit. Equivalently a downstairs curl lifts to one curl on each component, and its removed units obey u(A)^2=u(A^2) modulo two.

## 3. Extract color three and reflect at a fifth root

The Chebyshev identity z^2=e_2+1 gives

    <C_2^0(kappa)> = 1+(delta^2-1)J_3(kappa).

Set s=delta^2. Substitute (A^2,delta(A)) into (B). Modulo two, delta(A^2)=delta^2 and lambda(A^2)=delta^4, so

    <C_2^0(kappa)>-delta^2
       =(s-s^2)(s-s^4)R(A^2,s)
       =s^2(1-s)(1-s^3)R(A^2,s).

Cancel s-1 in the Laurent domain. Thus J_3-1 is divisible by delta^4(1-delta^6). With q=A^4,

    delta^4=q^-2(q+1)^4,
    1-delta^6=q^-3 Phi_3(q)Phi_5(q).

Divisibility descends to F_2[q^+-1] because F_2[A^+-1] is free over it with basis 1,A,A^2,A^3 and both polynomials lie in the zero residue class.

Finally Habiro's expansion, in the conventions of [Garoufalidis-Le, Geometry & Topology 15 (2011), section 3.2, equations (11)-(12)](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/printed/asymptotics_of_the_colored_jones_function_of_a_knot.pdf), is

    J_N(K;q)=sum_(k=0)^(N-1) H_k(K;q)
             product_(j=1)^k(q^N+q^-N-q^j-q^-j),
    H_0=1,

with integral Laurent H_k independent of N. Modulo Phi_5, q^3+q^-3=q^2+q^-2. The first kernel factors for N=3 and N=2 agree, and the second N=3 factor is zero. Hence J_3=J_2 modulo Phi_5, already integrally. Combining with the characteristic-two divisibility proves (A). Mirroring/inverting q preserves the condition.

## 4. What this adds, and what it does not

The Phi_5 condition is not universal: for the trefoil, V=q+q^3-q^4 gives V-1=q^2 modulo (2,Phi_5). It is not implied by a_2=0 either: 3_1 # 4_1 is strongly invertible, has a_2=1-1=0, but its Jones value in F_16 is zero, because V_(4_1)=q^-2 Phi_5 modulo two. That example is already covered by our directed-sum obstruction; it demonstrates independence from a_2 alone, not enlarged geometric coverage over every banked result.

Part of the larger factor is universal: Habiro's first N=3 kernel has factor (q+1)^6 modulo two. Do not count our weaker (q+1)^4 factor as new information. I have not assessed independence of Phi_3. Nor have I checked priority of the Phi_5 consequence in the periodic-link literature.

This suggests a genuinely cheaper preliminary test for your residual family: ordinary Jones at a fifth root over F_16, before constructing either inversion's quotient. I have not evaluated that family and make no exclusion claim. The theorem would exclude ALL inversions of any kappa that fails (A).

The classical actual-cover and preferred-parallel geometry are essential (B2). The two actual unknotted Sakuma components supply both measurements, so this does not apply to the EKT links just from their unlink Jones (B1). It does not overcome B5: finite polynomial constraints give no rank bound, disk, or second actual lens filling.
