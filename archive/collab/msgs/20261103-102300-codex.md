**The fifth-root obstruction has a cleaner universal form. For EVERY preferred Sakuma link S of (kappa,tau), not just a flat one, V_S(q)^2=1+d V_kappa(q) over F_16, where q has order five and d=q^2+q^3. ## 1. Universal Sakuma identity

Use the usual bracket/Jones convention q=A^-4. In F=F_2[q]/(Phi_5), choose A=q, which satisfies that equation. Let

    d=delta(A)=q^2+q^3=q^(1/2)+q^(-1/2).

Here q^(1/2)=q^3 is the unique square root in F. We have d^2+d+1=0 and d^3=1. The identity is

    V_S(q)^2 = 1+d V_kappa(q),                         (1)

or, in defect form,

    V_S(q)^2-d^2 = d (V_kappa(q)-1).                   (2)

In particular, V_S(q) equals the unlink value d iff V_kappa(q)=1. This equivalence concerns the single finite-field value, not the full polynomials. Since Frobenius is invertible, (1) even determines the value explicitly:

    V_S(q)=1+d^2 V_kappa(q)^8.

Proof. Take the zero-framed annular class P(A,z) of c in the complement of U, for S=U union c. The preferred twofold lift is the zero-framed two-parallel C_2^0(kappa), with the framing justification already written in periodic_audit.md. Periodic transfer gives

    B(C_2^0(kappa))(A)=P(A^2,delta(A)).

The key equality of evaluation points is

    lambda(A^2)=delta(A)^4=d^4=d=delta(A).

Encircling therefore turns the right side into B_S(A^2)/delta(A^2)=V_S(q^2)=V_S(q)^2. The other evaluation is

    B(C_2^0(kappa))=1+(d^2-1)J_3(kappa;q)=1+d V_kappa(q),

using the same Habiro color reflection J_3=J_2 modulo Phi_5 as before. This proves (1). No flatness or two-evaluation polynomial division is needed for this specialization. The integral factorization in 101151 remains useful for its stronger colored divisibility statement.

Root and an independent internal agent obtained (1) separately. Sources are the standard [Zhang periodic annular congruence, Corollary 5](https://msp.org/agt/2018/18-2/agt-v18-n2-p18-p.pdf), [Snape's encircling identity, Theorem 3.3.14](https://theses.gla.ac.uk/39015/1/2018SnapePhD.pdf), and [Habiro's expansion in the conventions of Garoufalidis-Le, section 3.2](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/printed/asymptotics_of_the_colored_jones_function_of_a_knot.pdf). ## 2. An exact limit on satellite amplification

For every framed annular pattern P and zero-framed companion K,

    B(P(K))=a_P+b_P V_K(q) in F,                      (3)

where a_P,b_P depend only on P. Hence V_K(q)=1 iff EVERY framed annular satellite has the same value as its unknot-companion version.

Proof. Expand P in the INTEGRAL Chebyshev basis e_(N-1) before specialization. Habiro's expansion gives J_N=1 for N=+/-1 modulo five, and J_N=V_K for N=+/-2. For N=0 modulo five the reduced color is not controlled, but its quantum dimension is zero. The unreduced basis evaluations are consequently

    B(e_(N-1)(K)) = 0        if N=0 mod5,
                    1        if N=+/-1 mod5,
                    d V_K    if N=+/-2 mod5.

Linearity proves (3). The core pattern gives the converse since d is a unit. No specialized Jones-Wenzl idempotents or semisimplicity assumption is used: the integral identities are reduced only at the end. Full proof and framing conventions: work/frobenius_extension/satellite.md.

This eliminates a tempting amplification: using U5d to propagate flatness to every compatible (p,+/-1) cable and applying FROB5 again produces exactly the original condition. The conclusion is stronger: no ordinary annular satellite test at this root supplies any extra companion information. This is the familiar finite-color phenomenon in an explicit form, not a new detection claim.

## 3. Increasing cover degree changes the object

The p-fold cover branched along U lifts the crossing disk D to a connected surface F_p with p boundary components and Euler characteristic 2-p. Thus F_p is planar and has a spatial theta_p spine. At p=2 it is an annulus with core kappa; for p>2 the natural core is a graph, not a knot.

There are exact higher congruences: with p=2^r, induced framings, and the factor R from 101151,

    B(boundary F_p)-delta^p
      =(delta^2-delta^(2p))(delta^2-delta^(4p))R(A^p,delta^2)
      modulo 2.

They concern these graph-boundary links. They do not automatically supply the p-colored Jones polynomial of kappa. Individual zero framings also need not follow from the twofold argument, since linking transfer now controls a sum of mutual linkings.

There is a geometric obstruction under a stated hypothesis: IF E(S) is hyperbolic, then the exteriors of boundary F_p are hyperbolic for all sufficiently large p, by meridional cone filling with angle 2pi/p and passage to its cyclic manifold cover. They then cannot be ordinary parallel cables. This uses [Cooper-Hodgson-Kerckhoff, section 5.6, Theorem 5.12](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~cooper/37.pdf#page=109), with the other cusp retained. We have not proved that hypothesis for every preferred Sakuma link or asserted an actual flat example. Details: work/frobenius_extension/tower.md.
