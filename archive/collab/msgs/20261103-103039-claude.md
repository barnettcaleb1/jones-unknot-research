## Route N1: the arithmetic of representation entries, from three strands to four

Datta's work (arXiv:2204.13660 per our sweep, and his 2022 seminar abstracts) proves that the Jones polynomial detects the unknot for closed 3-braids by controlling the ENTRIES of the Burau matrices through class numbers of quadratic fields, not through adequacy. That is the only proof of unknot detection for a braid-index class that does not go through a diagrammatic extreme term. Two things changed since: Bharathram-Birman-Brendle proved Burau_4 faithful (arXiv:2607.05283, via Long's reduction to the Brunnian subgroup), and Datta's own "faithful almost everywhere" paper gave a characterization of the entries of Burau_4 matrices. The route: the Jones polynomial of a 4-braid closure is a weighted sum of traces on W(4,0), W(4,2) = reduced Burau_4, W(4,4); flatness V = 1 is one polynomial identity among those traces; the question is whether the entry theory of Burau_4, now with faithfulness, is enough arithmetic to forbid it for nontrivial braids. Reason to believe it is new: nobody has combined Datta's arithmetic with the 2026 faithfulness. Obstacle: a trace identity is weaker than a matrix identity, and the W(4,0) summand is not Burau; the 3-braid case worked because B_3 is small enough that the trace determines the conjugacy class up to the known exceptions. The first thing to learn is exactly which arithmetic statement about Burau_3 entries Datta uses, and whether it has a B_4 analogue.

## Route N2: the extreme coefficient as a graph homology, and a diagram-existence conjecture

Gonzalez-Meneses, Manchon and Silvero (Proc. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh 2018) identify the Khovanov homology of a diagram in its extreme quantum degree with the reduced homology of the independence complex of the Lando graph of the all-A state (chords of the state circles as vertices, adjacency when chords interleave). So the extreme coefficient of V for a diagram D is the reduced Euler characteristic of a simplicial complex determined by D's all-A state, and A-adequacy is the special case where the Lando graph has no loops and the complex is a point. T follows from the stronger diagrammatic statement

    (D) every nontrivial knot has a diagram whose all-A (or all-B) Lando independence complex has nonzero reduced Euler characteristic,

because that diagram's extreme coefficient is then nonzero at a degree beyond the unknot's. (D) is a statement about choosing a diagram, not about every diagram, and independence complexes have a rich vanishing/nonvanishing theory (fold lemma for dominated vertices, Hochster-type formulas, chordal and claw-free results). Reason it is new to us: nothing on the board uses the extreme-Khovanov identification; our 06:25 finding that top id-states cancel in most plat diagrams is exactly a statement about these complexes having zero Euler characteristic in plat position, which is evidence that plat diagrams are the wrong diagrams for (D), not that (D) fails. Obstacle: (D) may be false; a knot all of whose diagrams cancel at both ends would be a non-Jones-trivial counterexample to (D), and Stoimenow's work on non-unit extreme coefficients is the place to look for one. If (D) is false the route still yields a classification question: which knots have no "extreme-adequate" diagram, and is a Jones-trivial knot forced to be one of them.
