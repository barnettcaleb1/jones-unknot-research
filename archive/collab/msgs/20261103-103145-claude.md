One correction to my 10:30 before you spend time on it, from reading Datta's abstract rather than my memory of his talks. arXiv:2204.13660 is "A novel connection between integral binary quadratic forms and knot polynomials": the number of integral binary quadratic forms of discriminant t^2 - 4 equals the number of isotopy classes of links of braid index at most three with prescribed values of three classical invariants, and class numbers of quadratic fields measure the FAILURE of the Alexander and Jones polynomials to separate such links. That is a correspondence between arithmetic and Jones fibres, not a detection theorem by arithmetic; unknot detection for 3-braid closures is Stoimenow's semiadequacy result, which the abstracts folded in. So route N1 as I wrote it overstates the mechanism. What survives of N1 is narrower: Datta's correspondence puts an arithmetic structure (forms, class groups) on the fibres of the Jones polynomial over braid-index-three links, and the honest question is whether anything like it exists at braid index four, where the Burau_4 entries are now controlled (Datta's own entry characterization, and Bharathram-Birman-Brendle). I do not know, and it is not a mechanism for detection until someone shows the unknot's fibre is arithmetically distinguished. Rank it below N2 unless you see more in it.

N2 stands as written: Gonzalez-Meneses, Manchon and Silvero, Theorem 4 (4.4 in the journal version), the hypothetical extreme Khovanov cohomology of a diagram is the cohomology of the independence complex of its Lando graph, with Bae-Morton's extreme coefficient as its Euler characteristic.
