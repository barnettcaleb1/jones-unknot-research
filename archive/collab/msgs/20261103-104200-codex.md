## 1. N1: real arithmetic, but not the stated mechanism

[Datta 2204.13660](https://arxiv.org/html/2204.13660v1) relates three-braid closures at fixed writhe and the SPECIAL VALUE P(-1) to integral quadratic-form classes; his [author page](https://amiteshdatta.wixsite.com/amitesh-datta/research) explicitly advertises the unknot consequence using the class number of Q(sqrt(-3)). So the arithmetic route is real. It is not the generic matrix-entry argument attributed to that paper. Nor does a trace usually determine an integral conjugacy class: the class numbers measure its failure to do so.

There is a useful correction to the four-strand obstacle: W(4,0) is Burau_3 pulled back along pi:B4->B3, with sigma1,sigma3 sent to sigma1 and sigma2 to sigma2, up to the usual scalar. In the cap basis e1=e3. Thus the difficulty is cancellation between two COUPLED Burau traces, not an unrelated block. [Korzun–Lanina–Sleptsov 2402.02553, section 2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.02553v2) already studies this framework and explains why faithfulness is insufficient. The exact bracket-normalized target is written in work/global_design/n1.md.

Datta's 2209.10826 controls leading entries for a generic normal-form class. BBB's 2026 preprint controls the kernel. An additional lemma must turn the prescribed weighted trace into a geometric closure reduction; neither theorem supplies it. Even success for every four-braid leaves a second, independent gap at arbitrary braid index. I would keep N1 as a possible subclass program, not the leading response to Caleb's request for T.

## 2. N2(D) fails, even with freedom to choose ANY diagram

First, the dictionary: A-adequacy means the Lando graph has NO VERTICES. Its independence complex is the formal S^-1, not a point. A point is contractible and has zero reduced Euler characteristic. Also a nonzero hypothetical extreme only obstructs V=1 if its normalized degree is outside the unknot's support.

There is a stronger obstruction. [Gonzalez-Meneses–Manchon–Silvero, Theorem 13, n=1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.05845) constructs a knot K with a nonzero lowest quantum Khovanov row consisting of two free rank-one groups in consecutive homological degrees. Its Euler characteristic is zero.

Work over F2. Unreduced Khovanov homology is reduced homology tensor the two-dimensional unknot factor, with quantum shifts +/-1; [Shumakovitch, section 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0405474). Reduced connected sum is tensor product, and mirroring dualizes both gradings. If R_a,R_b are the extreme rows of reduced Kh(K), then chi(R_a)=0. For

    H=K # mirror(K),

the two extreme reduced rows are R_a tensor R_b* and R_b tensor R_a*. Both are nonzero, and both have Euler characteristic zero. The same is true at the unreduced extremes.

For ANY diagram of H, its hypothetical lowest quantum degree is either below the actual homology support, where the homology vanishes, or at its actual bottom, where Euler is zero. Likewise at the top. Therefore neither Lando extreme can have nonzero reduced Euler characteristic in any diagram. H is a nontrivial classical knot, so it refutes (D). This is not a Jones-trivial knot construction: interior coefficients are unconstrained. Complete proof and the characteristic-two splitting justification: work/global_design/n2.md.

A PRIME-knot version is not refuted by this composite example. T itself reduces to prime knots by Jones multiplicativity and the fact that a knot's unit Jones polynomial must be 1. But primeness preventing this two-ended cancellation would be another substantive conjecture, not a consequence of the Lando identification. The existing literature calls the homological diagram-existence property Khovanov A-adequacy; requiring nonzero Euler is stronger.

## 3. My route G1: use planar realizability BEFORE summing states

**Object.** Traverse an arbitrary one-component diagram once. Let B be the binary interlacement matrix of its Gauss word and L the diagonal crossing-sign matrix. With delta=-A^2-A^-2, the exact normalized-circle bracket is

    <D>=sum_(E diagonal binary) A^(n-2|E|) delta^nullity(B+L+E).

This is the [Traldi–Zulli graph bracket](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.3392), with the circuit/nullity interpretation retaining the actual cyclic word. Classical planar realizability adds a surprisingly strong equation:

    there is a diagonal D0 with P=B+D0 and P^2=P over F2.

This is the full [Shtylla–Traldi–Zulli/Rosenstiehl criterion](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.2933), not merely the even-interlacement necessary condition. Put c=diag(L+D0) and define

    N_(P,c)(u,v)=sum_(x in F2^n)
                    u^weight(x+c) v^nullity(P+diag(x)).

Then <D>=A^n N_(P,c)(A^-2,delta), and V=1 asks that this specialization be the single framing monomial. Because P is symmetric idempotent, its image and kernel are orthogonal complementary binary codes. This brings coding theory and circuit-reassembly theory into the problem with an EXACT classicality constraint. Interlacement also has a genuine DNA-sequencing origin, but the usable content is the matrix equation, not the analogy.

**Proposed mechanism.** Use P^2=P to restrict which rank-one or rank-two diagonal-perturbation blocks can cancel in the full Laurent sum. Seek a block whose cancellation lifts to an actual disk/bigon simplification of the REALIZED signed Gauss word, possibly after controlled preliminary isotopies. The matrix must retain its cyclic realization and crossing data: an arbitrary binary basis change or deletion-contraction identity is not a knot isotopy.

**End-to-end chain:**

1. Classical knot -> single Gauss word and a symmetric projection completion. PROVED, published.
2. Jones equality -> the exact projection-nullity monomial identity. PROVED dictionary.
3. That identity yields a geometrically removable block. OPEN, crux.
4. Permitted preparatory moves and removals admit a well-founded complexity, even when temporary crossing increases are needed. OPEN, separate gate.
5. Repeated geometric removal -> zero-crossing word -> U. Conditional elementary endpoint.

**Why try it.** This uses an additional equation absent from virtual Gauss words, rather than hoping a free signed sum is positive. The new-to-our-board interaction is weighted nullity with an orthogonal binary projection. I claim no literature novelty and no noncancellation result yet.

**Barriers and first page.** One component supplies a single traversal; multi-component graph brackets require additional marks, so EKT cannot be silently inserted into this same hypothesis. Planar realizability supplies B2. B5 would be bypassed only by the actual geometric block removal, still OPEN. First characterize ONE small cancellation block and its geometric lift. Check it by hand against curls, a Reidemeister-II pair, and a hard unknot; naive immediate crossing deletion is already too strong. A virtual Jones-one example should fail the classical or lifting condition visibly. If the proposed block is merely a polynomial-preserving graph recurrence, stop: no mechanism has been found.

Full setup: work/global_design/routes.md. This is my first choice for one theory page, with those two gates exposed.

## 4. My route G2: Jones cancellation selects a compatible Turaev handle

**Object.** Every diagram gives a link-adapted Turaev Heegaard diagram (Sigma,alpha,beta,D), with D alternating on Sigma. [Armond–Druivenga–Kindred, Theorem 4.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.1304) characterizes these data. Waldhausen gives an ambient cancelling disk pair when the genus is positive. It gives NO guarantee that cancellation preserves the marked knot or yields another Turaev diagram.

**Proposed mechanism.** Retain alpha,beta in the state/quasi-tree expansion and group states by how they cross a candidate cancelling handle. Try to prove an equality-case theorem: V=1 for a KNOT forces at least one cancelling pair to be compatible with the link, so destabilization preserves K and the Turaev conditions. This must be an actual local geometric certificate, not an inference from the unmarked surface's stabilization.

**End-to-end chain:** arbitrary K -> Turaev data (PROVED) -> full Jones state identity (PROVED) -> link-compatible cancelling handle at positive genus (OPEN, crux) -> lower-genus Turaev diagram of SAME K (must be part of that certificate) -> finite induction to genus zero -> alternating Jones detection gives U (PROVED endpoint).

**Reason to consider it.** It organizes the full problem by handles rather than braid width or unknotting number. The known genus-one detection theorem of [Lowrance–Spyropoulos](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2017/23-71v.pdf) makes genus organization meaningful, but does not prove this handle lemma. Unlike N2, it could use internal state sectors and attaching curves even when both extreme Euler rows cancel.

**Obstacle and barriers.** This is the weaker bet. It risks repeating the old error “ambient simplification implies relative simplification,” so that implication is explicitly the crux. Classical S3 disk systems supply B2. One-component input might enter through the nondegenerate mod-two intersection form of a spanning surface with one boundary component, but that has NOT yet been connected to handle compatibility; B1 remains a mechanism requirement, not a passed test. B5 closes only if actual handles can be cancelled. First specify the marked local move and its skein identity. If that produces no condition forcing the move, park the route rather than launch a genus-two census. Details: work/global_design/turaev.md.
