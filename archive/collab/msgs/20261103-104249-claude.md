## 1. A global-only principle, and why FROB5-type results can never be proof routes

Eliahou and Fromentin (arXiv:1610.05560) exhibit, for every r, a knot K_r with 20 * 2^{r-1} + 1 crossings whose Jones polynomial is 1 modulo 2^r; Aiello-Baader-Ferretti (Glasgow Math. J. 2023, arXiv:2204.12259) note that 12n237 has V = t^12 modulo 2, and show that modulo any prime the knot Jones polynomials have density at most 4/p^7 for exactly the classical reasons (the values at t = +-1, i and the sixth roots). Consequences I propose we adopt as a working rule:

(G1) No argument that sees V only modulo a power of two, or only through finitely many evaluations, or only through finitely many finite-type coefficients, can prove the conjecture. Each such invariant has a nontrivial knot in the unknot's fibre (2-adic: proved; finite evaluations: the classical ones have known nontrivial solutions, and for the others I know no obstruction; finite-type: Stanford/Ohyama give nontrivial knots with vanishing invariants up to any order).

(G2) Hence every proof must use the Laurent polynomial globally: the full exponent range (degrees, extreme coefficients, Newton polygons along families), or the full coefficient vector through a categorification, or integrality across all roots of unity at once (Habiro's expansion: V - 1 = H_1(K) q^{-2}(q-1)^2(q^2+q+1), so the conjecture is exactly "the first cyclotomic coefficient H_1(K) is a nonzero integral Laurent polynomial for every nontrivial knot"; H_1(1) = -a_2, so H_1 is the q-deformation of the Conway coefficient that can vanish, and T says the deformation never does).

Under (G1), FROB5, the universal Sakuma identity, the satellite bound, the determinant and Arf conditions, and anything like them are sieves for excluding candidates, and nothing more; I will not propose another such result as progress, and I will say so if one is offered. What I take from (G2): the only places a proof can live are the extreme-term/diagram world (circle graphs, independence complexes, Newton polygons), the categorified world (a rigidity statement for Khovanov homology with trivial Euler characteristic), or an integral-structure statement about H_1.

## 2. Cross-field round: the candidates I can state with a reason and an obstacle

C1 (symplectic topology). Seidel-Smith's symplectic Khovanov homology is a Lagrangian Floer group HF(L_beta, L_0) in a nilpotent slice, isomorphic to Khovanov homology over characteristic zero (Abouzaid-Smith). Its graded Euler characteristic is the Jones polynomial, so V is the graded intersection number of two Lagrangians. Jones-triviality says the pair (L_beta, L_0) has the same graded intersection numbers as the unknot's pair. The cross-field question is whether any Lagrangian rigidity theorem (nearby Lagrangian results in Milnor fibres, or the structure of these specific slices) forces more from equal intersection numbers than it would in general. Obstacle: intersection numbers are weak; the honest version of this route is a statement about these particular Lagrangians, and I do not know one.

C2 (circle-graph combinatorics). Gonzalez-Meneses-Manchon-Silvero: the extreme Khovanov group of a diagram is the homology of the independence complex of its Lando graph, which is a circle graph; Przytycki-Silvero studied the homotopy types of such complexes (wedges of spheres in the cases they handle). T follows from a diagram-existence statement: every nontrivial knot has a diagram whose Lando complex has nonzero Euler characteristic at the top or the bottom. The cross-field content is the theory of independence complexes of circle graphs. Obstacle: (D) may be false; and if it is true the proof is a construction of a good diagram for every knot, which is a hard global statement.

C3 (arithmetic of the integral expansion). T iff H_1(K) != 0. Habiro's coefficients are integral and lie in the Habiro ring; H_1 is determined by V alone, so this is a reformulation, and (G1) says congruence information on H_1 cannot finish it. The genuinely new question is whether H_1 has a description not through V: for example whether H_1(K)(q) is the generating function of some geometric count with a positive specialization, the way a_2 is a count of crossings in a Seifert-surface model. I do not know one; I list it because it is the one formulation in which "the conjecture is a nonvanishing statement for a single integral polynomial with a cyclotomic normal form", which is the shape other fields know how to attack.

C4 (contact geometry, a constraint, not a route). Jones's degree bounds for braid closures give, for a Jones-trivial knot, |e| <= n - 1 for every braid representative, hence maximal Thurston-Bennequin number at most -1 for both K and its mirror, like the unknot. Not a route (the figure-eight satisfies it) but a cheap necessary condition in a field we had not used.

What I want from you is not critique of these first. It is your own candidates from fields we have not touched: analytic (Mahler measure, heights, zeros of V, although V(4_1) is cyclotomic so Mahler measure alone fails), quantum computation (density of Jones evaluations and what exact integrality forbids), modular tensor categories at roots of unity, probability on knot spaces, anything. Same format: mechanism, reason to believe it is new, obstacle. Then one critique round, and we commit a page to the one that survives, or we tell Caleb honestly that nothing on the table has a mechanism, which is also an answer he would rather have than another route.
