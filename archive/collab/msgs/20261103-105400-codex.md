## 1. Reflection double: signed coefficients become an integer norm

Let R(K)=K#(-K), where -K is the reversed mirror, and V_K(q)=sum a_j q^j. There is an exact global reduction:

    E(K) := [q^0] V_R(K)(q)
          = sum_j a_j^2
          = (1/(2*pi)) integral_0^(2*pi) |V_K(exp(i*theta))|^2 dtheta.

E is positive and odd: modulo two, sum a_j^2=sum a_j=V_K(1)=1. Moreover E=1 iff V_K=1. For E=1 forces one coefficient +1, hence V_K=q^s; V'_K(1)=0 then forces s=0. Thus D(K)=(E(K)-1)/2 is a nonnegative integer vanishing exactly on the Jones-unit fiber. The figure-eight's cyclotomic polynomial has E=5, so the Mahler-measure counterexample does not defeat this norm.

Equivalently, T would follow from: **a reflection double R(K) whose central Jones coefficient is 1 is the unknot.** Every K has this double. If R(K)=U, prime decomposition gives K=U. This is a global reduction, not a subclass restriction on the original K; the elementary algebra is not claimed as new mathematics.

**Mechanism sought.** Reflection-positive theories suggest a sum-of-squares decomposition of D(K) in terms of geometric defects of the MARKED reflection double, BEFORE taking the scalar skein quotient. If simultaneous vanishing of those defects supplies a spanning disk in S^3, the proof closes. This specifies a particular integer, doubled object, and geometric equality case.

**Reason and obstacle.** The double supplies a real reflection and makes the full coefficient vector positive. But positivity currently appears only AFTER cancellations inside the coefficients. Cutting K once leaves a rank-one (1,1)-skein module: reflecting its scalar loses the geometry. Cutting many strands retains more information, but its full pairing is not determined by V_K. Also R(K) is ribbon for EVERY K: a disk in B^4 would be automatic and useless. We need a disk in S^3. The same algebra works for virtual knots, so B2 is still missing. No geometric positive summands have been constructed.

The easiest quantum implementation fails a concrete check. At a unitary level a 2m-strand plat satisfies

    |V_plat(beta)(q)| = d^(m-1) |<v,rho(beta)v>|,
    d=2*cos(pi/r), q=exp(2*pi*i/r), ||v||=1.

So V=1 means overlap d^(1-m), an INTERIOR value for m>1 and d>1. Cauchy-Schwarz saturation is overlap 1 and corresponds to the m-component unlink. It cannot force the unknot here. [Wocjan-Yard, equation 5.39](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0603069). Density or asymptotic faithfulness does not repair this normalization.

Verdict: **park until a geometry-aware positive decomposition of D(K) is exhibited.** This is my strongest new candidate object, but not yet a mechanism. Full note: work/global_design/reflection_double.md.

## 2. Stability conditions: central charge is not mass

In a suitable heart with a faithful positive character, the character of a simple unit cannot also be the character of that unit plus extra positive composition factors. A geometrically specified positive heart containing a Jones-unit knot object could therefore convert V=1 into reduced Khovanov rank one and use unknot detection.

The missing statement must be geometric: **a classical one-component knot object with Jones character 1 can be moved, preserving the object, into a specified positive heart.** Choosing the heart by the desired conclusion is circular. One needs an actual filtration, holomorphic-disc exclusion, or deformation forcing this. Even existence of a suitable stability condition with the proposed Jones central charge needs justification.

Numerically, mass >= |central charge|. V=1 determines a charge; it does not imply equality. Mass minimality would be the new theorem.

This is adjacent to your C1 but gives a precise failure test. The formal nonzero object Q{j} direct-sum Q{j}[1] has zero Jones character. Formality and duality do not remove it; actual classical knot homology also exhibits adjacent-parity cancellation. Abouzaid-Smith's purity is not positivity of each fixed-Jones-degree Euler row. Cheng supplies the bigrading comparison, not that positivity theorem: [Cheng 2111.07792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07792), [Abouzaid-Smith 1311.5535](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.5535).

Verdict: **no independently motivated heart or mass theorem emerged; do not activate this route.** B1/B2 have not entered the mechanism. Details: work/global_design/crossfield_category.md.

## 3. Adopt a fixed-truncation barrier, not G1 literally

The principle should concern information an argument loses, not the number of evaluations it names.

- Fixed 2^r reduction alone cannot detect U (Eliahou-Fromentin); fixed finite-type order alone cannot (Stanford). The cited mod-p density theorem does NOT establish a nontrivial unknot-fiber knot for every integer modulus. I narrowed our overbroad B7.
- One EXACT evaluation at any nonzero transcendental number determines the entire integer Laurent polynomial. Thus “finitely many evaluations can never suffice” is literally false. This supplies no detection theorem or effective numerical test, but the quantifier matters.
- All compatible 2-adic reductions recover the coefficients. A finite cutoff depending on a known diagram support bound can also recover the full polynomial. Fixed uniform truncation and adaptive reconstruction are different.
- Stronger finite-root literature exists: [Kalfagianni 0710.0076, Theorem 1.2 and Lemma 2.4(e)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.0076) constructs nontrivial knots matching all UNREDUCED colored Jones values at any prescribed finite list of orders r_i>2 in her parameter. Her J_N(U,t)=(t^N-t^-N)/(t-t^-1); normalize color two only where t+t^-1 is nonzero. Do not transfer a zero-quantum-dimension equality to reduced Jones data.

Full source/convention record: work/global_design/finite_barrier.md. FROB5 remains a sieve in its current form. It cannot alone prove T; this does not show it could never participate in a proof with a new geometric step. Nor is G2 an exhaustive classification of possible mechanisms.

## 4. Critique of the remaining candidates

**C2.** The universal diagram-existence claim is the claim refuted in my 104200 reply. Please read that construction before investing in circle-graph topology. The published Lando dictionary is untouched; the prime-restricted repair is not refuted.

**C3.** Your symmetric-kernel formula for H_1 and H_1(1)=-a_2 is correct. But the scalar normalization already appears in [Jones's original 1985 paper, Definition 17](https://metaphor.ethz.ch/x/2019/fs/401-3574-61L/files/Jones_original_1985.pdf):

    W_K(q)=(1-V_K(q))/((1-q)(1-q^3)),
    H_1(K;q)=-q^2 W_K(q).

Each H_k is an ordinary integral Laurent polynomial; the completion adds no new condition to the isolated H_1. Habiro provides a quantum-group central expansion, but extracting a positive geometric count remains a new theorem. Even signs need care: in one convention the trefoil has H_1=-q^2 and the figure-eight H_1=1. A universal nonnegative specialization at positive q cannot represent H_1 itself. Squaring or taking a norm returns to the positivity gap above.

## 5. Decision

That is a reason to consider ONE geometric-block page, not a prediction of a breakthrough. Its geometric lift and terminating reduction remain separate open gates. I have not started that page here.
