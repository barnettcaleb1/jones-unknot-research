## 1. A kernel decomposition that makes the entire state sum transparent

Over F2 let P=P^T=P^2, C=im(P), and C-perp=ker(P). For a coordinate set S and its diagonal projector D_S,

    ker(P+D_S) = (C intersect F2^S) direct-sum (C-perp intersect F2^(E-S)).   (1)

Proof: if Pv=D_Sv, put u=Pv and w=(I+P)v. Then u=D_Sv is supported in S, w=D_(E-S)v is supported in its complement, and v=u+w. The converse is immediate.

Let G generate C, let k=rank(G), and let r be its column-matroid rank. With T=E-S this gives

    nullity(P+D_S)=k+|T|-2r(T).                                             (2)

Also P=G^T(GG^T)^(-1)G and Cauchy-Binet says det(GG^T) equals the number of matroid bases modulo two. Thus the LCD condition is exactly odd basis count.

For the natural checkerboard choice C is the cut space of the Tait graph, G is its reduced incidence matrix, and k=r(E). Formula (2) is the number of state boundary circles minus one. Therefore the full projection enumerator is the signed Tutte state sum, with no extra scalar information. The checkerboard interpretation was already present in [Shtylla-Traldi-Zulli's final Remark](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.2933), and the Jones/projection connection predates it; see [Traldi-Zulli, Remark 3.1](https://sites.lafayette.edu/traldil/files/2010/06/topreals.pdf).

This corrects the strategic emphasis in 104200: P^2=P is real classical input, but its code interpretation does not add a new restriction beyond the existing planar signed-graph formulation. Arbitrary LCD codes are not a substitute: the circle-graph/planarity conditions remain essential.

## 2. A complete local block calculation, with geometry

Write the bracket in these coordinates as

    F = sum_(T subset E) A^(sum_(e in T) epsilon_e - sum_(e notin T) epsilon_e)
                        delta^(k+|T|-2r(T)),
    delta=-A^2-A^-2, epsilon_e in {+1,-1}.

For a parallel nonloop pair e,f with opposite signs, fix an outside state T and put d=r(T+e)-r(T), either 0 or 1. Relative to selecting neither edge, the four selections contribute

    1+(A^2+A^-2)delta^(1-2d)+delta^(2-2d)=1.                               (3)

Deleting both edges does not lower rank in an odd-basis matroid: otherwise every basis contains exactly one of e,f, and exchanging them pairs the bases. Thus (3) deletes the pair from F exactly. The dual operation contracts an opposite-signed series pair. In projector coordinates, equal rows can be deleted in pairs while retaining idempotence, since the equal rank-one contributions cancel over F2.

These really lift geometrically. Reembed a plane graph so a parallel pair bounds an empty bigon, preserving the signed abstract graph. [Cheng-Gao, Theorem 4.1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10114-013-0679-5) realizes the reembedding through diagram isotopies and mutations; the bigon then admits RII. Apply the dual argument to a series pair. Mutation preserves unknot status, so this is adequate for a detection induction, although it need not preserve the original knot type at every intermediate step. An isolated interlacement coordinate is a Tait loop or bridge and gives a nugatory crossing, removable by the usual ball rotation; it need not be an immediately empty monogon.

There is no third kind of two-coordinate twin block. If two rows of P agree outside their two positions with nonzero common outside vector v, and their internal block is [[a,b],[b,d]], the off-block equations of P^2=P give (a+d)v=0. Hence a=d. Either the full rows of P agree, or the rows of I+P agree: parallel or series. If v=0, the two-by-two idempotence equations force b=0, leaving isolated coordinates.

This classifies these local blocks and their outside-state-by-outside-state cancellation. It does not classify cancellations among different outside states. Written algebra: work/global_design/projection_codes.md. Geometry and conventions: work/global_design/projection_geometry.md.

## 3. An infinite obstruction to forcing a local block

**Proposition (written proof; independent internal checks, your read requested).** For every m>=4 with 3 not dividing m, there is a classical 2m-crossing unknot diagram whose graphic projection has no isolated or two-coordinate twin block. None can be created by Whitney moves preserving its cycle matroid.

**Construction and proof.** Take the plane wheel W_m with m rim vertices and a hub. Its reduced mod-two Laplacian, deleting the hub, is I+Adj(C_m). A kernel vector satisfies

    x_(i+1)=x_i+x_(i-1).

All nonzero recurrence orbits have period three. The cyclic length-m condition therefore leaves only the zero vector when 3 does not divide m. The reduced Laplacian is invertible; equivalently the wheel has an odd number of spanning trees. By the component-number theorem ([Silver-Williams, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.6815)), its medial shadow is one-component.

Choose a base point and give that shadow the descending assignment: the first visit to each crossing is over. This is an actual unknot diagram, so V=1. Its unsigned Tait graph is still W_m.

But W_m is simple and 3-edge-connected. Its cycle matroid has no loops, coloops, parallel pairs, or series pairs. By the block characterization above its projector has neither isolated coordinates nor twins. These matroid properties survive Whitney 2-isomorphisms and embedding changes. Hence no move from Section 2 can even start. QED.

This is stronger than a small hard-unknot test: it is an infinite structural obstruction, established without a census. It is not a Jones counterexample. It refutes only forced local-block reduction, including its matroid-preserving preliminary moves. It does not refute a route using RIII/Delta-Y, temporary stabilizations, or other transformations that change the matroid. Nor does it address arbitrary hidden Conway spheres unrelated to these graph moves.

## 4. What this means for the global strategy
