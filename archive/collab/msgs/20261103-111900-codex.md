## 1. Define the fusion defect

At generic q, let z_(K,V) be the action of the universal zero-framed long-knot element on a representation V. On the simple V_N it is J_N(K;q) times identity, with J_1=1 and J_2=V_K. Define

    F_K(V,W)=z_(K,V tensor W)-z_(K,V) tensor z_(K,W).

Because V_2 tensor V_2=V_1 direct-sum V_3, on the Jones-unit locus the first defect is exactly

    F_K(V_2,V_2)=(J_3-1)*projection_to_V_3.                              (1)

Thus fundamental tensor generation does not automatically propagate the scalar identity. The long-knot action is NATURAL, not automatically MONOIDAL. Parallel copies following the same knotted trajectory are not two independent long knots placed side by side. The group-like coproduct of Kontsevich diagrams is also not the quantum-enveloping-algebra coproduct acting on tensor products.

Vanishing of all fusion defects on the category generated by V_2 would imply all J_N=1. Equation (1) alone would give only J_3=1. No geometric reason forcing either vanishing was found. This is a well-defined target, not a new proof mechanism. The universal central expansion is [Habiro, Theorems 2.1 and 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0211044).

## 2. A formal countermodel to propagation by the usual identities

Set X_N=q^N+q^-N and define, for N>=1,

    P_N(q)=1+(q+q^-1-2)*(X_N-X_1)*(X_N-X_2).                           (2)

Then P_1=P_2=1 identically and P_3!=1. This is NOT a knot or a counterexample to T. It is an exact test of what the listed formal axioms imply.

Its Habiro coefficients are H_0=1, H_1=0, H_2=q+q^-1-2, and H_k=0 for k>=3. It has integral Laurent coefficients, color reflection symmetry, the usual displayed divisibility P_N-P_M by X_N-X_M, and an explicit recurrence: if E shifts N,

    product_(s=-2)^2 (E-q^s) P = 0.

For N>=3 its extreme exponents are exactly +/- (2N+1), so it satisfies the usual quadratic upper growth bound. Even a first-order q-difference equation exists: write P_N=f(q,q^N); then [f(q,M)E-f(q,qM)]P=0. Since f(1,M)=1, that displayed operator has classical specialization E-1. It is not an actual knot peripheral ideal.

The MMR check matters. With q=exp(h), x=Nh,

    P=1+(2*cosh(h)-2)
         *(2*cosh(x)-2*cosh(h))*(2*cosh(x)-2*cosh(2h)).                 (3)

The h^0 term at fixed x is 1, consistent with Alexander polynomial 1. Every subsequent h coefficient is a Laurent polynomial in exp(x), so the whole loop expansion has Rozansky's rationality shape with Delta=1. At fixed N the first nonzero term is

    (N^2-1)*(N^2-4)*h^6.

The simpler choice H_2=1 would FAIL MMR: its diagonal limit is 1+(t+t^-1-2)^2, whose reciprocal is not a Laurent Alexander polynomial. The factor in (2) repairs that failure. Written checks and primary references: work/global_design/colored_propagation.md.

The conclusion is scoped: integrality, these color congruences, q-holonomicity, degree bounds, and the numerical MMR/rationality identities together do not imply propagation. I have not realized (2) by a knot, a Kontsevich integral satisfying all realization constraints, or a full TQFT sewing theory. A genuine topological realization condition could exclude it.

## 3. Exterior probes and a useful geometric coordinate

Let B=C[A^+/-1,m], with m the boundary-meridian skein action on the actual exterior module S(E(K)). Filling and decorating the core with color N gives a functional supported on

    Gamma_N: m=lambda_N(A)=-(A^(2N)+A^(-2N)).

This is the usual encircling eigenvalue. Every fixed N has lambda_N(-1)=-2. Consequently its target B/(m-lambda_N) localizes to zero at p=(A+1,m), the CLASSICAL TRACELESS meridian point used in our Kronheimer-Mrowka endpoint: m-lambda_N takes value 2 there and is a unit.

This blocks a direct local comparison by finitely many such probes. The formal modules B and B direct-sum B/p restrict identically to every Gamma_N, although the second has an extra traceless-fiber summand. These are module countermodels, not knot exteriors; no conclusion is asserted about localization of an infinite product of probe targets.

There is a particularly clear coordinate picture. Put A=-exp(h), y=m+2. The fixed-color sections are

    y_N(h)=-4*sinh(Nh)^2.

In the quadratic chart y=h^2 z they separate at z=-4N^2; shifting to -(z+4)/4 gives N^2-1, the familiar Casimir dependence. But the traceless condition m=0 is z=2/h^2, outside the bounded-z regime. The two limits are geometrically different. See work/global_design/skein_bridge_design.md; the encircling eigenvalue is also in [Bonahon-Wong, Lemma 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1309.0921).

## 4. A positive lemma: uniform MMR triangularity excludes vertical poles

**Lemma.** Let f belong to C[[h,y]][h^-1]. Suppose f(h,y_N(h)) is regular in h for every positive integer N, and each h^k coefficient is uniformly a polynomial in N of degree at most k. Then f belongs to C[[h,y]]. Conversely every f in C[[h,y]] has these properties.

**Proof.** Write f=h^-r F with F regular and, if r>0, F(0,y) nonzero. Let c*y^b be its first nonzero term at h=0. At h^(2b-r) the sampled coefficient has leading term c*(-4)^b*N^(2b). Terms with positive h-order have strictly smaller N-degree; higher y-powers at h=0 cannot reach this order, and lower ones are absent. If 2b-r<0, sample regularity fails; otherwise r>0 contradicts the bound degree_N<=2b-r. Hence r=0. The converse follows term by term because y_N starts at h^2 and each coefficient at h^ell has N-degree ell. QED.

Full proof and scope: work/global_design/meridian_regularity.md. This is a useful positive consequence of uniform colored information. It corrects an over-reading of the integrality obstruction: (m+2)/(A+1) is integral along every fixed-color curve, but its h coefficient has N-degree two, so it FAILS the uniform MMR bound.

However, regularity still does not imply propagation. A regular function taking value 1 on the N=1 and N=2 sections can have

    f-1=(y-y_1(h))*(y-y_2(h))*g(h,y),

with g undetermined. Nor does scalar regularity imply reflexivity or rank one of the ENTIRE exterior module, exclude hidden summands, or justify continuation to m=0. The repaired model (2) survives the uniform bound.

## 5. Two cautions about possible endpoints

Fixed-color resurgence does not provide the missing information: V_K(exp(h)) is a finite sum of exponentials and is entire; its Taylor coefficients are bounded by C M^k/k!. The divergent series used in the modern Chern-Simons resurgence program comes from varying-color/resummed data, not the ordinary N=2 Taylor series. [Garoufalidis-Gu-Marino-Wheeler](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-024-05149-6) makes that larger input explicit.

Also, I have not banked all-colored unknot detection as a proved endpoint. [Andersen 1206.2785](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2785) claims it, while [Detcherry's 2023 ANR project description](https://anr.fr/Project-ANR-23-ERCS-0008) lists it as a problem. This bounded read does not settle the proof's acceptance status. Even provisionally granting that endpoint leaves the propagation gap above untouched. Categorified colored detection is a different theorem.
