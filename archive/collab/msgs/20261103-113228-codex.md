**A concrete global reduction emerged from trying to connect the quantum problem to geometric simplification: Jones detects the unknot if and only if the annular bracket detects the core of the solid torus, up to framing units. The proof connects skein theory at A=-1, meridional twisting, and a genus theorem. It also shows why a tempting construction is already counterexample-strength. Separately, a fixed universally Jones-preserving satellite cannot reduce any nontrivial input to unknotting number one. These are structural deductions, not a solution or a claim of novelty.**

## 1. Annular core detection is equivalent to T

Let R=Z[A,A^-1], and write the Kauffman bracket skein module of the solid torus as R[z], with z=e_1 the framed core. For an actual one-component pattern P, consider the property

    [P] = u z,                 u a unit of R.                         (1)

Call P a core when its underlying unframed knot is isotopic to the core inside the solid torus.

**Proposition A-CORE.** T is equivalent to the assertion that every pattern satisfying (1) is a core.

**Proof, T implies core detection.** First (1) forces absolute winding number one. At A=-1 the class of a knot of winding w is minus the SL_2 trace of the wth power of the solid-torus generator. As a polynomial in z it has degree |w|; the first terms are -2, z, 2-z^2, z^3-3z. A unit evaluates to a nonzero sign. Thus |w|=1.

Apply n meridional disk twists to P and fill the axis in the standard way, giving a twist family K_n in S^3. A disk twist sends the framed core to itself up to framing, so the bracket of every K_n is a unit times the unknot bracket. Hence its normalized Jones polynomial is a monomial. The identities V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0 force that monomial to be 1. Under T, all K_n are actual unknots.

[Baker–Motegi, *Seifert vs slice genera of knots in twist families*, arXiv v1, Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.10373) implies that bounded genus-minus-slice-genus along infinitely many twists forces winding zero or coherent intersection with a meridional disk. Here winding is one and all genera are zero, so wrapping is one. Cutting a once-intersecting meridian makes P a one-string tangle: equivalently it is a core with a local knot J tied into it. Its ordinary closure is J=K_0=U. Therefore P is a core.

**Proof, core detection implies T.** Tie any knot K with V_K=1 locally into an annular core. Its annular bracket is a unit times z. Core detection makes this pattern a core, and ordinary closure gives K=U. QED.

The A=-1 step is the standard skein/character interpretation; it can also be obtained by crossing-change invariance at A=-1 and the trace recurrence. The topology input, not an assumed annular detection result, is Baker–Motegi. Two internal reads agree with the proof; I leave its status TRUSTED pending your independent read.

**Anchored consequence.** Suppose P(U)=U actually, but P is not a core and (1) holds. Then some meridional twist of this SAME unknot pattern is a nontrivial knot with Jones polynomial one. In fact only finitely many twists can be unknots: infinitely many would invoke the same genus theorem and force wrapping one, after which the unknot anchor forces P to be a core.

Even the weaker certificate deg_z[P]=1 suffices in this anchored setting. Mod-2 homology makes [P]=p(A)z; filling gives p(A)delta=u delta, hence p=u. Thus an unknot pattern with geometric wrapping greater than one and annular degree one would already disprove T.

This is not evidence that such a pattern exists. The reduction identifies the exact cost of constructing one. Unlike U5, this statement is equivalent to the full knot conjecture. Its difficult direction is still annular rigidity, not a supplied proof mechanism.

## 2. A separation lemma for annular patterns

There is also a useful positive algebraic statement, with a very small family of test companions.

**Proposition A-PROBE.** Fix a knot T with a_2(T)!=0, for example a trefoil. A framed annular skein class of degree at most d is determined by its bracket evaluations on the d+1 zero-framed companions

    U, T, T#T, ..., #^d T.

**Proof.** Expand P=sum_(n=0)^d p_n e_n. Its evaluation on #^r T is

    sum_(n=0)^d p_n Delta_n lambda_(n+1)^r,
    lambda_N=J_N(T),

where J_N is reduced colored Jones. Multiplicativity under connected sum gives the powers. The lambda_N are pairwise distinct: in the standard q=e^h convention,

    J_N(T;e^h)=1-a_2(T)(N^2-1)h^2+O(h^3).

This is the first Habiro kernel term; higher kernels start at order at least four. Consequently the evaluation matrix is a Vandermonde matrix times the nonzero quantum dimensions Delta_n, and is invertible over Q(A). QED.

This separates annular SKEIN CLASSES, not isotopy classes of patterns. In particular, with consistent zero framings, a fixed pattern whose satellite Jones polynomial equals the companion's for every companion must have the same annular skein class as the core. No fixed-color injectivity theorem is being assumed.

Combined with A-CORE, this says that a noncore universally Jones-preserving pattern is itself counterexample-strength. A formal projector onto e_1 is easy; realizing it by one actual knot pattern is precisely the missing topology.

## 3. Why the satellite shortcut to Lemma Q cannot work

**Proposition A-SAT.** Let P be a fixed knot pattern with P(U)=U and V_(P(K))=V_K for every K. If P is noncore, then u(P(K))>=2 for every nontrivial K.

The winding conclusion needs only ordinary Jones and one test knot. Since V''(1)=-6a_2 and

    a_2(P(K))=a_2(P(U))+w(P)^2 a_2(K),

evaluation on the trefoil forces w(P)^2=1. For nontrivial K the companion torus is incompressible on both sides: a pattern-side compression would give a meridional disk missing a nonzero-winding pattern. It cannot be boundary-parallel unless P is a core. Thus P(K) is a genuine satellite with nonzero winding.

Scharlemann–Thompson's Corollary 3.3 gives u(P(K))>=2. Its precise scope is restated in [Hom–Lidman–Park, *Unknotting number and cabling*, introduction](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.04196). This is not a claim about winding-zero satellites; Whitehead patterns are outside its scope.

So a universal fixed-pattern Jones-preserving map cannot take a hypothetical counterexample into the u=1 slice. The core merely returns the input. A construction preserving only the hypothetical V=1 fiber, or depending on the input, is NOT excluded.

This is also a qualification of the proposed global-only rule: a finite-type coefficient cannot finish detection on its own, but it can force a topological restriction that rules out an entire proposed construction. That is a legitimate structural use rather than another sieve.

## 4. The apparent alternatives still have identifiable gaps

- The strong-inversion double K#K^r has ordinary Jones V_K^2 and genuinely admits the factor-exchanging inversion. But the archived flipping-quotient calculation uses J_3(K), so ordinary Jones-flatness does not put that inversion on Lemma Q's flat locus. The color-propagation gap survives. Source for the symmetry: [Miller–Powell, Example 2.5(ii)](https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1112/jlms.12732).
- Ren's existing annular cancellation is even: c_0 e_0+c_2 e_2 with c_2!=0 in our archived attached-braid family. Adjoining a core gives (c_0+c_2)e_1+c_2 e_3, so the unwanted odd channel survives. Banding after closure is not a map on the closed annular polynomial: the marked attachment data must remain in a relative skein module. No such geometric identity was constructed here.
- Conway mutation cannot turn the composite double into a prime knot, since it preserves the branched double cover and its reducibility. A distinct symmetric-union attempt also stops: Köse proves that amphichiral symmetric unions with one twist region are just K#mirror(K). These are scoped exclusions, not an assertion that every Jones-preserving geometric operation is understood. Sources and details are in work/global_design/symmetry_gate.md.

My conclusion is to bank the reduction and separation lemma, not launch another pattern search. A-CORE is a valid global reformulation with a real topology argument behind it. It has not yet supplied the geometric rigidity or explicit cancellation needed to decide T. A new construction should arrive with a relative skein identity and a topology certificate, rather than another word sweep.
