Astra,

An addendum to the cross-field round, from Caleb directly. His themes, in his words: dynamical approaches; entropy or energy functionals; perturb the knots, make them fill up more space, duplicate, move around; show that systems are equivalent to other systems; projection analysis; think outside the box. Please take these as a second list alongside your own, in the same format (candidate statement, mechanism, why not excluded by the global-only principle, obstacle, first lemma). Here are the mathematical hooks I see in each, so that you can go past them rather than repeat them.

Projection analysis. A space curve has a diagram for every generic direction v in S^2 and V is the same for all; by the Gonzalez-Meneses-Manchon-Silvero identification, a Jones-trivial knot has vanishing Lando Euler characteristic at both ends in EVERY direction. Milnor proved total curvature > 4 pi for nontrivial knots by averaging a diagram statistic (number of maxima) over directions; Freedman-He express the average crossing number through the Mobius energy; the writhe integral is the direction-average of diagram writhe. So the question is whether some extreme-state statistic, forced to vanish in every direction when V = 1, has a direction-average that is an integral-geometric invariant provably nonzero for nontrivial curves. Sign cancellation across directions is the obvious obstacle, and configuration-space integrals compute finite-type invariants, which the principle forbids; the statistic has to be one that sees the exponent range, not a coefficient.

Energy. Parseval turns T into an energy statement: (1/2 pi) int |V(e^{i theta})|^2 d theta = sum of the squared coefficients, which is 1 exactly when V = 1 and at least 2 otherwise; and |V(e^{i theta})|^2 = V_{K # K*}(e^{i theta}) is a nonnegative trigonometric polynomial. So T says every nontrivial knot has Jones L^2-energy at least 2. The geometric energies (Mobius, ropelength, total curvature) only bound crossing number from above, hence Jones span from above; a lower bound would need a different mechanism, and Kanenobu's families (bounded span, unbounded crossing number) say it cannot be crossing number itself.

Dynamics. Twist families and braid powers give linear recurrences for V (your Newton-polygon lemma is the right algebra), so Jones-trivial members of any such family form a finite union of arithmetic progressions, and an infinite progression forces root-of-unity eigenvalues on the support. Pseudo-Anosov growth versus Jones growth (Band-Boyland for Burau) is the natural place for an entropy argument, but a single knot has no family unless we choose one through it; the choice (twist at a crossing, powers of a braid representative, cabling) is the design decision.

Equivalent systems. Potts and random-cluster partition functions on the Tait graph, Temperley-Lieb transfer matrices, Wilson loops in Chern-Simons, anyon amplitudes at roots of unity with Jones's unitarity bound |V(zeta_r)| <= d^{n-1}, sums over state surfaces. The question for each is whether that system has a theorem (positivity, reflection positivity, a vacuum-uniqueness or perfect-fidelity no-go across all levels) that forbids a nontrivial knot from looking like the unknot at every level at once.

Perturbation and duplication. What V_K determines: V of connected sums, the mirror, the whole twist family at any crossing, band-move partners through the skein relation; what it does not: cables, satellites, Whitehead doubles (those need J_3). One structural consequence worth stating: a minimal Jones-trivial knot either has a V-preserving crossing, which generates an infinite Jones-trivial family, or every one of its crossings is Jones-rigid.
