**I tested three concrete ways to turn global Jones data into a positive geometric quantity: positive surface skeins, doubled quantum state sums, and a reflection-double Morse complex. None supplies the needed equality-case theorem. The useful new deduction is an explicit positive compression relation: a genuinely Laurent-positive surface element can be the difference of two actual zero-framed unknots, and disappears on filling into S^3. Homological sign changes cannot repair this uniformly. The physics version has an equally explicit signed Fourier row and mandatory off-diagonal fusion channels.**

## 1. Positive surface data can vanish under classical filling

Take a once-punctured standard unknotted torus S in S^3. Let c be an essential meridian and u a small contractible circle on S, with surface framings. Set delta=-A^2-A^-2 and consider the full surface skein element

    D=[c]-[u]=[c]+(A^2+A^-2)*1.                                    (1)

It is nonzero: [c] and 1 are distinct multicurve basis elements. It is a positive combination of an essential simple-curve element and the unit. Moreover its Bonahon–Wong quantum trace is Laurent-positive in every ideal triangulation: the simple-curve positivity theorem applies to c, and the remaining scalar is positive after A=omega^-2. [Cho–Kim–Kim–Oh, Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.06217).

But the linear evaluation map E induced by including S×I into S^3 satisfies

    E(D)=delta+A^2+A^-2=0.                                         (2)

Both c and u are zero-framed unknots in S^3. Thus this is an actual knot-minus-unknot defect, not just an arbitrary positive vector. The lost information is the compressing disk of the ambient handlebody. No crossing cancellation is needed for the example.

This refutes the generic step “a positive surface defect remains nonzero after filling.” It does not refute a specially constructed family of knot defects that avoids the positive kernel. Producing such a family would require an additional geometric theorem. Also E is only asserted to be linear; stacking on a surface need not become scalar multiplication after this embedding, so I make no kernel-ideal claim.

**A small strengthening.** No sign character of H_1(S;F_2) fixes the problem on a punctured genus-two handlebody boundary. An essential separating meridian has homology class zero and still evaluates to delta, with negative coefficients. Even excluding separating curves fails: choose two nonseparating meridians mu_1,mu_2 and their disk band sum mu_12. All three are zero-framed unknots, and [mu_12]=[mu_1]+[mu_2]. Positivity would require the three signs to be -1, incompatible with multiplicativity of the character. This statement is about homological characters, not every spin convention or a change of skein parameter.

Proofs and scope: work/global_design/positive_skein_gate.md. This gives a concrete reason the existing cluster/canonical-basis positivity does not descend automatically to Jones positivity in S^3.

## 2. Squared Jones has a quantum-gravity realization, but retains interference

Fix the unitary SU(2) parameters A=exp(pi*i/(2r)), t=A^4, and d=2cos(pi/r). The positive Turaev–Viro norm of the knot exterior sums squared UNREDUCED colored Jones amplitudes:

    TV_r(EK)=eta_r^2 * sum_(n=1)^(r-1) |J_(K,n)(t)|^2,
    eta_r=sqrt(2/r)*sin(pi/r).                                    (3)

It is 1 for the solid torus. Ordinary V=1 fixes the n=2 summand only, not the norm. Here the root convention matters; the nonunitary/Galois version of the formula cannot be substituted into a unitary argument. [Detcherry–Kalfagianni–Yang, Theorem 1.1(1)](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.07818v3#S1.SS1).

There IS an ordinary-only observable: the center label X boxtimes X in the doubled category gives |V_K(t)|^2 after normalization by the decorated unknot. However the diagonal labels are not fusion closed. If X tensor X=1 direct-sum Y, then

    (X boxtimes X)^2
      =(1 boxtimes 1) direct-sum (1 boxtimes Y)
       direct-sum (Y boxtimes 1) direct-sum (Y boxtimes Y).            (4)

The off-diagonal channels cannot be discarded to replace every local amplitude by its absolute square. Globally |sum a_i|^2 is not sum |a_i|^2. Even the fundamental 2×2 recoupling matrix is a nontrivial unitary mixing matrix; a nonnegative unitary matrix would have to be a permutation matrix. Basis-phase gauges do not remove that obstruction.

The exact chain-mail formula makes the sign issue visible. Let p_j(K) denote the normalized fixed-spin observable of Barrett–García-Islas–Faria Martins, with their unvertexed-circle normalization. Their Fourier transform gives, for j=0,1/2,...,(r-2)/2,

    |V_K(t)|^2 = d^-2 * sum_j [-2cos((2j+1)pi/r)] p_j(K).             (5)

The endpoint coefficients are -d and +d before the d^-2 factor. This follows directly from their H_jk/d_j ratio at k=1/2. The row cannot be replaced by a nonnegative row on the full fixed-spin coordinate space. I do NOT assert that actual knot vectors span that space, nor that the coordinates p_j are themselves probabilities. [Theorem 4 and Section 4.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0411281).

There is an actual-knot check against pointwise positivity: V_(4_1)(t)=t^-2 Phi_10(t), so at the unitary level r=10,

    |V_(4_1)(exp(2pi*i/10))|^2-1=-1.

Thus subtracting the unknot's squared amplitude is not a nonnegative defect at every unitary level. Averaging coefficient energy via Parseval does not immediately fix it: exact root grids include Galois points that are not all unitary. Full derivation and normalization checks: work/global_design/tv_positivity_gate.md.

## 3. What the global coefficient norm can and cannot measure

Our earlier E(K)=sum a_j^2 is still a valid positive integer, with E=1 iff V=1. But it cannot be a positive count of ordinary geometric complexity. For every q>1 coprime to 3,

    V_(T(3,q))(t)=t^(q-1)(1+t^2-t^(q+1)),
    E(T(3,q))=3,             (E-1)/2=1,                            (6)

while the genus is q-1. The factorization is immediate from the torus Jones formula. So a putative defect count must allow one unit of defect for arbitrarily large genus. It may be a coarse obstruction to triviality, but cannot assign a uniform positive cost to every handle or unknotting move.

The most concrete Morse proposal also fails without extra input. Over a field, the quantum-zero sector of the reflection-double complex is End_(q=0)(C_K). Its homology is

    H^r = direct_sum_(i,j) Hom(Kh_red^(i,j)(K),Kh_red^(i+r,j)(K)).    (7)

Actual classical knots have adjacent homological groups in the same quantum degree, as in the GMS example already used for N2-D. Equation (7) then gives nonzero odd homology. No valid chain-homotopy Morse reduction can leave only even critical generators. On a single row with even/odd dimensions a,b, reflection gives Euler number (a-b)^2 while retaining odd dimension 2ab. Squaring the index did not remove the cancellation.

This is stronger than the abstract observation that Euler characteristic forgets parity, but is still a scoped obstruction: a different geometric complex is not excluded. Proof and references: work/global_design/positive_geometry_gate.md.

## Decision

A positive proof remains conceivable, but the standard ways of importing positivity do not provide it. The specific missing theorem is now sharper: construct a knot-dependent positive defect whose evaluation is determined by V, whose nonzero terms survive ambient compressions and off-diagonal recoupling, and whose vanishing gives a disk in S^3. None of the tested constructions has those properties. Restricting an unsupported positivity claim to the Jones-unit locus would merely restore the original gap.

I am parking this generic positivity pass. These results prune mechanisms; they are not new detected subclasses or a claimed advance toward a complete proof.
