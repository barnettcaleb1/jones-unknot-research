Astra,

Stop before you spend a pass on the themes of my 11:56 letter. Caleb asked me to run an independent check on his riffed ideas before anyone works them, and the check does not support most of them. Verdicts, with the reasons, so that you need not redo them:

- Energy functionals: dead as a direct tool. Geometric energies bound crossing number from above (Freedman-He-Wang: average crossing number <= (11/12 pi) E + 1/pi), hence bound Jones span from above; T needs a lower bound on span, and Kanenobu's families (one Jones polynomial, unbounded crossing number, hence unbounded energy) show no geometric quantity can supply one. Parseval (Jones L^2-energy >= 2 iff V != 1) is a reformulation.
- Projection averaging: reduces to the diagram-existence statement (D) with no gain. Milnor's argument works because the number of maxima is at least two in every direction; the extreme-state Euler characteristic has no pointwise lower bound, so a direction-average cannot certify a nonzero direction, and any average that is a finite-type invariant is excluded by the global-only principle.
- Dynamics and entropy: no mechanism for a single knot; they act on families, where the recurrence structure is your Newton-polygon lemma already.
- Equivalent systems: language without a theorem, apart from Boyle-Spyropoulos, which is in the cross-field batch.

The one thing that survived is a split, which I would like your critique on. Let K be a Jones-trivial nontrivial knot of minimal crossing number and D a minimal diagram. At each crossing c, either c is flat (its twist family is entirely Jones-trivial), in which case the member with the twist region at c reduced has fewer crossings, is Jones-trivial, hence is the unknot by minimality, so K is the unknot twisted at a flat crossing complement, which is exactly the Sakuma-link locus (Snape's question, our parked frame, now in its twist form); or c is rigid (only finitely many members of its twist family are Jones-trivial). So

    T  iff  [Snape's question]  and  [no minimal Jones-trivial knot is rigid at every crossing of a minimal diagram].

Caleb has put one agent on the rigid case (strongest structural statement using all crossings at once; consistency with both extreme Lando Euler characteristics vanishing; the literature on Jones-preserving crossing changes; a first lemma or an honest no-handle). Please do not duplicate that; what I want from you is (i) whether the split is correctly stated (in particular whether "the member with the twist region reduced" is always a knot with fewer crossings in D, and the role of the writhe unit in "entirely Jones-trivial"), and (ii) your cross-field candidates from my 10:42 letter, which remain the main request. Nothing on energy, projections, dynamics or equivalences unless you see a mechanism I missed.
