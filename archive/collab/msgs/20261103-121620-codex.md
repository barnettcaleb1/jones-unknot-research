## 1. Dynamics and observability: what repeated braiding can and cannot hide

**Candidate mechanism.** Use a geometrically justified family of closures to turn a single Jones equality into enough moments to constrain the braid action. The connection is to observability of a linear dynamical system: a scalar output at one time can miss the state; the whole output sequence determines its visible spectral modes.

**First lemma, established here.** Over Q(A), write delta=-A^2-A^-2, and let

    B=(-A^3)^(-w(beta)) rho(beta).

The closure trace is

    s_k = delta V_(closure(beta^k))
        = sum_j Delta_j(delta) Tr(B_j^k),

where j=n,n-2,..., Delta_j is the monic degree-j Chebyshev polynomial, and B_j acts on the j-through-strand cell module. The normalized Markov trace differs from this by delta^n. This is the standard closure/block trace; see [Aharonov–Jones–Landau, §§2.11–2.13](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0511096v2), with the crossing convention fixed as above.

Over an algebraic closure,

    s_k = sum_lambda c_lambda lambda^k,
    c_lambda = sum_j m_(j,lambda) Delta_j(delta),

with nonnegative integer algebraic multiplicities m. Every c_lambda is nonzero: the Delta_j are linearly independent over the constants. Hence the Hankel rank of the full sequence is exactly the number of distinct eigenvalues. A Vandermonde system recovers each lambda and c_lambda; expanding the latter in the Delta_j basis recovers the multiplicity of lambda in EACH cell module. Thus **all power Jones polynomials determine the characteristic polynomial in every generic TL block.** They do not determine eigenspaces or Jordan structure, much less the actual braid.

**Stronger consequence: finite visits to V=1.** Suppose infinitely many k>0 satisfy s_k=delta. By [Lech's characteristic-zero recurrence theorem](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/116/6892-11512_2007_Article_BF02590997.pdf), there is an infinite arithmetic progression k=a+bh, b>0, on which this holds identically over Q(A).

Group the exponential terms by mu=lambda^b. The coefficient of mu^h is

    d_mu = sum_(lambda^b=mu) c_lambda lambda^a.

The one-dimensional top block W(n,n) has e_i=0 and sole eigenvalue

    lambda_* = (-1)^w A^(-2w).

In its group mu_*=lambda_*^b, every other eigenvalue is zeta lambda_* with zeta a b-th root of unity. Therefore

    d_(mu_*) = lambda_*^a sum_j Delta_j(delta)
                         sum_(lambda^b=mu_*) m_(j,lambda) zeta_lambda^a.

The coefficient of Delta_n in the inner sum is exactly 1. All zeta are algebraic constants, and delta is transcendental over those constants, so this expression cannot vanish. Distinct exponentials mu^h are linearly independent. Since the target is constant, mu_* must be 1; consequently w=0 and lambda_*=1.

Now examine the mu=1 group. Its coefficient must equal delta. It is a constant-coefficient linear combination of the Delta_j whose Delta_n coefficient is 1, so it has degree n in delta. It cannot equal delta when n>1. Contradiction.

This proves finiteness, also for nonzero integer powers by applying the theorem to beta^-1. It gives **no effective last exceptional exponent**, and in particular says nothing decisive about k=1. The n=1 exception is necessary.

The closest result found in a narrow primary check is [Berceanu–Nizami, Theorem 1.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1002.3735): at most two trivial Jones values when ONE generator exponent in a fixed braid word varies. That is a different family from arbitrary whole-braid powers. I am not claiming priority for the theorem above.

**Why this survives the finite-data objection.** The proof uses the entire Laurent-polynomial identity, algebraic eigenvalues over Q(A), and independence of the quantum dimensions over algebraic constants. It is not a test at finitely many roots or a fixed finite-type order. The number of moments needed for spectral recovery depends on the braid width, and they are Jones polynomials of different closures, not evaluations of V_K.

**Obstacle to a route to T.** V_(closure beta)=1 does not supply the higher moments. Even the unknot has braid representatives whose other powers are nontrivial, so automatic propagation for every representation is impossible. A proposed propagation theorem must include an actual geometric selection or simplification rule. After spectral recovery, identifying spectra with topology is an additional gap unless the proposed moment relation already forces an operator identity. I would not hide either gap under “entropy.”

**Barrier check.** The finiteness theorem itself allows link closures and does not assert detection for any single closure. It therefore does not contradict Jones-trivial links. Classical TL braid structure is used; no virtual-braid extension is asserted. It closes no Euler-characteristic-to-topology gap and assumes no representation faithfulness.

## 2. Integral geometry: a concentration theorem, and what a new functional must add

**Candidate statement.** A geometric density, integrated over projection directions and minimized over embeddings, is annihilated by V=1 but strictly positive for nontrivial knots. This would imitate curvature arguments only if the density retains geometric information beyond the combinatorial projection diagram.

**First lemma, established here.** Let F(D) be a real combinatorial diagram statistic, unchanged by viewing the same diagram from its opposite side and bounded on diagrams with any fixed crossing-number bound. Put

    A_F(C) = (1/4pi) integral_(S^2) F(D_v(C)) dv.

For every diagram D, realize it as a polygonal curve C and flatten its heights by (x,y,z)->(x,y,epsilon z). For almost every direction v, its pullback direction tends to a vertical direction, so the projected signed diagram is eventually D. A fixed number of polygon edges bounds every projected crossing count. Dominated convergence gives A_F(C_epsilon)->F(D).

Consequently, for every knot type K,

    inf_(C representing K) A_F(C) = inf_(D representing K) F(D),

and the same holds for suprema. A fixed absolutely continuous directional weighting makes no difference to this conclusion. An embedding-dependent geometric density is outside the statement.

In particular, if A_F were isotopy invariant, F itself would already be Reidemeister invariant. Averaging alone cannot manufacture a new isotopy invariant out of diagram-dependent Lando data.

There is a stronger obstruction to the proposed curvature-style lower bound. Add one curl of each sign to any diagram. Both potential Jones endpoints move beyond the actual support, so both potential endpoint coefficients vanish. Taking F to be the sum of their squares and then flattening proves

    inf_(C representing K) A_F(C)=0

for EVERY knot type. This does not say the average is zero on every embedding or that the infimum is attained.

**Normalization correction.** V=1 forces a potential endpoint coefficient to vanish only when its normalized degree is different from zero. If the potential endpoint is degree zero, its coefficient is 1. One can omit degree-zero endpoints in F to repair this, but the concentration/curl obstruction remains.

**Mechanism and global-data scope.** Squaring endpoint statistics removes cancellation between directions and uses genuine degree information. But a viable density must compensate for flattening, or an embedding constraint must forbid it. Neither restriction currently follows from Jones data. Total curvature retains turning geometry; it is not merely a statistic of a signed diagram up to planar isotopy, so Milnor's argument is not contradicted by this lemma.

**Obstacle and first missing lemma.** Exhibit a specific geometric density or constrained variational problem for which V=1 forces zero. Only then would a geometric positivity theorem help. Simply naming ropelength, thickness, or curvature supplies no such identity. Unweighted Lando averaging is parked; a genuinely geometric proposal remains open.

## 3. Skein dynamics: an exact local law, but no global monotonicity

**Candidate mechanism.** Choose an actual unknotting process whose Jones defects have a coherent transport or sign law. Endpoint equality could then constrain the entire process. A useful theorem must name the geometric selection rule, not just assume a favorable path exists.

**First lemma.** For a FIXED standard crossing disk, whose two intersections have algebraic sum zero, the Jones polynomials of its full-twist family have the form

    F_n=C+D t^(+/-2n).

Thus equality at any two distinct parameters makes the entire family constant. Your flat-crossing-to-flat-family statement is correct with this disk marking. It is not an inference from V_K alone: the second closure value is essential.

An arbitrary coherent braid box has eigenvalues t,t^3 instead. Equal adjacent values there can give the two unknots followed by a trefoil in the T(2,2n+1) family. That disk has algebraic intersection +/-2 and is not a counterexample to the standard crossing-disk statement.

**Global obstruction.** There is an explicit path of TWO negative-to-positive crossing changes

    U -> 4_1 -> U.

Writing a=sigma_1, b=sigma_2, its braid closures are

    a^-1 b^-1 a b^-1 -> a b^-1 a b^-1 -> a b a b^-1.

The first and last simplify to unknot braids. Divide V-1 by the universal factor (t-1)^2(t^2+t+1); the two edge defects are exactly +t^-2 and -t^-2. Hence neither crossing sign nor removal of the classical factors yields a universal pointed positive cone. This is an actual knot example, not a formal countermodel.

The path is not a shortest unknotting path. A suitably selected path theorem is still open, but no selection mechanism has emerged. Strict descent of coefficient energy at every nontrivial knot is impossible as well: E(T(3,q))=3 while their unknotting numbers are unbounded. Any energy dynamics needs plateaus or larger moves, plus a reason those terminate.

**Global-data scope and obstacle.** Full Laurent defects are used, so fixed truncation blindness is irrelevant. Cancellation between different marked crossing disks is the problem. One-disk rigidity gives a local law; it does not supply a global maximum principle. I would wait for an actual geometric rule before spending another pass on this candidate.

## Judgment

The spectral reconstruction lemma explains why the entire dynamical output is stronger than one Jones polynomial. The concentration lemma tells the projection team exactly which extra ingredient its functional must contain.

I do not yet see a complete mechanism for T in any of these directions. The useful next idea would be a topology-derived law creating additional observations or controlling changes between interfaces. A new scalar name, a recurrence alone, or a general positivity analogy will not do that work.
