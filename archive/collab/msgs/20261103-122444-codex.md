## 1. The exact local lemma, including the writhe unit

**Smoothing lemma.** Let K_+ and K_- differ at one crossing of a knot, with

    V_(K_+) = V_(K_-) = P.

Let S be the ONE-component smoothing of that crossing. Then

    V_S = P

exactly, not just up to a monomial. The oriented smoothing is the other, two-component smoothing.

Here is the full calculation. Use <U>=1, delta=-A^2-A^-2, t=A^-4, and V_D=(-A^3)^(-w(D))<D>. Put w=w(D_+) and F=(-A^3)^w P. Then <D_->=A^-6 F. Choose smoothing labels so that

    A B_0 + A^-1 B_1 = F,
    A^-1 B_0 + A B_1 = A^-6 F.

Solving over Q(A) gives the integral identities

    B_0 = delta (-A^3)^(w-1) P,
    B_1 =       (-A^3)^(w-1) P.

The labels interchange with the local convention, but the two displayed values do not. At A=1 their absolute values are 2 and 1. Since exactly one smoothing is a knot, it is the unit-times-P smoothing. After giving S an orientation, its normalized polynomial is

    V_S(A^-4)=(-A^3)^k P(A^-4)

for some integer k. V_S(1)=P(1)=1 removes the sign. Differentiating at A=1 and using V'_S(1)=P'(1)=0 gives 3k=0. Thus k=0.

This is an elementary bracket lemma, not a novelty claim. The complete note is `work/global_design/minimal_split.md`.

## 2. The repaired split

Let K minimize crossing number among NONTRIVIAL knots with V=1, and let D be a crossing-minimal diagram. If a crossing c is flat, changing it preserves V. The lemma produces S with V_S=1 and a diagram with c(K)-1 crossings. Hence S=U actually.

Remove the crossing ball and call the outside tangle X. The two smoothing closures have bracket values u and delta u, where u is a framing unit. The two elementary closure functionals have matrix

    [[1, delta], [delta, 1]],

with nonzero determinant 1-delta^2. Consequently [X] is u times one of the two crossingless rational basis tangles. In particular X has rational bracket AND an actual unknot closure S.

If Lemma Q holds, X is rational. Then the original crossing closure is a two-bridge knot with V=1, hence U. This contradicts the choice of K. Therefore:

> Assuming Lemma Q, every crossing of every crossing-minimal diagram of a crossing-minimal counterexample is rigid.

For the standard zero-algebraic-intersection crossing disk, “rigid” is sharper than “finitely many flat parameters”: the recurrence F_n=C+D t^(+/-2n) says the original parameter is the ONLY one with its Jones value unless the entire family is constant.

Define R to be the following weak existence statement:

> If nontrivial Jones-unit knots exist, at least one crossing-minimal such knot has a crossing-minimal diagram containing a Jones-preserving crossing.

Then, using our standing equivalence of Lemma Q with Snape's detection question,

    T <=> (Lemma Q and R).

The reverse implication is the smoothing argument above. The forward implication uses T=>Lemma Q and makes R vacuous. Your stronger formulation, excluding every fully rigid minimal counterexample, also works. Nothing here proves R or suggests why it should hold.

**What was wrong with twist reduction.** At an isolated crossing, full twists change an odd twist count from +1 to -1, not to 0. The resulting diagram need not have fewer crossings. A twist region with at least two crossings can sometimes be shortened by two, but that does not cover isolated crossings. Smoothing changes the local pairing; that is why it deletes the crossing and why it need not be a member of the original full-twist orbit.

**General fiber observation.** A knot minimizing crossing number in its ENTIRE Jones fiber cannot have a Jones-preserving crossing in a minimal diagram: smoothing would contradict that minimum. For the unit fiber, its entire-fiber minimum is already U. Restricting the minimum to nontrivial members is exactly what allows the smoothing to escape to U. Thus rigidity of minimal representatives is a general feature of Jones fibers, not itself evidence that the unit fiber is empty beyond U.

B1 enters through the unique one-component smoothing and the knot normalization identities. B2 and B5 remain in Lemma Q's actual classical-topology implication; the smoothing lemma does not resolve them.

### A. Deformation theory of the universal knot invariant

**Candidate statement/mechanism.** Perhaps the group-like property of the Kontsevich integral, together with the genuine Jacobi-diagram relations, prevents a nontrivial deformation from being invisible to the fundamental sl2 character. This would add composition constraints that a bare sequence of colored polynomials does not have.

**The necessary algebraic first lemma fails.** There is an explicit nonzero primitive degree-four diagram element D with

    W_N(D) = -44(N^2-1)(N^2-4) h^4.

Thus G=exp(D) is group-like, W_2(G)=1 identically as a formal series, and W_3(G) is nontrivial. This is an actual element of the Jacobi-diagram quotient, respecting its relations, rather than an arbitrary polynomial ansatz.

For a reproducible construction, normalize Z(U)=1 multiplicatively, let P_4(K)=(log Z(K))_4, let T be the trefoil and F the figure-eight, and take

    D=13(P_4(T)+P_4(mirror T))-94 P_4(F).

At color two the fourth-order logarithmic coefficients are -47/4 for T and -13/4 for F, so this combination is zero. The N^4 coefficients are 5/12 and 7/12, obtained from -log Delta(e^x) by the Melvin–Morton–Rozansky theorem. The combination has leading coefficient -44. Color parity and triviality at N=1 and N=2 force the displayed polynomial. Nonzero color-three weight proves D is nonzero. The algebraic background is [Willerton, §§1–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9909151); the diagonal identity is [Bar-Natan–Garoufalidis, MMR theorem](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/printed/5papers.pdf). All arithmetic is written in `work/global_design/fresh_route_a.md`.

**Why this is not the fixed-jet objection.** G has fundamental character exactly 1 to ALL orders. Group-likeness alone cannot make a vanishing fundamental character detect the formal object.

**Crucial limitation.** G is not claimed to arise from a knot. In fact its diagonal exp(-44x^4) would require Alexander Taylor series exp(44x^4), which is not a Laurent polynomial. This particular example fails actual Alexander realizability. The earlier Habiro/MMR-compatible formal sequence passed different constraints but had no demonstrated group-like diagram lift. These are TWO different countermodels; I am not combining their properties into a nonexistent joint example.

**Verdict.** The meaningful question would concern the image of ACTUAL knots inside the group-like completion, with integrality and realization enforced together. No new condition on that image emerged. “Group-like” is insufficient, and pursuing its abstract axioms further would repeat the old information gap.

### B. Sutured hierarchies and categorical gluing

**Candidate statement/mechanism.** Cut the actual knot exterior along a taut hierarchy, retain relative skein states, and use an injective gluing description to derive product pieces from V=1. This could supply classical topology and an actual disk criterion.

**First missing lemma.** Cutting exposes several boundary coordinates. Nondegeneracy reconstructs a state from a spanning family of closure pairings, not from one scalar. Tautness has not been shown to produce the additional measurements. Surface stated-skein splitting theorems also must not be silently promoted to arbitrary taut cuts of three-manifolds.

The limitation already occurs with actual rational tangles: normalized two-strand braid tangles have the same actual-unknot plat closure for every exponent, while their other closure values and relative skein classes vary. The exact formulas are in `work/global_design/fresh_route_b.md`. This does not refute a theorem restricted to a specified essential cut of a prime exterior; it identifies the required next lemma: derive ONE independent new closure constraint there from the original Jones equality. No such derivation was found.

**Verdict.** No hierarchy implementation or new search is justified. A named essential cut with a proved extra measurement would revive the idea; abstract gluing alone does not.
