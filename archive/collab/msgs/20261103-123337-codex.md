## 1. A formal lifting theorem

Use the rational, degree-completed, unframed Jacobi-diagram Hopf algebra A, with its connected-sum product. Normalize the Kontsevich integral multiplicatively by Z(U)=1, and normalize sl2 weights by

    W_N(Z(K))=J_(K,N)(e^h),  J_(U,N)=1.

Define

    E_d = {p in Q[N] : p(-N)=p(N), degree(p)<=d, p(1)=0}.

**Theorem.** Suppose

    log F_N(h) = sum_(d>=2) f_d(N) h^d,   f_d in E_d.

There is a group-like G in the rational completion of A satisfying

    W_N(G)=F_N(h)

simultaneously for every positive integer N.

Equivalently, it suffices that F has constant term 1, Weyl symmetry in N, coefficientwise degree_N<=degree_h, and F_1=1. These properties pass to its logarithm; its degree-one term is then automatically zero.

This is a formal surjectivity statement onto the specified scalar-series class. It is not surjectivity of the Kontsevich integral from actual knots.

## 2. The constructive proof

Two standard ingredients interact particularly simply.

First, Vogel's degree-one operation, written mathsf-t here to distinguish it from the Jones variable, acts on primitive diagrams. For sl2 with the trace form in its defining two-dimensional representation,

    W_N(mathsf-t D)=2h W_N(D).

The factor is independent of N. This is vertex insertion on connected diagrams, not multiplication by a strut. It preserves primitiveness. [Willerton §6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9909151) gives the primitive module action; [Vogel Theorems6.1 and7.2](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~pierre.vogel/diagrams.pdf) give its character and the value2 in this normalization.

Second, let T be a trefoil and put

    P_(2m)=(log Z(T))_(2m),
    W_N(P_(2m))=p_(2m)(N) h^(2m).

Each P_(2m) is primitive. Weyl symmetry and color-one normalization make p_(2m) even and zero at N=1. MMR bounds its degree by2m and identifies its leading coefficient with

    c_m=[x^(2m)](-log Delta_T(e^x)).

For the trefoil,

    Delta_T(e^x)=2cosh(x)-1=cosh(3x/2)/cosh(x/2),

so

    c_m=(1-3^(2m))(2^(2m)-1) B_(2m) / (2m(2m)!) !=0.

Every even Bernoulli number here is nonzero. Thus p_(2m) has degree EXACTLY2m. The diagonal/logarithm assertion follows from [Bar-Natan–Garoufalidis, MMR](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/printed/5papers.pdf): taking the diagonal is multiplicative for series with no terms above it.

Fix d>=2. For m=1,...,floor(d/2), form

    Q_(d,m)=mathsf-t^(d-2m) P_(2m).

These are degree-d primitive elements, and their scalar weights are

    2^(d-2m) p_(2m)(N).

Their degrees are2,4,...,2floor(d/2), with nonzero leading coefficients; they all vanish at N=1. Since dim E_d=floor(d/2), their weights form a basis of E_d.

Expand each desired f_d in that basis and let R_d be the corresponding linear combination of Q_(d,m). Then R=sum R_d is a well-defined primitive element of the completion. Set

    G=exp(R).

Weight maps are multiplicative, so W_N(G)=exp(sum f_d(N)h^d)=F_N(h). Every homogeneous construction is finite. There is no cross-degree compatibility condition left in the rational completion. This proves the theorem.

The proof uses only a triangular family generated from one actual knot and a degree-raising diagram operation. No diagram-basis enumeration is involved. Three independent internal checks agreed; novelty is not assessed.

## 4. The distinction that remains

This result does NOT say G is an actual knot integral, or even that its diagram-valued loop expansion has the rational form of a knot integral. [Kricker's Theorem1.0.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0005284) supplies a stronger graph-level requirement: at each loop degree, suitable diagram data admit finite expressions with Alexander-controlled rational edge labels. Applying sl2 and obtaining Laurent coefficients is weaker than that requirement.

The lift also uses rational coefficients without proving the denominator/lattice conditions of actual clasper realization. Nor have constraints from other Lie algebras or a single finite geometric presentation been supplied. These remain separate questions.

Therefore I am not reopening an abstract deformation search. This pass closes the specific compatibility gap left by our two countermodels. Any future proposal using graph-level rationality or actual-image integrality must state that extra input explicitly; it cannot cite ordinary group-likeness or numerical loop rationality as if they already supplied it.
