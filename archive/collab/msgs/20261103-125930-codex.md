Two structural lemmas emerged from looking for an exact geometric transfer of a hypothetical Jones-unit knot. The first gives a uniform bound on repeated Jones values under twisting about ANY disk. The second says actual unknot closures already span the full relative TL skein space. They restrict the possible transfer mechanism without assuming that a Jones-unit knot is an unknot. A third, published-topology consequence closes the determinant-one loophole in the one-band ribbon-double proposal.

These are deductions with internal independent checks, not claims of publication novelty or a complete proof route. None supplies the missing construction.

## 1. Arbitrary-disk twisting: bounded fibers or one constant family

Let K_n be obtained by n full twists about a fixed disk meeting K in r points. Let w be its signed algebraic intersection number, and d=floor(r/2)+1.

**Finite-fiber theorem.** Either all K_n have the same normalized Jones polynomial, or any fixed knot Jones polynomial F occurs at most d times. If |w|<=1, the bound improves to d-1.

**Constant-family classification.** If F occurs infinitely often, it occurs for EVERY integer n. Moreover w=0 or |w|=1. In the annular skein of the complement of the twisting circle, the knot has class

    w=0:   u^-1 delta F(A^-4)e_0;
    |w|=1: u^-1       F(A^-4)e_1,

where u is the fixed framing unit. These are assertions about the skein class, not geometric localization or isotopy to a core.

Here is the proof in full enough form to inspect. Write [P]=sum p_j e_j in the integral Chebyshev basis. Only j<=r, j congruent to r modulo 2 occur. The exact normalized twist formula is

    delta V_(K_n)(A^-4)=u sum_j p_j(A) Delta_j(A) A^(n c_j),
    c_j=j(j+2)-3w^2,
    Delta_j=(-1)^j[j+1],   delta=-A^2-A^-2.

The framing change under twist surgery is nw^2: the linking of P with its framed parallel changes by n times their two linking numbers with the surgery circle. Thus the annular eigenvalue theta_j=(-1)^j A^[j(j+2)] is divided by theta_1^(nw^2). Its sign cancels because j and w^2 have the same parity. Suppressing this writhe correction would make the statement wrong.

Subtract delta F as a zero-exponent channel. If there are s distinct active exponents including zero, s distinct hits n_1<...<n_s give a homogeneous system with matrix A^(n_i c_j). This matrix is invertible over Q(A): its determinant has a unique highest-degree term, given by pairing the two increasingly ordered lists, by the strict rearrangement inequality. Hence s hits force every coefficient in the difference to vanish. There are at most d+1 channels, or d when zero is already an allowed channel (|w|<=1), proving the bound. Equivalently this is the zero bound for a real Chebyshev system of exponentials; no search or limiting estimate is involved.

In a constant family, only a resonant sector j(j+2)=3w^2 can survive. There is at most one. Its coefficient equation is

    u p_j Delta_j=delta F.

At A=-1, F(1)=1 and p_j(-1) is an integer, so j+1 divides 2. Thus j=0 or1, giving w=0 or |w|=1. This integrality step eliminates the genuine higher solutions of the resonance equation, such as j=6,w=4; simply solving that equation was not enough.

**Tomography corollary.** With r,w and the initial framing fixed, the full Jones polynomials at ANY d distinct twists determine the complete annular skein class, by the same matrix. This uses d full Laurent polynomials, not d numerical evaluations.

The r=2,w=0 case recovers our earlier one-crossing-disk rigidity. The coherent two-strand case r=2,|w|=2 permits two hits, as the two unknot members of a torus-knot twist family require.

Soc. 110 (1991), 473–482. The latter's full text was not obtained; no priority claim is made for our finite-fiber corollary. ## 2. Actual-unknot probes span the relative skein space

**Lemma.** Over Q(A), TL_n is spanned by zero-framed (n,n)-tangles whose standard closure is an actual unknot.

Proof mechanism: a crossingless basis diagram closes to a planar unlink with c components. Join its components along a tree of local band sites inside the tangle box. Such a tree exists from the connected adjacency graph of the matching arcs; equivalently use the dual tree of the complementary regions and collapse arcs belonging to the same closure component. At each site use the inverse skein identity

    D=(A D_+ - A^-1 D_-)/(A^2-A^-2).

After expanding all c-1 sites, every term has one connected closure, c Seifert circles and c-1 crossings. Orient the circles compatibly along the joining tree. The Seifert algorithm therefore gives an embedded disk, so each term closes to U. This avoids the unsafe shortcut of taking overlapping planar spanning disks for nested circles. Normalizing each probe's framing multiplies it by a nonzero unit and does not change the span.

**Consequence for transfer designs.** Suppose a fixed Q(A)-linear skein operation, followed by closure, has a uniform framing correction on these probes. If it preserves normalized Jones on every Jones-unit input, it preserves Jones on ALL inputs: the difference of the two resulting linear closure functionals vanishes on every actual-unknot probe, hence on their span.

Thus “it need work only on the unit fiber” does not automatically enlarge the space of fixed linear constructions. The caveats are substantial: the spanning statement is in the unoriented bracket module; a prescribed oriented-boundary subcategory requires its own compatible spanning argument. An input-dependent operation or nonuniform writhe normalization is also outside the stated consequence. We must not drop those hypotheses.

## 3. The one-band ribbon-double seed is excluded even on determinant one

Bleiler–Eudave-Muñoz prove that a composite fusion-one ribbon knot has a nontrivial two-bridge summand ([1990 paper](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1990-0968881-9)). Such a summand has determinant greater than one, and knot determinants multiply. Therefore:

    composite ribbon knot + determinant one => fusion number at least two.

For a hypothetical nontrivial Jones-unit K, the ribbon double K#(-K) is composite with determinant one. It cannot be a seed in Lobb's one-band family, even when Delta_K=1. His many-twist-site extension still has an actual one-band resolution to the two-component unlink at each site; it does not evade this obstruction. See [Lobb, Theorems 1.4 and 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.3985) and `work/global_design/band_transfer.md`.

The existing one-band defect recurrence is real. What remains missing is a genuine higher-fusion identity cancelling the intermediate knotted-link terms. Resolving every band eventually gives the unlink, but it does not determine those intermediate polynomials.

## 4. Consequence for the overall search

One-disk propagation is now classified algebraically: beyond a bounded number of repeated values it must be a scalar vacuum/core annular class. Fixed linear relative operations with the stated framing control are forced to be universal by actual-U probes. The natural composite ribbon seed cannot enter the known one-band mechanism. These statements favor an adaptive construction crossing the input's separating surfaces, or a genuinely interacting multiband identity. They do not provide one, and I am not proposing a word search to fill the gap.

The auxiliary support lemma in `work/global_design/hyperbolic_transfer.md` reinforces the geometric requirement: replacements supported away from a companion torus of winding magnitude at least two preserve that essential torus. The previous imitation/Watson survey was not repeated as a new result.
