The one-disk result has an exact multivariable extension, with a useful connection to toric algebra and hyperbolic filling. In any fixed finite full-twist template, EVERY Jones-polynomial fiber is a finite union of integer solution sets of linear equations. For two sites, infinite fibers are certified by specific binomial factors of the defect polynomial. A Jones-unit component whose active augmented exterior is hyperbolic would then yield infinitely many counterexamples by Dehn filling.

We have the algebraic classification and the geometric implication. We do not have an actual marked diagram satisfying both premises, and excluding such components would still leave isolated unit parameters. This is not a full proof route, and no novelty claim is made.

## 1. Exact linear structure of the parameter fiber

Fix s twist boxes and every outside tangle and gluing map. Box i has r_i strands, algebraic oriented strand count w_i, and n_i full twists. Full twists preserve endpoint permutation, so an initial knot remains one component for all n in Z^s.

The generic TL full-twist decomposition and exact writhe correction give a finite sum with slopes

    b_(i,j)=j(j+2)-3w_i^2,
    j=r_i,r_i-2,...,0 or1.

Indeed the raw eigenvalue has exponent j(j+2)-3r_i, while one full twist changes writhe by w_i^2-r_i. This integer is even, eliminating any parameter-dependent sign. The projectors and outside contraction have fixed rational coefficients in A. Clearing one parameter-independent denominator D(A) therefore gives

    D(A) delta V_(K_n)(A^-4)=sum c_(a,b) A^(a+b dot n).

For a fixed target F, subtract D delta F and combine identical pairs (a,b). Write the resulting Laurent defect as

    Phi(A,z)=sum_(i=1)^M c_i A^(a_i) z^(b_i),
    c_i nonzero integers.

**Theorem.** The set of integer n satisfying V_(K_n)=F is exactly a finite union of sets

    {n in Z^s : M_P n=v_P},

with integral matrices and right-hand sides. Every nonempty component is n_0+ker_Z M_P, the integer points of a rational affine flat. Points and the whole parameter space are allowed.

**Proof.** Enumerate the finite set of partitions of the monomials whose coefficient sum is zero in every block. For each such balanced partition, impose equality of a_i+b_i dot n within each block. These are integer linear equations, and every solution makes the polynomial zero. Conversely every zero partitions its monomials by their common A-exponent, and every resulting block has coefficient sum zero. No inequalities between blocks are needed: two cancelling blocks can coincide without spoiling cancellation. The kernel lattice is saturated because M(kv)=0 implies Mv=0. QED.

Thus there are no nonlinear integer curves or one-sided rays intrinsic to a component of this fixed full-twist model. Half-twist conventions first require parity/connectivity branches. Parameter-dependent moving twist sites, changing strand counts, or arbitrary braid powers are outside the hypothesis.

This is an elementary function-field version of an exponential-equation phenomenon. The finite support makes the proof explicit; no ineffective arithmetic finiteness theorem is required.

## 2. Two sites: a factor is a complete certificate for an infinite line

For s=2 and Phi nonzero, an infinite fiber contains an entire line

    n=n_0+k v,   k in Z,   v primitive and nonzero.

That line lies in the fiber if and only if Phi has the Laurent binomial factor

    z_1^(v_2) z_2^(-v_1)
       - A^(n_(0,1)v_2-n_(0,2)v_1).

To see this, substitute z_i=A^(n_(0,i))t^(v_i). Vanishing at t=A^k for all k forces the substituted Laurent polynomial to be identically zero. The kernel of this substitution is the principal binomial ideal displayed above, by a unimodular change of lattice coordinates. Conversely the factor plainly vanishes on the line.

Therefore a nonzero two-site defect with no such factor has only finitely many Jones-target parameters. This concerns the exact full Laurent polynomial, not its evaluations at roots of unity.

There is an especially small form for two ordinary two-strand disks with w_1=w_2=0. Put x=A^(8n_1), y=A^(8n_2). The exact normalized polynomial is

    V_(K_n)=a+b x+c y+d xy,   a,b,c,d in Q(A).

Unless the entire template has V=1, a unit line with BOTH coordinates varying must have direction (1,1) or (1,-1). The complete conditions are:

- n_1+n_2=m: b=c=0 and a+d A^(8m)=1.
- n_1-n_2=m: a=1, d=0 and b A^(8m)+c=0.

These follow by grouping the four slope values 0,v_1,v_2,v_1+v_2. Any other oblique direction makes them distinct and would force the whole template to be unit. Both displayed conditions are also sufficient. They give exact targets for an interacting two-site construction, but no such counterexample template is asserted.

Full proof: `work/global_design/multitwist_locus.md`.

## 3. The geometric implication: fill constant coordinates first

For a positive-dimensional Jones-unit affine component, retain its twisting circles and first fill every coordinate CONSTANT on that component. Call the remaining link exterior M_active. Choose an integer direction in the component that is nonzero in every remaining coordinate; such a direction exists because finitely many proper rational subspaces cannot cover its lattice.

**Hyperbolic implication.** If M_active is finite-volume hyperbolic, all sufficiently large parameters along this line give nontrivial hyperbolic knots with V=1. Infinitely many knot types occur.

Proof: every active filling slope mu_i-n_i lambda_i tends to infinity in length. Hyperbolic Dehn filling gives hyperbolic knot exteriors. Their volumes approach vol(M_active) strictly from below, ruling out a finite list of knot types. This uses [Thurston, Section 5.8](https://library.slmath.org/nonmsri/gt3m/PDF/Thurston-gt3m.pdf), not a Jones-to-volume assertion.

Equivalently, an affine component consisting entirely of actual unknots has a NONHYPERBOLIC active exterior. For a hyperbolic parent, the actual unknot parameters lie in a finite union of coordinate hyperplanes n_i=c, corresponding to exceptional slopes. Constant coordinates must be filled before using this statement: a fixed exceptional filling can destroy hyperbolicity and leave a whole line of unknots.

So the concrete counterexample target is now two independently checkable objects: an exact balanced-support/binomial identity and a hyperbolic active augmentation. Neither one implies the other. No example meeting both is known here.

## 4. Why this does not collapse to Lemma Q

One parameter along a diagonal is not one crossing disk. For opposite twists with equal local winding, every diagonal pair of colors has zero combined slope. Multiple quantum dimensions can survive together, so the one-disk integrality argument no longer isolates e_0 or e_1.

There is an elementary geometric calibration: take two parallel copies of the crossing circle generating a nontrivial twist-knot family K_m with K_0=U. Then K(p,q)=K_(p+q), so every K(k,-k) is U although either site alone can make a nontrivial knot. The circles cobound a knot-disjoint cancellation annulus; the active exterior is nonhyperbolic. Thus an all-unknot diagonal does not force each circle individually nugatory.

Relatedly, in a common marked-surface realization, a product of nonzero twists on two disjoint nonisotopic essential curves is not conjugate to a single Dehn-twist power. This does not rule out arbitrary Kirby moves or coincident closed-knot families; it does rule out the automatic single-disk reinterpretation.

Full geometric notes: `work/global_design/multitwist_geometry.md` and `work/global_design/unknot_multitwist.md`. Their additional annular results are scoped observations, not part of a general cosmetic-multitwist classification.

## 5. Prior art and remaining gap

The multivariable twist-bracket is already [Champanerkar–Kofman 2006, Proposition 2.1](https://msp.org/agt/2006/6-4/agt-v6-n4-p03-p.pdf). Their Kanenobu examples explicitly exhibit constant-sum fibers. Our contribution in this pass is the elementary exact balanced-partition deduction, its binomial formulation and the precise active-filling test; priority is unassessed.

The missing mathematics is topological: does a Jones-unit affine component necessarily have nonhyperbolic active augmentation, and if so what actual cancellation surface accounts for it? A counterexample to that assertion would solve T negatively. A proof would exclude this robust source of counterexamples, but would NOT exclude isolated Jones-unit parameter points or control arbitrary knots across all templates. Those are separate gaps.

No new sweep is justified by the classification alone. It becomes useful when a proposed geometric construction supplies a reason for the binomial identity or for its impossibility.
