Claude,

I tested one specific additional constraint on Q-JOINT: could the nonabelian part of a minimal colored recurrence prevent the ordinary color from becoming trivial? There is an elementary stability theorem: **a rational correction in the color variable preserves every non-L−1 factor of the primitive classical minimal recurrence, including multiplicities.** Consequently one can formally erase any finite initial segment of colors without erasing those factors. This is an algebraic result with a proof, not an actual-knot construction. The first geometric seed I tried fails an elementary compatibility check, and that failure matters.

## 1. The stability theorem

Let K=Q(q,M), sigma(M)=qM, and R=K[E;sigma]. For a q-holonomic sequence J, put F=J−g(q,q^N), where g is any rational function in K. Let P_J and P_F be their minimal annihilator generators over R. Normalize each to have minimum coefficient valuation zero at h=q−1. Write bars for their nonzero reductions in Q(M)[L]. Then

    bar(P_F) divides (L−1)bar(P_J),
    bar(P_J) divides (L−1)bar(P_F).

Thus every factor other than L−1 has the same multiplicity in both. The L−1 multiplicities differ by at most one. Degree in L may drop under specialization; no regular monic cyclic basis is assumed. M-only factors are units in the coefficient field and are not compared.

Here is the whole proof. Set r=P_J g, a rational function. Since P_J F=−r, either P_J already annihilates F, or

    Q=(E−sigma(r)/r)P_J

annihilates it. Sigma fixes h and induces the identity on the residue field Q(M). Therefore sigma(r)/r is h-integral and reduces to 1, even when r has a zero or pole at h=0. The coefficient-minimum valuation on R is multiplicative: after scaling, the product reduces to a product of nonzero polynomials in the domain Q(M)[L]. Hence Q is primitive and bar(Q)=(L−1)bar(P_J). Minimality gives Q=R_F P_F; valuation multiplicativity makes R_F primitive too, so reduction gives the first divisibility. Reverse J and F for the second.

There is also an exact difference-module explanation. The shift spans of J and F have the same quotient after adjoining and then dividing out the rational-sequence module K. Their nontrivial simple composition factors agree; only a trivial rank-one factor can change. This is a common quotient, not a claim that the extensions split.

Proof and independent internal read: `work/global_design/ore_correction.md`. Novelty unchecked. I suggest TRUSTED pending your read.

## 2. Application: erase finitely many cyclotomic coefficients

In our reduced, zero-framed convention,

    J_N=Σ_k H_k(q) C_(N,k),
    C_(N,k)=∏_(j=1)^k(q^N+q^−N−q^j−q^−j).

Every fixed C_(N,k) is Laurent polynomial in q and M=q^N. Thus

    F_N=J_N−Σ_(k=1)^r H_k C_(N,k)

has F_1=...=F_(r+1)=1, retains all H_k for k>r, and retains all non-L−1 factors of the primitive classical minimal recurrence. This works for each fixed finite r. It says nothing about an infinite subtraction or actual knot realization.

For r=1, if a_2(K)=0, H_1(1)=0. The correction then leaves the MMR diagonal 1/Delta_K unchanged. It also preserves scalar all-loop rationality: at q=e^h, z=q^N,

    g=H_1(e^h)(z+z^−1−2cosh h),

whose h-coefficients are Laurent polynomials in z and whose constant term is zero. Subtracting these from P_l(z)/Delta(z)^(2l+1) preserves the permitted denominator form. Habiro integrality survives, and the existing Q-JOINT triangular construction supplies a rational completed group-like diagram lift. This does not supply a graph-rational lift or an integral knot-realization theorem.

The correction has only linear color-degree support. Therefore quadratic extreme degrees and coefficients survive whenever the original extremes strictly outrun that support. That is a conditional observation, not a verified assertion about the seed below; no new degree computation was made.

## 3. The first actual-image check kills the convenient seed

The pilot b(31,13) has a_2=0 and Conway polynomial 1−2z^4. Its AJ theorem is available: [Le–Zhang, Corollary 1.9](https://letu.math.gatech.edu/Papers/AJ_Conjecture4.pdf) covers two-bridge knots with prime-order lens double cover. Deleting H_1 therefore retains its nonabelian classical recurrence factors and its Alexander diagonal, while making F_2=1.

But it CANNOT be the colored sequence of any knot. MMR would require Delta_new=Delta_pilot, hence determinant 31. Ordinary Jones would require determinant |F_2(−1)|=1. The actual-knot identity |V(−1)|=|Delta(−1)| rules it out immediately.

This is a useful warning against assembling independently compatible pieces. The AJ-proved example and the Alexander-one example cannot be interchanged in the argument.

An Alexander-one seed such as Kinoshita–Terasaka avoids that particular contradiction; its nontrivial Jones polynomial is explicit in [Eisermann–Lamm, Example 6.7/Table 2](https://pnp.mathematik.uni-stuttgart.de/igt/eiserm/publications/symjones.pdf). The algebraic theorem still applies to its colored sequence. However, this pass has NOT certified AJ for that seed, a higher-Habiro nonvanishing calculation, or the required extreme-degree hypotheses. I am not claiming a single demonstrated model satisfying all the proposed geometric constraints. Details: `color_surgery.md` and `aj_image_gate.md`.

## 4. What this changes, and where I stop

The recurrence's classical nonabelian factors cannot alone force a finite color to be nontrivial: they survive these corrections exactly. A proof through AJ needs an actual-image constraint tying the allowed solution and its initial conditions to the knot, beyond the classical symbol. Strong AJ peripheral ideals, graph-level rationality and geometric realization have not been tested away by this argument.

Also, [Dunfield–Garoufalidis, Corollary 1.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405353) makes full-colored unknot detection conditional on AJ. It is not a theorem converting J_2=1 into all colors being 1. We must retain both gaps in this route.

I am banking the stability lemma and parking further recurrence work. No KT recurrence hunt, no computation of the pilot quotient, no claim that this advances an end-to-end proof. The useful result is an exact description of what the classical recurrence forgets; the determinant check exhibits one genuine geometric constraint that it forgets.
