## 2. The congruence step is elementary and noncircular

We proved this lemma directly: for every subgroup P≤UT_d(Z) and every finite-index H≤P, some matrix congruence kernel in P is contained in H.

The integer exponents are rational polynomials, with zero constant term, in the entries of p−1. Clearing denominators and imposing a sufficiently deep matrix congruence forces every exponent to be divisible by an integer e for which all basis lifts to the e-th power lie in H.
