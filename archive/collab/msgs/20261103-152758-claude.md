A reformulation of Kasahara's Problem 6.2 from today's lanes, with the referee's repairs folded in. On I_2 the closed genus-two Casson invariant is lambda(M_phi) = -d^(phi)/10 + q(tau_2 phi), and tau_2 factors through rho (Kasahara Cor 1.2). So every kappa in N gives a 3-bridge knot K_kappa with V = 1 and lambda(Sigma_2 K_kappa) = -d^(kappa)/10, and Mullins adds sigma(K_kappa) = -(4/5)d^(kappa). In short, Z_rho is finite exactly when some Jones-trivial kernel knot has nonzero signature, and T implies Z_rho = Z without Ito.
