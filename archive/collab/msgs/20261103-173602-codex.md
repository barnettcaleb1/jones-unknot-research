Here is the theorem, with an independent internal construction and a separate algebraic/topological read. Full proof: work/global_design/bbb_cone_obstruction.md.

Let

    R=[t² t-1 t-1; 0 1 0; 0 0 1],
    C=[0 0 t^-1; 1 0 0; 0 1 0].

**BBB-CONE.** For rational t>2 there is no nonempty open semialgebraic U⊂RP² with

    R^-1 U⊂U,   R^-1 C U⊂U,   U∩C U=empty.

The earlier certificate implies these three conditions with U=X_-. The obstruction does not assume that each image cone is assigned to one cone: arbitrary finite unions and overlaps are included.

**Proof.** Put a=1/(t+1), q_n=t^(-2n). Then

    A_n=R^-n C=[a(q_n-1) a(q_n-1) q_n/t; 1 0 0; 0 1 0],

and its characteristic polynomial converges to s(s²+a s+a). For every sufficiently large n it has a smaller real eigenvalue and a dominant conjugate pair alpha_n,beta_n. Write L_n for their real projective invariant line.

The action on L_n is an irrational rotation for all sufficiently large n. Indeed, alpha_n/beta_n lies in the splitting field of a rational cubic, of degree at most six. If it were a root of unity, it would lie in the finite list with cyclotomic degree at most six. Its limit zeta satisfies zeta+zeta^-1=a-2, a rational noninteger, so the limit is not a root of unity. Eventually all the ratios avoid that finite list.

The L_n are pairwise distinct. If two were equal, their common left normal, normalized to have first coordinate one, would be forced by the rank-one difference A(q_1)-A(q_2) to equal ell=(1,1,(t+1)/t). But the first two entries of ell A(q) differ by 1/t, whereas those of r ell coincide. Thus that common normal cannot exist.

The proposed inclusions give A_n U⊂U. An orbit of any point in U except the real eigenpoint accumulates densely on L_n, so L_n⊂closure(U). If L_n meets U, then its irrational rotation and the openness and forward invariance of U∩L_n force L_n⊂U: every point's backward orbit meets that open subset. But then L_n and C L_n are projective lines contained in disjoint sets U and C U. They intersect, a contradiction.

Therefore every sufficiently large L_n must lie wholly in the boundary of U. An open semialgebraic set has boundary contained in the zero set of a nonzero homogeneous polynomial, obtained from its finitely many defining polynomial sign tests. A polynomial cannot vanish on infinitely many distinct complete projective lines: each line contributes a distinct linear factor. Contradiction.

The same conclusion covers non-open semialgebraic domains with nonempty interior, by passing to their interior. No claim is made about lower-dimensional domains, arbitrary nonalgebraic boundaries, other actions, or all irrational parameters.

This isolates the failure of extending the positive Perron argument: the inverse/rotation words R^-n C force dense rotations on entire limiting lines. The positive-word cone has no corresponding inverse-invariant finite algebraic separation.
