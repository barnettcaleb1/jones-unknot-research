## 1. C1: determinant 25 is not the only obstruction to the proposed seed replacement

The standard unlink-resolution mechanism gives

    V_(p,q)=1+(V_(0,0)-1) A^(8(p+q)).

Therefore replacing its seed by a known determinant-one but Jones-nontrivial ribbon knot cannot produce a Jones-unit sum line. This rejects that particular Kinoshita–Terasaka/Delta=1 replacement strategy before computation. An actual unknot seed is a different possibility algebraically; in the original homology-preserving construction it cannot produce nontrivial knots, by Khovanov unknot detection. Do not extend that latter assertion to arbitrary four-closure identities without the categorical hypotheses.

The general gate makes the design freedom precise. Use the ABSOLUTE bracket, delta=-A²-A^-2, and let the two marked antiparallel full-twist sites be replaced by I or e, e²=delta e. For the fixed outside contraction compute

    B00=F(I,I), B10=F(e,I), B01=F(I,e), B11=F(e,e),
    kappa=(-A³)^(-w0)/delta.

Then

    a=kappa B11/delta²,
    b=kappa(B01/delta-B11/delta²),
    c=kappa(B10/delta-B11/delta²),
    d=kappa(B00-B10/delta-B01/delta+B11/delta²).

So b=c=0 means exactly delta B10=B11=delta B01. The entire line p+q=m is Jones-unit iff, in addition,

    B11+(delta² B00-B11)A^(8m)=(-A³)^(w0)delta³.

These are three denominator-free Laurent identities. “One turnback frees the other site” must prove the FRAMED relation F(e,T^q)=A^(-6q)F(e,I), and its counterpart. Merely having two bands incident to the same components does not prove this.

If L is the inherited oriented one-turnback link, then a=V_L/delta and d=V_seed-a. Only for a split L=J1 disjoint-union J2 does a=V_J1 V_J2. For the two-component unlink a=1. Thus cancelling the defect of a Jones-nontrivial seed requires changing the full turnback link, not just its component labels or Alexander polynomial. Local long-knot decorations retain connected-sum factors and do not provide that interaction.

The complete derivation and normalization cautions are in work/global_design/c1_candidate_gate.md. In particular delta=0 at the determinant evaluation; do not evaluate rational JW coefficients there before cancelling denominators.
