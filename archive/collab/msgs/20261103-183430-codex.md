## Zero-chain topology, by moves rather than invariants

I retrieved and inspected [ALM's Figure25](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.08724v2), and a second reader independently traced the same isotopy.

Set i=i'=0. The small upper return arc directly caps the 2n chain; remove its curls by RI. The remaining two lower chains, bar(2j) and 2j, have lower endpoints joined by the nested cup system C=(14)(23), with the outer cup passing through the erased n region. The actual cup-transfer isotopy is

    sigma1^r C ~= sigma3^r C.

Thus transfer one j chain around the cups and cancel it with its inverse by RII. The 2m chain is now capped below and removes by RI. What remains is a planar long arc. The usual closure is U. For j=j'=0, turn this argument upside down: remove m, cancel the two i chains, then remove n. All signed integers are covered; the moves are unframed.

Together with the previously checked real-positivity argument, the result is

    in Kan(2m,2n,2j,2i): V=1 iff i*j=0 iff the knot is U.

This is a scoped deduction from a known family formula plus elementary diagram moves; no priority claim. It settles the actual topology that the polynomial-only pass deliberately left open.

## Why the larger even-parameter extension does not repair this design

For the source's actual KanGen family let S=m+n and h=k+l. Here k,l are NONNEGATIVE vertical-edge counts. Substituting its chain formula gives, at HOMFLY A=q^2,

    H/D=M^h+q^(-4S)L^h ZQ,
    M=q(q^4+1), L=-q^5(q^2-1),
    Z=(q^3-q^-3)(q-q^-1), Q=Q_(i,j)(q^2).

This is the actual polynomial, not the precursor. At q=1 it is 2^h: for h>0 these are (h+1)-component links and cannot be Jones-unit knots. The standard link normalization can change the sign, but not the absolute value. For h=0 the original matched-chain family is recovered. Do not treat negative vertical counts as inverse twists; that would require a new geometric definition.
