## 1. Integer-twist star–triangle identities are topological moves

The purpose was to replace a piece of an actual unknot by a different physical network with the same full boundary partition function. The archived five-channel condition is

    abc=Q(Q+a+b+c),   Q=2-y-y^(-1),   y=-A^4.

Triangle weights are Q/a,Q/b,Q/c, with scalar factor Q+a+b+c. Both channels and scalar factors must be retained.

For an integer parallel twist bundle let a_n=y^n-1. Its series dual has weight Q/a_n. We proved:

    If a,b,c each lie in {a_n,Q/a_n: nonzero integer n},
    the full star–triangle identity holds exactly when
    the three effective weights include a_1 and a_(-1).

The third arm can be any allowed bundle. This is not a parameter search. Sketch of the four cases:

- No series arms: degree comparison forces a positive exponent1 or its inverse. With a=a_1 the defect factors as (y-1)(b+(y-1)/y)(c+(y-1)/y), forcing an opposite single crossing.
- One series arm Q/a_p: after clearing denominators the equation is (y^q-y^p)(y^r-y^p)=(y^p-y)(y^p-y^(-1)). Cyclotomic factor multiplicities and lowest degrees force {q,r}={1,-1} when |p|>1; p=+-1 reduces to the preceding factorization.
- Two series arms: the equation is a_r(Q-a_p a_q)=Q(y^(p+q)-1). Highest/lowest degrees force a single-crossing arm; the same factorization finishes.
- Three series arms: y^(p+q+r)+y+y^(-1)=y^p+y^q+y^r. Positive-coefficient exponent multisets force both1 and-1.

Crucially, the surviving solutions are actual tangle identities. In braid coordinates the move is

    sigma1 sigma2^n sigma1^(-1)=sigma2^(-1) sigma1^n sigma2.

It is the braid relation for n1 and its conjugated powers for every signed n. Rotations give the series-box versions. Thus these physical replacements cannot change an unknot into a nontrivial knot. The elementary signed-graph correspondence is in [Kauffman, A Tutte polynomial for signed graphs](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/TuttePolySignGraphs.pdf); the integer-bundle classification is written out in work/global_design/potts_twist_gate.md, independently read here. No novelty claim.

This does NOT classify arbitrary rational-tangle arms, singular effective reductions, larger network identities, or equality after only one chosen closure. It closes the specified nondegenerate integer-box design, not the entire statistical-mechanics route.

## 2. The three-strand antisymmetrizer is not an actual braid construction

The idea was to make a knot modification whose entire HOMFLY defect lies in the three-strand antisymmetric channel, killed at SL2. A sufficient braid-level realization would have [beta]-uI in that Hecke ideal. Then beta is projectively scalar in the full generic TL3 image. Its two-dimensional block at the modular specialization makes beta central in B3. Write beta=Delta^(2k); its two generic TL eigenvalues are A^(6k) and A^(-6k). Scalarity forces k0, hence beta is trivial.

Thus no nontrivial THREE-STRAND BRAID realizes this exact defect. A formal antisymmetrizer, an alternating sum of clasper surgeries, or a leading finite-type identity does not evade the distinction. The broader single-projector obstruction was already banked in work/research45/quantum_identities.txt; it is not new work here. A nonbraid tangle or closure-specific cancellation is outside this argument, but I supplied no such actual object.
