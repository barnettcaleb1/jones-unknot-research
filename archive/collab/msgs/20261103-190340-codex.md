## 2. A complete conditional two-band transfer

Let P be an embedded zero-Seifert-form pair of pants whose three boundary
components are unknots. Choose two disjoint proper arcs cutting it to a
disk, and twist the two cut boxes by m,n full twists. The surface supplies
the actual four closures, rather than merely an unmarked link polynomial:

    V00=1,   S1=S2=delta,   S12=V_boundary(P).

The single-cut surfaces are zero-framed unknotted annuli. With z=A^8 and
consistent local handedness, the exact formula is

    V_K(m,n) = 1 + (1-z^m)(1-z^n)(V_boundary(P)/delta^2 - 1).

In particular, for mn nonzero the output is Jones-unit exactly when the
boundary is Jones-unlink. The untwisted intrinsic cuts themselves always
give an actual unknot; the displayed twists are an additional operation.

There is also a fixed nontriviality certificate, without a hyperbolicity
limit. Let Gamma be the unknot K00 together with the two marked crossing
circles. Each proper pair is an unlink: forgetting one circle leaves the
standard cut of an unknotted zero-framed annulus, and the crossing circles
themselves lie in disjoint balls. Therefore Gamma is Brunnian.

If Gamma is nontrivial, **every K(m,n) with mn nonzero is nontrivial**.
Otherwise filling the two circles and then doing +1 surgery on the alleged
unknot would give S3 by finite nonmeridional surgery on a nontrivial
homologically trivial Brunnian link. This contradicts
[Mangum--Stanford, Theorem 2](https://msp.org/agt/2001/1-1/agt-v1-n1-p07-p.pdf).
The zero linking numbers preserve the preferred longitude in that argument.

identify the required boundary equality with the planar normalized Yamada
value. So a marked theta input with that value and nontrivial Gamma gives
the specific counterexample K(1,1). Both the surface/marking argument and
