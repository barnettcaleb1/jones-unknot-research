## 1. The missing implication

Retain P, L=boundary(P), the two cut arcs, K00=U, and the two crossing
circles from190340. Gamma=K00 union Ca union Cb is Brunnian by the actual
annulus cuts. Gamma is not L.

Suppose Gamma is the unlink. In the double cover branched over K00, which
is S3, the four lifts of Ca,Cb form an unlink: use splitting balls
disjoint from K00. The specified crossing disks lift to two disjoint
embedded annuli Aa,Ab. Their cores kappa_a,kappa_b can be simultaneously
isotoped to a selected boundary component of each annulus, so the cores
are an unlink and their annular framings are zero.

Restoring each removed strip is precisely annular-longitude surgery on
its lifted core. Local marking: before restoration the strip ball has
two vertical branch strands; after the other planar smoothing its
middle crossing disk misses the branch set, so its two lifted boundary
circles bound disks in the new filling. Thus there is no extra +/-1
in the filling coefficient. Restoring both strips gives

    Sigma2(L) = S3_(0,0)(U2) = #2(S1 x S2).

Plotnick's unlink-cover classification, explicitly stated in
[Gorchakov Thm2.15](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.12557v1#S2), gives L=U3.
I checked Gorchakov's statement, not Plotnick's original article.
[Kauffman--Simon--Wolcott--Zhao, §3 p209](https://www.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/ThetaCurves.pdf)
then gives Theta planar. Contradiction. This uses disjoint annuli upstairs,
not an assumption that nugatory disks can be replaced simultaneously.

Therefore Gamma is nontrivial. The previous application of
[Mangum--Stanford Thm2](https://msp.org/agt/2001/1-1/agt-v1-n1-p07-p.pdf)
now proves nontriviality of every K(m,n) with mn nonzero unconditionally
within this marked class.

## 2. Determinant and unknotting number

P has zero 2-by-2 Seifert matrix, hence H1(Sigma2(L))=Z^2. The two smoothing
surgeries on kappa_a union kappa_b are integral; their symmetric linking
matrix B presents this homology. Coker B=Z^2 forces B=0. Thus both
smoothing framings and lk(kappa_a,kappa_b) are zero, independently of the
preceding hypothetical unlink argument.

The cover of K(m,n) is obtained by slopes mu+/-2m lambda and
mu+/-2n lambda, with lambda the zero-framed smoothing slope. Equivalently
the coefficients are +/-1/(2m), +/-1/(2n), and zero parameter means
meridional filling. Algebraic splitting and numerator one give H1=0.
So det K(m,n)=1 for all parameters. These upstairs coefficients are
distinct from the downstairs crossing-circle coefficients +/-1/m.

Changing one crossing in the first full-twist box of K11 cancels the
pair and gives K01=U. Nontriviality makes u(K11)=1. This argument does
not claim Delta_K=1 or a2(K)=0.
