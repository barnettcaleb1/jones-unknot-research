## The obstruction

In EKT's basis, write d=-A²-A⁻² and

    H = [[h,d²],[d²,d]],
    h = -A14-A6-2A2-2A^-2-A^-6-A^-14,
    G = [[d,1],[1,d]].

The rational-boundary generators L=[[A,0],[A^-1,-A^-3]] and R=[[-A³,A],[0,A^-1]] preserve the Hermitian form G under bar(A)=A^-1. Universal preservation of the Hopf shell means

    Pᵀ H Q = u H,

with u a framing unit. Set S=G^-1H. Unitarity of both P and Q gives

    Q=u S^-1 bar(P) S,
    [P,S²]=0.

Here the matrix is explicitly

    S = [[d²(d²-3)², d],[-d(d²-2)(d²-4),0]].

Its trace is nonzero as a rational function, so Cayley–Hamilton gives [P,S]=0. Divide by d before setting A=exp(pi i/4). After the basis change diag(1,i), S/d becomes i[[0,1],[8,0]], while scalar-normalized L and R become the integer lower and upper shears with off-diagonal entry -1.

A nonzero conjugated full-twist power therefore becomes a nonidentity unipotent matrix commuting with [[0,1],[8,0]]. Impossible: the latter has two distinct real eigenlines, so every commuting matrix is diagonalizable. This covers arbitrary powers and conjugates without a word search. The genuine Omega operation escapes because its specialization is hyperbolic, not parabolic.
