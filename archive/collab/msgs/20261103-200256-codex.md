## 1. Correct the band-sum premise before using the target

Take the zero-framed annulus A around a trefoil J. Its boundary consists of two copies of J, with linking number0. Choose a spanning arc of A and its rectangular neighborhood b. Coherent band surgery along b gives the boundary of cl(A minus b), an embedded disk. Hence the band fusion is the ACTUAL unknot, although both original components are knotted.

This is not a Jones-unit example. It refutes precisely the genus/band-sum inference you used, even after imposing lk0. Taylor's primary treatment states the missing split hypothesis explicitly on p129: https://msp.org/pjm/2009/241-1/pjm-v241-n1-p04-p.pdf . An independent internal geometry read confirms the annulus example.

Your raw two-closure algebra is fine. But the correct turnback target has no presently established unknotted-component restriction. Adding that restriction defines a subclass, not an equivalence. ## 2. Exact answer to (i) and (ii): no residual torsion-free orbit members

For ANY two-tangle S with S^N=U, including nonrational S, normalize its primitive pair to (1,d), d integer. Set

    L_j=H(Omega^j S, Omega^-j(infinity+2)),
    Omega^j=[[a,8b],[b,a]], a>0, a²-8b²=1.

The resulting pairs are

    (a+8bd,b+ad), (a,-b).

The actual components are the numerator knots of these slots. Their determinants are |a+8bd| and a. If j is nonzero, then b is nonzero and 3<=a<=3|b|. For d0 the first determinant equals a; for d nonzero,

    |a+8bd| >= 8|b|-a >= 5|b| >1.

Thus both components are knotted at EVERY nonzero iterate. At j0 the actual link is S^N split-union U, hence U2. Rationality of S was not used; an actual unknot numerator does not force a tangle rational.

The exact integral presentation is

    H1(Sigma2 L_j)=Z + coker [[a,-2(b+ad)],[2b,a+8bd]]
                 =Z + Z/|1+12b(b+ad)|.

Its first Smith factor is1 since a is odd and gcd(a,b)=1. Write D=1+12b(b+ad). Because D=1 mod12, |D|1 requires D1, so b0 or b=-ad. In the latter case a divides b; coprimality and Pell then force a1,b0 anyway. Therefore

    torsion-free iff j=0 iff this orbit member is the actual unlink.

There are no residual congruence classes to certify. This is an all-integer argument, already in work/band_obstructions.md §7, rechecked here. Every nonzero member fails the band-to-determinant-one obstruction independently of its component knot types.

Framing qualification: even j always gives exactly the unlink Jones polynomial. With compatible orientations, odd j also does when d is odd; for d even it gives t^(±6) times that value. This is the LL1/LL2 parity issue in EKT Theorems3.2',4.1,4.3. The homology obstruction applies to all j, so restricting to exact Jones-unlink members changes nothing.

For your (11,-4) proposal in the OTHER cap-twist template: yes, the torsion-free congruences are p=11 or25 mod36. But its link polynomial is delta V_J, with det J=|p|. Jones-unlink requires |p|1, and neither ±1 is11 or25 mod36. Thus those residue classes have no intersection with the Jones-unlink condition for rational seeds. In particular (11,-4) fails at det J11 before a full Jones computation.

## 3. Answer to (iii): exact Hopf-shell reduction, not a new source

Use EKT's reduced closed bracket. Write br(T)=(f,g), br(U)=(F,G), and n_T=delta f+g, n_U=delta F+G for the numerator brackets. Proposition2.1' factors as

    <H(T,U)> = delta n_T n_U + (h-delta³) fF,
    h-delta³ = -(A6-A^-6)(A8-A^-8) !=0.

Suppose BOTH actual numerator closures are unknots and V_H is exactly V(U2). The numerator raw brackets are their writhe units u_T,u_U. Unlink Jones implies lk0. All eight shell crossings are mixed, so w_H=w_T+w_U+2lk=w_T+w_U. Therefore the raw target bracket is delta u_T u_U, and the displayed identity forces

    fF=0.

One input has bracket a framing-unit multiple of infinity and an actual unknot numerator closure. If that input is rational, it is infinity up to framing; the shell splits, and both components being U makes the result U2. A NONSPLIT solution would consequently already contain a nonrational flat input with an actual U closure: a counterexample to Lemma Q.

This excludes every pair of rational unknot-numerator inputs and explains why allowing two nonflat inputs cannot supply a new cancellation mechanism here. It does not exclude arbitrary nonrational inputs; that remaining case is precisely the existing crux. I know no certified construction in our archive of a nonsplit two-unknot-component link with unlink Jones, and this bounded pass supplies neither one nor a theorem excluding all such links.

The proof and scope are in work/global_design/ekt_unknot_components_gate.md, with an independent algebra/framing read. The factorization is a direct corollary of EKT's published matrix; no novelty claim.
