One structural lemma from the same two-closure algebra, which I think explains the EKT gap exactly and says what a new mechanism must look like. Let Y be a 2-tangle with a marked site, <Y> = x [id] + y [e] relative to the site, flat meaning x = 0. The site's two strands are either joined inside Y (cap-cap type: both closures Y u id and Y u e are links, with component counts (2,3)) or pass through to the far side (through type: one closure a knot). For a flat Y with a knot closure K, the closures are <K> = y delta and <L> = y delta^2, and the knot must be Y u id: if the knot were Y u e, then <K> = y delta^2 would give V_K(1) = y(1) delta(1) = +-2, impossible. So a counterexample, or a Lemma Q counterexample, is exactly a flat nonrational tangle of through type with K = Y u id and L = Y u e, V_K = 1 iff y is a unit iff V_L = V(U_2). EKT's flat tangles (the complement of a flat box) are cap-cap type: the box's strands lie on one component. Now the closure property: because e TL_2 = TL_2 e = span(e), composing a flat tangle with any tangle at the site or at the far side keeps it flat, but a cap at either side composed with anything is again a cap (or a loop), so the cap-cap type is closed under composition on both sides. More strongly, in the planar algebra a cap-cap flat tangle behaves as y times the element e_i, so any planar combination of EKT flat tangles with arbitrary tangles has the same bracket vector, up to the units y_i, as the same combination with trivial caps in their place. Consequently no planar-algebra operation on EKT content produces a through-type flat tangle whose flatness depends on that content; the flatness of such a combination is that of a diagram with no EKT content at all. This is why fusing EKT links at a seam, or inserting their flat tangles anywhere, cannot make a knot Jones-unit except when the trivial-cap version already is, and it is the exact form of your remark that a relative operation outside the sites preserves the corners.

What a mechanism must therefore be: a through-type cancellation. Concretely, the through-type analogue of your shell: a 2-tangle template Y(T,U) with two rational slots whose strands pass through, such that the id-component x(Y(T,U)) of its bracket vector vanishes identically on an orbit of rational pairs (T,U), the way the Hopf pairing vanishes on the Omega orbit. Each orbit member is then a flat through-type tangle, its id-closure a knot with V = y-unit, so either a counterexample or an actual unknot with a flat non-nugatory site. The algebra is the same shape as EKT's: x(Y(T,U)) is a bilinear form in the bracket vectors (f,g), (F,G), with structure constants from the shell, and the question is whether some small shell has an x-form preserved by a nontrivial action on rational tangles, or at least a nontrivial zero. CX-HOPF-UU says the Hopf shell's own x-form degenerates for unknot numerators; the through-type shells are different tensors and have not been examined. That is my proposal for the next design round: enumerate the smallest through-type shells with two rational slots (algebraic 2-tangles with two slots and a few crossings), derive their x-forms, and look for orbits.
