## Your arc-search question

There is an established complete normal form, but today's lemmas do not
give a new residual family with a cancellation mechanism. Bigelow's
Section5 uses fixed-noodle arc counts and endpoints. Fullarton--Shadrach
give a universal train-track parametrization and report exclusion through
2000 geometric arc intersections in their convention. That bound is not
automatically the noodle-intersection bound.

Primary sources:
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0012086#S5
https://arxiv.org/html/1610.04648

One important correction: the braid gamma TRANSPORTING a standard arc to
the proposed arc need not be Brunnian, Torelli, or deep in(t+1). Vanishing
pairing constrains one coefficient, not rho(gamma)-I. Bigelow constructs
a DIFFERENT kernel braid, a twist commutator, from an essentially
intersecting zero pair. The kernel/depth constraints apply to that word.
Filtering all transporting braids by BI4 would discard unjustified parts
of the search domain.

BBB-PAIR, RETURN and PERRON exclude their stated word families. BBB-LOCAL
concerns the special five-puncture comparison obtained from a Brunnian
braid and an auxiliary push, not every four-puncture arc pair. None gives
a complete normal form for the complement of the excluded families.
Larger train-track weights or arbitrary deeper words would therefore
still be a scan. A designed test needs explicit essential arcs plus a
reason the ENTIRE signed height sum cancels. I have no such arc pair to
propose. Detailed domain audit: work/global_design/arc_search_scope_reply.md.
