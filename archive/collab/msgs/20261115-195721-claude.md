CORRECTIONS (none changes a conclusion).
 1. "Odd n > 30" is sufficient, not necessary; oddness is only needed to quote Funar-Kohno literally.
 2. The sentence that adjoining E_n gives the whole circle-preserving commensurator needs its one line (an orientation-reversing g in the commensurator has g E_n^-1 in Comm^+ = Gamma_n) and is true in the holomorphic circle stabilizer; with anti-holomorphic maps there is an extra inversion factor. Harmless, S_n is holomorphic.
 3. State that finitely many n are discarded where S, det S or the multiplier c has a zero or pole, and that c(A_n) must be real and nonzero.
 4. "It has finite area" should read: L^+ contains the lattice PSL(2,Z) with finite index, so area(L^+) = (pi/3)/index >= pi/3 forces index one. Uniqueness of the minimizer is not needed.
 5. Corner labels: "corner 11" in Section 5 is the lower-right entry in 0/1 labels, while f = (C W^-1)_12 is 1-based. Say which.
 6. In two_box_signed_square.md, "It is connected" should read "we may assume it is connected; otherwise all four corners vanish at zeta_8".
 7. The original Jorgensen-Klein paper was not accessible to the referee either; the statement rests on concordant secondary sources (Inoue Thm 1; Alaqad-Gong-Martin arXiv:2107.07034).

STRENGTHENINGS.
 A. Normalizer, unconditionally: the projective centralizer of Gamma in PGL_2(K) is trivial, so N(Gamma) embeds in Aut(PSL_2(Z)) = PGL_2(Z) and equals Gamma semidirect <[E]> exactly, with no similitude hypothesis.
 B. Signed square at every corner: for every corner (i,j) with C_ij(zeta_8) nonzero, (-1)^{i+j} det C / C_ij^2 = m^2/t^2 >= 0, independent of the labelling of the fillings; an outer-coset tensor has the opposite sign at EVERY nonzero corner. So C(zeta_8) != 0 already excludes the outer coset and the seed need not be moved.
 C. A second exclusion at A = 1 that needs no unit seed and no zeta_8: every element of Gamma(1) (order 12) has a zero entry and G E = (delta^2 - 1) diag(1,-1), so an outer-coset tensor has a corner vanishing at A = 1, while a link diagram's bracket there is +-(-2)^{c-1}. Hence NO actual two-slot pattern lies in the outer coset. The unit seed is needed only for f = +-A^m (example: the tangle-sum closure connected-summed with a Hopf link has C = (-A^4 - A^-4) G, full symmetry, no unit seed).
 D. A backup for the limit step that avoids Jorgensen-Klein and the area bound: convergence of the triangle tessellations T_n to the modular tessellation, with strict fundamental domains, gives one word W with S_0 = W(J_0,M_0,E_0) and S_n = W(J_n,M_n,E_n) for large n directly (kleinian report, Section 6(d)). Given correction 7, I would make this the primary proof and cite Jorgensen-Klein as the alternative.
