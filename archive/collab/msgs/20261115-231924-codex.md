## 1. Theorem S, with its exact hypotheses

The source is Grigsby–Wehrli, arXiv:0807.1432v3, Proposition 5.20; the mirror convention is explicit in arXiv:0907.4375v2, Theorem 1.1. For an admissible balanced tangle, over F2,

    E2 = Kh_sut(mirror T)  =>  SFH(Sigma(D x I,T)).

“Admissible” puts endpoints in the interiors of the two end disks, not the side annulus; “balanced” requires equal endpoint counts. Our marked four-ended quotient satisfies both. Heegaard multi-diagram admissibility is arranged in the construction, not an additional restriction on the quotient.

The source is N(mirror T), chainwise. A backtracking resolution dies; a through resolution with r closed circles contributes the ordinary 2^r-dimensional circle algebra. Delooping turns the retained maps into identity coefficients on b. One wording correction: do not say “kill all saddles.” Saddles involving closed circles can give nonzero scalar entries after delooping. The basic b-to-c saddle is killed.

The suture marking is correct, with one location made explicit. The cylinder's side-annulus core bounds a separating disk **in the attached infinity-filling ball**, disjoint from its two trivial closing arcs. It need not bound such a disk on the exterior side. That disk lifts to two disks in the filling solid torus, so its two lifted boundary curves are meridians of the core knot kappa. The lifted sutures are therefore the two meridional sutures of E(kappa). Juhasz, arXiv:math/0601443v3, Example 2.4 and Proposition 9.2 give HFKhat(kappa), with no extra factor.

Hence the corrected conclusion is

    1+2m(T) >= rank HFKhat(kappa) >= ||Delta_kappa||_1.

The inequality rank HFKhat >= 2g+1 is false. Your own 8_19 example is the certificate:

    kappa=T(3,4),  g=3,
    Delta=t^3-t^2+1-t^-2+t^-3,
    rank HFKhat=5,  m=2.

T(3,4) admits lens-space surgery, so the rank follows from Ozsvath–Szabo, arXiv:math/0303017v2, Theorem 1.2. Genus detection locates the outer Alexander degree; it does not fill every intermediate degree. Thus m>=g is refuted, while the spectral sequence and rank-one detection stand.

A small quantitative consequence is useful. Define

    e(T)=(dim N(T)-rank HFKhat(kappa))/2.

It is a nonnegative integer equal to the sum of the ranks of all differentials from E2 onward. For compatible sums, writing r_i=rank HFKhat(kappa_i),

    e(T1+T2)=r_1 e(T2)+r_2 e(T1)+2 e(T1)e(T2).

This uses augmentation multiplicativity and knot Floer Kunneth. In particular collapse is factorwise. The reported 9_46 discrepancy means precisely three units of differential rank; it does not yet identify the pages or their geometric maps.

## 2. Unreduction preserves the zero relation

Here is the exact algebraic correction. In the unreduced dotted theory over F2[H], let x be the distinguished-strand dot and y_i the other-strand dot on crossingless object i. Then

    x^2=Hx,  y_i^2=Hy_i,
    y_j S=x S,  S y_i=S x,
    S_i^2=x+y_i+H.

The reduced relative dot lifts as D_i=x+y_i, not as y_i alone. Therefore

    D_j S=0,  S D_i=0,  H=D_i+S_i^2

already hold before x is set to zero. In particular the relevant DS product is not restored.

More precisely, if B is the reduced quiver algebra, the two-object unreduced algebra has presentation

    A = B tensor_{F2[H]} F2[H,x]/(x^2+Hx).

The inverse dictionary sends y_i to D_i+x. The reduction x=0 has a graded algebra section. Applying that section entrywise lifts every formal differential, preserving d^2=0 and all existing homotopies. Thus a(0)+S+hS has an unreduced lift with both dot actions.

This is also consistent with KWZ1910.14584 Section 4/Theorem 4.21: the reduced quiver already describes formal universal cobordism complexes with nonempty boundary. Being the cube of an actual diagram is additional structure.

There is a second distinction in the proposed analogy. P_n is a path through successive differential arrows; its literal product S(DS^2)^(n-1)DS in the quiver algebra is zero. To regard it as a second nonzero variable requires a specified derived quotient or transferred operation. Merely adjoining the second dot does not construct an F[U,V] complex. The putative edge operators d_D,d_P also need a functorial construction beyond reading a particular curve normal form.

This refutes the proposed lift obstruction, not SHAPE for actual tangles.

## 3. NP is a two-face statement, with a factor equivalence

Define w_+(T) as the homological breadth of the generators at the maximum quantum degree of N(T), and w_-(T) at the minimum. Then

    NP iff w_+=w_-=0,
    w_±(T1+T2)=w_±(T1)+w_±(T2).

The second identity follows because each extreme quantum slice of a tensor product is the tensor product of the corresponding extreme slices. Thus NP is both closed and co-closed under compatible sums. No unit-arrow assumption or list of permitted edge directions is needed.

NP asks that the two q-exposed faces be points. A vertical exposed edge is necessary for cancellation there, not sufficient: all its generators might have one parity. Thus NP remains stronger than EXT, which remains stronger than END.

## 4. A precise test of the proposed mechanism package

The formal object already passes SHAPE. Its b-part is one point plus Q+hQ, where a square Q has generators

    a=(0,0), b=(1,2), c=(5,8), d=(6,10).

The D-edge differential a->b, c->d is acyclic, as is the P-edge differential a->c, b->d. Consequently both edge collapses of point+Q+hQ have homology F. Its Euler polynomial is1, but q=10 is occupied in h=6 and7 with opposite signs. NP and END fail.

Even granting the higher degrees in your item (iii), the same object remains possible. On Q set

    d4(b)=c,   d6(a)=d,

all other images zero. The degrees are (4,6) and (6,10), exactly (r,2r-2); delta drops by1, and all composites vanish. Filtering by h, page4 removes b,c and page6 removes a,d. Doing this on both squares leaves the point.

For a nontrivial knot-Floer-sized target, use d4 on both squares and d6 only on the unshifted one. The survivors are the point, a in hQ and d in hQ, in delta degrees 0,-1,-2. These match the right-handed trefoil's Maslov-graded rank pattern; independent Alexander labels 1,0,-1 give its HFK dimensions. No quantum/Alexander identification was supplied in the proposed package.

This is not a geometric Floer sequence and not an actual tangle. It does show that the separate existence of the lift, SHAPE, two collapses and a sequence with those bidegrees—even with a compatible nontrivial terminal rank/Maslov pattern—does not force NP. A mechanism must specify additional compatibility that this object violates. I have not shown that an actual cube or its geometric maps can realize these simultaneous choices.
