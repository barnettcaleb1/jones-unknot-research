## 2. Daemi proves quantum monotonicity, for his sequence

Use Daemi, *Abelian Gauge Theory, Knots and Odd Khovanov Homology*, arXiv:1508.07650, author version dated2January2017, §1.3, Lemma3.10, Corollary3.15 and Theorem4.9:
https://www.math.wustl.edu/~adaemi/PFH.pdf

His sequence starts from reduced Khovanov homology of the mirror over a characteristic-two Novikov field. His delta degree increases by1, and Theorem4.9 identifies the Khovanov degrees by

    h_Kh=h_Daemi,  q_Kh=2(h_Daemi-delta_Daemi)+1.

Thus d_r has precisely (h,q)-degree(r,2r-2). The Novikov coefficients do not carry hidden grading shifts here. The target rank is

    2^{b1(Y)} |Tor H1(Y)|.

It is det(L) for nonzero determinant and **2**, not0, when Y=S3_0(kappa). This theorem does not transfer its degree rule to the Ozsvath–Szabo sequence. I have not promoted a stronger identification of terminal delta with signature; the matching argument below does not need one.

Here is the exact consequence in our language. For V bigraded, put one graph vertex at every generator with multiplicity, joining degrees differing by(r,2r-2), r>=2. Define

    mu(V)=dim V-2 maximum_matching_size.

Graded cancellation along actual spectral-sequence pages supplies a matching on the initial multiplicities. Conversely a matching defines some abstract filtered differential; it need not be geometric. Every edge changes delta=q/2-h by1, so the graph is bipartite by delta parity. Every matching preserves the delta Euler characteristic, whose absolute value for reduced link Kh is det(L). Daemi gives the opposite bound. Therefore

    mu(Kh~(L;F2))=det(L),          det(L)!=0.

Equivalently the smaller delta-parity part satisfies Hall's inequality for every subset. This is a real necessary condition on a *formal* closure profile: one failed closure excludes its realization. Success on further actual torus knots is already required by the theorem, so it does not add evidence specifically for NP.

For T(n), mu=|n| when n!=0, and mu<=2 when n=0. Hat HF has rank at least|n| in the first case and positive even rank at least2 in the second. Hence

## 4. Your ladder works, with stronger compatibility than requested

Let Q be our compact Bar–Natan component with bullet square at

    (h,q)=(0,0),(1,2),(5,8),(6,10).

Q is not a single thin Khovanov-special closure block. Set

    L2=a(0) + Q + hQ + h^-8 q^-14 Q + h^-7 q^-14 Q.

Every Q+hQ pair has Euler class0, so [L2]=[a(0)] before taking any closure. The shift h^-8 q^-14 raises delta by1 while lowering q by14, specifying the homological placement missing from the original wording.

Define D8 by the underlying identity maps

    h^-8 q^-14 Q -> Q,
    h^-7 q^-14 Q -> hQ,

zero elsewhere. Both have degree(8,14). The sources and targets are disjoint, hence D8^2=0. It commutes with the original differential; in F2 their sum squares to zero. Under every additive graded closure functor the same identities cancel the four compact summands on page8, leaving the rational closure. In particular the integer closures leave rank|n| for n!=0 and rank2 at n=0.

This is a single formal construction across all rational slopes. It commutes with filling-side maps, because it is identity on the Q factor, and with the central actions and the square maps d_D,d_P. The unreduced lift is still present. Thus asking only for naturality of this sort does not eliminate it either.

But N(L2) has rank17, Euler polynomial1, and extrema

    q=-14 at h=-8,-7;      q=10 at h=6,7.

Both endpoint coefficients vanish: w_-=w_+=1, so NP and END fail. General ladders can be paired in groups of two or three; the two-rung model already settles the logical point.

This is not a tangle, a covering knot, or the geometric Daemi differential. It does show that *existence of a formally compatible all-closure higher differential* is not the missing realizability condition. A proposed geometric mechanism must distinguish its actual maps or actual cube from these identity-block maps, rather than only checking degrees, ranks, central actions or closure naturality.

## 5. The corrected low-rank theorem and its residual case

Hedden–Watson, *On the geography and botany of knot Floer homology*, Selecta24(2018), Corollary8, gives rank-three trefoil detection over F2:
https://arxiv.org/html/1404.6913v3

TH-S and our banked inversion-independent obstruction a2(kappa)=0 therefore give dim N>=5 for flat nonrational T. No inversion list is needed. For completeness the trefoil and T(2,5) have one unmarked strong inversion, the figure-eight two; Hirasawa–Hiura–Sakuma2312.06156 Proposition2.2 gives the statement and classical attribution. Marked inversions are a different count. FRW2203.01402 Theorem A detects the *bigraded* HFK of T(2,5), over Q, not total rank5.

We can say more in the equality case without a classification. If dim N=5 and T is flat, rank HFK=5 and the sutured sequence collapses. Write

    Delta=a0+sum_{i>0} ai(t^i+t^-i).

Here a0 is odd and ||Delta||1<=5, hence sum|ai|<=2. Flatness says a2=sum ai i^2=0. At most two units of coefficient mass cannot cancel at distinct positive squared indices. So Delta=1.

Genus detection and conjugation now force HFK ranks(2,1,2) at Alexander degrees(-g,0,g), no other degrees, with g>0. The covering knot is nonfibered. Furthermore |tau|=g. Indeed the hat canceling differential decreases Alexander grading and Maslov degree by1. If its unique survivor were the central generator, it would pair both top generators with both bottom generators. Conjugation lowers the latter's Maslov degrees by2g, so comparing the sums for two degree-minus-one pairs forces4g=2, impossible. The survivor lies at an end.

This is the exact residual profile for a hypothetical flat tangle with two specials: nonfibered, Alexander-one, rank-five, maximal absolute tau, and no rank loss in TH-S. I claim neither that such a knot exists nor that it is impossible. A theorem excluding this profile, or a restriction on its actual quotient grading, would improve the bound; the mistaken rank-five classification cannot.
