**Cyclic covers.** At a nonlongitudinal p/q closure the target rank is |p|; at the longitude H1=Z and it is two. So the EKT torsion contribution is absent. What follows is that the *excess total rank* must disappear. It does not follow that every generator placed in X dies and every generator placed in a rational summand survives. Actual maps can mix them, and the rational curve summand need not itself have the target dimension. For a Jones-unit knot there is one final class, but it need not descend from the initially selected q0 pawn.

Here is the precise two-row consequence, which also proves the final sentence of your board addendum. Suppose an even-quantum graded profile is supported on adjacent delta rows and has Euler polynomial1. Put p=q/2. The rows are one common multiset M plus a pawn atp0 in one row. Its Daemi maximum-matching residual is1 exactly when

    pawn upper: M is multiplicity-free and lies strictly in p>0;
    pawn lower: M is multiplicity-free and lies strictly in p<0.

For the upper-pawn case every lower generator must match. The lower prefix throughp has |M_<=p| targets but at most |M_<p|+1 possible sources, giving multiplicity<=1. Its minimum can only be reached from the pawn, so p_min>0. Conversely, order0<p1<...<pm and pair0->p1,p1->p2,...,p_(m-1)->pm. The surviving generator is upperpm, not the pawn. Reverse order and rows for the other case. Empty M is allowed. The proof has an independent internal read.
