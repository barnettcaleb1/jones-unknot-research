## 2. Two-bridge Theorem A: the geometric input can be proved

The relevant group is the mixed three-strand subgroup with labels (1,2,2), equivalently Artin B_2. In the report's handedness its generators are Y=sigma_1^2 and X^-1=sigma_2. Its central quotient is C_2*Z. Calling it the full B_3 action on a fixed ordered colored tensor product is inaccurate.

Here is the all-word justification needed before applying the automaton. Insert f_2 on the single doubled edge k of the quotient theta graph. This extracts the normalized spin-two eigenvalue uJ. Slide that same projector along k by naturality of colored crossings; idempotence allows it on both band legs. We do not need the claim that every arbitrary internal correction turnback independently closes in one predetermined way.

The resulting multiplicity space is

    H=Hom(V_1,V_1 tensor V_2 tensor V_2), dim H=2.

The band cup E_1 is the color-zero fusion of the two V_2 legs. The closing functional phi is the color-one fusion on the adjacent (V_1,V_2) pair. Thus E_1 is an X-eigenvector and phi is a left Y-eigenvector. Normalize their trivial-theta pairing to one and their local eigenvalues to one. In the basis (E_1,F), F in ker phi,

    X=[[1,xi],[0,-t]],   Y=[[1,0],[eta,t^-3]].

The eigenvalue ratios are the ordinary colored crossing ratios. The mixed central full twist acts as a scalar on the fixed total color; hence (YX^-1)^2 is scalar. YX^-1 is nonscalar, so it has trace zero. This forces

    xi eta=t^-3(1-t^4).

## 3. Theorem B: accept the nonunit conclusion, repair its boundary cases

Put E=Y^-1X, so E^2=t^4 I, and use

    F(n_0,...,n_(k-1))=e_1^T E Y^n_(k-1) ... E Y^n_0 e_1,

with n_i nonzero for i>=1. The printed span formula is false before the initial degenerate portion is removed. An exact counterexample to that formula is

    F(-1,1)=t+t^4-t^5,

of span four, whereas its unnormalized use gives three. The repair is exact:

* If n_0=-1, remove the initial string of -1 entries; each contributes just t^4. The first remaining exponent, if any, is >=1 or <=-2.
* If n_0=0, remove it and the immediately following string of +1 entries. The state is (1,-1). At the first remaining exponent n replace it by n-1, since Y^n(1,-1)=Y^(n-1)e_1. Again the new initial exponent is >=1 or <=-2.
* If nothing remains, the coefficient is a monomial. Otherwise apply the generic automaton to the remaining sequence m_0,...,m_(r-1).

For completeness, the post-E states at t=infinity are

    R: deg(v_2/v_1)=-1, leading coefficient 1;
    S_j: deg(v_2/v_1)=-4-j, leading coefficient (-1)^j, j>=0.

Positive exponents give the pre-E degree -3 and return to R. A negative exponent -m from R gives degree 3m-1 and goes to S_0. From S_j, m>=2 gives degree 3m-3 and goes to S_0; m=1 gives the exact pre-E form -1+t^3 r_old and goes to S_(j+1). There is numerator cancellation in this last transition, but it is controlled exactly. The factor multiplying v_1 has a unique leading term throughout: pre-E degree is never -4. This establishes closure of the automaton, including arbitrarily many consecutive -1 entries.

The reciprocal identity also holds exactly:

    F(n_0,...,n_(k-1))(t^-1)
      =t^-4k F(-n_0-1,-n_1,...,-n_(k-1))(t).

It follows from conjugation by D=diag(1,-t^3). For the normalized sequence the resulting span is

    c_0 + sum_(i>=1) c_i,
    c_0=3m_0+1 if m_0>=1, and 3|m_0|-2 if m_0<=-2,
    c_i=3|m_i|-2+2[sign(m_i)!=sign(m_(i-1))].

Every term is positive. Both extreme coefficients are +/-1. Full details, including the degree-gain table and the positive-block formula, are in `work/theory_restart/twobridge_automaton_referee.md`.

The remaining topological implication needs only one direction. The fully degenerate cases reduce, modulo the mixed centre, to the cup/cap double coset: X powers at the band cup and Y powers at the other closing vertex. These powers slide into the respective closing neighborhoods; the central twist contributes ribbon framing. Their core theta graph is trivial. This proves zero span implies the trivial underlying theta graph in the cases reached by the automaton; we do not assert an independent classification of all unknot words.

## 4. Where ladders sit in the braid picture

At the abstract-complex level Q+hQ is the associated graded object of a contractible cone on id_Q. That explains the degree (1,0). It does not make every occurrence of that profile an unreduced geometric Reidemeister-II pair.

The report's positive polynomial matrices reproduce the Poincare polynomial of the proposed example with crossings and their inverses. Its categorical lift CAT/CAT' is explicitly left unconstructed in Sections 4 and 8. Therefore “B is the actual unreduced complex with the RII differential deleted” is, at present, a model rather than a proved chain-level identification. Even proving that one example would not classify general ladders. Deleting a differential is not an operation on tangles. The actual endpoint counterexample above contains a Jones-neutral four-square configuration, so invisible configurations are not confined to formally invalid objects; their occurrence as a summand does not realize the whole flat ladder by itself.

A precise higher-band statement would start with a proved marked presentation, its mixed colored representation rho_n, and its actual cup c and cap phi. Then

    J=unit * phi rho_n(w)c.

After choosing a basis and a common denominator, this is a weighted sum over state paths. A useful no-tie theorem must prove that the sum of coefficients of all maximal paths and of all minimal paths is nonzero, and that their total exponents differ, for every nontrivial geometric double coset. A unique maximal/minimal path is a sufficient special case, not the statement forced by the topology. The theorem must be invariant under the allowed braid and cap moves; an arbitrary word normal form has not supplied that.

For two band legs the projective ratio automaton supplies exactly this information. With more legs there are several competing channels and leading residues, not one ratio. Neither faithfulness of the entire representation nor tropical growth alone prevents this one matrix coefficient from cancelling. The real missing input is a closed state system with geometric trivial cases and controlled tie coefficients. Theorem B supplies it only in dimension two. I would finish and record that class result before launching any larger-braid search.
