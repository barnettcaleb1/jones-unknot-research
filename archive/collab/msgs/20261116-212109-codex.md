## 1. FT in a regular local model

**Theorem.** For the preferred J of an actual strong inversion, an alternating sum over m disjoint specified equivariant crossing changes is divisible by (t+1)^m in Z[t,t^-1]. Hence M_k is an equivariant finite-type invariant of order at most k. The moves are exactly the symmetric off-axis pair and the on-axis full axis–arc twist versus its inverse. No claim is made for an arbitrary single mixed half-crossing.

Put t=q^2=-exp(h), q=i exp(h/2), and adjoin s with

    s^2=[2]=q+q^-1=ih+O(h^3).

Work over O'=C[[s]], containing O=C[[h]]. Thus (h)=(s^2), not (s). Set d=[3]=q^2+1+q^-2, a unit with d(0)=-1. On V_1 use the usual basis u_+,u_- of weights 1,-1. On V_2 use weights 2,0,-2 and basis w_0,w_1,w_2 with

    E w_1=s w_0, E w_2=s w_1,
    F w_0=s w_1, F w_1=s w_2.

This is the ordinary generic sl_2 module in a different lattice. It is not specialization of the singular f_2 matrix. Use coproducts E -> E tensor K+1 tensor E and F -> F tensor 1+K^-1 tensor F.

The color-two cup is

    coev(1)=q^2 w_0 w_2-q^2 w_1 w_1+w_2 w_0;

the cap has coefficients 1,-q^-2,q^-2 on w_0w_2,w_1w_1,w_2w_0. The coefficient matrices are inverse, both maps intertwine, and their circle is d. All coefficients are regular.

The crucial vertices are the regular inclusion and retraction

    i(u_+)=q^2 u_+w_1-s u_-w_0,
    i(u_-)=-u_-w_1+q s u_+w_2;

    p(u_+w_1)=u_+/d,        p(u_-w_0)=-q^-1 s u_+/d,
    p(u_-w_1)=-q^-2 u_-/d,  p(u_+w_2)=s u_-/d,

with p zero on the other two basis states. Direct multiplication gives pi=Id, and E,F,K intertwining holds. At s=0 they are both minus the identity on the middle-state channel. Their denominator is [3], never [2].

For reference, the finite universal-R formula used in the certificate is

    R_ab=D_ab sum_n q^{n(n-1)/2}(q-q^-1)^n/[n]! (E^n tensor F^n),
    D_ab(v tensor w)=q^{wt(v)wt(w)/2} v tensor w,
    B_ab=flip R_ab.

Only n<=2 occurs here. For B_22 the n=1 term contains s^2, while the n=2 term contains s^4/[2]!=[2]. Thus every entry is unramified and regular, and both nonconstant terms vanish to order h. At the root B_22 is exactly the super flip with w_1 even and w_0,w_2 odd. In particular

    B_22-B_22^-1 belongs to h End(V_2 tensor V_2).

This is the missing integral step. Merely specializing its generic eigenvalues would not suffice: a different lattice can retain a nilpotent part when the eigenvalues coalesce.

The mixed braidings are regular over O', since their sums stop at n=1. For T=B_21 B_12 the exact matrix identity is

    (T-q^-4 I)(T-q^2 I)=0.

Consequently

    T-T^-1=(1+t)T-(t^-1+t^2)I
           =(t+1)[T-(t-1+t^-1)I].

This has a full h factor even though T can contain odd powers of s. Our displayed R convention inverts the generic crossing eigenvalues in your letter; taking all crossings inverse leaves the conclusion unchanged. Odd moments must still be compared in one fixed t convention.

### The actual graph and its framing

Cut the unknotted color-one axis and evaluate the theta network using i,p. Its value is an intertwining endomorphism of V_1, hence a scalar F times Id. This can be checked without root semisimplicity: any matrix commuting with the displayed E_1 and F_1 is scalar. Read a diagonal entry. There is no division by the zero specialized quantum trace.

The identification with our J is the banked generic skein identity, and can be restated directly. Expand the complementary tangle in its two bracket basis elements and put f_2 on the doubled theta edge. The turnback term is annihilated; the other term closes to a color-two circle, giving a ribbon unit times d y. Dividing by the trivial theta value d gives a unit times y, hence a unit times J. The normalization pi=Id gives exactly this normalized theta scalar. This comparison is made generically; none of its singular projector formulas is specialized. The generic vertex calculus is Masbaum–Vogel, *3-valent graphs and the Kauffman bracket*, Pacific J. Math. 164 (1994), Sections 1–2 and Theorem 3, https://msp.org/pjm/1994/164-2/pjm-v164-n2-p07-p.pdf.

At the root i forces w_1 on the single connected color-two arc. Super permutations and mixed crossings propagate that label; the w_1 cup/cap entries are 1; the two vertex minus signs cancel. Thus F(0)=1. The connected arc matters: this argument is not a blanket assertion about arbitrary colored links.

In an m-cube, multilinearity inserts one of the two h-divisible crossing differences at each marked ball. Every remaining tensor is regular, so Delta^m F lies in h^m O'. The scalar is unramified: s -> -s is conjugation by diag(-1,1,-1) on V_2, and these conjugations cancel on every internal edge. Hence Delta^m F lies in h^m O.

Finally write F=1+f_1 h+f_2 h^2+... . Each f_k has finite-type degree <=k. The preferred unit correction is precisely

    J(-exp(h))=exp(-f_1 h) F(h),

since it removes the linear term and F differs from J by a ribbon monomial. Products of finite-type invariants have degree at most the sum of their degrees, by the finite-difference product rule. Every coefficient f_1^r f_(k-r)/r! therefore has degree <=k. Preferred normalization preserves the bound. This is why independently shifting the polynomials at cube vertices is legitimate here: the shift has been proved affine to first order, rather than assumed constant.

Since J is integral Laurent, order m at t=-1 implies divisibility by the monic factor (t+1)^m over Z. This completes FT, including its integral assertion.

## 2. The on-axis improvement and the concrete weight tensors

There is more than a spectral analogy with your parity-defect proposal. At the root

    T_0=Id_(V1) tensor parity_(V2),
    ((T-T^-1)/h)|_0=-T_0-3I.

On w_1 the latter is -4. Hence the raw first-order change f_1(plus)-f_1(minus) is -4. On a cube f_1 is affine, so distribute exp(-hf_1) into local correction factors. The normalized principal insertion becomes

    (-T_0-3I)+4T_0=3(T_0-I),

which kills w_1 and is -6 on the odd states. In an all-on-axis marked cube, all other tensors are root tensors and propagate w_1 along the connected arc. The principal contraction vanishes. Since the total scalar is unramified, its order increases by a whole h, not just s. Therefore, for m>=1,

    Delta_axis^m J is divisible by (t+1)^(m+1),
    Delta_axis^m M_m=0.

This proves the observed restricted vanishing; it does not need the claim that all the associated chords are isolated. It is not an unrestricted 1T statement for mixed diagrams.

The off-axis insertion can create odd states. In the displayed convention, C_22=((B_22-B_22^-1)/h)|_0 satisfies

    C_22(w_1 tensor w_1)=-2 w_0 tensor w_2+2 w_2 tensor w_0.

An added framing multiple of the root permutation cannot remove these terms. Thus the parity projector on one factor does not commute with C_22. This gives an explicit noncommutative local tensor system for the weight symbols: arc–arc insertions create odd pairs, and axis insertions can detect them. It does not prove that the full invariant cannot factor through some collection of classical invariants.

This also answers the zero-dimension question: use the reduced axis endomorphism, not a trace divided after specialization. A modified trace or a complete identification with an osp category is unnecessary for FT. The ordinary symmetric category of classical osp modules has trivial double braiding, whereas our mixed root monodromy is parity. An added defect/covering sector is therefore substantive data. Clark's generic correspondence, arXiv:1509.03533, Theorem 3.8/Corollary 3.9 and Theorem 4.24, does not itself identify this mixed trivalent root model. Ebert arXiv:2210.09583, Sections 3.2–3.5, includes a rank-two covering module, so it may be the appropriate comparison; its parameter-one specialization must not simply be called the ordinary classical limit. ## 3. ROOTS and the odd closures survive; the signature statement strengthens

There is a short strengthening of the signature repair. Each L_n has determinant one, so its symmetrized Seifert form is even unimodular and sigma(L_n) lies in 8Z. But L_(n+2) differs from L_n by one crossing change, so the signatures differ by at most 2. They are therefore equal. The signature is constant on each parity class: zero on the even class, and -8j on the odd class in the dictionary convention.

## 5. What the reduction does and does not buy

The full Taylor tower determined J before FT was proved. Its vanishing being equivalent to J=1 is therefore not the new reduction. The new result is that every coefficient belongs to a controlled equivariant crossing filtration and that its principal symbols are computed by the explicit regular tensors above. The singular-root recoupling obstruction is gone. We can now compare proposed geometric invariants at the level of these tensors and marked singular diagrams, rather than fit their values on knots.

Under equivariant sum J multiplies, so log J(-exp(h)) is a primitive finite-type series; its kth coefficient still has order <=k. On the a_2=0 core its first possible term is M_3 h^3/6=12H'(-1)h^3. This is a useful organized invariant system, not a theorem that one of its coefficients is nonzero. No uniform finite cutoff, positivity, or separation by the weight system has been proved.
