## 1. The covering comparison

Put Q=-t^-1. The original color-two evaluation and the folded ordinary
Jones evaluation have Dubrovnik/BMW parameters

    r_- = Q^-2, d_- = 1-Q-Q^-1;
    r_+ = Q^3,  d_+ = (Q+Q^-1)^2.

Their braid eigenvalues are respectively (Q,-Q^-1,Q^2) and
(Q,-Q^-1,Q^-3). The contraction is the trace channel, and the skein relation
B-B^-1=(Q-Q^-1)(I-E) holds on every channel. Thus these are actual bulk
skein evaluations. Exactly,

    r_+-r_-=Q^-2(Q^5-1),
    d_+-d_-=Q^-2 Phi_5(Q).

The missing axis information is supplied by ALL winding generators, not
just the Hopf value. Let A_n be the annular closure of
sigma_1...sigma_(n-1). Turaev's annulus theorem supplies generation by their
products; only generation, not an idempotent basis or specialized linear
independence, is needed. A convenient precise source is Pokorny's 2021
thesis, Section **2.4**, printed pp.27–28, citing Turaev, J. Math. Sci.52
(1990),2799–2805:
https://escholarship.org/content/qt0dx137gc/qt0dx137gc.pdf .

For the original mixed full twist T, the eigenvalues are Q^2 and -Q^-1,
with reduced weights 1 and -(Q+Q^-1). Hence

    L_n^- = Q^(2n)-(Q+Q^-1)(-Q^-1)^n.

For the folded fundamental crossing B, use T_fold=-Q^(-3/2)B. The singlet
and triplet give

    L_n^+ = Q^(-3n)+(Q^2+1+Q^-2)(-Q^-1)^n.

The exact identity is

    L_n^- - L_n^+
      = Q^(-3n)(Q^(5n)-1)
        -Q^-2 Phi_5(Q)(-Q^-1)^n.

Framings matter here. The mixed T(2,2n) closure, with one component the axis,
is A_n with moving-component framing zero; A_n has framing n-1. Thus the
original value is r_-^(n-1)L_n^-. The actual double lift of A_n is
T(n,2), with diagram writhe 2(n-1); its two-strand presentation T(2,n) has
writhe n. Since theta_U=-Q^(3/2), the lifted framed value is

    theta_U^(n-2) Tr(B^n)=r_+^(n-1)L_n^+.

The factors differ by Q^(5(n-1)). All generator values therefore agree at
primitive fifth roots. Products on the original side are compositions of
scalar operators on the simple axis; on the cover side the disjoint cycle
braid blocks lift to split blocks. This proves the comparison for every
closed annular diagram. It does not require a full defect-category equivalence.

For an arbitrary quotient theta graph, take a neighborhood ball of one
whole axis edge and its vertices. Its inside tangle is the normalized
projector P_1=ip; the complement is an arbitrary (1,2)-tangle A. Eliminate
the vertices using

    P_1=(T+Q^-1 I)/(Q^2+Q^-1).

Both closures still have an unknotted axis, so the annular comparison applies.
In the cover the vertices are the fundamental cup/cap, with singlet projector

    P_0=(T_fold+Q^-1 I)/(Q^-3+Q^-1).

The coefficients agree at Q^5=1 and their denominators are nonzero. The
local T-to-T_fold framing is fixed with arbitrary outside tangle: the mixed
full twist lifts to one fundamental crossing; closing it is the meridian,
whose lift is the zero-framed unknot. This forces the single inverse-curl
factor theta_U^-1. Thus the projector step uses an actual relative map,
not an operator guessed from trace data.

Finally, let F,G denote these raw reduced evaluations. Each is a ribbon
