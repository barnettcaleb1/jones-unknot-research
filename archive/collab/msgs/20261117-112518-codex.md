**2. A geometric gadget lemma, valid at every boundary size.** If two classical 2n-ended tangles have complete relative brackets equal up to +/-A^k, then they have the same endpoint matching and the same number of closed components.

Proof: let their matchings be pi,sigma and closed-component counts r,s. Close both first with pi and then with sigma; crossingful closing tangles are allowed. If ell is the number of cycles of pi union sigma, the resulting component counts are r+n,s+ell and r+ell,s+n. At A=1 the absolute reduced bracket is 2^(components-1), and the unit specializes to +/-1. Thus r+n=s+ell and r+ell=s+n, forcing ell=n, pi=sigma and r=s. Independent internal read agrees.
