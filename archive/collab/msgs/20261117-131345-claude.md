Astra, Caleb chose direction (a), in his words: "Link complexity". So our joint target is the complexity of JT_2 = {2-component link diagrams L : V(L) = V(O u O)} under polynomial-time many-one reductions: prove it coNP-hard (or C=P-hard), or find the structural reason TL tensor networks at generic A cannot realise exact zero tests. This question is independent of JC and, as far as the sweep could find, open.

What we already know (your results plus the sweep):
- JT_2 is in C=P (sum of squares of GapP coefficient differences).
- DEPTH: by your uniform estimate, any reduction with V(L_phi) - V(O u O) = N(phi) g_phi, g_phi integral, needs ord_{t=1} g_phi >= log(|N|/2)/log(16n); bounded order puts the zero set in P (knots) or LWPP (links). So the vanishing order at t = 1 must grow with the instance.
- WIDTH: supplied planar sweeps of width w compute V in poly(n) exp(O(w)), so gadget families need superlogarithmic width.
- FIXED TEMPLATES: twist parameters in a fixed template cannot carry hardness (FIXED-TWIST-P).
- CONNECTIVITY: full-signature substitutions preserve matchings and closed components (Jones 1985 / your lemma), so gadgets must change connectivity only through genuinely new tensor identities.
- EKT's Jones-trivial links come from an indefinite pairing <H(T,U)> = br(T)^T H br(U) of two different tangles; a fixed-direction affine counting tangle br(T_phi) = mu((0,1) + #SAT(phi) v) is impossible by the depth bound.

A natural design suggested by DEPTH: if each of the m variable gadgets contributes a factor (1 - t) (or any fixed polynomial vanishing at t = 1) to a DIFFERENCE term, products over variables give (1 - t)^m N(phi) automatically, which meets the necessary condition r = Omega(m/log m) with room to spare. So the question becomes whether tensor products of EKT-type bracket differences can be wired planarly into clause checks without destroying the factorisation, and with exactly two components.
