**1. CONSTANT-BRAID: exact generic constant gates have finite projective image.** Fix N physical strands and a standard generic Jones/TL module over C(A). Let Gamma be a braid subgroup preserving a generic code W. Suppose one common basis of W makes each generator act as

    rho_W(beta_i)=u_i(A) C_i,

with u_i nonzero rational functions and C_i constant complex matrices. Then the projective group of the C_i factors through the permutation image of Gamma in S_N. In particular its order is at most N!.

Proof. At A=1, delta=-2 and (I+e_i)^2=I, so every pure braid specializes to the identity on the full module. For a pure beta, the eigenvalues of u_beta(A)C_beta are among the full braid eigenvalues, all tending to1 as A tends to1. Therefore all eigenvalues of the constant C_beta agree. At a regular unitary specialization with sufficiently high level, the full finite module has a positive-definite invariant form; its invariant code inherits it, so C_beta is diagonalizable. Hence C_beta is scalar. The projective kernel contains Gamma intersect P_N, proving the assertion.

A code basis may have a pole or degenerate at A=1: the argument uses ambient eigenvalues there, and a different regular unitary point to remove Jordan blocks. It does not assume a regular limiting code. For the positive-form specialization see [Jones–Reznikoff, section2](https://msp.org/pjm/2006/228-2/pjm-v228-n2-p02-p.pdf) and [Freedman–Larsen–Wang, equations14–15](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/universality-02.pdf); the note verifies the finite-dimension hypotheses. No density theorem or faithfulness claim is imported.

Consequences:
- An exact generic braid compiler cannot realize a constant gate library with infinite projective group on one invariant code.
- If a proposed m-bit reversible library contains the alternating permutation group on its 2^m basis states, N! >= (2^m)!/2 forces N>=2^m for m>=2. This is conditional on realizing the entire specified gate group, not one accepting amplitude.
- Even a rational-code projective Hadamard H=[[1,1],[1,-1]] is excluded by a separate integral-lattice argument: braid matrices and their inverses are integral at the prime(2), so every eigenvalue has 2-adic valuation0, whereas lambda H has eigenvalues +/-lambda sqrt2 and valuation v2(lambda)+1/2. This cannot vanish for lambda in Q(A).

These restrictions do not cover postselection, leakage followed by projection, changing codes, general noninvertible tangles, or gates depending genuinely on A. They explain why approximate universality at a selected root does not automatically provide the generic exact compiler we need.

**2. The product factor exists formally; a carrier must eliminate all lower subsets.** For a multilinear context C,

    C(T_1-S_1,...,T_m-S_m)
       = sum_E (-1)^(m-|E|) C(T_i for i in E; S_i otherwise).

If T_i-S_i=kappa_i D_i, the left side has factor product kappa_i. But it is a signed sum of actual diagrams, not automatically one diagram.

For a proposed background carrier P_i+z_i D_i, write h_E=C(D_i in E; P_i outside E). Exact reusable multiplication requires

    C(P_i+z_iD_i)=h_empty+H product z_i

and therefore h_E=0 for **every nonempty proper subset** E, with h_full=H!=0. This is also necessary if the identity is required on every Boolean input, by multilinear interpolation. One mixed derivative calculation does not check these conditions.

There is a sharp obstruction to the bare crossing version. In TL2,

    R_+ - A^2 R_- = A^-1(1-A^4)e.

After extracting the displayed factors, each h_E is a monomial unit times the bracket of an actual smoothing/reference link. It is nonzero, since its A=1 value is nonzero. Thus no actual outside context kills the proper subsets for m>=2 in this architecture. This does not exclude EKT divided defects that are themselves signed combinations rather than actual smoothings.

A pure nonzero vector divisible by A-1 cannot itself be an actual complete tangle bracket: every actual closure has nonzero bracket at A=1. The vanishing signal must live beside a background, not replace the whole physical tensor.

**3. TL2 distinguishes a scalar binary cancellation from reusable multiplication.** Let ell(I)=alpha!=0, ell(e)=beta and Z=beta I-alpha e. Then

    ell((I+xZ)(I+yZ))
       =alpha+xy alpha beta(alpha delta-beta).

This formal binary AND is real algebra, but the open output still contains (x+y)Z. Closing kills it; it cannot simply be fed into a next gate as a product-only state.

For m>=3, no serial invertible-background TL2 carrier can have only a constant term and a nonzero full product in independent inputs. Singleton vanishing puts all defects on the same line KZ; pair vanishing then gives ell(Z²)=0. The quadratic relation of Z forces ell(Z^k)=0 for every k>=1, killing the full product. This is a fixed two-state serial restriction, not a theorem about arbitrary planar networks.

**4. The projector escape works algebraically and creates too many components.** Set p0=e/delta and p1=I-p0. Then

    product_i(p0+z_i p1)=p0+(product z_i)p1.

After clearing denominators, the integral carrier is C(z)=e+z(delta I-e), and

    product_i C(z_i)
      =delta^(m-1)[e+(product z_i)(delta I-e)].

Suppose actual two-string tangles realize these C(z_i(A)), up to monomial units, with z_i(1)=0 as in your proposed per-variable vanishing signal. Each specializes to +/-e. Our two-closure A=1 argument already determines matching and closed-component count from that specialized equality: each has cup-cap matching and no closed component. Serial composition therefore creates exactly m-1 closed components internally. Every external closure adds at least one more. For m>=3 the result cannot have two components.

Those internal components need not be split or unknotted; their number is the obstruction. Outside wiring cannot fuse components that have already closed inside. A seam cut or additional boundary legs changes this problem and is a possible escape, requiring a new tensor certificate.

Formal matrix algebras do permit multiplication: M_i=I+z_i E_(i,i+1) in Mat_(m+1), with output M_11+M_(1,m+1), gives1+product z_i. The matrices and the output functional are still uncompiled. These M_i are nontrivial unipotents, so they cannot be generic braid restrictions either. This control prevents mistaking the two-state obstruction for a universal algebraic impossibility.

**5. The common prefactor needs classical roots as well as Taylor depth.** To avoid half powers use

    F_L(t)=t^-1/2 V_L(t)+1+t^-1 in Z[t,t^-1].

Every two-component link has (t³-1)|F_L. If F_L=N D integrally with abs(N)>2, the Arf evaluation at i forces Phi4|D. If one common D realizes counts1,2,3, the sixth-root evaluation forces Phi6|D: the three values would form -sqrt3+jC, j=1,2,3, inside the signed real/imaginary powers prescribed by [Lickorish–Millett, Theorems1 and3](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~millett/Papers/1986Millett6LickorishCommMathHelv.pdf); the only such progression has C=0. The elementary proof is in the root note.

Thus a common D_m=(1-t)^m C(t) needs C divisible by Phi3 Phi4 Phi6 under these count hypotheses. Adding those fixed factors passes this arithmetic screen; it does not solve realization. Depth at least2 also forces linking number0. No knot-specific J-root law is imported into this link statement.
