## Agent: rigid-case


### strongest_true_statement

Conventions: <crossing> = A<A-sm> + A^{-1}<B-sm>, V(t) = (-A^3)^{-w}<D> at t = A^{-4}; at a positive crossing the A-smoothing is the oriented one.

THEOREM (unit-fiber crossing formula). Let D be ANY diagram of K with V(K)=1, n crossings, writhe w, so <D> = (-A^3)^w is a monomial. For a positive crossing c let L0(c) be the oriented smoothing (2-component link, linking number l_c) and S(c) the other smoothing (a knot with n-1 crossings; w(S) = w-1-4 l_c, since crossings between the two arcs flip sign). Then EXACTLY
 V(K_-(c)) = t^{-1} + t^{3l_c-1}(t-1) V(S(c)),
 V(L0(c)) = -t^{-1/2} - t^{3l_c+1/2} V(S(c)),
 P_c := V(K_-(c)) - 1 = t^{-1}(t-1)( t^{3l_c} V(S(c)) - 1 ).
(Negative c: mirror, t <-> t^{-1}.) Proof: A<L0> + A^{-1}<S> = (-A^3)^w gives <L0> from <S> = (-A^3)^{w(S)}V(S); insert in <D_-> = A^{-1}<L0> + A<S>, normalize by (-A^3)^{w-2}, and use the skein relation for L0.

COROLLARIES. (a) c is flat iff t^{3l_c}V(S(c)) = 1 iff (l_c, V(S(c))) = (0,1). Hence, with minimality (S(c) has <= n-1 crossings so it is the unknot or has V != 1), every crossing of a universally rigid minimal D is of exactly one type:
 Type I: S(c) is an unknot and l_c != 0; then V(K_-(c)) = t^{-1} - t^{3l-1} + t^{3l} (a trinomial), V(L0(c)) = -t^{-1/2} - t^{3l+1/2} (a binomial), and V(i) = +-1 forces l = 0 or 3 mod 4 (det K_- = 1 or 3). l=-1 is the unknot->trefoil/Hopf pattern.
 Type II: S(c) is a nontrivial knot with V(S(c)) != 1 and all skein data are affine in V(S(c)) as above.
(b) All crossing-change and smoothing polynomials of D are determined by the n smaller knots S(c) and the integers l_c: the unit fiber linearizes every skein triple (in a general fiber the analogous identity carries the non-monomial P and has no such reading).
(c) Twist family at c: V_n(c) = 1 + P_c (1 + t^2 + ... + t^{2(n-1)}) (mirror for the other sign); the family meets the unit fiber only at n=0, span V_n(c) = span P_c + 2|n| - 2 for |n| large, and the extreme coefficients of V_n(c) are those of P_c, i.e. of t^{3l_c}V(S(c)) - 1.
(d) Lando: in every diagram of K both extreme state coefficients vanish and -(n+2s_B-2) <= 3w <= n+2s_A-2; for the twisted diagrams D_n(c) the extreme state coefficients equal +-(extreme coefficients of P_c), so the Lando independence complexes of D_n(c) have Euler characteristic read off from S(c).

### all_crossings_argument

No, and the failure point is sharp. Summing the per-crossing formulas gives one identity with one unknown. Let B_D(A,d) = sum_s A^{sigma(s)} d^{|s|-1} be the two-variable state sum (the Bollobas-Riordan/Tutte polynomial of the Tait graph); V is its restriction to the curve d = -A^2 - A^{-2}. Differentiating along the curve and using B = (-A^3)^w there:
 sum_c A<D_A(c)> = (n+3w)/2 (-A^3)^w + (A^2 - A^{-2}) Phi_D,
 Phi_D := sum_s (|s|-1) A^{sigma(s)} d^{|s|-2}|_{curve} = d_d B on the curve.
So sum over positive c of t^{3l_c}V(S(c)) (plus the mirror sum over negative c) equals an explicit expression in n, w and Phi_D. Phi_D is the normal derivative of B_D off the curve: a diagram quantity, not a knot invariant, and not constrained by V=1 (unknot diagrams realize arbitrarily complicated Phi_D). Every all-crossings sum of skein data (oriented smoothings, crossing changes, twist families) factors through this single Laurent polynomial, so no counting, degree or Newton-polygon argument can close without an independent handle on Phi_D.

Why the other routes fail: (1) The per-crossing rigidity conditions t^{3l_c}V(S(c)) != 1 are inequations (open conditions); open conditions never combine to a contradiction by counting, and F4 shows they hold for crossing-minimal representatives of every fiber. (2) Degrees: Kauffman bounds give max/min deg V(S(c)) from the (n-1)-crossing diagram S(c), but there is no lower bound on span V(S(c)) (S(c) may be an unknot, Type I), so no degree mismatch is forced. (3) Newton polygon: B_D has nonnegative coefficients and collapses to a monomial on the curve; every unknot diagram does the same, so positivity alone cannot see knottedness. The only ingredient that distinguishes D from a rigid unknot diagram is knot detection (Kh != Kh(U), Kronheimer-Mrowka), which is invisible to B_D and to all bracket-level skein identities; Khovanov skein sequences at each crossing have the same defect: no additive identity over all crossings.

### unit_fiber_specific

Unit-fiber only (use <D> monomial): the exact affine formulas V(K_-(c)) = t^{-1} + t^{3l-1}(t-1)V(S(c)), V(L0(c)) = -t^{-1/2} - t^{3l+1/2}V(S(c)), the criterion flat iff (l_c, V(S(c))) = (0,1), the Type I/II dichotomy, the Type I trinomial/binomial values, and the statement that all skein data are determined by the smaller knots S(c) and the integers l_c. In a general fiber <D> = (-A^3)^w P and the flat criterion reads <S(c)> = (-A^3)^{w-1}P, which has no (l, V(S)) reading. Also unit-fiber: Lando vanishing in every diagram, the bound |3w| <= n + 2s - 2 for every diagram, and the identification of the extreme coefficients of the twisted diagrams D_n(c) with those of t^{3l_c}V(S(c)) - 1. Fiber-generic (consequences of F2/F4 only): the geometric twist recurrence, "family meets fiber once", the inequation form of rigidity, and the Phi_D obstruction. The Arf/det restrictions on Type I (l = 0,3 mod 4) are unit-fiber but root-of-unity, so F5 applies to them.

### first_lemma_or_no_handle

First lemma to attempt (Lemma T, Type I classification): if c is a Type I crossing of a minimal Jones-trivial K, then l_c = -1 (after mirroring), i.e. K_-(c) has the trefoil's Jones polynomial and L0(c) the Hopf link's. Equivalently: no knot has V = t^{-1} - t^{3l-1} + t^{3l} and no 2-component link with lk = l has V = -t^{-1/2} - t^{3l+1/2}, for l not in {0,-1}. Sketch: Arf (V(i)) kills l = 1,2 mod 4; for l = 0,3 mod 4 one needs whole-polynomial input: Lee's theorem puts rank-2 Lee generators of L0(c) in homological degrees 0 and 2l, so Kh(L0(c)) has rank >= 4 while its Euler characteristic is a binomial; combine with the Kh skein triangle at c (Kh(K) = Kh(U) + cancelling pairs) and the span bound span V <= c(K_-) to force l = -1. If Lemma T holds, Type I crossings are "unknot-like" (unknot diagram with a trefoil crossing change) and the counterexample's structure is carried by Type II crossings, where S(c) is a nontrivial smaller knot; this reduces R to statements about the n knots S(c).

Honest limit: beyond Lemma T, the rigid case has no bracket-level handle. Everything derivable from F1-F3 at all crossings factors through the n inequations t^{3l_c}V(S(c)) != 1 and the one unknown Phi_D, and rigid unknot diagrams satisfy all of them; the only distinguishing input is knot detection (Khovanov), which must be imported, not derived.
