The full proof notes are self-contained mathematical arguments. The Python scripts preserve the computations used for examples, and JSON files preserve diagrams and results. They expect Spherogram 2.4.1, Regina 7.4.1, knot_floer_homology 1.2.2 and the workspace package paths described in the notes. The preexisting planar Temperley-Lieb engine is not bundled; its m=2,...,12 results are in physics_check.json, and the all-m formula is proved in physics.txt. The source-code excerpts mentioned in whitehead_quotient_followup.txt are not bundled; their pinned public source URL and license are identified there.
