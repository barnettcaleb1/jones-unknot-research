JONES UNKNOT DETECTION: AN ARITHMETIC ROUTE, SIMPLIFIED AND CLOSED
1 November 2026

STATUS

No proof of Jones unknot detection and no nontrivial knot with Jones
polynomial identically one has been obtained. The result below explains
why one proposed shortcut fails. It is an elementary deduction, not a
claim of a new theorem in the literature or progress toward a full proof.

The useful correction in this pass was to replace an elaborate
three-strand commutator construction by ordinary two-strand torus knots.
The elaborate construction had not addressed a stronger mathematical
question. Continuing to refine it would have been a diversion.

A SHORT, EXACT COUNTERTEST FOR FINITE ARITHMETIC PROBES

Choose any finite nonempty list of finite fields F_(q_i), of
characteristics p_i. Set

  m = lcm_i [p_i (q_i-1)],       K = T(2,2m+1).

Then K is a nontrivial classical knot, but

  V_K(t) = 1 for every nonzero t in every selected field F_(q_i).

This is equality of evaluation functions over those fields. It is not
an identity of integer Laurent polynomials, nor even necessarily a
coefficientwise identity modulo the selected characteristics. There is
no bound on the knot's complexity independent of the selected fields.

Proof.

In the convention V_unknot=1 and V_trefoil=t+t^3-t^4, the standard
two-strand torus knot formula is

  V_T(2,2m+1)(t) = t^m (1+t+t^2-t^(2m+2))/(1+t).       (1)

The quotient is a polynomial; its numerator vanishes at t=-1.
For a nonzero t in F_q with t != -1, the condition (q-1)|m gives
t^m=1. Substitution into (1) gives V_K(t)=(1+t)/(1+t)=1.

At the excluded point use the polynomial value, rather than dividing
by zero. Differentiating the numerator in (1), or performing polynomial
division first, gives the integer identity

  V_T(2,2m+1)(-1) = (-1)^m (2m+1).                    (2)

Every p_i(q_i-1) is even, so m is even. Also p_i divides m. Thus (2)
reduces to 1 in each selected characteristic, including characteristic
two where -1=1. This covers every nonzero parameter.

Over the integers, however, (2) is 2m+1>1. Since the unknot has value
one at every parameter, K is nontrivial. In particular its determinant
is 2m+1. All positive multiples of m yield further examples. QED.

For example, selecting F_2, F_3 and F_5 gives m=60 and K=T(2,121).
Every nonzero parameter in these three fields gives value one, while
the integer value V_K(-1) is 121.

For completeness, formula (1) follows directly from the two-strand
Temperley--Lieb algebra. Put delta=-A^2-A^-2 and e^2=delta e. The braid
generator is g=A I+A^-1 e. For n>0,

  g^n = A^n I + [((-A^-3)^n-A^n)/delta] e.

Closing I gives delta and closing e gives 1. Multiply the resulting
bracket by (-A^3)^(-n), put n=2m+1 and t=A^-4, and simplify to (1).
This derivation takes place over the rational function field; the
result is a polynomial identity before any specialization. Consequently
the exceptional specialization t=-1 is handled legitimately by (2).

WHAT THIS RULES OUT, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

An unbounded search cannot promote agreement in a prescribed finite
collection of fields into exact Jones equality. A sequence passing more
and more tests can simply have increasing knot complexity. The same knot
is not being shown to pass all tests simultaneously.

Finite tests remain valid exact certificates when coupled to sufficient
degree and coefficient bounds. For instance, an integer polynomial of
degree at most D and coefficient magnitude below p is zero if it
vanishes at an element of F_(p^k) having minimal polynomial degree k>D.
After shifting Laurent exponents this applies to Jones polynomials too.
The missing construction must meet these bounds for the very same knot;
the example above does not. No claim that arithmetic methods in general
are impossible follows.

OTHER AUDITS FROM THIS PASS

1. The published Eliahou--Fromentin family has V(K_r)=1 modulo 2^r.
   Its recurrence gives, with N=2^(r-1),

     content(V(K_r)-1)=2^r,
     span V(K_r)=14N+1,
     both extreme coefficients=2^N.

   Here content means the positive gcd of all coefficients. Thus this
   particular sequence has explicitly nonzero coefficients at every
   stage. Its growing divisibility does not produce an exact Jones-one
   knot. The all-r induction and exact seed checks are preserved in the
   accompanying evidence archive. These deductions are not claimed to
   be previously unknown.

2. Quantum approximation does not itself give an exact polynomial
   identity. If P=V-1, H is its coefficient l1 norm, and zeta has order r,
   algebraic norm gives |P(zeta)| >= H^(-(phi(r)-1)) whenever P(zeta)
   is nonzero. Beating this bound certifies an exact value at that root;
   a separate degree bound is needed to certify P=0. No construction
   meeting both requirements with certified knot nontriviality emerged.

3. Finite-quotient pigeonholing using the ambient Temperley--Lieb algebra
   did not supply a generic kernel element within its own safe degree
   and height reconstruction range. This is a failure of that counting
   argument, not a lower bound for every structured braid construction.

RESEARCH ASSESSMENT

The error in the research process was allowing formal reductions,
necessary conditions and failed shortcuts to accumulate as though they
constituted a developing proof. They do not. This pass closes a proposed
arithmetic shortcut; it supplies no new bridge from V_K=1 to K=unknot.

The earlier audit of Carmi--Cohen identifies a specific problem with the
claimed fixed-clasp isotopy in Proposition 5.12(G2). That finding concerns
the supplied proof, not the truth of the unknot-detection conjecture.
The arXiv record checked on 1 November 2026 still lists only v1. Manolescu's
2026 ICM survey, Section 2, describes Jones unknot detection as open.
