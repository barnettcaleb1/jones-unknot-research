JONES UNKNOT INVESTIGATION: CABLING AND CLASSICAL REALIZATION
31 October 2026 — bounded continuation

STATUS
There is still no general proof and no constructed counterexample. This pass
produced a proved conditional reduction and a precise algebraic target for a
counterexample. The hypotheses missing from both are substantial. No novelty
claim is made for any deduction here.

1. ONE HIGHER COLOR WOULD FORCE ALL COLORS — WITH A GLOBAL QUANTIFIER

Write H_N(K) for the reduced, zero-framed dimension-N colored Jones polynomial,
so H_1=1 and H_2=V. The following UNIVERSAL statements are equivalent:

 (i)   For every knot K, V_K=1 implies H_3(K)=1.
 (ii)  For every knot K, V_K=1 implies H_N(K)=1 for all N.
 (iii) Jones-one knots remain Jones-one under one fixed cable of winding r>=2,
       with slope one meridian plus r preferred longitudes.
 (iv)  Jones-one knots remain Jones-one under the positive untwisted
       Whitehead-double operation.
 (v)   Every fixed satellite pattern P with P(U)=U preserves Jones-one knots.

Statement (iii) is equivalent for EACH fixed r>=2. These are reductions, not
proofs that any of the statements is true. In particular, knowing H_2=H_3=1
for ONE knot is not the same as the universal assertion (i).

Proof of the substantive cable implication.
The standard cabling formula expresses H_N(C_r(K)) as a sum of N terms
 c_k H_{|2rk+1|}(K),   k=-(N-1)/2,...,(N-1)/2.
The coefficients sum to one and have pairwise distinct lowest q-exponents.
Consequently no nonempty subset of them sums to zero.

Suppose the Jones-one class is closed under this cable. It is already closed
under connected sums. If color N is one throughout that class, apply the cable
formula to K, K#K, ..., K^(#N). Multiplicativity gives the moment equations
 sum_k c_k H_{|2rk+1|}(K)^m = 1,  m=0,...,N.
Grouping equal values and applying a Vandermonde matrix forces every value
to be one. In particular color r(N-1)+1 is one throughout the class.
Starting at N=2 produces the unbounded sequence of trivial colors r^a+1.

Habiro's expansion implies that, for each fixed d, the h^d coefficient of
H_N(K;e^h)-1 is a polynomial in N. Its infinitely many zeros force it to vanish
identically. Thus every color is one. This step uses exact Taylor coefficients,
not an exchange of asymptotic limits.

For winding two, the ordinary cable formula is
 V_{C_2(K)} = a H_3(K) + b,  a+b=1, a!=0.
This proves equivalence with (i). A fixed satellite has a finite colored-Jones
expansion, so (ii) implies (v), and (v) implies (iii).

The Whitehead bridge is particularly explicit:
 V_{Wh+(K)}(t)-1 = ((1-t^3)/(1+t)) (H_3(K;t)-1).
The rational prefactor is nonzero. Hence (iv) is equivalent to (i).
We checked the identity independently against actual Whitehead-double and
two-parallel diagrams for 5_1 and 10_132, and their previously computed
color-three polynomials. Those two knots have the same ordinary Jones but
different Whitehead responses. General dependence on ordinary Jones therefore
fails; a successful proof must exploit the particular fiber V=1.

There is also an unconditional individual-knot statement: all ordinary Jones
values on the closure of {K,U} under connected sums and one fixed C_r are one
if and only if all colored Jones polynomials of K are one. This is an expanded
test family, not a result about the ordinary Jones polynomial of K alone.

What this does not finish.
No proof of (i) has been found. Even all-colored unknot detection is still
explicitly posed as an open question in the 2025 updated Kirby problem list,
p32. The proved Melvin–Morton–Rozansky consequence of all-colored triviality
is Delta_K=1. The categorified colored-Jones detection theorem is different.

An explicit formal obstruction to purely algebraic shortcuts.
Let P(N,k)=product_{j=1}^k(q^N+q^-N-q^j-q^-j). The sequence
 F_N=1+(q-1)P(N,2)
has integral cyclotomic coefficients, F_1=F_2=1, and F_3!=1. It is also
q-holonomic: product_{j=-2}^2(E-q^j) annihilates it, where E shifts N by one.
Its MMR diagonal is one. These conditions therefore do not by themselves
force color-three promotion. This is a formal sequence, not a knot invariant
known to be realized by any knot.

2. A FORMAL TANGLE WHOSE REALIZATION WOULD GIVE A COUNTEREXAMPLE

The previous pass's four-special-component cancellation model survives the
stronger algebraic tests attempted here. Let A be the rational-arc Bar–Natan
object and X the standard 12-generator s_4(infinity) object. Consider
 D = A direct-sum q h X direct-sum q h^2 X.
Its Khovanov cone splits into the rational component and four shifted special
components X, X[1]{2}, X[1], X[2]{2}.

We explicitly constructed the higher-operation extension of X, the corrected
lift of its central-G nullhomotopy, and the full 24-generator cone. The naive
G-times-identity map upstairs would have omitted four correction arrows;
these are included. Exact checks pass in characteristic two and with signed
integer coefficients. An independent audit checked the operation formulas,
gradings, signs, and all possible path lengths.

The model passes these specified tests:
 - ordinary tangle-algebra equations and the G mapping cone;
 - rational-filling pairings and their Euler characteristics;
 - the listed central connectivity actions;
 - the required A-infinity object and cone extensions;
 - a formal unreduced rational Frobenius/Lee lift.

The last check matters: unreduced Lee differentials have quantum jumps four,
whereas the reduced Bar–Natan bars here have jumps two. The relative-dot lift
converts each reduced length-one bar into a length-two unreduced bar over Q,
so this is not a contradiction. The formal slope-one closure has unreduced
rational Kh rank18 and Lee rank2.

The geometric consequence is sharp and conditional. If a SINGLE classical
tangle realizes D with the prescribed relative gradings, then its slope-one
closure has
                  V_K=1,    rank reduced Kh_F2(K)=17.
The rational part contributes rank1 and the four special parts contribute
four each. Euler contributions from the special parts cancel. An overall
grading shift causes no loophole: a signed-monomial Jones polynomial of a
knot is forced to be1 by V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0. Rank17 excludes the unknot.
Its infinity closure has reduced Kh rank1 and hence is actually the unknot.

NO CLASSICAL TANGLE REALIZING D HAS BEEN CONSTRUCTED. An object in an additive
algebraic category is not automatically the invariant of a diagram. We have
not checked every conceivable geometric or naturality condition. The result
rules out proving non-realizability merely by reimposing the specific tests
listed above. Realizing this object would already solve the problem negatively.

3. A GEOMETRIC REDUCTION THAT WORKS, AND AN ANCHOR THAT CANNOT WORK

For every K, the oriented double K#rK has a strong inversion exchanging the
summands; r means orientation reversal, not mirror. First taking the ribbon
double R=K#r(mK), then R#rR, shows that Jones detection for strongly invertible
ribbon knots would imply Jones detection for all knots.

This does not turn that same composite knot into a rational filling beside
an actual unknot. In fact no nontrivial composite determinant-one knot has
such a presentation. Its double branched cover is a reducible integer homology
sphere. An unknot filling would make it surgery on a knot in S3, contradicting
Gordon–Luecke's theorem that an integer homology sphere obtained by such surgery
is irreducible. Thus any nontrivial Jones-one knot obtained from an actual
unknot-anchored rational-tangle problem must be prime.

The strong-inversion quotient still exists, but its other branch fillings
are different knots. For the swapping inversion on K#rK, a relevant response
is Wh+(K). Its Jones polynomial depends on H_3(K), precisely the missing bridge
in section1. The two approaches therefore converge on the same unproved step.

4. A PROPOSED SIGN GENERALIZATION WAS FALSE

The prior Kinoshita–Terasaka two-band family was excluded for all full twists
because its three leading coefficients had the same sign. An actual analogous
8_19 template instead gives leading degree/coefficient pairs
                    (5,-2), (5,+1), (4,-1).
Thus mirror symmetry and unlink cuts do not force a common sign. Degree parity
still excludes every twist in this example: no same-parity subset of leading
coefficients sums to zero. This family was already excluded by its nonzero
second Jones derivative, so this is a falsification of a proposed proof
mechanism, not an advance into the Alexander-one counterexample search.
