WHY THE FORMAL COMPONENT-REMOVAL CONSTRUCTION CANNOT EXIST
Research note, 1 November 2026

Status
------
This is a proved obstruction to one proposed construction. It is not a
proof of Jones unknot detection, nor a counterexample. No novelty claim
is made for the elementary surgery lemma used below.

The idea
--------
Eliahou, Kauffman and Thistlethwaite construct nontrivial links with the
Jones polynomial of an unlink. Their two-slot outside tangle has reduced
Kauffman-bracket pairing

    H = [[h, delta^2], [delta^2, delta]],
    delta = -A^2-A^(-2),
    h = -A^(-14)-A^(-6)-2A^(-2)-2A^2-A^6-A^14.

An extra split unknotted component multiplies a bracket by delta. The
tempting shortcut is therefore to divide this entire pairing by delta,
hoping to obtain a knot-producing version of the same invariant mechanism.
The division does give Laurent polynomials:

    G = H/delta = [[g, delta], [delta, 1]],
    g = A^12-A^8+2A^4+2A^(-4)-A^(-8)+A^(-12).

It also preserves the algebraic intertwining identity responsible for the
EKT construction. But it cannot be realized by a single actual outside
tangle with two disjoint four-ended filling slots. Here is a complete
topological obstruction.

A general lemma
---------------
Let K be a knot of determinant one. Choose two disjoint embedded bands
on K. Suppose that performing either band move alone produces a link of
determinant zero. Then performing both produces a link whose determinant
is either zero or a perfect square.

Proof. The double cover of S^3 branched over K is an integral homology
sphere Y. The two disjoint band moves lift to integral surgeries on a
two-component link in Y (the Montesinos trick). Write the surgery linking
matrix as

    B = [[a,b],[b,c]],   a,b,c integers.

The single surgeries have first homology presented by [a] and [c]. Their
zero determinants force a=c=0. The double surgery therefore has first
homology presented by [[0,b],[b,0]]. This homology is infinite when b=0;
otherwise its order is b^2. This is exactly the asserted determinant.
End of proof.

Application to G
----------------
Fill each of the two slots by one of the two crossing-free rational
tangles, 0 or infinity. If G were realizable, its four closures would have
brackets g, delta, delta, 1. Put zeta=exp(pi*i/4). Then

    delta(zeta)=0,    g(zeta)=-8.

The absolute bracket value at zeta equals the link determinant: the
Jones writhe correction has absolute value one. Thus these four closures
would have determinants

    [[8,0],[0,1]].

The infinity/infinity closure is a knot: its bracket at A=1 has absolute
value one, whereas an r-component link has absolute bracket value
2^(r-1) there. Its determinant is one. Switching either slot from
infinity to 0 is a band move, and the slots are disjoint. The lemma says
the double switch must have square determinant, contradicting 8.

This argument does not assume the determinant-one anchor is an unknot.
It therefore does not use the conjecture being investigated. Common
framing monomials cannot repair the contradiction, because their absolute
values at zeta are one.

What this establishes
---------------------
Formal division by an unlink factor does not necessarily correspond to
removing a component geometrically. Here that distinction is decisive,
even though the quotient has integral Laurent coefficients and retains
the desired algebraic symmetry. The entire proposed H/delta mechanism
is excluded, independently of crossing number or band complexity.

This does not classify all outside tangles or all invariant pairings.
It does not exclude every possible conversion of a Jones-trivial link
into a knot. A separate five-component input examined in this pass passes
a necessary homological test, but no suitable bands or Jones-one knot
were constructed; that test is not evidence of a counterexample.

Assessment of the larger search
-------------------------------
We have not obtained a complete proof scaffold with an independently
tractable missing step. Algebraic identities, finite necessary tests,
and formal tangle models have repeatedly left geometric realizability
unproved. Closing one of those routes is a useful negative result, but
does not establish that the general conjecture is close to resolution.

The appropriate standard for another construction is an actual diagram
or a theorem ensuring realization, followed by an exact Jones calculation
and an independent certificate of nontriviality. For a proof, a proposed
bridge must explain why ordinary Jones data forces a known topological
unknot criterion; merely assuming that bridge restates the hard part.

Verification
------------
Two independent mathematical audits checked the surgery argument,
distance-one fillings, anchor hypothesis and normalization phases. Exact
Laurent arithmetic checked the original invariant pairings; the quotient
identity and root-of-unity values were checked separately. The proof
above depends on no finite enumeration or numerical approximation.
