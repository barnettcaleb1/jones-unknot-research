JONES UNKNOT DETECTION: CONCRETE INTERDISCIPLINARY EXPERIMENTS
31 October 2026

Outcome: no proof of Jones unknot detection and no counterexample. This
pass establishes a complete obstruction for one tangle family, identifies
a failed geometric repair as a specific known knot, and eliminates every
assignment in one proposed seven-node interaction model. The claims below
are restricted to those constructions. No novelty claim is made.

1. A DNA TANGLE AMBIGUITY, ITS PRECEDENT, AND A LITERATURE PROBLEM

Use the standard rational tangles <r>, numerator closure N, and horizontal
tangle addition +. The plus signs below are geometric operations, not
ordinary addition of fractions. Set

  X = <-1> + <1/2> + <1/3>,     R = <1/4>.

In the Spherogram conventions used by the certificates,

  N(X) = N(R) = unknot,
  N(X+<1>) = N(R+<1>) = T(2,-5).

These are actual knot equivalences: the first diagrams reduce to one
crossing-free unknot, and the second diagrams reduce to identical planar
diagrams for the closure of sigma_1^(-5). Jones equality was not used to
infer unknotness or isotopy.

X is nonrational. Its double branched cover is Seifert fibered over a disk
with exceptional multiplicities 2 and 3. Killing the regular fiber gives
the nonabelian quotient C2*C3 of its fundamental group. A rational tangle
has solid-torus cover, whose fundamental group is cyclic. Adding the
integral summand changes the boundary marking, not this obstruction.

An equivalent ambiguity is already in Ernst--Sumners (1999), Example 3,
pp.33--34, DOI https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004198002989 . The authors list
both O=(1/2,2/3,-1) and O=-1/4 with the SAME reaction tangle -1 and the
same two products. Mirroring and redistributing integral twists gives the
pair above. Author-uploaded full text:
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/231762263_Solving_tangle_equations_arising_in_a_DNA_recombination_model

This conflicts with the unframed Conjecture 4 as printed in Sikora,
arXiv:2005.08162v2, which asserts that a rational solution of a two-probe
system forces every solution to be rational. The same assertion appears
in the published article's introduction. This is a problem with that
subsidiary conjecture, not with the paper's framed implication from Jones
detection. Relevant statement and distinction:
https://arxiv.org/html/2005.08162#S1.SS1

The first pair of diagrams has writhes 0 and 4; the second pair has writhes
-5 and -5. Thus the usual Kauffman brackets of the first outputs are 1
and A^12, while the second outputs have identical brackets. A common
framing adjustment cannot erase defects of four twists and zero twists
simultaneously. This example therefore does not refute Jones detection.

2. ALL INTEGER TWISTS OF THIS FIXED X ARE EXHAUSTED

For every nonzero integer j and every rational tangle R', the conditions

  N(R') = unknot,
  V(N(R'+<j>)) = V(N(X+<j>))

hold exactly when j=1 and R'=<1/4>. In particular no rational comparator
gives the corresponding two framed equalities for any j != 0.

Here is the exhaustive reduction. A rational tangle with unknot numerator
must be <1/k>, k an integer; k=0 includes infinity. Determinants give

  |1+jk| = |6j-1|.

Consequently k=-6, or j(k-6)=-2. The second branch consists only of
(j,k)=(1,4),(2,5),(-1,8),(-2,7). Exact normalized Jones calculations
exclude the last three. The remaining branch is infinite, but can be
excluded symbolically rather than by a bounded search.

For that calculation use delta=-A^2-A^-2, e^2=delta e, b=A^-1+Ae,
N(f+ge)=delta*f+g. This formal A is the inverse of the geometric bracket
variable above. For any integer j,

  delta*A^j*N((f+ge)b^j)
    = (delta^2-1)f + (-1)^j*A^(4j)*(f+delta*g).

After the writhe correction for X versus <-1/6>, the even-j difference
has the form C0*(1-A^(4j)), with
  C0=A^6+A^-2-A^-18-A^-26 != 0.
It can vanish only for j=0. For odd j the difference is
C0-A^(4j)*C1, where the support endpoints of C0 are [-14,50] and of C1
are [-14,46]. Equality would require j=0 from the lowest exponents and
j=1 from the highest exponents, a contradiction.

An independent Regina calculation checked the four exceptional pairs and
representative values on the infinite branch, confirming the orientation
and normalization conventions. The symbolic argument, not the finite
checks, covers all integers. Full proof and coefficient data are archived.

3. MIRROR COMPENSATION PRODUCES THE KNOWN KNOT 10_153

To repair the framing defect, vertically stack each tangle with its mirror:
S_X = X * mirror(X), S_R = R * mirror(R). The numerator of a vertical
stack is the connected sum of the two numerator closures, so both initial
outputs are actual unknots. The framing mismatch cancels there. However,

  N(S_X+<1>) = 10_153,     N(S_R+<1>) = unknot.

The X diagram simplifies from 13 to 10 crossings and has exactly the same
PD crossing set as the standard 10_153 diagram. The other three diagrams
in this two-probe comparison simplify to actual unknots. The polynomial
calculated independently by Regina is

  V_10_153(t) = -t^4+t^3-t^2+t+1+t^-2-t^-3+t^-4-t^-5.

This agrees with the tabulated polynomial:
https://katlas.org/wiki/10_153
It is not 1, even though its value at t=-1 is 1. Thus this construction
preserves determinant one but fails the full Jones test. It also has
nontrivial Alexander polynomial, so it is not a candidate hidden from
every elementary invariant. The repair fails in the second response.

There is also an obstruction to a broader version of this repair. In the
inverse-A skein convention, X has vertical-identity coefficient
g_X=A^4+A^-8-A^-12. Under vertical stacking this coefficient multiplies.
It is not a unit in Z[A,A^-1], so no finite vertical stack containing X
can have bracket equal to the rational-infinity bracket (0,1), even up
to a framing unit. This excludes exact skein inversion by stacking; it
does not exclude all ways of making an individual closure have Jones 1.

4. COMPLETE ELIMINATION OF THE FIXED SEVEN-NODE PHYSICS MODEL

Let C be a symmetric nonnegative integer matrix with diagonal
(0,0,0,0,1,1,1), and define

  Z_C = sum_d (-q)^(d^T C d) product_i(q^(-C_ii)x)^d_i
                            / product_i(q^2;q^2)_(d_i),
  H_N(y) = (y;y)_(N-1) [x^(N-1)] Z_C,  y=q^2.

The nine edges between nodes of the same parity receive the multiset
{0,0,0,0,2,5,6,9,11}; the twelve between opposite parities receive
{0,0,0,0,0,0,1,1,4,7,10,10}. These are abstract interaction models,
not an enumeration of knots. Every assignment has

  H_1=H_2=1,
  H_3=1+(y-1)(y^2-1)(y^4-1)(y^5-1).

For these to be normalized colored Jones polynomials of a zero-framed
knot, Habiro's cyclotomic expansion requires

  H_4 = 1+y^3*A_(4,2)+c_3(y)*A_(4,3),
  A_(N,k) = product_(j=1)^k(1-y^(N+j))(1-y^(j-N)),
  c_3 in Z[y,y^-1].

Since A_(4,3) has a zero of order six at y=1, the derivatives of H_4
of orders zero through five must be

  (1,0,0,0,4320,86400).

There are 2,514,758,400 labeled assignments. An explicit S4 x S3 orbit
partition reduces complete checking to 29,106,000 cases. The exact counts
after successive necessary tests are:

  all reduced assignments                    29,106,000
  second derivative zero                        141,944
  third derivative zero                             455
  fourth derivative equal to 4320                      2
  fifth derivative equal to 86400                      0

The last two cases give fifth derivatives 79200 and 144000. Both fail.
Independent full-polynomial calculations and a separate ordered-word
enumeration confirm these values. Root reran the full enumeration and
the independent polynomial audit successfully. No floating-point
comparisons or unfinished search limits enter the exclusion.

Therefore rearranging the edges of this proposed seven-node model cannot
produce even a compatible colored Jones sequence. This does not exclude
other multisets, other node counts, other specializations, or actual knots
whose ordinary Jones polynomial is 1.

References for the colored expansion and a geometrically constrained
alternative to arbitrary interaction matrices:
Habiro, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0605314 (cyclotomic expansion, Section 6).
Stosic--Wedrich, https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.10837 (tangle gluing and quivers).

Research consequence: this particular DNA family and this interaction
multiset have clear stopping points. Further attempts should change the
geometry or the interaction data; increasing the same twist bound or
reshuffling the same edges cannot repair these failures.
