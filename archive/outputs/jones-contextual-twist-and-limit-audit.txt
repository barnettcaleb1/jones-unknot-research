JONES UNKNOT DETECTION: TWO EXACT GATES FOR A GEOMETRIC CONSTRUCTION
1 November 2026. No full proof or counterexample obtained.

Main result
===========
For one fixed disk-twist family, infinitely many Jones-one members force
its entire zero-framed annular skein to equal that of a local unknot
(winding zero) or a core strand (winding plus or minus one). Higher
winding is impossible. This is proved using integral skein coefficients,
not by assuming that colored Jones polynomials detect the unknot.

There is also a finite certificate: unless the whole family has Jones
polynomial one, at most floor(r/2)+1 distinct parameters can have that
polynomial, where r is the number of disk intersections. Full Laurent
polynomials are tested here, not numerical values or finite Taylor jets.

A concrete sufficient counterexample construction would be a hyperbolic
two-component link, with one component an unknot axis, whose other
component has one of those exact annular skeins and the corresponding
winding. Long surgery fillings would then be nontrivial Jones-one knots.
No such link has been produced. The theorem narrows this construction;
it neither excludes isolated counterexamples nor reduces every possible
counterexample to this construction.

The separate convergence test closes another shortcut. For a nonunit
Jones polynomial, its vanishing order relative to one is at most its
span, hence at most the knot's crossing number. Combined with the
classical ropelength bound, n-fold Taylor agreement requires ropelength
at least (4*pi*n/11)^(3/4). A sequence converging in C1 to a smooth
embedded knot eventually has constant knot type. An exact counterexample
cannot first appear only at the limit of such nonexact approximations.

What changed in the research decision
====================================
Stop looking for infinite Jones-one disk-twist families of winding at
least two. At winding zero or one, require an actual annular pattern
with the exact scalar/core bracket before any further surgery analysis.
Do not pursue shrinking finite-order corrections under uniform smooth
geometric control: that proposed completion is impossible unless an
exact Jones-one example has already occurred.

The appended proofs are internal research deductions checked against
standard primary formulas. No novelty or external-review claim is made.


PART I: CONTEXTUAL FULL-TWIST PROOF
=================================

PASS 46: CONTEXTUAL FULL TWISTS AND THE SINGLE-CHANNEL GATE
2026-11-01. Deduction from standard annular skein formulas; no novelty claim.

Outcome
-------
For an arbitrary fixed exterior, an infinite full-twist family of knots
can have Jones polynomial one only in the following two situations:

  winding zero: the initially zero-framed annular bracket is delta;
  winding +/-1: the initially zero-framed annular bracket is z.

Here delta=-A^2-A^-2, z is the annular core, and the empty link has
bracket one. In particular, larger-winding channels cannot give a new
contextual escape, even when a meridional disk has arbitrarily many
intersection points. This is stronger than merely listing possible
twist eigenvalues: integrality and the unknot Jones value remove all
higher single-channel possibilities.

This does not prove the surviving patterns geometrically trivial. A
hyperbolic annular knot with one of these skein classes would yield
actual nontrivial Jones-one knots under all sufficiently long twists.
No such pattern was constructed here. The winding-zero case returns
to the previously identified scalar-pattern realization problem.

1. Geometry and conventions
---------------------------
Let C be an unknot disjoint from an oriented knot P in S^3. Its exterior
is a solid torus V. Fix the standard meridian-longitude marking and
write w=lk(P,C), equivalently the signed intersection sum with a
meridional disk of V. Let r be the geometric intersection count with
one chosen transverse disk, so |w|<=r and w=r modulo two.

Choose the framing of P which is zero in the original S^3 embedding.
Let P_k be the knot obtained by k meridional twists of V. This is an
actual knot for every integer k; twists do not alter component count.
Equivalently, it is surgery on the unknot C with the corresponding
reciprocal-integer slope. Choose the sign of k so that the annular
twist operator has eigenvalues theta_j below.

An ambient torus twist changes the transported framing of P by k*w^2.
This is the usual surgery linking formula: the framing change is the
product of the winding numbers of P and its parallel push-off. It can
also be checked in an r-strand disk diagram: ordinary braid twisting
adds k(w^2-r) to the oriented writhe, and transporting the individual
ribbon framings adds kr. The final total is kw^2, independent of r.
The familiar w=1 core acquires one framing twist, calibrating the sign.

Consequently, to compute the normalized Jones polynomial of P_k one
must remove (-A^3)^(kw^2), not an r-dependent framing correction.

2. Exact spectral formula with integral coefficients
----------------------------------------------------
The annular skein is Z[A,A^-1][z]. Use the monic Chebyshev basis

 e_0=1, e_1=z, e_(j+1)=z e_j-e_(j-1).

Write the initially zero-framed bracket of P as

                   [P]=sum_j a_j(A)e_j,

where a_j belong to Z[A,A^-1]. Because the change from powers of z to
the e_j basis is monic triangular, this coefficient integrality is
literal, not rational-projector integrality. Only j congruent to w
modulo two occurs, and j<=r.

Let Delta_j=<e_j> and theta_j be the annular twist eigenvalue. Then

 Delta_j=(-1)^j[j+1],       theta_j=(-1)^j A^(j(j+2)),
 [n]=(A^(2n)-A^(-2n))/(A^2-A^-2),       Delta_1=delta.

The normalized Jones polynomial, with t=A^-4, is therefore

 V_(P_k)(A^-4)
   =delta^-1 sum_j a_j(A)Delta_j
                   (-A^3)^(-kw^2) theta_j^k
   =sum_j b_j(A) A^(k d_j),

 b_j=a_j Delta_j/delta,        d_j=j(j+2)-3w^2.       (1)

The signs cancel because j-w^2 is even. The numbers d_j are distinct
as j varies over nonnegative allowed labels. Formula (1) keeps all
exterior complexity in the fixed integral coefficients a_j.

Primary eigenvalue and annular-basis source: Masbaum, Skein-theoretical
derivation of some formulas of Habiro, AGT 3 (2003), equations (2)--(4),
printed p.539, and the twist discussion preceding them:
https://msp.org/agt/2003/3-1/agt-v3-n1-p17-p.pdf
Yokota's Twisting formulae of the Jones polynomial, Math. Proc. Cambridge
Philos. Soc. 110 (1991),473--482, concerns precisely winding/wrapping and
meridional twisting. Only its publisher extract was accessible this
pass; no uninspected theorem or novelty claim is attributed to it:
https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100070559

3. Finite certificate; no infinite-root fallacy
----------------------------------------------
For distinct integers d_1,...,d_s and distinct integers k_1,...,k_s,
the matrix (A^(d_i k_l)) is invertible over Q(A). Sort both integer
lists increasingly. Strict rearrangement shows that the identity
permutation gives the unique largest exponent in the determinant.
Its coefficient is nonzero, so the determinant cannot vanish.

Thus a nonzero sum of s distinct monomial exponentials has at most
s-1 integer zeros. Apply this to V_(P_k)-1. If q coefficients a_j
are nonzero, then a nonconstant family has at most q Jones-one
parameters, or at most q-1 if some active d_j is zero. Also
q<=floor(r/2)+1. The assertion concerns distinct parameters, not
necessarily consecutive ones.

In particular, infinitely many Jones-one parameters force (1) to be
identically one for every k. Linear independence then forces exactly
one active label j, with

                j(j+2)=3w^2,       a_j Delta_j=delta.    (2)

This argument does not treat an exponential expression as an ordinary
polynomial in k. The generalized Vandermonde supplies the precise
finite-dimensional certificate.

4. Integrality removes every higher channel
-------------------------------------------
Evaluating the second equality in (2) at A=1 gives

               a_j(1)(-1)^j(j+1)=-2.

Since a_j(1) is an integer, j+1 divides two. Hence j=0 or j=1.
Equation (2) now gives w=0 or |w|=1, respectively. Moreover (2)
determines the entire annular bracket, not merely its value at A=1:

       j=0: a_0=delta and [P]=delta;
       j=1: a_1=1     and [P]=z.

Conversely either displayed annular class, with its corresponding
winding, makes (1) identically one. Thus the criterion is exact.

The eigenvalue equation alone is a Pell equation
(j+1)^2-3w^2=1 and admits higher solutions such as (j,|w|)=(6,4).
Those are unrealizable as a single surviving channel with Jones-one
closure because of the integral dimension equation above. Keeping
only the spectral equation would falsely leave these possibilities.

An initial framing other than zero multiplies the entire annular
class by one power of -A^3. Removing that common factor gives exactly
the statement above; arbitrary channel-dependent framing changes
are not allowed.

5. Actual calibration tests
---------------------------
For the r=3 coherent unknot closure of the braid sigma_1 sigma_2,
the winding is three. Inserting full twists gives the actual torus
knots T(3,3k+1), whose Jones polynomials in one chirality convention are

        V_k=t^(3k)+t^(3k+2)-t^(6k+2).

At k=0 this is one. For every nonzero integer k the three exponents
are distinct, so it is not one. The two frequencies are t^(3k) and
t^(6k), exactly those predicted by (1): labels j=3,1 have d=-12,-24.
Thus a genuine unknot anchor does not itself annihilate either channel.
The torus formula was independently derived in research40 from TL_3.

For a zero-winding Whitehead crossing-circle context, the familiar
unknot-to-trefoil twist family has

 V_k=1+(t+t^3-t^4-1)*(t^(-2k)-1)/(t^2-1).

The source's integer parameter m is -k in our positive annular-twist
convention; in particular k=-1 gives the displayed trefoil polynomial.
The frequencies are 1 and t^(-2k)=A^(8k), exactly the zero-winding labels
0 and 2 in (1). Its second coefficient is nonzero. This actual marked
example was already verified in research36/descent_audit.txt and is
only a normalization check here, not a new construction or a generic
two-strand argument.

6. Full endpoint and its unresolved geometric content
-----------------------------------------------------
Suppose an actual P in V has [P]=delta,w=0, or [P]=z,|w|=1, and
suppose the two-component exterior S^3\nu(P union C) is hyperbolic.
Then (1) gives V_(P_k)=1 for every integer k. Hyperbolic Dehn surgery
implies that for all sufficiently large |k| the P_k exteriors are
hyperbolic, so these knots are nontrivial. This would be a complete
counterexample construction with an independent nontriviality witness.
For the filling input, see Hodgson--Kerckhoff, Universal bounds for
hyperbolic Dehn surgery, Annals of Mathematics 162 (2005), introduction:
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v162-n1-p06.pdf

The missing statement is the existence of that actual hyperbolic
pattern, not a formal choice of a_j. The derived condition is much
stronger than having one unknot closure. Conversely, an actual
unknot anchor plus these annular equalities would suffice even if
nontriviality were certified by a different topological method.

No such pattern is supplied by this pass. Local unknot patterns and
the ordinary core satisfy the required brackets but have trivial
twist outputs. Ren's known annular degree-drop example retains a
nonzero higher channel and does not satisfy either equality. The
generalized wrapping-degree conjecture cannot be used to infer
geometric locality from these skein classes; it is already false.

This is a contextual all-exterior reduction for a single full-twist
orbit, not a proof of Jones detection, not a reduction of every
hypothetical counterexample to this orbit, and not an exclusion of
isolated Jones-one parameters or multiple coupled twist sites.

PART II: GEOMETRIC LIMIT PROOF
=============================

PASS 46 — FINITE-ORDER JONES CORRECTIONS CANNOT FIRST CLOSE IN A TAME LIMIT
2026-11-01

Verdict
=======
There is a complete compactness implication, but it does not give a new
construction: sufficiently controlled geometric convergence makes the knot
type eventually constant. An exact Jones-one limit would therefore already
be an exact Jones-one knot at finite stages. Conversely, if every stage has
V != 1, arbitrarily high Taylor agreement forces unbounded geometric
complexity quantitatively. This rules out a proposed escape by shrinking
successive corrections while retaining uniformly thick, bounded-length rope.

1. An exact order bound, including the Laurent-support subtlety
==============================================================
For V_K != 1 define

  nu(K) = ord_{t=1}(V_K(t)-1),   s(K)=maxdeg V_K-mindeg V_K.

Then

                   nu(K) <= s(K) <= c(K).                 (1)

Here c is the minimal crossing number. No claim of novelty is made:
Kauffman--Saito--Sawin, On Finiteness of Certain Vassiliev Invariants,
q-alg/9507035, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1 (PDF pp.2--4), already give the
crossing-level conclusion. We supply an independent elementary argument
that also treats the potentially troublesome one-sided exponent window.

https://arxiv.org/pdf/q-alg/9507035

Do NOT simply write span(V-1)<=span(V): it is false, for example for the
trefoil V=t+t^3-t^4. The needed extra input is the universal Jones identity
V(omega)=1 at a primitive cube root omega. Together with V(1)=1 and
V'(1)=0, these are recorded as V=1 mod (t-1)(t^3-1) in Stoimenow,
Polynomial values, the linking form and unknotting numbers, Proposition
3.2, citing Jones's original result:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0405076

Proof of the first inequality in (1).
If the exponent interval of V contains zero, adjoining/subtracting its
constant term cannot increase its span. A nonzero Laurent polynomial of
span s has at most s zeros at 1, counted with multiplicity.

Otherwise mirror if necessary and write V=t^M P(t), M>=1, deg(P)=s.
If s=0, V(1)=1 makes V=t^M, contrary to V'(1)=0. Thus s>=1.
A Laurent polynomial with r distinct exponents has order at most r-1
at 1, by the Vandermonde matrix for Euler derivatives (t d/dt)^j.
Since V-1 has at most s+2 terms, the only exceptional possibility is
nu=s+1. In that case the unique polynomial supported on M,...,M+s
whose Taylor jet through degree s equals 1 is

  V(t)=1-(1-t)^(s+1) Q(t),
  Q(t)=sum_{j=0}^{M-1} binom(s+j,s) t^j.                 (2)

Existence follows by truncating (1-t)^(-s-1): the displayed V is divisible
by t^M, has degree M+s, and differs from 1 to order s+1 at t=1.
Uniqueness follows from the same Vandermonde matrix on M,...,M+s.

The coefficients a_j=binom(s+j,s) are strictly positive and increasing.
Q has no unit-circle zero. For M=1 this is immediate. For M>=2, if
Q(z)=0 and |z|=1, then

  a_(M-1) z^M = a_0 + sum_{j=1}^{M-1}(a_j-a_(j-1)) z^j.

The weights on the right are all positive and sum to a_(M-1).
Equality in the triangle inequality forces z^0 and z^1 to have the same
phase, hence z=1, where Q is positive. Therefore (2) contradicts
V(omega)=1. This excludes nu=s+1 and proves nu<=s.

The second inequality in (1) is the usual bracket state bound: for a
connected c-crossing diagram the all-A/all-B state circle counts satisfy
s_A+s_B<=c+2, so the bracket span is at most 4c. Writhe normalization
does not change span, and t=A^-4 divides the span by four.

2. Quantitative geometric deterioration
=======================================
Let length be L and thickness tau be the RADIUS of the largest embedded
normal tube (equivalently reach), so Rop=L/tau. The classical bound is

             Rop(K) >= ((4*pi/11)c(K))^(3/4).             (3)

This convention and estimate appear in Cantarella--Kusner--Sullivan,
On the Minimum Ropelength of Knots and Links, Invent. Math.150 (2002),
257--286: definition in the introduction and Section1; Section9, printed
article p.279 (preprint p.23), gives the crossing estimate. Their Lemma6
and Theorem7 also give the positive-thickness compactness/isotopy result.
https://www.jasoncantarella.com/downloads/papers/ropelength/ropelen.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-002-0234-y

Consequently every actual knot satisfying V!=1 and
V=1 mod (t-1)^n obeys

  c(K)>=n,
  Rop(K)>=((4*pi/11)n)^(3/4),
  tau <= L * (11/(4*pi*n))^(3/4).                         (4)

Thus bounded physical length forces thickness to zero at least at this
rate; fixed positive thickness forces length to diverge. This applies to
every realization of the knot type, not just a poor diagram produced by
the construction. Arbitrary rescaling cannot help because ropelength is
scale invariant.

The already certified finite-order inverse knots R_N in
work/research28/quantum_promotion.txt have V_R_N!=1 and
V_R_N=1+O((t-1)^(N+1)). Applying (4) yields c(R_N)>=N+1 and
Rop(R_N)>=((4*pi/11)(N+1))^(3/4). This is a consequence of their
established properties, not a new realization claim.

3. Why convergence cannot postpone the exact example to infinity
================================================================
Suppose smooth parametrized embeddings gamma_n:S^1->R^3 converge in C^1
to a smooth embedded gamma. A normal tube around gamma exists. For large
n, gamma_n lies in it and is transverse to its normal fibers; the closest
point projection has degree one and is a diffeomorphism on gamma_n.
Sliding each point along its normal fiber gives an isotopy, extended to
an ambient isotopy. Hence K_n=K for all sufficiently large n.

If ord_1(V_Kn-1)>=n, this fixed polynomial has arbitrarily high vanishing
order and is exactly 1. If all K_n are nontrivial, K is nontrivial. But
the same conclusion holds for EVERY sufficiently late finite stage:
the counterexample has not been created only by a limiting process.
In particular, a sequence all of whose Jones polynomials are nonunit
cannot have such a C^1 embedded limit.

For the compactness version, bounds L<=L0 and tau>=tau0>0 yield bounded
curvature and equicontinuous tangents in constant-speed parametrizations.
After translations and a subsequence one obtains a C^1 embedded limit;
positive thickness prevents new self-contact. This is the situation of
the cited Lemma6 and Theorem7. Alternatively (3) gives a crossing bound,
so (1) directly proves that n beyond the bound already forces V_Kn=1.

Uniform C^0 convergence alone is insufficient. Tie a fixed nontrivial
local knot into a shrinking ball along a round circle. The resulting
nontrivial knots converge uniformly to that unknotted circle, with lost
thickness and no C^1 convergence to its smooth parametrization. Infinite
accumulating modifications may instead produce wild or singular limits;
the ordinary Jones polynomial and the question here concern tame knots.
Bounded length alone is meaningless as a topological compactness input,
since any knot can be rescaled to prescribed length.

4. Exact scope of the conditional endpoint
==========================================
The following are equivalent as existence statements:

 (a) There is a nontrivial tame knot with V=1.
 (b) There are nontrivial K_n with V_Kn=1 mod(t-1)^n and uniformly
     bounded ropelength.

(a) implies (b) by a constant sequence. Conversely (1)--(3) force an
exact Jones-one finite stage once n exceeds the common crossing bound.
Thus a bare uniform-geometry hypothesis is not an independently easier
bridge. For the known all-finite-order constructions with V!=1 it is
actually impossible, quantitatively. This pass produces an obstruction
to the proposed limiting mechanism, not a solution of unknot detection.
