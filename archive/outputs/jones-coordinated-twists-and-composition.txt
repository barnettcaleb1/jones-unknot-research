JONES UNKNOT DETECTION: COORDINATED TWISTS AND GEOMETRIC COMPOSITION
1 November 2026. Research deductions; no full proof or counterexample.

Outcome
-------
The previous one-disk restriction cannot exclude coordinated twists.
An actual unknot shows why: two higher channels can survive and cancel
along the opposite-twist direction. Their joint skein coefficients have
quantum-dimension denominators, despite the underlying diagram being an
ordinary uncolored knot.

Two concrete attempts to exploit annular degree cancellation were ruled
out. Stacking any positive number of identical Ren-core-and-braid blocks
always retains annular degree two. Nesting two standard Ren patterns as
satellites instead gives degree eight. These conclusions cover arbitrary
parameters in the stated constructions, not just a finite search.

1. Exact theorem for repeated Ren blocks
---------------------------------------
Use sigma_i=A*1+A^-1*e_i and delta=-A^2-A^-2. In the six-strand braid

 sigma_2^-1 sigma_1^3 sigma_2^-1 sigma_4 sigma_5^-3 sigma_4,

cap positions 3 and 4 at both ends to obtain the four-strand tangle T.
This is the core of Ren's annular wrapping-number counterexample.

THEOREM. For every four-strand braid beta and every integer k>=1,
the annular closure of (T beta)^k has degree exactly two.

Proof. The four-through coefficient of T is zero. The other thirteen
Temperley--Lieb diagrams span a two-sided ideal, so the same is true
for every power. On that ideal, the coefficient of z^2 in annular
closure is the trace in the three-dimensional two-through module.

Put x=-A^-4 and use the integral gauge in the retained core certificate.
The core and braid are represented, up to nonzero scalar monomials, by
Laurent matrices M(x), U(x) over Z. Set u=(1,0,1)^t, v=(1,0,-1),
w=-15u-25(0,1,0)^t. Exact expansion of the actual core gives

 M(1)=w v,     M(1)=0 modulo5,     M'(1)=2I modulo5.

Every U_0=U(1) has determinant one and fixes u. The coefficient whose
nonvanishing is required is f_k=tr((MU)^k).

Suppose f_k were identically zero. The rank-one formula at x=1 gives
0=(v U_0 w)^k, hence v U_0(0,1,0)^t=0. On the primitive integral quotient
Z^3/Z u, the induced determinant-one matrix is triangular. Its two
diagonal entries must both be epsilon, where epsilon is +1 or -1.
Therefore tr(U_0^k)=1+2 epsilon^k, which is either 3 or -1.

On the other hand, modulo five,

 M(x)U(x)=2(x-1)U_0+O((x-1)^2).

The coefficient of (x-1)^k in f_k is consequently
2^k(1+2 epsilon^k), nonzero modulo five. This contradicts f_k=0.
Thus z^2 survives, proving the theorem. No division by k is used, so
multiples of five cause no exception. The proof also gives
ord_(x=1) f_k<=k. End proof.

The actual core expansion, its integral matrix gauge, and all fourteen
basis trace identifications were rechecked. The all-k conclusion follows
from the proof, not from testing finitely many powers.

Scope: this covers repetition of the SAME block T beta. It does not
cover arbitrary products with different intervening braids. Nonzero
annular degree also does not imply a nontrivial ordinary Jones polynomial
after filling the annulus; some ordinary closures are unknots.

2. Satellite composition has the opposite effect to the intended one
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Write a standard Ren pattern as [P]=b_P+a_P z^2, with a_P nonzero.
For another such pattern Q=K(p,q), with odd top-twist parameters p,q,
the exact two-parallel cable coefficient is

 [z^8][Q^(2)] = A^(4(p+q)) h(A),
 h(A)=-A^-24(A^4-1)^4(A^4+1)^2
                    *(1+A^4+A^8+A^12+A^16+A^20+A^24),

which is nonzero. Satellite insertion is linear on skeins and sends
z^2 to the actual two-parallel cable, so

 [P(Q)]=b_P+a_P[Q^(2)].

Its z^8 coefficient is a_P A^(4(p+q))h(A), hence its degree is eight.
Replacing [Q^(2)] by [Q]^2 would be an invalid substitution. Framing
adjustments multiply the relevant highest coefficient by a unit and do
not remove it. The cable calculation is retained from the earlier exact
certificate; Ren already reports the degree-eight phenomenon for the
original example. No novelty claim is made.

3. Why coordinated twists remain outside the single-disk obstruction
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The actual braid closure

 K_(m,n)=closure(sigma_1^(2m) sigma_1 sigma_1^(2n))

has two disjoint twist disks, each of linking number two. Along n=-m it
is literally the one-crossing unknot. Set D=A^4+1+A^-4. Its normalized
Jones expression is

 V_(m,n)=(A^-6/delta) A^(-12(m+n))
            -(A^-2 D/delta) A^(-4(m+n)).

Both channels are nonzero; their coefficients at A=1 are -1/2 and 3/2.
Along the opposite-twist line both frequencies become zero and the sum
is one. This is geometric cancellation, not a counterexample.

More substantially, in the generic tensor Chebyshev coordinates for
the exterior of the two unlink axes, this actual zero-framed knot is

 A^-6(e_0 tensor e_0) - A^-2/D (e_2 tensor e_2).

The denominator occurs before Jones normalization. It follows from the
connected-sum skein relation D e_2(y)=e_2(x_1)e_2(x_2) over Q(A), where
y is the connected sum of the two core circles. Thus the integral
single-annulus divisibility argument cannot be multiplied across disks.
One must retain the actual integral module and group coincident twist
frequencies before drawing conclusions.

4. An explicit torsion relation, and the missing knot construction
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The connected-sum calculation also supplies

 (A^2-A^-2) J_1=0,
 J_1=[x_1 disjoint-union x_2]-[y disjoint-union U].

This is a difference of two actual TWO-component links in the axis
exterior. Every filling into S^3 kills this torsion difference because
the S^3 skein module is torsion-free. In the standard reciprocal-integer
fillings here, both underlying links are unlinks. Their individual
component framings can differ, but their total framing and brackets agree.

An actual knot-minus-unknot torsion relation, with appropriate framing
control and an independently nontrivial filling, would complete a
counterexample construction. The displayed relation does not provide
one: its standard bandings give the same knot, and arbitrary banding is
not an operation on an unmarked closed skein class. A usable relation
must first be established in a relative skein module retaining the band
attachment points. That relative geometric construction is still missing.

A separate source check found a 2026 equal-Jones coupled-twist family
with partial knot 4_1. Its determinant is always 25, so it cannot contain
an unknot. Annulus-twist nontriviality theorems supply no Jones identity
for the same marked construction. No actual candidate emerged.
