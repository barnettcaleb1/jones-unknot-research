JONES UNKNOT DETECTION: CREATIVE RESEARCH PASS
31 October 2026

OUTCOME
No proof or counterexample to ordinary Jones unknot detection was found.
The strongest verified deduction connects Khovanov persistence, instanton
gauge theory, and the topology of a Seifert-surface complement. It excludes
some abstract Jones-one models from being actual knots and gives a precise
geometric target. No claim of novelty is made for these combined consequences.

CONCRETE RESTRICTED CONSEQUENCES
Normalize the unknot Jones polynomial to1. Let r be total reduced rational
Khovanov dimension and R total unreduced rational Khovanov dimension.

Using the mapping-cone theorem in Bhat--Li--Ye's December2025 preprint:
  Any nontrivial knot with V=1 has R>=8.
  If r=5, then R=10, Delta=1, and genus g>=2.
  In this r=5 case, KHI is C^2 at Alexander degree g, C at0,
  and C^2 at -g, with no other support.
  Its Seifert-surface complement has NONISOLATING guts a solid torus with
  four longitudinal sutures (Li--Ye model M1). The isolating exclusion is
  the additional diagram-level deduction explained below.
  Every proper rational tangle replacement K->J has reduced rank r(J)>=5.

The last condition includes crossing changes. Thus a rank-five candidate
cannot be one proper rational tangle replacement away from an unknot or
trefoil. It would also contradict the separate proposed nonvanishing of
first instanton differentials for every nontrivial knot.

Proof sketch:
Known rank-one and rank-three detection imply r>=5 and r is odd. If r=5,
Baldwin--Sivek give KHI rank5. Determinant1 excludes their fibered alternatives,
leaving the sparse2/1/2 pattern and Delta1. Li--Ye identify M1 and exclude
genus1. Bhat--Li--Ye's first differentials change Alexander degree by +/-1,
so they vanish on the support {-g,0,g}, g>=2. Their mapping cone has dimension10.
The unreduced instanton spectral sequence gives R>=10, while the standard
reduced/unreduced triangle gives R<=2r=10.

For generalized Lee homology, write b for the number of torsion blocks and
b1 for the number with exponent1. Specialization gives
  r=1+2b, R=2+4b-2b1.
Thus r5,R10 implies two blocks, both length>=2. Gujral--Wang's module lemma
then gives the tangle-replacement inequality. If r>=7 instead, R>=r+1>=8,
proving the general lower bound. If R=8, necessarily r=7 and all3 blocks
have length1. The Jones Euler identity then forces Rasmussen s in
{-6,-2,2,6}. Hence a nontrivial Jones-one knot with s=0 would have R>=10.

This is a checked application of stated theorems, not a fresh verification
of all analytical constructions in the cited instanton preprint. The full
source/convention audit is included in the evidence archive.

WHAT THE CREATIVE CONNECTION ADDS
Khovanov homology is more than the signed Euler characteristic recorded by
Jones. Persistence describes how its extra generators cancel; instanton
theory constrains whether such cancellation data can arise from a knot.
An explicit integral algebraic model with Jones1, reduced rank5, unreduced
rank6 and Lee rank2 passes elementary algebraic tests but fails the geometric
rank10 requirement above.

However, there are stronger ABSTRACT models with reduced rank5 and unreduced
rank10 that survive Lee theory, the Dowlin relative grading constraint, and
basic bifiltered knot-Floer symmetry. Their exact all-genus certificates are
included. They are NOT claimed to be knot complexes.

The next useful target is geometric: rule out the sparse2/1/2 pattern for
knots with NONISOLATING M1 guts. A nonzero next-to-top Floer group would do
this in the rank-five case. This would only exclude the smallest remaining
reduced rank; the general Jones problem still allows ranks7,9,... .

The geometry is nonempty. Baker--Manjarrez-Gutierrez construct the genus-two
knot16n332130 with isolating M1 guts. Its displayed Jones polynomial is
 -q^-16+q^-14+q^-8-q^-6+q^-4-q^-2+1-q^4+q^6,
and its displayed HFK Alexander ranks are2,4,5,4,2. Thus it provides a real
test diagram with substantial next-to-top homology, not a counterexample.
Their Theorem1.1 also gives handle number2 for all M1 knots.

A rejected shortcut is worth preserving: the two SFH generators of the
four-longitude solid torus lie in different Spin^c structures. One cannot
assign them an intrinsic relative Maslov gap1 and assume that gap survives
arbitrary gluing. The embedding/gluing data contain essential information.

REPAIRED ARGUMENT: EXCLUDING THE ISOLATING BRANCH
This additional deduction uses the published Floer and sutured-manifold
results, without the Bhat--Li--Ye preprint input needed for R=10.

In the rank-five case, Li--Ye and Dowlin force total HFK rank5. Its Alexander
support is2/1/2 at g,0,-g. The spectral sequence to HFhat(S3) must cancel four
generators in pairs. Using Maslov drop1, strictly decreasing Alexander
degree, and HFK symmetry, the two top Maslov degrees differ by2g-1.
The complete elementary pairing argument is in homology_rank5_audit.txt.

For isolating M1, Baker--Manjarrez-Gutierrez give two product surfaces:
F_a of genus a>=1 with one boundary and F_b of genus b>=0 with four
boundaries, where a+b=g-1. Attach F_a x I to the guts first. The result N_a
is F_a x I with three parallel boundary sutures. Now its two SFH classes
belong to the SAME Spin^c structure.

Juhasz Example7.5/Figure3 provides the diagram for a=1. Replacing its central
handle by a handles leaves two intersection points and a connecting bigon
domain with a handles. As in that source, stabilize the diagram away from
this domain to realize its homology class by a topological Whitney disk.
The added intersection has multiplicity zero, so the index is unchanged.
The Lipshitz index formula gives
  mu(D) = e(D)+n_x(D)+n_y(D)
        = (1-2a-1/2)+1/4+1/4 = 1-2a.
There are no periodic domains and no positive index-one disk contributing
to a differential. Hence the two homology generators have Maslov gap2a-1.
The other product surface is built from three incoming annular collars by
2b+2 surface1handles. Thickening yields product1handles; their cocore disk
decompositions preserve the same-Spin^c relative grading by Juhasz Prop5.4.
The final top HFK gap is therefore2a-1<=2g-3, contradicting2g-1.

The local diagram, index calculation, product-handle reconstruction and
grading-preservation hypotheses were independently checked in this pass.
This is a research deduction with an explicit proof, not a claim of novelty
or external peer review. The nonisolating case has no such isolated bounding
surface; its two guts classes remain in different Spin^c structures until
the later knot-complement gluing. This argument does not apply there.

ARITHMETIC AND COLORED JONES: A FORMAL OBSTRUCTION
The idea was to use integrality, recurrences, or analytic continuation to
recover higher colors from ordinary Jones1. An explicit formal sequence
shows that these properties alone do not suffice.

Let P(N,k;q)=product(j=1..k)(q^N+q^-N-q^j-q^-j), and let a_k be the integer
coefficients of1/(1+4s^2+s^3). Define
  F_N(q)=sum(k=0..N-1) a_k P(N,k;q).
Then F_1=F_2=1 but F_3=1-4P(3,2) is nontrivial. The sequence has integer
cyclotomic coefficients and is q-holonomic. Its formal leading loop term is
1/Delta(t), with
  Delta(t)=1+4(t+t^-1-2)^2+(t+t^-1-2)^3.
This NONCONSTANT polynomial satisfies Delta(1)=Delta(-1)=1 and Delta''(1)=0.
Even its higher loop coefficients obey the usual rationality denominator
bounds. These statements have direct algebraic proofs and exact certificates.

This is not a knot counterexample. Full knot-specific weight-system and
Frobenius compatibility identities are not established for this sequence.
It identifies the missing ingredient: geometric realizability, rather than
formal integrality or the mere existence of a recurrence.

QUANTUM INFORMATION AND STATISTICAL MECHANICS: EXACT TESTS
Two actual knots,5_1 and10_132, have the same full ordinary Jones polynomial
but different dimension-three colored Jones values. Independent exact
Temperley--Lieb and R-matrix computations, with framing/Markov checks,
are included. Thus ordinary Jones does not universally reconstruct cable
data. Special rigidity at V=1 remains possible.

Likewise the unknot value is not generally an extremal quantum overlap:
different plat presentations of the actual unknot can produce different
normalized states. Cauchy--Schwarz saturation cannot simply be assumed.

For a signed Tait graph, the knot determinant equals
  tau(G)*abs(det(I-2K_N)),
where K_N is the negative-edge transfer-current matrix of a uniform
spanning tree. This connects knot theory to electrical networks and
determinantal probability. Conway and Kinoshita--Terasaka diagrams each
have155 spanning trees: cancellation78-77 gives determinant1.
Their Jones first derivative at -1 is48. But T(3,5) has determinant1 and
first derivative0 while its second derivative is-48, so one response is
insufficient. Exact interaction expansions using signed Green-function
minors reproduce these moments.

Two appealing shortcuts fail on explicit diagrams: the full Jones activity
weights need not factor over edges, and the positive activity deformation
need not satisfy Rayleigh inequalities. Even an actual unknot can fail
these properties. A successful moment argument needs a new cancellation
constraint that respects knot topology.
