JONES RESEARCH: AN EXACT KNOT FAMILY AND THE ZERO-FACTOR TARGET
31 October 2026. Parallel research continuation.

STATUS

The general Jones unknot-detection problem is not solved here. This pass
obtains an exact factorization for an infinite family of actual nontrivial
knots, identifies the precise scope of the anchored-tangle approach, and
gives a geometric criterion that would certify a counterexample immediately.
These are internally checked deductions; priority in the literature is not
claimed. The full-polynomial condition remains essential.

1. ACTUAL KNOTS, WITH AN EXACT FORMULA FOR THE DIFFERENCE FROM ONE

Start with a trefoil J_0 and its strong inversion. Form J_r by r untwisted
Whitehead operations, carrying the compatible inherited inversion along.
Take the quotient of E(J_r), with meridian and preferred longitude marked,
and fill along either half-integral slope +1/2 or -1/2. Call the resulting
branch knot B_r. Both choices, and the standard inherited clasp choices,
satisfy the following statement.

For an integer k_r and a sign epsilon_r depending on those choices,

 V_(B_r)(t)-1 = epsilon_r t^(k_r)
                 (t-1)^(r+2) (t^2+t+1)
                 (t^2+1)^(r+1) (t^3+1)^(r+1).               (1)

The monomial ambiguity is in the DIFFERENCE V-1, not in a comparison of
unnormalized Jones polynomials. It cannot make the displayed difference zero.

Consequences:

 * Every B_r is an actual nontrivial knot of unknotting number exactly one.
 * ord_(t=1)(V_(B_r)-1)=r+2, and ord_(t=-1)(V_(B_r)-1)=r+1.
 * The absolute first nonzero Taylor coefficient at t=1 is 12*4^r.
 * The knots are pairwise distinct, already by their Jones polynomials.

Here 'Taylor coefficient' means derivative divided by factorial. Thus the
absolute derivative of order r+2 is (r+2)!*12*4^r. The derivatives through
order r+1 at t=1 coincide with those of the unknot. This asserts vanishing
for the finite-type coefficients extracted from ordinary Jones; it does
not assert vanishing of every finite-type invariant in those degrees.

The existence of nontrivial knots with arbitrarily deep finite-type
vanishing is classical. The result established here is the particular
geometric family and its complete factorization, including exact orders
at both 1 and -1. Its originality has not been established.

2. PROOF OF THE FACTORIZATION

Use delta=-A^2-A^-2 and t=A^-4. For a preferred exterior quotient T, put

 x(T) = (f_0(T)/f_infinity(T)-delta)/(1-delta^2).

The previous report proves the exact shell identity x(P(T))=d_P(A)x(T).
The meridional closure is an actual unknot, so its bracket is a framing
unit and the ratio is defined.

Here the Whitehead factor can be computed completely, up to a Laurent
unit and mirroring, from one rational input that yields a trefoil.
An actual trefoil-exterior quotient is obtained as follows. Set

 R=<-1>+<1/2>+<1/3>,     T=rotation(R)+<-6>.

The numerator of R is an actual unknot. The branched cover of R is
Seifert fibered over D^2(2,3), identifying the exterior as a trefoil
exterior up to chirality. Rotation makes its denominator the meridian.
The minus-six adjustment makes the numerator determinant zero, uniquely
selecting the preferred longitude. Direct two-dimensional skein algebra
gives

 f_infinity(T)=A^-18,
 f_0(T)/f_infinity(T)=-A^2+A^6-A^22-A^30,
 x(T)=A^2(A^4-1)(1+A^8)(1+A^12).                             (2)

For example, subtracting delta in the middle line gives
 A^-2(1+A^8)(1-A^24), which verifies the last line directly.

The appropriate signed unit-twist input to the Whitehead shell produces
a trefoil. That input has x=-A^2 or -A^-2. A trefoil has a unique strong
inversion up to equivalence. Its meridian and preferred longitude remove
the Dehn-twist marking ambiguity; the remaining Conway half-turns act
trivially on the rational skein basis. Consequently

 d_Wh(A) = a Laurent unit times
                 (1-A^4)(1+A^8)(1+A^12).                     (3)

Mirroring this displayed factor multiplies it by -A^-24. Thus it has the
same factorization up to a unit for the mirrored choices as well. This
also checks the previous first-jet result: the absolute derivative at
exp(pi i/4) is 48. The absolute derivative at A=1 is 16.

The exact normalized half-integral closure formula is, for one choice,

 V_B(A^-4)-1=A^-10(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1)x(T).                    (4)

For the opposite choice the prefactor is
 -A^-2(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1).
To verify (4), the two-crossing closure functional is
 C_2=A^-2 f_infinity+(1-A^4)f_0, with C_2(Q_0)=A^6.
The two added crossings contribute the same writhe increment for T and
Q_0: the cap has the meridional endpoint pairing, so the orientations
on the outside tangle arcs agree. Normalizing by A^-6 gives (4), with
no unknown additive monomial.

Multiply (2) by r factors (3), then by (4). Changing from A to t yields
(1). The remaining unit has integer t exponent because both sides are
ordinary knot Jones polynomials. All displayed factors are nonzero.
The half-integral closure changes to the meridional unknot by one
crossing change, so u(B_r)<=1. Nonzero V_(B_r)-1 gives u(B_r)=1.

The base calculation was also checked independently on actual diagrams
with Spherogram and Regina. In Regina's chirality convention, the two
base diagrams give V-1=t^-2 F_0(t) and V-1=-F_0(t), where F_0 is the
product in (1) for r=0. The evidence archive contains PD data and a
standalone exact-arithmetic certificate. The all-r claim follows by
the shell identity, not by testing finitely many r.

3. THE ANCHORED TANGLE TARGET HAS AN EXACT, RESTRICTED SCOPE

The following existence statements are equivalent:

 (a) A nontrivial unknotting-number-one knot has Jones polynomial one.
 (b) A nonrational actual four-ended tangle has an actual unknot
     meridional closure and its entire skein vector is proportional
     to the preferred rational-zero tangle.

For (a)->(b), remove an unknotting crossing. The two crossing fillings
have Jones polynomial one; the oriented skein relation makes the
oriented smoothing's polynomial exactly that of the two-component
unlink. The unknot filling identifies the cover as a knot exterior.
The determinant-zero smoothing is its longitude. The common-writhe,
two-closure argument then determines its entire vector as rational-zero.
A rational outside tangle would make the original knot a determinant-one
two-bridge knot, hence an unknot, which is impossible.

For (b)->(a), use the half-integral closure (4). Its Jones polynomial is
one. Its cover is 1/2 surgery on the nontrivial knot covering the tangle,
so is not S^3 by the no-nonmeridional-S^3-surgery theorem. Hence the
branch knot is nontrivial; changing the cap crossing unknots it.

This does NOT reduce the full Jones conjecture to unknotting number one.
Sikora's general tangle equivalence ties a hypothetical Jones-one knot
locally into a rational tangle; its meridional closure still contains
that knot. An actual unknot anchor is a stronger requirement.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08162 (Section 9)
https://people.math.harvard.edu/~kronheim/wittenp.pdf (Introduction)

4. AN ESSENTIAL ZERO FACTOR WOULD CERTIFY A COUNTEREXAMPLE

Let P union C be a nonsplit link of two unknots, with linking number zero
and a componentwise strong inversion. Its normalized quotient shell
has multiplier d_P. If d_P is identically zero, insert the unit-twist
rational input. The output is the flat preferred quotient of the knot
P_1 obtained by 1-surgery on the axis C.

P_1 is provably nontrivial. Regard C as a knot in the solid torus E(P).
It has winding zero and is not contained in a ball. Gabai's Corollary2.5
says that no nontrivial surgery on such a knot produces a solid torus.
Thus the exterior E(P_1) is not a solid torus. In fact the same argument
works for every nonzero integral twist input.

The half-integral branch closure of the output consequently gives a
nontrivial Jones-one knot of unknotting number one, by Section3. Once
an actual essential zero shell is found, no additional search for a
nontrivial closure is necessary.

Conversely a split pattern link has d_P=0: its zero and unit-twist inputs
both produce preferred unknot exterior quotients. Those inputs span
the rank-two skein module, so the shell has rank one.

The unresolved implication in this class is therefore

                  d_P=0  ==>  P union C is split.

This implication has not been established here. Ordinary Conway
mutation of a split quotient shell cannot produce the needed nonsplit
example: its torus lift preserves the covering exterior and its boundary
compressibility. Serial composition of nonzero shell factors also cannot
produce zero, since their Laurent-polynomial factors multiply.

Gabai, Foliations and the topology of 3-manifolds II, Corollary2.5:
https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214441487

5. TWO TEMPTING PROOF ROUTES THAT THIS PASS RULED OUT

First, all derived classical linking invariants are insufficient for
recovering d. Twice Whitehead-doubling one component of the Hopf link
gives a boundary link. Its eta functions, Cochran derived invariants
and Milnor invariants vanish, while its multiplier is d_Wh squared,
which is nonzero. Thus the full eta sequence, not merely its first
coefficient, misses this Jones information.
Meilhan--Yasuhara, Theorem4.1 and Corollary4.2:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0704.3093

Second, increasing a fixed perturbative order does not settle exact
vanishing. For each N, sufficiently many Whitehead layers make every
rational-input covering knot's nonzero rational surgeries agree with
the corresponding lens spaces in LMO degrees through N, while the
shell factor remains nonzero. This uses the classical finite-type
vanishing of Whitehead iterates and the degree bound in the rational
surgery formula, not any assumption that ordinary Jones determines LMO.
The detailed quantifiers and degree audit are in homology.txt in the
evidence archive. There is no claim that one fixed finite knot fools
all orders simultaneously.
Birman, New points of view in knot theory, p.283, Remark(ii), attributing
the Whitehead-iterate construction to Lin:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9304209
Ito, On LMO invariant constraints for cosmetic surgery and other surgery
problems for knots in S^3, Proposition3.1:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.01755v2

These results favor an exact geometric argument about the whole factor,
or an actual construction making it vanish. No such construction and no
proof of the general unknot-detection statement were obtained in this pass.

6. AN ATTRACTIVE CONVERSE THAT FAILED ITS GEOMETRIC CHECK

We also tried to turn ANY anchored flat quotient into an essential zero
shell. The proposed construction was to Whitehead-double its covering
knot, then undo the clasp. Algebraically this would work: the zero and
unit-twist rational inputs would both give flat quotients, forcing d=0.

The required symmetry is missing. In both standard inherited inversions,
the Whitehead clasp crossing circle is acted on FREELY. The two fixed
points of the Whitehead knot are at the opposite cap. Thus the clasp
circle does not become a four-ended boundary sphere in the quotient,
as the shell construction requires. A perpendicular local axis can fix
the circle, but has not been extended over the doubled companion with
the required zero linking number and inherited involution.

This was checked against the actual primary Figure4 in
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05819 . The proposed converse remains
conditional, not a theorem. In particular the zero-shell target has not
been proved equivalent even to the whole anchored-tangle target.
