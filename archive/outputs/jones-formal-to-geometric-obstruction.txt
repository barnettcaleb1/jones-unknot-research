JONES UNKNOT DETECTION: WHY THE FORMAL-TO-GEOMETRIC SHORTCUT FAILS
1 November 2026. Research audit; not a proof of unknot detection.

Result and decision
-------------------
No proof or nontrivial Jones-one knot was obtained. This pass closes three
specific shortcuts: realizing a surgery twist by a finite annular link,
realizing a Jones--Wenzl projector by an ordinary tangle, and turning an
additive matrix identity automatically into a braid relation. These are
obstructions to proposed methods, not evidence that the main theorem is
nearly proved. No novelty or external-review claim is made.

The most useful result is small and exact: a finite annular gadget cannot
reproduce a twist even on two elementary test channels. Its failure is
visible in the first derivative, before any large diagram computation.

1. A two-channel obstruction
---------------------------
Use the Kauffman bracket with empty link equal to one. Write the annular
skein module as Z[A,A^-1][z]. The meridian and positive-twist eigenvalues
on channel i are

    lambda_i = -A^(2i+2) - A^(-2i-2),
    theta_i  = (-1)^i A^(i(i+2)).

In particular, inserting a finite annular skein F(A,z) acts in channel i
by F(A,lambda_i). These conventions are Masbaum's equations (2)--(4),
printed page 539, in the primary source below.

PROPOSITION. Let F(A,z) be a finite polynomial in z whose coefficients
are rational functions regular at A=1. Let g(A) be regular at A=1 with
g(1) nonzero. There is no such F satisfying both

    F(A,lambda_0(A)) = g(A),
    F(A,lambda_2(A)) = g(A) A^(8r)

for a nonzero integer r.

Proof. Every channel satisfies lambda_i(1)=-2 and lambda_i'(1)=0.
Consequently the chain rule gives

    d/dA F(A,lambda_i(A)) at A=1 = F_A(1,-2),

independently of i. The proposed right sides have derivatives g'(1)
and g'(1)+8r g(1), respectively. They differ. Contradiction. End proof.

The proposition permits A-dependent coefficients and any common regular
normalization nonzero at A=1. For an actual framed annular link, taking
g=F(A,lambda_0) is its ordinary closure bracket. Its value at A=1 is a
nonzero signed power of two, so this natural normalization cannot help.
The normalized responses all have first derivative zero, whereas the
required channel-2 twist response has derivative 8r.

An equivalent exact factorization is

    lambda_2-lambda_0 = -A^-6 (A^4-1)^2 (A^4+1),
    theta_2-theta_0   = (A^4-1)(A^4+1).

The difference of two polynomial substitutions is divisible by the first
expression. Its double zero at A=1 cannot produce the second expression's
simple zero. The degree-one formal interpolant instead requires

    (theta_2-theta_0)/(lambda_2-lambda_0) = -A^6/(A^4-1).

This is exactly the first nonconstant coefficient in Habiro's completed
twist expansion in Masbaum's equation (7), printed page 540. The pole
records the obstruction. Clearing its denominator changes the vacuum
normalization to a scalar vanishing at A=1, which is not the closure
bracket of an actual finite uncolored link.

Scope. The result excludes a fixed closed annular insertion implementing
the twist in arbitrary external contexts. It does not exclude actual
Dehn surgery, drawing an open full-twist braid on a chosen set of strands,
singular formal skeins, root-of-unity quotients, or an identity needed
only in one particular exterior. Testing channels 0 and 2 assumes no
deduction from ordinary to colored Jones polynomials: a universal
operation on ordinary parallel strands must also act on their linear
combinations.

2. Rational interpolation still requires increasing size
--------------------------------------------------------
Even allowing poles, a polynomial F in Q(A)[z] that implements a positive
or negative twist on channels 0,...,N must have z-degree at least N.

The Nth divided difference is

    sum over i=0,...,N of
       theta_i^(+/-1) / product over j!=i of (lambda_i-lambda_j).

The denominator's degree at A=infinity in term i is
    i^2-i+N^2+3N.
For positive twist, the term degrees are 3i-N^2-3N, uniquely largest at
i=N. For negative twist, they are -2i^2-i-N^2-3N, uniquely largest at i=0.
Thus the divided difference is nonzero, proving the degree bound. The
same argument works for the even channels 0,2,...,2N. Hence no finite
rational annular polynomial implements the twist on every even channel.
This is consistent with the completed, rather than finite, construction
in the literature.

3. Two supporting checks on formal algebra
------------------------------------------
A Jones--Wenzl projector cannot be a single ordinary tangle, even after
multiplication by a nonzero rational scalar. For width at least two it
annihilates an adjacent cup-cap diagram. If an actual tangle represented
it, composing and closing would yield an actual link with identically
zero bracket. That is impossible because an actual link has nonzero
bracket at A=1. Clearing denominators does not change the contradiction.
This does not exclude identities involving a difference of actual tangles.

An additive matrix identity is also not a braid relation. The entire
generic three-strand Temperley--Lieb algebra satisfies the standard
degree-four alternating matrix identity. Nevertheless, the actual braid
images of sigma_1^3 and sigma_2^3 freely generate a group. Specialization
at A=exp(pi*i/4), after accounting for the scalar character, gives the
cubes of the two elementary parabolic matrices; their actions satisfy
the elementary ping-pong criterion. Thus even within the actual small
braid algebra, an additive identity coexists with freely generated gates.

4. What would constitute a complete route
-----------------------------------------
The proposed counterexample route was: realize a formal quantum identity
by one finite geometric gadget; insert it into an unknot; prove the result
has exactly the same whole Jones polynomial; certify that the resulting
single-component knot in S^3 is nontrivial. The universal realization step
fails here. Later steps do not deserve further work for that gadget.

A construction tailored to one exterior remains logically possible, but
none was produced. It must provide an actual diagram and an exact full
polynomial identity, not a signed sum, a finite Taylor approximation, or
a formal object whose realizability is assumed.

The parallel homological audit reached the same strategic boundary:
known component classifications of Khovanov curves do not classify which
graded collections arise from classical tangles. Existing formal
cancelling collections are not actual knot counterexamples. Assuming
that every Jones-one closure has rank-one homology merely assumes an
equivalent form of the original conjecture.

The research correction is to distinguish an explicit missing geometric
theorem from a proof scaffold that has earned further investment. These
tests close proposed shortcuts; they do not supply that missing theorem.
