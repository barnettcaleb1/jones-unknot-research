STATUS

No proof of ordinary Jones unknot detection and no nontrivial Jones-one knot
has been obtained. This report records a deduction from established theorems,
internally checked during this pass. It makes no claim of publication novelty.

The methodological error was allowing successful local calculations to stand
in for a complete route to the conjecture. In particular, our construction
for the unknotting-number-one case has no established reduction from an
arbitrary hypothetical counterexample. Another calculation in this pass
reproved a weaker version of our earlier satellite-rigidity result. That
calculation is recorded as duplication, not new progress.

The main useful outcome below closes an entire proposed construction. It
does not supply the missing general proof.

1. A FRAMED TWO-CLOSURE UNIQUENESS RESULT

Use two-string tangles with the same marked boundary. Write C_P(X) for the
link obtained by closing X with the rational probe P. Require the two probes
P and Q to have distinct slopes, not merely different framings.

Claim. Let R be rational. Suppose

               C_P(X) = C_P(R),     C_Q(X) = C_Q(R)

as balanced framed links, in Sikora's convention. If either shared underlying
link is the unknot, then X is rational. In fact X and R are the same balanced
framed tangle.

Proof.

(i) Two distinct rational probes determine the full Kauffman bracket vector
of a tangle. This is Sikora's Theorem 21: the two closure functionals form
an invertible matrix over the rational-function field. Consequently [X]=[R].
This uses the full framed equalities, not just equality of normalized Jones
polynomials for the two closures.

(ii) Assume the P-closure is the unknot. Let M_X and M_R be the oriented
double branched covers of the tangle balls. Filling along the slope alpha
specified by P gives S^3 in both cases. Thus M_X is the exterior of a knot J
in S^3, with meridian alpha. Since R is rational, M_R is a solid torus and
is the exterior of the unknot. The common boundary marking identifies these
meridian slopes.

(iii) Choose an auxiliary rational probe H whose closure with R is the
two-component unlink. Such a probe exists for every rational tangle. Its
filling slope lambda is the preferred longitude of M_R, since the filling
is S^1 x S^2. The equality [X]=[R] implies equal brackets after the H-closure,
and hence equal determinants, both zero. Therefore H_1(M_X(lambda); Z) is
infinite. For a knot exterior in S^3, the only slope with this property is
the preferred longitude: p/q filling has first homology Z/pZ when p is
nonzero, and infinite first homology when p=0. Hence the common marking also
identifies the preferred longitudes.

The auxiliary probe need not be distinct from Q; they coincide if Q already
gives the unlink. This does not affect the argument.

The determinant step requires a normalization detail. If the unreduced
bracket convention assigns delta=-A^2-A^-2 to a circle, first divide the
closed bracket by delta symbolically. Only then specialize to the determinant
root. Direct specialization of the unreduced bracket would vanish for every
nonempty link and give no information. Writhe corrections have modulus one
at this root and do not affect whether the determinant vanishes.

(iv) The Q-probe now represents the same rational surgery coefficient r in
the two meridian/longitude systems. It is nonmeridional because Q and P have
distinct slopes. The actual equality of Q-closures gives an orientation-
preserving diffeomorphism

                        S^3_r(J) = S^3_r(U).

Theorem 1.1 of Kronheimer--Mrowka--Ozsvath--Szabo, Monopoles and lens space
surgeries, implies J=U for every rational r, including r=0. Thus M_X is a
solid torus. The branched-cover characterization of rational tangles implies
X is rational. Finally, Sikora's Corollary 20 says that the full bracket
distinguishes balanced framed rational tangles, so [X]=[R] gives X=R.

Orientation matters here. The covers inherit the orientations of the marked
tangle balls, and the closed-link equivalences are orientation-preserving
ambient isotopies. The common meridian and longitude slopes, together with
the boundary orientation, give the same signed surgery coefficient. Merely
matching unoriented lens-space homeomorphism types would not justify (iv).

2. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE DNA CONSTRUCTION

An unframed two-probe ambiguity between a rational and a nonrational tangle,
with an unknot among the shared products, cannot have equal total framing
defects at the two probes. If it did, one common framing adjustment on the
unknown tangle would repair both equations, contradicting the claim above.

This explains why repairing the earlier four-versus-zero framing discrepancy
was not merely a difficult parameter choice. The entire target, with an
unknot as one shared product, is excluded under these hypotheses.

The result does NOT exclude a Jones-one knot with an unknotting move. In that
situation the second closure may have only the POLYNOMIAL of a specified
link; step (iv) requires its actual geometric link type. Nor does it settle
two-probe systems with two nontrivial rational-link products: their covers
need not be knot exteriors in S^3, so the cited surgery theorem is unavailable.

3. THE SATELLITE DUPLICATION AND ITS LIMITED NEW CONSEQUENCE

Our earlier jones-satellite-rigidity.txt already proves that if a fixed,
zero-framed satellite pattern P satisfies

                       V_{P(K)} = F(V_K)

for every companion K, with one rational function F, then F is constant
at even winding and is c V_K at odd winding. This pass independently derived
only the polynomial special case. That algebraic result is not new.

One additional combination is useful. Assume P(U)=U, P is geometrically
essential, its outputs on nontrivial companions are genuine satellites, and
the universal rational law is defined at 1. If even one such output has
unknotting number one, Scharlemann--Thompson's satellite obstruction forces
the winding of P to be zero. Our earlier rigidity result then forces F=1.
Consequently V_{P(J)}=1 for EVERY companion J. In particular, an ordinary
trefoil companion would already give a nontrivial counterexample.

Thus this proposed device would have to construct a counterexample outright;
it cannot obtain its success from a hypothetical Jones-one input. This does
not prove such a device impossible. It excludes its advertised role as an
input-dependent reduction within this fixed universal-law framework.

Companion-dependent operations, several independent infection sites, and
identities assumed only on the unknown Jones-one class remain outside this
argument. None of those alternatives was completed here.

4. WHAT THIS CHANGES

Stop searching for a framed rational/nonrational two-probe ambiguity with an
unknot product. Stop treating the universal fixed-satellite construction as
an established generic-to-unknotting-number-one reduction. Do not count an
equivalent reformulation, a formal skein element, or a further necessary
condition as a complete proof scaffold.

The independent arc audit supplies an additional candidate rejection test,
documented in the evidence archive. It constructs no nontrivial elementary-
skein arc. Its infinite-family application has explicit exceptional cases;
those cases are not evidence that a counterexample exists.

There is currently no complete proof scaffold with a demonstrably easier
missing lemma. Any further research must state an implication all the way
to the original conjecture, identify its unproved geometric step, and explain
why the proposed input can plausibly supply it. The new uniqueness deduction
is a reason to retire one route, not a basis for promising an imminent answer.
