WHY THE SURGERY ROUTE DOES NOT YET REACH JONES UNKNOT DETECTION
1 November 2026 — bounded audit, pass 51

Status: no proof of Jones unknot detection and no counterexample. The
previous two-actual-closure tangle argument survived another internal
audit, but remains without external validation or a novelty assessment.
This pass verifies a restricted extension and identifies why it does not
provide a generic proof. Further calculations within this route are not
justified without a new geometric input.

THE RESTRICTED EXTENSION

Let X be a framed two-string tangle whose full Kauffman bracket equals
that of a rational tangle R. If the double branched cover M of X is both
irreducible and Floer simple, then X is rational. Consequently X and R
agree as balanced framed tangles.

Here Floer simplicity can be characterized by the existence of at least
two distinct L-space Dehn fillings. Neither Floer simplicity nor
irreducibility is supplied by equality of brackets.

Argument. Choose rational caps which turn R into the unknot and the
two-component unlink. Their cover slopes mu and lambda intersect once.
Bracket equality supplies determinants one and zero for the corresponding
closures of X. Thus Y=M(mu) is an integral homology sphere, M is the
exterior of its filling-core knot C, and lambda is C's preferred longitude.
This does not identify Y with S3. Write Delta_C for the symmetric Seifert
Alexander polynomial, normalized by Delta_C(1)=1.

The longitude branch link L has the unlink Jones polynomial up to a signed
monomial. In particular J_L(-1)=0 and |J_L'(-1)|=1. Garoufalidis's formula
therefore gives |lambda_G(Y_0(C))|=1/6. In this doubled normalization the
zero-surgery formula, valid for an arbitrary integral homology sphere Y, is

                 lambda_G(Y_0(C))=Delta_C''(1)-1/6.

Hence |6 Delta_C''(1)-1|=1. Integrality excludes the alternative 1/3 and
forces Delta_C''(1)=0. There is no ambient Casson term in the zero-surgery
formula. Poudel equation (4.1) gives the same formula in Lescop's convention
as lambda_L=Delta_C''(1)/2-1/12. The normalization and its general-Y
extension were separately checked; details are retained in the audit notes.
[G, P, GH]

Floer simplicity gives a normalized torsion series D(t)/(1-t) with
coefficients zero or one. Thus the nonzero coefficients of D alternate
+1,-1,+1,...,+1. After symmetrizing,

 Delta_C(t)=(-1)^k + sum_(j=1)^k (-1)^(k-j)(t^n_j+t^-n_j),
                         0<n_1<...<n_k.

Therefore Delta_C''(1)=2 sum_j (-1)^(k-j)n_j^2 is positive unless k=0:
pair terms from the largest exponent downward. Its vanishing forces
Delta_C=1. If the boundary were incompressible, the Floer-simple norm
formula would give Thurston norm deg D-1=-1, impossible. The boundary
compresses; irreducibility then makes M a solid torus. The branched-cover
criterion makes X rational, and the bracket distinguishes framed rational
tangles. [RR, S]

Without irreducibility the same argument stops at

                    M=(S1 x D2)#Z,

where Z is an integral homology sphere. The connected-sum formula for
Heegaard Floer homology makes Z an L-space. It does not make Z equal S3.

THE GENERIC TEST THAT THE ROUTE FAILS

Tie an arbitrary knot J into one arc of R, with framing adjusted to the
Jones normalization. Call the resulting tangle X_J. Directly,

             [X_J]=V_J(A^-4)[R],
             Sigma_2(X_J)=(S1 x D2)#Sigma_2(J).

Thus a hypothetical nontrivial Jones-one knot produces exactly the bracket
equality we want, while its topology survives as a closed connected-sum
factor. For every nonlongitude slope p/q the filling is

                       L(p,q)#Sigma_2(J).

This tangle cover is Floer simple if and only if Sigma_2(J) is an L-space.
Even proving that implication from V_J=1 would leave the closed factor
uncontrolled. Assuming irreducibility instead excludes this construction;
it does not provide a replacement construction for arbitrary J.

Crucially, C and J are different knots. In this construction the auxiliary
core C is an unknot in a ball of Sigma_2(J), so Delta_C=1 already. The new
derivative constraint is therefore automatic, irrespective of the topology
of J. It supplies no extra information on the generic candidate.

A CONCRETE CHECK AGAINST AN OVERSTRONG CONCLUSION

Let P be the Poincare homology sphere and take Z=P#(-P). This is a
nontrivial integral homology sphere with minimal Heegaard Floer rank,
Casson invariant zero, and correction term d=0. Ozsvath and Szabo explicitly
discuss this example as an invisible homology sphere. [OS]

For M=(S1 x D2)#Z, every nonlongitude filling L(p,q)#Z is an L-space and
has the same Casson-Walker invariant and correction terms as L(p,q).
Nevertheless it is not a lens space. Thus these tests, even for all slopes,
cannot replace actual lens-space identifications in the earlier proof.

This is a counterexample to that proposed inference, not a Jones-one knot.
It is reducible and does not refute a statement with an irreducibility
hypothesis. No rational-bracket equality is claimed for this explicit model.

RESEARCH CONSEQUENCE

The previous proof used actual rational-link closures to remove Z. A generic
argument has only polynomial equalities, which do not supply that topology.
Sikora Theorem 24 explicitly identifies rational-tangle bracket detection
with the original Jones conjecture. Merely replacing the actual closures
by their polynomials would assume the missing theorem. [S]

The serious process error was continuing to refine the downstream surgery
argument without a mechanism supplying its upstream geometric hypotheses.
This pass closes that attempted extension. It does not justify another
parameter search, a claim that a solution is close, or a promise of a proof.
