GRAPH CANCELLATION: TWO PRECISE LIMITS ON A PROPOSED PROOF
1 November 2026

No proof or counterexample to Jones unknot detection was obtained. This
pass tested two possible general mechanisms on actual diagram models.
The results below reject those mechanisms in their stated generality;
they do not reject every graph-theoretic approach or a special theorem
using the full hypothesis V_K(t)=1.

1. Enough possible cancellations do not imply valid cancellation

The Roberts--Jaeger spanning-tree model gives an explicit differential
for reduced, characteristic-two Khovanov homology, with its grading
collapsed to delta and coefficients extended to a rational-function field.
For two connected resolutions differing at two crossings, the coefficient
is 1/w_1+1/w_2. Each w_i is the sum of independent arc weights on the
unmarked circle in the corresponding intermediate resolution.

We tested the actual standard ten-crossing positive braid closure
(sigma_1 sigma_2)^5, namely T(3,5). Exact reconstruction gives:

    Connected-resolution generators:                 121
    Counts by cube size 2,4,6,8,10:          25,50,35,10,1
    Nonzero differential-support edges:               500
    Maximum disjoint matching pairs:                   60
    Distinct generators used by the matching:         120

Thus there are enough nonzero, invertible differential entries to pair
every generator except one. Nevertheless, the homology has dimension
SEVEN, by Roberts's published computation and Jaeger's identification.
The actual total differential rank is consequently (121-7)/2=57, not60.
The rank57 conclusion uses those published results; it is not claimed
as a new rational-function matrix elimination.

Why the proposed cancellation fails: a valid algebraic Morse matching
needs acyclicity. Reverse each matched differential arrow in the saved
matching. One explicit directed cycle, with resolutions encoded as
ten-bit integers, is

    15 -> 159 -> 135 -> 207 -> 195 -> 231 -> 39 -> 63 -> 15.

Every60-pair matching must fail the acyclicity requirement: an acyclic
one would reduce the complex to a single generator, contradicting rank7.
The displayed cycle directly verifies the failure for the saved witness.

This falsifies the proposed implication from a nearly perfect support
matching and generic nonzero coefficients to one-dimensional homology.
It is an actual classical knot example, not a formal chain complex.
Its Jones polynomial is t^4+t^6-t^10, so it is NOT a counterexample to
Jones unknot detection. Its determinant is one, but its full Jones
polynomial retains information that the matching argument did not use.

Adding generic arc weights cannot by itself fix this: Jaeger proves that
for a knot these markings can all move to the basepoint. The added term
there is w*x, which vanishes in reduced homology. The deformation preserves
the homology; it is not an arbitrary generic matrix perturbation.

2. Electrical elimination loses a genuine boundary interaction

For a Potts/random-cluster star with three boundary vertices and arm
weights a,b,c, put lambda=Q+a+b+c. Its five boundary-connectivity
coefficients are

    (lambda, ab, bc, ac, abc).

A triangle matching the first four coordinates must have pair weights
ab/lambda, bc/lambda, ac/lambda, and overall factor lambda. The fifth
coordinate agrees precisely when

    abc = Q(Q+a+b+c),

assuming nonzero generic weights and lambda. Otherwise the remaining
three-terminal coefficient is

    kappa = abc * (Q*lambda-abc) / lambda^2.

On the Jones specialization, writing x=A^4,

    Q=x+2+x^(-1),  u=-1-x,  v=-1-x^(-1),

each crossing edge has weight u or v. The condition holds for the six
mixed-sign choices, giving the familiar Reidemeister III cases. For
three u-edges the obstruction is

    abc-Q*lambda = -(x+1)^3*(x-1)^2/x^2,

and for three v-edges it has x^3 in the denominator. Neither is identically
zero. Arbitrary electrical elimination therefore does not preserve the
full Jones boundary data while staying within pair interactions.

Keeping the extra interaction is a correct computation, but supplies no
simplification theorem. A single closed Jones value does not determine
all five boundary coefficients, so V=1 does not make each local kappa
vanish. This is the exact unproved step in that proposed argument.

Verification and conclusion

The positive braid, planar diagram, one-component condition, all121
states, all500 supports,60 globally disjoint pairs and explicit cycle
were reconstructed and independently checked. The star calculation was
verified in exact Laurent arithmetic for all eight crossing-sign choices.

The matching example makes the cancellation issue precise; the electrical
calculation identifies information discarded by a proposed simplification.
Neither supplies the missing implication from full ordinary Jones
polynomial one to a topological unknot criterion. These mechanisms are
closed in the stated forms; a larger enumeration is not justified by them.
