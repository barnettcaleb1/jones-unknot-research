JONES UNKNOT DETECTION: POSITIVITY, EXACT EXCEPTIONS, AND FINITE CERTIFICATES
31 October 2026. Continuation of the bounded research passes.

This pass extended the preceding proof to arbitrary-length all-odd pretzels,
completely classified a mixed four-strand subfamily, and obtained a finite
geometric certification tool for independent twist families. It also found
actual counterexamples to two tempting extensions of our proposed methods.
The general Jones unknot problem is not solved here. No novelty is claimed.

1. POSITIVITY FOR EVERY ALL-ODD PRETZEL KNOT

Let K=P(p_1,...,p_(2g+1)) with every p_i odd. Then

                    V_K''(1)=V_K'''(1)=0  implies K=U.

There is no bound on g or on the signed twist parameters. The transferable
idea is to find a sign-definite invariant after imposing the vanishing of
an earlier invariant.

Here is the full short argument. Set S=sum p_i, n=2g+1, and let e_j denote
the elementary symmetric polynomials in the parameters. In a consistent
Jones or mirror-Jones convention, put D=t d/dt. Exact differentiation of
the general pretzel bracket gives

 D^2V(1)=-(3/2)(e_2+g),
 D^3V(1)=-(9/4)[e_3+S(e_2+2g)].

The normalization V(1)=1,V'(1)=0 makes simultaneous ordinary second/third
vanishing equivalent to simultaneous logarithmic second/third vanishing.
Newton's identities then give

        sum p_i^2=S^2+n-1,          sum p_i^3=S^3.

Mirror if necessary so S>0; S is odd, so it cannot vanish. Since each of
the other n-1 parameters has square at least1, the square equation implies
|p_i|<=S for every i. Consequently every term in

        sum (S-p_i)(p_i^2-1)
          = S(sum p_i^2-n)-(sum p_i^3-S)=0

is nonnegative. Therefore each p_i belongs to{-1,1,S}. If S>1, the square
equation permits exactly one copy of S; the remaining parameters have
equal numbers of+1 and-1. If S=1, every parameter is+/-1 with one surplus+1.

The result is an actual unknot. Cyclically rotate the unique nonunit box to
the first position. All remaining rational tangles have slopes+1 or-1 and
sum to the integer tangle0. The closure reduces to N(1/S), an unknot.
If every box is a unit, their sum is the integer tangle1. No arbitrary
permutation of nonunit pretzel boxes is being mistaken for an isotopy.

In fact the stronger sign certificate, assuming only V''(1)=0, is

             D^3V(1)=(3/4) sum (S-p_i)(p_i^2-1) >=0.

Equality occurs exactly in the unknot configurations above.

Verification: the moments were derived independently by two routes and
checked against17,676 exact full Laurent-polynomial computations, with
additional independent evaluations of actual diagrams in Regina. These
checks audit the algebra; the positive-sum argument establishes the result
for all parameters.

Literature: Stipsicz--Szabo already derived the square/cube conditions for
five odd strands in Proposition6.6 of Purely cosmetic surgeries and pretzel
knots, and treated them by a sign/case argument. Their larger-strand surgery
argument uses genus. The positive-sum proof here is a compact derived
extension; its novelty has not been established. General pretzel detection
and low-order formulas also have prior literature.

2. A COMPLETE MIXED FOUR-STRAND CLASSIFICATION

For every four-strand pretzel K with at least one parameter+/-1,

 V_K''(1)=V_K'''(1)=0
   implies K is U, P(8,-3,-3,1), or its mirror.

Consequently vanishing of derivatives2,3,AND4 detects the unknot throughout
this entire class. This is an exhaustive symbolic classification, not a
bounded parameter search.

Proof. Such a pretzel is a knot exactly when one parameter is even. Mirror
to make the selected unit parameter+1, and move that unit box to the end.
Unit boxes commute with other boxes by isotopy. Cyclic rotation permits the
notation P(p,q,r,1), where p is even and q,r are odd. Write

 q=a+b, r=a-b, T=q+r+1, Q_j=q^j+r^j+1.

The independently checked mixed moments are

 D^2V=(3/4)(3-Q_2-2pT-p^2),
 D^3V=(3/4)(2Q_3-2T+p+3pT^2-p^3), provided D^2V=0.

The quadratic condition gives

 b^2=1-a^2-p^2/2-p(2a+1).

Substituting this into the cubic condition factors it completely:

          0=-(p+2a)^3+4(p+2a).

Thus c=p+2a is0,-2,or2. For c=0 or-2 the quadratic condition becomes
b^2=(a+1)^2. One of q,r is-1; cancel that box with+1. The remaining
two-box pretzel is T(2,c+1)=T(2,+/-1), an unknot.

For c=2, we get (a+1-b)(a+1+b)=4. The factors are integers of the same
parity, so they are(2,2) or(-2,-2). This leaves only
P(0,1,1,1), an unknot, and K*=P(8,-3,-3,1).

The exceptional knot has the exact Jones polynomial

 V_K*(t)=1+t^-8(t-1)^4 Q(t),
 Q(t)=t^8+2t^7+4t^6+5t^5+6t^4+5t^3+4t^2+2t+1.

Hence its second and third derivatives vanish, its fourth derivative is
24Q(1)=720, and its determinant is V_K*(-1)=33. The determinant proves
nontriviality independently of the conjecture being investigated. Mirroring
preserves the order-four leading Taylor coefficient. This excludes the
only nontrivial possibilities when the fourth derivative also vanishes.

The full polynomial was checked independently using Temperley--Lieb
arithmetic and Regina on an actual planar diagram. A further independent
exact polynomial calculation verifies the elimination identity and every
branch. The original15-crossing diagram simplifies to14crossings; no claim
of minimal crossing number is needed.

Scope: this does not cover four-strand pretzels without a unit parameter.
For example P(40,-15,-13,3) also has zero second/third derivatives but
determinant5025. Its diagram and full Jones polynomial were independently
checked and are included in the evidence.

3. A FINITE GEOMETRIC CERTIFICATE FOR TWIST FAMILIES

Fix the diagram outside one two-strand twist ball, and let K_n insert n
full twists, with initial parity fixed. If three distinct K_n are certified
unknots, EVERY K_n is an unknot.

Reason: in the double branched cover, the fixed outside tangle becomes a
manifold M with torus boundary; the twists give different Dehn fillings.
Two unknot fillings give two S^3 fillings. Gordon--Luecke's theorem forces
M to be a solid torus. The outside tangle is therefore rational, and each
K_n is two-bridge with determinant |an+b| for fixed integers a,b. Three
values equal to1 force a=0, so every closure is a two-bridge unknot.

The coherent family T(2,2n+1) has exactly two unknot members and also contains
trefoils, showing why two arbitrary samples do not suffice.

For d independent disjoint two-strand twist sites, certifying the3^d grid
members n_i in{0,1,2} as unknots certifies every integer tuple, by applying
the lemma one coordinate at a time. This can turn axis-aligned families
from the cancellation solver into complete geometric proofs. It requires
actual unknot recognition; three Jones-one evaluations are not substituted
for three unknot certificates. Coupled directions such as(n,-n) are not
covered, since one parameter then changes two physical sites.

Inputs: Gordon--Luecke's no-nonmeridional-S^3-surgery theorem, and the
solid-torus/rational-tangle equivalence. The latter is used explicitly in
the mathematical study of DNA recombination. This is a concrete biological
connection through a shared geometric method.

4. TWO BROADER PROOF ROUTES THAT WERE STRESS-TESTED

Ribbon geometry and cancellation

Our proposed one-band ribbon route asks whether extreme Lee-bar source
gradings must have unequal even/odd multiplicities. Official KnotInfo data
for12,965 prime knots through13crossings were checked. All511 smooth-slice
entries (known ribbon in these tables) have short bars certified by their
reduced/unreduced ranks and pure parity at both source extremes. A subset
of24 has fusion number one explicitly certified by source-backed criteria.
This is evidence, not a proof of the proposed geometric condition.

The broader claim for ribbon knots with the same short-bar algebra is false.
The actual knot R=12n25#mirror(12n25) has fusion number exactly2 and its
extreme sources cancel at BOTH ends. The two groups at q=-14 have h=-8,-7;
those at q=12 have h=6,7, each of rank1. Its determinant is121, so this is
a counterexample to the proposed proof mechanism, not to Jones detection.
Exact fusion2 follows from the bridge-three construction and the theorem
that a composite fusion-one knot must have a two-bridge summand.
https://knotinfo.org/homelinks/database_download.php
https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1990-0968881-9

Root-of-unity quantum reconstruction

Habiro's cyclotomic expansion proves that all root-of-unity colored values
whose colors are congruent to+/-1 or+/-2 contain exactly ordinary Jones
information. They add no information about the next fixed color. As an
actual-knot check,5_1 and10_132 have equal ordinary Jones polynomials but
different color-three polynomials, while that entire infinite set of root
evaluations agrees.

The apparent continuity route toward the volume conjecture also fails:
for the figure-eight, color r-2 at an rth root tends to1, yet its first
derivative in the quantum parameter already has the positive exponential
growth rate of the color-r Kashaev sequence. The evidence contains the
exact positive-product comparison and its independent audit. Ordinary
Jones-one might satisfy an additional special rigidity theorem, but root
reflection and continuity alone do not supply it.
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0211044
https://letu.math.gatech.edu/Papers/Lectures_Luminy_2014_new.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0502428

The resulting research target is sharper: seek nonnegative combinations of
Jones moments on their earlier vanishing loci, and use geometric filling
theorems to certify cancellation families. The results above demonstrate
both mechanisms and give explicit examples that any proposed extension
must survive. They do not establish a universal positivity identity or
reduce arbitrary knots to finitely many twist patterns.
