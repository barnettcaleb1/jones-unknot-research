JONES UNKNOT DETECTION: A CONCRETE FAILURE IN THE CLAIMED PROOF
Audit of Carmi–Cohen, arXiv:2606.22410v1
Checked 31 October 2026, America/Chicago

RESULT AND SCOPE

The relative-boundary isotopy asserted in Proposition 5.12(G2), pages 45–46,
does not preserve the four clasp crossings as claimed. Reading the actual
source figures, its pictured endpoint switches all four clasp crossings
and reverses the arrows on the closed twist component. Those changes matter
in the fixed oriented crossing cube used by the argument.

A seven-crossing, exact state-sum certificate distinguishes the initial
tangle from the claimed fixed-clasp endpoint. Their closed Kauffman brackets
have spans 20 and 4. Reidemeister moves cannot change bracket span.

This identifies a specific failure of the geometric argument supplying
hypothesis (C2). It does not refute a conditional theorem while leaving all
its hypotheses true, and it does not disprove Jones unknot detection.
No proof or counterexample to the main conjecture has been obtained here.

1. THE CLAIM BEING TESTED

The machine has four clasp crossings Y and an odd residual twist Z of
length L. The exceptional state has all signs equal to s. In (G2), the
paper claims that two end crossings of Z can be removed by Reidemeister
moves while keeping Y fixed. It then inserts a canceling pair, obtaining
a length-L state with one residual sign changed and the same four clasp
signs. This is used to transfer vanishing at coefficient level r from a
nonexceptional state to the exceptional state.

The explicit illustration for L=3 uses source images omega_1.png through
omega_5.png. Inspecting those images reveals that the over/under choices
at all four clasps switch between the first and last images. Reversing
the drawn orientation of the closed component restores the clasp-sign
caption, but does not give the asserted state of one fixed oriented cube.

2. A FINITE, ORIENTATION-INDEPENDENT CERTIFICATE

Label the upper/lower left clasps a,b, the upper/lower right clasps c,d,
and the three central crossings z0,z1,z2. Close the two upper boundary
ends together and the two lower boundary ends together using disjoint
exterior caps. This is an unoriented closure test: it need not respect
the displayed arrows. Any oriented relative-boundary isotopy would also
be an unoriented isotopy and remain one after this closure.

The following planar-diagram codes use cyclic ports with the first pair
of opposite ports underneath. Edge labels are zero-based; each occurs twice.

  (5,8,6,9), (9,4,0,5), (3,0,4,1)]

Claimed fixed-clasp endpoint (only z0 switched):

Actual final source picture (four clasps and z0 switched):

  (8,6,9,5), (9,4,0,5), (3,0,4,1)]

For bracket convention delta = -A^2-A^-2 and one circle equal to 1,
an exact enumeration of all 128 smoothing states gives:

 Initial:
   A^-15 - A^-11 + 2A^-7 - A^-3 + 2A - A^5.
   Span = 20.

 Claimed fixed-clasp endpoint:
   A^-5 + A^-1.
   Span = 4.

 Actual final picture:
   A^-9 - A^-5 + 2A^-1 - A^3 + 2A^7 - A^11.
   This is A^6 times the initial bracket.

Thus the actual pictured endpoint is consistent with the two framing
changes in the movie. The endpoint named by the proof is different.
The first and claimed brackets cannot be related by any signed monomial,
so no writhe convention or component reorientation resolves the discrepancy.

The source-to-diagram reconstruction passed a planarity check. Jones
computations in Regina, an independent frontier bracket calculation, and
an independent exhaustive bracket state sum agree. The short exhaustive
verifier in the accompanying evidence needs only standard Python.
The remaining human-checkable input is the port map read from the figures;
that map and the full reconstructions are included in the evidence.

3. WHY THE ORIENTATION CHANGE IS NOT HARMLESS

Use the paper's variables x=t^(1/2), p=x-x^-1. With both seed strands
upward and one fixed orientation of the closed component, the source's
s=- instance has signs

 Initial:       clasps (-,-,-,-), residual (-,-,-).
 Pictured end:  clasps (+,+,+,+), residual (+,-,-).

Reversing the closed component changes the normalization. The corrected
relation supplied by the movie has a factor x^(12s), together with the
four flipped clasp signs. For a compatible vertical closure in the s=-
case, the exact polynomials are

 Initial:       x^-2 + 2x^-6 + x^-10.
 Pictured end with fixed orientation: x^10 + 2x^6 + x^2.

Their ratio is x^-12. This compatible closure checks the orientation
factor; it is not the closure used to distinguish the tangles above.

Condition (C1d) ranges over all clasp choices, so switching Y alone would
not settle the issue. The monomial factor is crucial: in the paper's
normal form V=A(p)+xB(p), multiplication by x^12 mixes coefficient levels.
Vanishing of the coefficient at level r in one state does not imply
vanishing at r after this multiplication. The higher-level assumptions
do not supply the missing lower-level vanishings. The stated descent
therefore requires a new argument.

4. AN INDEPENDENT CHECK ON THE COMPRESSION STRATEGY

There is also a simple obstruction to a universal degree-compression
repair. Start with the three-crossing trefoil diagram and switch its
unknotting crossing, obtaining an actual unknot diagram. At that marked
crossing add positive full twists. Its fixed oriented smoothing has
H=-x-x^5. The exact skein relation gives

 R_0=1,
 R_(m+1)=x^4 R_m + p x^2 H,
 R_m=1+D*(x^(4m)-1)/(x^4-1),
 D=x^2+x^6-x^8-1 != 0.

Consequently its degree grows without bound. More generally, for an
unknot-Jones anchor, a uniform degree bound in this family is equivalent
to H=-(x+x^-1), the unlink Jones polynomial. The anchor alone does not
force that smoothing value.

The paper's exact paired-clasp skein identity reads

 V_-+ + V_+- = x^-4 V_++ + x^4 V_-- - p^2 V_00.

Therefore four uniformly bounded clasped states cannot recover this
unbounded residual family with fixed coefficients. Using the paper's
other direct local reductions, the locked state satisfies T_m=C-p^2 R_m,
with C of bounded degree. For the three-crossing unknot host, those
reductions give deg(C)<=18. At L=7, the leading coefficient b_21(T) is +1,
above the claimed threshold 20. This last statement is an algebraic
consequence of those other stated local identities; it is not presented
as an independently reconstructed full-host PD calculation.

The unknot diagram here is nonminimal. Restricting a new theorem to
minimal diagrams of hypothetical nontrivial Jones-one knots would evade
this particular anchor example, but would require a new proof. It would
also have to handle the crossing-flipped hosts used later in the paper.

RESEARCH CONSEQUENCE

The preprint's supplied argument does not establish its conclusion.
The local compression mechanism cannot be used as a proven bridge to a
solution. Further progress must supply an actual constraint on the
missing smoothing channel, or use a different mechanism. The calculations
above are a checked obstruction to this proposed route; no novelty or
independent expert endorsement is claimed.
