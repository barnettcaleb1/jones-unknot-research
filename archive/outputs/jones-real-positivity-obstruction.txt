A LIMITATION OF REAL-PARAMETER POSITIVITY FOR JONES UNKNOT DETECTION
1 November 2026

Status: a verified obstruction to one proposed proof mechanism. This is not
a proof or counterexample to Jones unknot detection. No novelty is claimed.

THE RESULT

There is one explicit sequence of nontrivial ribbon knots R_n, each having
determinant one and signature zero, such that

  V_R_n(1) = V_R_n(-1) = 1,
  lim_(n -> infinity) V_R_n(t) = -infinity

for every fixed real t outside {0,1,-1}.

Consequently, no fixed nonzero real Jones parameter makes the normalized
ordinary Jones scalar a universally nonnegative norm with equality at the
unknot detecting that knot. Away from +/-1 the scalar can be negative; at
+/-1 these explicit nontrivial knots already have the unknot's value.

This rules out a direct nonnegative partition-sum formula for that scalar
with positive normalization. It does not rule out other analytic functions
of Jones data or a different positive geometric observable.

THE FAMILY AND PROOF

Let K_n be the torus knot T(3,6n-1), n>=1. Let -K_n denote its mirror with
orientation reversed, and set R_n=K_n#(-K_n). The standard reverse-mirror
construction gives a ribbon disk. Jones is unchanged by orientation reversal.

The exact polynomials, with the trefoil convention V=t+t^3-t^4, are

  V_K_n(t) = t^(6n-2)(1+t^2-t^(6n)),
  V_R_n(t) = (1+t^2-t^(6n))(1+t^-2-t^(-6n)).                 (1)

The second identity follows from connected-sum multiplicativity and mirror
inversion. An independent derivation of the first is given below.

Put a=t^2. For real nonzero t, a>0, and (1) becomes

  (1+a-a^(3n))(1+a^-1-a^(-3n)).

If a>1, the first factor tends to minus infinity and the second tends to
1+a^-1>0. If a<1, their roles reverse. If a=1, their product is one.
These observations prove all the asserted limits and evaluations.

Expanding (1) gives

  3+t^2+t^-2-t^(6n)-t^(-6n)-t^(6n-2)-t^(-6n+2).

Its extreme terms cannot cancel, so each R_n is nontrivial. Also
det(R_n)=|V_R_n(-1)|=1. Signature vanishes by additivity and mirroring.
A diagram formed by the indicated mirror pair has writhe zero; writhe
zero here is a property of this diagram, not a knot invariant.

For example, R_1=T(3,5)#(-T(3,5)) has

  V_R_1(2) = V_R_1(-2) = -4661/64.

EXACT THREE-STRAND CALCULATION

Set delta=-A^2-A^-2 and t=A^-4. The two-dimensional Temperley--Lieb block is

  e1=[[delta,1],[0,0]],  e2=[[0,0],[1,delta]],
  rho(sigma_i)=A I+A^-1 e_i.

Then

  C=rho(sigma1 sigma2)=[[0,-A^-4],[1,-A^-2]],
  C^2=[[-A^-4,A^-6],[-A^-2,0]],  C^3=A^-6 I.

For q not divisible by three, tr(C^q)=-A^(-2q). The remaining
one-dimensional block sends each sigma_i to A. The reduced bracket closure
functional is tr_2+(delta^2-2)rho_1: checking the basis I,e1,e2,e1e2,e2e1
gives exactly the planar closure values delta^2,delta,delta,1,1.

Thus closure((sigma1 sigma2)^q) has reduced bracket

  -A^(-2q)+(A^4+A^-4)A^(2q).

Its writhe is 2q. Multiplication by (-A^3)^(-2q) gives

  V_T(3,q)(t)=t^(q-1)+t^(q+1)-t^(2q).

Putting q=6n-1 proves (1). The q=1 and q=2 cases give the unknot and
trefoil respectively and check the normalization.

WHAT THIS CHANGES IN THE RESEARCH STRATEGY

Reflection sends t to t^-1. On the unit circle this is complex conjugation,
and V_(K#-K)=|V_K|^2 is nonnegative. On the real axis away from +/-1,
inversion is not conjugation; the displayed family shows why treating
reflection there as a positive adjoint is invalid.

The entire polynomials in (1) vary with n and none equals one. This result
therefore supplies no conclusion about a hypothetical nontrivial knot with
V identically one. It closes the proposed direct real-parameter positivity
argument, while leaving the original goal unresolved.

VERIFICATION AND SOURCES

verifier checked the TL relations, all five closure weights, C^3, and the
closed polynomials for n=1,2,3. These finite checks supplement the general
derivation above; they are not the proof of the infinite-family statement.
