AN EXACT TEST OF A VIRTUAL-KNOT ROUTE TO A JONES COUNTEREXAMPLE
Research note, November 1, 2026

RESULT AND LIMIT

For the explicit three-crossing virtual diagram below, every embedding of its
supporting thickened torus as a standard unknotted Heegaard torus in S^3 has the
following property: if the resulting classical knot has Jones polynomial 1,
then it is the unknot.

This covers every integral change of meridian and longitude, not a bounded
sample. It closes this particular proposed counterexample construction. It
does not prove the general Jones unknot-detection conjecture, cover other
virtual diagrams, or cover embeddings on knotted tori. No novelty or external
peer-review claim is made for this internal research result.

THE IDEA

Virtual knots admit unit Jones polynomials. Represent such a diagram on a
surface, then embed the thickened surface into S^3 to obtain an ordinary knot.
The obstacle is concrete: virtual evaluation treats every smoothing circle
as an unknot, while an essential surface circle can become a framed torus knot.
We retain that information exactly and test whether cancellations can still
produce Jones polynomial 1. Virtual nontriviality alone would not certify
nontriviality of the resulting classical knot.

1. AN ACTUAL SURFACE DIAGRAM

Use cyclic crossing-port rows, with the first and third ports undercrossing:

  D = [(1,4,2,5), (5,3,0,2), (3,1,4,0)].

Join equal edge labels and cap the boundary components of the resulting
oriented ribbon graph. Its three face cycles, in dart numbering 4*i+port, are

  (0,10,2,4), (1,11,7,3,5,9), (6,8).

There are 3 vertices, 6 edges, and 3 faces, so the supporting closed surface
is a torus. Opposite-port traversal gives one knot component, with crossing
signs (-,+,+) and writhe 1.

An integral cohomology basis assigns edge voltages

  edges 0,1,3: (0,0); edge 4: (1,0);
  edge 5: (0,1); edge 2: (1,1).

Edges are directed from their smaller numbered dart. The face relations in
these coordinates vanish. Write mu and lambda for the corresponding primitive
homology basis; their geometric intersection number is 1.

Put delta=-A^2-A^-2. Use the unreduced surface bracket, so a contractible circle
contributes delta and the empty diagram contributes 1. The eight smoothing
states, grouped by actual surface isotopy class, give

  F_D = C + (A-A^-3) mu^2 + A lambda^2,
  C   = A^-7 - 2A - A^5.                                  (1)

Here mu^2 and lambda^2 mean two disjoint parallel curves, not an assumption
that the subsequent S^3 evaluation is multiplicative. Forgetting both slopes
and replacing each pair by delta^2 gives -delta*A^3. The reduced virtual
bracket is therefore -A^3, and its normalized Jones polynomial is 1.

2. KEEP THE TORUS FRAMING

An oriented Heegaard marking sends

  mu -> u=(p,q), lambda -> v=(r,s), with ps-qr=1.

Let E(p,q) be the unreduced S^3 bracket of two parallel primitive (p,q)
curves with their torus framing. Choose the convention in which one curve's
surface self-framing is pq and a positive kink contributes -A^3. Then

  E(p,q)=2+A^[4q(p+1)] [2p+1]_A
           -A^[4q(p-1)] [2p-1]_A,                         (2)
  [n]_A=(A^(2n)-A^(-2n))/(A^2-A^-2).

Negative integers use [-n]_A=-[n]_A. Formula (2) follows from the doubled-slope
case of the torus skein action in Almeida--Gelca, Proposition 3.2, after
inverting their bracket variable. In particular E(1,0)=delta^2, whereas
E(1,1)=1+A^4+A^8+A^12. Treating the latter as an unframed unlink would be wrong.

The actual framed bracket is the linear evaluation

  F = C+(A-A^-3)E(p,q)+A E(r,s).                          (3)

Write X=pq, U=p^2+q^2, Y=rs, W=r^2+s^2. Its value and first derivative at A=1
are F(1)=2 and F'(1)=6+24Y. Every actual framed knot satisfies

  F=delta*(-A^3)^f V_K(A^-4),
  V_K(1)=1, V_K'(1)=0.

Consequently its framing is f=1+4Y. Thus V_K=1 is exactly the identity

  F=-delta*A^(3+12Y).                                    (4)

This determines the needed framing without guessing an ambient diagram writhe.

3. TWO COEFFICIENTS CLASSIFY ALL MARKINGS

At A=exp(h), direct expansion of (2) gives

  E(p,q)=4+24Xh+(80X^2+16U)h^2
           +(448X^3+320XU+64X)h^3/3+O(h^4).

The h^2 coefficient of (4), and then its h^3 coefficient after imposing the
first equation, require

  W=4Y^2+3Y+1-6X,                                        (5)
  2U=-10X^2+20XY+6X-Y^2-Y+2.                             (6)

These are only necessary conditions initially. Their exact sufficiency for
the surviving cases will be checked below.

The determinant condition supplies two useful integer identities:

  XY=(ps)(qr)=t(t+1)>=0, where t=qr,
  (ps+qr)^2=1+4XY.                                       (7)

If nonzero integers a,b have product Z, then

  2|Z| <= a^2+b^2 <= Z^2+1.                              (8)

The upper bound is simply (a^2-1)(b^2-1)>=0.

If X=0, primitivity of u implies U=1, and (6) implies Y(Y+1)=0. If Y=0,
primitivity gives W=1 and (5) gives X=0. These cases yield exactly

  pq=0, p^2+q^2=1, rs in {0,-1}.                         (9)

Otherwise X and Y have the same sign by (7). If X=-x and Y=-y with x,y>=1,
(5) and (8) imply 3y(y-1)+6x<=0, which is impossible.

For X,Y>0, (5) and W<=Y^2+1 imply X>=Y(Y+1)/2. Equation (6) also forces
X<=2Y: if X>=2Y+1, its right side is at most -4X-Y^2-Y+2<0. Hence Y<=3.
The remaining integer possibilities are exhausted as follows:

  Y=1: X=1 gives U=8, impossible for pq=1; X=2 gives W=-4.
  Y=2: X=3 gives U=22, impossible for pq=3; X=4 gives W=-1.
  Y=3: X=6 would require (ps+qr)^2=73, impossible.

This proves (9) for every integral determinant-one marking. There are eight
such signed matrices. Conversely, when (9) holds, full Laurent substitution
in (3) gives

  Y=0:  F=-delta*A^3;
  Y=-1: F=-delta*A^-9.

Both satisfy (4), so the classification is exact, not just a low-order filter.

4. WHY EVERY SURVIVOR IS AN UNKNOT

There is a short geometric argument, independent of numerical projections.
The ribbon diagram has an exact piecewise-linear realization on a flat torus.
In crossing, edge-midpoint, face-center order, its twelve vertices are

  (0,0), (-1/2,-19/30), (-1/2,-11/30),
  (-5/8,-1/2), (-1/4,-13/60), (1/4,13/60),
  (-3/8,-1/2), (1/4,-13/60), (-1/4,13/60),
  (-1/2,0), (0,-1/2), (-1/2,-1/2).

Use the integral edge voltages from section 1. The barycentric face triangles
are all nondegenerate and consistently oriented; their signed areas total -1.
The induced torus map has degree -1 and no folds, hence is a homeomorphism.
This validates the actual ribbon-surface diagram, including its crossing ports.

Along its six oriented edges, the longitude coordinates at start, midpoint,
and end are respectively

  (0,       -13/60, -11/30),
  (-11/30,  -1/2,   -19/30),
  (-19/30,  -47/60, -1),
  (-1,      -73/60, -41/30),
  (-41/30,  -3/2,   -49/30),
  (-49/30,  -107/60,-2).

The longitude strictly decreases throughout and makes exactly two turns.
Separate overcrossings and undercrossings by small normal displacements; these
leave the longitude coordinate unchanged.

For every surviving marking, u is a coordinate-axis slope. Therefore mu is
a meridian of one of the two complementary unknotted solid tori. Relative to
that solid torus, the longitude coordinate is plus or minus the base longitude
above. Push the collar knot into that solid torus while retaining this angle.
It is a closed two-strand braid.

Every two-strand braid is sigma_1^k. Its connected closure has odd k and is
the torus knot T(2,k), whose determinant is |k|. For example this follows by
evaluating its Alexander polynomial, proportional to (t^k+1)/(t+1), at -1.
Jones polynomial 1 gives determinant |V_K(-1)|=1. Thus |k|=1 and the knot is
the unknot. This completes the stated family exclusion.

Orientation-reversing surface markings can be handled by ambient reflection,
mirroring the crossing and framing conventions together. One must not apply
the same unmirrored surface bracket across that convention change.

VERIFICATION AND REPRODUCTION

The companion evidence archive contains three independent parts:

  * exact eight-state ribbon-surface and homology calculation;
  * the all-integer proof, exact Laurent checks, and two independent jet checks;
  * exact rational flat-torus and longitude-monotonicity certificates.

The core verification scripts use only Python's standard library. As additional
checks, all eight surviving markings were embedded on a rational polyhedral
torus, projected with exact rational arithmetic, and simplified to one circle.
Their planar diagrams are also included. Those extra computations are not
needed for the closed-two-braid argument.

A non-axis control identifies a global mirror convention between that planar
projection and the algebra above: its Jones variable is inverted. Unit Jones
and unknot type are unchanged. The evidence records this inversion explicitly;
nonconstant polynomials from the two conventions must not be equated directly.

No passage of this argument assumes the general Jones unknot conjecture.
