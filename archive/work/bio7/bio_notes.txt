1. TWO PROBES COMPLETELY RECOVER THE TWO-STRAND SKEIN RESPONSE
Use R=Z[A,A^-1], delta=-A^2-A^-2, e^2=delta e, and b=A*1+A^-1*e.
The planar braid closure functional is ell(1)=delta, ell(e)=1; one circle
has bracket1. Let [T]=f*1+g*e and F_n=ell(T b^n). These are FRAMED brackets;
known writhe corrections convert oriented product Jones polynomials to F_n.
Since (b-A)(b+A^-3)=0,
 F_(n+2)=(A-A^-3)F_(n+1)+A^-2 F_n, for all integers n.
Moreover
 F_0=delta f+g,
 F_1=-A^3 f-A^-3 g.
Let D=A^3-delta A^-3=A^-1(delta^2-1). Then
 D f=-A^-3 F_0-F_1,
 D g=A^3 F_0+delta F_1.
D is a nonzero Laurent polynomial, so two symbolic probes recover the entire
bracket vector. Infinite rounds at the SAME synapse provide no additional
Jones information. This is a sharp finite-dimensional observability statement.
The stdlib verifier response_check.py checks165 actual algebraic-tangle
expressions,1650 recurrence instances, and both reconstruction identities.

At a fixed numerical A, observability fails when A^8+A^4+1=0, equivalently
when t=A^-4 is a primitive third root of unity. At a simple such root, the
derivatives of the two inverse numerators divided by D' recover f and g
at that root, by l'Hopital. Thus varying the coupling can recover information
which more rounds at the same singular coupling cannot.

2. AN EXPLICIT HIDDEN CHANNEL AND AN EXACT DESIGN EQUATION
After adjusting framing so an actual unknot substrate has ell(T)=1,
every bracket vector is uniquely
 T(h)=e+h(1-delta e), h in R.
The original closed Jones observation sees no h at all. One extra twist reads
 F_1=-A^-3-A^-1(delta^2-1)h.
For a second tangle S=u+v e,
 ell(T(h)S)=u+delta v+(1-delta^2)v h.
This makes the inverse problem concrete: recover h from intervention data,
then impose geometric constraints from the enzyme/tangle model.

Two independently prepared substrates compensate to retain the two-unlink
bracket exactly when
 ell(T(h)T(k))=delta+(1-delta^2)(h+k-delta h k)=delta,
 equivalently (1-delta h)(1-delta k)=1.
Over R this requires both factors to be Laurent monomial units. Consequently
arbitrary cancellation designs cannot be realized even algebraically. This
recovers the earlier rank-two unit obstruction; it is not new generic progress.

A narrower constructive target survives: find a NONRATIONAL two-string tangle
with bracket e and a CERTIFIED ACTUAL UNKNOT closure ell(T). Then the crossed
closure ell(Tb) has bracket -A^-3. Its magnitude at A=1 forces it to be a knot,
since for a c-component link |V(1)|=2^(c-1). Its normalized Jones is a monomial;
the knot identities V(1)=1,V'(1)=0 force that monomial to be1. If this crossed
closure were also an actual unknot, the double branched cover of T would have
two distinct distance-one S3 fillings. The nonmeridional S3-surgery theorem
forces that cover to be a solid torus, making T rational, a contradiction.
Thus such a T would construct a counterexample with rational-tangle unknotting
number1. This is a RESTRICTED REALIZABILITY TARGET, not a reduction of every
potential Jones counterexample. The opposite planar closure is delta and
is NOT the knot closure used in this argument.
Relevant primary tangle/Jones equivalences and surgery discussion:
Sikora, https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08162 .

3. A GENUINE EPistasis TEST: TWO SILENT MOVES INTERACT
Take the3braid
 w=[1,2,1,-2,-1,-2,1,2].
The first six generators are identity by the braid relation, so its closure
is the unknot closure of sigma1 sigma2. Switch crossing positions0 and2,
numbered from zero. Each SINGLE switch still gives an actual unknot, but the
DOUBLE switch gives a trefoil. Exact Jones response table:
 (00,10,01,11)=(1,1,1,t^-1+t^-3-t^-4).
Both first-order differences vanish at the base state, while the mixed
difference is t^-1+t^-3-t^-4-1, nonzero.
This is a literal knot analogue of biological epistasis: individually silent
changes have a joint effect. It is not merely a formal polynomial example.
unknot_epistasis.py saves the four PD codes and checks simplification to
0,0,0,3 crossings, each with one component. Exact TL3 evaluation independently
checks all four Jones polynomials. No full knot invariant is inferred from
this marked response: even the ACTUAL UNKNOT has nonzero interactions.

An even simpler warning is the unknot closure of the2braid sigma1: adding a
positive full twist gives a trefoil with V=t+t^3-t^4; adding a negative full
twist gives another unknot. V=1 never implies that its perturbed products
must retain V=1.

4. WHAT THE BIOLOGY CONNECTION SUGGESTS NEXT
Move the synapse or use two interacting sites, rather than indefinitely
repeating one operation. For m marked sites the entire response is a
multilinear functional on a tensor product of m rank-two skein spaces.
Two independent probes per site recover that functional from2^m measurements;
they do not automatically recover the full Catalan-dimensional skein state
on all4m boundary points. Mixed responses identify which sites interact and
can guide exact counterexample construction toward coupled regions rather
than independent local knots. The example above is a necessary calibration
test for any such interaction criterion.

For the mathematical unknot problem, a possible research program is to impose
topological realization constraints on these measured hidden coefficients:
certified unknot substrate, no local knot, prime tangle, and controlled
branched-cover exterior. In the rational subclass, classical tangle fractions
make reconstruction rigid; outside it, geometric realizability is the missing
step. The DNA literature earns its rigidity from multiple ACTUAL topological
products and structural hypotheses, not from one scalar Jones value.

5. THE UNAVOIDABLE LIMIT OF A FIXED TWO-STRAND OBSERVATION
Conway mutation fixes both basis elements of the two-strand bracket space.
Thus a tangle and its Conway mutant have the same bracket vector and all
outside Jones intervention responses, even if the tangles are nonisotopic.
Actual mutant knots, such as Conway and Kinoshita--Terasaka, show that this
can conceal genuine topological differences. One must move/enlarge the
accessible region or use information beyond the fundamental bracket.
Primary skein proof of mutation invariance:
https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2010/16-7p.pdf, Section3.

These results identify both a usable experimental-design principle and a
precise realizability problem. They neither prove Jones unknot detection nor
produce a counterexample, and no novelty claim is made for the elementary
skein identities.

6. DIRECT EXISTING BIOLOGY--JONES BRIDGE: SIKORA AUDIT
Source: Adam S. Sikora, arXiv:2005.08162v2, Sections4,8,9,
https://arxiv.org/html/2005.08162 . This is a KNOWN research direction, not
an original theorem of this investigation.

Fix two rational probes T1,T2 distinct as UNFRAMED tangles. Theorem21
reconstructs [X] from the two framed closure brackets. If [Ti]=(ai,bi), the
measurement matrix has rows (delta ai+bi, ai+delta bi), and determinant
(delta^2-1)(a1 b2-a2 b1), nonzero because the rational slopes differ.
Theorem24 proves that bracket recognition of rational tangles among all
tangles is equivalent to Jones unknot detection. Theorem3 then follows:
assuming JC, a framed reaction system admitting a rational solution admits
no other solution.

The positive inverse-design target is therefore precise: find a nonrational
X and rational R with certified actual isotopies
 N(X+Ti) = N(R+Ti), i=1,2,
and compatible framings. This is a topological construction problem, rather
than a polynomial guessing problem. The branched-cover version seeks a
non-solid-torus exterior with two fillings matching the solid-torus model,
with compatible peripheral markings and deck involutions. Matching abstract
filled 3-manifolds alone is insufficient to certify the branch links.

Framing calibration: fix all blackboard-framed input diagrams. Track the
signed Reidemeister-I defects k1,k2 in the two certified closure isotopies.
A single framing change of X aligns both output framings exactly when
k1=k2. Adding framing to a probe shifts both candidate products equally and
does not repair a mismatch. Sikora works up to BALANCED framed isotopy,
which is more flexible than individually preserving every component framing.
Under that stated convention, total kink defect is the relevant calibration.

A necessary negative control is already in Lemmas14--15: the unframed
system N(X)=U=N(X+1) has the two rational solutions infinity and -1/2.
Their closure framing DIFFERENCES are respectively +1 and -3. Consequently
they never give two solutions to the same framed system. The HTML has an
irrelevant typo in the denominator q in Lemma14 and conflicting absolute
framing offsets for -1/2 in Lemma15; the difference +1 versus -3 is consistent
and is all we use. An unframed ambiguity alone does not threaten JC.

7. EXACT BRAID PROOF OF THE EPistasis CALIBRATION
Independently supplied and checked by the braid agent. Put a=sigma1,
b=sigma2 and use aba=bab. The base word is
 w=aba b^-1 a^-1 b^-1 ab=ab.
Flip crossing0: a^-1 b a b^-1 a^-1 b^-1 ab = a^-1 b.
Here b a b^-1 = a^-1 b a, followed by free cancellation.
Flip crossing2: a b a^-1 b^-1 a^-1 b^-1 ab = b^-2 a b,
using a b a^-1 = b^-1 a b and free cancellation. This is conjugate to
b^-1 a. Both single-flip closures are unknots by Markov destabilizations.
The double-flip word reduces to a^-2 b^-2 a b. It is conjugate to a^-3 b:
cyclically rotate to b^-1 a b a^-2 b^-1, substitute
b^-1 a b = a b a^-1 to get a b a^-3 b^-1, cyclically rotate to
a^-3 b^-1 a b, and apply the same relation to get a^-2 b a^-1.
Moving the last a^-1 to the front gives a^-3 b. The closure destabilizes
to a^-3, a negative trefoil. The exact TL3 Jones table independently confirms
V=t^-1+t^-3-t^-4 for the double flip, and V=1 for the other three states.
