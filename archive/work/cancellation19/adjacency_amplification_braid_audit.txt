INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF CONDITIONAL ADJACENCY AMPLIFICATION
31 October 2026. Braid/geometry track. No novelty claim.

Verdict: the theorem in adjacency_amplification.txt passes, conditional on
the previously established actual preferred-flat-core/u=1 equivalence.
In particular the result is NOT a conversion of an arbitrary Jones-one
knot to the unknotting-number-one sector.

The construction starts from an actual nontrivial (n+1)-adjacent knot,
not an abstract Brunnian link with unmarked slopes. Its reverse crossing
arcs over U lift to a componentwise strongly inverted Brunnian link
B=C union D1 union ... union Dn. Every original surgery coefficient is
+/-1/2, and the full original surgery has cover Sigma2(K_seed) != S3.
The latter inequality follows from the Smith theorem and the genuine
nontriviality of K_seed.

SIMPLER ESSENTIALITY ARGUMENT FOR THIS CHOSEN SEED

Perform the prescribed surgeries on D1,...,D(n-1), retaining C and Dn,
whose new image is P. Each of C and P is an unknot, since the sublink
consisting of that component and the operated components was proper in
B, hence an unlink. Their linking number remains zero, because all
original pairwise linkings vanish. The ambient manifold is S3.

The pair C union P is NOT the unlink. If it were, completing the two
remaining prescribed surgeries beta_C and beta_P would give S3: their
numerators are +/-1 and both components would be unknotted and split.
But surgery order does not matter, and this complete surgery is precisely
Sigma2(K_seed), known not to be S3. This contradiction proves that P is
an essential winding-zero pattern in E(C).

This argument is sufficient here and avoids extending a reducing-filling
theorem to an arbitrary number of boundary tori. The stronger all-r
reducing-filling lemma in the main note is not needed for amplification.

Insert the hypothetical nontrivial flat strongly inverted J at C using
the preferred untwisted gluing. The surgeries on D1,...,D(n-1) commute
with infection. The old zero-linking conditions ensure that no longitude
correction occurs. Their final remaining knot is exactly P(J), whose
companion torus is incompressible because both the pattern and J are
nontrivial in the required sense. Thus P(J) is not the unknot.

Its last surgery coefficient remains beta_P=+/-1/2. The knot-complement
surgery theorem of Gordon--Luecke implies that this nonmeridional surgery
on nontrivial P(J) cannot produce S3. Therefore the complete infected
surgery is not S3. This proves full-surgery nontriviality directly and
also avoids a separate appeal to the self-adjacency classification.

Alternatively, the main note's application of Lidman--Moore Proposition
3.1 is valid: every proper sublink of the infected link is an unlink,
all pairwise linking numbers are zero, and a hypothetical S3 result for
the full surgery would make an S3 self-adjacency. Proposition3.1 would
force the whole link to be an unlink, contradicted by P(J). The precise
statement was independently read in the primary paper, Section3:
https://arxiv.org/html/2308.06211#S3

ACTUAL CROSSING CHANGES, NOT JUST DISTANCE-TWO SLOPES

The original (n+1) disjoint crossing neighborhoods have fixed markings.
Infection changes only C's quotient ball. All n other crossing balls,
their rational tangles, and their replacement tangles remain literally
unchanged. The boundary gluing uses the original peripheral markings;
zero pairwise linking gives no surgery correction to the other preferred
longitudes. Hence all surviving local operations are the original
ordinary crossing changes, simultaneously, not merely arbitrary
rational replacements with distance two.

The infected pre-surgery ambient manifold is S3 with an orientation-
preserving involution having fixed points. Its quotient branch knot is
an actual unknot by the Smith theorem. Crossing changes preserve the
component count, so the final branch set is also a knot. A diagram
containing the n disjoint marked crossing neighborhoods exists by taking
a projection compatible with those finitely many balls.

Every proper subset of the surgeries is surgery on an actual unlink and
has cover S3. Thus undoing any nonempty subset of the final n changes
gives an unknot. The final cover is not S3 by the argument above, so the
final knot is nontrivial. Its unknotting number is therefore exactly one.

JONES FRAMING AUDIT

The actual flat input is [T_J]=u[Q0], with u a signed A-Laurent unit,
not an arbitrary rational factor. Applying the meridional unknot cap
proves this assertion. Skein multilinearity replaces T_J by Q0 while
multiplying the final bracket by this one fixed unit. With Q0 inserted,
the final closure is the original seed with only n of its n+1 reverse
crossing changes made, hence an actual unknot.

Consequently the final Jones polynomial is a signed monomial. For a knot
its exponents are integral, V(1)=1 removes the sign, and V'(1)=0 removes
the exponent. Thus it is exactly 1. No equality of diagram writhes is
assumed, and no colored-Jones promotion is used.

CONCLUSION

An actual u=1 Jones counterexample implies an actual nontrivial n-adjacent
Jones counterexample for every n>=2 (and n=1 is the original hypothesis).
Conversely any such example is itself u=1. Therefore existence of a
counterexample within u=1 is equivalent to existence within each fixed
n-adjacent class. This does not assert the resulting knots are distinct
as n varies and does not settle the unrestricted Jones conjecture.
