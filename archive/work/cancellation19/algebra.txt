EXACT CANCELLATION AUDIT: CONNECTED SUM, PARALLEL INPUTS, AND WIDTH
31 October 2026. Internal research note. No generic detection or novelty claim.

1. THE CORRECT MULTIPLICATIVE COORDINATE

Let R=Z[A,A^-1], delta=-A^2-A^-2. In the horizontal two-tangle skein
algebra S=R[e]/(e^2=delta e), Q0=1 and Q_infinity=e. The denominator
functional is the algebra homomorphism f_infinity(1)=1,
f_infinity(e)=delta. Write v=e-delta; then v^2=-delta v.

For an actual unknot-anchored preferred exterior quotient T, divide
its bracket by the framing unit f_infinity(T). Its exact normalized
vector is

                    T_hat=1+x(T)v,
                    x(T) in R.

Define

                    y(T)=1-delta*x(T).

Horizontal composition gives

 x(T1+T2)=x1+x2-delta*x1*x2,
 y(T1+T2)=y(T1)*y(T2).                                      (1)

These are polynomial identities, not formal asymptotics.

2. GEOMETRY AND PREFERRED MARKING OF THE COMPOSITION

Choose the horizontal sum so its gluing disk is the two-punctured disk
that caps the right end of T1 and left end of T2 in their meridional
closures. Its branched double cover is an annulus with meridional core
in both covering knot exteriors. Gluing these exteriors along that
annulus is the standard exterior of J1#J2. The involutions glue to the
equivariant connected sum. The meridian closure downstairs is the
connected sum of the actual two meridional unknots, hence U. Preferred
longitudes concatenate across the meridional annulus; no extra meridian
twist is introduced. Thus (1) applies to preferred quotients of the
resulting directed strongly invertible connected sum.

The standard geometric equivariant connected-sum construction is stated
explicitly in Miller--Powell, Strongly invertible knots, equivariant
slice genera, and an equivariant algebraic concordance group, Definition
2.9 and Remark2.10 (following Sakuma):
https://www.maths.gla.ac.uk/~mpowell/equivslice-020223.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.11571
The meridional-annulus argument above identifies its quotient operation
and fixes the peripheral marking used here.

3. AN EXACT NO-CANCELLATION THEOREM

For any finite list of actual preferred unknot-anchored quotient tangles,

        T1+...+Tk is Jones-flat  iff  every Ti is Jones-flat.   (2)

Proof. Flat means x=0, equivalently y=1. By (1), flatness of the sum
implies product(y_i)=1 in the Laurent polynomial ring R. Consequently
each y_i is a Laurent unit epsilon*A^m. Also y_i=1-delta*x_i, so

epsilon*A^m == 1 mod(delta).

Since delta=-A^-2(A^4+1), this congruence forces m=4n and
epsilon=(-1)^n: reduce exponents modulo8 in R/(A^4+1), whose basis is
1,A,A^2,A^3. Thus y_i=(-A^4)^n.

Let zeta=exp(i*pi/4). The longitude of every actual knot exterior has
zero determinant. Since

 f0(T_hat)=delta+(1-delta^2)*x(T),

and delta(zeta)=0, this gives x(T)(zeta)=0. But

 x(T)=[1-(-A^4)^n]/delta
       ==> x(T)(zeta)=-n*zeta^2,

including negative n (by the Laurent geometric-series identity).
Therefore n=0, so y_i=1 and x_i=0. The reverse implication is immediate.

An equivalent short package is

                 y(T) in 1+(A^4+1)^2 R.                     (3)

Indeed x(zeta)=0 and integrality imply Phi8=A^4+1 divides x; delta
contains another Phi8. The only Laurent unit in (3) is 1. This proves
(2) directly. No positivity assumption and no finite-order truncation
replace the exact polynomial argument.

In particular a mirror, a reversal, or an allegedly inverse preferred
quotient cannot cancel a non-flat summand. A hypothetical flat core can
be copied into any number of equivariant connected summands and remains
flat, but no first flat core is created by this operation.

4. WHY DUPLICATING AN INPUT DOES NOT IMPLEMENT ADDITION OF SHELL FACTORS

Let F_i be normalized compatible shells with factors d_i in R. Inserting
the SAME normalized input T into each shell, then taking the equivariant
connected sum of the outputs, gives

 y_out=product_i(1-delta*d_i*x),
 x_out=[1-product_i(1-delta*d_i*x)]/delta.                    (4)

For two branches this is

                 x_out=(d1+d2)x-delta*d1*d2*x^2.

Thus the apparent additive term has an unavoidable nonlinear correction.
If k nonzero d_i occur, (4) has degree k in x, with nonzero leading
coefficient (-delta)^(k-1) product(d_i). A single-input shell is linear:
x_out=d*x. Hence a nontrivial parallel duplicate-input construction
cannot be represented by a single compatible two-component shell.

The same conclusion holds if one only asserts equality on all rational
probes Q_n. Here x(Q_n)=[1-(-A^4)^n]/delta; these are infinitely many
distinct elements of Q(A). A polynomial of degree >=2 cannot agree with
a linear function on all of them. In fact k+1 distinct probes suffice.

Geometrically, merging several surgery axes into one does not preserve
all their independent-surgery connected-sum outputs. Some interactions,
marking changes, or additional components must enter. Formula (4) detects
that missing geometric input. This is an exact barrier, not an assertion
that arbitrary multi-input tangles are impossible.

5. SERIAL CIRCUITS AND THE NEXT NON-EXCLUDED CLASS

Changing basis between stages does not help serial cancellation: any
finite product of invertible rational-tangle operations and nonzero-d
shell maps has nonzero determinant over Q(A). A commutator of invertible
maps also has full rank. This rules out a fixed-width serial construction
of a rank-one shell from such blocks, even when intermediate maps do not
preserve the preferred Q0 line.

A genuine additive construction must therefore use a connected geometry
with an additional intermediate skein channel and a subsequent
contraction, not serial multiplication or uncorrected duplication of one
input. This is a necessary structural feature, not an actual essential
zero shell. The unknot anchors and componentwise strong inversion still
have to be established geometrically for any such contraction.

6. SWAPPED PRIME FACTORS: VERIFIED LOCAL FORMULA, DECOMPOSITION SOURCE GATE

For the standard flipping inversion on D(K)=K#K^r, its preferred quotient
is the usual double tangle. Its meridional closure is U and its longitude
closure is the zero-framed two-parallel of K. This geometric double-tangle
identification appears in Lewark--Zibrowius, Rasmussen invariants of
Whitehead doubles and other satellites, Remark3.10 (and the cited
Montesinos correspondence). DMT arXiv:2606.05819 Example2.21 separately
defines the flipping inversion.

Let H3(K) be the reduced 3-dimensional colored Jones polynomial, U=1,
and Delta2=delta^2-1. The two-parallel expansion 2 x 2 = 1 + 3 gives

       R_D=(1+Delta2*H3(K))/delta,
       x_D=(1-H3(K))/delta,
       y_D=H3(K).                                           (5)

All framings in (5) are zero/preferred; changing framing without restoring
it would invalidate the comparison. Equation (5) does not infer H3 from
ordinary Jones. It identifies the exact additional information in the
swapped-factor block.

A known theorem of Sakuma (On strongly invertible knots, Theorem1 and
Lemma1.2(2)) decomposes arbitrary directed strong inversions into
strongly invertible prime knots and these flipping prime blocks. Snape's
thesis quotes this result verbatim. The primary author-hosted article and
1984 RIMS precursor were located, but fetching the full theorem failed
in this bounded check. Therefore the unconditional global decomposition
claim is NOT counted as a newly independently checked ingredient here.
Once the stated primary theorem is checked, (2) and (5) immediately show
that a flat arbitrary strongly inverted core must have each equivariant
prime block flat: a prime strongly invertible flat core or a prime K
with H3(K)=1. The latter is a separate colored constraint, not permission
to replace it by V_K=1.

7. EVEN A FORMAL INTEGRAL SUPERPOSITION CANNOT CANCEL ASSOCIATED FACTORS

There is a second obstruction independent of whether a proposed additive
controller has been geometrically realized. Suppose all its resolved
branches are normalized compatible shells F_i=diag(1,d_i), and

              d_i=u_i*d0,   d0 nonzero,   u_i=+/-A^k_i.

Suppose the controller's skein expansion is an integral Laurent linear
combination F=sum_i a_i F_i, a_i in R. Preserving the normalized unknot
channel requires sum_i a_i=1. If its transmission vanished, then

                        sum_i a_i u_i=0.

Reduce modulo the maximal ideal (2,A-1). Every Laurent unit u_i becomes
1 in F2. The left side becomes sum_i a_i=1, a contradiction.

Therefore NO such integral controller cancels the full transmission.
This covers arbitrarily many mirrors/framing variants of one fixed
Whitehead-tower factor, not just a pair. It allows negative coefficients;
positivity is not being assumed. It is an exact ring obstruction to an
identically zero polynomial, despite using a simple residue to certify it.

A successful additive construction must have either genuinely different
nonassociated branch factors, branches that do not separately preserve
the normalized anchor, or a geometry not captured by this affine
resolution. Rational-function coefficients such as division by2 would
evade the formal ring test but are not supplied by ordinary framed-tangle
skein expansion. This is a necessary condition, not a realization theorem.

8. FIRST ACTUAL OVERLAPPING TL3 CIRCUIT: EXACT PRODUCT OBSTRUCTION

Embed an actual preferred cap-trivial quotient on adjacent strands as

                  T_i(x)=I+x(e_i-delta I),  i=1,2.

Let u2 be the cup on strands2--3, a morphism from one strand to three;
let d1 be the cap on strands1--2. Their empty composite is the snake,
d1*u2=I on the remaining strand. Thus closing that remaining strand has
an actual unknot as the baseline, with no division by delta and no
formal projector.

Insert T1(b) and then T2(a) between these cup/cap morphisms. Preferred longitude closure has two components, so each gate has
identity endpoint connectivity and no closed components. The cup2-to-cap1
wiring forces the three strand orientations to alternate (+,-,+). The two
strands in each gate are therefore oppositely oriented, matching its
meridian-cap orientation up to reversing both strands. Normalize each gate by its actual meridional framed-unknot
unit. This removes precisely its writhe contribution; the cups and caps
add none. Therefore the following is an exact normalized knot-Jones
formula, not equality merely up to a framing monomial:

       V_K(A^-4)=1+(1-delta^2)*a*b.                          (6)

For verification, put v_i=e_i-delta. We have d1*v1=0 and v2*u2=0.
The only nonconstant term is a*b*d1*v2*v1*u2. The TL3 relations
(e_i^2=delta e_i, e1e2e1=e1, e2e1e2=e2) give

                  d1*v2*v1*u2=(1-delta^2) I.

As a and b lie in the integral domain R, (6) equals1 iff at least one
gate was flat already. This eliminates the first genuinely overlapping
two-gate construction. It neither relies on a braid kernel nor replaces
actual tangles by a formal linear combination.

Longer alternating circuits can have additional terms. No claim that
(6) classifies all TL3 networks is made. A next attempt needs enough
interleaving to escape this two-gate product formula, while retaining a
geometric unknot anchor. Merely placing two known non-flat preferred
quotients on overlapping strand pairs is insufficient.

9. INDEPENDENT AUDIT: BRUNNIAN INFECTION DOES GIVE THE MISSING CONVERSE

The tangle track supplied a different, genuinely interacting construction.
Its geometry and algebra pass this independent audit. Let P,C,D be a
nontrivial, three-component Brunnian link in S3 with a componentwise
strong inversion. Every proper sublink is the actual unlink. Let J be a
nontrivial strongly invertible knot with an actual preferred flat quotient
T_J. Infect the C component by J using the usual meridian-longitude swap,
and retain the two-component link

                         B_J=P(J) union D(J).

Because P union C and D union C are split unlinks, both retained components
are actual unknots. Their linking number is zero. The compatible strong
inversions glue and remain strong on both retained components. Thus B_J
is in precisely the class of two-unknot winding-zero shells under study.

ALGEBRA: Consider the quotient of the original three-component exterior.
It is a two-input tangle operator, one input being the C quotient and the
other being the eventual D companion. Inserting T_J=uQ0 into the C hole
multiplies the entire remaining D-to-P skein map by u, by skein linearity.
Inserting Q0 instead restores P union D, a split unlink. Its shell map has
rank one and transmission zero. The fixed framing scalar u disappears
under meridional anchor normalization. Hence

                           d_(B_J)=0 identically.            (7)

This does not require colored promotion and does not use finite jets.
It also shows why no formal additive controller was needed: geometric
infection uses an already flat tangle as input to an interacting Brunnian
three-component shell.

ESSENTIALITY: Perform 1/n surgery on D in the ORIGINAL Brunnian link.
Call the retained two-link P_n union C. Its components are still unknots
and linking zero, by pairwise splitness. For |n|>=2 this link is nonsplit.
Indeed the original nonsplit link exterior is irreducible; its D-meridian
filling is E(U2), a reducible manifold with no lens-space summand. If the
1/n filling were also split, it would again be E(U2). These two slopes
have distance |n|. The Culler--Gordon--Luecke--Shalen reducible-filling consequence
(Corollary2.4.7, as applied in Lidman--Moore Proposition3.2) says that
two such fillings at distance >=2 force a lens-space summand in one;
this is impossible here. It is important to use this precise theorem,
not to state an unrestricted distance<=1 theorem for all reducible
fillings.

The nonsplit P_n union C is an essential winding-zero pattern. After
inserting nontrivial J, its companion torus is incompressible on both
sides, so P_n(J) is nontrivial. Surgery on D(J) in B_J yields precisely
P_n(J), since the surgeries/infection occur on disjoint boundary tori.
If B_J were split, twisting its unknotted D(J) component could not change
the other unknot. Thus B_J is nonsplit. Taking n=2 is enough.

EXISTENCE OF THE SEED: Lidman--Moore, Adjacency of three-manifolds and
Brunnian links, Nagoya Math.J.261 (2026), e10, Theorem1.4 proof, gives the
needed primary mechanism. Start with a nontrivial 3-adjacent-to-U knot,
change all three distinguished crossings to U, and lift the knotting
arcs to Sigma2(U)=S3. The deck involution strongly inverts EACH lifted
component. Montesinos surgery slopes are half-integral, hence their
Proposition3.2 excludes proper Hopf pairs and proves every proper
sublink is the actual unlink. The full link is nontrivial: surgery on a
three-component unlink at the resulting +/-1/2 slopes would give S3,
whereas the original nontrivial knot cannot have S3 as double branched
cover (Smith conjecture). Thus this is not just a surgery-Brunnian link
or a link in some other ambient manifold.

CONSEQUENCE: Together with the previously established essential-zero-shell
=> nontrivial u=1 Jones-one knot => nonrational anchored flat tangle
implications, this Brunnian infection closes the equivalence WITHIN THE
UNKNOTTING-NUMBER-ONE PROBLEM. It supplies a conversion, not an example:
no nontrivial flat J is known here. It does not convert an arbitrary
ordinary Jones-one knot into a flat quotient and therefore does not
settle the full Jones unknot-detection problem.

10. EXACT MULTILINEAR TRANSMISSION OF THE BRUNNIAN CONSTRUCTION

For the three-component seed B=P union C union D, let F be its two-input
quotient skein operator. Normalize by the single fixed framing unit in
F(Q0,Q0), so that this value is Q0. The split unlink C union D gives

                 f_infinity o F = f_infinity tensor f_infinity.

One way to see the framing assertion is to choose an equivariant splitting
sphere for the component-preserved unlink; its quotient separates the two
cap evaluations. The resulting functional is their product up to one
fixed framed-unknot unit, fixed by evaluating Q0,Q0. The usual uniqueness
of the componentwise strong inversion of an unlink gives the same
conclusion. No normalization depending on the inserted companions is used.

Pairwise splitness gives the stronger exact identities

             F(Q0,T)=f_infinity(T)Q0,
             F(T,Q0)=f_infinity(T)Q0.

In the unimodular R-basis (Q0,v), v=Q_infinity-delta Q0, these imply
F(Q0,v)=F(v,Q0)=0. The meridional functional identity implies
F(v,v) belongs to its one-dimensional kernel Rv. Consequently there is
one uniquely determined c_B in R with

       F(Q0+xv,Q0+yv)=Q0+c_B*x*y*v.                         (8)

There is no unspecified sign in (8) after v and the peripheral markings
are fixed. In particular the infected two-component link of Section9
has exact normalized transmission

                         d_(B_J)=c_B*x(J).                  (9)

Geometric nontriviality of B does NOT prove c_B nonzero. If c_B=0, the
fixed seed itself, combined with any nontrivial strongly invertible core,
would provide an essential zero shell and hence a Jones counterexample.
If c_B is nonzero, (9) shows that this construction propagates a flat
input but cannot create the first one.

A potential direct certificate for c_B nonzero is already built into
the adjacent-knot construction. If the seed is the lift associated to a
3-adjacent-to-U knot K with V_K != 1, its full surgeries have slopes
+/-1/2. The two input probes are Q_(+/-2); the output has a half-integral
cap. The exact cap formula of pass18 then gives

 V_K-1=(nonzero cap factor)*c_B*x(Q_(+/-2))*x(Q_(+/-2)).

Thus a verified nonconstant Jones polynomial of that source K certifies
c_B != 0, with no need to reconstruct the entire quotient diagram.
A particular source is now independently certified. The script
stoimenow_k3.py reconstructs Stoimenow's displayed K3, with 38 initial
crossings. Every nonempty subset of the three marked crossings simplifies
to a crossing-free single unknot. Regina gives the source Jones polynomial;
independent_pd_bracket.py recomputes it by an independent frontier
Kauffman state sum on the 30-crossing simplified PD, with a known-trefoil
convention check, and obtains exactly the same integer coefficients.
The diagram wiring was independently compared with the primary figure.
Exact integer-Laurent multiplication also verifies the factorization

 V_K3(t)-1 = -t^(-12)(t-1)^7(t+1)^3(t^2+1)
                         *(t^2+t+1)*(t^2-t+1)^2.

The explicit half-surgery factors are
 x(Q2)=A^2(A^4-1), x(Q_-2)=-A^(-6)(A^4-1),
 h_+=A^(-10)(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1),
 h_-=-A^(-2)(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1).
Consequently this particular seed has, up to a signed A-Laurent unit,

 c_B = (t-1)^4(t+1)^3(t^2+1)(t^2-t+1)^2
     = (t-1)^4(t+1)(t^2+1)(t^3+1)^2 != 0.

This verifies the nonzero coefficient without reconstructing the full
Brunnian quotient diagram. In the chosen v convention c_B has A-exponents
2 modulo 4, so writing it as a t-Laurent polynomial suppresses an A^2
unit. No exact monomial or overall sign is asserted by the displayed
up-to-unit equality.

More generally, a componentwise strongly inverted n-component Brunnian
link, with one output and n-1 inputs, has normalized operation

 F(Q0+x1v,...,Q0+x_(n-1)v)
                 =Q0+c_B*(product_i x_i)*v.                 (10)

Every mixed multilinear term containing at least one Q0 and at least one
v vanishes because removing that input component leaves a proper unlink.
The all-v term lies in Rv by the product meridional functional. Formula
(10) identifies the exact geometric limitation: this entire Brunnian
class forces multiplicative behavior of the defects, even though its
link geometry is genuinely interacting. Additive cancellation requires
a pattern beyond these proper-unlink conditions, or a seed whose own
coefficient c_B already vanishes.
