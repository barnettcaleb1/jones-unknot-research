BRUNNIAN INFECTION REPAIRS THE ZERO-SHELL CONVERSE
31 October 2026. Internally audited research deduction; no novelty claim.

RESULT AND SCOPE

The following existence statements are equivalent, using the preferred
meridian/longitude conventions of our satellite-transmission note:

(A) A nontrivial strongly invertible knot J has a preferred quotient
    tangle with the same projective Kauffman bracket as the rational
    longitude-zero tangle Q0.
(B) There is a nonsplit two-component link B=P union D, both components
    unknotted, linking number zero, with a componentwise strong inversion,
    whose normalized quotient-shell factor d_B(A) is identically zero.
(C) There is a nontrivial knot of unknotting number one with Jones
    polynomial identically 1.

The earlier report establishes (B) implies (A) and (C), and equivalence
of (A) and (C). The NEW deduction here is (A) implies (B), through an
actual geometrically realizable Brunnian-link construction. It repairs
the failed Whitehead-clasp converse without assuming the clasp surgery
circle is strongly inverted. It is NOT a proof or disproof of any of
these equivalent statements, and it does not identify them with the
unrestricted Jones unknot-detection conjecture.

All assertions concern whole Laurent polynomials, not finite jets.
The proof needs one hypothetical flat J but no further unproved geometric
realizability statement. No actual nontrivial flat J is currently known.

1. EXISTENCE OF AN ACTUAL STRONGLY INVERTED BRUNNIAN TRIPLE

Fix a nontrivial 3-adjacent-to-the-unknot knot K: a diagram has three
marked crossings, and changing ANY nonempty subset makes the unknot.
Such actual knots exist. One explicit published family is Stoimenow's
K_n in equation (10), printed p476, of:
A. Stoimenow, Some applications of Tristram--Levine signatures and
relation to Vassiliev invariants, Adv. Math.194(2005),463--484,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2004.07.003
https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/stoim2.pdf .
The text immediately after (10) states n-adjacency; Lemma6, pp477--479,
proves K_n nontrivial. Take n=3. No Jones-detection conjecture is used in
that lemma: its argument uses a particular known Jones evaluation of an
auxiliary braid closure to establish nontriviality.

Change all three marked crossings of K to obtain an actual unknot U.
Lift the three reverse crossing arcs in the double cover Sigma2(U)=S3.
Each arc lifts to a circle; the deck involution reverses it and fixes its
two endpoints. Thus this is an ACTUAL componentwise strongly inverted
three-component link L=P union C union D in S3.

The precise primary justification that L is Brunnian is in the proof of
Theorem1.4 of Tye Lidman and Allison H. Moore, Adjacency of three-manifolds
and Brunnian links, arXiv:2308.06211:
https://arxiv.org/html/2308.06211 .
The published paper appeared online 7 January 2026 in Nagoya Math. J.,
https://doi.org/10.1017/nmj.2025.10092 .
Their proof lifts the knotting arcs over U, applies the Montesinos trick,
and uses Proposition3.2 to show every proper sublink is an actual unlink;
the reverse crossing slopes are all +/-1/2. In particular this is stronger
than merely having pairwise linking numbers zero or surgery-trivial
proper sublinks.

The full three-component link L is not an unlink: if it were, simultaneous
+/-1/2 surgeries would give S3, whereas the result is Sigma2(K); Smith's
unknot theorem would then force K=U. Since every proper sublink is an
unlink, a split L would itself be an unlink. Thus L is nonsplit and its
exterior M is irreducible.

Do NOT replace L by the Borromean rings. The required componentwise
strong inversion is not available for that link; the nonzero triple
Milnor invariant obstructs reversing all three component orientations.
Our existence proof uses the published higher Brunnian construction.

2. ONE FIXED NONZERO TWIST MAKES AN ESSENTIAL PATTERN

Perform 1/n surgery on D, measured with its usual preferred meridian and
longitude, and call the other two components P_n and C. The ambient
manifold remains S3 because D is an unknot. Each remaining component is
still an unknot because both P union D and C union D were actual unlinks.
Their linking number remains zero: all pairwise linking numbers in L
were zero, so the surgery linking-number correction is zero.

For every |n| >= 2, P_n union C is NONSPLIT. Indeed, meridional D filling
of M is E(P union C)=E(U2), a reducible manifold. If the 1/n filling were
split, its two individually unknotted components would again give E(U2).
These two reducing slopes have distance |n|. The exact version of the
reducing-filling theorem needed here is Culler--Gordon--Luecke--Shalen,
Corollary2.4.7: for an irreducible manifold, two reducible fillings on a
torus boundary component at distance at least two force a nontrivial
lens-space summand in one filling. Neither E(U2) has such a summand.
This is precisely the application in the n=3 case of Lidman--Moore's
proof of Proposition3.2, and gives the contradiction.

Primary reference:
M. Culler, C. McA. Gordon, J. Luecke, P. B. Shalen,
Dehn surgery on knots, Ann. Math.(2)125(1987),237--300, Corollary2.4.7.
The exact multi-boundary E(U2) application and reference are printed in
Lidman--Moore, Proposition3.2 proof, cited above. No stronger general
bound on all reducible-filling slopes is required here.
Choose n=2 once and for all; no asymptotic or hyperbolicity assumption is
needed.

Hence P_2 in V=E(C) is an essential winding-zero pattern with P_2(U)=U.
Here essential means not contained in an interior ball of the solid
torus; for an unknotted axis C this is equivalent to P_2 union C being
nonsplit. The inherited involution strongly inverts both components.

3. INSERT THE HYPOTHETICAL FLAT COMPANION BEFORE THE TWIST

Assume (A), with an actual nontrivial strongly invertible J. Replace
N(C) by E(J), using the usual untwisted satellite identification

    mu_J -> lambda_C,      lambda_J -> mu_C.

Because C is an unknot, the new ambient manifold is S3. On the boundary
both strong inversions act by -I, and the peripheral gluing can be chosen
equivariantly. The involution extends over the new S3. It still strongly
inverts P and D, whose two fixed points have not been changed. Denote the
resulting two-component link by

    B_J = P(J) union D(J).

Each component is an ACTUAL UNKNOT. The pairs P union C and D union C were
unlinks, so each of P and D separately lies in a ball in V disjoint from
C; companion insertion leaves those balls and their contained unknots
unchanged. The two containing balls need not be disjoint, and no such
assumption is made. Linking number is unchanged by this infection and is
zero. Thus B_J meets every geometric hypothesis of the rank-two shell
formalism. Its zero-companion closure is P(J)=U.

Surgery on D is disjoint from the C gluing torus, so the operations
commute. More explicitly, D is local in V because D union C is the unlink;
1/n surgery can be realized in a ball in V, giving V again with its outer
boundary marking unchanged. The surgery framing corrections on C and P
are n*lk(D,C)^2 and n*lk(D,P)^2, both zero. Consequently the n-th rational
probe of the shell B_J has core knot EXACTLY the ordinary untwisted
satellite P_n(J), with its preferred meridian and longitude, not a
framing-shifted or differently surgered version.

4. THE TWO PROBES FORCE AN ENTIRE ZERO FACTOR

Put R=Z[A,A^-1], delta=-A^2-A^-2. With the standard rational basis,

    f_inf(Q0)=1, f_inf(Qinf)=delta,
    x(T)=(f_0(T)/f_inf(T)-delta)/(1-delta^2).

The normalized quotient shell F_P of any actual winding-zero,
componentwise strongly inverted two-unknot pattern P(U)=U satisfies

    F_P(Q0)=Q0,       x(F_P(T))=d_P(A)*x(T).

This established transmission identity uses full preferred markings.
It applies to the pattern P_2 union C. Because J is flat, x(T_J)=0,
its satellite P_2(J) has flat preferred quotient as well.

Now apply the shell identity to B_J. Its input Q0 has output the preferred
quotient of U, hence defect zero. Its input Q2 has output the preferred
quotient of P_2(J), hence defect zero. The input Q2 has

    x(Q2) = (1-(-A^4)^2)/delta != 0.

Since R is an integral domain, d_B*x(Q2)=0 forces

    d_B(A) IDENTICALLY ZERO.

There is an independent multilinearity explanation. Quotient E(P,C,D)
by the simultaneous strong inversion: this is a genuine two-input tangle
shell, obtained by deleting three balls from the quotient S3, with the
outer output and two input four-punctured spheres. Insert T_J at the C
input. Since T_J is projectively Q0, multilinearity makes its remaining
shell projectively identical to the shell obtained by inserting Q0.
That latter filling is the actual unlink P union D. Its normalized
transmission factor is zero. Thus the entire B_J shell has zero factor;
the two-probe argument above provides a separate algebraic check.

Only these two independent rational probes are used. There is no need
to make n=1 essential, and no dual surgery circle is introduced.

5. THE RESULTING SHELL IS ESSENTIAL

P_2(J) is nontrivial. The torus separating E(J) from the essential pattern
exterior is incompressible on both sides: E(J) has incompressible boundary
because J is nontrivial, and the pattern-side boundary is incompressible
because P_2 is not local in V. Their union is therefore an incompressible
torus in E(P_2(J)), which is impossible in an unknot exterior. Equivalently,
this is the elementary nontriviality theorem for an essential satellite
with nontrivial companion.

If B_J were split, it would be the two-component unlink, and every 1/n
surgery on its second component would leave its first component an
unknot. But its n=2 output is the nontrivial knot P_2(J). Contradiction.
Thus B_J is a NONSPLIT actual zero-factor shell, establishing (B).

6. WHAT THIS CHANGES

The zero-shell target has no additional existential realization gap beyond
the already identified unknotting-number-one Jones problem. The Brunnian
construction converts ANY hypothetical actual flat core into an actual
essential zero shell. Conversely, earlier work converts any such shell
into an actual nontrivial Jones-one knot with unknotting number one.

The construction escapes the type-C locality obstruction because D is
not required to bound an invariant disk meeting P in exactly two points.
It is a generalized local surgery in the solid torus; a disk may meet P
in more points. Pairwise unlinking does not make the full three-component
configuration trivial, and that collective interaction is exactly what
survives companion insertion.

This equivalence is progress in organizing the geometric search; it does
not produce a counterexample, and it does not prove Jones detection even
for unknotting-number-one knots. A generic Jones-one knot is not known to
admit a reduction into this anchored class. That separate generic gap
remains explicit.
