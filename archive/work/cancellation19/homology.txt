PASS 19: GEOMETRIC AUDIT AND THE ACTUAL GAUGE-THEORY GAP
31 October 2026. Independent bounded audit; no general detection proof.

1. INDEPENDENT TYPE-C LOCALITY AUDIT: PASSES

The theorem in geometry.txt is sound with its full hypotheses: the
ambient solid torus has the inherited strong inversion, the disk itself
is invariant and lies entirely in that solid torus, its boundary is
strongly inverted, and its two punctures have algebraic intersection zero.

I checked the two delicate steps independently.

(a) An invariant containing ball really exists. The split two-component
link exterior admits an essential sphere whose involution orbit is one
sphere or two disjoint spheres. Every essential sphere separates the two
components. The ball containing the local knot and excluding the axis
component is therefore mapped to another ball with the same property.
Distinct disjoint boundary spheres would make these balls nested; the
involution would reverse a strict inclusion, impossible. The chosen ball
is invariant. Its boundary involution preserves orientation, so it has
exactly two fixed points; the global fixed circle consequently meets the
ball in one interval. There is no unaccounted closed component, since the
global fixed set is a single circle which already crosses the boundary.

(b) Shrinking the crossing disk is a framed isotopy in the knot complement.
The disk involution is a reflection. Exchanged punctures would have the
same oriented intersection sign, since both disk and knot orientations
reverse. Zero linking thus makes both punctures fixed. The fixed segment
joining them is unique within the solid torus's two fixed intervals, and
lies in the containing ball. A thin disk neighborhood of a slightly
extended segment lies in that ball and contains both punctures. The
annulus between its boundary and the original disk boundary avoids the
knot. Sliding across it preserves the disk longitude/framing. Surgery can
therefore be done inside the ball. A 1/n surgery on this disk-bounding
circle again produces a ball, relative to its outer boundary.

No arbitrary equivariant isotopy of an entire disk through the knot is
needed; the explicit puncture-free annulus is sufficient. This also
explains why hypothetical twisting of the disk does not invalidate the
argument. Finite-sequence use requires the stated symmetry hypotheses at
each stage, as in an equivariant sequence.

I read the precise sphere input in Lackenby, Links with splitting number
one, proof of Proposition 4.1, PDF p11 (printed p10), and the disk model
in Boyle--Chen, Lemma 6.5. Their solid torus in Lemma 6.5 has a fixed CORE
CIRCLE, allowing two connecting intervals. Our solid torus is strongly
inverted and has two fixed ARCS, giving the uniqueness needed here.
This difference is helpful, not a contradiction.
https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/lackenby/spl29121-geom-ded.pdf
https://arxiv.org/html/2412.09797v2

Scope exclusions are essential: invariance of the boundary alone does
not supply an invariant disk; a free crossing circle is different; a disk
entering the companion exterior is different. No claim about all shells.

2. A REAL NONPERTURBATIVE INPUT, BUT NO JONES BRIDGE

Imori--Park--Taniguchi, Unknotting number, ribbon concordance, and singular
instantons, July 2026 preprint, Theorem 1.2 / Theorem 4.2: a slice knot
receiving ribbon concordance from K1#K2, with one summand's enriched
instanton local class nonzero, requires both signs in any sequence of
null-homologous unknotting twists. In particular its unknotting number
exceeds one. Corollary 1.3 gives Wh+(K)#-Wh+(K), for nonslice strongly
quasipositive K, as an explicit example with untwisting number exactly two.

The operative input is geometric: definite cobordisms plus the
Chern--Simons filtration. Equality in the appropriate filtered bound
forces an irreducible SU(2) representation, contradicting a cyclic
cobordism-complement group. This is stronger information than Euler
characteristic. See Proposition 2.1, Theorem 3.3, and the proof on pp7--9.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.12768

Consequences for the present strategy: a reduction to unknotting-one that
preserves this ribbon-concordance structure cannot work for the detected
class. Replacing a composite ribbon target by a prime hyperbolic ribbon
upper target does not remove the obstruction. But Jones=1 supplies none
of the required sliceness, local-class, or filtered-map equalities. Thus
this theorem currently blocks a proposed reduction; it does not rule out
a Jones-one unknotting-one knot. We found no valid way to infer a
Chern--Simons equality from a Jones identity.

3. THE PREVIOUS FORMAL MODEL PASSES THE EXTRA u=1 TORSION TEST

Alishahi--Dowlin, The Lee spectral sequence, unknotting number, and the
Knight Move Conjecture, Theorem 1.2, bounds the X-torsion order by u(K).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.07875

The earlier formal model has only length-one bars in the relevant X
variable. Its Q[G] Smith factor G^2 is not a length-two X-bar: in the
unreduced Frobenius algebra X=G+2z and X^2=G^2. Multiplication by X has
matrix [[G,0],[2,-G]], with Q[G] Smith form diag(1,G^2), but its cokernel
is annihilated by X. The previously verified crossing-change-style
nullhomotopy therefore survives this necessary u=1 condition. Do not
misread the change of variables as a new obstruction.
See work/proof13/homology/unreduced_lee_check.txt for the original check.

4. NO FINITE-TYPE SHORTCUT EVEN WITH u=1 FIXED

Ohyama--Taniyama--Yamada's theorem, restated as Theorem 1.1 in the new
Meilhan--Ogita--Yasuhara preprint, says: for any knot K and finite n there
is a knot J with u(J)=1 agreeing with K on every finite-type invariant
through degree n. Setting K=U defeats any fixed finite collection of
Jones Taylor coefficients even after restricting to unknotting-one.
The approximating knot may depend on n; no limiting knot or exact
Jones-one knot follows.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39474
Original source listed there: Y. Ohyama, Web diagrams and realization of
Vassiliev invariants by knots, J. Knot Theory Ramifications 9 (2000),
693--701; and the earlier Ohyama--Taniyama--Yamada reference.

5. FAITHFULNESS LOGIC

Bharathram--Birman--Brendle, arXiv:2607.05283 v2 (September 2026), claims
faithfulness of B4 Burau and obtains B4 Jones faithfulness. It is a
preprint; this pass did not independently verify its full technical proof.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.05283

Ito, arXiv:1402.2028, Corollary 3, proves the direction
  B4 Burau nonfaithful  =>  Jones does not detect the unknot.
The converse is not supplied. Hence the new faithfulness claim does not
establish unknot detection. A faithful operator representation still
need not have a trace that distinguishes its unknot closures.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.2028

OUTCOME

The concrete result of this pass is the independently audited local
type-C obstruction. The strongest newly checked gauge input obstructs
ribbon-concordance-preserving simplifications but does not close the
Jones-to-geometry gap. No counterexample and no general proof resulted.
