INDEPENDENT AUDIT: BRUNNIAN INFECTION REPAIRS THE CONVERSE
31 October 2026

VERDICT

The construction below passes an independent geometry and linear-algebra
audit. It uses the previously established normalized quotient-shell law.
It gives a conditional reduction, not a Jones-one knot by itself.

Hypothesis: there exists a nontrivial strongly inverted knot J whose
preferred quotient tangle is Jones-flat, i.e. has the normalized skein
class of the rational zero tangle Q0.

Conclusion: there exists an actual nonsplit, componentwise strongly
inverted two-component unlink-type link L_J (both individual components
are unknots, linking number zero) whose normalized shell multiplier is
identically zero.

1. AN ACTUAL SYMMETRIC BRUNNIAN THREE-LINK EXISTS

Stoimenow's construction (10), printed p476, supplies n-trivadjacent
knots K_n; Lemma 6, printed pp477--479, proves K_n is nontrivial. Use n=3.
Here n-trivadjacency means that changing any nonempty subset of the
specified three crossings gives the unknot. This is an exact geometric
condition, not merely vanishing of finite-type invariants.
https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/stoim2.pdf

Change all three crossings, yielding U, and lift the three reverse
crossing arcs to the double cover of S3 branched over U, itself S3.
The lifts are three circles P,C,D. The deck involution preserves each
circle individually and reverses it, with the two lifted arc endpoints
as fixed points. Thus this is one componentwise strong inversion of the
whole three-link, not three unrelated symmetries.

Lidman--Moore's proof of Theorem 1.4 establishes precisely that the lifted
link is Brunnian and its reverse surgery slopes are +/-1/2. Every proper
two-component sublink is an actual unlink. Their argument uses the
classification of simultaneous surgeries to S3; it does not infer unlink
type solely from pairwise linking numbers.
https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/477B1EF74CDC457D6FEA1B628CBB670C/S0027763025100925a.pdf/adjacency-of-three-manifolds-and-brunnian-links.pdf

The complete lifted link is not the three-component unlink. If it were,
the full collection of +/-1/2 surgeries would give S3, so Sigma_2(K_3)
would be S3. The Smith theorem then makes the branch knot K_3 an unknot,
contradicting Stoimenow's lemma. A split Brunnian three-link is an unlink,
so the lifted link is nonsplit.

Independent alternate existence input: Howards--Luecke, Strongly n-trivial
Knots, Section 6, Theorem 6.1 and Figure 3, explicitly construct nontrivial
strongly 2-trivial knots. Their Definition 1.3 explains that this means
3-adjacent to U. The same cover argument applies.
https://users.wfu.edu/howards/papers/ntrivial.pdf

2. THE TWISTED PATTERN IS ESSENTIAL

Let V=E(C), and perform 1/2 surgery on D. Since C union D is an unlink,
D is an unknot in a ball contained in V. This surgery can be supported in
that ball and identified with a ball afterwards, fixing its boundary.
Consequently the resulting ambient solid torus is identified with V
relative to its boundary. In particular the preferred companion marking
is preserved. Denote the image of P by P_2.

P_2(U)=U because P union D is an unlink. Also P_2 has winding zero in V;
the zero-linking framing of C is unchanged by the surgery supported away
from C. Componentwise strong inversion extends over the rational filling.

Let M=E(P,C,D). Nonsplitting makes M irreducible, and its boundary tori
are incompressible. The meridional filling on the D boundary is reducible,
since it is the exterior of the unlink P union C. If the 1/2 filling were
also reducible, Culler--Gordon--Luecke--Shalen, Corollary 2.4.7, would
force one filled manifold to contain a lens-space summand, because the
slopes have distance two. Neither filled manifold can have such a
summand: both are link exteriors in S3. Hence P_2 union C is nonsplit,
and P_2 is not contained in a ball in V. This is exactly the application
of CGLS used in Lidman--Moore's proof of Proposition 3.2. It avoids an
unqualified claim that all reducible fillings of multi-boundary manifolds
have distance at most one. Their earlier Proposition 3.1 instead uses
Gordon--Litherland's weaker general bound of four, with denominator five.
https://arxiv.org/html/2308.06211#S3

For any nontrivial companion J, P_2(J) is nontrivial. Indeed the companion
torus is incompressible on the pattern side (otherwise the pattern would
be local) and on the J-exterior side. Gluing gives an incompressible torus
in E(P_2(J)), whereas the unknot exterior is a solid torus and has none.

3. INFECT BEFORE TWISTING: NO DUAL-CIRCLE ISSUE

Insert J into V first, using its preferred framing and a compatible
strong inversion. Set

                 L_J = P(J) union D(J).

The pairs P union C and D union C were both actual unlinks. Each of P,D
therefore lies individually in a ball in V, so P(J) and D(J) remain
unknots. They need not lie in one common ball disjoint from C. The joint
linking number remains zero under the zero-framed infection. The common
strong inversion extends across the companion exterior and strongly
inverts both components.

Twisting D(J) by 1/2 produces P_2(J), because the original D surgery was
relative-boundary in V. This identifies the marking as well as the knot
type, and avoids every potential ambiguity involving a surgery dual.

L_J is nonsplit: if its two unknotted components were split, twisting
D(J) could not change P(J)'s unknot type. But the result P_2(J) is
nontrivial by Section 2.

4. TWO INDEPENDENT RATIONAL PROBES FORCE d=0

View L_J as a shell, with pattern P(J) and axis D(J). Its Q0 input gives
the strongly inverted unknot P(J), hence the normalized output is Q0.
Its Q2 input gives the preferred quotient tangle of P_2(J).

The latter is also Jones-flat: P_2 is a compatible winding-zero pattern
with P_2(U)=U, so the established shell transmission law sends the
Jones-flat quotient of J to Q0. Preferred longitude compatibility follows
from winding zero, and the identification in Section 2 fixes the external
marking.

In the normalized basis (Q0,v), where v=Q_infinity-delta Q0, the shell
map is diag(1,d). The second probe is independent of Q0: its defect is

      x(Q2) = (1-A^8)/delta = A^2(A^4-1),
      delta = -A^2-A^(-2),

which is a nonzero Laurent polynomial. Therefore

             0 = x(output Q2) = d * x(Q2)

forces d=0 in the Laurent polynomial domain. We thus obtain an actual
essential zero-multiplier shell from any nontrivial flat core J.

5. BOUNDARY AND NONCLAIMS

This repairs the earlier failed Whitehead-clasp converse. It does not
contradict the type-C two-puncture locality lemma: no invariant disk with
exactly two punctures is supplied or required. D is a local unknot, but
its spanning disks may meet P in more points or may require a different
equivariant disk model.

The result does not manufacture a flat core from an arbitrary knot with
ordinary Jones polynomial one. Thus it does not establish the generic
Jones conjecture or a generic reduction to unknotting number one. It
establishes the converse within the already-defined anchored-flat/u=1
sector. No claim of novelty or independent verification of every cited
published theorem is intended.
