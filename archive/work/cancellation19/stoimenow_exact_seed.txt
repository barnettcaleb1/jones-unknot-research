31 October2026. Concrete computation; no novelty claim.

SOURCE AND GEOMETRY

A. Stoimenow, Some applications of Tristram--Levine signatures and
relation to Vassiliev invariants, Adv. Math.194(2005),463--484,
equation(10), printed p476, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2004.07.003 .
Primary PDF: https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/stoim2.pdf .
The actual PDF diagram was rendered and visually inspected. The supplied
script reconstructs its n=3 case with all initial commutator signs positive:

    beta3 = sigma1^2 sigma2^2 sigma1^-2 sigma2^-2.

Replace each sigma_i^epsilon by

    sigma_(2i)^epsilon sigma_(2i-1)^epsilon
    sigma_(2i+1)^epsilon sigma_(2i)^epsilon.

This is a six-strand doubled braid with32 crossings. Number its top and
bottom endpoints1,...,6 from left to right. At the top, cups pair(1,2),
(3,4),(5,6). A single horizontal bridge crosses OVER each odd vertical
strand and UNDER each even vertical strand. Connect its left endpoint
around the outside to bottom1 and its right endpoint to bottom6. The
remaining bottom cups pair(2,3),(4,5). The even-strand crossings of the
horizontal bridge are the three marked crossings. This is exactly the
wiring and crossing data shown in the published figure, specialized to3
pairs. It yields a38-crossing one-component diagram of writhe-6.

The script stores its full initial PD and the seven diagrams obtained by
changing every nonempty subset of the three marked crossings. All seven
simplify to an actual crossing-free one-component diagram. This is a
geometric simplification certificate, in addition to the published
3-adjacency theorem; their unknot status is not inferred from Jones=1.

EXACT JONES POLYNOMIAL

In the variable t (Regina's printed x is sqrt(t)), the computed polynomial
is most compactly specified by

 V_K3(t)-1 = -t^-12 (t-1)^7 (t+1)^3 (t^2+1)
                    (t^2+t+1)(t^2-t+1)^2.

Thus K3 is nontrivial, and its Jones polynomial is NOT1. The Jones
calculation from the original38-crossing PD agrees exactly with the
calculation after simplifying the diagram. The complete coefficient list
is in stoimenow_k3.json. Integer Laurent division and independent
multiplication verify the displayed factorization exactly.

THE ACTUAL BRUNNIAN INTERACTION FACTOR

Lift the three reverse crossing arcs after changing all marked crossings
to the unknot. As in brunnian_reduction.txt, this gives an actual nontrivial
componentwise strongly inverted Brunnian triple B in S3. For its preferred
two-input quotient shell, with v=Qinf-delta Q0, write

    F_B(Q0+x*v,Q0+y*v) = Q0 + c_B(A)*x*y*v.

The two-input identity and normalization were independently audited on
the braid track. Full three-component surgery along the lifted crossing
slopes(+/-1/2) gives the branched double cover of K3. Correspondingly the
two input probes are Q_(+/-2), and the remaining output cap is slope+/-1/2.

With delta=-A^2-A^-2:

 x(Q2)=A^2(A^4-1),
 x(Q-2)=-A^-6(A^4-1),
 h_+(A)=A^-10(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1),
 h_-(A)=-A^-2(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1).

Here h_+ and h_- are the normalized output-cap differences on v. Hence,
up to a nonzero signed Laurent monomial and possible simultaneous mirror
convention,

 c_B(A) = [V_K3(t)-1]/[(t-1)^3(t^2+t+1)],  t=A^-4.

Therefore this ACTUAL seed has interaction factor

    c_B  ~  (t-1)^4(t+1)^3(t^2+1)(t^2-t+1)^2
         = (t-1)^4(t+1)(t^2+1)(t^3+1)^2.

The symbol ~ means equality up to a signed A-Laurent monomial (and
consistent choice of global mirror); no claim is made that the phase is
fixed by the displayed formula. Its nonvanishing and all factor
multiplicities are exact. In the preferred A grading c_B has exponents
congruent to2 modulo4, so c_B/A^2 is naturally a Laurent polynomial in t.

For infection by a preferred quotient with defect x(J), the resulting
one-input shell factor is d=c_B*x(J). Since c_B is nonzero and the
coefficient ring is an integral domain, this explicit seed gives a zero
shell if and only if J was already flat. In particular trefoil infection
cannot produce a counterexample. This establishes a complete exact
exclusion for every nonflat companion in this fixed construction, without
bounding crossing number, twist parameters, or finite-type degree.
