PRIMARY SOURCES AND PRECISE SCOPE

1. Hedden–Watson, Does Khovanov homology detect the unknot? (2008)
https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.4423
https://arxiv.org/html/0805.4423v1
Theorem 1: for tangle-unknotting-number-one knots, rank of reduced Kh over F2 is 1 iff the knot is the unknot. Definition 3: a rational tangle replacement makes the unknot; includes u=1. Proof uses rank HFhat(Sigma2 K) <= rank reduced Kh(K) and surgery formulas. This is NOT ordinary Jones detection. Their Lemma 4 relates the class to surgery descriptions; do not silently extract stronger equivariance statements than needed from its converse. Introduction explicitly separates the open Jones problem from their categorified theorem. Inspected theorem and proof setup, not merely abstract.

2. Y. Miyazawa, The Jones polynomial of an unknotting number one knot, Topology Appl. 83(3) (1998), 161–167.
https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-8641(97)00107-7
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166864197001077
Publisher abstract explicitly uses Casson's invariant of the double branched cover to derive relations among Jones, signature and Conway for u=1, obstructing u=1 for some knots. It does not claim unknot detection. This is important PRIOR ART for U4's Casson/Mullins strategy. Abstract retrieved through search; publisher full-text open returned 403; exact interior formulas not audited here.

3. A. Stoimenow, Polynomial values, the linking forms and unknotting numbers, Math. Res. Lett. 11 (2004), 755–769.
https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/mrl/2004/0011/0006/MRL-2004-0011-0006-a004.pdf
Remark 6.1 describes Miyazawa Theorem 8 as relating V'(-1), determinant, and the Conway degree-four Vassiliev coefficient. It compares Miyazawa's obstruction with Traczyk's and linking-form tests. This corroborates that the 1998 paper contains necessary conditions, not a stated detector. Earlier author preprint also inspected:
https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/papers/unkn1.pdf
Title there is The Jones and Q polynomial of an unknotting number one knot, version Nov 4 1999. Theorem 2.1 derives another knot's Jones polynomial from the u=1 skein pair; its tests become vacuous when V_K=1. No claimed generic reduction or u=1 detector found.

4. Y. Miyazawa, Arf invariants of strongly invertible knots obtained from unknotting number one knots, Osaka J. Math. 32 (1995), 193–206.
https://doi.org/10.18910/7378
https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/7378/ojm32_01_14.pdf
Main theorem: Arf(C) = a4(K) mod 2, where C is a surgical strongly invertible knot associated with a chosen unknotting crossing of K, and a4 is Conway's z^4 coefficient. PDF OCR garbles theorem subscript, but p205 example explicitly confirms a4. This is another prior-art connection between K and the auxiliary kappa, not a detection theorem.

5. A. S. Sikora, Tangle Equations, the Jones conjecture, slopes of surfaces in tangle complements, and q-deformed rationals, Canad. J. Math. 76 (2024), 707–727; arXiv v2 Aug 5 2023.
https://arxiv.org/html/2005.08162v2
https://doi.org/10.4153/S0008414X23000755
ArXiv Theorem 24(c): generic Jones conjecture is equivalent to bracket equality with a rational tangle forcing equality of framed tangles. This is unrestricted rational-tangle detection; it does not add an actual unknot closure or supply Lemma Q. Theorem 1.3 in published numbering: Jones implies uniqueness of a rational solution among all solutions to TWO framed tangle equations. The converse is not asserted. One third-party search synopsis incorrectly reverses this implication; ignore it. Full-text search found no occurrence of 'unknotting'.

6. Sikora IPAM talk abstract, Oct 15 2026, Tangle Equations, the Jones Unknot Conjecture, and Lens Space Surgeries.
https://www.mathinstitutes.org/videos/25091
Abstract addresses systems N(X+T1)=L1, N(X+T2)=L2 and framed rational-solution uniqueness. A failure disproves Jones; branched covers of the unframed version give lens-space surgery questions and possible counterexample guidance. No assertion of a one-actual-filling theorem, Floer simplicity from bracket data, Lemma Q, or u=1 detection. Only abstract inspected; video not audited. Thus it does not fill U5.

7. S. Jabuka, The signature of an even symmetric form with vanishing associated linking form (2012).
https://arxiv.org/html/1204.4965v2
Theorem 1.1 explicitly states that an even unimodular symmetric integral form has signature divisible by 8. For a classical knot with determinant 1, its symmetrized Seifert matrix is even unimodular, giving sigma(K)=0 mod 8. This supplies a primary literature statement for the algebraic theorem behind U4's mod-8 step. No novelty claim warranted for this step.

https://www.math.ubc.ca/node/3411
Claims no rational closure of the (3,-2) pretzel tangle has the unknot Jones polynomial, using immersed-curve invariants of Kotelskiy–Watson–Zibrowius. This is a specific-tangle result, not Lemma Q in general. Only abstract inspected; no proof audit. Relevant as a narrow example of the desired mechanism if separately assigned later.

9. K3 problem-list Apr2026, p36 (Gujral–Wang result).
https://bpb-us-e2.wpmucdn.com/websites.umass.edu/dist/b/22144/files/2026/04/K3-problem-list-watermarked.pdf
The u=1 corollary concerns Shumakovitch's torsion conjecture: if K has no 2-torsion in Kh and J is obtained by a proper rational tangle replacement, reduced Kh rank(K)<=rank(J). This needs information beyond Euler characteristic. It is not ordinary Jones detection.
