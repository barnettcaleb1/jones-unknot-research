1. The fixed raw integer-twist family needs an orientation hypothesis

Put delta=-A^2-A^-2. In the horizontal-composition TL_2 convention, write
0 for the identity tangle and infinity for the cup-cap, so infinity^2=
delta infinity. The positive raw integer crossing is

 b=A 0+A^-1 infinity,

and reduced numerator closure satisfies N(0)=delta, N(infinity)=1.
For [X]=f 0+g infinity, direct multiplication gives

 F_+=<N(X+1)>=-A^3 f-A^-3 g,
 F_-=<N(X-1)>=-A^-3 f-A^3 g.

Let s be the oriented crossing sign of the RAW +1 drawing, and w the
outside writhe. Thus s=+1 or -1, and the two total writhes are w+s,w-s.
Since both normalized Jones polynomials equal 1,

 F_+=(-A^3)^(w+s), F_-=(-A^3)^(w-s).

The two closure functionals are independent over Q(A). Writing u=(-A^3)^w,
their unique solution is

 s=+1: [X]=u 0;
 s=-1: [X]=u infinity.

Thus the rational bracket conclusion follows directly and does NOT depend
on an all-twists inference. However the integer-twist families differ:

 * In the first case their normalized Jones polynomials equal those of
   N(0+(2n-1))=T(2,2n-1).
 * In the second case their normalized Jones polynomials equal 1 for all n.

The exact countermodel to the unconditional algebraic inference is X=0:
N(X-1) and N(X+1) are both actual unknots, whereas N(X+3) is a trefoil.
This tests the inference from two Jones-one endpoints. It is NOT a genuine
counterexample to the strict hypothesis u(K)=1, since its endpoint K=U.
It shows why EARLY-02's antiparallel/fixed-smoothing condition cannot be
discarded when using that theorem.

One can choose the other crossing-ball marking (a quarter-turn exchanges
the two smoothing directions and interchanges the signs of the crossing
caps). With sign/reindexing chosen to retain K_0=U and K_1=K, the twist
disk can be taken antiparallel. It is essential to state this choice;
the resulting family need not be the original raw N(X+(2n-1)) family.

The statement about a scalar annular crossing-circle pattern is a further
application of the previously checked EARLY-02/LATE-31 results to this
antiparallel, algebraic-winding-zero family. It should not be conflated
with the relative tangle equality [X]=u infinity: the objects and skein
modules are different. In the old annular normalization that conclusion
is [P]=delta; the relative equality has a framing unit u.

2. Preferred slopes give a clean, coordinate-independent repair

Let M=Sigma_2(X). The actual unknot closure gives an actual S^3 filling.
Removing its filling solid torus exhibits M=E(kappa), with meridian mu.
The deck involution gives the usual strong inversion of kappa. Let lambda
be its preferred longitude.

The other crossing cap alpha has Delta(alpha,mu)=2. Therefore

 alpha=p mu+2 epsilon lambda, epsilon=+/-1, p odd.

Since |H_1(M(alpha))|=det(K)=|V_K(-1)|=1, |p|=1. Slopes are unoriented,
so after changing the representative's overall sign we can write

 alpha=mu+2 epsilon lambda.

Consequently the original endpoint K, independently of any twist-family
claim, has branched double cover S^3_{epsilon/2}(kappa).

The oriented smoothing L_0 satisfies

 V_(L_0)=-(t^(1/2)+t^(-1/2))

by the oriented Jones skein relation with V_+=V_-=1. In particular its
determinant is zero. Its filling slope is distance one from mu, so in the
knot exterior this is exactly lambda. This independently identifies the
preferred smoothing direction.

Define the REPAIRED family by the rational caps

 alpha_n=mu+2 epsilon n lambda, n in Z;
 K_n'=branch link of M(alpha_n).

These are knots: their cap connectivity is constant modulo 2, as for the
two original odd crossing caps. They satisfy K_0'=U and K_1'=K, and

 Sigma_2(K_n')=S^3_{epsilon/(2n)}(kappa)

with n=0 interpreted as meridional filling. This is the antiparallel twist
family after an appropriate crossing-ball marking. The direct rational
bracket equality above, in this preferred marking, identifies all its
closure brackets with the rational reference tangle's brackets up to a
framing monomial. The reference is the solid-torus quotient with meridian
closure U; each numerator-one filling gives a two-bridge knot of
determinant one, hence U. Normalization leaves a monomial knot Jones
polynomial; V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0 force that monomial to be 1. Equivalently,
apply the already checked antiparallel skein recurrence. Thus all K_n'
have Jones polynomial 1.

For comparison, BEFORE repairing the marking, raw caps have the form

 alpha_n=mu+2n beta,
 beta=a mu+s' lambda, s'=+/-1,
 alpha_n=(1+2an)mu+2s'n lambda.

The endpoint determinant condition gives |1+2a|=1, hence a=0 or a=-1.
The first branch has numerator 1; the second has numerator 1-2n. The
latter is precisely the overlooked possibility. At n=1 both still give
a +/-1/2 surgery, so this error does not damage U4.

The equivalence kappa nontrivial iff X nonrational uses the standard
classification of tangles with solid-torus double branched cover and the
fact that a knot in S^3 has solid-torus exterior iff it is the unknot.
It does not follow from bracket equality alone.

3. U4 is valid; its proof is independent of the all-n correction

Let B be a Seifert matrix for K. B+B^T is even integral and, since det K=1,
unimodular. The signature of an even unimodular integral form is 0 modulo
8. Hence sigma(K)=0 modulo 8. The crossing-change signature bound gives
|sigma(K)|<=2u(K)=2, so sigma(K)=0.

In the normalization used by Greene--Watson, Mullins' formula is

 lambda_G(Sigma_2 K)=-V'_K(-1)/(6 V_K(-1))+sigma(K)/4.

The input V_K=1 and sigma(K)=0 makes this zero. Their lambda_G is twice
the integer-valued Casson normalization on integral homology spheres;
vanishing is unaffected by that convention.

In the integer-valued Casson normalization,

 lambda(S^3_(1/m)(kappa))=(m/2) Delta_kappa''(1).

Our endpoint is +/-1/2 surgery, so m=+/-2. Therefore

 0=lambda(Sigma_2 K)=+/-Delta_kappa''(1)=+/-2 a_2(kappa),

and a_2(kappa)=0. Overall orientation/sign conventions do not affect the
conclusion. No Floer simplicity, second actual filling, or generic Jones
unknot theorem is used here.
