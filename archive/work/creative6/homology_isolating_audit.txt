2026-10-31. Audit by homology agent of the tangle agent's proposed lemma.

Verdict: the local diagram and product-handle argument are sound. Combined
with the cited isolating decomposition, the lemma excludes ISOLATING M1
from the rank-five Jones-one case. It does not exclude nonisolating M1.

1. I independently inspected work/creative6/juhasz-fig3.png. The region D
between alpha and beta has one boundary component, two convex corners,
one handle, and no boundary punctures. Hence chi(D)=-1,
e(D)=-1-2/4=-3/2, and n_x+n_y=1/2. Its Maslov index is -1.
Putting a handles in that same region changes only chi(D), to1-2a,
so the index is1-2a. The region remains disjoint from the boundary and
connects the two intersection generators. The positive domain has negative
index for a>=1, and the reverse domain has negative multiplicities, so no
index-one differential kills these two classes. The two classes lie in
the same Spin^c structure, in contrast to the bare four-longitude torus.

2. N_a=V union(F_{a,1} x I) is F_{a,1} x I with three parallel sutures:
the longitudinally attached solid torus is an annular collar. The generalized
Figure3 diagram gives this model by the same collar decomposition as in
Juhasz Example7.5. It has H_2=0, so the relative Maslov grading is integer.

3. The remaining F_{b,4} really does have a relative handle decomposition
starting with THREE incoming annular collars and adding2b+2 surface
one-handles. The first two connect the three components, producing a
four-boundary sphere; the remaining2b handles come in genus-adding pairs.
All feet can be placed on outgoing boundaries. After multiplying by I,
each attaching disk meets the sutures in one arc: these are product
three-dimensional one-handles. Thus their cocores are product disks.
This validates the crucial final gluing step, beyond a mere Euler count.
Any annular gluing twists can be absorbed into the collar identifications;
the relative handle construction does not assume a canonical marking.

4. Product-disk decompositions preserve total SFH rank. The relative
Maslov grading of the two same-Spin^c classes is preserved by the
decomposition map, precisely as Juhasz Proposition5.4(1) states. Applying
the Seifert-surface decomposition then gives the top HFK Maslov gap2a-1.
Sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0802.3415, Example7.5 and Prop5.4.

             top gap=2a-1 <=2g-3.
The independently checked rank-five sparse HFK argument requires gap2g-1.
These are incompatible. Thus no rank-five Jones-one candidate has the
isolating M1 geometry described in that classification.
