Statistical mechanics / electrical networks: a precise bridge and its limits

Most useful direction

Treat the full Jones polynomial as an interacting deformation of a determinantal
spanning-tree observable at t=-1. The determinant point is tractable electrical
network theory; the activity deformation contains the difficult extra information.
Do not assume that the deformation remains determinantal or positive: exact tests
below refute both assumptions, even for actual small knots and an actual unknot.

1. Exact electrical-network identity

Let G be a connected signed Tait graph, B a reduced vertex-edge incidence matrix,
N its negative edges, and L=BB^t the unsigned reduced Laplacian. Put
  tau=det L, K_N=B_N^t L^-1 B_N.
The signed reduced Laplacian is L_s=L-2B_N B_N^t. The matrix-tree theorem and
matrix determinant lemma give
  det L_s = sum_spanning_trees T (-1)^|T intersect N|
          = tau det(I-2K_N).
Its absolute value is the knot determinant |V(-1)|. Equivalently this is the
parity expectation of a determinantal uniform-spanning-tree process.

Every eigenvalue of K_N lies in [0,1]. If r eigenvalues are strictly between 0
and 1 and all satisfy |1-2lambda| >= epsilon, then
  det(knot) >= tau epsilon^r.
Thus tau epsilon^r>1 is an exact conditional certificate ruling out V=1.
This is useful for structural graph families with a provable spectral gap; it
is not a generic detector. Determinant-one knots can approach the half-eigenvalue
precisely enough to attain equality.

Actual tests, all independently checked against Regina Jones polynomials:
  trefoil: tau=3, determinant=3.
  figure-eight: tau=5, determinant=5.
  8_19: tau=35; negative-edge count polynomial 4+16z+15z^2.
  Conway 11n34 and Kinoshita--Terasaka 11n42:
    tau=155; count polynomial 77z^2+78z^3; signed count=-1.
For each of the last two diagrams the nontrivial transfer-current eigenvalue is
78/155, at distance 1/310 from 1/2. The remaining eigenvalues are 0 or 1.
Thus even a single nontrivial spectral mode already permits determinant one.

2. Exact interacting deformation

The spanning-tree activity expansion writes V(t)=sum_T epsilon_T t^j(T).
At t=-1, omega_T=epsilon_T(-1)^j(T) is a diagram-wide sign eta times
(-1)^|T intersect N|. Hence, exactly as a finite sum,
  V(-exp(h)) = eta tau E_uniform_tree[(-1)^|T intersect N| exp(h j(T))].
In particular V'(-1)=-sum_T omega_T j(T). Jones one would require the signed
activity measure to be a single atom at zero, not merely its total mass to be one.

The bridge to three-manifold topology is established, not speculative: Mullins'
formula relates V'(-1)/V(-1), together with the knot signature, to the
Casson--Walker invariant of the branched double cover. In the normalization
lambda(Poincare sphere)=-2, it is
  lambda(Sigma_2 K) = signature(K)/4 - V'(-1)/(6 V(-1)).

For the displayed KT/Conway diagrams V(-1)=1 and V'(-1)=48. Their signed first
activity moment is -48. This detects the mismatch hidden by 78-77=1.
But T(3,5)=10_124 has V(-1)=1 and V'(-1)=0, while V''(-1)=-48. Even the first
response is therefore insufficient. Its polynomial is t^4+t^6-t^10.

3. Two tempting positivity shortcuts are false

Edge-factorized deformation. Suppose one could write every Jones tree term as
  W_T(t)=C(t) product_(e in T) w_e(t).
Then two pairs of trees with the same total edge incidence must have the same
product of weights. For KT, the verifier records four concrete trees with equal
total incidence, but their Jones exponent sums are -5 and -4 and their sign
products are -1 and +1. The ratio is -t^-1, not 1. Thus no such edge weighting
on the original Tait graph exists for this activity expansion. The ratio becomes
1 exactly at t=-1: the obstruction is an interaction appearing away from the
determinantal point. This does not rule out different expanded-graph formulas.

Real stability. Even if signs are discarded, the positive activity deformation
  P_u(z_e)=sum_T u^j(T) product_(e in T) z_e
need not be strongly Rayleigh. For 8_19 at u=1/2 and edges 0,3, the exact values
are Z=67/128, Z_0=43/128, Z_3=19/64, Z_03=25/128, giving
  Z_0 Z_3 - Z Z_03 = -41/16384 < 0.
KT gives another exact violation, -61065/32. Trefoil and figure-eight pass the
tested fields, so testing only the simplest examples would conceal the failure.

An actual eight-crossing unknot also fails edge factorization and Rayleigh
positivity. Its braid word is
  sigma1 sigma2 sigma1 sigma2^-1 sigma1^-1 sigma2^-1 sigma1 sigma2.
The first six letters are the identity by the braid relation, so its closure is
the unknot. Its displayed diagram has no immediate type-I/II simplification
(eight crossings remain), although global simplification gives zero crossings.
Therefore Jones one does not force an immediate local graph reduction, and
activity interaction or loss of stability alone cannot certify nontriviality.

4. A constructive target that survives these tests

Replace the desired global determinant by a controlled interaction expansion.
If j(T) has a representation on spanning trees
  j(T)=sum_(S subset E, |S|<=d) a_S product_(e in S) 1[e in T],
the first response can be computed from at most d-edge Green-function minors.
Indeed let W=diag(edge signs), L_s=BWB^t, and
  K_s=W B^t L_s^-1 B.
For the normalized signed tree measure, the algebraic determinant identity gives
  E_signed[product_(e in S) 1[e in T]] = det K_s[S],
so
  E_signed[j]=sum_S a_S det K_s[S].
Products of this expression similarly give every fixed activity moment. No
positivity is claimed: K_s is generally non-Hermitian. When det L_s=+/-1,
its entries and all principal minors are integers, permitting modular
nonvanishing certificates analogous to the successful braid-jet obstruction.

Exact first falsifiable test completed: solve for such an interaction energy
by rational linear algebra, and independently reconstruct the moments from
the Green-function minors. For the fixed diagrams and edge orders tested:
  8_19: minimum degree 3; 27 nonzero interaction coefficients.
  10_124: minimum degree 2; 10 coefficients; signed moments 0,-48.
  KT: minimum degree 3; 45 coefficients; signed moments -48,96.
  eight-crossing unknot: minimum degree 4; 31 coefficients; moments 0,0.
All values agree exactly with direct tree enumeration and Jones derivatives.
There is no claim that interaction degree is invariant or detects knots.

The missing theorem is a structural bound or cancellation constraint for these
interactions that survives diagram moves and excludes every nontrivial knot
with all signed moments trivial. A reasonable narrower target is a class of
signed/ribbon graphs with bounded interaction complexity and an arithmetic
obstruction to vanishing moments. Merely asserting real stability is false.
Already-known almost-alternating/Turaev-genus-one detection should be used as
a benchmark, not advertised as a new target; genuinely new results must go
beyond those classes or provide a new general certificate.
