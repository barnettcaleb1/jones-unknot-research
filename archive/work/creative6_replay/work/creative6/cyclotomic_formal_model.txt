FORMAL COLORED-SEQUENCE COUNTERMODEL, 31 OCTOBER 2026

Purpose: test whether cyclotomic integrality, q-holonomicity, and the leading
Alexander/loop compatibility can by themselves promote ordinary Jones=1 to
all colored Jones polynomials=1. They cannot. This is NOT a knot, and is NOT
a counterexample to Jones unknot detection.

Use the dimension-N convention, so ordinary normalized Jones has N=2.
Put
  P(N,k;q) = product_{j=1}^k (q^N+q^(-N)-q^j-q^(-j)).
Let a_k be the integer coefficients of
  sum_{k>=0} a_k s^k = 1/(1+4s^2+s^3).
Thus a_0=1, a_1=0, a_2=-4, a_3=-1, a_4=16, and
  a_k + 4a_(k-2) + a_(k-3) = 0
with negative-index coefficients zero, for k>=1.
Define
  F_N(q) = sum_{k=0}^{N-1} a_k P(N,k;q).

Exact consequences:
1. Each F_N is an integer Laurent polynomial; P(N,k)=0 for k>=N.
2. F_1=F_2=1, but F_3=1-4P(3,2) is not 1.
3. F_N(1)=1, F_N'(1)=0, and F_N(q)=F_N(q^(-1)).
4. The family is q-holonomic. The sequence a_k has a constant-coefficient
   linear recurrence and is therefore q-holonomic. The kernel is
   q-hypergeometric, and the q-holonomic closure properties under products
   and finite summation apply. This is an existence argument for a recurrence,
   not a computed minimal annihilator.
5. In the formal loop limit q=exp(h), t=q^N held as an independent variable,
   each kernel factor has constant h coefficient t+t^(-1)-2. Hence the
   h^0 series is 1/Delta(t), where, writing s=t+t^(-1)-2,
   Delta(t)=1+4s^2+s^3
           =t^3+t^(-3)-2t^2-2t^(-2)-t-t^(-1)+5.
   This is a formal expansion around t=1, not an asserted analytic limit.
6. Delta is symmetric, Delta(1)=Delta(-1)=1, and Delta''(1)=0.
   Thus it passes the basic Alexander normalization, determinant-one, and
   vanishing degree-two Conway tests. This does not identify a knot realizing
   F, even though Delta has the familiar algebraic shape of an Alexander
   polynomial.

The precise conclusion is that the properties in items 1-6 do not imply
F_3=1 from F_2=1. No claim is made that F satisfies every knot-specific
weight-system identity, all higher loop constraints, or the Frobenius
gluing conditions of the 2026 unified invariant. In particular, this does
not refute a geometric rigidity theorem specifically for actual knots
with Jones=1.

The standard-library script cyclotomic_formal_model.py checks the identities
for N=1,...,15 and the recurrence through k=23 by exact integer/rational
arithmetic. The arguments above, rather than the finite checks, establish
the assertions for all N.

Research target made more precise: characterize which cyclotomic sequences
are geometrically realizable, and whether geometric realizability forces
a_1(q)=0 => a_2(q)=0. Ordinary Jones1 sets a_1(q)=0; it does not constrain
the higher coefficients by formal interpolation alone.
