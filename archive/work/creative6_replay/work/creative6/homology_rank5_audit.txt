RANK-FIVE JONES-ONE CANDIDATES: AUDITED RESTRICTED CONSEQUENCES
Audit date: 2026-10-31. This is a deduction from cited results, not a proof that
the Jones polynomial detects the unknot. One input is a December 2025 preprint.

CONVENTIONS
K is a classical knot in S^3. V_U=1. Write r=dim_Q Kh_red(K;Q),
R=dim_Q Kh(K;Q), both ordinary even Khovanov homologies. The graded Euler
characteristics are V_K(q^2) and (q+q^-1)V_K(q^2), respectively, up to the
harmless convention q -> q^-1. KHI is instanton knot homology over C.
I^natural and I^sharp(S^3,K) are reduced and unreduced singular instanton
homology. I^sharp(S^3,K) must not be confused with I^sharp(S^3), the framed
invariant with no knot. All total dimensions ignore grading shifts/mirroring.

RESTRICTED THEOREM
Suppose V_K=1, K is nontrivial, and r=5. Using Bhat--Li--Ye Theorem 1.4,
the following are necessary:
 (i) Delta_K=1; K is nonfibered; g(K)>=2.
 (ii) KHI(K)=C^2 in Alexander grading g, C in grading 0, and C^2 in
      grading -g, with no other support. Each outer summand has Euler
      characteristic zero. The guts are a solid torus with four longitudes.
 (iii) dim_C I^sharp(S^3,K;C)=10 and R=10.
 (iv) The generalized Lee complex over Q[X] has exactly two noncontractible
      torsion blocks, and their exponents are both >=2.
 (v) Every proper rational tangle replacement K -> J satisfies
      dim_Q Kh_red(J;Q)>=5. In particular K cannot be obtained from the
      unknot or a trefoil by a single proper rational tangle replacement.

PROOF AND SOURCE AUDIT
1. Baldwin--Sivek, An instanton take on some knot detection results,
   Propositions 2.3--2.4, gives dim KHI=5 and the possible Alexander supports.
   In the nonfibered case symmetry and top-rank detection force (ii); the
   Euler characteristic forces Delta=1. In the fibered genus>=2 case the
   five one-dimensional summands lie in +/-g, +/-(g-1), 0. Their Alexander
   polynomial has the form a(t^g+t^-g)+b(t^(g-1)+t^(1-g))+c, with a,b,c=+/-1.
   Delta(1)=1 forces c=1 and b=-a, so Delta(-1)=1+4a(-1)^g is 5 or -3.
   The genus-one fibered case is the figure eight (determinant 5).
   Thus determinant 1 excludes the fibered cases.
   Source: https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/7b21593a-d136-413c-879a-411a7d85de92/content
   Exact short statement (Prop2.3, p4): "Then KHI(K; Q) has total dimension 5."
   In this paper its symbol Kh denotes REDUCED homology, as specified in
   Theorems1.1--1.2. Passing Q -> C does not alter dimensions.

2. Li--Ye, The guts of nearly fibered knots, Theorem1.4 and Remark2.1,
   classifies the top-instanton-rank-two case. The top Euler characteristic
   zero forces model M1; M2 and M3 have top Euler characteristic +/-2.
   Example2.2 excludes M1 in genus one, proving g>=2 without any change
   of Floer theory or coefficient field.
   Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.05382 (v3, Jan1,2026), pp2,6--7.
   Published: Algebraic & Geometric Topology26(2026),1385--1393.
   Exact short statement (M1): "M is a solid torus and gamma consists of four longitudes."

3. Bhat--Li--Ye, Instanton 2-torsion and fibered knots, Theorem1.4,
   expresses I^sharp(S^3,K;C) as the homology of the mapping cone of
   d_1^+ + d_1^-: KHI(K) -> KHI(K). Their proof of Proposition1.10
   explicitly gives Alexander shifts +/-1. The support {-g,0,g}, g>=2,
   has no adjacent integers, so both maps vanish. The zero-map cone has
   dimension 2 dim KHI=10.
   The introduction explicitly cites KM Theorem8.2 for the spectral sequence
   from UNREDUCED Kh(mirror K;Z) to this I^sharp(S^3,K;Z). Extension to C
   gives R>=10; there is no local-coefficient substitution in this argument.
   Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24206v1 (Dec30,2025 preprint), Thm1.4,
   introduction paragraph1 and proof of Prop1.10.
   Exact short phrase: "the (unreduced) Khovanov homology".
   This audit verifies the statement, conventions and its application; it
   does not independently reconstruct all analytic proofs in that preprint.

4. The standard reduced/unreduced exact triangle gives R<=2r=10.
   This is an inequality over Q (or C), not the F2 direct-sum theorem.
   Combining it with step3 gives equality R=10.
   Source: Baldwin--Sivek, Khovanov homology detects the trefoils,
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.07634, p2, displayed general facts (1),(2).
   The unreduced dimension is unchanged by mirroring over a field.

5. Schuetz describes the based generalized Lee complex over Q[X] as a
   direct sum of one free Q[X] piece and two-term pieces with map X^k.
   Contractible k=0 pieces may be removed. Setting X=0 gives ordinary
   reduced Kh, so if m is the number of k>=1 pieces, r=1+2m.
   Setting T=X^2=0 gives ordinary unreduced Kh. A k=1 piece contributes
   dimension2; each k>=2 piece contributes dimension4. Consequently
               R=2r-2m_1,
   where m_1 counts k=1 pieces. Thus r5,R10 implies m=2,m_1=0.
   Source for decomposition: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.05156, Section2.1,
   equation(1), p2; Section3 identifies the torsion exponents with those
   of reduced rational Bar--Natan homology. Formula above is elementary
   specialization of those blocks, not an additional theorem assumed.

6. Gujral--Wang's proof supplies Q[H]-module maps f,g for a proper rational
   tangle replacement, with fg=gf=H. Their Lemma3 says that if every torsion
   exponent on the K side is at least2, the torsion-summand count cannot
   decrease. The reduced rank equals1+2 times that count, proving (v).
   Their published theorem assumes absence of integral2-torsion only to
   deduce absence of exponent-one summands; here step5 gives that directly.
   Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.06163, Lemma3 and proof of Theorem1.
   Published AGT25(2025),4073--4075. This strengthened application uses the
   explicit proof, not the converse of their 2-torsion implication.

WHAT DOES NOT FOLLOW
R=10 and r=5 are algebraically compatible. It is not proved that every
nontrivial knot has a length-one rational Bar--Natan torsion summand.
The published Gujral--Wang discussion raises precisely that question.
Likewise Bhat--Li--Ye Question1.13 asks nonvanishing of d_1^+ and d_1^-
for nontrivial S^3 knots; our hypothetical rank-five candidate would answer
it negatively. Neither question is an available theorem.
Absence of length-one rational bars does NOT by itself imply that integral
Khovanov homology has no2-torsion: only the opposite-direction implication
is used by Gujral--Wang. No such converse is claimed here.

EXPLICIT FORMAL MODULE MODELS AND THE REAL GEOMETRIC OBSTRUCTION
Let A=Z[X,h]/(X^2-hX), deg X=deg h=-2. Let U=q A in homological degree0.
For k>=1 let B_k be q A -> q^(1+2k) A in degrees0,1 with differential
(2X-h)^k. Let C_k=U direct-sum B_k direct-sum B_k[1], where [1] raises
both homological positions by1. These are ABSTRACT complexes; none is
claimed to arise from a classical knot diagram.
For every k, their ordinary reduced Euler characteristic is1, and their
unreduced Euler characteristic isq+q^-1. At h=1 each B_k is contractible
because (2X-1)^2=1, leaving rank2. The involution X -> h-X extends to a
chain involution by multiplying the target of B_k by(-1)^k.
For k=1, rational unreduced Poincare polynomial is
 q+q^-1 + q^-1(1+t)(1+t q^4), rank6;
reduced polynomial is1+(1+t)(1+t q^2), rank5.
There are precisely two Z/2 torsion groups, in bidegrees(1,1),(2,1).
Thus this model passes Euler, Lee, module, and simple conjugation tests,
but steps1--4 prove it cannot be realized by a classical knot.
For k>=2, ordinary unreduced differential is zero, giving rank10 and
reduced rank5. These avoid the newly proved rank obstruction while still
being merely formal. This is the remaining geometric realization gap.

RESEARCH TARGET NARROWED
It suffices for the rank-five case to prove that a classical knot whose
guts are M1 and whose KHI has support {-g,0,g} has a length-one rational
Bar--Natan torsion summand (or a nonzero instanton d_1 map). The existing
results alone do not establish this. The general Jones unknot problem
also permits reduced rank7,9,... and remains untouched by the rank-five
assumption. This note makes no novelty or general-resolution claim.

ADDENDUM: THE DOWLIN GRADING BRIDGE ALSO HAS AN EXPLICIT FORMAL ESCAPE
Dowlin's spectral sequence is over Q, from reduced Kh(K) to HFK(mirror K).
Its delta conventions are q-2h for Kh and2M-2A for HFK, identified up to
an overall shift. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.07848, Theorem1.1 and
the following paragraph on p1. No absolute-shift claim is needed below.
Li--Ye's top-rank equivalence and Dowlin's total-rank inequality force
HFK total rank5 in the restricted case, hence that spectral sequence
collapses in dimension and its delta multisets agree up to shift.

Sparse HFK of rank5 and Delta1 has two top Maslov degrees a,b of opposite
parity, two bottom degrees a-2g,b-2g, and one central degree c. The
HFK-to-HFhat spectral sequence must cancel two pairs. A central survivor
would require both top classes to kill the bottom classes; adding the two
Maslov-drop-one equations gives4g=2, impossible. After mirroring we may
put the surviving generator at Alexander g and Maslov0. The other top
degree is then1-2g, the center is2-4g, and the bottom degrees are-2g and
1-4g. Thus the half-delta multiset is
                  {-g,-g,1-3g,1-3g,2-4g}.
This elementary pairing argument was independently checked by the braid
agent. It assumes the usual degree-one homological drop in the HFK-to-HF
spectral sequence and strictly decreasing Alexander grading.

For EVERY g>=2, the following abstract reduced Kh polynomial realizes
exactly that multiset while satisfying Euler1:
 P_g=q^(-2g)+t^(4g-2)+t^(4g-1)q^(2g)
                  +t^(2g-1)q^(-2g)+t^(2g)q^(2g).
The free Lee class is at (h,q)=(0,-2g). One bar runs
 (4g-2,0) -> (4g-1,2g), of X-length g;
the other runs (2g-1,-2g) -> (2g,2g), of X-length2g.
Both lengths are>=2, so its unreduced rank is10. The cancellation
 q^(-2g)+1-q^(2g)-q^(-2g)+q^(2g)=1
is exact. Its s=-2g obeys the genus bound; because Dowlin targets the
mirror, the formal tau(K)=-g is compatible with s(K)=-2g.

An abstract bifiltered CFK-infinity complex also realizes the HFK pattern.
Over Q[U,U^-1], choose generators a,b,c,a',b', with all first filtrations0,
Alexander gradings g,g,0,-g,-g and Maslov gradings
0,1-2g,2-4g,-2g,1-4g. Define
 d a=d a'=0,
 d b=a'+U^g a,
 d b'=U^g a'+U^(2g) a,
 d c=b'-U^g b.
The differential squares tozero and lowers Maslov by1 and both filtrations.
Its homology is Q[U,U^-1]. The hat differential is b->a', c->b', leavinga.
The filtration-exchanging chain involution sends
 a->U^-g a', a'->U^g a, b->U^-g b', b'->U^g b, c->-c.
These identities are checked for arbitrary g by exact affine arithmetic in
homology_formal_certificate.py and its JSON output. This is NOT a claim
of knot realization or satisfaction of all possible knot-Floer operations.

Optional integral torsion does not rescue a purely abstract argument:
one can append an abstract Koszul complex over A on the sequence(2,h),
 A -> A direct-sum q^2 A -> q^2 A,
with maps (2,h) and(-h,2). It is contractible rationally and at h=1,
but at h=0 has nonzero2-torsion. This leaves the rational model intact.
No claim is made that this augmented complex is a knot complex.

ADDENDUM: REFUTED MASLOV-GAP SHORTCUT IN THE GUTS
It would be useful if the two generators of SFH(M1) intrinsically had
relative Maslov gap1 and annulus gluing preserved that gap. The premise is
wrong. Juhasz, The sutured Floer homology polytope, Proposition9.1,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0802.3415, p34, says the two generators of
SFH(T(1,0;4)) lie in DISTINCT Spin^c structures, differing by the solid
torus core generator. Thus there is no intrinsic integer relative Maslov
grading between them to carry through gluing. The gluing may identify
their ambient Spin^c data, but the resulting gap requires extra analysis.
This failure was independently found by the tangle agent.

SMALL-RANK COROLLARY
Any nontrivial V=1 knot has R>=8, using the preprint input above. Indeed
r is odd and at least5 (rank1 detects the unknot; rank3 detects trefoils).
If r5 then R10. Otherwise r>=7 and the block formula gives R>=r+1>=8.
If R=8, necessarily r=7 and all three torsion bars have length1.
The reduced Euler identity is q^s+(1-q^2)sum_i epsilon_i q^(a_i)=1.
Differentiation at q1 gives s=2sum_i epsilon_i, hence s is one of
{-6,-2,2,6}. No contradiction for this rank-eight case has been proved.
