ISOLATING M1: A RELATIVE MASLOV GAP COMPUTATION
2026-10-31. Proposed deduction for independent audit. It does NOT cover the
non-isolating M1 case and is not a general Jones detection theorem.

Claim. Suppose a minimal genus Seifert surface S for a knot K in S3 has
isolating M1 guts. Write the two product fibers as F_a and F_b, with boundary
counts1 and4, respectively, and genera a>=1,b>=0. Then g(K)=a+b+1 and the two
top HFK classes have absolute Maslov difference 2a-1. In particular the
difference is at most2g(K)-3.

GEOMETRIC INPUT
Baker--Manjarrez-Gutierrez, arXiv:2305.08928v2, Proposition2.9 and Section5.1,
gives precisely this decomposition into a solid torus V with four longitudinal
sutures, F_a x I attached along one suture, and F_b x I attached along the
other three. The fourth boundary of F_b is the knot suture. Euler characteristic
gives chi(S)=chi(F_a)+chi(F_b)=1-2a-2-2b, hence g=a+b+1.

LOCAL SUTURED MANIFOLD
Let N_a=V union_(annulus) (F_a x I). The attaching annulus is a longitude on V
and the unique vertical boundary of F_a x I. Absorbing V as a boundary collar
shows that N_a is F_a x I with THREE parallel copies of its boundary suture.

Juhasz, The sutured Floer homology polytope, Example7.5 and Figure3,
https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-3/gt-v14-n3-p02-s.pdf, pp1343-1345,
gives an explicit balanced diagram for N_1. The source explicitly identifies
it as the four-longitude solid torus glued to punctured-torus x I along one
suture. We inspected the actual Figure3 pixels, saved as juhasz-fig3.png.

The diagram surface has genus1 and THREE boundary circles. Its alpha and beta
curves intersect in exactly two points x,y. The CENTRAL region D between the
curves is a bigon with a handle: a genus1 surface with one boundary component
and two convex corners. It contains none of the three boundary punctures.
The other three regions each contain a boundary puncture. The source says
D gives a Whitney disk class from one generator to the other and that there
are no periodic domains. Thus x,y occupy the same Spin^c structure.

For arbitrary a>=1 replace the one handle INSIDE D by a handles, leaving
alpha, beta, all their intersections, and the three boundary circles intact.
This gives the identical collar construction with F_a in place of F_1,
hence a diagram for N_a. The central region D_a now has chi(D_a)=1-2a,
but exactly the same two convex corners and multiplicities. There are still
no nonzero periodic domains: the manifold N_a is the handlebody F_a x I,
so H2(N_a)=0. The two generators remain in the same relative Spin^c class.

LIPSHITZ INDEX
For the domain with multiplicity1 on D_a and0 elsewhere,
 e(D_a)=chi(D_a)-2/4=1/2-2a,
 n_x(D_a)=n_y(D_a)=1/4,
 so mu(D_a)=e(D_a)+n_x(D_a)+n_y(D_a)=1-2a.
For a=1 this is -1, checked directly on the displayed diagram. Each additional
handle lowers the index by2. Reversing the two generators changes the sign.
The only positive domain is D_a and has negative index; its negative has
negative multiplicities. Hence there is no holomorphic index1 differential.
Equivalently SFH(N_a) has its two generators with relative gap2a-1.

REATTACHING THE REMAINING PRODUCT REGION
Build the genus-b,four-boundary surface F_b from THREE annular collars of the
incoming (glued) circles by 2b+2 surface1handles. Two handles connect the three
collars into one component; each pair of the other2b handles adds a genus.
All feet lie on the outgoing boundaries, so thickening by I turns every one
into a PRODUCT3-dimensional1handle (its attaching disks each cross the
suture in one arc). Absorb the three initial collars into the three sutures
of N_a. The Seifert complement W is therefore N_a plus 2b+2 product1handles.
Cutting their cocore product disks recovers N_a. This is also the annulus-to-
disk trade of Baker--Manjarrez-Gutierrez Lemma2.7 applied to the NON-isolated
annuli, retaining the isolated piece.

Juhasz Proposition5.4, pp1330-1334, preserves the relative Maslov grading
within each source Spin^c structure under the surface decomposition maps.
Unlike the original bare guts generators, the two N_a classes are already in
the SAME Spin^c class. Hence their gap is preserved on passage to SFH(W),
and then to top HFK(K,g) by the Seifert surface decomposition. Product disks
preserve total rank, so these are exactly the two top classes.

APPLICATION TO THE PROPOSED RANK-FIVE GEOMETRIC TARGET
If a separate argument forces a rank-five Jones-one candidate's top HFK
Maslov gap to equal2g-1, then the above calculation rules out ISOLATING M1
for that candidate. The contradiction is2a-1<=2g-3<2g-1.
That separate HFK constraint is not proved in this note.
NON-ISOLATING M1 is unaffected: the bare solid-torus generators occupy
distinct Spin^c classes, so their mutual Maslov gap cannot be transported by
Proposition5.4 in this manner.

REAL EXAMPLE CHECK
Baker--Manjarrez-Gutierrez's genus2 isolating example16n332130 has a=1,b=0.
Their displayed HFK table (actual pixels saved as baker-table.png) has top
classes at Maslov2,3, gap1, agreeing with2a-1. Alexander rows2,1,0,-1,-2
have total ranks2,4,5,4,2, giving total17. The outer Euler characteristics
vanish, and the central3-2 gives Alexander polynomial1. Thus the geometric
type is realized and is not itself an obstruction; sparse total rank is the
extra target.
