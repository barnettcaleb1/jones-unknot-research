GEOMETRIC REALIZABILITY FILTER FOR A JONES-INVISIBLE TANGLE
Bounded research pass, 31 October 2026. No resolution of the Jones conjecture.

UPDATE AFTER INDEPENDENT AUDIT: the hypothesis below can be weakened to an
actual unknot infinity filling and a zero filling with the Jones polynomial
of the two-component unlink. Garoufalidis' determinant-zero extension gives
|lambda_G(S3_0J)|=1/6, while his one-knot surgery formula gives
lambda_G(S3_0J)=2a_2(J)-1/6. Thus |12a_2(J)-1|=1, and integrality forces
a_2(J)=0. The independent homology track developed this strengthening;
the tangle track checked the source formula and normalization. See its
companion audit and whitehead_quotient_followup.txt. The all-filling proof
below remains valid but is no longer the strongest hypothesis obtained.

1. The statement obtained

Let T be an actual four-ended tangle with an actual unknot rational filling.
Suppose its framed Kauffman bracket is the bracket of a rational tangle R
(an overall framing unit is harmless). Normalize the boundary marking so
R is Q_0 and the unknot filling is infinity. Let J be the knot in S^3 whose
exterior is the double branched cover of T. Then

                    a_2(J) = Delta_J''(1)/2 = 0.

Here Delta is the symmetric Alexander polynomial with Delta(1)=1. This
is a necessary geometric condition; it does NOT say Delta_J=1, J is
trivial, or that the formal type-D example is impossible. The deduction
uses established surgery and branched-cover formulas; novelty is not claimed.

Proof, with normalization recorded:

(i) The initial boundary normalization is possible because the rational
comparison's anchor filling has determinant 1 and is therefore a rational
unknot: its filling slope has distance one from the solid-torus meridian.
The infinity filling of the cover is S^3, so the cover is the exterior
of its filling core J. Its infinity slope is the meridian. The zero
branch filling has the Jones polynomial of the two-component unlink,
hence determinant zero. Thus zero is the preferred longitude: it is the
unique primitive slope whose filling has infinite first homology.

(ii) For every odd positive integer p, the branch filling K_p is a knot
and its cover is S^3_p(J). The comparison rational filling B_p has cover
L(p,1). Equality of the two tangle brackets gives equal Jones polynomials
after oriented normalization. More explicitly, a potential discrepancy
is a signed Laurent monomial; V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0 for knots force that
monomial to be 1. Odd |H_1| also guarantees that the branch link has one
component. Consistent reversal of all cover orientations only reverses
the signs below and has no effect on the divisibility conclusion.

(iii) Use the Casson normalization of the Casson-Walker invariant:

  lambda(S^3_{p/q}(J)) - lambda(L(p,q)) = (q/p) a_2(J),
  lambda(Sigma_2(K)) = -V_K'(-1)/(12 V_K(-1)) + sigma(K)/8.

The second convention has the right-handed trefoil signature -2.
Sources sometimes use twice this lambda; both coefficients then double.
Subtract the second identity for K_p and B_p and use the first:

                  sigma(K_p)-sigma(B_p) = 8 a_2(J)/p.

The left side is an integer. Every odd p therefore divides a_2(J).
Unbounded p forces a_2(J)=0. The same argument for coprime odd p and
arbitrary q gives 8q a_2(J)/p. Once a_2=0, corresponding nonzero-
determinant knot fillings also have the same signature.

Finite useful test: a single odd p not dividing a_2(J) disproves the
all-filling Jones identity for that companion. For a_2=+/-2, p=3 already
forces the impossible signature difference +/-16/3.

Primary formula sources:
* Lidman--Sivek, https://www.ma.imperial.ac.uk/~ssivek/papers/smalldeterminant.pdf,
  Eq. (2.1): Casson-normalized surgery formula, A(K)=Delta''(1)/2.
* Greene--Watson, https://arxiv.org/html/1106.5559, Theorem 13, citing
  Mullins, Topology 32 (1993), Theorem 5.1. Greene--Watson use twice-Casson
  normalization: their coefficients are -1/6 and +1/4; divide by 2.
* Kotelskiy--Watson--Zibrowius (KWZ), Khovanov homology and strong inversions,
  https://msp.org/obs/2022/5-1/obs-v5-n1-p10-p.pdf, Section 1, supplies the
  preferred meridian/longitude marking and Montesinos correspondence.

2. Application to the previously constructed formal object

The earlier formal object has, over F_2, one rational component r_1(0)
and four special components s_4(infinity), with grading shifts whose
Euler characteristics cancel. If realized by an actual tangle, its
infinity filling is the actual unknot by reduced Khovanov detection.
All rational Jones pairings equal those of Q_0; its slope-one filling
would have Jones 1 and reduced Khovanov rank 17. Such a realization must
therefore have a strongly invertible companion J with a_2(J)=0.

Its nonzero rational-filling rank is |p|+16|q|. In particular the 1/n
fillings have rank 1+16|n|, NOT bounded rank: the meridian has determinant
intersection |q| with p/q. Consequently the surgery rank-growth argument
does not by itself contradict the formal object.

3. Four actual small seeds with exactly the desired ungraded pattern

KWZ Table 1 lists all strong inversions of prime knots through nine
crossings. The entries with k=0 and four shortest special components are
6_1, 8_20, 9_42, and the first strong inversion of 9_46. (The arXiv v1
uses the name s_2 for the same shortest curve that the published paper
calls s_4.) Actual diagram computations give:

    companion     a_2
    6_1           -2
    8_20          +2
    9_42          -2
    9_46          -2

All four companions fail the geometric filter, regardless of attempts
to choose formal grading shifts. Their p=3 branch filling cannot have
the comparator Jones polynomial. This is an actual obstruction beyond
the ungraded curve-shape test; it does not classify larger companions.

Reproduction: realizability_small_seeds.py/.json, containing original
PD codes, exact Regina Jones polynomials, and a_2=-V''(1)/6. Trefoil
and figure-eight controls give +1 and -1 respectively.
An independent check from Seifert matrices is saved in
realizability_seifert_audit.json. For a 2g-by-2g Seifert matrix V, put
B=(V-V^t)^(-1)V; then tr(B)=g and a_2=(g-tr(B^2))/2. Exact rational
arithmetic gives the same four values and a_2(K(31,18))=0.

4. Floer-rank tests and two instructive false positives

The scripts compute Ahat_s = C{max(i,j-s)=0} from the UV=0 knot Floer
complex. Missing mixed arrows decrease both filtrations and therefore
vanish in this boundary subquotient. All differentials are checked to
square to zero. For positive p>=2g-1, the large-surgery formula gives

       rank HFhat(S^3_p(J)) = p + sum_s(rank Ahat_s - 1).

Kinoshita--Terasaka 11n42 has Ahat ranks 5,9,5 at s=-1,0,1 and rank 1
elsewhere, yielding exactly p+16. Its Alexander polynomial is 1.
However this is a false positive: it is NOT strongly invertible.
Akbulut--Ruberman, Proposition 2.6, prove its symmetry group trivial:
https://www.selmanakbulut.com/papers/absolute.pdf . No quotient tangle
for this companion was constructed or claimed. The Conway knot also
has large-positive excess 16, but is noninvertible; it likewise fails
the geometric symmetry requirement.

A genuinely strongly invertible false positive is the two-bridge knot
K(31,18). It has genus 2, tau=-1 in the saved convention,

 Delta=-2t^2+8t-11+8t^-1-2t^-2,

so a_2=0, and again Ahat ranks 5,9,5 give p+16. Nevertheless its ACTUAL
quotient curves do not resemble the desired formal four-component
pattern: it is the mirror of 8_14, whose KWZ row has k=4 and FIFTEEN
shortest special components for both strong inversions. Mirroring
changes the sign of k, not the component count. The actual kappa rank
is 60 rather than 16.

Identification uses the primary table listing 8_14 as K(31,12):
https://www.numdam.org/item/AFST_2020_6_29_4_761_0.pdf, p.786.
Since 12*18=-1 mod 31, Schubert equivalence gives the mirror. Exact
Regina Jones comparison agrees. The root's data are saved in
rational_zero_a2_test.json. Thus symmetry, a_2, and the complete large-
positive Floer rank bound still do not determine actual tangle geography.

The independent computation here establishes the large-positive rank
formula, not a separately audited all-rational surgery formula.

5. A remaining natural geometric test, not a candidate claim

Actual untwisted Whitehead doubles of both trefoil chiralities and both
clasp signs were constructed using Regina and converted to PD diagrams.
They all have genus 1 and a_2=0. Three cases have Ahat_0 rank 9 and
large-positive excess 8; the positive tau=1 case has Ahat_0 rank 7 and
excess 6. These pass the coarse p+16 Floer inequality. Their PD codes,
Jones polynomials and UV=0 complexes are in realizability_whitehead.json.

No strong-inversion quotient diagram, quotient Jones vector, or immersed-
curve computation for these Whitehead companions was obtained in this
pass. They are a sensible next actual-geometric test, not proposed
Jones counterexamples. In particular the familiar quotient construction
whose branch set is a Whitehead double has a DIFFERENT cover companion
(a connected sum); it must not be substituted for a strong inversion
of the Whitehead companion itself.

6. Reproduction and scope

Scripts: realizability_small_seeds.py, realizability_floer_test.py,
realizability_whitehead.py; output JSON files preserve diagrams and
calculation inputs. Packages in work/braid_classification/packages:
Spherogram 2.4.1, Regina 7.4.1, knot_floer_homology 1.2.2.

The main advance of this pass is the all-filling geometric a_2 filter
and its successful exclusion of four actual curve-shape matches. No
general classical-realizability theorem or actual invisible tangle has
been established. The useful next target is an actual quotient tangle
of an a_2=0 strongly invertible companion, with its gradings computed;
formal direct sums and coarse surgery ranks are not adequate substitutes.
