INDEPENDENT AUDIT: ALL-RATIONAL-FILLING JONES FLATNESS FORCES a_2=0

Verdict: the proposed lemma is valid with the hypotheses and conventions below.
It is a necessary condition on actual tangle realization, not an obstruction
to every knot with Jones polynomial one.

Let T be a classical four-ended tangle with a marked rational-filling coordinate.
Assume its infinity filling is the actual unknot. Assume its zero filling has
determinant zero. Finally, for an unbounded set of odd positive integers p,
assume the normalized Jones polynomial of T(p) equals that of the standard
rational-zero comparison tangle filled at p. The latter closure is the
appropriate chirality of T(2,p), with double cover the corresponding L(p,1).
Equality for ALL rational fillings is more than this argument needs.

1. The branched double cover M of T has a filling M(infinity)=S3. The core J of
the attached solid torus is a knot in S3, and M is its exterior. The infinity
slope is its meridian mu. No incompressibility hypothesis is needed.

2. The determinant-zero condition says H1(M(0);Q) has positive rank. In a knot
exterior in S3, there is exactly one primitive slope with this property: the
preferred longitude lambda. Thus the zero slope is lambda, not lambda+k mu.
This is the step that prevents an unnoticed framing translation of p.

The standard rational coordinate has distance one between 0 and infinity, so
after choosing oriented generators it sends p/q to p mu+q lambda, up to the
consistent sign choices inherent in the orientation convention. Such a global
sign change affects the displayed surgery formula's sign but not its vanishing
conclusion.

3. The p-filling therefore covers p-surgery on J, whereas the comparison covers
p-surgery on U. Both branch links are knots for odd p. One justification is
that the branched cover has H1 of odd order p, while the mod-two first homology
dimension of a link's double cover is its number of components minus one.
Alternatively, equality of normalized Jones polynomials implies equality of
component counts from their values at 1, and the comparison is a knot.

4. Use the Casson-Walker normalization agreeing with the integer-valued Casson
invariant on integral homology spheres. Lidman--Sivek Eq. (2.1) says

  lambda_C(S3_(p/q)(J))-lambda_C(S3_(p/q)(U))
      = (q/p) a_2(J),
  a_2(J)=Delta_J''(1)/2,

with Delta(t)=Delta(t^{-1}) and Delta(1)=1.

Greene--Watson Theorem 13 quotes Mullins in TWICE this normalization: their
Poincare sphere has lambda=-2. Dividing their formula by two gives

  lambda_C(Sigma_2(L))
      = -V_L'(-1)/(12 V_L(-1)) + sigma(L)/8,

for nonzero determinant. The signs are checked against their stated
positive-(3,5)-torus-knot example, with signature -8.

5. Equality of the Jones polynomials cancels the logarithmic-derivative terms.
For integer p the two formulas therefore give

  sigma(T(p))-sigma(T_zero(p)) = 8 a_2(J)/p.

With a globally opposite convention the right side has the opposite sign.
The left side is an integer (indeed even). Thus p divides 8 a_2(J). This cannot
hold for an unbounded set of positive integers p unless a_2(J)=0. More generally
at a coprime p/q slope the same argument gives p divides 8 a_2(J), because q
is invertible modulo p. Once a_2=0, the compared signatures agree at every
nonzero odd-numerator filling for which Jones equality is assumed.

FORMAL-D APPLICABILITY

If a claimed full tangle module yields the rational-zero graded Euler pairing
at every filling up to a common monomial grading shift, there is still a
normalization check. At an odd-numerator filling, both actual and comparison
closures are knots. Write V_actual(t)=epsilon t^a V_comparison(t). Evaluation
at t=1 gives epsilon=1. Both normalized knot Jones polynomials have derivative
zero at 1, so differentiating gives a=0. Hence the monomial ambiguity disappears
at each knot filling. For the zero filling, a monomial cannot change whether
V(-1) vanishes, so the longitude identification is unaffected. This verifies
the needed implication from the stipulated Euler-pairing identity; it does not
independently establish that the formal module is realized by any tangle.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT IMPLY

* a_2(J)=0 does not force J=U or Delta_J=1.
* Equality at one fixed numerator p gives only a divisibility condition.
* This argument does not establish a reduction from arbitrary Jones-one knots
  to the stipulated unknot-anchored all-filling tangle.
* Higher derivatives of ordinary Jones cannot simply replace the first
  derivative in Mullins' formula. A new formula relating a higher cover
  invariant to these branch-link data would be needed. Manifold WRT/LMO data
  involve different, generally colored inputs; no such identification is
  licensed by the present argument.
