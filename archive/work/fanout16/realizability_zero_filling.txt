TWO-FILLING REALIZABILITY OBSTRUCTION
Independent derivation and normalization audit, 31 October 2026.
The derivation was independently checked by the tangle agent against the sources.

STATEMENT

Let T be a classical four-ended tangle. Suppose its infinity rational filling
is the actual unknot, and its zero rational filling L has the Jones polynomial
of the two-component unlink, possibly multiplied by a signed Laurent monomial.
Let J be the knot in S3 whose exterior is the double branched cover of T.
Then

                         a_2(J) = Delta_J''(1)/2 = 0.

Delta is symmetric and Delta(1)=1; a_2 is the integral second Conway
coefficient. No Jones assumption at any other filling is needed. In particular,
this strengthens the earlier all-rational-filling argument in
realizability_casson_audit.txt. It is a consequence of established formulas,
not a claimed new theorem in the literature or a solution of Jones detection.

GEOMETRIC MARKING

The infinity filling of M=Sigma_2(T) is S3, so M is the exterior of the core J
of the filling solid torus. Infinity is the meridian. The zero branch filling
has determinant zero, hence H1(M(0);Q) is nonzero. For an S3 knot exterior the
unique primitive slope with this property is the preferred longitude. Thus
M(0)=S3_0(J). This identification does not assume T rational or M incompressible.
The standard 0 and infinity slopes have distance one.

GAROUFALIDIS'S FORMULA AT DETERMINANT ZERO

Use the Jones convention J_L(t)=(-1)^(c-1) V_L(t^-1), with c components and
sqrt(-1)=i. Garoufalidis, Theorem 1.1 in the published paper (Theorem 1 in
the arXiv HTML), states

  i^(sigma(L)+nu(L)) lambda_G(Sigma_2(L))
        = J_L'(-1)/6 + J_L(-1) sigma(L)/4.

Here nu(L)=b1(Sigma_2(L)); for the present zero surgery it is 1. The invariant
lambda_G uses the doubled Casson-Lescop normalization. For the two-component
unlink, J_U2(t)=t^(1/2)+t^(-1/2), so

                        J_U2(-1)=0,  J_U2'(-1)=-i.

Consequently the hypothesized polynomial gives

                     |lambda_G(S3_0(J))| = 1/6.                 (1)

The signature and nullity phase has absolute value one. No signature-zero
assumption is needed. A signed monomial multiplier also has absolute value
one at -1. Its derivative contributes zero because J_U2(-1)=0. Thus (1)
remains true under the stated monomial ambiguity. Evaluation at 1 shows that
an actual normalized link polynomial of this form belongs to a two-component
link, as required by the unlink comparison.

ZERO-SURGERY NORMALIZATION, CHECKED WITHOUT MIXING b1 CONVENTIONS

Garoufalidis Section 4.1 defines lambda_G directly by a surgery formula. For
one knot J with integer framing f, its terms are

  D = zeta(J),     H0 = f sign(f)/4,
  H1 = -1/6,      H2 = -f^2/12,

and the sum is multiplied by sgn'([f]). The exceptional convention is
sgn'([0])=1. In H2, the one-component L8 is just f^2. The Alexander-derived
term zeta(J) is independent of the surgery framing.

At f=+1 all three H terms sum to zero, and sgn'=1. Hence

              zeta(J)=lambda_G(S3_1(J))=2 a_2(J).

The final equality is the usual +1 Casson surgery formula in the doubled
normalization. This normalization can also be checked entirely in our tangle
setting: the +1 filling is a determinant-one branch knot. Garoufalidis's
formula there reduces to the Mullins formula quoted by Greene-Watson as
Theorem 13, with coefficients -1/6 and +1/4 and Poincare-sphere value -2.
It is twice the integer-valued Casson normalization. Thus this calibration
does not presume that a normalization change at b1=0 automatically extends
to b1=1: we pass between the two framings in Garoufalidis's own surgery formula.

At f=0 only H1 remains, and therefore

                    lambda_G(S3_0(J))=2 a_2(J)-1/6.             (2)

As a separate consistency check, Kerler's Lemma 2 quotes Lescop's usual
b1=1 formula lambda_L(M)=Delta_M''(1)/2-Delta_M(1)/12.
For S3_0(J) this reads a_2(J)-1/12, exactly half of (2).
In particular S1 x S2 has values -1/6 and -1/12 respectively. One must not
apply the rational-homology-sphere orientation-sign rule indiscriminately to
this b1=1 invariant; the direct formula (2) avoids that issue.

Combining (1) and (2) gives

                              |12 a_2(J)-1|=1.

Its two rational solutions are a_2=0 and a_2=1/6. Integrality excludes the
second, proving the statement. Different coherent orientation conventions
can reverse intermediate signs but cannot yield a nonzero integral solution.

More generally, for ANY longitudinal branch link L of a strongly invertible
S3 knot J, the same calculation before substituting the unlink polynomial
gives the exact magnitude identity

                       |J_L'(-1)| = |12 a_2(J)-1|.

It is also valid with V in place of J: their derivatives at this zero differ
by a unit complex phase. In particular the root of its Jones polynomial at
t=-1 is always simple. Indeed 12 a_2(J)-1 cannot vanish for integral a_2.
This is a constraint on actual longitudinal quotient links, not on arbitrary
determinant-zero links or arbitrary formal tangle modules.

SCOPE AND IMMEDIATE CONSEQUENCES

* Any geometric realization of the earlier Jones-invisible formal tangle
  must satisfy a_2(J)=0 already from its infinity and zero closures.
* This rules out the previously tested candidate companions 6_1, 8_20,
  9_42 and 9_46, whose a_2 values are -2, +2, -2, -2.
* A nontrivial L-space knot, or its mirror, cannot be the companion. Its
  Alexander polynomial has the form
    (-1)^k + sum_(i=1)^k (-1)^(k-i)(t^n_i+t^-n_i),
  where 0<n1<...<nk and k>=1. Thus
    a_2=sum_(i=1)^k (-1)^(k-i)n_i^2 > 0:
  pair adjacent terms starting from the largest; each difference of squares
  is positive, and if k is odd the remaining n1^2 is positive as well.
  The form and nontriviality follow from the L-space surgery knot Floer
  theorem and genus detection, not merely from a lens-surgery assumption.
* a_2=0 does not imply Delta=1, J=U, or impossibility of the formal tangle.
  There are actual nontrivial strongly invertible knots with a_2=0.
* No generic reduction from an arbitrary Jones-one knot to the stipulated
  two-filling tangle has been established here.
* We have not promoted higher ordinary Jones derivatives to higher cover
  invariants. Such an extension would need an additional actual theorem.
