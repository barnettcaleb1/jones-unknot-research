WHITEHEAD COMPANION QUOTIENT: BOUNDED FOLLOW-UP

Outcome: inherited strong inversions and exact diagram constructors located;
no marked exterior quotient diagram, quotient Jones vector, or kappa lower
bound obtained. The existing Whitehead PD/HFK data do not establish any
Jones-invisible tangle candidate.

The geometric existence question is settled by a primary source, rather
than an assumption: Dai--Mallick--Taniguchi, Exotic disks and singular
instanton Floer homology, arXiv:2606.05819, Example 2.22 and Figure 4,
explain the two inherited strong inversions of Wh_i(K), with the clasp at
either fixed point of the companion inversion. This applies to the trefoil.
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05819

Sano, Involutive Khovanov homology and equivariant knots II,
arXiv:2608.07114, develops computations of involutive Khovanov invariants
of these symmetric knots. Those invariants are not the kappa invariant
of the exterior quotient tangle. No relation furnishing the needed
kappa>16 bound was obtained from that paper.
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.07114

The author's actual source code provides a concrete starting fixture:
https://github.com/taketo1024/yui/blob/5fa6f2765db65763297a3497c6e5a03d07cf50fd/lib-link/src/inv_link/construct.rs
Its whitehead_double_at constructs the symmetric diagram at a chosen
fixed edge, preserves the axis, and has explicit tests for both clasp
placements, mirroring, and zero Seifert framing. The companion trefoil
test datum is used in its tests. The source's InvLink retains the edge
involution, which our earlier generic Regina PD diagrams do not retain.
The two relevant source files are archived here as yui_construct.rs and
yui_inv_link.rs, with yui_LICENSE. We have read the construction; we did
not compile or run it in this pass.

Search of that repository's quotient/branch implementations found no
branched-exterior-quotient constructor. The missing geometric operation
is explicit: convert the symmetric Whitehead knot plus its fixed axis
to the marked tangle (S^3 minus nu(Wh(K)))/tau, identify its meridian as
infinity and Seifert longitude as zero, then compute two bracket closures.
An involution-labelled PD code does not by itself supply that marked
quotient PD code. The longitude marking is essential, not optional.

The usual Whitehead-clasp tangle replacement must not substitute for
this operation. Its double cover has companion K#K^r, whereas the target
here is the exterior of Wh(K). Dai--Mallick--Taniguchi Example 2.22
explicitly distinguishes the deck involution of Sigma_2(Wh(K)) from
the lift of the inherited Whitehead inversion. Confusing those gives
a calculation of a different tangle.

Concrete next test, if this route is resumed: use one yui symmetric
trefoil Whitehead fixture and draw only its exterior quotient zero
filling. Verify infinity is geometrically U and zero has determinant0.
Its exact zero-link Jones polynomial alone can disprove the proposed
skein-equivalence. Computing an entire involutive Kh complex first
would not resolve this missing quotient input.
