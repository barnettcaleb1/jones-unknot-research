ASSESSMENT

I did not identify a prior source proving the exact balanced-framed,
two-distinct-rational-probe theorem among arbitrary tangles. There is positive,
recent evidence that its proposer still treated it as a conjecture: Sikora's
IPAM talk dated 15 October 2026 explicitly calls it the Uniqueness of Rational
Solutions Conjecture. Its abstract describes the lens-space-surgery route to
potential counterexamples rather than an established proof. The host's program
also lists the talk. This is considerably better evidence of recent open status
than the absence of search hits, but it is not proof of novelty or priority.

Primary event record:
https://www.mathinstitutes.org/videos/25091
Host schedule:
https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-i-hyperbolic-structures-and-quantum-invariants/?tab=schedule

The most significant attribution issue is METHOD overlap: Gainullin's 2018
unknot-characterization proof already combines Casson–Walker surgery,
vanishing of the second Alexander derivative, the alternating L-space
Alexander form, and genus detection. A defensible presentation should identify
that antecedent. The proposed novelty, if the mathematical argument survives
specialist review, would be applying a related strategy to the full framed
tangle conjecture using two matched fillings and Floer simplicity, without
assuming the auxiliary homology sphere is an L-space.

No source located licenses wording such as "the first proof," "new theorem,"
or "previously unknown implication." Recommended status: "a candidate
deduction of Sikora's framed rational-solution uniqueness conjecture from
published surgery and Floer-theoretic results; priority unverified."

EXACT TARGET AND SOURCE VERSIONS

Sikora's current arXiv record lists v1, 17 May 2020, and v2, 5 August 2023;
the journal reference is Canadian Journal of Mathematics 76 (2024), 707–727.
The target is Conjecture 2 in v2 / Conjecture 1.2 in the journal. Proposition
1 / 1.1 proves uniqueness only among rational solutions. Theorem 21 / 8.1
proves uniqueness of their bracket vector. Theorem 24(c) / 9.2(c), detection
of a rational tangle among arbitrary tangles by its bracket alone, is the
stronger Jones-equivalent statement. The distinct probes must be unequal
after forgetting framing. The framed equivalence is balanced isotopy.

The v1 title ends "and quantum continued fractions" and its abstract
conjectures broader uniqueness of framed solutions. It should not substitute
for the narrowed target in v2. The journal's 2024 erratum corrects only the
author's misspelled name; it does not announce a mathematical amendment.

RELATED RESULTS AND WHAT THEY DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY SUPPLY

1. Unknot-product case: an existing surgery characterization is very close.

Kronheimer–Mrowka–Ozsváth–Szabó, Monopoles and lens space surgeries,
Theorem 1.1, says that equality of oriented r-surgeries on a knot K in S^3
and the unknot, for a finite rational r, forces K to be the unknot. The
paper identifies Property R and Gordon–Luecke as earlier special cases.

Inference for the candidate: if one actual product is the unknot, its cover
gives S^3. After the candidate's peripheral-coordinate identification, the
other matched filling falls under the classical characterization. Thus an
unknot-product restriction should not be marketed as a new surgery-rigidity
theorem. This does not by itself handle the candidate's general case, whose
chosen determinant-one filling is only known to be an integral homology
sphere. Determinant one is not a proof that it is S^3.

https://arxiv.org/html/math/0310164v2
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2007/165-2/p03

2. Gainullin's theorem and the particularly close proof strategy.

Fyodor Gainullin, Heegaard Floer homology and knots determined by their
complements, AGT 18 (2018), 69–109, Theorem 8.2, assumes K is
null-homologous in an L-space Y. Matching the absolutely graded Floer
module of its surgery to that of Y#L(p,q) forces K to be unknotted.
The argument on pp. 104–105 uses precisely the Casson–Walker / Alexander
second derivative / alternating coefficients / genus-detection chain.

Inference: this is direct prior art for the strategy, but not a direct
application covering the full candidate. Its auxiliary Y is not established
an L-space, and its comparison surgery is L(p,q), not Y#L(p,q).
Choosing an actual lens-space filling as ambient Y instead does not
automatically fix this: the filling core is generally homologically essential,
whereas Gainullin requires a null-homologous knot. These hypotheses should
be checked explicitly before claiming that this theorem already contains
the candidate's entire result.

https://msp.org/agt/2018/18-1/agt-v18-n1-p03-p.pdf

3. Rasmussen–Rasmussen already supply the relevant structural machinery.

Floer Simple Manifolds and L-Space Intervals, arXiv:1508.05900v2
(24 November 2015), Proposition 1.3 relates two L-space fillings to Floer
simplicity; Proposition 1.4 supplies the torsion coefficient restriction;
Corollary 2.3 relates torsion degree to Thurston norm under boundary
incompressibility. Their generalized-solid-torus theory is substantial
prior work, not a new consequence first discovered here.

Inference: the potentially new step is the particular assembly with framed
bracket constraints and two SAME-COEFFICIENT oriented lens-space fillings.
The cited statements do not themselves state a framed-tangle theorem.

https://arxiv.org/html/1508.05900

4. The S^1 x S^2 branch has established rigidity tools.

Ni–Vafaee, Null surgery on knots in L-spaces, Trans. AMS 372 (2019),
8279–8306, Theorem 1.1, proves fibering for a knot exterior in S^1 x S^2
admitting an L-space surgery. Remark 3.2 identifies this with the spherical
braid description. The candidate's torsion-free homology condition is the
extra ingredient used to reduce the fiber to a disk.

https://www.its.caltech.edu/~yini/Published/NullSurgery.pdf

Gillespie, L-space fillings and Generalized solid tori, arXiv:1603.05016,
Theorem 1.2, characterizes manifolds with every non-longitudinal filling an
L-space by genus zero and an L-space filling. Alishahi–Lidman–Lipshitz,
Detecting Heegaard Floer homology solid tori, arXiv:2505.01217 (2 May 2025),
Theorem 1.1, characterizes Heegaard Floer homology solid tori by a
nonseparating sphere in the longitude filling. Neither statement alone
identifies an arbitrary rational homology solid torus as an actual solid
torus; the newer paper explicitly includes non-solid-torus examples.

https://arxiv.org/html/1603.05016
https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01217

5. Multiple lens fillings alone do not provide the proposed rigidity.

Baker–Doleshal–Hoffman, On manifolds with multiple lens space fillings,
arXiv:1308.5002 / Bol. Soc. Mat. Mex. 20 (2014), 405–447, studies
non-solid-torus manifolds with several lens fillings, including hyperbolic
families. The paper also has an erratum, DOI 10.1007/s40590-014-0048-0.
Such results show why "two lens fillings" is not a substitute for matching
the oriented lens spaces at the same peripheral coefficients as a fixed
solid torus. No classification from this paper was used to claim the full
candidate already known.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.5002
https://doi.org/10.1007/s40590-014-0016-8
https://doi.org/10.1007/s40590-014-0048-0

6. Cosmetic-crossing results have narrower configurations.

Lidman–Moore, Cosmetic surgery in L-spaces and nugatory crossings,
arXiv:1507.00699, applies surgery characterization to crossings with
branched L-space covers and additional homological constraints. This is
related technology, not a theorem about all pairs of rational probes.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.00699

UNFRAMED AND POLYNOMIAL-ONLY CLAIMS MUST REMAIN SEPARATE

The candidate requires two actual balanced-framed link equalities. Removing
their framing conditions changes the problem: distinct underlying rational
tangles can solve an unframed system, and compatible framings cannot simply
be assumed. Conversely, replacing actual product equalities by bracket or
Jones equality removes the topological lens-filling hypotheses used in the
candidate. Neither change is a harmless reformulation. This assessment
does not claim the stronger bracket-detection statement is proved, or that
the unframed rationality conjecture follows.
