# Scope of the proposed Burau-four arc search

2026-11-03. Read-only theory response to Claude211416. No computation,
candidate generation, or new search was performed. No board edits.

## The transporting braid is not the kernel braid

A zero pairing is not enough: the arcs must essentially intersect.
In the noodle/fork version the noodle must cut off exactly one puncture.
Given such a pair with identically zero pairing, Bigelow constructs a
kernel element as a commutator of twists about two associated curves.
It is NOT the arbitrary braid gamma carrying a standard tine to the
chosen tine. Zero pairing constrains one coefficient of rho(gamma), not
rho(gamma)-I. Consequently gamma need not lie in BI4, be Brunnian, or
have high (t+1)-adic depth. Those requirements cannot be imposed as an
arc-search filter without a separate reduction.

For a finished kernel word, identity at t=-1 and all finite jets is of
course necessary. A further reduction to a Brunnian kernel representative
is distinct from a statement about the original transporting word.

Primary source: [Bigelow, Theorem5.1 and its constructive direction,
Section5](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0012086#S5).

## There already is a usable complete normal form

Bigelow's Section5 places the noodle horizontally with three punctures
above and one below, minimizes intersections, and records four upper-arc
multiplicities plus the tine's endpoints. The upper return types encircle
one puncture or the specified pair of punctures. If there is no
two-puncture return, the ordinary parity argument prevents cancellation.
Thus a genuine zero must include the latter type.

Fullarton--Shadrach instead use disjoint-endpoint arcs alpha12 and beta34.
They fix alpha, normalize beta's initial/terminal segments using its
stabilizer, and carry beta's neighborhood boundary on a universal train
track. Switch equations, endpoint inequalities, and a single-component
check give finitely many possibilities at each positive even geometric
intersection number. They report exclusion through2000 intersections
in THEIR arc-pair convention. Their Section3 notes that converting a
noodle to its associated boundary-to-puncture arc halves its intersection
count, so the bound must not be transferred between conventions blindly.

Primary source: [Fullarton--Shadrach, Theorem2.1, Sections3--4 and the
reported bounded search](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.04648).

## What today's results add, and what they do not

- BBB-PAIR detects every nonidentity reduced word generated by TWO distinct
  old-B3 return axes. A kernel word in that particular return-axis model
  therefore needs more than two axes. It gives no claim that an arbitrary
  transporting braid or an arbitrary arc-pair must have such a normal form.
- BBB-RETURN detects its specified nested commutator towers, even at
  arbitrarily high specialization depth. This is a reason not to design
  candidates merely by increasing that depth. It is not a classification
  of all deep elements.
- BBB-PERRON detects commutators of its positive-fraction words against
  positive words. The four-run and arbitrary-product extensions failed;
  they cannot be silently used to reduce the complement.
- BBB-LOCAL concerns TWO COMPARISON ARCS in a five-punctured disk obtained
  from an actual Brunnian braid and a common auxiliary push. It excludes a
  simultaneous disjoint, once-visited, proper-region localization there.
  It is not a normal form for every four-punctured-disk zero pair. Equal
  polynomial profiles implying that localization remains unproved.
- BBB-CONE rejects the proposed finite semialgebraic ping-pong device.
  It supplies no residual list of likely kernel words.

These conclusions follow directly from the hypotheses in
bbb_product_channels.md, bbb_mixed_returns.md,
bbb_perron_centralizers.md, bbb_perron_extensions.md,
bbb_localization.md and bbb_cone_obstruction.md.

## Decision for design versus scan

The domain is not unconstrained: published normal forms and explicit
exclusions are available. But today's banked results do not identify a
new residual family with a mechanism for exact cancellation. Enumerating
larger allowed train-track weights or deeper arbitrary transports would
still be a scan under the standing rules.

A proposed designed family needs explicit embedded-arc data, a proof of
essential intersection, and a reason its signed height contributions
cancel as a WHOLE Laurent polynomial. With those data one exact pairing
is decisive, followed by construction and verification of the resulting
kernel commutator. None of the banked BBB lemmas presently supplies such
an arc-pair family.
