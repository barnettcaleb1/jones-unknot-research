# Detecting a nested-commutator family in Brun_4 intersect BI_4

2026-11-03. Node: BBB-RETURN, supporting BBB-MULTISET.

Status: TRUSTED, written proof with independent internal algebraic read and Claude's independent read in reply164223. Novelty unchecked. No numerical search was used. This is a theorem about the specified braid family, not the universal multiset assertion or the Jones unknot conjecture.

## The theorem

Use the standard unreduced Burau representation over Q(t), acting on columns, with the i-th generator having block

    [ 1-t   t ]
    [  1    0 ]

on coordinates i,i+1. Let Delta_3^2=(sigma_1 sigma_2)^3 be the full twist on the first three strands. Fix nonzero integers r,s and any u in B_3. Define braid words

    b_0 = [u, sigma_3^(2r)],
    b_j = [Delta_3^(4s), b_(j-1)]  for j>=1,

where [x,y]=xyx^-1y^-1.

**Theorem.**

1. rho_4(b_1)=I if and only if u belongs to <sigma_1>.
2. If u does not belong to <sigma_1>, then rho_4(b_j) is nonidentity for every j>=1, and these images are pairwise distinct.
3. If u is a nontrivial Brunnian three-braid, every b_j is Brunnian on four strands and

       rho_4(b_j) = I mod (t+1)^j     for j>=1,

   while its generic Burau image is never identity.

The orders at t=-1 are lower bounds, not claimed exact. An exact order at t=1 is obtained in the proof.

## 1. A column stabilizer on three strands

Write U=rho_3(u), z=(1,1,1)^T and e=exp(u). Every Burau matrix fixes z. With bar(t)=t^-1, the explicit Hermitian form

    H = [   1    -1    -t ]
        [  -1     1    -1 ]
        [ -t^-1  -1     1 ]

is preserved by both standard B_3 generators. This is checked directly, or by their orthogonal eigenspaces. Its determinant is -(t+1)^2/t.

The 1-eigenspace of rho_3(sigma_1) is

    F_0 = span(z,e_3).

Its orthogonal complement is the line generated by v_12=(-t,1,0)^T, whose H-norm is (t+1)^2/t. Both summands are therefore nondegenerate over Q(t).

If Ue_3=e_3, then U fixes F_0 pointwise and preserves its orthogonal line. On that line its eigenvalue is its determinant, (-t)^e. Hence

    U = rho_3(sigma_1^e).

Faithfulness of Burau on B_3 gives u=sigma_1^e. Conversely every such power fixes e_3.

Thus the exact column stabilizer is

    Ue_3=e_3  iff  u in <sigma_1>.

Here faithfulness on three strands is the classical Magnus–Peluso theorem; no four-strand faithfulness input is used.

## 2. The rank-one crossing and the two-block twist

Work now in V=Q(t)^4. Set D=1+t+t^2 and decompose

    V = E direct-sum F,
    E = ker(1,t,t^2) in the first three coordinates,
    F = span(z_3,e_4),  z_3=(1,1,1,0)^T.

The two relevant matrices are

    S = rho_4(Delta_3^(4s)) = t^(6s) I_E direct-sum I_F,
    U = rho_4(u) = U_E direct-sum I_F.

The standard full-twist formula follows directly by multiplying the B_3 generators; the old reduced summand E has full-twist eigenvalue t^3, while z_3 is fixed.

Let R=rho_4(sigma_3^(2r)). Write

    R=I+cP,      c=t^(2r)-1,
    P=v ell,    v=(0,0,-t,1)^T,
    ell=(0,0,-1,1)/(t+1).

Since ell(v)=1, P is a rank-one projector. Its eigenvalue in R is t^(2r), distinct from 1.

Split v=x+y and ell=a+b according to E direct-sum F. Explicitly,

    x=-t(e_3-t^2 z_3/D),     y=e_4-t^3 z_3/D.

Both x and b are nonzero. The overlap scalar is

    alpha=a(x)=t/D,     beta=b(y)=1-alpha.

## 3. The noncancellation step

Put B=[U,R] and d=t^(-2r)-1. Since U acts identically on F,

    B=(I+cP_U)(I+dP),       P_U=UPU^-1.

Its E<-F block is

    B_EF = [c(1+d eta) U_E x + d x] b,
    eta = a(U_E^-1 x)+beta.

Suppose B commutes with S. The two eigenvalues of S are distinct, so B_EF=0. Because d x is nonzero, the coefficient of U_E x cannot vanish. Therefore U_E x=lambda x for some nonzero scalar lambda.

Substitute eta=alpha/lambda+beta. Using d=-c/(1+c), the block becomes

    B_EF = c/(1+c) (1+c alpha)(lambda-1) x b.

The crucial factor is

    1+c alpha = (1+t^2+t^(2r+1))/(1+t+t^2).

Its numerator is a sum of three Laurent monomials with positive coefficients; it is nonzero for every integer r, including when degrees coincide. Thus lambda=1.

Since U fixes z_3 and x=-t(e_3-t^2 z_3/D), it fixes e_3. Section 1 gives u in <sigma_1>. Such u commutes with sigma_3^(2r), so B=I.

We have proved the stronger equivalence

    [S,[U,R]]=I  iff  [U,R]=I  iff  u in <sigma_1>.

This proves part 1. The proof is global over Q(t): no fixed root evaluation or finite jet supplies the noncancellation step.

## 4. Every further left-nested commutator survives

Assume u is not in <sigma_1>. Then B_0=[U,R] does not commute with S. Its off-diagonal blocks in E direct-sum F are therefore not both identically zero.

The splitting admits a basis regular and invertible at t=1, since D(1)=3. Also B_0(1)=I because R(1)=I. Write h=t-1 and let

    (B_0)_off = h^k C + O(h^(k+1)),     C nonzero,

where k>=1 is finite and C is off-diagonal. Let J be the projector onto E in this basis. Conjugation by S leaves diagonal blocks exactly unchanged, so

    S B_0 S^-1 - B_0
       = 6s h^(k+1) [J,C] + O(h^(k+2)).

Multiplication by B_0^-1=I+O(h) preserves the leading term. Inductively, for B_j=[S,B_(j-1)],

    B_j-I
       = h^(k+j) (6s)^j ad_J^j(C) + O(h^(k+j+1)).

On off-diagonal matrices ad_J^2 is the identity. Every displayed leading coefficient is nonzero. Hence

    ord_(t=1)(B_j-I)=k+j    for every j>=1.

This proves nonidentity and pairwise distinctness. It also explains why initial diagonal terms of B_0 at lower orders cause no problem: they cancel exactly in S B_0 S^-1-B_0 before multiplication by B_0^-1.

## 5. Membership in the difficult Brunnian slice

Suppose now that u is a nontrivial Brunnian three-braid. It cannot lie in <sigma_1>, since forgetting strand 3 would leave that power unchanged.

The word b_0=[u,sigma_3^(2r)] is pure and Brunnian on four strands. Deleting strand 4 kills the second factor; deleting any of the first three strands kills u. Normality of the Brunnian subgroup then gives the same property for all b_j.

At t=-1, D(-1)=1 and t^(6s)=1. Thus S(-1)=I, directly from the regular two-block full-twist formula. The original braid matrices have no singularity there; no specialization of the rank-one formula with denominator t+1 is needed.

It follows that B_1=I mod (t+1). The matrix commutator filtration gives

    B_j=I mod (t+1)^j.

Combined with section 4, this proves part 3.

An explicit seed is u=[A_13,A_23], using A_13=sigma_2 sigma_1^2 sigma_2^-1 and A_23=sigma_2^2. These generate the free point-pushing group on the third strand, so their commutator is nontrivial and Brunnian. All the displayed b_j are therefore explicit examples.

## 6. Scope relative to BBB-MULTISET

This theorem detects an entire family inside Brun_4 intersect ker(rho_4 at -1), and includes arbitrarily deep elements in that specialization filtration. It does not claim that every braid in this intersection has the displayed nested-commutator form, nor faithfulness on the subgroup generated by arbitrary products of these words. Pairwise distinctness of the tower is for fixed u,r,s.

It also does not certify the particular unmodified Gamma attached to a member of the family. A chosen scalar Moody probe could remain blind even when the full matrix is known to be nonidentity.

The outer full twist here is on the SAME three-strand block that contains u. This placement matters. In particular, no identification with the different overlapping-block word Phi_0 in BBB-GAP.md is asserted, and Claude's pending explicit Gamma check has not been duplicated.

The geometric localization implication remains OPEN. The current source analysis identifies allowed four-to-three/one disk splices as the precise obstruction to inferring annular confinement from an extremal auxiliary-height packet.

## Prior-art scope

The reduction to faithful three-strand Burau, invariant Hermitian forms, rank-one projectors, and formal commutator filtrations are standard tools. The exact combined family statement above has not received a novelty check.

Related primary sources inspected in this pass: [Beridze–Traczyk, Forks, Noodles and the Burau Representation for n=4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.02641) and [Beridze–Bigelow–Traczyk, On the Burau Representation of B4 modulo p](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.11730). These papers study other degree and free-subgroup mechanisms; they are not cited as proving this family statement.
