# Known-class audit of K111

2026-11-03, prompted by Caleb's question. Two independent internal reviews.

The label "strongest candidate" was not supported by a certificate that
K111 lies outside existing Jones unknot-detection classes. Its exact
nonunit polynomial rejects it, but that calculation does not itself
establish new class coverage or a previously unknown example.

Established exclusions, assuming the archived construction and polynomial:

- Nontriviality plus determinant1 exclude alternating, two-bridge,
  quasi-alternating and the homologically thin/A-knot classes.
- Vanishing v2 excludes positive knots and their mirrors.

Unresolved memberships: semiadequate (either A or B), Montesinos, closed
three-braid, Whitehead double of a semiadequate companion, and the specific
Suzuki-graph family of Stoimenow Proposition4.4. The endpoint triples
(1,-5,10) and (-1,5,-10) pass the basic semiadequacy coefficient tests.
The minimal crossing number has not been certified to exceed24; a large
constructed diagram alone cannot do that.

Stoimenow4.4 is an existence theorem for some strongly n-trivial examples,
not a detection theorem for all strongly n-trivial knots. Source:
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0606255 , especially Sections3.2,3.3,4.3,4.4,6.

A proposed genus/Whitehead exclusion during this audit was withdrawn:
Howards--Luecke's old preprint says3g-1, while the published abstract gives
6g-3, insufficient for the three selected crossings of K111. No genus
or Whitehead exclusion is banked from that argument.

Verdict: the construction was tested exactly and rejected; it is NOT a
certified example outside all previously settled Jones-detection classes.
No new candidate generation or broad autonomous research was started.
