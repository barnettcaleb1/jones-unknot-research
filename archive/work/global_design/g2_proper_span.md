# The proper-span obstruction to a nonzero-exponent Jones kernel

Date: 2026-11-03. Node: G2-ZRHO-SPAN.

Status: TRUSTED elementary written deduction, with independent internal and Claude154624 reads. No novelty claim. This strengthens only the exponent-sum corollary of G2-PAIR, not its freeness or fixed-spin-line conclusions.

## Statement

Let W be the generic five-dimensional genus-two Jones module over Q(A), with its nondegenerate invariant Hermitian form H. For separating curves gamma_i, let T_i be the positive separating twists and ell_i their spin lines. Write

    U = span_K(ell_1,...,ell_k),
    G = <T_1,...,T_k>,
    N = ker(rho) intersect I_2.

If U is a proper subspace of W, then

    d_hat(N intersect G) = 0.

The conclusion holds even for the projective kernel. In particular it holds for every subgroup generated by ANY FOUR separating twists. U need not be nondegenerate, the lines need not be distinct, and no homological-splitting condition is required.

## Proof

Let P_i be the H-orthogonal rank-one projector onto ell_i. Rescale the raw TL operator of the upstairs separating twist by its eigenvalue on ker P_i. This gives

    R_i = I + (A^24 - 1) P_i,
    det R_i = A^24.

The actual normalized genus-two operator differs from R_i by a scalar, so a word is projectively trivial under rho exactly when the corresponding product of the R_i is scalar.

Because H is nondegenerate and dim U < 5,

    dim U-perp = 5 - dim U > 0.

Every P_i annihilates U-perp. Consequently every R_i and its inverse acts as identity on the same nonzero subspace. Suppose a word f has total signed twist exponent s and its rescaled product is lambda I. Applying it to a nonzero vector in U-perp gives lambda = 1. Taking determinants then gives

    1 = det(product R_i) = A^(24s),

so s = 0 because A is indeterminate. By the normalization d_hat(T_i)=1, s=d_hat(f). This proves the assertion.

No G2-ORTH, pair-freeness theorem, Taylor expansion, Casson formula or signature assertion is used.

## Consequence and limit

A generic Jones-kernel element with nonzero d_hat must, in EVERY expression as a product of separating twists and their inverses, use spin lines spanning all of W. In particular every such expression uses at least five distinct spin lines.

This is a necessary condition, not a construction or an exclusion of full-span words. For full span there is no common fixed vector. The determinant alone allows a scalar relation: if product R_i = lambda I, then lambda^5 = A^(24s), which does not force s=0. Thus the proof does not extend to the whole Torelli group and does not determine generic Z_rho.

It also does not exclude zero-exponent kernel words in a proper-span subgroup. The stronger two-generator freeness and fixed-spin-line exclusions remain separate results in g2_two_twist_freeness.md.
