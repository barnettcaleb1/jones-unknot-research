# N1: arithmetic, Burau entries, and what the four-strand trace actually asks

Bounded primary-source/theory read, 2026-11-03. No computation. This is a critique and a precise target, not an audit of the 2026 faithfulness proof.

## 1. Correct the source/mechanism, without denying Datta's arithmetic corollary

[Datta, arXiv:2204.13660](https://arxiv.org/html/2204.13660v1), Theorems 1.1/6.1, counts three-braid link classes with specified writhe and the SPECIAL VALUE P(-1), by binary quadratic forms of discriminant t²-4. Theorem 1.2 gives finiteness of fibers of the Alexander/Jones polynomial. Its mechanism is B3 -> SL2(Z), the central extension, the classification of integral conjugacy classes, and Birman-Menasco's closure classification. It is not an entrywise theorem for generic Burau matrices. Trace does not generally determine an integral conjugacy class; the class numbers measure precisely that failure.

Important qualification: [Datta's author research page](https://amiteshdatta.wixsite.com/amitesh-datta/research), item4, explicitly advertises the three-braid unknot-detection consequence of class number one for Q(sqrt(-3)). Thus do NOT say there is no arithmetic proof/corollary. The arXiv paper does not single it out as a theorem, while the author page does. Item2 lists a separate coefficient-formula paper for all three-braids and generic four-braids as “arXiv preprint available shortly”; that is distinct from 2204.13660 and was not available for checking here.

[Datta, arXiv:2209.10826](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.10826), Theorems1.4–1.5, proves generic non-kernel statements from a Garside/positive-word condition. The cancellation theory controls leading entries/rows, not the Jones Markov trace. It may be useful, but replacing its kernel target by a prescribed weighted-trace target is an additional theorem.

[Bharathram-Birman-Brendle, 2607.05283v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05283v2), proves/claims Burau4 faithfulness and derives Jones-representation faithfulness. Its point-pushing/Brunnian mechanism detects nonidentity operators. No assertion of Jones unknot detection is made. For this design round, treat it as the 2026 preprint claim already on the board, not a proof independently verified here.

## 2. The small TL4 block is not an unrelated obstacle

Let pi:B4 -> B3 send sigma1 and sigma3 to sigma1, and sigma2 to sigma2. The two-dimensional W(4,0) representation factors through pi and, after the usual scalar normalization, is Burau3.

Elementary reason: in the two cap-basis states (12)(34) and (14)(23), e1=e3. Thus rho(sigma1)=rho(sigma3), and the braid relations descend to B3. The remaining generic two-dimensional module is the standard two-dimensional Burau/Hecke module. This is explicitly used in [Korzun-Lanina-Sleptsov, arXiv:2402.02553v2, section2.3, equations(2.17),(2.22)](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.02553v2).

So N1's obstacle should not be described as “W(4,0) is not Burau.” Both nontrivial blocks are Burau-type. What is unknown is their COUPLED trace cancellation for a single braid beta and its quotient pi(beta).

For a completely explicit target, let rho_j be the bracket-normalized TL action on W(4,j), sigma_i=A I+A^-1 e_i, delta=-A²-A^-2, and e the exponent sum. The unreduced closure bracket is

    B(beta-hat)=tr rho_0(beta)+(delta²-1)tr rho_2(beta)
                +(delta^4-3delta²+1) A^e.

Therefore V(beta-hat)=1 is exactly

    tr rho_0(beta)+(delta²-1)tr rho_2(beta)
       +(delta^4-3delta²+1) A^e = delta(-A^3)^e.        (*)

This derivation uses the quantum dimensions of the three TL4 summands. As a normalization check, identity gives 2+3(delta²-1)+(delta^4-3delta²+1)=delta^4.

With t=A^-4 and rho_0=A^e Burau3(pi(beta)), rho_2=A^e Burau4(beta), equation (*) for a knot closure (e odd) becomes

    tr Burau3(pi(beta))+(t+1+t^-1)tr Burau4(beta)
      +(t²+t+1+t^-1+t^-2)
       =t^(-(e+1)/2)+t^(-(e-1)/2).

This is a hand-derived convention-specific identity, not an additional theorem of detection. It makes clear why proving a nonzero entry or even faithfulness does not suffice: neither excludes the coupled scalar equality.

## 3. Closest direct prior program and the missing step

Korzun-Lanina-Sleptsov's 2024 paper studies precisely closed-four-braid Jones-triviality using this character/trace structure, derives hypothetical HOMFLY families, and explicitly notes in section2.3 that faithfulness does not settle Jones detection. It predates BBB, but much of N1's proposed arithmetic/trace framework is already present. I located no full four-braid detection result in this bounded read; absence in a search is not an exhaustive status proof.

The exact new lemma N1 would need at width four is:

    A four-braid with one-component closure satisfying (*) has an
    unknot closure (preferably proved by a constrained normal form
    or by closure-preserving reductions).

BBB would help only after a further argument upgrades (*) to an operator identity or to a geometric arc condition. That upgrading step is the crux. Datta's entry formulas may constrain (*) for a generic normal-form class, but the exceptional class must be dealt with, and positivity of one row is not positivity of the weighted trace.

Even proving this would establish only braid index at most four. Arbitrary knots have unbounded braid index. A second, independent mechanism would be needed to reduce V=1 braids to four strands or to generalize the trace-descent theorem to all n. Neither follows from B4 faithfulness or quadratic class-number theory.

## 4. Global version, honestly scoped

The structurally appropriate all-n target is a Markov-compatible DESCENT statement: every one-component Jones-flat braid on more than one strand can be transformed, by conjugacy and exchange moves, to a destabilizable braid. Iteration gives the unknot. Such reductions already characterize the actual unknot in the Birman-Menasco theory; what is missing is forcing their existence from the weighted trace.

This is not yet a new mechanism: without an arithmetic or normal-form inequality that forces a destabilization/exchange configuration, it is another statement of the central difficulty. I would not recommend allocating a large design round to N1 on the strength of BBB alone. A legitimate bounded first step would identify one concrete trace-leading-coefficient statement in Datta's admissible-path formalism that forces a reduction and remains compatible with stabilization. I have not found that statement.

Barriers: the one-component braid permutation must enter the descent (B1); exchange/destabilization uses classical braid closures in S3 (B2); B5 is bypassed only if actual geometric reduction is proved, not by entrywise rank or faithful representation alone. The kernel-to-counterexample barrier B8 means an all-n proof must also contain full TL faithfulness strength somewhere, even if hidden in the descent.
