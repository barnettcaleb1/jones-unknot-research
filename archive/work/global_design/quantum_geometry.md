# Ordinary Jones, quantum propagation, and the exterior: a precise missing bridge

Design pass, 2026-11-03. No computation. Conclusion: I found no justified global mechanism from J_2=1 to character-variety triviality. The most precise new formulation is a monoidality defect of the universal long-knot invariant. It identifies exactly which extra information a tensor/fusion argument would need; it does not prove that information follows.

## 1. Fusion supplies a defect, not propagation

Use zero framing and normalized colored Jones J_N with J_1=1 and J_2=V. At generic q the universal long-knot element z_K is central in the appropriate completed quantum enveloping algebra, and on the simple N-dimensional representation V_N it acts as J_N(K;q) times the identity. The central expansion and colored evaluation are described in [Habiro, Theorems 2.1 and 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0211044).

The family of actions z_{K,V} is natural in V. Naturality is weaker than monoidality. Define the fusion defect

    F_K(V,W)=z_{K,V tensor W} - z_{K,V} tensor z_{K,W}.

For the fundamental object X=V_2, the decomposition X tensor X=V_1 direct-sum V_3 gives

    F_K(X,X)|V_1 = 1-J_2^2,
    F_K(X,X)|V_3 = J_3-J_2^2.

Consequently on J_2=1 the entire first defect is

    F_K(X,X) = (J_3-1) projection_onto_V_3.

This is an exact algebraic reduction of the first color-propagation step. If one could prove F_K=0 on all pairs of objects generated by X, then tensor generation and passage to direct summands would give J_N=1 for every N. Vanishing of F_K(X,X) alone gives only J_3=1; it is not the full propagation theorem.

The geometric content would have to be a theorem that the ACTUAL long-knot natural transformation becomes monoidal when its fundamental value is the identity. No such theorem emerged. Coloring one knot by X tensor X represents parallel copies following the same knotted trajectory; it is not the tensor product of two independent long knots placed side by side. The latter is z_{K,X} tensor z_{K,X}. Conflating these operations would set the defect to zero by fiat.

Likewise, the group-like property of the Kontsevich integral uses the coproduct on Jacobi diagrams, not the quantum-enveloping-algebra coproduct that acts on V tensor W. It does not make the central long-knot element group-like for the latter coproduct. The fundamental representation generates the tensor category, but specifying a natural transformation on that one object does not specify its values on tensor powers unless monoidality is already known.

This gives a precise revival criterion for a tensor/category route: an independent geometric relation forcing these fusion defects to vanish. Without it, this is a useful definition of the missing bridge, not a new proof mechanism. B1 and B2 must enter the proof of such a relation; the formal tensor algebra by itself supplies neither.

## 2. Ordinary-Jones Taylor series do not contain the proposed resurgent data

For a fixed knot write V_K(q)=sum_j a_j q^j. Then

    V_K(exp(h)) = sum_j a_j exp(jh)

is an entire function of h. If M=max |j| and C=sum |a_j|, its Taylor coefficients satisfy

    |[h^n]V_K(exp(h))| <= C M^n/n!.

Its ordinary Borel transform is also entire. On V_K=1 all positive-degree coefficients vanish. Thus one cannot extract nontrivial Borel singularities or nonabelian Chern-Simons actions from this fixed-color Taylor series using the standard divergent-series resurgence mechanism.

The relevant analytic geometry uses a different limit: color N grows while h tends to zero with u=N h fixed. [Garoufalidis-Le, analytic MMR Theorem 1](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/analyticMMR.pdf) proves that J_N(exp(u/N)) tends to 1/Delta_K(exp(u)) near u=0. This depends on the entire varying-color sequence, not its N=2 slice. In two-variable terms, knowing a function on u=2h does not determine it off that curve.

The modern resurgence program makes that extra input explicit. [Garoufalidis-Gu-Marino-Wheeler, Resurgence of Chern-Simons theory at the trivial flat connection](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-024-05149-6) uses an extended matrix of (x,q)-series associated to flat connections and analytic continuations of colored Jones/Kashaev data. It is not a theorem recovering that matrix from V_K alone. This distinction survives any choice of terminology for perturbative or nonperturbative saddles.

An analytic continuation or q-holonomic uniqueness argument therefore has two missing pieces: the equation/operator itself, which is knot-dependent, and enough data to select its solution. J_1=J_2=1 does not say that the constant sequence is annihilated by the knot's recurrence. Regularity or tame growth alone cannot repair that issue; finite Laurent-polynomial functions of x=q^N already give very tame alternatives vanishing at those two colors. The separate colored_propagation.md note makes this underdetermination explicit.

## 3. The all-colored endpoint must not be silently banked

There is an actual literature-status conflict to retain rather than resolve by choosing the optimistic citation. [Andersen 1206.2785](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2785) explicitly claims that equality of all colored Jones polynomials with the unknot implies unknothood; the current arXiv record is the single 2012 version and lists no journal reference. Some older papers cite the announcement as a theorem. However, [Detcherry's author-responsible 2023 ANR project summary](https://anr.fr/Project-ANR-23-ERCS-0008) explicitly lists colored-Jones unknot detection as a problem for the proposed research program.

This bounded read does NOT audit Andersen's proof and does not establish its precise acceptance status as of 2026. It does establish that our scaffold should not silently mark the endpoint PROVED on the strength of old announcement citations. Treat it as an unverified additional input unless a vetted theorem/proof is supplied. Categorified colored-Jones detection is a different, proved statement and does not resolve this issue.

Even granting all-colored detection provisionally, the fusion/color-propagation step from ordinary Jones remains open. Thus an endpoint audit cannot be used to hide the more immediate gap.

## Recommendation

Do not launch a general AJ, recurrence, or state-integral computation. The concrete target is an actual geometric reason for vanishing of the fusion defect on the Jones-unit locus. At present no such reason has been found. The fixed-color analytic obstruction and the tensor-product distinction rule out two shortcuts; neither proves the desired positive bridge.
