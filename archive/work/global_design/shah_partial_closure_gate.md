## What it supplies and what it does not

It passes the relevant partial-closure determinant screen:

    det P(-2,3,4)=|(-2)(3)+(-2)(4)+(3)(4)|=2.

Thus it is a legitimate example of the kind of determinant2 link that the
changed-shell condition permits. This does not give a two-slot shell.
The primary pictures specify only the one marked replacement T -> R;
they do not give two disjoint sites with C01=C10=L and C11 an actual U.

Using the EXISTING pretzel boxes as rational slots remains a Montesinos
construction after arbitrary rational substitutions, including the proposed
boundary operations. Whenever those closures are knots, known Jones
unknot detection for Montesinos links rules out a counterexample. The
relevant source is Stoimenow, Corollary4.1:
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0606255 .

Consequently the natural two-existing-box construction is closed without
computing an orbit. A new adapter must change the outside topology enough
to escape that class, and carry actual marks for all four closures. Calling
the inserted tangle nonrational does not provide that change. No such
adapter was obtained in this pass; no knot candidate is asserted.

This is not an exclusion of all uses of Shah's replacement. A non-Montesinos
outside shell, with its own marked compatibility identity and topology,
would be new evidence and would justify reopening it.
