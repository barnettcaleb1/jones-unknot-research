MID-01  D/N  Reversible finite classical positivity cannot model Jones
------------------------------------------------------------------------
At A=i*r, r>1, q=r^-4, d=r^2+r^-2, write B_i=rI-r^-1 e_i.
Its eigenvalues are r and -r^-3; B_i^-1 has dominant eigenvalue -r^3.
Perron--Frobenius for a proper cone rules out a scalar gauge making both
directions preserve one proper cone on the ordinary two-channel module.

Stronger finite-state theorem: if a three-strand model has finite entrywise
nonnegative invertible crossing matrices whose inverses are nonnegative,
and a fixed closure depending otherwise only on strand count and writhe,
it cannot recover Jones on all knot closures. Such matrices are monomial.
For a common even permutation period m, their powers commute, but the knots
 beta_+=sigma1^m sigma2^m sigma1 sigma2,
 beta_-=sigma2^m sigma1^m sigma1 sigma2
have equal writhe and model matrix while their actual Jones difference is
 -q^(m+2)(1+q+q^2)(sum_(j=0)^(m-1)(-q)^j)^2 !=0 for q>0, q!=1.
The same argument covers a positive finite retract with a pointed polyhedral
physical cone: cone automorphisms permute finitely many extreme rays.
It does not cover arbitrary nonpolyhedral density-operator models, nonpositive
projections, or infinitely many states; those retain separate information gaps.
Control calculation gives |V(q)|<=q^((p-3b-n+1)/2)(1+q)^(n-1) for an n-braid
with p positive and b negative crossings. V=1 implies p-3b<=n-1 and its mirror
inequality. For positive braid knots this gives the known unknot detector.
Proof/data: work/fanout16/physics.txt; physics_check.json.

MID-02  D/N  Globally periodic unknot rotors and a clasper-anchor failure
------------------------------------------------------------------------
An n-rotor in an unknot remains an unknot after the usual flip if its standard
local rotation extends globally preserving that unknot, with disjoint fixed
axis, and the flip normalizes the rotation with the standard marked action.
Proof: unknot+axis is a Hopf link; quotient Conway sphere has a rational side
by compression in T^2 x I; color-preserving Conway mutation preserves the Hopf
pair; lift over its distinguished axis. Does not apply to an asymmetric stator
where the local rotation has no global extension.
An explicit four-Y-clasper incidence model with one shared invariant surgery
circle cannot obtain its single-unknot anchor by direct pairwise inverse
channel cancellation: either cancellation pairing forces two separate non-axis
components. This excludes that anchor, not all shared-clasper constructions.
Proof: work/fanout16/geometric.txt, sections A,E. No actual successful rotor
or essential zero pattern was constructed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

is E(J) for J in S^3. A determinant-zero slope is the preferred longitude.
For the longitude branch link L_J,
 |V'_L_J(-1)|=|12 a_2(J)-1|,
where Delta_J is symmetric, Delta_J(1)=1 and a_2=Delta_J''(1)/2.

MID-04  D  Sato--Levine criterion and exact shell transmission
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For an oriented pattern link P union C with C unknotted, lk(P,C)=0, and a
componentwise strong inversion compatible with insertion, its quotient shell
defines a rank-two skein map F_P. If beta(P union C), the Sato--Levine invariant,
is nonzero, F_P is injective over Q(A). Proof: rational inputs Q_n give 1/n axis
surgeries P_n with a_2(P_n)=a_2(P_0)-n beta; rank-one outputs would be
proportional, and their actual unknot meridian closures force a framing-unit
ratio. MID-03 would force equal a_2, contradiction.
If P(U)=U, normalize the fixed meridional unit. In basis
 Q0, v=Q_infinity-delta Q0,
 F_P=diag(1,d_P(A)), d_P in Z[A,A^-1]. For
 x(T)=(f0(T)/f_infinity(T)-delta)/(1-delta^2),
 x(F_P(T))=d_P x(T). Factors multiply under compatible shell composition.
This is the quotient-shell factor, NOT the full annular bracket of P.

MID-12  C/D/N  Connected-sum amplification and the precise missing bounds
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For any knot J and hypothetical nontrivial V_K=1, all J#K^m stay in the
whole Jones fiber of J, while reduced rational Kh rank r, quantum diameter h,
and bridge number b satisfy
 r(J#K^m)=r(J)r(K)^m; h(J#K^m)=h(J)+m h(K);
 b(J#K^m)=b(J)+m(b(K)-1).
Nontriviality gives r(K)>1, b(K)>1 and h(K)>0 (the latter uses reduced/
unreduced exact sequence plus rational Lee). Therefore bounding ANY ONE of
r,h,b in ANY ONE nonempty whole Jones fiber would prove detection.
No such whole-fiber bound was proved. Prime-only/fixed-template bounds omit
exactly the connected sums above. Existence of some bounded fiber is as hard
as detection, since if detection holds the unit fiber is a singleton.
Subexponential r(K^m) or sublinear h(K^m) on a cofinal subsequence suffices.
Sublinear Turaev genus also suffices: reduced diagonal spread is additive,
bounded by Turaev genus, and a Jones-one nontrivial knot must have positive
diagonal spread. These are complete CONDITIONAL endpoints, not progress on
the needed estimate. A factorwise paired-skein attempt cannot give sublinear
cap windows: one cap retains K^(m-1), hence linear quantum diameter.
Proofs: work/research25/anchored_fiber_audit.txt;
work/research20/homology_scaffold.txt;
work/research21/global_homology_bridge.txt.
