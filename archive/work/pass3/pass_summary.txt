Third bounded Jones investigation, 31 October 2026

Outcome: no general proof, no nontrivial Jones-one knot, no unresolved candidate.

Main constructive test
----------------------
Use the quotient B4 -> B3 sending sigma1,sigma3 to a and sigma2 to b.
Its kernel is free on u=sigma1 sigma3^-1 and
v=sigma2 u sigma2^-1. The six fixed quotient lifts use exponent tuples
(-5,2,6,2), (-2,-1,1,7), (-2,-1,3,5), (-2,2,3,2),
(-1,1,2,3), (-2,2,1,4) in a^r b^s a^t b^w.
All have writhe5, so a connected Jones-one closure would automatically be
nontrivial by the unknot writhe bound for four-strand braids.

Every freely reduced kernel word of length at most16 was examined:
86,093,441 words, 258,260,640 connected closure instances across six seeds.
A modular evaluation excluded all but245. Full exact Laurent evaluation
rejected all245. These are diagram instances, not distinct knot types.
This is an exhaustive radius16 result for these six exact cosets, not a
classification of all four-braids or all quotient words.

Reproduce from work/pass3:
  clang++ -O3 -std=c++17 coset_search.cpp -o coset_search
  ./coset_search 16
  python3 coset_verify.py
The source tables and exact evaluator are included. The enumeration took
about33seconds here. The verifier also checks known knots and scattered
modular samples. Scripts overwrite their adjacent JSON/text records.

Assessment
----------
These are bounded exclusions and restricted mathematical results.
They do not resolve the Jones unknot-detection problem, establish that a
proof is close, or justify treating more of the same search as a solution.
