Ren's capped core cannot lose its remaining annular Jones channel

Statement. Let T be the four-strand tangle obtained from the six-strand braid

  sigma_2^-1 sigma_1^3 sigma_2^-1 sigma_4 sigma_5^-3 sigma_4

by capping strands 3 and 4 at both the top and bottom, retaining the outside
strands 1,2,5,6. For every four-strand braid B, the annular closure of TB has
Kauffman bracket degree exactly two in the essential-circle variable z.
There is no bound on the length of B or on its positive or negative exponents.
The closure need not be a knot. Braids on both sides of T are also covered,
since annular closure is cyclic.

This rules out annular bracket degree zero in this entire family. It does
not say that the ordinary S^3 Jones polynomial cannot equal one: the top
braid sigma_1^-1 sigma_3 gives an ordinary unknot, while retaining a nonzero
annular z^2 coefficient. The two conditions must not be conflated.

Conventions and exact coefficient calculation

Use delta=-A^2-A^-2 and sigma_i=A I+A^-1 e_i. The element T in TL_4 has
zero coefficient on the all-through diagram. This is reproduced by expanding
the ten crossings in TL_6, adding the two caps, and collecting TL_4 diagrams.
Consequently it lies in the ideal of diagrams with at most two through
strands. Multiplication by any braid preserves that ideal, so the degree of
the annular bracket is at most two.

The three-dimensional TL_4 representation rho with two through strands has
basis v_1,v_2,v_3 and
  e_i v_i=delta v_i, e_i v_(i+/-1)=v_i, all other images zero.
For every nonidentity TL_4 basis diagram, its matrix trace is its annular
z^2 coefficient. For the identity, the trace is 3 and the z^2 coefficient
is zero. Thus on the ideal containing TB the z^2 coefficient equals tr rho(TB).
The verifier checks this identity separately on all fourteen TL_4 diagrams.

Put x=-A^-4 and S=diag(1,A^2,A^4). Define
  M(x)=A^-2 S^-1 rho(T) S,
  R_i(x)=A^-1 S^-1 rho(sigma_i) S=I+E'_i,
where row i of E'_i has diagonal x-1, predecessor entry -x and successor
entry 1; all other entries vanish. Each R_i has determinant x, and
  R_i^-1=I-x^-1 E'_i.
Therefore every braid matrix U(x) is in GL_3(Z[x,x^-1]), including braids
with negative crossings. If w is the braid exponent sum, then
  [z^2] <annular(TB)> = A^(w+2) tr(M(x)U(x)).
All scalar factors are nonzero monomials; no division by delta is involved
in this trace identity or in the following specialization at x=1.

The exact matrix M is as follows. Unlisted entries M12 and M32 are zero.
  M11=-x^5-2x^4-3x^3-4x^2-3x-2
  M13=x^3+2x^2+3x+3+3x^-1+2x^-2+x^-3
  M21=-x^5-3x^4-4x^3-5x^2-5x-4-2x^-1-x^-2
  M22=-x^3-x^2+x^-1+x^-2
  M23=x^3+2x^2+4x+5+5x^-1+4x^-2+3x^-3+x^-4
  M31=-x^4-2x^3-3x^2-3x-3-2x^-1-x^-2
  M33=2x+3+4x^-1+3x^-2+2x^-3+x^-4.

In particular,
             [-15   0  15]                [-33   0   0]
  M(1) =    [-25   0  25],     M'(1) =   [-40  -8 -15].
             [-15   0  15]                [-15   0 -18]

Hence M(1) is zero modulo 5 and M'(1) is 2I modulo 5.

Proof of nonvanishing for every braid

Let u=(1,0,1)^t, v=(0,1,0)^t and r=(1,0,-1). The specialized generators
R_i(1) fix u and have determinant one; their inverses do also. Thus U_0=U(1)
is an integral determinant-one matrix fixing the primitive vector u. Moreover,
  M(1)=(-15u-25v)r,
so
  tr(M(1)U_0)=-15rU_0u-25rU_0v=-25rU_0v.

Suppose the entire Laurent polynomial tr(MU) were zero. Its value at 1
would be zero, forcing rU_0v=0. Consider the integral quotient Z^3/Z u.
It is free, with basis the classes of e_1 and e_2, since e_3=-e_1 in this
quotient. The first coordinate of a class is exactly the functional r.
Thus the induced two-dimensional integer matrix has upper-right entry zero.
Its determinant is one, because U_0 fixes u and det U_0=1. Its diagonal
entries are integers with product one, hence are both 1 or both -1.
It follows that
  tr U_0=1+2=3, or tr U_0=1-2=-1.

Now differentiate the allegedly zero Laurent polynomial. Since all entries
of U and M are integral Laurent polynomials, their derivatives at 1 are
integers. Reduction modulo 5 gives
  (tr(MU))'(1)
   =tr(M'(1)U_0+M(1)U'(1))
   =2 tr U_0 (mod 5).
The right side is respectively 6=1 or -2=3 modulo 5. It is never zero.
This contradicts identically zero tr(MU), proving that the annular z^2
coefficient is nonzero for every B. Together with the upper bound, its
annular bracket degree is exactly two.

Reproduction and independent checks

Run: python3 -S tangle_all_braids_obstruction.py
Required accompanying files are tangle_core_stdlib.py and search.py.
The verifier uses only the Python standard library and exact integers.
It rebuilds the 132-dimensional TL_6 expansion from the ten crossings,
performs the two caps, constructs the fourteen-dimensional TL_4 element,
and constructs the three-dimensional matrix M from TL generator products.
It reads no saved coefficient JSON or knot-theory software output. It then
checks the two exact matrices, the modulo-five identities, the annular
trace identity on all fourteen basis diagrams, and eight noncommuting
mixed-sign braid examples against the separate full TL_4 computation.

The matrix, gauge, all basis trace identities, and integral quotient argument
were also checked independently by the braid subagent from a separately
constructed exact TL_4 matrix. These are internal checks, not claims of
external peer review.
