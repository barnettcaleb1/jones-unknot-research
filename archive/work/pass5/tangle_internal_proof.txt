An all-integer classification of a mirrored internal Ren family

Conventions: delta=-A^2-A^-2 and sigma_i=A I+A^-1 e_i.
Let beta(r,k)=(sigma_2^r sigma_1^k sigma_2^r)
              (sigma_4^-r sigma_5^-k sigma_4^-r).
Cap strands 3 and 4 at both ends, retaining the four outside strands.

Proposition. The resulting four-strand tangle has zero coefficient on
the all-through Temperley--Lieb diagram if and only if
  (r,k)=(-1,3),(-1,-1),(1,1),(1,-3).
Consequently this is also exactly when any braid attached to its four
outside strands has annular bracket degree at most two.

Proof. Work in the five-dimensional TL_6 module with four through strands.
The basis vector v_i has its unique cup at adjacent positions i,i+1.
Then e_i v_i=delta v_i, e_i v_(i+/-1)=v_i, and all other images vanish.
The cap functional at positions 3,4 takes v_3 to delta, v_2 and v_4 to 1,
and v_1 and v_5 to 0.

Put z=-A^-4. After removing the scalar A^s, sigma_i^s acts as
  I+h_s e_i,  h_s=(z^s-1)/delta.
The scalar factors from the mirrored halves cancel. Acting on v_3,
the left half produces v_3+b(r,k)v_2+h_r h_k v_1, where
  b(r,k)=2h_r+delta h_r^2+h_r^2 h_k=h_(2r)+h_r^2 h_k.
The right half gives the reflected contribution with parameters -r,-k.
The two halves act trivially on each other's v_1,v_2 or v_4,v_5 terms.
Thus the all-through coefficient F is delta+b(r,k)+b(-r,-k).

Set D=delta^2=2-z-z^-1, C_j=z^j+z^-j, and S=C_r-2. Direct expansion yields
  delta^3 F = D^2+(D-1)S^2+(4D-2)S+S C_(r+k).                 (1)
For r=0 this is D^2, not zero. If |r|>1, specialize z to any nontrivial
|r|-th root of unity. Then S=0 and D is nonzero, so (1) is not identically
zero. For r=+/-1, S=-D and (1) simplifies to
  D(C_2-C_(r+k)).
This vanishes identically exactly when r+k=+/-2, giving the four pairs.
This proof covers all integer parameters, including zero and negative ones.

The all-through diagram is the quotient TL_4/I_2, where I_2 is the ideal
spanned by diagrams with at most two through strands. Its coefficient is
therefore multiplied by A^(exponent sum) by any attached four-strand braid.
No other TL_4 diagram can contribute z^4 under annular closure.

The new family (-1,-1) and its mirror do not give annular-invisible knots.
For (-1,-1), attach sigma_1^p sigma_3^q on the four remaining strands.
The z^2 coefficients of the four resolved blocks (I,e_1,e_3,e_1 e_3) are
  -A^6-A^-6, A^8, A^-8, 0.
Therefore the full z^2 coefficient a satisfies
  delta A^(-p-q) a=z^(p-2)+z^(q+2)-z-z^-1.                  (2)
Linear independence of Laurent monomials makes (2) zero exactly when
the multisets {p-2,q+2} and {1,-1} agree. The only possibilities are
  (p,q)=(1,-1),(3,-3).
In both cases the complete annular bracket equals delta. Crucially,
the actual knot-plus-axis diagrams simplify to the two-component unlink
in both Regina and Spherogram. Thus both patterns lie in a ball in the
solid torus and are unknots. Their mirrored cases are identical in scope.
The two remaining internal pairs are the original Ren family and its mirror,
whose commuting top-twist closures were classified in pass 4.

Verification files:
  tangle_internal_family.py/.json: exact formula checks, full four-block
    Laurent coefficients, axis-link PD codes, simplification results.
  tangle_four_strand.py/.json: the exact TL_4 core, arbitrary four-strand

Limits: The proposition concerns this two-parameter internal family.
It does not classify arbitrary mirrored three-strand words, and the two-site
top-twist conclusion does not classify arbitrary four-strand top braids.
The latter is a distinct larger family now represented exactly by TL_4 data.

A new three-site family has a short exact obstruction

Let T be the original Ren capped core, and attach sigma_1^p sigma_3^q
sigma_2^r, with arbitrary integer p,q,r. Its annular bracket always has
degree exactly two. The following establishes nonvanishing without any
bound on the parameters or any numerical approximation.

Write a0,a1,a3 for the z^2 coefficients of T,Te_1,Te_3, respectively, and
put U=-A^-6+A^-10-A^6+A^10. The exact TL_4 calculation gives
  [z^2](Te_2)=delta U,
  [z^2](Te_1e_2)=[z^2](Te_3e_2)=U,
and both blocks involving e_1e_3 have zero z^2 coefficient. Consequently,
with x=-A^-4 and h_s=(x^s-1)/delta, the channel a is
  a=A^(p+q+r) [a0+h_p a1+h_q a3+h_r U(delta+h_p+h_q)].

The expression N=A^-2 delta^2 A^(-p-q-r) a is a Laurent polynomial in x.
Its constant and linear Taylor coefficients at x=1 vanish. Its quadratic
Taylor coefficient is 25(p+q), and its cubic coefficient is
  (25/2)(p^2+q^2)+(35/2)p-(85/2)q+59-8r(p+q).
If a were identically zero then q=-p, after which the cubic coefficient is
  25p^2+60p+59=(5p+6)^2+23>0,
a contradiction. This uses finite Taylor data only as an obstruction to
equality; it does not infer equality from finite Taylor data.

The coefficients and all substitutions are computed over exact rational
numbers in tangle_three_site_taylor.py/.json. The independent affine-
exponent partition solver in tangle_three_site_locus.py/.json gives the
same empty zero locus in all eight parity classes. Eight mixed-sign triples
also checked the cleared-denominator expression against the TL_4 evaluator.
