BOUNDARY TEST OF EXTREME LEE-BAR IMBALANCE
2026-10-31. Bounded exploratory pass; no novelty or Jones-detection claim.

OUTCOME
The proposed fusion-one statement survives a complete small-knot table test.
Its apparently natural extension from fusion-one to ribbon knots with Lee
extortion order one is FALSE, with an actual fusion-two counterexample:

                R = 12n25 # mirror(12n25).

This is not a knot with Jones polynomial one. Its determinant is121.
It is a counterexample to the proposed proof mechanism's extreme-parity claim.

CONVENTIONS
P_K(t,q) is rational reduced Khovanov Poincare polynomial, with Euler
characteristic V_K(q^2), and the unknot polynomial1. For s=0 and all
generalized-Lee Q[X] bars of length1,

 P_K = 1+(1+t q^2) S_K,             S_K has nonnegative integer coefficients.
 V_K(q^2)-1 = (1-q^2) S_K(-1,q).

The proposed sufficient geometric condition is that at at least one of the
minimum and maximum quantum degrees supporting S_K, the even and odd
homological source counts differ. It would imply V_K !=1 for a nonempty
bar collection. It remains unproved here for fusion-one ribbon knots.

ACTUAL COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE BROADER CONDITION
KnotInfo's rational data give for K=12n25:
reduced rank21 and unreduced rank22. For a generalized-Lee Q[X] complex,
the free summand contributes ranks1 and2, a length1 bar contributes2 and2,
and each bar of length>=2 contributes2 and4. Thus R_unred-r_red=1 forces
all bars to have length1. The full bigraded identities are checked in
composite_counterexample.py, independently of that rank argument.

The nonzero coefficients of S_K are the following (h,q,multiplicity):
 (-2,-4,1),(-1,-4,1),(0,-2,1),(1,0,1),(1,2,1),
 (2,2,2),(3,4,1),(4,6,1),(5,8,1).
In particular its minimum source quantum degree already cancels.

Mirror duality gives S_mK=t^-1 q^-2 S_K(t^-1,q^-1). Reduced Kh obeys
the connected-sum product formula. Therefore the actual knot R has

 S_R = S_K+S_mK+(1+tq^2)S_K S_mK.

The two source extremes are:
 q=-14: h=-8 and h=-7, multiplicity1 each;
 q= 12: h= 6 and h= 7, multiplicity1 each.
Both extreme signed counts are zero. Every bar of R is still length1:
the connected-sum generalized-Lee complex is the tensor product over
Q[X]; tensoring two length1 two-term complexes splits into two shifted
length1 complexes. The result has reduced rank441 and unreduced rank442.

This R has fusion number EXACTLY2. KnotInfo gives bridge(K)=3. The standard
mirror-double ribbon presentation gives fusion(R)<=bridge(K)-1=2.
Bleiler--Eudave-Munoz prove that a composite fusion-one ribbon knot has a
two-bridge summand. The only prime factors of R are K and its mirror,
both bridge3, so fusion(R) cannot equal1. Nontriviality excludes fusion0.
This does not falsify the fusion-one conjecture, but proves one-band geometry
cannot be replaced by sliceness plus the same short-bar algebra.

THREE-UNKNOT TWIST-SAMPLE LEMMA: INDEPENDENT AUDIT
Parent proposed: a fixed two-strand full-twist family K_n with three distinct
unknot members consists entirely of unknots. The proof is sound subject to
the standard rational-tangle characterization below. Let M be the double
branched cover of the complementary two-string tangle. The different twist
integers give different filling slopes. One S^3 filling identifies M with
the exterior of its filling-core knot in S^3. A second different S^3 filling
forces this knot trivial, since no nontrivial knot admits nonmeridional S^3
surgery. Thus M is a solid torus. A two-string tangle whose double branched
cover is a solid torus is rational (standard equivariant disk theorem).
All K_n are then rational closures, with determinant |an+b|. Three distinct
n with determinant1 force a=0; hence all rational closures have determinant1
and are unknots. No initial irreducibility assumption is needed after the
first S^3 filling. The coherent T(2,2n+1) family has exactly two unknots and
does not contradict the three-sample statement. This does not turn three
Jones-one samples into three unknot samples without separate recognition.
