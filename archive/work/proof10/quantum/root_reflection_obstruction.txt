ROOT-OF-UNITY COLOR STRIPS DO NOT ADD COLORED INFORMATION
2026-10-31. Exact reductions and an actual-knot stress test.
No proof or counterexample to ordinary Jones unknot detection is claimed.

CONVENTION.
H_N(K;q) is the zero-framed, reduced N-dimensional colored Jones
polynomial, with H_1=1, H_2=V, and H_N(unknot)=1. Habiro's symmetric
cyclotomic form is
 H_N = sum_{k=0}^{N-1} a_k(q) P(N,k),
 P(N,k)=product_{j=1}^k(q^N+q^-N-q^j-q^-j),
where a_k is an integral Laurent polynomial and a_0=1.

EXACT INFORMATION-EQUIVALENCE THEOREM.
Fix a positive integer s. The data consisting of H_N(K;zeta) at every
root zeta of order r and every positive color N congruent to one of
 +/-1,...,+/-s modulo r contain exactly the same information as the
finite list of Laurent polynomials H_1(K;q),...,H_s(K;q).

Proof. At q^r=1, q^N+q^-N depends only on N modulo r up to sign.
If N is congruent to +/-j, the product P(N,k) vanishes as soon as
k reaches the least positive representative of +/-j. Thus the
cyclotomic sums for H_N and H_j reduce to identical finite sums.
This is the Kirby--Melvin color reflection/periodicity principle,
here derived directly from the integral cyclotomic expansion.
Consequently the initial s Laurent polynomials determine every
specified root evaluation. Conversely, for any fixed j<=s, the
evaluations with N=r-j and arbitrarily large r recover H_j at
infinitely many distinct roots, which determines its Laurent polynomial.

In particular, all levels and all colors N congruent to +/-1 or
+/-2 supply exactly the ordinary Jones polynomial, and no more.
If V_K=1, this entire infinite set of growing-color values equals1.
This conclusion is real, but cannot by itself reconstruct color3.

ACTUAL-KNOT COUNTERTEST TO RECONSTRUCTION.
The explicit braids below have equal full ordinary Jones polynomials:
 5_1: [-1,-1,-1,-1,-1]
 10_132: [-1,2,-3,-2,3,-4,3,-2,1,-3,2,-3,4,-3,-3,-2].
The exact Temperley--Lieb calculation gives for both
 V(A)=A^8+A^16-A^20+A^24-A^28.
The exact spin-one R-matrix calculation at quantum-group parameter2
(the square root of the ordinary Jones variable, up to mirroring)
gives different reduced color3 values:
 H_3(5_1)=208357048576,
 H_3(10_132)=12565016366179/4.
Therefore the two knots are nonisotopic, yet all their root-of-unity
data in the entire +/-1,+/-2 strip agree at every level.

The accompanying script reruns both exact calculations and checks
180 reflection identities in Z[q]/(q^r-1), for r=3,...,14, with
deliberately arbitrary Laurent cyclotomic coefficients. Those checks
validate implementation; the preceding proof covers all levels.

PRECISE DIVISIBILITY LEFT BY ORDINARY JONES EQUALITY.
If V_K=V_L, then a_1(K)=a_1(L), since
 H_2=1+a_1(q)(q^2+q^-2-q-q^-1).
Hence for every N>=3,
 H_N(K)-H_N(L) is divisible in Z[q,q^-1] by
 P(N,2)=q^(-2N) product_{j=1,2}(q^(N-j)-1)(q^(N+j)-1).
For V_K=1, take L=unknot. This is a useful exact candidate filter.
The divisor has Laurent support from -2N to2N, of width4N.
Consequently a defect with support width less than4N must vanish.
For N=3 its cyclotomic factorization, up to a monomial, is
 (q-1)^4 (q+1)^2 Phi_4(q) Phi_5(q).
It vanishes at exactly the low orders1,2,4,5, with the listed
multiplicities. Sampling only those roots cannot resolve color3.
The divisor is optimal for arbitrary cyclotomic coefficient sequences
(set only a_2 unequal); optimality for sequences realized by knots is
not asserted.

AN ATTRACTIVE ASYMPTOTIC BRIDGE FAILS SHARPLY.
One might hope that the growing colors r-2, whose normalized values
are forced to1 by V=1, control color r at the same rth root and
therefore hyperbolic volume. Even for actual knots, there is a severe
boundary effect between these colors. For every knot, H_(r-1)(zeta_r)=1.
For the figure-eight knot E, H_(r-2)(zeta_r)=V_E(zeta_r) tends to1,
whereas H_r(zeta_r) grows exponentially. Moreover the missing
exponential behavior is already present in the first q derivative
of H_(r-2) at the root. This last assertion admits a short exact proof.

FIGURE-EIGHT DERIVATIVE LEMMA.
Let zeta_r=exp(2pi*i/r) and theta=pi/r. Then
 lim (2pi/r) log |dH_(r-2)(E;q)/dq at q=zeta_r|
 equals the corresponding limit for H_r(E;zeta_r), namely the
hyperbolic volume of E, approximately2.0298832128.
In contrast, (2pi/r)log|H_(r-2)(E;zeta_r)| tends to0.

Proof. The figure-eight has a_k=1 for every k. Put
 f_1=2cos(4theta)-2cos(2theta),
 Q_r=sum_{k=2}^{r-3} product_{j=3}^k
                  4sin((j-2)theta)sin((j+2)theta).
All terms of Q_r are positive for r>=6. Differentiating the
cyclotomic sum, only the unique vanishing factor j=2 contributes
to terms k>=2. Direct differentiation gives
 H'_(r-2)(zeta_r)=(-2i/zeta_r)*[
   (r-2)sin(4theta)+sin(2theta)+r*f_1*sin(4theta)*Q_r ].

To compare growth rates, write
 h_k=product_{j=1}^k4sin^2(jtheta), so H_r(zeta_r)=sum_{k=0}^{r-1}h_k.
The product appearing in Q_r obeys the telescoping identity

 product_{j=3}^k4sin((j-2)theta)sin((j+2)theta)
 ------------------------------------------------ =
                         h_k

 sin((k+1)theta)sin((k+2)theta)
 ---------------------------------------------------------------.
 16sin((k-1)theta)sin(k theta) product_{j=1}^4sin(jtheta)

For 2<=k<=r-3 this ratio is bounded above by C*r^6. At a largest
term h_k, whose index is asymptotic to5r/6, the ratio is bounded
below by c*r^4. The largest term is in this index range for all
sufficiently large r and grows exponentially. Since a sum of r
positive terms lies between its largest term and r times that term,
 c*r^3*H_r <= Q_r <= C*r^6*H_r
for large r, after changing positive constants. Thus Q_r and H_r
have the same exponential growth rate. The coefficient
r*f_1*sin(4theta) has magnitude asymptotic to48*pi^3/r^2; the other
two terms in the derivative bracket are bounded. These polynomial
factors and the bounded subtraction do not change the positive
exponential rate. This proves the derivative assertion from the
known figure-eight Kashaev limit. Finally
 H_(r-2)(zeta_r)=1+2cos(4pi/r)-2cos(2pi/r)=1+O(r^-2),
which proves its zero rate.

IMPLICATION FOR THE PROOF SEARCH.
The root-reflection mechanism yields a genuine exact reduction, but
its infinite set of higher-color outputs is just a re-encoding of
the ordinary Jones polynomial. Any argument using it to obtain new
geometry needs additional information transverse to this set, such
as controlled colored derivatives or values in an interior fusion
channel. The actual figure-eight calculation prevents assuming such
control from generic continuity or polynomial dependence alone.
No assertion is made that the special fiber V=1 cannot satisfy an
additional rigidity theorem; that would be the genuinely new step.
