INDEPENDENT AUDIT: FOUR-PRETZEL KNOTS WITH A UNIT BOX
31 October2026. Checked algebra and topology; no novelty claim.

Claim. Among all four-strand pretzel knots having at least one parameter+/-1,
V''(1)=V'''(1)=0 leaves only the unknot and
 K*=P(8,-3,-3,1), or its mirror.
Consequently V''(1)=V'''(1)=V''''(1)=0 detects the unknot in this entire class.

Conventions and geometry. A four-strand pretzel is a knot precisely when
exactly one parameter is even. Mirror if necessary to make the selected unit
parameter+1. Unit boxes commute with other boxes by an actual isotopy, as
recorded in the introduction of Stipsicz–Szabo, arXiv2006.06765. Thus the
parameters can be arranged as(p,q,r,1), with p even and q,r odd, without
assuming arbitrary permutation of nonunit boxes. Set q=a+b,r=a-b, where a,b
are integers of opposite parity.

Use D=t d/dt; mirror changes do not affect the joint derivative-zero condition.
The independently derived mixed-four moment identities give
 D²V=(3/4)(3-Q2-2pT-p²),
 and under D²V=0,
 D³V=(3/4)F3,
 F3=2Q3-2T+p+3pT²-p³,
 where T=q+r+1 and Qj=q^j+r^j+1.
The ordinary Jones first derivative always vanishes, so ordinary derivatives
2and3 vanish exactly when these two logarithmic moments vanish.

Substituting T=2a+1 and Q2=2a²+2b²+1, D²V=0 becomes
 b²=1-a²-p²/2-p(2a+1).
As Q3=2a³+6ab²+1, direct substitution into F3 gives
 F3=-(p+2a)^3+4(p+2a).
Therefore c=p+2a belongs to{0,-2,2}.

If c=0 or-2, substitution in the square equation gives
 b²=(a+1)².
Thus one of q,r equals-1 and the other is2a+1. Cancel the opposite integer
boxes-1,+1. The remaining two-strand pretzel is the torus knot
 T(2,p+2a+1)=T(2,c+1), hence the unknot because c+1=+/-1.

If c=2, substitution gives
 b²=(a+1)²-4,
 so (a+1-b)(a+1+b)=4. The two integer factors have the same parity; the only
possibilities are(2,2) and(-2,-2). Therefore
 (a,b,p)=(1,0,0) or(-3,0,8).
The first case is P(0,1,1,1), the connected sum of three T(2,1) unknots.
The second is K*=P(8,-3,-3,1). Both obey the required parameter parity.
There are no omitted free parameters or bounded-search assumptions.

The actual PD diagram of K* was independently evaluated with Regina and TL2:
 V_K*(t)=1+t^-8(t-1)^4 Q(t),
 Q=t^8+2t^7+4t^6+5t^5+6t^4+5t^3+4t²+2t+1.
Thus det(K*)=33 proves nontriviality, and V_K*''''(1)=24Q(1)=720.
For the mirror the order-four leading Taylor coefficient is unchanged, since
substitution t->t^-1 changes (t-1)^4 by a unit with value1 at t=1.
Hence the fourth derivative excludes the only nontrivial possibilities.

This is a complete class result, independently audited from the equations in
mixed_moments.py and the exact diagram certificates in mixed_counterexample.py.
It makes no claim about four-strand pretzels without a unit parameter or about
the generic Jones unknot problem.
