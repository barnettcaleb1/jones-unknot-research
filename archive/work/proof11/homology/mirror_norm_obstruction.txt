WHY MIRROR DOUBLING DOES NOT TURN JONES INTO A POSITIVE HOMOLOGY NORM
Status: exact algebra plus actual-knot countertest; not a Jones-one counterexample.

Let H=Kh_red(K;Q). Write e_j and o_j for the dimensions in even and odd
homological degrees at quantum degree j. Over Q, mirror duality and the
connected-sum Kunneth theorem identify Kh_red(K#mirror(K)) with H tensor H*.
At quantum degree zero, its Euler coefficient and its actual dimension are
  E0 = sum_j (e_j-o_j)^2,
  D0 = sum_j (e_j+o_j)^2.
Consequently
  D0-E0 = 4 sum_j e_j o_j.
The ordinary Jones polynomial of the mirror double supplies E0, not D0.
If V_K=1 then E0=1, but no control of the discarded nonnegative quantity
sum_j e_j o_j follows from this identity.

This loss occurs for actual classical knots, not just abstract complexes.
The knots 5_1 and mirror(10_132) have the same Jones polynomial and different
rational reduced Khovanov homology, of ranks 5 and 11. Their mirror doubles
therefore have equal Jones polynomials and reduced ranks 25 and 121.
For 5_1, (E0,D0)=(5,5). For 10_132, (E0,D0)=(5,21).
The raw 10_132 quantum-degree (even,odd) dimensions are
 -14:(0,1), -12:(1,0), -10:(0,1), -8:(2,1), -6:(1,1),
 -4:(1,0), -2:(1,1).
The computed exact data appear in verify_obstructions.json.
These examples refute a universal formula recovering the homology norm from
the ordinary mirror-double Jones polynomial; they do not settle its unit fiber.

A MORE GENERAL TRACE OBSTRUCTION
For a bigraded super vector space H, let c_m cyclically permute H^tensor m
using the Koszul signs, and let Q denote total quantum grading. Then
  str(c_m q^Q)=chi_H(q^m).
Only basis tensors x^tensor m contribute. If x has parity p, the cyclic
permutation gives (-1)^((m-1)p) and supertrace gives (-1)^(mp), whose product
is (-1)^p. For a permutation with cycle lengths l_i, the trace is therefore
product_i chi_H(q^l_i). Thus these canonical permutation traces only repack
Euler characteristics; an equal-quantum even/odd pair remains invisible.
An ordinary unsigned flip is not the symmetric braiding in chain complexes;
using it without Koszul signs generally fails to commute with the differential.
Ordinary dimensions on homology are valid extra invariants but are not supplied
by the ordinary Jones trace.

GEOMETRIC RT VERSION
Cut a knot along a single arc. At generic q the fundamental representation is
simple, so its (1,1)-tangle RT operator is a scalar by Schur's lemma. With
framing normalization that scalar is V_K. Reflecting the tangle therefore
produces a positive scalar norm |V_K|^2, but proving the scalar equals 1 gives
no additional constraint on the knot embedding. Cutting into more strands
creates a larger operator space; its positive norm is measured by additional
closures/cables, not by the given scalar Jones polynomial.
