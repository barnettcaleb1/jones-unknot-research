UNKNOT-ANCHORED MULTICURVES: WHAT IS RIGOROUS AND WHAT IS STILL MISSING
2026-10-31. This note separates a geometric constraint from a realizability
claim not established by the cited paper.

SOURCE AND PRECISE SCOPE
Kotelskiy--Lidman--Moore--Watson--Zibrowius, Cosmetic operations and Khovanov
multicurves, Math. Ann. 389 (2024), 2903--2930, Section 4:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11139753/
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-023-02697-5
The paper explicitly works over F_2. Integer Euler characteristics are still
formed from the bigraded dimensions, but these are not rational-Khovanov ranks.

If T(infinity)=U, choose meridian-longitude parametrization in the double
branched cover. Section 4 proves that Kh(T) is a single r_1(s), with s an even
integer, together with special curves all of slope infinity. There are special
curves if T is non-rational (Theorem 2.6). The reason is an actual rank-one
pairing at infinity, not just equality of Jones polynomials: the rational
component meets the vertical anchor arc once, and all special components are
disjoint from it. The paper's cosmetic conclusion uses equality of
full homology, not just equality of Euler polynomials.

EXACT EULER EQUATION FOR ANY OTHER FILLING
Write C_i for the shifted special components and R for the shifted rational
component. Let a_r be the rational filling arc, and define
 E_i(r;q)=sum_(h,j) (-1)^h q^j dim_F2 HF(a_r,C_i)^(h,j).
The direct-sum pairing theorem gives
 J^Kh_T(r)(q)=E_R(r;q)+sum_i E_i(r;q),
where J^Kh is the graded Euler normalization determined by reduced Khovanov
homology. For knot fillings, J^Kh_T(r)(q)=V_T(r)(q^2). The standard common
orientation/framing normalization is included in each summand. The anchor gives E_i(infinity;q)=0 separately for every i. Thus the
anchor does not, by itself, compare the absolute shifts of different C_i.

A PRECISE SUFFICIENT LEMMA TO TARGET
For a filling r at which the rational sector has Euler polynomial equal to the
unknot, it would suffice to show
  sum_i E_i(r;q) != 0 whenever at least one C_i is present.
This is weaker than requiring every homological group to have one parity.
A still stronger but concrete sufficient test is that at one extremal quantum
degree of the aggregate special sector the signed dimension is nonzero.
If the sum vanishes, its positive and negative terms must balance at every
quantum degree. Merely knowing each E_i is nonzero does not rule that out.
For a filling whose rational sector is not the unknot, the exact equation
required instead is sum_i E_i(r;q)=1-E_R(r;q); the proposed zero-sum lemma alone
would not handle that more general case.

WHY THE GEOGRAPHY STATEMENT DOES NOT ALREADY PROVE THE LEMMA
Take one allowed graded vertical special curve C and its homological shift
C[1], with identical quantum grading. These satisfy the same slope/length
geography. Pairing with any arc is additive and commutes with grading shifts,
so E_(C[1])(r;q)=-E_C(r;q) for EVERY rational filling r. Both are invisible to
the anchor. Accordingly R union C union C[1] has the same Euler polynomial
as R under every rational filling, while retaining extra homology away from
the anchor. This is an exact formal curve/complex model, NOT an asserted
classical tangle invariant. It may fail further realization constraints,
including constraints on being the cone of the actual Bar-Natan invariant.
The source's curve classification permits arbitrary overall component shifts
as abstract bigraded complexes; it does not assert every such collection is
realized by a classical tangle, nor prove the needed exclusion.

WHAT ADDING JONES SAMPLES CAN AND CANNOT DO
At the decategorified tangle level, all rational closures are linear functionals
on the two-dimensional Temperley--Lieb/Kauffman bracket skein of a four-ended
ball. Additional Jones samples can recover that skein vector, but cannot
separate two curve models whose full decategorifications agree. In particular
no number of Euler-only fillings detects the formal C plus C[1] sector.
A proof must constrain classical-tangle realization or use genuinely additional
homological/geometric data; an argument based only on the stated slope
geography and more Jones fillings would omit precisely this point.

The generic-tangle agent separately investigates a single-special-component
case and its exact Euler polynomial; this note does not preempt that calculation.
