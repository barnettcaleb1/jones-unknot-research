FIXED SATELLITE RIBBONIZATION: EXACT OBSTRUCTIONS AND THE REMAINING GAP
Status: elementary consequences of standard satellite and colored-Jones formulas;
not a solution of Jones unknot detection and no novelty claim. 2026-10-31.

CONVENTIONS
H_N(K;q) is the zero-framed, dimension-N colored Jones polynomial normalized
by H_N(U;q)=1. Thus H_2=V and H_1=1. Quantum integers are
[n]=(q^(n/2)-q^(-n/2))/(q^(1/2)-q^(-1/2)). Coefficients may lie in Q(q^(1/4)).
A fixed satellite pattern is embedded using the zero framing of each companion.

1. A UNIVERSALLY SLICE PATTERN HAS WINDING ZERO
Suppose P(K) is slice for every knot K. Litherland's signature formula gives
  sigma_P(K)(omega)=sigma_P(U)(omega)+sigma_K(omega^w),
where w is the algebraic winding number of P. Since P(U) is slice, its signature
vanishes at regular omega. If w is nonzero, choose K a trefoil and omega away
from the finitely many exceptional roots with omega^w in an open arc on which
the trefoil signature is nonzero. This contradicts sliceness of P(K). Thus w=0.
This includes every fixed universally ribbon or universally fusion-one pattern.
It DOES NOT apply if sliceness is assumed only for companions whose Jones
polynomial is 1; the latter unknown locus might have further restrictions.

2. WINDING ZERO IMPLIES A FINITE EVEN-LABEL EXPANSION
The annular Kauffman bracket skein is Q(A)[z], graded by mod-2 homology, with
z of odd parity. Skein resolutions preserve mod-2 homology. A winding-zero
pattern therefore has only even powers of z. The Chebyshev basis S_j(z),
S_0=1, S_1=z, S_(j+1)=z S_j-S_(j-1), respects this parity. Consequently
  V_P(K)(q)=sum_(j even, 0<=j<=J) b_j(q) H_(j+1)(K;q)
for finitely many coefficients b_j independent of K. Fixed pattern framing,
quantum dimensions, and Jones normalization are absorbed in b_j. Zero-framed
companions are essential in making these coefficients independent of K.
The displayed identity is a standard satellite skein evaluation, not a claim
that ordinary Jones determines the colored polynomials on its right.

3. A FINITE-FREQUENCY TEST THAT EXCLUDES THREE UNIVERSAL IDENTITIES
Let K_m be the chirality of T(2,2m+1) with the following fusion formula:
  H_(j+1)(K_m;q) = [j+1]^-1 sum_(a=0)^j
    (-1)^(j-a) [2a+1] q^((2m+1)(a(a+1)-j(j+2))/2).
The opposite chirality replaces q by q^-1 and gives the same conclusions.
One obtains the formula by fusing j tensor j into labels 2a and including
zero-framing correction. At m=0 it is identically 1. For j=1 and m=1 it is
q^-1+q^-3-q^-4, agreeing with the mirror of the usual positive trefoil.

As a function of m this is a sum of nonzero exponentials q^(lambda m), where
  lambda_(j,a)=a(a+1)-j(j+2).
For even j all lambda are even. For j=1 the two slopes are -3 and -1.
For even j>=2 the least slope is -j(j+2), occurring exactly once, and the
largest is -j. All slopes are nonpositive. Distinct exponential sequences are
linearly independent over Q(q^(1/4)): evaluate m=0,...,n-1 and use the
Vandermonde determinant. The least slope from the largest label J in a finite
nonzero even-label sum cannot be canceled by any smaller label.

It follows that NO fixed winding-zero pattern P can satisfy, for every K,
  (a) V_P(K)=V_K;
  (b) V_P(K)=V_K^2;
  (c) V_P(K)=V_K V_mirror(K).
For (a), target slopes are odd. For (b), target slopes are -6,-4,-2, whereas
any nonconstant even-label sum has an uncanceled least slope at most -8.
For (c), target slopes include +2 with nonzero coefficient, whereas all
available slopes are nonpositive. Constants cannot realize any of the three.
This rules out these natural one-input universal ribbonization identities.
The argument concerns fixed finite patterns, not limits or K-dependent patterns.

4. THE LOGICAL GAP BETWEEN AN IDENTITY AND PRESERVING THE UNIT FIBER
The desired weaker implication is
  V_K=1 ==> V_P(K)=1.
The frequency argument does NOT refute this implication: it tests all torus
knots, most of which do not satisfy the antecedent. With P(U)=U, the implication
would amount to proving
  sum_(j even>=2) b_j(q)(H_(j+1)(K;q)-1)=0
on the Jones-one locus. Habiro's expansion shows V_K=1 kills its first
cyclotomic coefficient, but does not algebraically force the subsequent
coefficients to vanish. There is no known implication here from our work, and
no actual knot counterexample to such an implication has been constructed.
A successful reduction also needs P(K) nontrivial whenever K is nontrivial;
a local unknot pattern preserves the unit fiber vacuously and is useless.

5. WHY A TWO-INPUT MIRROR CONSTRUCTION ESCAPES THE WINDING ARGUMENT
For a two-companion infection the signature contributions are
sigma_K(omega^w1)+sigma_mirror(K)(omega^w2).
These cancel when |w1|=|w2|. Thus a fixed two-input construction fed K and its
mirror need not have winding zero in either input. The standard connected sum
K#mirror(K) is ribbon and has Jones V_K V_mirror(K), so it preserves V=1.
However it need not have fusion number one. In particular our prior exact
example 12n25#mirror(12n25) has fusion number 2: bridge number 3 gives the upper
bound, and the Bleiler--Eudave-Munoz theorem that a composite fusion-one ribbon
knot has a two-bridge summand gives the lower bound. Both prime summands here
have bridge number 3. See work/proof10/homology/result.txt.
Simply tying K into a single local arc and its mirror into another keeps the
connected-sum factors. More involved interactions can escape this particular
obstruction, but require an independent calculation of their Jones behavior.
No construction with all three properties (fusion one, nontriviality transfer,
and Jones-one preservation) has been obtained.

6. ODD COLORS DO NOT LOSE THE ORDINARY JONES POLYNOMIAL AS A WHOLE
A finite winding-zero pattern accesses finitely many odd-dimensional colors.
The ENTIRE sequence of odd-dimensional colored Jones polynomials determines
H_2: at each odd level r the root-reflection identity gives
  H_(r-2)(K;zeta_r)=H_2(K;zeta_r).
Here r-2 is odd, so infinitely many odd levels supply infinitely many values
of the Laurent polynomial H_2. Polynomial identity then gives uniqueness.
Thus our finite-pattern obstruction must not be exaggerated to a loss of all
information in the complete odd-color sequence. See the normalization audit in
work/proof10/quantum/root_reflection_obstruction.txt.

INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED STRENGTHENING
The braid agent subsequently proved the stronger statement that if a fixed
satellite satisfies V_P(K)=f(H_N(K)) for a rational function f and every knot K,
then f is affine; its colored expansion has only colors H_1 and H_N. For N=2,
winding-zero parity therefore forces f constant. The proof compares the top
two coefficients of the torus-family polynomials G_j(x), x=q^-m: their ratio
is universally -q[3], while the ratio for a degree-d polynomial in G_j is
-d q[3]. Lower colors and lower powers miss these two degrees. Rational f is
polynomial by a Bezout argument after substitution. I independently audited
both steps and found no framing or parity gap. Full proof and exact checks:
work/proof11/braid/satellite_functional_rigidity.txt
