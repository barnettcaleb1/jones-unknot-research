INDEPENDENT AUDIT: FIXED SATELLITES CONSTANT ON ORDINARY-JONES FIBERS
2026-10-31. Verdict: the theorem and the stated finite test bound are valid
with the zero-longitude satellite convention. No claim of novelty is made.

1. PRECISE THEOREM

Let q be an indeterminate, F=Q(q^(1/4)), and let H_N(K;q) be the reduced,
zero-framed, N-dimensional sl_2 colored Jones invariant, normalized by
H_N(U)=1, H_1=1, H_2=V. Consider a finite combination

    F_c(K)=sum_(N=1)^M c_N(q) H_N(K;q),       c_N in F.

Assume that for ALL oriented knots A,B,

    V_A=V_B  implies  F_c(A)=F_c(B).

Then c_N=0 for every N>=3. Conversely, any combination c_1+c_2 V has
the stated property. Thus no regularity, rationality, computability, or
pointwise-in-q assumption on the dependence is necessary: it may be an
arbitrary function on the set of realized full Jones polynomials.

For a fixed single-component satellite pattern P, formed using the
preferred zero-framed companion longitude, the conclusion becomes

    V_(P(K)) = V_(P(U))                         if winding(P) is even,
    V_(P(K)) = V_(P(U)) V_K                    if winding(P) is odd.

These formulas characterize its invariant action, not the topology of P.
In particular, if P(U)=U, the two possible actions are 1 and V_K.

2. ALL-COLOR WITNESS USED IN THE PROOF

Take the knots specified by the explicit braid words

    K: B_2, [-1,-1,-1,-1,-1],
    L: B_4, [1,1,1,-2,-1,-1,-2,-3,2,-3,-3].

They have equal ordinary Jones polynomial

    q^-2+q^-4-q^-5+q^-6-q^-7.

The exact computation and Habiro argument in
all_color_collision_proof.txt prove, for every N>=3,

    H_N(K;e^h)-H_N(L;e^h)
      =8(N^2-1)(N^2-4)h^5+O(h^6),

so this difference is a nonzero Laurent polynomial. The exact Habiro
coefficient identity is

    Delta a_2 = q^-15(q^2-1)(q^3+1)(q^5+1).

The ordinary and color-three calculations, normalization, and this factor
were independently audited; this is not inference from finitely many colors.

3. CONNECTED SUM AND LINEAR INDEPENDENCE

Reduced colored Jones is exactly multiplicative under connected sum when
all knots have zero framing:

    H_N(A#B)=H_N(A)H_N(B).

There is no extra quantum-dimension factor in the reduced convention.
The connected sum of zero framings is again the zero framing.

For every knot J, V_(K#J)=V_(L#J). The hypothesis therefore gives

    sum_N c_N (H_N(K)-H_N(L)) H_N(J)=0.                 (1)

The functions H_N(J) are linearly independent over F. Here is a direct
proof that also verifies the needed fixed-variable interpretation.
For J_m=T(2,2m+1), m>=0, choose a chirality convention in which

    H_(j+1)(J_m)=G_j(q^-m),

    G_j(x)=1/[j+1] sum_(a=0)^j (-1)^(j-a)[2a+1]
      q^((a(a+1)-j(j+2))/2) x^(j(j+2)-a(a+1)),

where [n]=(q^(n/2)-q^(-n/2))/(q^(1/2)-q^(-1/2)).
All x-exponents are nonnegative; the a=j exponent is j. The leading
degree and coefficient are

    deg_x G_j=j(j+2),
    [x^(j(j+2))]G_j=(-1)^j q^(-j(j+2)/2)/[j+1] != 0.

Thus the G_j have pairwise distinct degrees. A finite relation among
H_N(J) on every knot gives a polynomial in F[x] vanishing at every
x=q^-m. These are infinitely many DISTINCT elements of F because q
is an indeterminate. Hence the polynomial is zero, and its distinct
leading degrees force every coefficient of the relation to vanish.

Applying this to (1), c_N Delta H_N=0 for each N. The field F is an
integral domain and Delta H_N !=0 for every N>=3, so c_N=0 for N>=3.
For N=1,2 the differences vanish, and no restrictions on c_1,c_2 follow
or are needed. This completes the algebraic theorem.

4. SATELLITE EXPANSION AND THE FRAMING AUDIT

The Kauffman bracket skein module of a solid torus is F[z]. Its monic
Chebyshev/Jones--Wenzl basis is

    S_0=1, S_1=z, S_(j+1)=z S_j-S_(j-1).

For a fixed framed pattern P, write [P]=sum_j b_j S_j, a finite sum.
Inserting a zero-framed companion K evaluates S_j as Delta_j H_(j+1)(K),
where Delta_j is the nonzero quantum dimension with the chosen bracket
sign convention. The ordinary Jones normalization divides the resulting
bracket by the unknot bracket and corrects the pattern's framing. Both
operations are factors independent of K; hence the required finite
expansion has coefficients c_(j+1) independent of K.

The independence of that framing correction needs to be stated, rather
than assumed from a blackboard diagram. Choose a ribbon framing of P and
let f_P be its induced self-linking after the standard embedding about U.
If the companion embedding is changed from zero framing to integer f,
the induced pattern self-linking changes by w(P)^2 f. This is the standard
linking-number satellite formula, applied to P and its parallel push-off:
each has winding w(P). In particular, every embedding using the preferred
zero longitude induces the same self-linking f_P, irrespective of K.
One can either choose f_P=0 initially or absorb its fixed twist correction
into c_N. Arbitrary companion-dependent blackboard cabling is not allowed.

The theorem remains valid for any fixed additional integer framing of the
companion embedding: absorb that fixed Dehn twist into the pattern first.
It need not apply to a rule choosing a different framing from K's diagram
or other companion-dependent data.

5. PARITY AND THE CLASSIFICATION FOR KNOT PATTERNS

Skein smoothing preserves the mod-2 homology class in the solid torus.
The core has mod-2 class 1 and S_j contains only powers z^(j mod 2).
Consequently a pattern of winding w has b_j=0 unless j=w mod 2.
Normalization by nonzero scalar factors preserves this restriction.

The algebraic theorem allows only labels j=0,1. Thus an even-winding knot
pattern has only j=0, giving a constant; an odd-winding knot pattern has
only j=1, giving a scalar times V. Evaluation at U identifies that scalar
or constant as V_(P(U)), proving the two displayed formulas in section 1.
Negative winding changes no part of this parity argument.

6. FINITE COLLISION-PAIR TEST: SHARPENED LINEAR-SIZE BOUND

The original polynomial-root argument below gave a correct quadratic
bound. A valuation determinant improves it to ONE PROBE PER POSSIBLE
HIGHER COLOR. This is the preferred certificate.

Let j_1<...<j_s be any finite set of possible labels j>=2. For a satellite
pattern of wrapping at most r, take exactly the labels 2<=j<=r of its
winding parity. For m=1,...,s define the equal-Jones pairs

    K_m=K#T(2,2m+1),     L_m=L#T(2,2m+1).

Then equality of satellite Jones on these s pairs forces every higher
color coefficient to vanish. For a pattern of wrapping at most r,

    s=floor(r/2)                       for even winding,
    s=max(0,floor((r-1)/2))             for odd winding.

Thus floor(r/2) pairs always suffice; often fewer do. If s=0, the skein
degree and parity already imply the classification without any tests.

PROOF OF THE INVERTIBLE SAMPLING MATRIX

Form E_(m,i)=H_(j_i+1)(T(2,2m+1);q), with m=1,...,s. In the torus
formula from section 3, the q-valuation of the a-th summand, including
the denominator [j+1], is

    -m j(j+2)-j(j+1)/2 + m a(a+1)+a(a-1)/2.

For m>=1 and a>=1, its difference from the a=0 valuation is strictly
positive (for a=1 it is 2m). Therefore

    val_q E_(m,i)=-m j_i(j_i+2)-j_i(j_i+1)/2,
    leading_q E_(m,i)=(-1)^(j_i).

Set lambda_i=j_i(j_i+2) and mu_i=j_i(j_i+1)/2. The determinant term
indexed by a permutation sigma has valuation

    -sum_(m=1)^s m lambda_(sigma(m)) - sum_i mu_i.

Both the m's and the lambda_i's are strictly increasing. Strict
rearrangement says the first sum is uniquely maximal for sigma=id;
equivalently, the displayed valuation is uniquely minimal there. One
can prove this directly: swapping an inversion raises the sum by the
positive product of the two differences. Thus no other determinant
term can cancel its lowest q-power. In particular,

    val_q det E=-sum_(i=1)^s i lambda_i-sum_i mu_i,
    leading_q det E=(-1)^(sum_i j_i) !=0.

No specialization of q is used. These are valuations of rational
functions at q=0, where every nonzero rational function has a finite
Laurent leading term.

The s satellite-output equalities give

    E d=0,     d_i=c_(j_i+1)(H_(j_i+1)(K)-H_(j_i+1)(L)).

Since E is invertible, each d_i=0. The all-color collision theorem
makes its colored difference nonzero, forcing c_(j_i+1)=0 for every i.

EXACT SMALL-SIZE VERIFICATION

finite_probe_determinant_check.py performs exact integer Laurent
arithmetic in u=q^(1/2), including division by [j+1] with zero remainder.
It checks the full torus polynomials' valuations for j=2,...,10 and
m=1,...,6 (54 checks), and computes nine full Laurent determinants
through size 4, for even labels, odd labels, and a mixed-parity case.
Every predicted valuation and leading sign agrees. These finite checks
support the normalization; the strict-rearrangement proof gives all s.

EARLIER QUADRATIC BOUND, RETAINED FOR COMPARISON

Let the largest nonzero label j=N-1 in the expansion be J>=2. Define

    K_m=K#T(2,2m+1),     L_m=L#T(2,2m+1).

Every pair has equal ordinary Jones. Its satellite-output difference is

    D(q^-m),
    D(x)=sum_(j=0)^J c_(j+1) Delta H_(j+1) G_j(x).

The j=0,1 terms vanish. The leading term of D comes uniquely from j=J
and is nonzero. Therefore deg D=J(J+2). A nonzero polynomial of this
degree has at most J(J+2) roots in F. Thus at least one of the
J(J+2)+1 pairs indexed by m=0,...,J(J+2) is distinguished. In fact all
but at most J(J+2) nonnegative integers m yield distinguished pairs.

If the pattern has geometric wrapping number at most r, then J<=r.
To see this, cut along a meridional disk meeting P in at most r points.
Resolve crossings without changing the boundary endpoints. Each essential
annular component uses at least one disk intersection, so no state has
more than r essential components. The skein polynomial therefore has
degree at most r, as does its expansion in the monic S_j basis.

It follows that agreement on the fixed r(r+2)+1 pairs

    m=0,1,...,r(r+2)

forces all higher-color coefficients to vanish. This correct bound is
superseded by the s-probe determinant certificate above.

7. STRESS TESTS AND LIMITS

* Fixed formal q is essential. At one numerical specialization, quantum
  dimensions can vanish, torus sampling points can coincide, and colored
  functions need not be independent.
* The hypothesis covers all knots, including connected sums. Constancy
  on prime knots alone does not supply equation (1).
* A satellite depending on K, an infinite completed skein expansion, or
  nonlinear combinations such as products of colored invariants are not
  included unless first reduced to a fixed finite linear expansion.
* The proof classifies universal dependence across every Jones fiber.
  It does not exclude the weaker implication V_K=1 => V_(P(K))=1.
* A pattern with constant annular skein is not thereby geometrically
  inessential, and an action equal to V does not prove core isotopy.
