ALL-ODD PRETZELS: A COMPLETE LOW-ORDER RIGIDITY PROOF
MIXED PRETZELS: AN EXPLICIT SHARP FAILURE
31 October2026. Independently derived and checked; novelty not claimed.

1. Result
For every odd-strand pretzel knot P(p_1,...,p_(2g+1)) whose parameters are
all odd, V''(1)=V'''(1)=0 forces the actual unknot. This holds for arbitrary
integer parameters and arbitrary g, with no search bound.
The statement fails for pretzel knots with one even parameter: the actual
four-strand knot P(8,-3,-3,1) has both derivatives zero but determinant33.

2. Derivation of the general all-odd moments
Use y=A^4 and D=y d/dy. Let n=2g+1, S=sum p_i, and e_j be the elementary
symmetric polynomials in the parameters. Put d=y+2+y^-1 and C=d-1.
Diagonalizing the rank-two Temperley–Lieb multiplication gives the bracket
 A^S [prod_i(C-y^-p_i)+C prod_i(-1-y^-p_i)] / d^(g+1).
The knot Jones normalization is therefore
 J(y)=[C prod_i(1+y^p_i)-prod_i(C y^p_i-1)] / d^(g+1).
This is V(y) or V(y^-1), depending on the initial crossing convention.
The signed monomial normalization can also be recovered uniquely by the
universal identities V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0, avoiding any writhe ambiguity.
Direct differentiation at y=1 yields
 D²J(1)=-(3/2)(e_2+g),
 D³J(1)=-(9/4)[e_3+S(e_2+2g)].
Because J'(1)=0, the joint vanishing of ordinary second/third derivatives is
equivalent to the joint vanishing of these logarithmic derivatives.
By Newton identities the resulting conditions are exactly
 P_2=sum_i p_i²=S²+2g,   P_3=sum_i p_i³=S³.

3. The positive sum argument
S is odd, hence nonzero; mirror to assume S>0. Every other parameter has
square at least1, so P_2=S²+2g gives |p_i|<=S for each i. Now
 sum_i (S-p_i)(p_i²-1)
   =S(P_2-n)-(P_3-S)=0.
Every summand is nonnegative. In fact, under the quadratic zero condition
alone, D³J(1)=(3/4)sum_i(S-p_i)(p_i²-1)>=0. Thus the cubic invariant has a
definite sign on that zero locus and vanishes only in the configurations
described next. If it vanishes, each p_i belongs to {-1,1,S}.
If S>1, the square identity forces exactly one parameter equal to S; the
remaining2g parameters comprise g copies each of+1 and-1. If S=1, every
parameter is+/-1 with one surplus+1.

This conclusion is geometric, not merely polynomial. When S>1, cyclically
rotate the pretzel boxes so the unique S box comes first. Every remaining
rational tangle is1/(+/-1)=+/-1; their horizontal sum is the integer tangle0.
The numerator closure is therefore N(<1/S>+<0>)=N(1/S), an unknot. When
S=1, all tangles are integers with sum1, again giving the unknot. No arbitrary
permutation of general pretzel boxes is being treated as an isotopy.

4. Priority and primary literature
Stipsicz–Szabo, Purely cosmetic surgeries and pretzel knots (2020),
 https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06765

and treats the constraints by a longer sign/case argument. Their >=7-strand

Our all-length nonnegative-sum argument is a compact deduction from these

Their introductory discussion also explicitly records that+/-1pretzel boxes
can commute with other boxes and opposite+/-1pairs can be eliminated.
Sleptsov, arXiv1512.07192, already computes pretzel finite-type invariants
through order6. No exhaustive novelty search has been undertaken.

6. Explicit boundary: one even parameter
For P(p,q,r,s), p even and q,r,s odd, let T=q+r+s,
Q_2=q²+r²+s²,Q_3=q³+r³+s³. In one consistent mirror convention,
 D²V=(3/4)(3-Q_2-2pT-p²),
 D³V=(3/2)(Q_3-T)-6p+(9/4)p(T²+Q_2)+(9/2)p²T+(3/2)p³.
After D²V=0, the second equation simplifies to
 D³V=(3/4)[2Q_3-2T+p+3pT²-p³].
At(p,q,r,s)=(8,-3,-3,1), T=-5,Q_2=19,Q_3=-53, and both vanish.
The ordinary Jones polynomial, independently computed from the actual planar
diagram by Regina and from TL2, is
 V(t)=1+t^-8(t-1)^4 Q(t),
 Q(t)=t^8+2t^7+4t^6+5t^5+6t^4+5t^3+4t²+2t+1.
Therefore
 V''(1)=V'''(1)=0, V''''(1)=720, V(-1)=33.
It is a genuine nontrivial knot because its determinant is33. Its original
15-crossing diagram simplifies to14crossings; minimal crossing number is not
claimed. Its Jones span is12.
A second explicit example is P(40,-15,-13,3), determinant5025, with the same
zero second/third derivatives. See mixed_counterexample.py/json for both full
Jones polynomials, original PD diagrams, and independent checks.

The four-strand example also immediately gives infinitely many distinct
ordinary knots K^(#m): their Jones polynomials are V_K(t)^m, their determinants
are33^m, and their first nonzero derivative remains order4, with value720m.
This is an elementary connected-sum consequence, not a claim of an infinite
family of distinct prime pretzel knots.

7. Meaning for the generic problem
The all-odd proof succeeds because every twist parameter has |p_i|>=1 and the
zero-jet equations force a sum of individually nonnegative terms. The mixed
case has different quadratic/cubic constraints and no such sign conclusion;
P(8,-3,-3,1) is an explicit certificate of that failure. A generic proof cannot
infer Jones1 from only the first two nontrivial derivatives. Higher-order
constraints or a geometric positivity principle are necessary.
