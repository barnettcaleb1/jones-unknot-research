ONE ACTUAL JONES COLLISION DISTINGUISHED BY EVERY HIGHER COLOR
2026-10-31. Exact calculation plus Habiro's cyclotomic expansion.
No claim of novelty or of a Jones-unknot counterexample is made.

STATEMENT
Choose the chirality versions K=5_1 and L=10_132 specified by the braids
 K: B_2 word[-1,-1,-1,-1,-1],
 L: B_4 word[1,1,1,-2,-1,-1,-2,-3,2,-3,-3].
They have the same ordinary Jones polynomial
 V_K(q)=V_L(q)=q^-2+q^-4-q^-5+q^-6-q^-7.
For every integer N>=3, however, their reduced N-dimensional colored
Jones polynomials differ. More precisely, with ordinary Jones parameter
q=e^h and zero framing,
 H_N(K;e^h)-H_N(L;e^h)
   =8(N^2-1)(N^2-4)h^5+O(h^6).
The O(h^6) is for each fixed N. The displayed coefficient is nonzero
for every N>=3; no extrapolation from finitely many colors is used.

EXACT COLOR-THREE INPUT
Use quantum-group variable u, so the ordinary Jones variable is q=u^-2.
The zero-framed unreduced color-three polynomials, with unknot value
[3]_u=u^2+1+u^-2, are
 U_3(K;u)=u^40-u^32-u^30-u^28+u^14+u^12+u^10+u^8+u^6,
 U_3(L;u)=u^44-u^40-u^30-u^24+u^16+u^14+u^10+u^6+2-u^-4.
These are listed in the Knot Atlas A1 weight2 rows. The accompanying
script independently reproduces them from the two explicit braids with
exact Laurent-polynomial R-matrix arithmetic. The full ordinary Jones
identity is separately verified in the14-dimensional TL_4 skein algebra.

For u=e^s, exact exponent moments give
 U_3(K;e^s)-U_3(L;e^s)=-30720s^5-614400s^6+O(s^7),
with every coefficient in orders0 through4 equal to0. Dividing by
u^2+1+u^-2=3+O(s^2) gives leading reduced coefficient-10240s^5.
Since q=u^-2=e^h, s=-h/2, and therefore
 H_3(K;e^h)-H_3(L;e^h)=320h^5+O(h^6).
This explicitly fixes the possible sign and factor-of-two ambiguity.
For example, the A1 weight1 row divided by u+u^-1 agrees with the
ordinary Jones above under q=u^-2.

HABIRO PROPAGATION TO EVERY COLOR
Use the symmetric cyclotomic normalization
 H_N(K;q)=sum_(k=0)^(N-1) a_k(K;q) P(N,k;q),
 P(N,k;q)=product_(j=1)^k(q^N+q^-N-q^j-q^-j),
with a_0=1 and integral Laurent coefficients a_k(K;q).
Write Delta a_k=a_k(K)-a_k(L).
Equality of H_2 implies Delta a_1=0 identically, since P(2,1) is a
nonzero Laurent polynomial. Consequently
 Delta H_3=Delta a_2 P(3,2).
In fact the exact color-three polynomials give the stronger identity
 Delta a_2(q)=q^-15(q^2-1)(q^3+1)(q^5+1)
             =-q^-15+q^-13-q^-12+q^-8-q^-7+q^-5.
The verifier checks this by multiplying by P(3,2) and the quantum
dimension q+1+q^-1, obtaining exactly the unreduced polynomial difference
under u^2=q^-1. Its value at1 is0 and its derivative at1 is8.
At q=e^h,
 P(3,2)=40h^4+O(h^6).
The exact color-three calculation therefore gives
 Delta a_2(e^h)=8h+O(h^2).
In particular Delta a_2(1)=0; it is its first derivative, not its value,
that distinguishes these two knots. Their equal Alexander polynomials
are consistent with this vanishing value.

For arbitrary fixed N>=3,
 P(N,2)=(N^2-1)(N^2-4)h^4+O(h^6).
All terms k>=3 in the cyclotomic expansion are O(h^6), because their
kernels are products of at least three O(h^2) factors and the a_k are
Laurent polynomials regular at q=1. Multiplying the preceding two
expansions proves the claimed universal h^5 coefficient.

APPLICATION TO FIXED SATELLITES
This is the all-color premise needed for the parent's connected-sum
argument. If a finite linear combination sum_N c_N H_N is determined
by the entire ordinary Jones polynomial, compare K#J and L#J for every
knot J. Reduced colored Jones is multiplicative under connected sum,
so the resulting identities read
 sum_N c_N (H_N(K)-H_N(L)) H_N(J)=0.
The colored functions H_N(J) are linearly independent over Q(q^(1/4)),
as seen on the two-strand torus family by their distinct largest
frequency N^2-1. Thus each coefficient separately vanishes. The actual
pair above then forces c_N=0 for every N>=3. Only H_1=1 and H_2=V
remain. Unlike the preceding pass, no rational or polynomial formula
for dependence on V is assumed; equality of Jones fibers is enough.
This concerns universal dependence across all knots, not merely the
hypothetical fiber V=1.

CONSTRUCTIVE FINITE-PROBE COROLLARY
IMPROVEMENT: the quadratic bound proved below has been superseded by a
linear-size exact certificate. Let j_1<...<j_s list the possible higher
labels j>=2 in a fixed satellite's finite expansion. Then the s pairs
 K_m=K#T(2,2m+1), L_m=L#T(2,2m+1), m=1,...,s,
suffice: if their satellite Jones polynomials all agree, every higher
coefficient is zero. For wrapping at most r, parity gives
 s=floor(r/2) for even winding,
 s=max(0,floor((r-1)/2)) for odd winding.
Thus at most floor(r/2) fixed collision pairs suffice.

Indeed E_(m,i)=H_(j_i+1)(T(2,2m+1);q) has q-valuation
 -m j_i(j_i+2)-j_i(j_i+1)/2
and leading coefficient(-1)^(j_i), since for m>=1 the a=0 torus-fusion
summand is uniquely minimal. Strict rearrangement makes the identity
permutation the unique lowest-valuation term of det E, so E is invertible.
The satellite differences are E times the coefficient vector
 c_(j_i+1)(H_(j_i+1)(K)-H_(j_i+1)(L)); every colored difference is nonzero
by the theorem above. Hence equality on all s pairs forces all c_(j_i+1)=0.
Full proof and framing audit: whole_fiber_satellite_audit.txt section6.
Exact verifier: finite_probe_determinant_check.py/.json, including nine
full Laurent determinants through size4.

The earlier, weaker polynomial-root bound remains correct:
Let a fixed satellite's reduced colored expansion have largest nonzero
label J>=2, so its largest dimension is J+1. Set
 K_m=K#T(2,2m+1), L_m=L#T(2,2m+1), m>=0.
Every pair has identical ordinary Jones polynomial. Its satellite-output
difference is a polynomial D(x) over F=Q(q^(1/4)) evaluated at x=q^-m,
where
 D(x)=sum_N c_N (H_N(K)-H_N(L)) G_(N-1)(x).
The leading term comes uniquely from N=J+1 and is nonzero by the
all-color witness theorem. Hence deg D=J(J+2). A nonzero polynomial
has at most this many roots. Consequently the pattern distinguishes
K_m and L_m for all but at most J(J+2) nonnegative integers m.
In particular, among m=0,...,J(J+2), at least one pair distinguishes it.

If a pattern is known to have geometric wrapping number at most r,
its skein degree J is at most r. Therefore testing the fixed r(r+2)+1
collision pairs indexed by m=0,...,r(r+2) suffices to detect every
higher-color contribution. Agreement on all those pairs forces the
pattern's satellite Jones output to be c_0+c_1 V universally. This is
a finite certificate consequence of the theorem, not a claim that the
Jones-one locus itself has been classified.
