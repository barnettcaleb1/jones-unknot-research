INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE ONE-SPECIAL-COMPONENT BN OBSTRUCTION
2026-10-31. Verdict: no gap found in the restricted theorem.

Audited statement: an actual classical U-infinity-anchored four-ended tangle,
in the preferred zero-surgery parametrization, with reduced F_2 Kh multicurve
exactly r_1(s) union s_(4n)(infinity), n>=1, cannot have zero filling whose
Jones polynomial equals that of the two-component unlink. The components
have the standard rank-one local systems and multiplicity one. Length uses
the published KWZ2022 convention. No assertion about multiple special
components or general Jones unknot detection follows.

1. INPUT SHAPES

The primary published KWZ paper, Theorem 2.3 and Lemma 2.5, specifies the Kh
component classification and the pairing with s_(4n)(infinity), including
the relative bigradings. The displayed base pairs (q,h) are
(-5,-2),(-1,0),(1,1),(5,3); successive blocks are shifted by (8,4).
Consequently the special sector is thin, with no repeated q-exponents and
with a unique top h and no generator one h-degree below its top.

The determinant-zero condition of the preferred zero filling forces |s|=4n:
the special sector has determinant magnitude 4n, whereas the rational
sector has magnitude |s| (zero when s=0). This does not claim a stronger
absolute grading calibration than the source supplies. Put k=4n.

After choosing the chirality of the whole tangle and allowing arbitrary
bigrading shifts, the rational reduced Kh sector has one generator
    (q,h)=(2j+1,j),  j=0,...,k-2,k.
This is the thin alternating two-strand torus-link sector. The chosen
chirality is legitimate because the reflected special-sector shape agrees
with its original shape up to a shift. Its two ends both have q-gap four.

The canonical Euler polynomials are
 R= sum_(j=0)^(k-2) (-1)^j Q^(2j+1)+Q^(2k+1),
 E= Q^-1(1+Q^4)(1-Q^6) sum_(i=0)^(n-1)Q^(8i).
Their extremes are:
             bottom     next bottom     top       preceding top
 R              1            3          2k+1         2k-3
 E             -1            3          2k+1         2k-3.
The top coefficients are respectively +1 and -1. Direct summation gives
R+E=Q+Q^-1. Thus Euler cancellation alone is possible; the BN step is essential.

2. INDEPENDENT DERIVATION OF THE SHIFT UNIQUENESS

Suppose epsilon Q^a R+eta Q^b E=Q+Q^-1, with arbitrary monomial shifts
a,b and signs epsilon,eta. If a!=b, the larger of a+2k+1 and b+2k+1 is
uncanceled and must equal 1. Each sector's width exceeds two, so their
bottoms lie below -1 and must cancel. Therefore a+1=b-1, or b=a+2.
The E top is larger, forcing b=-2k and a=-2k-2. The common bottom is
-2k-1, but the next R term occurs at -2k+1, before the next E term.
It cannot cancel and is below -1 because k>=4. Contradiction.

Thus a=b. Unless epsilon=eta, the common top coefficient is +/-2,
incompatible with the right side. With epsilon=eta the identity R+E
forces a=b=0 and epsilon=eta=+1.

This argument checks all shifts, not merely a finite box of possibilities.
The Euler signs determine that the rational top has even h and the special
top has odd h; their actual integer h-shifts otherwise remain unrestricted.

3. PERMANENT TOP CLASS

Both top classes occur at the same q=2k+1, but their h parities differ,
so their h degrees differ. Let H be the larger. Exactly one generator has
h=H, and there are no generators above it. There is therefore no outgoing
differential with h-degree +1. There is no possible incoming source in its
own sector because that sector has a gap at H-1. The other sector can have
a source at H-1 only if its own top is H-1; in that case its unique such
generator has the same q as the target. It cannot support a differential
that strictly raises q. The top class consequently survives all pages.

This is a support argument on every page, not an assumption that the
spectral sequence preserves the direct-sum sectors. Cross-sector maps
are allowed and are excluded only at this particular class by their degrees.

4. THE ACTUAL REDUCED BN TARGET

Turner, Calculating Bar-Natan's characteristic two Khovanov homology,
arXiv:math/0411225v3, Theorems 3.1 and 3.2 and section 3.3, gives the
filtered characteristic-two deformation. The section 3.2 filtration yields
d_r of degree (h,q)=(1,2r), r>=1. This has the required positive q shift.
The unreduced homology has one class per orientation, with h degree twice
the linking between reversed components and their complement.

For the reduced link version, the specialized algebra is
F_2[x]/(x^2=x), with orthogonal idempotents x and 1+x. Reduction at the
basepoint by x=0 selects one idempotent, retaining exactly one orientation
class from each globally reversed pair. For two components the two remaining
classes have h degrees 0 and 2 lk(L). This operation is a filtered reduction
whose associated graded is ordinary reduced Kh. No extra h shift is introduced.
Turner's introduction explicitly mentions extension to reduced link homology,
although section 3.3's heading focuses on knots; the idempotent argument
verifies the needed link assertion without relying on that heading.

Jones equality with the two-component unlink forces two components by
V_L(1)=(-2)^(components-1), and forces lk=0 because for a two-component
link V_L'(1)=-3 lk(L) in the usual convention. Hence the reduced BN limit
is rank two entirely in h=0.

The permanent top class contradicts this target if H!=0. If H=0, the
initial page has only one generator in h=0, so its subquotient limit cannot
have dimension two there. Thus this second case is also excluded.

5. HYPOTHESES THAT MUST REMAIN EXPLICIT

The rank-one and single-special assumptions are substantive. Additional
components may provide incoming classes or a second class at maximal h.
The integer s and the shape of the special sector come from the actual
unknot anchor in preferred framing; this is not a theorem about arbitrary
formal pairs of curve types. Length divisibility by four is assumed, not
deduced from the general geography classification. Absolute grading shifts
are not assumed and are not needed.

CONCLUSION
The previously displayed formal Euler cancellation is excluded from actual
link realization with these exact two sectors. This is a valid restricted
positive obstruction, rather than a general Jones detection theorem.
