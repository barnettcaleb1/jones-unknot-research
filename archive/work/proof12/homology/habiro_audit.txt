INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE ALL-COLOR JONES-COLLISION ARGUMENT
2026-10-31. Data from work/proof12/braid/all_color_collision_check.json;
I independently recomputed Laurent moments and checked the deduction below.

Use the same chosen chiralities of K=5_1 and L=10_132 as the exact braid
certificate. Their H_2 agree as complete Laurent polynomials.
Let u denote the quantum-group variable in the unreduced color3 certificate.
The independently recomputed difference has u=e^s expansion
  U_3(K)-U_3(L)=-30720 s^5-614400 s^6+O(s^7).
In particular every coefficient of degree 0 through 4 is zero.
The reduced invariant divides by u^2+1+u^-2, whose constant is 3.
The ordinary Jones variable is q=u^-2=e^h, hence s=-h/2, and therefore
  H_3(K;e^h)-H_3(L;e^h)=320 h^5+O(h^6).

Write the standard normalized Habiro expansion as
  H_N(K;q)=sum_(k=0)^(N-1) a_k(K;q) B_(N,k)(q),
  B_(N,k)=product_(j=1)^k(q^N+q^-N-q^j-q^-j).
Here a_0=1 and the a_k are Laurent polynomials, so regular at q=1. Equivalent
standard bases can absorb fixed units into a_k without changing the argument.
Since H_2(K)=H_2(L), subtraction of their N=2 expansions gives Delta a_1=0
in the Laurent polynomial domain. Their N=3 expansions then give exactly
  Delta H_3=Delta a_2 B_(3,2).
At q=e^h,
  B_(N,k)=product_(j=1)^k(N^2-j^2) h^(2k)+O(h^(2k+2)).
The N=3 leading factor is (9-1)(9-4)=40. Consequently
  Delta a_2(e^h)=8h+O(h^2).
For every fixed N>=3, all k>=3 terms begin at order h^6. Thus
  Delta H_N(e^h)=8(N^2-1)(N^2-4)h^5+O(h^6),
which is nonzero. This is an all-N proof from a finite exact color3 certificate,
not an inference from testing a finite number of colors. It establishes that
every single nonfundamental color separates this actual ordinary-Jones pair.
It does not address the Jones-one fiber or imply existence of Jones-one knots.

The exact stronger identity was subsequently checked by independent Laurent
multiplication, not just by Taylor coefficients:
 Delta a_2(q)=q^-15(q^2-1)(q^3+1)(q^5+1)
 =-q^-15+q^-13-q^-12+q^-8-q^-7+q^-5.
Multiplying by B_(3,2)(q) and q+1+q^-1 reproduces the unreduced U_3 difference
after replacing u^2 by q^-1. Its value at1 is0 and its logarithmic derivative8.
The exact basis convention was verified in Garoufalidis--Le, Asymptotics of the
colored Jones function of a knot, Section3.2, equations(11),(12):
https://letu.math.gatech.edu/Papers/Asymptotics.pdf
Their opening normalization has J_(K,2) equal to ordinary Jones and J_(U,N)=1.
