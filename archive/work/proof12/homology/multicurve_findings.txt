PROOF12: ACTUAL UNKNOT-ANCHORED CANCELLATION AND ONE-SPECIAL-CURVE AUDIT
2026-10-31. No generic Jones detection theorem is claimed.

1. AN ACTUAL COUNTERTEST, NOT AN ABSTRACT COMPLEX
Watson, Khovanov homology and the symmetry group of a knot, Section 7,
printed page27 (arXiv1311.1085v4), computes kappa for a strong inversion of the
figure-eight knot. I inspected the original figure and independently transcribed
its entries:
 (u,2delta)=(0,-3),(2,-3),(3,-3),(5,-3),
            (4,-1),(6,-1),(7,-1),(9,-1).
Watson uses q=u-delta (half the usual Kh quantum grading). Therefore opposite
homological parities occur at exactly the SAME quantum grading q=9/2 and13/2.
Both grades cancel in the graded Euler polynomial, which is
 t^(3/2)+t^(7/2)-t^(15/2)-t^(19/2)
 =t^(3/2)(1+t^2)(1-t^6).
This is an actual classical tangle with an actual unknot infinity closure: its
tangle is the quotient of the figure-eight exterior under the strong inversion.
KWZ2022 Lemma3.6 identifies kappa with the special-sector pairing. Thus this
example refutes quantum-parity purity of the special sector even with an actual
unknot anchor. It does NOT refute aggregate nonvanishing or extreme imbalance;
its extremes survive.

2. THE PRECISE SINGLE-COMPONENT EULER FORMULA IS AVAILABLE
Use the PUBLISHED length convention of Kotelskiy--Watson--Zibrowius, Khovanov
homology and strong inversions, Open Book Series5 (2022),223--244.
Lemma2.5, printed page231, treats s_(4n)(infinity), of length4n. The arXivv1
calls the same curve s_(2n); do not mix the conventions.
The base pairing HF(a(0),s_4(infinity)) has (quantum Q exponent,homological h)
 (-5,-2),(-1,0),(1,1),(5,3).
For length4n it is the sum of n shifted copies, with quantum shifts
4(2i-n-1) and even homological shifts2(2i-n-1), 1<=i<=n. Hence its Euler
polynomial, up to an overall signed quantum monomial, is
 E_n(Q)=Q^(-4n-1)(1+Q^4)(1-Q^6) sum_(i=0)^(n-1) Q^(8i).
This is nonzero, with extreme coefficients +1 and -1. Every summand is
concentrated in the same delta grading. This proves per-component nonvanishing
for the specified curves, rather than assuming it.
Primary: https://msp.org/obs/2022/5-1/obs-v5-n1-p10-p.pdf
Local published PDF/text: kwz2022.pdf / kwz2022.txt.

3. THE s=0 ONE-SPECIAL SUBCASE IS VACUOUS, NOT A NEW DETECTION RESULT
Suppose an unknot-anchored tangle in preferred meridian-longitude framing has
one rational component r_1(s) and exactly one special component s_(4n).
The zero filling is the branch set of zero surgery, so its determinant is0.
Its special-sector delta-Euler characteristic has magnitude4n, by Lemma2.5.
For s!=0 the rational-sector magnitude is |s|; for s=0 that magnitude is0
(the unlink sector has two generators in adjacent delta gradings). Consequently
  |s|=4n,
with opposite signs for the two sectors. In particular s=0 is impossible in
this one-special setup. This follows from the same determinant argument used
on printed page236 of KWZ2022. Do not advertise an s=0 one-special nonflatness
statement as a non-vacuous theorem about actual tangles.
For general special lengths not divisible by4, this note makes no assertion
beyond the geography theorem; divisibility by4 is Conjecture3.10 in the paper.

4. EVEN A SINGLE SPECIAL CURVE CAN FORMALLY CANCEL AGAINST THE RATIONAL SECTOR
Define the reduced Khovanov Euler torus-link polynomial (one fixed chirality)
 R_(4n)(Q)=[Q^(-12n)+(Q^2+1+Q^-2)Q^(-4n)]/(Q+Q^-1).
There is an exact identity for every n>=1:
 R_(4n)(Q)+Q^(-8n) E_n(Q)=Q^(-12n)(Q+Q^-1).
Equivalently Q^(12n)R_(4n)+Q^(4n)E_n=Q+Q^-1.
Thus two appropriately shifted graded sectors have EXACTLY the unlink Euler
polynomial, despite a nonzero special component. This is not claimed realized
by a classical tangle. Whether these relative/absolute grading shifts can occur
with the actual unknot anchor is precisely the remaining geometric question.
The identity shows that per-component nonvanishing alone is insufficient.

Proof: sum_(i=0)^(n-1)Q^(8i)=(1-Q^(8n))/(1-Q^8), and
 (1+Q^2)(1+Q^4)(1-Q^6)/(1-Q^8)=1+Q^2+Q^4.
Multiplying the proposed identity by Q+Q^-1 immediately cancels the two terms
Q^(-4n-2)(1+Q^2+Q^4); the remainder is
Q^(-12n-2)(1+Q^2)^2, as required. This proves all n, independently of checks.

5. WHY A BARE BAR-NATAN CONE REQUIREMENT DOES NOT GIVE SIGN POSITIVITY
Kh(T) is a cone of the central G action on BN(T). The cone gives a necessary
nullhomotopy G·id on every summand; it does not identify an Euler polynomial
with a dimension. Homological shifts preserve the nullhomotopy property and
reverse Euler signs. Thus the summand-wise cone constraint is not, by itself,
a relative-grading positivity theorem. We have NOT proved that every formal
collection satisfying these properties is realizable, or even that the whole
formal collection admits every A-infinity extension required for a tangle.

The recent primary preprint On mutation invariance in Khovanov homology,
arXiv2602.23442, extends the component classification and its matrix-factorization/
homological-mirror-symmetry argument to every field. Theorem3.19 classifies each
component up to an overall bigrading shift; Lemma7.7 supplies the G-nullhomotopy.
Neither statement supplies a relationship between the shifts of different
components that rules out the cancellations above. The full extension structure
could contain more realization information, but no such theorem was extracted
in this bounded pass.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.23442

OUTCOME
We now have (i) an actual anchored parity-cancellation example, (ii) the exact
one-component formula, and (iii) an explicit identity showing why the rational
component cannot be ignored. The next meaningful lemma must constrain the
absolute placement of these components for actual tangles, not merely classify
their shapes or show that individual Euler polynomials are nonzero.
