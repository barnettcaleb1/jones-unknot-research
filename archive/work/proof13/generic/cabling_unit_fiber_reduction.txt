ONE FIXED CABLE AND THE FULL COLORED JONES FUNCTION
2026-10-31. Conditional reduction and unconditional test-family theorem.
No Jones-unknot proof, counterexample, or novelty claim.

THEOREM
Fix r>=2. Let C_r(K) be the cable with slope one meridian plus r preferred
longitudes, and zero framing on the resulting knot. Thus C_r has winding r
and C_r(U)=U. This is (1,r) in Kalfagianni--Tran's convention, or (r,1)
when winding is listed first. The following statements are equivalent:
(A_r) For every K, V_K=1 implies V_(C_r(K))=1.
(B) For every K, V_K=1 implies H_N(K)=1 for every N>=1.
(C) Every fixed knot pattern P satisfying P(U)=U preserves the V=1 fiber.

The substantive implication is (A_r)=>(B). The premise (A_r) is NOT proved.
It is exactly as strong as promoting Jones-one to all-colored-Jones-one.
In particular, it must not be treated as an innocuous consequence of cabling.

Individual-knot version: let G_r(K) be generated from K,U by connected sums
and C_r. Every knot in G_r(K) has ordinary Jones1 if and only if K has all
colored Jones polynomials1. Thus any nontrivial colored Jones polynomial
can be detected by ordinary Jones on some finite expression built using
only connected sums and this one fixed cable.

CONVENTIONS
Work over F=Q(q^(1/4)), q indeterminate. H_N is reduced, zero-framed,
dimension-N colored Jones: H_1=1,H_2=V,H_N(U)=1.
Write J_N=[N]H_N, [N]=(q^(N/2)-q^(-N/2))/(q^(1/2)-q^(-1/2)).
Negative colors satisfy J_(-N)=-J_N. Reduced connected sum is exactly
multiplicative, with no quantum-dimension or framing correction.

1. MOMENT LEMMA
Let c_1,...,c_s be nonzero field elements, sum c_i=1, with no nonempty
zero-sum subset. If sum_i c_i x_i^m=1 for m=1,...,s, then all x_i=1.

Proof. Add the automatic m=0 equation. Group equal x_i together, and add
the node1 with coefficient-1. There are at most s+1 distinct nodes. The
equations m=0,...,s form a Vandermonde system; the first as-many-as-needed
rows are invertible. Thus every grouped coefficient vanishes. Any node
other than1 would give a forbidden nonempty zero-sum subset. QED.
Repeated x_i and x_i=0 cause no problem. Positivity is unnecessary.

2. EXACT CABLE COEFFICIENTS
The standard zero-framed cabling formula gives
 J_N(C_r(K))=q^[r(N^2-1)/4] sum_(k in S_N) q^[-k(rk+1)] J_(2rk+1)(K),
 S_N={-(N-1)/2,-(N-3)/2,...,(N-1)/2}.
Therefore
 H_N(C_r(K))=sum_(k in S_N) c_k H_(M_k)(K),
 M_k=|2rk+1|,
 c_k=sgn(2rk+1) q^[r(N^2-1)/4-k(rk+1)] [M_k]/[N].       (1)

All N target colors are positive and distinct. A collision between opposite
signs would require r(k+k')=-1; k+k' is an integer, impossible for r>=2.
Evaluation on U gives sum c_k=1.

Crucial valuation fact: the c_k have pairwise distinct q-valuations,
strictly decreasing with target color. Set
 B=r(N^2-1)/4+(N-1)/2.
Then
 val_q c_k=B-rk^2-k-(|2rk+1|-1)/2.
For k>=0 this is v_+(k)=B-rk^2-(r+1)k; for k=-l<0 it is
 v_-(l)=B-rl^2-(r-1)l+1.
In increasing order of target color, negative and positive magnitudes
alternate, with k=0 first when N is odd. The successive differences are
 v_-(l)-v_+(l)=2l+1>0,
 v_+(l-1)-v_-(l)=2l(r-1)>0
whenever both entries occur. For odd N the initial k=0 to k=-1 comparison
is the second formula with l=1. Thus every nonempty coefficient subset
has a unique lowest-valuation term and cannot sum to zero.
The largest target color is r(N-1)+1; its coefficient has valuation zero.

For N=2 this simplifies to
 V_(C_r(K))=a_r H_(r+1)(K)+b_r H_(r-1)(K),
 a_r=q^((r-1)/2)[r+1]/[2],
 b_r=-q^((r+1)/2)[r-1]/[2],  a_r+b_r=1.
Two connected-sum moments force both colors1: subtracting the square
of the first moment from the second gives a_r b_r(x-y)^2=0.
For r=2 the lower color is H_1=1, so one cable equation already forces H_3=1.

3. FINITE POINTWISE TEST, THEN GLOBAL PROPAGATION
For any fixed actual knot K and fixed N>=2, the finite conditions
 H_N(C_r(K^(#m)))=1 for m=0,...,N
force H_(|2rk+1|)(K)=1 for EVERY k in S_N. Here K^(#0)=U,
so the m=0 equation is automatic. This is a direct application of the
moment lemma to (1), and does not assume V_K=1 or any global closure.
Actual H_N are nonzero since H_N(K;1)=1, though the algebraic lemma
itself does not require this extra fact.

For N=2 these are just the TWO ordinary-Jones tests on C_r(K) and
C_r(K#K), forcing H_(r-1)(K)=H_(r+1)(K)=1. For r=2, one test on
C_2(K) is enough because H_1(K)=1 already. The two-moment identity
in section2 verifies this special case explicitly.

Iteration is a separate matter: to apply the pointwise statement again
at larger colors requires control of those colors on the NEW cabled
connected sums. The global fiber-closure hypothesis supplies that
control as follows; the pointwise tests alone do not automatically do so.

Let S be the set of Jones-one knots and assume (A_r). S is automatically
closed under connected sums; the hypothesis makes it closed under C_r.
Suppose inductively H_N=1 on all S. For every K in S and m=1,...,N,
C_r(K#...#K), with m summands, lies in S. Apply H_N and use (1):
 sum_k c_k H_(M_k)(K)^m=1.
The moment lemma forces every H_(M_k)(K)=1, in particular color r(N-1)+1.
This holds for every K in S. Starting N_0=2 gives
 N_a=r^a+1, H_(N_a)(K)=1 for all a>=0 and every K in S.
These colors are unbounded. The excluded r=1 case would be the identity
operation and gives no propagation.

4. UNBOUNDED TRIVIAL COLORS IMPLY ALL COLORS
Use Habiro's integral cyclotomic expansion
 H_N(K;q)=sum_(k=0)^(N-1) a_k(K;q) P(N,k;q),
 P(N,k;q)=product_(j=1)^k(q^N+q^-N-q^j-q^-j),
 a_k in Z[q^+-1], a_0=1.
At q=e^h each kernel is O(h^(2k)). For any fixed d, the coefficient
of h^d in H_N(K;e^h)-1 uses only k<=floor(d/2). It is a polynomial p_d(N)
(in fact in N^2), because the kernel factors become 2cosh(Nh)-2cosh(jh).
This one polynomial is valid for EVERY positive integer N: terms k>=N
may be added freely since their j=N factor is identically zero.

The unbounded set of roots N_a forces p_d=0. This holds for all d.
Each fixed-color Laurent polynomial therefore has zero Taylor series at1,
hence is zero. No uniform asymptotic limit is used. This proves (B).

5. CONVERSE AND INDIVIDUAL-KNOT VERSION
A fixed zero-longitude satellite has finite expansion
 V_(P(K))=sum_N b_N H_N(K)
with coefficients independent of K. If all colors of K are1, its value
is sum b_N=V_(P(U)). Hence (B)=>(C)=>(A_r).
For the individual-knot version, run sections3--4 on S=G_r(K), assuming
all its ordinary Jones polynomials1. Conversely, all-colored triviality
is preserved by connected sums and by (1), so induction on expressions
in G_r(K) proves the converse.

6. WHAT IS MISSING
No nontrivial higher-color satellite is proved here to preserve the unit
fiber. Trivial local-knot operations and ordinary Jones-preserving mutation
give no new higher-color bootstrap. The preceding whole-fiber rigidity
theorem rules out deriving such an operation from universal dependence
on the full Jones polynomial across all fibers.

The exact missing geometric lemma is
 V_K=1 => V_(C_2(K))=1.
For one K its conclusion is equivalent to H_3(K)=1. Proving that implication
for EVERY K would, by the theorem above, force all colored Jones triviality.
Cabling identities alone do not prove it.

Cyclotomic integrality, q-holonomicity, and the MMR diagonal together do
not supply the lemma. Consider the formal family
 F_N=1+(q-1)P(N,2;q).
It has F_1=F_2=1 but F_3!=1, with integral Habiro coefficients a_0=1,
a_2=q-1 and all other a_k=0. Set z_N=q^N+q^-N and c_j=q^j+q^-j.
Then P(N,2)=(z_N-c_1)(z_N-c_2), so F_N is a linear combination over
Z[q^+-1] of q^(jN), j=-2,-1,0,1,2. With E F_N=F_(N+1), it satisfies
  product_(j=-2)^2(E-q^j) F = 0.
This gives an explicit q-holonomic recurrence; no closure theorem is needed.

At q=e^h, each factor 2cosh(Nh)-2cosh(jh) has coefficients whose
N-degree is at most their h-degree. Multiplication by e^h-1 therefore
makes the h^d coefficient of F_N-1 have N-degree at most d-1. The MMR
diagonal is consequently 1, corresponding formally to Alexander
polynomial 1. Equivalently, in the loop expansion with t=q^N independent,
 F=1+(e^h-1)(t+t^-1-2cosh(h))(t+t^-1-2cosh(2h))
has constant h-coefficient 1.

This family is NOT claimed to arise from a knot, and satisfying these
particular constraints does not establish all knot-specific identities.
It isolates the need for a knot-realizability input; the recurrence and
MMR diagonal alone do not repair the missing cabling-preservation lemma.

7. ALEXANDER CONSEQUENCE AND STATUS OF COLORED DETECTION
All-colored triviality implies Delta_K=1 by the proved
Melvin--Morton--Rozansky theorem. Therefore any K with Delta_K!=1 has
some finite expression in G_r(K) whose ordinary Jones polynomial is not1.

For fibered knots, Delta_K=1 forces genus0; for graph knots, the same
conclusion follows from their construction by connected sums and cablings
of torus knots and the Alexander cabling formula. Thus the expanded test
family detects every nontrivial fibered knot and graph knot. This is not
a statement that the ordinary Jones polynomial alone detects those classes,
and the general cabling-preservation hypothesis remains unproved.

All-colored Jones detection of the unknot is NOT invoked as a theorem.
Murakami--Murakami state it as Conjecture5.4 and derive it conditionally
from the Volume Conjecture plus MMR. Some2007 primary literature cites
an Andersen announcement as a result, but no complete proof was located.
A2025 updated primary problem list still explicitly asks whether a
nontrivial knot can have all colored Jones polynomials trivial:
https://math.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/surv-295-ruberman-watermarked-author-pdf.pdf
printed p32. The categorified colored-Jones detection theorem is different.
Even proving (A_r) would leave general all-colored triviality=>unknot
requiring separate justification.
