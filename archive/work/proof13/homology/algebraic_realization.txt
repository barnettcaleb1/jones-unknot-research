PROOF13: THE FOUR-SPECIAL MODEL PASSES FULL TANGLE-ALGEBRA EXTENSION TESTS
2026-10-31. This is an algebraic construction, NOT a classical tangle diagram.

RESULT
The four-special boundary model from proof12 is not excluded by the ordinary
Bar-Natan tangle algebra, its Kh mapping cone, rational-filling pairing functors,
central connectivity actions, or the A-infinity extension required in the 2026
geography theorem. We construct the requisite objects explicitly, including
the higher correction to the central-G cone map. There is a signed integral
version as well as the characteristic-two model.

This determines a useful stopping point: merely imposing those algebraic
extension conditions cannot establish the hoped-for no-cancellation statement.
It does NOT show that the object is the invariant of any actual tangle.
It does NOT provide a generic reduction from a Jones-one knot to this particular
unknot-anchored situation.

1. THE COMPLETE SPECIAL TYPE-D MODULE X
There are generators x0,...,x5 and y0,...,y5. Their idempotents are bullet for
indices0,5 and circle for indices1,2,3,4. The x row has (q,h) values
 (-5,-3),(-4,-2),(-2,-1),(0,0),(2,1),(3,2).
The y row is shifted from x by (q,h)=(2,1).
Along EACH row, arrows point from index i to i+1 with labels
  S, D, S^2, D, S.
There are also vertical arrows x0 -> y0 and x5 -> y5, labelled D over F_2.
Over Z take the first vertical label -D and the second +D, implementing the
rank-one local system -1. All other original labels have coefficient +1.
The B differential squares to zero because every two-step product is mixed DS
or SD. This is X=C(s4(infinity)), not just the four-dimensional closed pairing.

2. AN EXPLICIT A-INFINITY EXTENSION
Over F_2 add two arrows
  x1 --U--> y3,       x2 --U--> y4.
They have total degree (h,q)=(1,0). The signed integral version instead uses
  x1 ---U--> y3,      x2 --+U--> y4.
There is no change to any original arrow apart from the local-system sign above.

Here is the entire Maurer-Cartan check. The two mixed products involving D and U
from x1 to y4 cancel. The remaining mu2 terms are US from x0 to y3 and US from
x2 to y5. They are canceled by mu4 applied to the original paths with labels
  D,S,D,S^2       and       S^2,D,S,D,
respectively. Other four-step paths have zero mu4. Higher products involving a
new U times idempotent arrow vanish by strict unitality. Every remaining
six-step path has input quantum degree -10, while the underlying mu6 raises
quantum degree by12; B has no positive-degree output, so mu6 vanishes. No longer
paths occur. Over Z the signs agree with Remark9.5: -mu2+mu4=0.

3. G IS NULLHOMOTOPIC, AND THE CONE LIFT NEEDS EXTRA TERMS
The certificate solves delta H + H delta = G times identity over B, with
deg H=(h,q)=(-1,-2). The solution is integral: no division is required.
Over F_2 one explicit H has these arrows (unlisted arrows are zero):
 x1->x0:S; x2->x1:1; x3->x2:1; x4->x3:1; x5->x4:S;
 y0->x0:1; y1->x1:1; y2->x2:1; y3->x3:1; y4->x4:1; y5->x5:1;
 y1->y0:S; y2->y1:1; y3->y2:1; y4->y3:1; y5->y4:S.
The signed integral solution is recorded explicitly in check_integral_extension.json.

Crucially, we did NOT assume naive G times identity is closed over B^infinity_U.
The twisted morphism differential of H gives the following corrected map in F_2:
  G_tilde = G times identity
          + U(x1->x3 + x2->x4 + y1->y3 + y2->y4).
It is exact upstairs and reduces to G times identity at U=0. The certificate
computes it, including the mu4 terms; it does not simply discard higher products.
The analogous signed map is computed from the integral nullhomotopy and saved
in the integral certificate.

The complete 24-generator cone of G_tilde also satisfies MC directly. Its
source copy of X has shift q^-1 h^-1 (and hence negated internal differential
over Z), its target copy shift q^1 h^0, and its between-copy arrows are G_tilde.
The certificate checks every possible nonzero arity. Since G_tilde is exact in
the twisted category, this cone is equivalent there to the direct sum of the
two shifted X copies. Over B this is the standard splitting of a nullhomotopic
mapping cone.

4. THE FULL FORMAL BN AND Kh TANGLE OBJECTS
Let A=C(a(0)), the one-generator rational-arc BN object, and set
  D = A direct-sum q h X direct-sum q h^2 X.
The h shifts use the standard suspension sign on the differential over Z.
By the preceding nullhomotopy, its G cone is equivalent over B to
  C(r1(0)) direct-sum X direct-sum q^2 h X
             direct-sum h X direct-sum q^2 h^2 X.
This is exactly the proposed four-special model, now as a full type-D object.
X has12 generators, D has25, and its unsimplified mapping-cone presentation has50; these
counts are different from the rank18 of the zero-filling reduced Kh vector space.

A is trivially extendable. The explicit extension of X and the lifted cone map
give compatible extensions of D and its Kh cone. Alternatively the split cone
is the direct sum of four shifted copies of the explicitly extended X and the
two-generator rational cone. This also makes the extension property immediate.

5. WHAT HOLDS FOR ALL RATIONAL FILLINGS
Pairing with any rational filling is an additive gluing functor and preserves
mapping cones. Thus it applies to the entire construction, not just the a(0)
pairing used in proof12. For every such pairing F, the extra Euler contribution
is
  Euler(F(X)) (1-Q^2-1+Q^2)=0.
Therefore all these formal filled Euler polynomials equal those of the rational
anchor. At infinity the special-sector pairing is zero, while the rational
anchor pairs to the one-dimensional unknot group.

Moreover G times identity on X is nullhomotopic. After G is inverted, or after
specializing G=1, X is contractible; the same is true after any gluing functor.
Consequently the deformed filling homology agrees with that of the rational
anchor for every filling. This is stronger than prescribing a differential on
one chosen rank18 vector space.

All three central actions D_circle, D_bullet, and S^2 on X are nullhomotopic.
The characteristic-two certificate records their explicit nullhomotopies, and
an additional integral linear calculation gives integral nullhomotopies too.
The rational arc supplies its ordinary connectivity, so the corresponding
connectivity/basepoint-action condition is met.

6. PRECISE LIMITATION
D is an object of the formal tangle algebra / additive cobordism-complex
category, and passes the required A-infinity-extension test. We have not shown
that it is in the image of the invariant of actual classical tangle diagrams.
In particular, no actual cube of crossing resolutions, connected tangle, or
unknot-anchored classical embedding has been constructed. The derived category
admits direct sums and shifts; those operations do not by themselves realize
a prescribed object as the invariant of a single geometric tangle.

We also do not claim every conceivable naturality requirement of a fully
geometric tangle/cobordism functor follows merely from these objectwise checks.
The concrete requirements checked are the B module, G cone, all rational
pairing/cone operations, deformed filling ranks, listed central actions, and
the B^infinity_U object and cone extensions. Any remaining obstruction must
use additional geometric realization information or a stronger specified
compatibility condition, rather than reimposing these tests.

7. CONDITIONAL GEOMETRIC CONSEQUENCE: REALIZATION WOULD GIVE A COUNTEREXAMPLE
There is a sharper interpretation of the remaining realization problem.
Suppose a classical tangle T has this bigraded type-D invariant D, allowing
ONE global bigrading shift while preserving the specified relative shifts.
Then its slope1 filling would be a NONTRIVIAL knot with Jones polynomial1.

Indeed, rational gluing gives the same Euler polynomial as the rational anchor
at every slope, up to the global monomial. The rational tangle Q0 with slope1
filling is the unknot, so the actual slope1 filling has signed-monomial Jones
polynomial. The identity |V_L(1)|=2^(number of components-1) forces a knot.
For knots, V_K(1)=1 fixes the monomial sign and V'_K(1)=0 fixes its exponent.
Thus the normalized Jones polynomial is exactly1, even without an absolute
grading/framing calibration. The derivative identity follows from the Jones
skein relation: changing a self-crossing does not change V'_K(1), and every
knot can be changed to the unknot.
Its reduced F_2 Kh rank, however, would be
  dim HF(a1,r1(0)) + 4 dim HF(a1,s4(infinity)) = 1+4*4 = 17.
The first equality follows by the curve pairing decomposition. The rational
rank is1, while KWZ2022 Lemma2.5 and the proof of Lemma3.6 give special rank4
for every integer filling. Thus this knot cannot be the unknot, whose rank is1.
No surgery argument or hypothetical geometric nontriviality assumption is needed.

For completeness, the infinity filling has reduced F_2 Kh rank1, since its
special-sector pairings vanish. It is consequently the unknot: rank1 over F_2
implies rank1 over Q, and apply Kronheimer--Mrowka's unknot-detection theorem.
