UNREDUCED RATIONAL LEE AUDIT OF THE FORMAL TANGLE MODEL
2026-10-31. Formal Frobenius-algebra lift; no classical diagram is asserted.

THE POSSIBLE ISSUE
The reduced BN differential can have quantum jump2, whereas the ordinary
unreduced rational Lee spectral sequence has jumps4r. Applying the latter
degree rule directly to the reduced model would be invalid. Conversely, merely
checking reduced groups does not itself prove an unreduced lift exists.

EXPLICIT RELATIVE-DOT LIFT
Distinguish the reduced parameter h from the unreduced parameter G. In the
unreduced universal Frobenius algebra adjoin a marked-component dot z, with
 z^2+Gz=0.
For an ordinary component dot Y, put d=Y-z and send
 h -> G+2z.
Since Y^2+GY=0, a direct calculation gives
 d^2+(G+2z)d = (Y^2+GY)-(z^2+Gz)=0.
The relative dot on the marked component is z-z=0, as required by the reduced
tangle category. The other Frobenius relations transform correctly:
 Delta(1)=1 tensor d+d tensor1+(G+2z)1 tensor1,
 epsilon(1)=0, epsilon(d)=1.
Indeed, substitute Y=d+z in the ordinary formula for Delta(1). Thus the same
formal cobordism complex can be interpreted using relative dots, over the
rank-two basepoint algebra A=Q[G,z]/(z^2+Gz). The reduced specialization z=0
recovers the original parameter h=G. This is a formal algebraic lift of the
maps as well as their groups, compatible with composition and gluing.

WHY THE LEE QUANTUM RULE IS SATISFIED
Set alpha=G+2z. Then alpha^2=G^2. Replacing h by alpha turns any free Q[h]
complex into a complex over this basepoint algebra. Equivalently, first replace
h by a variable X and regard the resulting Q[X] complex as a Q[T] complex via
T=X^2. It is then a formal generalized Lee complex with deg T=-4; base extension
T=G^2 recovers the BN form. This gives the correct unreduced quantum filtration.

The essential single-bar calculation is especially transparent. A reduced
length-one h bar becomes multiplication by alpha on A. In the basis (1,z),
this multiplication is the matrix
                  [ G   0 ]
                  [ 2  -G ].
The entry2 is a unit over Q. Grading-respecting cancellation leaves a single
G^2 bar, up to multiplication by a nonzero constant, since the determinant is
-G^2. Thus a reduced quantum-jump2 cancellation produces the required ordinary
unreduced quantum-jump4 Lee cancellation. At G=0 the original matrix has rank1;
at G=1 it is invertible. The involution z -> -G-z sends alpha -> -alpha, so
there is no missing elementary root-exchange symmetry in this construction.

APPLICATION TO THE SLOPE1 FORMAL FILLING
The reduced Kh rank is17 and its reduced BN free rank is1. Its additional
torsion is annihilated by h, because the full X object has h-action nullhomotopic.
Over Q[h] the extra part therefore consists of eight length-one bars. The
unreduced formal Kh rank is consequently 2+8*2=18, and the eight jump4 Lee
cancellations leave rank2. Its Euler polynomial is Q+Q^-1, as required for a
Jones-one knot. These are consistency statements about the explicit formal
complex; they do not realize a knot.

PRIMARY CROSS-CHECK
Schuetz, A note on the X-torsion order of a knot, arXiv:2412.05156, Sections2--3:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.05156
Section3 identifies generalized Lee X with the BN relative-dot operation
2X-H in the convention X^2=XH, and describes unreduced blocks (2X-H)^k whose
reductions are H^k. This is the same change of variables, with the opposite
sign convention for the BN parameter. We use it to cross-check the algebra,
not as a theorem that every formal complex is geometrically realized.

SCOPE
The listed reduced extension tests and this formal unreduced Frobenius/Lee
lift are satisfied. This is not a claim that every necessary geometric or
naturality condition for classical tangle realization has been checked.
