INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE FORMAL s4 EXTENSION
Auditor: generic_braid. 2026-10-31.

Verdict
-------
No error found in the claimed formal A-infinity extension and lifted G-cone.
Both the F2 and signed integer computations satisfy the actual finite
Maurer-Cartan equations of the KWZ algebra, with the stated gradings and
idempotents. This is an algebraic model and an obstruction to deductions
using only these necessary algebraic conditions. It is not a realization
by a classical tangle, nor evidence for an actual Jones counterexample.

Both scripts were rerun with Path.write_text disabled. Additional independent

1. Algebra and mu4
-----------------
Definition 2.6 gives DS=SD=0, q(S)=-1, q(D)=-2, and G=S^2-D.
The scripts' suppressed-idempotent multiplication is correct on every
composable path that occurs. Endpoint-idempotent compatibility was checked
separately, including the U times identity arrows.

Definition 10.1 cancels the sign (-1)^(m-1) from the B-infinity disk
operations. Thus all four displayed mu4 values in Remark 10.3 have positive
sign. The implementation correctly assigns ONE value U when all four
powers are one: this input is described in two displayed patterns, but the
patterns are alternative descriptions of the same operation, not summands.
Reversing a forward graph path to the source's (a4,...,a1) input convention
preserves the resulting value. An independent source-pattern implementation
agreed on all 226 arity-four paths across X, the one-H calculation, and the
cone. Higher operations containing a primitive idempotent vanish; U
multilinearity therefore kills operations containing the added U-idempotent
arrows, except ordinary multiplication.

2. Signs and shifts
-------------------
The integer local system places a minus sign on x0 -> y0. The new arrows
have signs - on x1 -> y3 and + on x2 -> y4. These satisfy the full signed
MC equation, with additive-enlargement factor (-1)^(ell/2) for even arity ell.
This factor is not merely assumed from the MC-specific statement: Def. 9.3
reduces mod 2 to sum_p p*(r_p-r_(p-1)). Every differential arrow and every
H arrow has odd shift difference, since the underlying algebra degrees
are even. Thus the same factor applies to paths containing one H, despite
H having total homological degree -1 instead of +1.

The integer nullhomotopy solves delta*H + H*delta = S^2-D. In the source's
additive convention, mu1(H) has the negative of this U=0 value. Therefore
using mu1(-H), as the integer script does, is correct. Its lifted map has
q-degree -2 and h-degree 0, and is
  G*id + U*(x1->x3 + x2->x4 + y1->y3 + y2->y4),
with all four displayed correction coefficients +1. Mod 2 this agrees with
the F2 calculation.

The cone uses copies q^-1 h^-1 X and q X. Every cone arrow has degree h^1 q^0.
The first copy's internal differential is negated, exactly as required by
Remark 9.5 for a homological shift by -1. The cone is strictly triangular.
The full signed cone MC equation vanishes.

3. Completeness in arity, not merely a truncation test
----------------------------------------------------
Independent path enumeration gives:
  X maximum path length 6;
  paths with exactly one H maximum length 8;
  cone maximum path length 7.
The scripts test all even arities through these maxima. There are no omitted
arity-12-or-higher contributions, and the extra arities 8 or 10 tested in
some places are harmless.

For the arity-six and arity-eight paths actually present, the zero claim
also follows from the quantum grading. After removing input U factors,
mu_(2m) would require a B-output of degree
  sum q(B-inputs) + 6*(m-1).
The available nonunit paths give positive degree, absent in B. Paths with
U-idempotent inputs instead vanish by the previous strict-unital argument.

Implementation-scope caveat: the helper named mu is a verifier for this
finite graph, not a general evaluator for arbitrary B_U-infinity inputs.
Its treatment of U powers in mu4 and in the higher-degree assertion is
sufficient here because every nonzero-U edge is U times an idempotent.
It should not be reused without modification for nonunit S or D inputs
carrying U powers. This does not affect any operation used in this model.

4. Exactness of the lifted map and scope of the integer claim
-----------------------------------------------------------
The lifted G is computed as the actual twisted-category boundary mu1(-H),
not guessed from its reduction modulo U. The A-infinity identities imply
it is closed, and its cone is homotopy equivalent to the cone of the zero
map (the direct sum of the corresponding shifted copies). The direct cone
MC calculation is an additional check of that statement's concrete model.

The source introduces Section 10 over fields, because its geometric and
multicurve equivalences have that scope. Section 9's disk operations are
defined over the commutative coefficient ring, and the concrete signed
identities here are integer identities with those structure constants.
Accordingly the claim can safely be called a signed formal algebraic
integral lift, and in particular gives a genuine formal model over Q.
It must not be called an integral geometric extension theorem for actual
tangles. No such claim was needed or verified.

Conclusion: the proposed model survives this independent extension audit.
The missing condition is geometric realizability, not an overlooked mu4
sign, duplicated operation, missing higher arity, or cone-grading mismatch.
