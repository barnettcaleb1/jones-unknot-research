INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE CONDITIONAL ACTUAL-REALIZATION CLAIM

Audited work/proof13/homology/algebraic_realization.txt Section5 and the
proposed consequence if its full bigraded object D is realized by ONE
classical tangle. This audit is of the consequence, not of the A-infinity
checker (which the braid agent is auditing) or of geometric realizability.

Let X=s4(infinity), and suppose the Kh multicurve really is
 r1(0) direct-sum X direct-sum q^2 h X direct-sum h X
                 direct-sum q^2 h^2 X,
with the specified relative bigradings, allowing one global overall shift.
Additive rational pairing preserves these relative shifts. Therefore the
special-sector Euler sum is E_X(1-q^2-1+q^2)=0 for every filling.

At slope1, r1(0) pairs with a(1) to the rank1 homology of the corresponding
rational unknot. Each X pairs to rank4. KWZ2022 Lemma2.5 computes rank4 at
slope0; the proof of Lemma3.6/Figure8 identifies HF(a(n+1),X)->HF(a(n),X)
as isomorphisms at positive integer fillings, and its conclusion identifies
the stable pairing with HF(a(0),X). Thus the total rank is17. The direct sum
of multicurve components remains a direct sum under the pairing, so there
is no hidden cross-component differential to subtract from17.

Framing-safe conclusion: any uncertainty from the ONE global bigrading
shift or gluing normalization makes the ordinary Jones only a signed
monomial, not necessarily visibly1. But for an actual nonempty link L,
|V_L(1)|=2^(component_count-1), so a monomial forces L to be a knot. Then
V_K(1)=1 and V'_K(1)=0 force that monomial to equal1. The reduced F2 Kh rank17
proves that this knot is not the unknot.

At infinity all special pairings vanish and the rational pairing has rank1.
The same component-count argument first makes this a knot, and Khovanov
unknot detection then identifies it as the actual unknot.

Therefore geometric realization of this specific full bigraded object WOULD
produce a genuine Jones-one counterexample. This deduction has no remaining
absolute-framing assumption. It does require the given relative shifts to
belong to a single geometric object; independent per-component shifts would
change the statement and cannot be granted by realization terminology.
