COMPOSITE DETERMINANT-ONE KNOTS CANNOT HAVE AN UNKNOT RATIONAL FILLING
Status: deduction from standard surgery theorems, not a novelty claim.

Proposition. Let L be a nontrivial composite knot with det(L)=1. There is
no Conway 2-tangle T and two rational fillings such that one gives L and
the other gives the actual unknot. Equivalently, L does not have rational
unknotting number one.

Proof. Suppose such T existed. The double branched cover of its unknot
filling is S^3; the filling solid torus lifts to a solid torus in S^3.
Let J be its core. Montesinos' trick identifies the double branched cover
Sigma_2(L) with a rational Dehn surgery on J. No symmetry assumption on J
is needed for the surgery obstruction below.

Write L=L1#L2 with both Li nontrivial. Then
 Sigma_2(L)=Sigma_2(L1)#Sigma_2(L2).
Neither summand is S^3: an involution on S^3 with knot quotient branch set
has unknotted fixed set, by the Smith theorem. Thus Sigma_2(L) is reducible.
Moreover its first homology has order det(L)=1, so it is an integer homology
sphere.

Gordon--Luecke, Knots are determined by their complements (1989),
Corollary3.1, states that any homology3-sphere obtained by Dehn surgery on
a knot in S^3 is irreducible. Applying this directly to J contradicts the
preceding paragraph. Equivalently, their Theorem3 gives a nontrivial
lens-space summand in a reducible surgery, whose first homology prevents
the result from being an integer homology sphere. The unknot case is
included in the corollary and also follows directly from its lens-space
surgeries. This argument does not require the cabling conjecture or its
strongly invertible special case.

Consequences for the present investigation.
* Every nontrivial Jones-one knot in a genuinely unknot-anchored rational
  filling problem must be prime, since V(-1) gives determinant1.
* The valid generic constructions K#rK, K#r(mK), and their iterated doubles
  produce composite knots. If K were a Jones-one counterexample, NONE of
  these composite knots could itself appear as the nontrivial rational
  filling of an actual unknot-anchored tangle. The restriction is stronger
  than the previous fusion-one obstruction.
* Strong invertibility of such a composite knot still gives a quotient
  tangle with an unknot meridional filling. There is no contradiction: its
  other fillings are branch sets of surgeries on the composite knot,
  not the composite knot itself. For the swapping inversion on K#rK,
  a relevant branch set is Wh+(K), which depends on color three.
* An unconditional concrete illustration is KT#r(mKT): it is a nontrivial
  composite ribbon knot with determinant1, and therefore cannot itself be
  changed to U by a single rational tangle replacement. No Jones1 claim
  is made for this example.
