PROOF13 TANGLE RESULTS

1. Universal leading-sign coherence for mirror ribbon doubles is FALSE.
A concrete two-band 8_19=T(3,4) mirror-double has actual all-cut unlink3,
but leading signs of its three twist coefficients are -,+,-. Full base,
cut and twist PDs are archived. This directed test stopped after four
valid triples on one knot. The parity/subset-sum certificate still excludes
all parameters; more simply V''(1)=-60. This is not a Delta1 advance.
See sign_counterexample_note.txt, sign_certificate.py.

2. Generic Jones detection is equivalent to detection restricted to
strongly invertible ribbon knots: form R=K#r(mK), then D(R)=R#rR.
The known swapping strong inversion needs no symmetry of K. Nontriviality
and Jones1 are preserved. A mirror reflection alone is not a strong
inversion, so the reverse/mirror distinction must be maintained.
See strong_inversion_reduction.txt.

3. This does NOT make the same Jones-one knot a rational filling adjacent
to an actual unknot. In fact every composite determinant1 knot is forbidden
from such a filling presentation, by Montesinos + Gordon--Luecke1989,
Corollary3.1: every integer homology sphere obtained by surgery on a knot
in S^3 is irreducible. No strong-invertibility assumption is needed for
this surgery obstruction. This is sharper than fusion>=2.
See unknot_anchor_primality.txt; the original statement and its short
proof reduction were checked, with an accessible2016 primary confirmation.

4. The natural quotient response of D(K)=K#rK is Wh+(K), whose branched
cover is 1/2 surgery on D(K). Exact identity:
 V_Wh+(K)-1=(1-t^3)/(1+t)*(H3(K)-1).
Therefore asking this quotient response to have Jones1 is EXACTLY asking
H3(K)=1. It is the same missing color3 promotion as the cable route, not
an independent geometric solution. Both local skein and zero-framed fusion
were derived and checked against actual Regina Whitehead/parallel diagrams
and the independent exact R-matrix certificate for 5_1 and10_132. These
witness knots have equal ordinary Jones but different quotient responses.
See whitehead_bridge_verify.py (stdlib), quotient_response_test.py/.json.
