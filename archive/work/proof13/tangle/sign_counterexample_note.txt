A DIRECTED COUNTERTEST OF COMMON LEADING SIGNS

The last KT-double calculation does not extend to all ribbon mirror doubles
by a universal common-leading-sign theorem. For the actual 8_19=T(3,4)
diagram in sign_counterexample.json, take the three ordered edges
(7,1),(0,0),(2,3) on face2. Join edge0 to its reflected copy to form
K#mirror(K), and use the other two mirror-joining sites as two ribbon bands.
All three joins simplify by Reidemeister moves to the actual3-component
unlink. The four cut PDs, simplifications, free unlink counts and exact Jones
polynomials are stored. All pairwise linking numbers of the cuts are zero.

Writing Q0 for the uncut knot and Qi=V(cut_i)/delta, Q12=1, the universal
full-band-twist formula is
 V(m,n)-1 = A t^(2m+2n)+B t^(2m)+C t^(2n),
 A=Q0-Q1-Q2+1, B=Q2-1, C=Q1-1.
Here the leading degree/coefficient pairs of A,B,C are respectively
 (5,-2), (5,+1), (4,-1).
Thus actual unlink cuts, reflection symmetry and a ribbon disk do not force
common signs. sign_countertest.py stopped at the first mixed-sign example,
after four valid edge triples on this one knot; it was not a broad search.

A slightly stronger finite certificate nonetheless excludes all twists:
full twists shift exponents by even integers. Terms attaining the global
highest exponent must therefore have the same original degree parity.
For odd degree the possible nonempty leading sums are -2,+1,-1; for even
degree the only sum is -1. None vanishes. This proves V(m,n) is never1 for
all integers m,n. More generally, for any finite Laurent sum
 sum_j H_j(t) t^(2 linear_j(n)), group nonzero leading coefficients of H_j
by maxdegree(H_j) modulo2. If no nonempty subset in any group sums to zero,
the sum cannot vanish for any integer parameter vector. This elementary
sufficient criterion strictly extends the common-sign criterion; it is
not asserted necessary or new.

Geometry/normalization was checked by inserting actual positive full twists
at each band separately and simultaneously. All three actual PD Jones
polynomials match the recurrence, with parameter -1 at a positive insertion.
See verify_counterexample.py and twist_checks.json.

Scope: this knot does not have Alexander polynomial1. Indeed every member
of this family has V''(1)=-60, also checked from the four Qs. The example is
useful as a falsification of generic geometric sign coherence; it does not
advance the determinant/Alexander-one search frontier. No further family
search was pursued after this counterexample.

is a standard-library verifier of the exact stored algebra.
