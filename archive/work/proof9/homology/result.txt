BAND DETECTION, LEE BARS, AND THE PRECISE DECATEGORIFICATION GAP
Bounded proof-mechanism audit, 2026-10-31. No general Jones detection
proof, no knot counterexample, and no novelty claim.

1. WHAT THE MULTICURVE THEOREM ACTUALLY PROVIDES
Kotelskiy--Lidman--Moore--Watson--Zibrowius, Cosmetic operations and
Khovanov multicurves, Theorems5.6--5.7, prove that a Conway tangle with
split zero closure has only slope-zero Khovanov curves. If the tangle is
not horizontally split, special components occur. Their geometric
pairings distinguish all full twists of the corresponding nontrivial
band. They work over F2. The result is categorical and does not assert
that the special components have nonzero graded Euler characteristic.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.14049 ; published text:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11139753/ .

Wang's stronger algebraic formulation makes the missing step explicit.
For the band sum K_b of a split link with connected sum K_#, there is a
nonzero complementary bigraded vector space H_b when b is nontrivial,
with reduced homology over Q satisfying
 Kh_red(K_(b+n)) = Kh_red(K_#) direct-sum h^(2n)q^(4n) H_b.
Hence, in normalized Jones conventions,
 V_(K_(b+n))(q^2)=V_(K_#)(q^2)+q^(4n) chi_q(H_b).
Theorems1.7--1.8 and Remark1.9 are the sources for reduced Q coefficients.
Wang explicitly leaves nonvanishing of the polynomial difference open
in his introduction; categorical detection does not settle it.
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.01070 , pp5--7.

In particular, for a nontrivial band on the two-component unlink,
one Jones-one member would give infinitely many pairwise distinct
Jones-one knots. Twisting cannot repair an Euler-zero complementary
summand: it only shifts that zero polynomial. This is a rigorous
amplification statement, not a contradiction.

2. NEW ADDITIONAL GEOMETRIC INPUT: ALL BARS HAVE LENGTH ONE
Let K have ribbon-fusion number at most1, i.e. arise from a two-component
unlink by one band. It is smoothly slice, so s(K)=0. Sarkar's Corollary1.2
bounds the X-extortion order of generalized Lee homology over Q by ribbon
distance, which is at most the number of bands. Therefore every nonzero
Lee torsion block has exponent1. The source is
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.11095 , Corollary1.2 and Section5.
This is stronger than merely knowing that Lee homology has rank two.

Use reduced quantum grading q and homological degree h, with unknot
generator(0,0). A length-one torsion block has reduced generators
 (i,a) and(i+1,a+2).
Write its source as t^i q^a. If S(t,q) is the sum of these source
monomials, with nonnegative coefficients, then
 P_red(K;t,q)=1+(1+t q^2)S(t,q),
 V_K(q^2)-1=(1-q^2)B(q),  B(q)=S(-1,q).
Thus Jones1 is equivalent to exact balance of even and odd source
multiplicities at EVERY quantum source grade. The bar lengths are fixed;
the remaining unknown is the parity distribution of their locations.

PRECISE SUFFICIENT LEMMA (proved elementary implication):
If a fusion-one ribbon knot has a nonempty Lee-bar collection, and at
some source quantum grade a the signed source multiplicity is nonzero,
then V_K is not1. In particular it suffices that all bars at a minimal
or maximal source quantum grade have the same homological parity.
Proof: that coefficient of B is nonzero, so (1-q^2)B is nonzero in the
Laurent polynomial domain.

A GEOMETRIC STATEMENT THAT WOULD NOW SUFFICE, BUT IS NOT PROVED:
Every nontrivial fusion-one ribbon knot has at least one extreme source
quantum grade with unequal even and odd source multiplicities.
This is a restricted and testable geometric statement. We do NOT infer
it from multicurve linearity, positivity of ranks, or ribbon functoriality.
It would settle the fusion-one subclass, not all classical knots.

3. ACTUAL TEST: KINOSHITA--TERASAKA ALREADY HAS INTERNAL CANCELLATION
Hom--Kang--Park explicitly state that KT has fusion number1:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.02832 , p2.
Sano's 2026 preprint gives its reduced rational homology, including
additional e-operator data, in Proposition6.1:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.17435 , pp76--77.
Using the ordinary underlying polynomial and Sarkar's length-one result,
the quotient S=(P_red-1)/(1+tq^2) is uniquely determined. Its source
multiplicities, written(h,q,m), are
 (-6,-12,1),(-5,-10,1),(-4,-8,1),(-3,-6,2),(-2,-6,1),
 (-2,-4,1),(-1,-4,1),(-1,-2,1),(0,-2,1),(0,0,1),
 (1,0,2),(2,2,1),(3,4,1),(4,6,1).
Conventions: Sano uses delta=2h+q; we reverse his homological grading,
so our delta=q-2h. Mirroring conventions do not affect the conclusions.

At source quantum grades-4 and-2, there is exactly one even and one
odd source: they cancel completely in B. Grades-6 and0 also have mixed
source parity, with multiplicities1 versus2. Therefore the stronger
claim that each source quantum grade has a uniform parity is FALSE for
an actual fusion-one ribbon knot. This is not merely a formal model.
The extreme source grades-12 and6 are each pure, so KT does not refute
the narrower extreme-source statement. We did not find an actual knot
refuting that narrower statement in this bounded pass; it remains an
unproved proposed bridge, not a theorem supported by the single example.

The exact extraction is in kt_bar_check.py/.json. The resulting reduced
rank is33, with16 length-one bars. The ordinary Euler polynomial agrees
with KT's nontrivial Jones polynomial. The new e-operator distinguishes
KT from Conway in Sano's computation, but no implication from Jones1
to vanishing of this extra structure is supplied by that paper.

4. A FORMAL STRESS TEST OF ALL THE PRESENTLY AVAILABLE CONSTRAINTS
For any even a,b, put
 P_red(n)=1+t^(2n)q^(4n)(1+t)(1+tq^2)(q^a+t^2q^b).
This is a nonnegative polynomial of rank9. It comes from one free
Q[X] summand and four two-term blocks with map X. Its Euler polynomial
is identically1, s=0, and all Lee bars have length1. Specialization
X^2=0 gives unreduced rank10, with polynomial
 q+q^-1+t^(2n)q^(4n-1)(1+t)(1+tq^4)(q^a+t^2q^b).
The free summand has inclusion and retraction. Its nonzero complement
obeys precisely Wang's full-twist grading law. These are ABSTRACT
complexes; no knot, tangle, ribbon movie, or multicurve realization is
asserted. They demonstrate that the listed categorical, torsion-length,
and twisting statements alone still permit complete Jones cancellation.
formal_band_test.py/.json checks the identities exactly.

5. CAREFULLY SEPARATED RANK CONSEQUENCE
Sarkar plus elementary block specialization gives, independently of
recent instanton preprints,
 r=dim_Q Kh_red=1+2m,  R=dim_Q Kh=2+2m=r+1,
where m is the number of length-one bars. If V=1 then B=0, so m is even
and r=1 mod4. Nontriviality implies m>0.

Our earlier rank-five synthesis uses Bhat--Li--Ye's December2025
preprint2512.24206 and proves that a nontrivial Jones-one knot with r5
has R10. Here r5 would instead force R6, a contradiction. With that
additional PREPRINT-DEPENDENT input, a nontrivial fusion-one Jones-one
knot must satisfy r>=9 and R>=10. This rank improvement is not the
mechanism that would prove detection, and is distinguished from the
source-backed fusion bound above.

6. SCOPE CAUTION: ALL RIBBON KNOTS IS NOT A WEAKER CONJECTURE
For any K, K#mirror(K) is ribbon and its normalized Jones polynomial is
V_K(t)V_K(t^-1). Thus any nontrivial Jones-one K would produce a
nontrivial ribbon Jones-one knot. Nontriviality follows, for example,
from additivity of Seifert genus. Therefore proving Jones unknot detection
for ALL ribbon knots would already prove it for all knots. Fusion-one
is a genuinely restricted hypothesis; it must not be replaced silently
by arbitrary ribbon knots in claiming a reduction.

CONCLUSION
The new geometric ingredient controls bar LENGTHS, but the actual KT
test proves it does not enforce parity positivity at all bar locations.
The exact unresolved issue is the extreme/source-parity geometry of
actual one-band ribbon diagrams. The multicurve theorem cannot be
presented as proving the missing noncancellation statement.
