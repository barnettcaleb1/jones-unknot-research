HANDLEBODY VACUUM SATURATION: A PRECISE SUFFICIENT LEMMA
2026-10-31. Research audit, not a Jones unknot-detection proof.
No novelty claim; the argument combines standard skein and 3-manifold facts.

RESULT.
Let H be an oriented handlebody, Sigma=boundary(H), and f an
orientation-preserving mapping class of Sigma. At SU(2) BHMV level
r-2, write v_r=Z_r(H) for the nonzero vacuum and rho_r(f) for the
projective mapping-class action. Then the following are equivalent:

 (i) f extends to a homeomorphism of H;
 (ii) rho_r(f) preserves the line C v_r at every level;
 (iii) rho_r(f) preserves that line at infinitely many unbounded levels.

At the standard unitary specializations these are also equivalent to
 |<v_r,rho_r(f)v_r>| = ||v_r||^2
at infinitely many unbounded levels. Projective phases do not matter.
The conclusion concerns a boundary map of a handlebody. No assertion
about equivariant extension over a branched cover or the Hilden subgroup
is made here.

KEY DISK-DETECTION LEMMA.
For a simple curve gamma on boundary(H), with its surface framing,
let s_gamma=[gamma]-delta*empty in the integral Kauffman bracket skein
module S(H), where delta=-A^2-A^-2. If the quantum images of s_gamma
vanish at infinitely many unbounded levels, then gamma bounds a disk
in H (a boundary-parallel disk is permitted if gamma is inessential).

Proof, including the passage between levels.

1. Choose a fixed trivalent spine of H. Over C(A), express the fixed
skein s_gamma as a finite linear combination of admissibly colored
spine graphs, with rational-function coefficients c_lambda(A).
This expansion is independent of the root of unity. It can be obtained
by finitely many Jones--Wenzl fusion and recoupling operations on the
fixed diagram; only finitely many labels and denominators occur.

2. Once r exceeds all the finitely many labels and half-sums of labels
at vertices, every graph in this expansion is r-admissible. Its quantum
image belongs to the BHMV colored-spine basis, so these images remain
linearly independent. The finitely many denominators are nonzero at
all but finitely many distinct specializations A_r. Thus quantum
vanishing at infinitely many r forces c_lambda(A_r)=0 infinitely often
for each lambda, hence c_lambda(A) is identically zero.

This is a finite-dimensional argument inside one fixed finite span;
it does not identify the full vector spaces at different levels or
assume their dimensions remain fixed. It works for any compatible
infinite sequence of distinct standard roots A_r, regardless of whether
their accumulation point is +1, -1, or another point.

3. The integral handlebody skein module is free over C[A,A^-1]: realize
H as the product of an interval with a planar surface, and use its
essential crossingless multicurves as basis. It is therefore torsion
free. The generic equality s_gamma=0 consequently holds integrally,
and can be specialized at A=-1. No denominator is specialized at -1.

4. At A=-1, every representation eta:pi_1(H)->SL_2(C) evaluates a
skein loop gamma to -tr(eta(gamma)), and delta to -2. This follows
directly from the SL_2 trace relation and does not require asserting
that an arbitrary skein algebra has zero nilradical. Our equality
implies tr(eta(gamma))=2 for every eta.

5. pi_1(H) is a finitely generated free group. Choose a faithful
Schottky representation into PSL_2(C), and lift its free generators
to SL_2(C). Every nonidentity word is loxodromic, hence has trace
different from +2 and -2 in any lift. Therefore gamma represents the
identity in pi_1(H). Dehn's lemma gives a properly embedded disk
bounded by the simple boundary curve gamma. This proves the lemma.

MAIN PROOF.

The implications (i)=>(ii)=>(iii) follow from TQFT functoriality.
For the converse, choose disjoint boundaries a_1,...,a_g of a complete
disk system in H. Let C_r(a) be the boundary curve operator, obtained
by placing the surface-framed curve in a boundary collar.

For each disk boundary, C_r(a_i)v_r=delta_r*v_r. The surface framing
agrees with the zero disk framing: a parallel boundary copy bounds
a parallel disjoint disk. Naturality gives
 C_r(f(a_i)) = rho_r(f) C_r(a_i) rho_r(f)^(-1).
If rho_r(f)v_r=c_r*v_r, this identity implies
 C_r(f(a_i))v_r=delta_r*v_r.
Thus [f(a_i)]-delta*empty has zero quantum image at all the infinitely
many specified levels. The disk-detection lemma makes each f(a_i)
a disk boundary in H.

These disjoint boundary curves bound disks with disjoint interiors,
by the usual innermost-circle surgery. They form a complete disk
system: their boundary complement is connected with the same cut
surface as the original complete system. Cutting H along the two
disk systems reduces the extension problem to a homeomorphism of a
sphere; this extends over the resulting 3-ball, and gluing the disks
back produces an extension of f. This proves (iii)=>(i).

At a unitary level, Cauchy--Schwarz gives
 |<v_r,rho_r(f)v_r>| <= ||v_r||^2,
with equality exactly when rho_r(f)v_r is a phase multiple of v_r.
That proves the saturation formulation without choosing the phase.

In fact a nonextending f fails to preserve the vacuum at every
sufficiently large level, not merely at some level: one failed disk
boundary gives a fixed nonzero rational spine coefficient, which has
only finitely many root-of-unity zeros or poles. Consequently the
unitary overlap inequality is eventually strict for nonextending f.

A CLOSED-MANIFOLD COROLLARY.
For a specified genus-g Heegaard gluing H union_f (-H), if the
absolute WRT gluing amplitude reaches the double-handlebody value
at infinitely many unitary levels, f extends over H, and the glued
manifold is #^g(S^1 x S^2). This is a maximal-value assertion for
that specified genus, not a claim that ordinary WRT values at the
S^3 value saturate a higher-genus presentation.

EXACT BASIC TEST.
For genus one, the modular S transformation sends a meridian to a
longitude and does not extend across the solid torus. In the
orthonormal SU(2) basis, vacuum overlap S_00 is
 sqrt(2/r)*sin(pi/r), strictly smaller than 1 for every r>=3.
The longitude skein is the nonconstant core variable z, rather than
delta. At A=-1 a diagonal representation with eigenvalues lambda,
lambda^-1 gives trace lambda+lambda^-1, which is not constantly 2.
Conversely a meridional Dehn twist extends and preserves the vacuum
line. These tests check both directions and the scalar normalization.

WHY THIS DOES NOT PROVE THE JONES CONJECTURE.

For a g-bridge plat and normalized cup state Omega, the ordinary
Jones evaluation has, up to a framing phase, the form
 V_K = d^(g-1) <Omega,U_beta Omega>,
where d=2cos(pi/r)>1 for r>3. Hence V_K=1 gives
 |<Omega,U_beta Omega>| = d^(1-g),
and exact leakage into the orthogonal complement is
 ||(I-|Omega><Omega|) U_beta Omega||^2 = 1-d^(2-2g).
For g>1 this is positive. The corresponding reflection Gram determinant
of Omega and U_beta Omega is also 1-d^(2-2g), not zero. Increasing
the number of levels never converts this interior value into equality.

Moreover the ordinary Jones representation of a punctured sphere
with all labels fixed at 1 is not the full TQFT space of a handlebody
boundary. At a classical specialization the fixed-label representation
can forget pure braiding; one cannot import the free-group character
argument into it. Passing to a branched cover or allowing arbitrary
colors adds data not supplied by the ordinary Jones polynomial.

The lemma precisely identifies a sufficient additional input:
projective vacuum preservation, or maximal reflected overlap, in the
full handlebody theories. Deriving that premise from V_K=1 remains
unproved. The present result is a valid endpoint for such a future
bridge, not evidence that the bridge itself holds.

PRIMARY REFERENCES AND THEIR ROLES.

Freedman--Walker--Wang, Quantum SU(2) faithfully detects mapping class
groups modulo center, Geometry & Topology6(2002),523--539.
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0209150
Fact2.6 supplies the colored-spine basis; proof of Proposition3.1
explicitly gives curve-operator covariance and the disk-boundary
eigenvalue. Their paper proves a different, related asymptotic
faithfulness theorem; the result above is derived here, not quoted
as their stated theorem.

Bullock, Rings of SL_2(C)-characters and the Kauffman bracket skein
module, Comment.Math.Helv.72(1997),521--542.
https://arxiv.org/abs/q-alg/9604014
Provides the A=-1 relation with trace functions. Only the elementary
evaluation map is needed here, not its injectivity.

Kuperberg, How Hard Is It to Approximate the Jones Polynomial?,
Theory of Computing11(2015),183--219, Theorem1.1.
https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v011a006/v011a006.pdf
States the plat return probability with the bridge-number factor.

Hovland, Bridge Positions and Plat Presentations of Links,
arXiv:2410.22556v2 (2026; accepted JKTR).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22556
Identifies bridge isotopy with Hilden double cosets and gives uniqueness
for each bridge number of the unknot. This supplies geometric context;
we do not prove the equivariant extension needed to connect our
handlebody statement to a Hilden-subgroup statement.
