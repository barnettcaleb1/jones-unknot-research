PROOF-ROUTE AUDIT: EXTREME STATE CANCELLATION
31 October 2026. Checked deduction and experiments; no novelty claim.

Known frontier.
Lowrance–Spyropoulos, The Jones polynomial of an almost alternating link,
NYJM23(2017), Theorem1.1: almost alternating and Turaev-genus-one links
cannot have the Jones polynomial of an unlink times a monomial.
Primary: https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2017/23-71p.pdf
Thus genus two is the first unexcluded genus for this strategy.

Exact layers.
For a connected diagram D with c crossings, all-A/all-B circle numbers
s_A,s_B, let g_D=(2+c-s_A-s_B)/2. Put M=c+2s_A-2. For a state S
obtained by changing b crossings from A to B, with s(S) state circles,
mu_A(S)=(s_A+b-s(S))/2 is a nonnegative integer. Expanding the usual
Kauffman state sum gives the coefficient of A^(M-4k):
 C_A,k = sum_{S:mu_A(S)<=k} (-1)^(s(S)-1) binom(s(S)-1,k-mu_A(S)).
There is an analogous formula from the B end. Let k_A,k_B be the first
nonzero layer indices. Then exactly
 span_t V_K = c-g_D-k_A-k_B.
In particular k_A+k_B<c-g_D is a sufficient nontriviality certificate.

The extreme coefficient is
 C_A,0=(-1)^(s_A-1) I(G_A,-1),
where G_A is the Lando graph and I its independence polynomial. If both
G_A and G_B are forests whose independence complexes are noncontractible,
then their reduced Euler characteristics are +/-1; both extremes survive,
and span=c-g_D>0 for a diagram with crossings. The empty graph is allowed,
with independence complex the (-1)-sphere. This is an elementary sufficient
criterion, extending the same calculation used in adequacy arguments.
Primary forest/independence results: Przytycki–Silvero, 2024,
https://arxiv.org/html/2305.18648 , Proposition3.6(2).
Extreme Khovanov/Lando correspondence: Gonzalez-Meneses–Manchon–Silvero,
https://arxiv.org/html/1511.05845 , Theorem4.

Actual countertests.
The local scripts reconstruct Lando graphs from state-circle counts, enumerate
full Laurent brackets, and independently compare with Regina Jones polynomials
for 254 actual diagrams: every prime knot through10 crossings, four11-crossing
knots, and one known8-crossing unknot diagram. All254 checks pass.
Files: lando_probe.py/json, extreme_layers.py/json.
Examples (these are diagram genera, not claims of minimal knot Turaev genus):

 10_133    10    2    0   0       8

In the minimal table diagram of11n118, G_A consists of two isolated vertices
and G_B of one isolated vertex. Both are forests, but both extreme Euler
characteristics vanish. Thus minimality+genus2+forest Lando graphs does not
force even one extreme coefficient to survive. For10_133 both complexes
are noncontractible and the sufficient criterion applies, even though one
of the Lando graphs is nonempty (two disjoint edges).

Stronger infinite obstruction: genus does not bound cancellation depth.
The standard Kanenobu diagram D(p,q) has Turaev genus2 whenever p,q are both
nonzero. This is explicitly computed in del Valle Vilchez–Lowrance,
On the arc index and Turaev genus of a link, arXiv2509.21274v2(8May2026),
proof of Theorem6.1, pp29–30:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.21274
Qazaqzeh–Mansour, Further study of Kanenobu knots, Proposition1.2 gives
 V_K(p,q)(t)=(-t)^(p+q)(V_K(0,0)(t)-1)+1,
 V_K(0,0)(t)=(t^-2-t^-1+1-t+t^2)^2.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.0683
Consequently for n>=1 the standard D(n,-n) has c=2n+8, g_D=2, and Jones
span8. Our exact layer identity therefore forces k_A+k_B=2n-2, unbounded.
For n>=3 these diagrams are within one crossing of minimal: the cited2026
paper records 2n+7<=c(K(n,-n))<=2n+8. No assertion that the standard diagrams
are always minimal, or that these knots have minimal Turaev genus2, is needed.
 n    c(D)   g_D   span V   k_A+k_B
 3     14     2      8          4
 5     18     2      8          8
10     28     2      8         18
This refutes any genus-only bound on cancellation depth for these diagrams.
These are actual nontrivial knots, not merely complicated unknot diagrams.

Research consequence.
Inspecting a fixed number of extreme coefficients cannot settle the genus2
frontier by a universal bounded-depth argument. A viable extension would need
to recognize and quotient the long cancellation mechanisms themselves, such
as opposite twist redistribution, then prove that every remaining minimal
core has positive Jones span. Root's exact affine-exponent method provides a
way to classify such mechanisms in each fixed twist template, but no theorem
currently makes the number of templates finite for arbitrary knots.
