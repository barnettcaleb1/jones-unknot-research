INDEPENDENT AUDIT: TWO JONES DERIVATIVES DETECT THE UNKNOT AMONG 3-PRETZEL KNOTS
31 October2026. Algebra checked by hand and independently against TL2.
This is a class result; no novelty claim and no general Jones theorem.

Let P(p,q,r)=N(<1/p>+<1/q>+<1/r>), with integer parameters. It is a knot
exactly when all three parameters are odd or exactly one is even. Set
 y=A^4, d=y+2+y^-1, x_p=(-1)^p y^-p, and S=p+q+r.
The bracket is A^S E/d, where
 E=x_p x_q x_r+(d-1)(x_p+x_q+x_r)+(d-1)(d-2).
The primary twist-bracket formula used by root is independently recovered
from the two-dimensional Temperley–Lieb algebra. The script pretzel_audit.py
checked2197 integer triples, and also symbolically audited all60 leaves of
the complete affine-exponent solver. That solver is unnecessary for the
short moment argument below.

The normalized Jones polynomial is E/(s d y^m) for the signed monomial
forced by writhe. Since V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0 for knots, s and m can instead
be determined from E at y=1 and its first logarithmic derivative there.
The parameter y is t or t^-1 depending on the A convention; either choice
preserves the condition V''(1)=V'''(1)=0. Multiplication by a nonzero analytic
factor also preserves the first nonzero derivative order. Consequently
these two Jones derivatives vanish if and only if the second and third
logarithmic derivatives of F=E-s d y^m vanish.

All-odd case.
E(1)=-4, (y d/dy)E(1)=4S. Thus s=-1,m=-S. Moreover
 F=(d-1)Q,
 Q=y^-S-y^-p-y^-q-y^-r+y+y^-1.
Q(1)=Q_log'(1)=0, and
 Q_log''(1)=2(pq+pr+qr+1),
 Q_log'''(1)=-3(p+q)(q+r)(r+p).
Both vanish only if some pair sums to zero. If p+q=0, then the quadratic
condition gives 1-p^2=0. Thus the triple is a permutation of(k,1,-1).
Conversely this makes Q identically zero.

One-even case.
Take p even, q=a+b,r=a-b odd; a,b are integers of opposite parity.
E(1)=4, E_log'(1)=4(a-p), so s=1,m=a-p. Direct differentiation gives
 M2=F_log''(1)=6(1-b^2-a^2+2pa),
 M3=F_log'''(1)=6a(1+3b^2-a^2-3p^2).
After imposing M2=0, the remaining equation is
 a[4-a^2-3(p-a)^2]=0.
If a=0, then b=+/-1, giving(k,1,-1).
If a!=0, solve the integer ellipse a^2+3(p-a)^2=4:
 * a=+/-2 forces p=a and b^2=5, impossible;
 * a=+/-1 gives p=0 or p=2a;
 * p=0 gives b=0 and triples(0,1,1),(0,-1,-1);
 * p=2a gives b=+/-2 and permutations of(1,-2,-3),(-1,2,3).
There are no other integer solutions.

All surviving knots are actual unknots.
Three-box pretzel cyclic rotation and reversal allow all permutations.
For(k,1,-1), adjacent integer tangles+1,-1 cancel, leaving N(1/k), an unknot.
For(0,1,1), the infinity tangle plus two integer twists has unknotted numerator
closure; its mirror is the same conclusion. The exceptional(1,-2,-3) has a unit twist box and is therefore a
two-rational-tangle Montesinos closure, hence a two-bridge link. Its
determinant is |pq+pr+qr|=|-2-3+6|=1, so it is the unknot. The same applies
to its mirror. Direct Reidemeister simplification is independently certified
in pretzel_audit.json for both signed exceptions, with original PD codes.
Thus V''(1)=V'''(1)=0 forces the actual unknot in this entire knot class.
The claim does not apply to arbitrary pretzel LINKS with multiple even
parameters, nor to arbitrary Montesinos knots with more general slopes.

Literature/novelty caution. Sleptsov, Vassiliev invariants for pretzel knots,
arXiv1512.07192(2015), already computes finite-type invariants up to order6.
The2025 paper Algebraic structures of Vassiliev invariants for knot families,
DOI10.1016/j.nuclphysb.2025.117268, discusses low-order invariants for these
families. We have not exhaustively checked whether the precise two-derivative
statement above has appeared before, and make no novelty claim.
