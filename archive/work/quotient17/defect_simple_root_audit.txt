EXACTLY ONE ROOT FACTOR IN A NONZERO-SATO--LEVINE SHELL
31 October 2026. Independent proof by generic_braid.

STATEMENT

Use the normalized shell map and notation from
whitehead_pattern_audit.txt, section7. More generally take a componentwise
strongly invertible link P union A with A=U, lk(P,A)=0, P(U)=U, and nonzero
Sato--Levine invariant beta. With the compatible preferred boundary markings,
the normalized shell map preserves the meridional closure functional and
Q0, and its other eigenvalue d(A) lies in Z[A,A^-1]. Its defect law is

                            x(F(T))=d(A)x(T),

where delta=-A^2-A^-2 and

            x(T)=(f0(T)/f_infinity(T)-delta)/(1-delta^2).

Then d(A) has EXACTLY ONE factor A^4+1. In particular this holds for each
standard positive or negative untwisted Whitehead shell. No explicit shell
matrix or quotient diagram is needed beyond the audited geometric normal
form and the standard filling identifications.

PROOF

Set zeta=exp(pi i/4), so zeta^4=-1, delta(zeta)=0, t=A^-4=-1, and

                   |dt/dA| at zeta=4,  |delta'(zeta)|=4.

Choose the rational input Q1 whose meridian disk has marked slope
lambda_U+mu_U. Its two marked closures have covers given by filling a solid
torus along slopes of distance one from this disk slope. Both are therefore
actual unknots: they are determinant-one two-bridge knots. In particular
f_infinity(Q1) and f0(Q1) are framing monomials, and

                         |x(Q1)(zeta)|=1.                 (1)

This is the crucial distinction from an actual preferred quotient of a
nontrivial S3 knot. Q1 is a rational filling probe; its marked zero slope is
not the longitude of its solid-torus covering exterior.

Under the shell gluing, Q1 performs +1 surgery on the unknot A. Its output
is the preferred exterior quotient of P1 in S3. Winding zero keeps the
marked outer zero slope equal to the preferred longitude. Thus the zero
closure L1 has double cover S3_0(P1), so V_L1(-1)=0. Its infinity closure is
U. Hence x(F(Q1))(zeta)=0. Equation(1) and the defect law give

                              d(zeta)=0.                  (2)

Write R(A)=f0(F(Q1))/f_infinity(F(Q1)). Its denominator is a framing unit.
The relation to the normalized Jones polynomial of L1 is a signed Laurent
monomial factor times V_L1(A^-4). That factor has absolute value one at
zeta. Since V_L1(-1)=0, differentiation of the factor contributes zero.
The audited Garoufalidis/zero-surgery identity consequently yields

                       |R'(zeta)|=4|12a2(P1)-1|.          (3)

The Hoste surgery formula gives a2(P1)=a2(P)-beta=-beta, since P=P(U)=U
before the A surgery. Therefore a2(P1) is a nonzero integer and

                       |R'(zeta)| >= 44 > 4.

Since delta(zeta)=0, differentiating the definition of x at zeta gives

                       x(F(Q1))'(zeta)=R'(zeta)-delta'(zeta).

The strict inequality above proves this derivative is nonzero, independently
of every signature, chirality, square-root, and writhe phase convention.
Differentiating the defect law and applying (2) now gives

              x(F(Q1))'(zeta)=d'(zeta)x(Q1)(zeta).

By (1), d'(zeta) is nonzero. Thus zeta is a simple root of d.

Finally, multiply the Laurent polynomial d by a power of A to obtain an
integer polynomial. The minimal polynomial of zeta over Q is A^4+1.
Equation(2) gives divisibility by A^4+1, while d'(zeta)!=0 excludes its
square. This proves the exact multiplicity assertion. The argument also
independently proves d is nonzero.

ITERATION

For a composition of r shells satisfying these hypotheses, the normalized
defect multipliers multiply. Therefore the composite multiplier has exact
order r along A^4+1. For an actual fixed input tangle with nonzero defect,
the local vanishing order of its output defect at zeta increases by exactly
r. If the input defect is identically zero, it remains zero; no finite-order
statement should be substituted for that case.

This describes how a nonzero full Jones-tangle channel can become invisible
to increasingly many derivatives at t=-1. It does not make the full channel
zero, nor promote the ordinary Jones polynomial of the cover knot into
knowledge of its quotient-tangle skein vector.

ADDENDUM: EXACT FIRST-DERIVATIVE MAGNITUDE AND THE ZERO-BETA CASE

The root agent's stronger calculation also passes independent audit. It
removes the beta!=0 hypothesis until the final conclusion.

Choose the standard integer crossing Q1=A Q0+A^-1 Q_infinity. Its
meridional bracket is A+A^-1 delta=-A^-3. After division by this framing
unit its vector is -A^4 Q0-A^2 Q_infinity. Writing z=-A^4, the normalized
integer rational tangle Q_n is therefore

               z^n Q0 + [(1-z^n)/delta] Q_infinity,

for every integer n. This follows also from the two-eigenvalue integer
twist formula. The expression is Laurent-polynomial for negative as well
as positive n: (1-z)/delta=-A^2, and the remaining geometric sum is
Laurent-polynomial in z. Its meridional bracket is exactly one. Thus

            x(Q_n)=(1-z^n)/delta,   x(Q_n)(zeta)=-n zeta^2.

The last identity follows either from the finite geometric sum or from
l'Hopital: z'(zeta)=-4zeta^3 and delta'(zeta)=-4zeta.

The output zero-closure ratio R_n=f0(F(Q_n))/f_infinity(F(Q_n)) satisfies

             R_n=delta+(1-delta^2)d x(Q_n).

The Q1 probe already proves d(zeta)=0 for EVERY shell under consideration,
even when beta=0. Differentiating gives the exact identity

                  R_n'(zeta)=delta'(zeta)-n zeta^2 d'(zeta).

The longitudinal derivative formula and a2(P_n)=epsilon*n*beta, where
epsilon=+1 or -1 records the coherent surgery convention, give

       |delta'(zeta)-n zeta^2 d'(zeta)|^2
                         =16(12 epsilon*n*beta-1)^2.

These are real quadratic polynomials in the integer n. Comparison of the
quadratic coefficients yields

                |d'(zeta)|^2=2304 beta^2,
                |d'(zeta)|=48 |beta|.

In fact the three actual probes n=-1,0,1 suffice for this coefficient
comparison; there is no asymptotic or uniform-family assumption hidden in
it. This also follows by adding the n=+/-1 identities and subtracting twice
the n=0 identity.

No phase correction is missing. A framed closure ratio can differ from the
normalized Jones polynomial by a monomial, but its magnitude at zeta is
one. The derivative of that factor contributes zero because the longitude
Jones polynomial vanishes at t=-1. Thus the displayed magnitude identity
is valid whether or not one separately fixes common oriented writhe.

Consequently, for these shells,

          beta=0  iff  d'(zeta)=0  iff  (A^4+1)^2 divides d(A).

Divisibility includes the possible identically-zero polynomial. If beta is
nonzero, d is nonzero and A^4+1 divides it exactly once, as above. If beta
is zero, this result alone neither proves nor disproves injectivity: d may
be zero, or a nonzero polynomial with a higher-order zero.
