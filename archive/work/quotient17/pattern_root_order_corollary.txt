ASSUMPTIONS AND NORMALIZATION

Let P union A be an actual two-component pattern link with A unknotted,
lk(P,A)=0, P(U)=U, and a component-preserving strong inversion giving the
four-ended shell map F. Normalize F by its fixed meridian framing unit.
As proved in whitehead_pattern_audit.txt, Section 7,
  F(Q_0)=Q_0,   f_infinity F=f_infinity,
  F(Q_infinity-delta Q_0)=d(A)(Q_infinity-delta Q_0),
where d is an integral Laurent polynomial, possibly zero in general.
Put delta=-A^2-A^-2 and
  x(T)=(f_0(T)/f_infinity(T)-delta)/(1-delta^2).
Then x(F(T))=d(A)x(T).

Set A_0=exp(i*pi/4), so t=A^-4=-1, delta(A_0)=0, |dt/dA|=4,
and |delta'(A_0)|=4. The denominator 1-delta^2 equals 1 there.

CLAIM 1. A^4+1 DIVIDES d FOR EVERY SUCH PATTERN.

Use the actual rational input Q_1 whose covering solid torus has disk
slope lambda_U+mu_U. Its infinity filling is S^3. Its input zero filling
is also S^3, since the zero slope lambda_U has distance one from that
disk slope. Thus both corresponding branch closures are actual unknots.
In particular f_infinity(Q_1) is a nonzero framing monomial and
  x(Q_1)(A_0)=f_0(Q_1)(A_0)/f_infinity(Q_1)(A_0) != 0.

Important marking distinction: Q_1 is a rational unknot exterior with a
TWISTED input longitude marking. Its input zero slope is not its own
preferred longitude. This does not affect the fixed shell construction.

After insertion Q_1 performs 1-surgery on A and produces the knot P_1
in S^3. Winding zero ensures that the fixed OUTPUT zero slope is the
preferred longitude of P_1. Therefore the output zero branch link has
determinant zero: f_0(F(Q_1))(A_0)=0. The meridian closure is an unknot,
so its bracket is nonzero at A_0. It follows that x(F(Q_1))(A_0)=0.
The transfer law now forces d(A_0)=0. The minimal polynomial of A_0
over Q is A^4+1. Since d is an integral Laurent polynomial, Gauss's
lemma gives divisibility by A^4+1 in Z[A,A^-1].

CLAIM 2. IF beta(P union A) != 0, THIS FACTOR HAS MULTIPLICITY ONE.

Hoste's surgery formula gives a_2(P_n)=a_2(P_0)-n beta. Here P_0=U,
so a_2(P_1)=-beta is a nonzero integer. The exact longitudinal
Garoufalidis--Lescop derivative identity is
  |V'_(L_1)(-1)|=|12 a_2(P_1)-1| > 1.
See ../fanout16/realizability_zero_filling.txt and
sato_levine_skein_injectivity.txt for normalizations and primary sources.

Orient the output meridian unknot, then orient the two components of
the zero branch link to agree on the two tangle arcs. The standard
zero and infinity closures add no crossings and have equal writhe.
Consequently R=f_0/f_infinity is EXACTLY the Jones polynomial of that
oriented zero branch link, after t=A^-4. This ratio identification does
not require linking number zero; choosing the compatible orientation is
enough. Orientation changes affect the derivative magnitude at -1 only
by a unit because the polynomial itself vanishes there.

At A_0 we have R=delta=0, hence x'=R'-delta'. But
  |R'(A_0)|=4 |12 a_2(P_1)-1| > 4=|delta'(A_0)|,
so x'(F(Q_1))(A_0) is nonzero. Differentiating the transfer law, using
d(A_0)=0 and x(Q_1)(A_0) != 0, gives
  x'(F(Q_1))(A_0)=d'(A_0) x(Q_1)(A_0).
Thus d'(A_0) != 0. The irreducible factor A^4+1 occurs exactly once.
In particular d is nonzero, recovering the injectivity criterion.

CONCRETE CONSEQUENCE: ACTUAL LINKS WITH ARBITRARILY DEEP HIDDEN DEFECT

Take an actual strongly inverted trefoil exterior T. Its a_2=1, so the
same magnitude inequality shows x(T) has a simple zero at A_0. It is
not identically zero. For the ordinary Whitehead pattern beta=+/-1,
and Claim 2 gives a simple zero of its nonzero factor d.

After r inherited untwisted Whitehead iterations,
  x(T_r)=d(A)^r x(T).
Thus x(T_r) is nonzero and has EXACT order r+1 at A=A_0. Since
  R(T_r)-delta=(1-delta^2)x(T_r)
and t=A^-4 has nonzero derivative at A_0, the Jones polynomial of the
actual longitudinal two-component branch link L_r differs from the
two-component unlink polynomial by a function with EXACT order r+1
at t=-1 (using either consistent local square-root branch).

Therefore its values and derivatives of orders 0 through r at -1
agree with the unlink, while derivative r+1 differs. Nevertheless the
full Jones polynomial differs. No formal-realizability assumption is
used: these are the inherited strong-inversion quotients of actual
iterated Whitehead doubles of a trefoil.

This supplies a precise limitation of any finite collection of local
Jones derivatives at the determinant root in the quotient-realization
problem. It concerns two-component longitudinal branch links, not an
ordinary Jones-one knot construction or a generic detection theorem.

BOUNDARY CHECK: COMPOSITE WHITEHEAD HAS beta=0 BUT FULL RANK

The composite pattern's rational-input family is Wh_0(Wh_n(U)). Its
outer Alexander polynomial is 1 for every n, so Hoste's formula gives
beta=0. Its transfer factor is d_Wh^2, however, which is nonzero and
has a zero of order two at A_0. Thus beta=0 does not imply rank loss;
it permits a higher-order zero rather than an identically zero map.
