EXACT FIRST JET OF THE QUOTIENT DEFECT MULTIPLIER
31 October 2026. Root derivation, independently audited.

Assume the compatible pattern P has winding zero and P(U)=U. Normalize its
map between rank-two skein modules as in sato_levine_skein_injectivity.txt:
F(Q_0)=Q_0, f_infinity F=f_infinity, and F(v)=d(A)v for
v=Q_infinity-delta Q_0, delta=-A^2-A^-2. Put z=-A^4 and
zeta=exp(pi i/4). The determinant argument already gives d(zeta)=0.

Choose the integer rational-tangle crossing convention

                     Q_1=A Q_0+A^-1 Q_infinity.

For n crossings the infinity closure is a chain of curls, so
f_infinity(Q_n)=(-A^-3)^n. The two twist eigenvalues have ratio z, giving

         x(Q_n)=(1-z^n)/delta,
         x(Q_1)=(1+A^4)/delta=-A^2.

This also follows by the recurrence x_(n+1)=z*x_n+(1-z)/delta, with x_0=0;
the formula works for negative n as well. At zeta, z=1 and delta=0, so
l'Hopital's rule gives

        x(Q_n)(zeta)=[-n z^(n-1) z']/delta' at zeta
                    =(4n zeta^3)/(-4zeta)=-n zeta^2.          (1)

The output F(Q_n) is the actual preferred exterior quotient of the pattern
knot P_n after 1/n surgery on the unknotted axis, up to the coherent sign
of n. Its meridian closure is U and its zero closure L_n is the preferred
longitude branch link. With the canonical orientation matching tangle arcs
to the infinity unknot, R_n=f_0(FQ_n)/f_infinity(FQ_n) is exactly normalized
Jones V_(L_n)(A^-4). No input-preferred-longitude assumption is needed.

Since R_n=delta+(1-delta^2)d*x(Q_n), equations d(zeta)=delta(zeta)=0 give

             R_n'(zeta)=-4zeta-n zeta^2 d'(zeta).             (2)

Hoste's formula gives a_2(P_n)=-n beta in a compatible convention, where
beta is the Sato--Levine invariant of P union axis. Reversing the twist
parameter changes the sign of n. The determinant-zero Jones/Lescop identity
and |dt/dA|=4 therefore imply

             |R_n'(zeta)|^2=16(1+12n beta)^2,                (3)

or the same formula with n replaced by -n. This holds for every integer n.
Both sides of (2)--(3) are real quadratic polynomials in n. Their quadratic
coefficients give

                   |d'(zeta)|^2=16*144 beta^2,
                   |d'(zeta)|=48 |beta|.                    (4)

Thus beta vanishes if and only if d'(zeta) vanishes. Nonzero beta is exactly
the condition that d has a simple zero at zeta. If beta=0, d is either zero
identically or has a zero of order at least two. Since d has Laurent-integer
coefficients, these statements are equivalently about multiplicity of the
cyclotomic factor A^4+1.

If all signs are fixed as in (3), comparison of the linear coefficient gives
Re(zeta*d'(zeta))=48 beta. Together with (4), this forces
d'(zeta)=48 beta zeta^-1. The magnitude (4) is the preferred statement because
it is independent of the chosen crossing/surgery sign convention.

The double-Whitehead composite gives a concrete nonzero d with beta=0 and
zero of order exactly two. Its higher iterates have higher exact orders.
This links a classical geometric link invariant directly to the first
nontrivial jet of a quantum skein transformation, without any promotion
from uncolored to colored knot invariants.
