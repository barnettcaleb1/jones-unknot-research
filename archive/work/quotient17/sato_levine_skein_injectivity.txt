SATO--LEVINE OBSTRUCTION TO LOSS OF QUOTIENT SKEIN INFORMATION
31 October 2026. Research deduction, not a novelty claim.

STATEMENT AND SCOPE

Let P union A be an oriented two-component link in S3 with A unknotted and
lk(P,A)=0. Regard P as a pattern in the solid torus S3 minus nu(A). Assume
a component-preserving strong inversion whose quotient is a shell between
two four-marked-point spheres, so insertion defines the linear map

              F_P : S(B3,4 points;Q(A)) -> S(B3,4 points;Q(A)).

Both skein spaces have dimension two. Mark the output boundary by the
meridian and preferred longitude of P. If the Sato--Levine invariant
beta(P union A) is nonzero, then F_P is injective.

The pattern knot P(U) need not be an unknot for this injectivity statement.
If it is an unknot, then F_P also preserves and reflects the preferred
rational-zero skein line. All statements concern the entire marked exterior
quotient tangle, not the ordinary Jones polynomial of its covering knot.

1. RATIONAL INPUTS AND THEIR MARKINGS

Let Q_n be the rational input whose solid-torus meridian disk has slope
lambda_U+n mu_U on the input boundary. Satellite gluing identifies
mu_U with lambda_A and lambda_U with mu_A. Thus Q_n performs 1/n surgery
on A. Since A is an unknot, the result is S3, with P replaced by a knot
P_n. Write a_n=a_2(P_n), where Delta_K(t) is symmetric, Delta_K(1)=1,
and a_2(K)=Delta_K''(1)/2 is the coefficient of z^2 in the Conway polynomial.

The outer meridian is fixed, and the outer longitude remains the preferred
longitude for every n. Indeed lk(P,A)=0, so lambda_P is already nullhomologous
in the two-component exterior; the surgery framing correction n*lk(P,A)^2
vanishes. The meridional branch closure of each F_P(Q_n) is an actual
unknot, since its double cover is S3. The inherited involution has fixed
points, so this is the usual strong-inversion quotient situation.

2. EXACT ALEXANDER VARIATION FROM HOSTE'S FORMULA

Use the Sato--Levine sign convention of Gorsky--Liu--Moore, Section 6.2:
their equations (6.2)--(6.3) give, for the algebraically split link P union A,

 lambda(S3_(+1,1/n)(P union A))
     = a_2(P) + n a_2(A) - n beta(P union A).

Here lambda is the Casson invariant normalized by lambda(S3_+1(K))=a_2(K).
Because A is an unknot, a_2(A)=0. Performing its surgery first gives S3,
and the remaining +1 framing on P is unchanged because lk(P,A)=0.
Therefore the same closed manifold is S3_+1(P_n), proving

                 a_n = a_0 - n beta(P union A).                 (1)

The sign changes with a convention for beta; nonvanishing is unaffected.
Only n=0 and n=1 will be needed.

3. THE LONGITUDINAL JONES DERIVATIVE REMEMBERS A_2 INJECTIVELY

For an actual marked knot exterior quotient T_J whose meridian filling is
the unknot, let L_J be its longitude branch closure. The longitude double
cover is S3_0(J). The Garoufalidis determinant-zero formula together with
the zero-surgery Lescop formula gives

                 |V'_(L_J)(-1)| = |12 a_2(J)-1|.               (2)

This was audited with conventions in
../fanout16/realizability_zero_filling.txt. In Garoufalidis's doubled-Casson
normalization lambda_G(S3_0(J))=2a_2(J)-1/6. His Theorem 1 has
i^(sigma+nu) lambda_G = J'(-1)/6+J(-1)*sigma/4; here nu=1 and J(-1)=0.
Changing Jones convention or branch-link orientation leaves (2) unchanged.

Multiplying V_(L_J) by any signed Laurent monomial does not change the
magnitude in (2): V_(L_J)(-1)=0 and that monomial has modulus one at -1.
The function a -> |12a-1| is injective on integers. Equality with the same
sign gives a=b; equality with opposite signs would give 6(a+b)=1, impossible.

4. PROOF OF INJECTIVITY

The map F_P is nonzero since its output meridional closure on Q_0 is an
unknot. Suppose its rank were one. Then

                   F_P(Q_1)=c(A) F_P(Q_0).

Evaluate the common outer meridional closure. Both sides are brackets of
actual unknots, so c(A) is a framing monomial. If the bracket convention
assigns delta to an unknot, its common delta factor cancels; the conclusion
is the same. In particular c is not an arbitrary rational function.

Now evaluate the common preferred-longitude closure. After the usual
writhe normalization, the two Jones polynomials differ by a signed Laurent
monomial. Formula (2) implies

                         |12a_1-1|=|12a_0-1|.

Integer injectivity gives a_1=a_0. Equation (1) then gives beta(P union A)=0,
a contradiction. Thus rank F_P=2, proving injectivity.

If P(U)=U, then F_P(Q_0) is itself the preferred rational-zero vector up to
a framing unit. Linearity and injectivity consequently give, for any input T,

 T is proportional to Q_0 iff F_P(T) is proportional to the output Q_0.

For actual knot exterior quotients the proportionality scalars are framing
monomials, by evaluating the meridian-unknot closure. Compatible finite
compositions of such pattern maps retain injectivity and this equivalence.

5. A SOURCE-BACKED FAMILY OF APPLICATIONS

Gorsky--Liu--Moore, Corollary 6.4, prove that a two-component L-space link
with linking number zero and both components unknotted has beta<=0, with
equality precisely for the unlink. Therefore every nontrivial link in this
class that ALSO admits the stipulated compatible strong inversion defines
an injective quotient skein map. Its unknotted pattern component ensures
preservation and reflection of the preferred rational-zero line.

The inversion hypothesis is essential to the formulation: it is not being
asserted that every L-space link has a suitable inversion. This application
uses a proved Floer-theoretic result only to certify beta is nonzero.

6. SHARP LIMIT: BETA ZERO DOES NOT IMPLY LOSS OF INFORMATION

Take the composition of two untwisted Whitehead patterns with compatible
inversions. Each individual map is injective by the twist-knot test in
whitehead_injectivity_audit.txt, so the composed map F_Wh squared is injective.

Its rational-input family consists of

                         Wh_0(Wh_n(U)),

up to the fixed choice of clasp convention. The outer untwisted Whitehead
double has Alexander polynomial one for EVERY companion; preferred longitude
is preserved at the intermediate winding-zero gluing. Consequently every
member of this rational-input family has a_2=0. Equation (1) shows that the
two-component link defining the composite pattern has beta=0.

Thus there is an actual compatible pattern with beta=0 and injective quotient
skein map. All its longitudinal derivative magnitudes in (2) equal one.
The Sato--Levine criterion is sufficient, not necessary, and even the entire
framing family at this Casson level cannot classify injectivity. This is a
concrete limit on trying to settle the realization question using only the
first derivative at -1.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

This supplies a geometric obstruction to hiding a non-flat exterior quotient
inside certain winding-zero satellites, despite such satellites often erasing
the companion's Alexander polynomial. It excludes inherited Whitehead and
iterated-Whitehead quotients of cores with a_2 nonzero from the formal
Jones-flat tangle target.

It does not prove that an arbitrary Jones-one knot has a Jones-flat exterior
quotient, does not promote ordinary Jones to colored Jones, and leaves cores
with a_2=0 unresolved. No generic Jones unknot-detection claim follows.

ADDENDUM: EXACT SCALAR TRANSMISSION LAW (ROOT'S FORMULATION, AUDITED)

For the same shell, let f_infinity and f_0 be the meridian and longitude
closure functionals. Filling the output meridian first leaves E(A), a solid
torus. Its inherited strong-inversion quotient is rational. Its compressible
slope lambda_A maps to mu_K on the input boundary. Consequently

                    f_infinity composed with F = u f_infinity

for one fixed framing unit u, independent of the inserted skein vector.
Normalize F by u^-1. Assume now also P(U)=U. With the standard basis
Q_0,Q_infinity, use f_infinity(Q_0)=1, f_infinity(Q_infinity)=delta,
f_0(Q_0)=delta, f_0(Q_infinity)=1, where delta=-A^2-A^-2.
The output longitude is preferred because the winding number is zero.
Thus F(Q_0) is on the Q_0 line. Evaluating f_infinity forces F(Q_0)=Q_0.

The one-dimensional kernel of f_infinity is spanned by

                         v=Q_infinity-delta Q_0.

Since f_infinity F=f_infinity, F preserves this kernel. Therefore, in the
basis (Q_0,v), F has diagonal matrix diag(1,d(A)). In particular F is
injective precisely when d(A) is nonzero.

For any T with f_infinity(T) nonzero, put T_hat=T/f_infinity(T). Its normalized
departure from Q_0 is measured by

           x(T)=(f_0(T_hat)-delta)/(1-delta^2),
           T_hat=Q_0+x(T)v.

The exact transformation law is

                            x(F(T))=d(A)x(T).

Compositions multiply their d factors. Nonzero beta is a sufficient geometric
certificate that d is nonzero. The composite-Whitehead example above has beta
zero but d nonzero. No claim is made that d is a unit of Z[A,A^-1], or that
this normalized defect is determined by the ordinary Jones polynomial of the
covering knot. The framing normalization is a single shell normalization,
not a choice depending on the input knot.
