INDEPENDENT GEOMETRIC AND NORMALIZATION AUDIT
31 October 2026. Auditor: generic_braid.

Verdict: the Whitehead quotient skein-map argument passes, with the exact
scope and normalization distinctions below. This is not an assertion that
the ordinary Jones polynomial detects the unknot. No novelty is claimed.

1. EXACT TWO-CLOSURE JONES DATA REALLY GIVE A PROJECTIVE SKEIN VECTOR

Let T be an actual four-ended tangle, with no closed components, in a fixed
marked ball. Let the two planar closures be denoted infinity and zero.
Suppose T(infinity) is an actual unknot and

                     V_{T(0)}(t)=-(t^(1/2)+t^(-1/2)).

The value at t=1 ensures T(0) has two components. Its two strands therefore
close separately in this closure. Choose the strand orientations to be the
ones induced by an orientation of the infinity-closure unknot. These also
orient the two zero-closure components. If this choice reverses a component
relative to the orientation used in the displayed Jones equality, it does
not change that equality: for two-component links V_L'(1)=-3 lk(L), so the
displayed equality forces lk(L)=0; component reversal changes V by a power
t^(+/-3 lk(L)), which here is one.

Draw both closure systems as planar caps outside the tangle disk. Neither
adds any crossing, and the orientations inside the disk now agree. Thus
the writhe of BOTH closed diagrams is one and the same integer w. With
delta=-A^2-A^(-2), the bracket closure values are

                     (-A^3)^w,       (-A^3)^w delta.

The standard rational reference Q0 has closure values (1,delta). The two
closure functionals on the two-dimensional tangle skein space have pairing
matrix [[1,delta],[delta,1]], up to simultaneous ordering of the bases.
Its determinant 1-delta^2 is nonzero in Q(A). Consequently

                          [T]=(-A^3)^w [Q0].

This is a statement in the fixed boundary marking. An arbitrary boundary
mapping class cannot be silently absorbed into the framing scalar.

The exact equality hypothesis matters. For merely
V_{T(0)}=+/-t^r V_{U2}, linking need not vanish, and the common-writhe step
does not prove the same projective conclusion.

2. THE WEAKER MONOMIAL CASE STILL SATISFIES THE CASSON FILTER

In that weaker case, V_{T(0)}(-1)=0 and |V_{T(0)}'(-1)|=1, using a local
choice of square root and |(-1)^r|=1. Differentiation of the monomial adds
no term because V_{U2}(-1)=0. In the convention of Garoufalidis's Theorem1
the same absolute statements hold.

If the double cover of T is E(J), with the infinity and zero fillings the
preferred meridian and longitude, respectively, the audited surgery formula
is lambda_G(S^3_0(J))=2a2(J)-1/6. The link formula gives
|lambda_G|=1/6. Hence |12a2(J)-1|=1, and integrality gives a2(J)=0.

Thus the rank-one contradiction below does not require an unjustified
assertion that a framing monomial is also the normalized Jones unit.

3. WHITEHEAD QUOTIENT MAP

Take the standard component-preserving strong inversion of the Whitehead
link P union A, compatible with the chosen strong inversion on the
companion. Removing invariant tubular neighborhoods of the two components
gives quotient underlying space S^2 times I, with four marked endpoints on
each boundary sphere. Inserting a four-ended tangle at the inner boundary
therefore induces a LINEAR map F between two-dimensional Q(A) skein spaces.
Only one input tangle is inserted. This is not a nonlinear colored-cabling
evaluation, and its input is not the scalar ordinary Jones polynomial.

Use the fixed peripheral identification mu_K -> lambda_A and
lambda_K -> mu_A, up to coherent signs. The rational input Q_n with disk
slope lambda_U+n mu_U therefore fills A along mu_A+n lambda_A, namely
1/n surgery. The n=0 output is the actual unknot exterior. The n=1 output
is a nontrivial twist-knot exterior with a2=+/-1. More generally a2=+/-n;
the sign is immaterial here. Since lk(P,A)=0, surgery changes neither the
outer meridian marking nor the outer preferred longitude marking.

The external meridional closures are actual unknots: each is the branch
set of the extended strong inversion on S^3. The standard equivariant
construction supplies the extension; the Smith conjecture identifies its
nonempty fixed circle as an unknot.

F(Q_0) is nonzero because its meridional bracket closure is a unit. If F
had rank one, F(Q_1)=c(A)F(Q_0). Evaluation under the SAME outer meridional
closure makes c a ratio of bracket values of actual unknots, hence a
framing unit, not an arbitrary rational function. The longitude closure
of F(Q_1) would then have Jones polynomial a monomial times V_{U2}. By
section2 its covering knot would have a2=0, contradicting a2=+/-1.
Therefore rank(F)=2.

4. INHERITED QUOTIENTS AND ITERATION

After the preferred meridian-longitude identification of input and output,
F preserves the projective rational-zero line: Whitehead doubling the
unknot gives the unknot. Since F is injective, it follows that

            F(T) lies on that line iff T lies on that line.

This conclusion concerns the PARTICULAR marked rational-zero line. From
the bare statement that F(T) equals some rational-tangle vector one cannot
infer that T is rational; the inverse of F need not preserve the set of
all rational-tangle vectors.

Combined with section1, an inherited Whitehead quotient with actual
meridional unknot closure and exact longitude Jones V_{U2} forces the
companion quotient onto the same rational-zero line, and hence forces
a2(companion)=0 by section2. The statement iterates for any finite sequence
of the standard positive or negative untwisted Whitehead constructions
with their compatible inherited inversions. Thus iterated Whitehead
doubles of a trefoil core cannot supply the proposed Jones-flat quotient,
even though the doubled knots themselves have Alexander polynomial one.

Not proved: the conclusion for a different, non-inherited strong inversion;
geometric triviality of a tangle on the rational-zero skein line; exclusion
of a2=0 cores; any ordinary-Jones-to-full-quotient reconstruction theorem.

5. THE GENERAL SATO--LEVINE CRITERION ALSO PASSES

Let L=P union A be any two-component link with A an unknot, lk(P,A)=0,
and a componentwise strong inversion whose exterior quotient is the marked
shell defining a map F as above. Neither P=U nor P(U)=U is required in this
section. Let P_n be the remaining knot after 1/n surgery on A. The ambient
manifold is S^3, and its preferred longitude for P_n is the same marked
outer slope, since lk(P,A)=0. Each output F(Q_n) therefore has an actual
meridional unknot closure and a longitudinal closure L_n with

                    |V_{L_n}'(-1)|=|12a2(P_n)-1|.

Suppose rank(F)=1. The meridional closure implies F(Q_0) is nonzero and
F(Q_n)=c_n F(Q_0), with c_n a framing unit. Longitudinal framed brackets
are proportional by that unit; after oriented writhe correction their
Jones polynomials differ by a signed Laurent monomial. Their value at -1
vanishes because each branched cover is zero surgery. Thus their first
derivative magnitudes at -1 coincide. Therefore

                    |12a2(P_n)-1|=|12a2(P_0)-1|.

The integer-valued function a -> |12a-1| is injective. Indeed the only
alternative to a=b in an equality of these magnitudes is a+b=1/6, which
is impossible for integers. Hence rank one would force every a2(P_n)
to be the same.

Hoste's algebraically split surgery formula supplies the contradiction
when the Sato--Levine invariant beta(L) is nonzero. In the convention of
Gorsky--Liu--Moore, Section6.2, equations(6.2)--(6.3), simultaneous
(1,1/n) surgery on P union A has Casson invariant

                   a2(P)+n a2(A)-n beta(L)=a2(P)-n beta(L).

If instead one first performs surgery on A, one is left with +1 surgery
on P_n in S^3. The remaining coefficient is still +1 because lk=0.
The one-knot surgery formula consequently identifies the same invariant
with a2(P_n), so

                         a2(P_n)=a2(P)-n beta(L).

Thus beta(L) != 0 implies rank(F)=2. This use of Hoste's formula has NO
L-space hypothesis; that subsection of the source explicitly starts with
arbitrary algebraically split links in integer homology spheres.

Conversely beta(L)=0 does not imply rank(F)=1. It only means this particular
rank test is inconclusive. The hypothesis P(U)=U is needed if one wants
F to preserve the preferred rational-zero line, not for injectivity itself.

6. PRECISE CONSEQUENCE FOR THE COUNTEREXAMPLE SEARCH

Call an actual preferred quotient tangle invisible when its meridional
closure is U and its longitudinal closure has exact Jones polynomial V_U2.
Section1 converts this into equality of its full skein vector with the
preferred rational-zero vector up to framing. If an invisible quotient is
obtained from another actual quotient by a compatible shell with beta !=0
and P(U)=U, injectivity transfers invisibility back to the input quotient.
For the backward normalized-Jones statement, its two closure diagrams again
have the same writhe after compatible orientations, as in section1.

Consequently a finite stack of such inherited satellite layers cannot be
the first source of invisible quotient behavior. Removing every specified
layer must leave an invisible core, and that core must have a2=0. In
particular no finite tower of the standard untwisted Whitehead patterns
over a trefoil core supplies the target. This is a genuine family exclusion
despite the Alexander polynomial of every outer Whitehead double being one.

The connection to ordinary Jones is restricted but exact. If a Jones-one
knot K has an actual unknotting crossing, remove that crossing ball. The
other crossing filling is U, and the oriented smoothing has V_U2 directly
from the Jones skein relation. The fixed outside tangle therefore satisfies
section1 and is invisible. Its covering exterior cannot have any of the
excluded inherited Whitehead-tower structures over a nonzero-a2 core.

No argument here assigns such an unknotting crossing to a general Jones-one
knot. Nor does it classify all possible strong inversions of a given cover
knot or all zero-beta shells. Those are genuine remaining geometric gaps.
