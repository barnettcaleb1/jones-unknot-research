WHITEHEAD QUOTIENT PATTERN: INDEPENDENT GEOMETRIC AUDIT
2026-10-31. Research deduction, internally checked; no novelty claim.

Scope. This concerns the exterior quotient of a strongly invertible knot,
with its meridian and preferred longitude marked. It is NOT the familiar
Whitehead clasp tangle whose double cover has companion K#K^r.

1. ACTUAL GEOMETRIC MAP

Write W=E(P union A), the standard Whitehead link exterior; A is the
unknotted axis and P is the Whitehead pattern component. The usual
component-preserving strong inversion strongly inverts BOTH components.
Its quotient is S^3 minus two balls, with four marked branch endpoints
on each boundary sphere. Gluing an actual four-ended tangle into one
boundary therefore defines an R-linear map F on the rank-two relative
Kauffman bracket skein module, R=Z[A,A^-1]. We may extend scalars to Q(A).

There are two inherited inversions of a Whitehead double, according to
which fixed point of the companion carries the clasp. Each is realized
by this construction, with the corresponding boundary marking/direction.
See Dai--Mallick--Taniguchi, arXiv:2606.05819, Example 2.22 and Figure 4.
The negative clasp follows by mirroring the pattern. The argument below
applies separately to all these choices; it does not classify unrelated
strong inversions of a Whitehead double.

2. SLOPES AND FRAMING

On the A boundary use meridian mu_A and preferred longitude lambda_A.
For V=S^3 minus N(A), its meridian is lambda_A and longitude is mu_A.
Satellite gluing identifies mu_K with lambda_A and lambda_K with mu_A.
The exterior of an unknot has contractible slope lambda_U. Its rational
quotient Q_0 therefore fills A along mu_A. More generally let Q_n be the
rational tangle whose covering solid torus has contractible slope
lambda_U+n mu_U. It fills A along mu_A+n lambda_A, namely 1/n surgery.
Since A is an unknot, this ambient manifold is S^3 for every integer n.
The image of P is the n-twisted Whitehead pattern on the unknot, a twist
knot. In a standard sign convention its Alexander polynomial is
  Delta_n(t)=-n(t+t^-1)+(1+2n),  a_2=-n.
Changing conventions changes the sign of n, never its nonvanishing.

Crucially, lk(P,A)=0. Thus lambda_P is already zero in H_1(W), and remains
the preferred longitude after either these fillings or a satellite
gluing of E(K). Equivalently the surgery-induced framing correction is
n*lk(P,A)^2=0. The output meridian is also fixed. These markings are what
allow comparison of the SAME output zero closure for all n.

arXiv:0706.2213v3, Remark 2(4)--(5): W(1/m)=E(J(2,-2m)), with the
displayed Alexander polynomial above.

3. WHY F HAS RANK TWO (NO QUOTIENT PD NEEDED)

For n=0 the pattern knot P_0(U) is an unknot. Its exterior quotient is a
rational tangle, with the marked longitude zero and meridian infinity.
Thus F(Q_0) is a nonzero framing monomial times the output Q_0.

Suppose rank(F)=1 over Q(A). Every F(Q_n) would be proportional to
F(Q_0). Each output is an ACTUAL knot exterior quotient with meridian
closure U. Applying that framed closure functional shows the scalar of
proportionality is a framing monomial, not an arbitrary rational function:
both framed unknot brackets are powers of -A^3. Consequently its zero
closure has bracket a monomial times delta, delta=-A^2-A^-2.

The independently audited Garoufalidis/Casson lemma says that any actual
exterior quotient with U meridian closure and zero-closure Jones a
monomial times the two-component unlink polynomial has a_2(companion)=0.
(See ../fanout16/realizability_zero_filling.txt.) Applied to n=1, this
contradicts a_2(P_1(U))=+/-1. Therefore rank(F)=2: F is injective over
Q(A), and hence on the integral skein module as well.

4. PRECISE CONSEQUENCE

For any strongly invertible companion K and the chosen inherited
Whitehead inversion, its exterior quotient is projectively skein-equal
to the preferred rational Q_0 if and only if K's exterior quotient is.
Indeed F(T_K) proportional to F(Q_0), together with injectivity, is
equivalent to T_K proportional to Q_0. The proportionality is fixed to
a framing monomial by the actual meridian-unknot closures.

In particular, a companion with a_2(K) nonzero cannot acquire this
property under untwisted Whitehead doubling, despite the double itself
having Alexander polynomial 1. This excludes the trefoil Whitehead
seed, both inherited clasp placements and either clasp sign, from the
previous formal Jones-flat tangle realization target. Repeating the
argument also excludes its iterated inherited Whitehead doubles.

5. ORIENTATION LEMMA: EXACT ZERO JONES EQUALITY IS ENOUGH

Let T be an actual two-string tangle whose standard infinity closure
is U, and whose standard zero closure L has EXACT normalized Jones
polynomial V_L=V_U2. Then [T] is a framing monomial times Q_0.

Proof. L has two components (also follows from V_L(1)=-2). The first
derivative formula V'_L(1)=-3 lk(L) follows directly by differentiating
the Jones skein relation: a mixed crossing change changes V' by -3 and
lk by +1, while a self-crossing change does not change V'; normalize on
the unlink. Thus lk(L)=0. Reversing one component multiplies Jones by
t^(-3 lk), so does not alter V_L. Orient U and then orient the two
components of L so the directions on both tangle arcs agree with U.
The standard zero and infinity closure arcs have no crossings. Hence
both closure diagrams have identical writhe w. Their framed brackets
are therefore (-A^3)^w delta and (-A^3)^w, respectively. The two closure
functionals have matrix [[delta,1],[1,delta]] on (Q_0,Q_infinity), whose
determinant delta^2-1 is nonzero. Thus [T]=(-A^3)^w[Q_0], as claimed.

This proves the Whitehead exclusion above also rules out EXACT
zero-filling Jones equality to U2, not merely a prescribed framed
vector. Conversely, a projective Q_0 vector with an actual meridian
unknot closure has exact V_U2 as its zero-closure Jones polynomial in
the compatible orientation: the common writhe is fixed by the meridian
closure. Its linking number is then zero, so the orientation choice
does not affect this conclusion. Therefore inherited untwisted
Whitehead doubling preserves this exact zero-Jones-unlink property in
BOTH directions.

The orientation lemma was independently checked by the homology agent
on 2026-10-31: no gap found for EXACT equality. For monomial-only
normalized equality, do not use this converse
without another orientation argument; the rank-two proof itself needs
only the separately established monomial version of the Casson lemma.

Orientation-reversal primary source: Lickorish--Millett, Some evaluations
of link polynomials, Comment. Math. Helv. 61 (1986), Jones reversing
result in Section 4.
https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~millett/Papers/1986Millett6LickorishCommMathHelv.pdf

7. STRONGER NORMAL FORM AND DEFECT-RECOVERY LAW

Root's additional structural deduction was independently audited here.
Let f_infinity and f_0 be the standard bracket closure functionals, with
f_infinity(Q_0)=1, f_infinity(Q_infinity)=delta. Meridionally filling the
P boundary of W restores E(A). Its compressible slope is lambda_A, which
is identified with the input mu_K. Therefore its quotient is exactly
the input rational infinity cap. It follows geometrically, for ALL
skein inputs, that
  f_infinity F = c f_infinity,
where c is one fixed framing monomial, independent of the input. There
are no extra closed branch components: this is the exterior quotient
of the strongly inverted unknot A, hence a rational two-string tangle.

Since F(Q_0) is proportional to the preferred output Q_0, applying this
functional shows that its scalar is the same c. Replace F by c^-1 F.
Then
  f_infinity F=f_infinity,   F(Q_0)=Q_0.
The vector v=Q_infinity-delta Q_0 spans ker(f_infinity), which is
F-invariant. Consequently F(v)=d v for a nonzero Laurent polynomial
d in Z[A,A^-1]. Nonvanishing is the rank-two theorem above. Integrality
follows because c is a unit and the change of basis (Q_0,v) is unimodular.
Equivalently the normalized matrix on (Q_0,Q_infinity) is
  [[1, delta(1-d)], [0,d]].

For any T with f_infinity(T) nonzero define its normalized defect
  x(T) = (f_0(T)/f_infinity(T)-delta)/(1-delta^2).
Writing T=f_infinity(T)Q_0+bv shows x(T)=b/f_infinity(T), hence
  x(F(T))=d x(T).
This applies automatically to an actual unknot-meridian exterior
quotient, whose framed f_infinity is a nonzero monomial. Thus Whitehead
doubling scales, but cannot erase, the non-rational Jones channel.
Recovery by division by d is over Q(A); d has not been shown to be a
unit. No explicit polynomial d was computed in this pass.
