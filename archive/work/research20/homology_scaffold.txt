GENERIC KHOVANOV SCAFFOLD: BOUNDED CANCELLATION, THEN CONNECTED-SUM AMPLIFICATION
Status: complete CONDITIONAL inference chains, not a proof of Jones detection.
No novelty claim. The sole proposed global geometric bound is unproved.

1. CONVENTIONS AND THE SIMPLEST COMPLETE CHAIN

Use normalized V_U(t)=1 and reduced even Khovanov homology over Q, with
Kh_red(U)=Q in bidegree (i,j)=(0,0). Its Euler polynomial is V_K(q^2).
Write r(K)=dim_Q Kh_red(K).

Proposed bridge R: for every realized Jones polynomial P, there is a finite
number B(P) such that r(K)<=B(P) for ALL classical knots with V_K=P.
No effective formula for B is required.

For based connected sums the normalized reduced chain complexes tensor over
Q. This follows directly from the state complexes: the two basepoint circles
are merged, their X-labelled reduced factors are replaced by one X-labelled
factor, and the reduced quantum normalization exactly removes the resulting
shift. The cube differential is the tensor differential. The field Kunneth
theorem therefore gives a bigraded isomorphism

  Kh_red(K#L;Q) = Kh_red(K;Q) tensor_Q Kh_red(L;Q).

Consequently r(#^m K)=r(K)^m, with no grading shift. Jones multiplicativity
gives V_(#^m K)=1 whenever V_K=1. Bridge R then implies

  r(K)^m <= B(1) for every m; hence r(K)=1; hence K=U.

The final implication is Kronheimer--Mrowka's theorem. Their Proposition1.2
uses reduced Kh of the mirror as the source of a spectral sequence; this
does not affect rank. Corollary1.3 and Proposition1.4 identify the relevant
rational rank lower bound with KHI. KHI has rank>1 for a nontrivial knot.
Thus rational rank1 is enough; no unsupported conversion from rational
rank1 to integral torsion-freeness is being used.

Primary source: Kronheimer--Mrowka, Khovanov homology is an unknot-detector,
https://people.math.harvard.edu/~kronheim/khovanov-unknot.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10240-010-0030-y

2. ALTERNATIVE BRIDGE: BOUNDED QUANTUM DIAMETER

Let h(K)=max{j:Kh_red^{i,j}!=0}-min{j:Kh_red^{i,j}!=0}.
Let w(K)=diam_q V_K(q^2)=2 span_t V_K(t).

Proposed bridge W: h(K)<=F(w(K)) for a finite-valued function F.
A weaker sufficient global formulation is: h is bounded on every exact
Jones-polynomial fiber, with bound H(P). An affine C*w+B bound suffices;
there is no need to prove F(0)=0 separately.

The same Kunneth isomorphism gives h(#^m K)=m h(K): the extreme tensor
classes are nonzero over Q. If V_K=1, m h(K)<=F(0) for all m, so h(K)=0.

RIGOROUS ENDPOINT h(K)=0 => r(K)=1:
Let the sole reduced quantum grade be j0. For the based Frobenius complex
with X of quantum degree -2, the standard reduced/unreduced sequence is

  0 -> C_red{-1} -> C_unred -> C_red{+1} -> 0.

Its connecting maps compare reduced groups separated by quantum degree2.
They vanish when h=0. Unreduced Kh is supported only in j0-1,j0+1, and its
total rank is 2r(K). The rational unreduced Lee spectral sequence has
differentials of bidegree (1,4s), s>=1, and rank2 limiting homology for a
knot. Its differentials must vanish on these two quantum levels, which are
only2 apart. Thus 2r(K)=2. Apply KM as above.

This avoids an unverified convention for a 'reduced Lee differential'.
Primary Lee source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0210213
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2004.10.015
The (1,4s) spectral-sequence grading is also in Rasmussen,
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0402131 .

The exact sequence also gives, for every knot,
  maxq Kh_unred = maxq Kh_red +1,
  minq Kh_unred = minq Kh_red -1.
At either extreme the other shifted reduced term vanishes. Hence the
unreduced quantum diameter is h(K)+2; no global splitting is asserted.

3. WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN GAINED

Both chains are genuinely generic: neither needs a reduction to unknotting
number1, strong inversion, a special tangle, or a Brunnian-braid family.
Both require only finite boundedness, not coefficientwise positivity and
not recovery of the full homology from Jones.

However, boundedness on the UNIT fiber alone is equivalent to Jones unknot
detection by these arguments. It is not an independently established
weakening. The broader all-fiber or all-span assertions are stronger,
independently testable geometric hypotheses. They presently have no proof
in this investigation. The complete chain isolates a bottleneck; it does
not solve it or establish its plausibility by itself.

Rank control and quantum-diameter control measure different failures of
Euler cancellation. Formally neither implies the other: large multiplicity
can sit in bounded grades, and a few generators can be arbitrarily far
apart. The rank version is the shorter route; the width version gives a
more specific geometric target for controlling the location of invisible
classes.

4. ACTUAL-KNOT KILL TESTS

(a) Same Jones does NOT determine Kh, and extreme cancellation is real.
Bar-Natan's original tables give a common Jones polynomial for 5_1 and
10_132 (in the chosen chirality):
  -t^-7+t^-6-t^-5+t^-4+t^-2.
Its q-diameter is10. Unreduced Kh for 5_1 has q-range[-15,-3], and that
for 10_132 has q-range[-15,-1]. Thus h(5_1)=10, h(10_132)=12. In particular
the tempting sharp bound h<=w is FALSE, even though a finite F is not
refuted. The unreduced ranks are6 and12, respectively.
Primary source, printed p16 / PDF p15, examples21--23:
https://www.math.toronto.edu/~drorbn/papers/Categorification/Categorification.pdf

(b) Standard infinite fixed-Jones families do not immediately refute either
boundedness bridge. Watson Theorem5.1 gives identical full bigraded Kh in
his simple-tangle twist families. Lobb Theorems1.2 and3.2 give identical
reduced/unreduced Khovanov--Rozansky homology in the corresponding fixed-sum,
fixed-parity Kanenobu/generalized ribbon families.
https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~liam/files/identical.pdf
https://maths.dur.ac.uk/users/andrew.lobb/kbu.pdf

(c) Moore--Starkston's genus-two mutation families illustrate an important
false test. Their Theorem12 (n>=8) has unreduced rank26, minimal q=-1 and
maximal q=2n+11. Both extreme coefficients survive in the Jones polynomial.
Thus h=2n+10 and w=2n+10. The Jones polynomials agree within each mutant
PAIR, not across all n. These examples have unbounded grading spread at
fixed Kh rank and bounded Jones coefficient l1-norm, but NOT fixed Jones
span. They kill a diameter bound based only on the coefficient l1-norm;
they do not kill W or R.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1204.2524 , Theorem12 and Corollary13.

(d) Concrete early kill test for the scaffold: Watson's Section7.3 asks
about replacing the simple tangles in his Jones-preserving twist operation
by NON-SIMPLE tangles. Iteration still preserves Jones, but the proven
full-homology constancy theorem no longer applies. Establishing unbounded
rational reduced Kh rank or quantum diameter along one such exact-Jones
family would refute the corresponding global bridge away from the unit
fiber. This is a sharper next test than enumerating unrelated small knots.

A targeted primary-source search during this pass found no established
fixed-Jones family with unbounded rational Kh rank or quantum diameter.
This reports the bounded search only; it is not a literature-wide claim
that no such family is known.

5. WHY THE PREVIOUS FORMAL TANGLE MODEL DOES NOT PROVE THE BRIDGE

The earlier formal D model (rational arc plus four shifted special curves)
has an Euler-one closure of rank17 and positive quantum width. The tested
integral type-D/A-infinity extensions and unreduced Lee local algebra do
not prevent its invisible summands. No classical tangle realizing D has
been constructed. Any proof of R or W must exploit actual geometric
realizability beyond those objectwise algebraic constraints. Merely
renaming the Euler characteristic a positive index does not supply it.

6. STRATEGIC VERDICT

This is a complete conditional route to the full theorem, with a single
clearly stated unproved geometric input. Prefer the rank version for the
shortest logical scaffold; retain the width version as a more concrete
support-control target. The next meaningful move is to prove boundedness
in a structurally broad Jones-preserving operation or to find a single
fixed-polynomial family with unbounded homology. More finite tables cannot
establish the required bound.
