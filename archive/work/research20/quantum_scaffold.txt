QUANTUM NORM ROUTE: AN END-TO-END CONDITIONAL SCAFFOLD
1 November 2026. Strategic reset, not a claimed solution or novelty claim.

ASSESSMENT

A genuine positive-norm identity has been established for one explicit
geometric family. It gives a credible narrow research target. It does
not currently give a route from an arbitrary Jones-one knot into that
family. A full proof would require two independent new ingredients:
(1) a nontriviality-preserving geometric reduction into the family; and
(2) a geometric classification of a particular quantum-vector stabilizer.
Neither follows from unitarity, braid-representation faithfulness, or the
classification of adjacent knots by spatial Suzuki graphs.

A. THE VERIFIED ALGEBRAIC SCOPE

Fix n>=3. Let beta be an actual Brunnian pure n-braid: deleting any strand
gives the identity braid. Replace every strand by its untwisted parallel
pair, and use the particular alternating-clasp closure in Stoimenow's
Eq(10) construction. Call the resulting knot K_beta. The statement below
is for that explicitly drawn construction, not for every Brunnian link,
every spatial Suzuki graph, or every n-adjacent knot.

Put delta=-A^2-A^-2. Let H be the generic Temperley--Lieb vacuum space on
2n+2 fundamental endpoints, in the order

   outer left, [first doubled strand], ...,
               [nth doubled strand], outer right.

It has the noncrossing-matching basis of size Catalan(n+1). Its Hermitian
form at |A|=1 is reflection followed by closure, with each closed circle
worth delta (without deleting a final circle). Let v be the adjacent-pair
matching (0,1),(2,3),...,(2n,2n+1). Let U=rho(beta-doubled) act on the
2n internal endpoints and fix the two outer endpoints. Put

   p_i=1-e_(2i-1)/delta,    P=product_(i=1)^n p_i,    w=P v,

where the index labels the adjacent endpoints of the ith internal pair.
These mutually commuting p_i are the color-three Jones--Wenzl projectors.
On a sufficiently short unit-circle arc about A=i, delta is near 2, the
finite Gram form is positive definite, p_i are orthogonal projections,
and U is unitary. The arc may omit A=i itself and other finitely many
scalar zeros. Positivity follows from the faithful positive TL form at
delta=2 and continuity of this fixed finite Gram matrix.

The actual reflected two-crossing clasp block has the exact skein form

   s_local = (1-A^4)*through-connection + A^-2*inner-cup.

Projecting its inner pair kills the cup term. Composing n such blocks
therefore gives the exact local-to-global identity

   P s = (1-A^4)^n P v,

where s is the reflected upper boundary state of the clasp closure.
This is an identity over Q(A), not a numerical observation.

Because beta is Brunnian, any color-one internal channel erases one
strand and leaves the identity braid. Naturality of the cable projectors
and purity give

   U-I = P(U-I)P.

In this closure the internal doubled braid has oriented writhe zero;
each original crossing contributes two signs of each kind. The clasp
has writhe -2n in the convention used by the explicit source diagram.
Its closure with beta=identity is an actual unknot. Combining these
facts gives the exact normalized identity

   V_(K_beta)(A)-1 = C_n(A) <w,(U-I)w>,
   C_n(A) = A^(2n)(A^4-1)^n/delta.                       (1)

Here V is the ordinary Jones polynomial with t=A^-4. On the positive
unitary arc, C_n is nonzero and

   Re((V_(K_beta)-1)/C_n)
                       = -1/2 ||(U-I)w||^2.             (2)

Thus an IDENTICAL Jones-one polynomial forces U(A)w(A)=w(A) throughout
that arc, hence as a generic rational-function vector identity. Conversely
that vector identity makes V_(K_beta)=1 by (1). Only this particular
vector is forced to be fixed. No conclusion U=I has been obtained.

Exact checks independently performed:
- The local projector identity was expanded in the two-matching skein
  space; it has coefficients (1-A^4), A^-2 as displayed.
- Direct full-space checks at n=1,2,3,4 agree with the general local proof.
- For beta=[sigma1^2,sigma2^2], the complete identity (1) was verified by
  integer Laurent arithmetic and its Jones polynomial exactly matches
  the independently computed source PD polynomial. The verifier clears
  denominators: with P_scaled=delta^n P, it checks

 delta^(2n+1)(V-1)
   = A^(2n)(A^4-1)^n
       <P_scaled v,(U-I)P_scaled v>.

These checks support the conventions; the general identity uses the
local clasp expansion and strand-deletion argument, not extrapolation.

B. WHAT THE STABILIZER TARGET MUST ACTUALLY SAY

Sufficient geometric stabilizer lemma S:

 For every Brunnian pure braid beta in the chosen construction,
 rho(beta-doubled)w=w over Q(A) implies K_beta is the unknot.

This is weaker and more appropriate than demanding beta=identity. A
nonidentity braid may conceivably preserve the quantum vector through a
geometrically harmless closure move. The proof needs only the knot-type
conclusion. No nontrivial Brunnian stabilizer giving a nontrivial closure
has been found, and no stabilizer classification has been proved.

Faithfulness of rho alone is insufficient: a faithful representation can
have large stabilizers of nonzero vectors. Also w is not a maximally
entangled vector recording the entire operator on all color-three
states. At n=3, the projected space is four-dimensional: one spin-zero
multiplicity plus the three-dimensional spin-one multiplicity sector.
The state chooses one vector in the latter, not three independent test
vectors. The two outer fundamental endpoints are essential. Omitting
them leaves only the one-dimensional invariant in spin1 tensor^3 and
would incorrectly make every commutator invisible.

By the already verified equivalence (1)--(2), lemma S is exactly Jones
unknot detection restricted to this geometric family, rewritten as a
stabilizer problem. It is a useful change of mathematical tools, not an
independently easier assertion that has already been justified. It is
plausible as a narrow target; its computational/representation-theoretic
tractability is still unestablished.

C. THE MINIMAL CONDITIONAL FULL PROOF

Generic reduction hypothesis G:

 Given an arbitrary knot K with V_K=1, there is a finite geometric
 construction producing a Brunnian pure braid beta in the above family
 such that V_(K_beta)=1 and K nontrivial implies K_beta nontrivial.

If G and S both hold, the full Jones detection theorem follows:

 1. Assume V_K=1 and K is nontrivial.
 2. G produces nontrivial K_beta with V_(K_beta)=1.
 3. The proven norm identity gives rho(beta-doubled)w=w.
 4. S makes K_beta an unknot, contradiction.
 5. Therefore K was the unknot.

This chain genuinely closes, but G is currently an unbuilt geometric
operation. Merely declaring G is not a research accomplishment. Without
a concrete operation and a skein identity proving its unit-fiber behavior,
the norm lemma has not reduced the full conjecture to one manageable
remaining statement.

A more informative factorization of G displays the present gaps:

 G1. Convert a hypothetical arbitrary Jones-one nontrivial knot into an
     actual Jones-one nontrivial knot of unknotting number one, while
     using only ordinary Jones information.

 Established step. Such a u=1 example gives a nontrivial preferred-flat
 strongly inverted core. Brunnian infection then gives, for every fixed
 n>=2, a nontrivial Jones-one n-adjacent knot. This is a conditional
 construction already verified in cancellation19/adjacency_amplification.

 G2. Convert one of these actual adjacent counterexamples into the
     specific doubled-Brunnian-pure-braid/clasp family, preserving both
     Jones-one and nontriviality.

Natural candidates for G1 have already exposed a concrete obstruction:
 the Whitehead-double and canonical strongly inverted doubling routes
 introduce color-three Jones data. Their required flatness is not known
 to follow from ordinary V_K=1. Connected sum preserves Jones-one, but
 does not supply unknotting number one. These examples are reasons to
 demand an explicit skein identity for any proposed new G1 operation.

 Neither G1 nor G2 is known here. The Askitas--Kalfagianni spatial Suzuki
 graph classification, as reproved by Lidman--Moore Theorem1.4, does NOT
 state that its arbitrary spatial graph can be replaced by a pure braid
 with this closure. The elementary distinction is that an arbitrary
 string link or spatial graph is not automatically a braid relative to
 its marked boundary. A braid-coverage theorem needs an actual geometric
 proof; one must not infer it from a diagram containing braided portions.

Primary locator for the exact graph classification:
https://arxiv.org/html/2308.06211#S1
Stoimenow's particular braided family is Eq(10), not the entire graph
classification:
https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/stoim2.pdf

D. WHY A DIRECT REFLECTION TRICK DOES NOT FILL G1 OR G2

A one-cut knot tangle is a morphism V->V for the simple fundamental object.
Its quantum image is scalar. Taking its reflection norm can therefore
only repackage the already known scalar V_K; it does not expose missing
geometric information. Claiming this scalar functor detects the trivial
one-strand tangle is the original Jones problem in another form.

Cutting more strands creates a genuinely higher-dimensional state, but
ordinary closure does not determine it. For a 2m-strand plat with unit
vacuum psi, the normalized Jones value is a phase times

       delta^(m-1) <psi,U psi>.

Thus V_K=1 imposes amplitude magnitude delta^(1-m), not the maximal
magnitude1 when m>1. Cauchy--Schwarz equality cannot be invoked. Reflection
and polarization produce additional closed diagrams (usually colored or
satellite information); their values are not supplied by V_K alone.

The explicit all-color Jones-collision pair already certified in proof12
has equal ordinary Jones but different every higher colored Jones. This
rules out a uniform reconstruction of those richer states from the full
ordinary Jones polynomial for all knots. It does NOT refute a special
rigidity statement only on the unknown V=1 fiber. Such a statement would
be a new theorem requiring a geometric argument, rather than an algebraic
consequence of cutting, doubling, or reflection positivity.

There is a fundamental difference between (2) and a proposed generic
norm formula: in (2), actual Brunnian deletion removes every unwanted
fusion channel, and an actual local clasp identity aligns the two
remaining boundary vectors. An arbitrary knot has neither established
property. The proof cannot transfer by analogy.

E. DECISION FOR FURTHER WORK

If the goal is a full solution, prioritize a concrete candidate for G1
or a direct replacement for G, and test whether its exact skein operation
uses only the V=1 hypothesis. Do not invest heavily in S under the belief
that it alone proves the generic conjecture.

If a publishable restricted theorem is an acceptable goal, S for a fixed
small n is a coherent target: the positivity identity has converted a
scalar cancellation question into a geometric vector-stabilizer question.
A first decisive test is whether the generic stabilizer within the
Brunnian subgroup consists only of closure-trivial elements. A failure
with a nontrivial closure would be an actual Jones counterexample; a
failure consisting of harmless elements only calls for the weaker S
formulation already used above.

The current outcome is an exact family identity and an honest conditional
proof architecture. No full proof, counterexample, generic-coverage
theorem, or stabilizer theorem has been obtained.
