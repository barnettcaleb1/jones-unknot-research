FULL TOPOLOGICAL PROOF SCAFFOLD: GENERIC BRIDGE, THEN RIGIDITY
1 November 2026. Conditional research architecture, not a proof of Jones
unknot detection and not a novelty claim.

MAIN CONCLUSION

There is a logically complete two-gate proof route:

  G: an arbitrary nontrivial Jones-one knot can be converted, preserving
     nontriviality, into a Jones-one knot of unknotting number one;
  R: a specified connected skein coefficient of every actual marked
     strongly inverted Brunnian triple is nonzero when the triple arises
     from a nontrivial 3-adjacent knot.

The intervening geometric constructions and algebra turn G's output into
an object violating R. Neither G nor R has been proved. R has the same
existential strength as Jones detection in the u=1 sector; its advantage
is a concrete coefficient to study, not an automatic simplification of
the problem. Three distinct, explicit designs for G appear below.

If one instead uses the positive quantum norm identity in the companion
quantum_scaffold.txt, there is an ADDITIONAL coverage gate: arbitrary
spatial Brunnian-Suzuki graphs have not been converted into that particular
doubled-pure-braid/clasp family. That gate must not disappear from the
proposed proof.

1. OBJECTS, MARKINGS, AND THE AUDITED DOWNSTREAM CHAIN

Use normalized Jones V_U(t)=1, R=Z[A,A^-1], t=A^-4, and
delta=-A^2-A^-2. Let C mean existence of a nontrivial knot K with V_K=1,
and C1 the same statement with u(K)=1. Plainly C1 implies C; the reverse
implication is the generic bridge still missing.

For a strongly invertible knot J, its exterior quotient is an actual
four-ended tangle T_J. The meridian filling is an ACTUAL unknot and the
preferred longitude specifies the zero filling. For the rational basis
Q0,Qinf let f_inf(Q0)=1, f_inf(Qinf)=delta and put

               v=Qinf-delta Q0,        f_inf(v)=0.

After removing the framing monomial of the meridional unknot, write
               [T_J]=Q0+x(J)v.
The preferred quotient is flat when x(J)=0. This is equality of the full
two-coordinate skein vector, not merely equality of one knot closure.

The prior implications were re-read and their essential dependencies
checked here. Their precise scope is:

  C1 <=> existence of a nontrivial strongly invertible flat core J
     <=> existence of an essential zero-factor two-component shell
     <=> existence of a nontrivial Jones-one n-adjacent knot,
         for each one fixed n>=2.

Here a shell is the componentwise strongly inverted exterior of a
two-component link of unknots with linking number zero; essential means
the link is nonsplit. Its preferred normalized quotient operator is
F(Q0+xv)=Q0+d(A)xv. Zero-factor means d is identically zero.

The first equivalence has two essential geometric steps. Removing an
actual unknotting crossing of a Jones-one knot gives both an actual
unknot filling and, by the oriented skein relation, the unlink Jones
polynomial for its smoothing. The determinant-zero smoothing is the
preferred longitude; the two closure functionals then force flatness.
Conversely, the marked 1/2 filling of a nontrivial flat core has monomial
Jones polynomial, hence Jones one because V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0. Its cover
is nonmeridional 1/2 surgery on nontrivial J, so is not S3 by the knot
complement theorem. Its actual crossing-change filling is U. Thus this
is a nontrivial u=1 knot. An arbitrary distance-two pair of slopes is
not being declared a crossing change without marking data.

The flat-core-to-zero-shell implication uses an ACTUAL strongly inverted
Brunnian triple lifted from a published nontrivial 3-adjacent knot.
Infection of one component by J makes the two-component shell factor
zero by skein multilinearity. Essentiality is proved by performing the
seed's prescribed surgery on another component: the resulting winding-
zero pattern is essential, and its satellite of nontrivial J has an
incompressible companion torus. These are geometric nontriviality checks,
not inferences from a Jones polynomial.

The amplification likewise uses an actual (n+1)-adjacent seed and its
marked +/-1/2 reverse-crossing surgeries. Insert the flat J at one
component. Every proper sublink of the remaining n-component link stays
an actual unlink. The final cover is not S3: the first n-1 prescribed
surgeries leave an essential satellite P(J), and its last +/-1/2 surgery
cannot give S3. The retained crossing neighborhoods prove ordinary
n-adjacency. Multilinearity makes the final Jones polynomial a signed
monomial, and the two knot normalizations make it exactly 1.

These are internally audited deductions using the cited published
inputs, not published new theorems whose priority or external review has
been established. Full marked arguments and independent audits are in
work/cancellation19/brunnian_reduction.txt and adjacency_amplification.txt.

2. A PRECISE SEPARATE RIGIDITY TARGET THAT CLOSES THE PROOF

Choose n=3 in amplification. Let L be the resulting nontrivial
3-adjacent Jones-one knot. Reverse all three distinguished crossings to
the unknot and lift the three reverse arcs into its S3 double cover.
The result B=P union C union D is an actual componentwise strongly
inverted Brunnian triple with inherited slopes +/-1/2. It is nontrivial:
if it were an unlink, full prescribed surgery would give S3 and force
L=U. The proof of Lidman--Moore Theorem 1.4 supplies the actual proper-
unlink property and these exact slopes.

The preferred two-input quotient shell has the exact form

        F_B(Q0+xv,Q0+yv)=Q0+c_B(A)xyv,     c_B in R.

Indeed filling either input with Q0 leaves a split pair, so both terms
linear in only one v vanish. The outer meridional functional is the
product of the two input meridional functionals; therefore the remaining
all-v term lies in Rv. This algebra does not imply c_B is nonzero.

In the inherited crossing marking, the two inputs are Q_(+/-2), with

   x(Q2)= A^2(A^4-1),     x(Q-2)= -A^-6(A^4-1).

The final +/-1/2 cap has nonzero defect coefficient, up to the fixed
framing convention,

   h_+= A^-10(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1),
   h_-= -A^-2(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1).

The normalized closure identity consequently gives

   V_L-1 = (nonzero Laurent unit) h_epsilon c_B
                         x(Q_(2epsilon1)) x(Q_(2epsilon2)).       (R0)

The reference contribution is 1, not an unknown monomial: the same
marked crossing neighborhoods applied with one proper input left at
Q0 give an actual unknot, and their local writhe changes fix the relative
normalization. The prior exact cap computation uses this marking; merely
knowing two unframed links are unknots would not justify (R0).

RIGIDITY LEMMA R (UNPROVED): For every B obtained in this way from an
actual nontrivial 3-adjacent knot, c_B(A) is not identically zero.

Now the complete conditional proof is seven lines:
  1. Assume a nontrivial K with V_K=1.
  2. G supplies a nontrivial u=1 Jones-one knot.
  3. The audited equivalence supplies a nontrivial flat core J.
  4. Amplification at n=3 supplies nontrivial 3-adjacent L with V_L=1.
  5. Lift its marked arcs to the actual triple B just described.
  6. Equation (R0), V_L=1, and the integral-domain property give c_B=0.
  7. R gives c_B!=0, a contradiction. Therefore K was U.

This is a full logical route, but R is not an independent easy invariant
bound: equation (R0) makes it exactly Jones detection on the marked
3-adjacent class, which amplification equates in existential strength
to u=1 detection. A useful attack on R must add a real mechanism, such
as a positive norm plus a geometric stabilizer theorem, not just rename
nonvanishing as essentiality. The computed Stoimenow triple is one
verified nonzero example, not a proof for all B.

3. BRIDGE DESIGN G1: CERTIFIED NON-MUTATION SKEIN MOVES

Here is an exact move test whose polynomial part is already proved.
Take two specified crossings, with signs (+,-), and reverse both. Write
K_(+-), K_(-+) for the two knot diagrams. In the associated two-crossing
square, let L_(0-) and L_(-0) be the oriented smoothing links. Subtract
the two oriented skein relations sharing K_(--):

 V_(K+-)-V_(K-+) = t(t^(1/2)-t^(-1/2))
                                    (V_(L0-)-V_(L-0)).            (G1a)

Consequently equality of the TWO ENTIRE smoothing-link polynomials
certifies that this paired crossing switch preserves the entire Jones
polynomial. It is enough to certify the smoothing links by isotopy, or
by another independently proved skein identity. Matching a few jets,
determinants, or ranks does not certify the move. This operation is not
restricted by definition to Conway mutation; some individual examples
may nevertheless be mutations or isotopies.

MISSING GEOMETRIC REACHABILITY LEMMA G1: For every nontrivial Jones-one
knot, there is a finite sequence of isotopies and paired switches
certified by (G1a), ending at a knot L with both (i) a displayed crossing
whose change gives an actual unknot, and (ii) an independent certificate
that L is nontrivial.

G1 implies G immediately. The polynomial preservation is proved;
reachability and the endpoint's topology are the remaining assertions.
No such complete move library or reachability theorem has been built.
Arriving at U is not a successful endpoint. Nor does assigning a skein
difference to a formal linear combination produce an actual knot.

REJECTED VERSION: use Conway mutations alone. Gordon--Luecke Theorem 7.1
proves that having unknotting number one is mutation invariant. Thus a
finite mutation sequence, even chosen adaptively from the entire diagram,
cannot carry a u>1 knot into u=1. A generic skein-equivalence bridge must
escape this operation class. The claim that EVERY Jones polynomial is
realized by a u=1 knot is much stronger than G and is not being assumed.

4. BRIDGE DESIGN G2: AN ACTUAL TWO-CUT DEFECT COMPILER

The most explicit all-diagram construction target is as follows. For
each knot diagram D of K, build an actual four-ended tangle S_D with
specified boundary marking, a signed framing monomial u_D, and a
nonzero c_D(A) in R, satisfying

         [S_D] = u_D Q0 + c_D(A)(V_K(A^-4)-1)v.                   (G2a)

Require two SEPARATE geometric certificates:
 (i) the marked meridian closure of S_D is an ACTUAL unknot;
 (ii) K nontrivial implies that the double cover of S_D is not a solid
      torus, equivalently its associated core J_D is nontrivial.

Then V_K=1 makes [S_D]=u_D Q0, (i) supplies the required S3 knot exterior,
and (ii) supplies the nontrivial flat core. Thus G2 directly gives G.
The nonzero c_D makes this an informative all-diagram identity; it is
not needed for the implication on the unit fiber itself.

A possible independent certificate for (ii) is a demonstrated epimorphism
pi1(E(J_D)) -> pi1(E(K)), since the unknot's cyclic group cannot map onto
the nonabelian group of a nontrivial knot. Another is an incompressible
companion torus with nontrivial companion. Neither certificate has been
constructed for the proposed compiler. In particular, an abstract group
presentation is not yet an embedded strongly inverted knot exterior.

Why two cuts matter: a construction that leaves K tied into one isolated
strand often retains a local-knot factor and loses the actual-unknot
anchor. Cutting and reconnecting at least two arcs could let the new
geometry interact with K globally. This is a design constraint, not a
theorem that two cuts suffice.

REJECTED FORMAL VERSION: the rank-one skein map T -> f_inf(T)Q0 is easy
to write. Its obvious geometric realization closes T as a local knot
beside a rational tangle; its meridian closure still contains K. Equality
V_K=1 does not turn that closure into an actual unknot. Similarly a
formal affine subtraction V_K-1 is not a permitted tangle operation
until an actual diagram realizes it.

REJECTED COLORED VERSION: the canonical strongly inverted double and
Whitehead constructions have defects governed by color-three Jones
data. Equation (G2a) must involve the ordinary V_K itself. Replacing it
by H3(K)-1 adds the unproved implication V_K=1 => H3(K)=1 and therefore
does not complete G2. Diagram dependence is allowed, but it must be
specified geometrically; it does not erase these requirements.

5. BRIDGE DESIGN G3: MINIMAL-GENUS COUNTEREXAMPLE DESCENT

There is a legitimate minimal-counterexample reduction before any hard
lemma. Jones multiplication gives V_(K1#K2)=V_K1 V_K2. If this product is
1, both factors are Laurent units. V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0 force each factor
to be 1. Seifert genus is additive and every nontrivial knot has positive
genus. Therefore a counterexample of minimum Seifert genus is prime.

MISSING DESCENT LEMMA G3: If a nontrivial Jones-one knot K has u(K)>1,
then a geometric construction supplies either
 (a) a nontrivial Jones-one u=1 knot, or
 (b) a nontrivial Jones-one knot L with g(L)<g(K).

If any counterexample exists, choose one of minimum genus. Alternative
(b) is impossible, so (a) holds; if its unknotting number was already one
there is nothing to do. Thus G3 implies G. For a surface-based proof one
may start with a certified minimal-genus Seifert surface for K and aim
to construct a surface for L of strictly smaller genus, together with
a topological certificate that L is not U. The latter condition cannot
be dropped: crossing changes that simply unknot K are always available
and prove no bridge.

This is a precise descent problem, but no operation satisfying it has
been found. Genus is chosen deliberately: crossing-number additivity is
not available. A proof must not silently take a prime summand after a
diagram simplification and assume it has fewer crossings. Nor may it
infer that skein smoothings remain knots or remain nontrivial.

6. THE QUANTUM NORM OPTION HAS THREE GATES, NOT TWO

The companion quantum_scaffold.txt establishes a norm identity for the
specific Stoimenow doubled-Brunnian-pure-braid/clasp construction K_beta.
On its positive unitary arc it has the form

   Re((V_(K_beta)-1)/C_n) = -1/2 ||(rho(beta_doubled)-I)w||^2.

For that family, Jones one forces rho(beta_doubled)w=w. The precise
additional rigidity lemma needed is that such a fixed vector forces
the ACTUAL CLOSURE K_beta to be U. Faithfulness of the representation
alone does not imply that conclusion about a vector stabilizer.

To use that result after any of G1--G3, a further COVERAGE lemma is
needed: the amplified nontrivial Jones-one adjacent knot must be turned
into a nontrivial Jones-one K_beta in this exact braid family. The
Askitas--Kalfagianni/Lidman--Moore spatial graph classification does not
assert this. An arbitrary spatial graph or string link is not automatically
a pure braid with the same marked closures. The chain is therefore

   generic bridge G + braid coverage + geometric stabilizer rigidity
                    => full Jones unknot detection.

Alternatively, proving the unrestricted marked-coefficient rigidity R
of Section 2 would avoid the braid-coverage lemma. At present neither
version has all its gates proved.

7. QUANTIFIER AND SCOPE TRAPS TO EXCLUDE FROM THE FINAL PROOF

* Amplification says: for every n there exists a possibly different K_n.
  It does not give one fixed knot adjacent for all n. Howards--Luecke's
  theorem applies to a fixed knot and bounds n by 3g(K_n)-1. The outputs'
  increasing genus is compatible with that theorem; it is no contradiction.
* A global bounded-genus assertion for all Jones-one knots is a disguised
  solution: connected sums of any nontrivial counterexample have unbounded
  genus and still Jones one. Finite cardinality of the Jones-one fiber is
  similarly not an independent cheap lemma. A restricted geometric bound
  requires an actual argument and must survive the amplification outputs.
* n-adjacent means any nonempty subset of specified ORDINARY crossings
  unknots the knot; it does not mean n unrelated unknotting crossings,
  n generalized crossing sets, or a braid representation.
* Actual unknot closure, nontriviality, preferred slope marking, and
  equality of the WHOLE Jones polynomial are separate side conditions.
* The equivalences in Section 1 concern existence in the u=1 sector.
  They do not prove G, and a zero-factor shell has not been produced.

RECOMMENDED RESEARCH ORDER

First choose one explicit operation for G2, or one geometric move class
for G1, and demand the two topological certificates alongside its exact
skein identity. This is the largest uncovered arrow. In parallel the
norm/stabilizer route gives a specific way to attack R on a limited
family, but extending that family must remain an explicit coverage task.
G3 is an alternative proof architecture if a genuine genus-reducing move
appears; it should not be advertised as an operation already available.
