ADVERSARIAL AUDIT OF JONES-FIBER BOUNDS
1 November 2026. Primary-source search plus elementary structural audit.

MAIN VERDICT

No actual KNOT family was located in this bounded search that has one
fixed full Jones polynomial and unbounded rational reduced Khovanov rank
or quantum diameter. This is not a proof that such a family is absent,
and it should not be reported as established boundedness. Familiar
infinite equal-Jones examples frequently have identical Khovanov homology,
so simply citing their infinitude does not falsify the proposed bounds.

The proposed factorization through bounded TWIST NUMBER is structurally
unhelpful: that hypothesis ALONE implies Jones unknot detection. The
additional categorical-stability hypothesis is unnecessary for that
conclusion. Restricted to the fiber V=1, bounded twist number is already
equivalent to Jones detection.

1. WHY BOUNDED TWIST NUMBER ALREADY CONTAINS THE WHOLE PROBLEM

Use the usual diagram twist number, minimizing over diagrams. For each
fixed N, knots admitting a diagram with at most N twist regions have a
uniform bridge-number bound B(N).

Elementary proof sufficient here: collapse every bigon chain to one
marked two-strand twist box. There are finitely many planar decorated
connection templates with at most N boxes. Reidemeister-II pairs can first
be removed inside a box. For each fixed template, place its boxes in
standard local height coordinates and put the finitely many remaining
arcs in Morse position. In each box the two strands are monotone; adding
any integer number of twists changes no maxima there. The external arcs
and the finite endpoint/parity choices have a fixed finite number of
maxima. Thus each template has a uniform bridge bound, and the finite
union does also. No sharp linear or square-root bound is needed.

Now suppose every knot in a fixed Jones fiber admits a bounded number of
twist regions. If K is nontrivial and V_K=1, every connected power K#^m is
in that SAME fiber. Schubert's exact theorem gives

       b(K#^m)=m(b(K)-1)+1.

Every nontrivial knot has b(K)>=2, so the right side is unbounded. This
contradicts B(N). Therefore the twist-bound hypothesis by itself proves
Jones detection; a further bound on Kh along template collision lattices
adds nothing to this implication.

Conversely, if Jones detects U, its unit fiber consists only of U and is
trivially twist-bounded. Thus the unit-fiber part of the proposed twist
hypothesis is exactly equivalent to the original conjecture. Requiring
it on every Jones fiber is stronger, not a decomposition into easier
known facts.

Primary proof of bridge additivity:
J. Schultens, Additivity of Bridge Number of Knots,
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0111032
https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~jcs/pubs/addbri15.pdf
Published Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc.135(2003),539--544,
DOI 10.1017/S0305004103006832.

Do not confuse the elementary boundedness argument above with Lackenby's
sharper O(sqrt(twist number)) bound, which has a tangle-prime hypothesis:
https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.5252 , Theorem1.13. That sharper theorem cannot
be applied indiscriminately to connected sums. Our finite-template
argument only needs some finite B(N), and has no primeness assumption.

2. THE FIXED-JONES/TWIST QUESTION HAS PRIOR LITERATURE

Stoimenow, Non-triviality of the Jones polynomial and the crossing
numbers of amphicheiral knots, Section8, Question8.1 explicitly asks
whether there are infinitely many knots with the same Jones polynomial
and arbitrarily large twist number. His Remark8.2 observes that the
Kanenobu examples have bounded twist number. Proposition8.2 gives the
contrasting result for links with at least two components.
Primary author PDF:
https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/progs/ntrivodd.pdf

This establishes that the proposed global twist-fiber bound is a
pre-existing question in substantive form, not a newly justified bridge.
This bounded search has not established its current global status.

The related Silver--Stoimenow--Williams connected-sum Corollary3.6 proves
twist growth when the Jones OR Alexander polynomial is nontrivial. Its
polynomial proof does not apply when both are1. The bridge argument above
avoids that restriction for connected powers; one should not cite their
Corollary3.6 alone for the hypothetical Jones/Alexander-trivial knot.
https://msp.org/agt/2006/6-2/agt-v6-n2-p04-p.pdf

3. ACTUAL EQUAL-JONES KNOT FAMILIES CHECKED

(a) Kanenobu / Watson
Watson's Lemma3.1 and Theorem5.1 exhibit infinite families with identical
bigraded Khovanov homology. These therefore DO NOT disprove either rank
or quantum-diameter bounds in a Jones fiber.
https://msp.org/agt/2007/7-3/agt-v7-n3-p11-s.pdf
DOI 10.2140/agt.2007.7.1389.

The primary distinction is relevant: Watson's remark immediately after
Lemma3.1 states that the Jones invariance needs no simplicity condition
on the two tangles, while his Kh invariance theorem assumes both tangles
are simple. Thus fixed nonsimple T,U with opposite twist powers are a
legitimate sharper test. Failure of the stronger Kh-isomorphism theorem
there would not by itself show unbounded support or rank.

Independent geometry audit of the homology track's proposed extension:
for fixed T,U,beta, the capped resolutions lose all variable twists as
Reidemeister-I curls after beta/beta^{-1} cancellation. Without simplicity,
the capped arc can carry a fixed local knot; it can be localized and moved
along its strand before the cancellation. Hence the exceptional resolution
links remain in a finite fixed list, at worst depending on parity and
orientation choices. The crossing-count comparison reverses both strands
inside the fixed tangles, so their internal writhe is unchanged. This
supports a fixed quantum-support bound via the two exact sequences. The
homology track is auditing the categorical argument; this note does NOT
claim uniform total rank from fixed support alone.

(b) Genus-two mutation
Moore--Starkston construct infinite families distinguished as bigraded
Kh groups from their genus-two mutants, but their theorem explicitly
keeps the total dimensions constant within the families. This is not an
unbounded-rank example. The existence of mutant pairs with different
bigradings likewise does not by itself give unbounded quantum diameter
inside one fixed polynomial fiber.
https://msp.org/agt/2015/15-1/agt-v15-n1-p02-s.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.2524
DOI 10.2140/agt.2015.15.43.

(c) Arbitrarily large finite collections of two-bridge collisions
Kanenobu constructs, for every N, N two-bridge knots sharing a polynomial,
including examples of different genera. The polynomial is allowed to
change with N. This does not give infinitely increasing complexity in
one FIXED fiber. In addition, these knots are alternating, so their
Jones span controls crossing number inside a fixed polynomial fiber.
https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/4334/ojm29_03_16.pdf
Genus and Kauffman polynomial of a 2-bridge knot (1992).

(d) Finite collision pairs / connected powers
Taking powers of two equal-Jones knots gives many collisions at P^m,
not one fixed P unless P=1. Hence the known 5_1/10_132 pair and similar
pairs cannot be promoted into a counterexample to fiber boundedness by
connected sum without already having the unknown Jones-one phenomenon.

(e) Generalized symmetric unions, rotants, and representation kernels
No primary source found in this search supplied an actual knot family
meeting BOTH requirements: one fixed full Jones polynomial AND proven
unbounded rational reduced-Kh rank/quantum diameter. Individual pairs,
finite-order mutations, or merely growing diagrams do not suffice.
This is a search limitation, not a classification theorem for those
operations.

4. LINKS ARE A REAL WARNING, NOT A KNOT COUNTEREXAMPLE

Eliahou--Kauffman--Thistlethwaite give infinite fixed-polynomial fibers
of multicomponent links; Stoimenow's Proposition8.2 records examples
with unbounded twist complexity. Their families can have two-bridge
components with unbounded determinant. Batson--Seed's link-splitting
spectral sequence gives a rank lower bound by the Kh of the split
components, so these are also a route to unbounded Kh rank in link
fibers. A band closure is not automatically Jones-preserving, so this
does NOT supply the required knot family.
https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/ekt.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.6240

The knot hypothesis must remain explicit in any proposed boundedness
statement. No unverified band-to-knot conversion should be used to claim
a falsification, and the earlier EKT/unknot-band obstruction should not
be rerun as if it were new.

5. STRATEGIC CONSEQUENCE

The connected-sum amplification argument is a valid way to show that a
fiber-bound theorem would imply detection. It does not itself establish
or explain that bound. On the unit fiber, rank boundedness (using reduced
Kh multiplicativity and unknot detection) already has exactly the same
existential content as Jones detection; quantum-diameter boundedness has
the same issue once zero diameter is shown to detect U.

A worthwhile new mechanism must therefore prove a genuinely local or
categorical restriction that yields the fiber bound without assuming a
geometric complexity bound that already implies detection. The nonsimple
Watson test can validate such a mechanism on one nontrivial family, but
it cannot supply generic coverage. The present audit removes the proposed
T+C factorization and finds no existing knot-family falsification of the
remaining general Kh-fiber-bound conjecture.
