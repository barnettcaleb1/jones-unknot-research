GLOBAL BRIDGE AUDIT: ASYMPTOTIC CONTROL AND A GEOMETRIC LIFT
Status: conditional full-solution scaffolds. None of the new coverage/lift
assertions is proved. The Watson cap-window lemma remains subject to its
separate geometric audit; the abstract two-triangle argument below does
not depend on accepting that particular realization.

1. WHAT IS GENUINELY WEAKER THAN A UNIFORM FIBER BOUND?

For any fixed knot K, write r(K)=dim_Q reducedKh(K) and h(K)=its quantum
diameter, with reduced U in (0,0). Kunneth gives
  r(#^m K)=r(K)^m,       h(#^m K)=m h(K).

For a Jones-one knot it therefore suffices to prove, along a subsequence
m_k tending to infinity, either
  log r(#^m_k K)=o(m_k),       or       h(#^m_k K)=o(m_k).
A constant bound on the whole Jones-one fiber is unnecessary. These are
weaker quantitative estimates than uniform boundedness, and they need
only concern connected powers of one knot rather than an entire fiber.
But on the unit fiber either condition is still equivalent to detection
once the rank-one/zero-width endpoint is included. Renaming the condition
as 'subexponential growth' does not prove a weaker theorem for free.

2. A PRECISE GEOMETRIC BRIDGE USING PAIRED SKEIN MOVES

Consider an actual oriented two-crossing configuration providing two
normalized skein triangles with a common intermediate link and matching
shifts on their knot terms. Let X and X' be those knot terms. Include in
E the quantum supports of their cap-link terms with the exact shifts in
the triangles. Exactness implies
  Kh^{i,j}(X) ~= Kh^{i,j}(X') outside E
for every i; use the adjacent homological cap groups as well when defining
the full exceptional bidegree set. This is a formal consequence of exact
sequences, not a positivity conjecture.

For a path of such paired-skein moves, the endpoint support is contained
in the starting support together with the UNION of all exceptional cap
supports. Path length does not enter this support bound. The quantum
interval controlling the union must include ABSOLUTE positions: bounding
the diameter of each cap separately is not enough if their centers drift.

NEW, UNPROVED GEOMETRIC LIFT TARGET:
For every K with V_K=1 and a cofinal sequence m_k, construct actual
paired-skein paths from #^m_k K to reference knots R_k such that the union
of (i) the quantum support of R_k and (ii) every shifted cap support on the
path is contained in one interval of length o(m_k).

Then h(#^m_k K)=o(m_k), so h(K)=0. The reduced/unreduced exact sequence
and rational Lee spectral sequence force reduced rank1, and KM gives U.
All implications after the displayed geometric target are established.

This formulation permits arbitrarily complicated templates and arbitrarily
many moves. It is not subject to the fixed-template bridge-number bound.
It also distinguishes the missing task from a bare homology inequality:
one must lift equality in the decategorified skein module to a sequence of
ACTUAL knot diagrams with controlled cap data. A formal skein expansion
is a linear combination of links, and does not provide such a path.

OFF-UNIT TEST / POSSIBLE GLOBAL VERSION:
Ask whether two actual knots of a given Jones polynomial can be joined by
such paths with cap windows controlled by that polynomial, allowing a
specified finite list of reference supports. This is stronger and
independently falsifiable. Neither Jones equality nor Watson's particular
twisting theorem supplies this global lift.

3. AN EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO THE MOST OBVIOUS CONSTRUCTION

The geometric lift cannot be obtained by independently applying a fixed
local move to each connected-sum factor. A cap link produced while only
one factor is changed has the form
  L_cap # K^{#(m-1)},
with connected sum on its based component. The reduced connected-sum
Kunneth formula, valid also when the based first factor is a link, gives

  h(L_cap # K^{#(m-1)}) = h(L_cap)+(m-1)h(K).

Thus if h(K)>0, even ONE such cap already has a linear-size quantum
window. Shifts do not change this diameter. Any successful sublinear lift
must reorganize many connected-sum factors jointly; repeated factorwise
moves cannot work. This is a rigorous restriction on the proposed method,
not evidence that the required global reorganization exists.

4. AN ALTERNATIVE WITH AN ESTABLISHED GEOMETRIC UPPER BOUND

There is a cleaner geometric quantity if one wants to leave quantum
support and saddle maps behind. Define the reduced diagonal spread
  d(K)=max(j/2-i)-min(j/2-i).
It is additive under connected sum. The spanning quasi-tree model gives
the established bound
  d(K)<=g_T(K),
where g_T is Turaev genus. This is a bound on DIAGONAL spread, not the
quantum diameter h above.

Primary sources:
Champanerkar--Kofman--Stoltzfus, Graphs on surfaces and Khovanov homology,
https://www.math.csi.cuny.edu/~ikofman/dkh_agt.pdf
Dasbach--Lowrance, A Turaev surface approach to Khovanov homology,
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/36798 , Theorem1.3.
Their reduced width convention is d+1, hence width<=g_T+1 is exactly the
inequality used here.

NEW, UNPROVED GEOMETRIC TARGET:
  V_K=1 => liminf_{m->infinity} g_T(#^m K)/m=0.

The complete implication chain is
  m d(K)=d(#^m K)<=g_T(#^m K), hence d(K)=0.
If reduced Kh is supported on one diagonal j/2-i=constant, then for each
j there is at most one possible i. Thus there is no Euler cancellation
at any quantum grade. Euler polynomial1 forces total reduced rank1.
KM gives the unknot. Lee theory is not needed for this diagonal version.

This requires only a sublinear Turaev-genus estimate, not bounded crossing
number, bridge number, number of templates, or full Kh rank. It supplies a
concrete established geometric-to-categorical inequality followed by one
missing topological bridge. However that new bridge is unsupported here,
and on the unit fiber its conclusion again implies the full conjecture.
The known general inequality g_T<=crossing number-Jones span does NOT
give it: at Jones span0 it only says g_T<=crossing number.

An unconditional consequence is that a nontrivial Jones-one K would have
d(K)>=1 and therefore g_T(#^m K)>=m. Thus a candidate sublinear diagram
construction would be decisive; a generic linear diagram estimate would
not be enough.

5. STRATEGIC CONCLUSION

Finite fixed templates cannot contain all connected powers of a
nontrivial knot when their bridge numbers are uniformly bounded. Replacing
that failed coverage statement by 'many templates' without tracking their
cap windows merely hides the same gap.

The two defensible replacements are:
  (A) a globally coordinated paired-skein lift with sublinear total cap
      window, using the exact support lemma; or
  (B) sublinear Turaev genus for connected powers, using the established
      diagonal-width bound.
Neither is established. Route(A) has the sharper immediate kill test:
factorwise moves provably fail, so a proposed construction must visibly
couple multiple summands. Route(B) has the cleaner geometric endpoint but
currently no proposed mechanism producing its needed diagrams.

Endpoint and normalization reference:
Kronheimer--Mrowka, Khovanov homology is an unknot-detector,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.4346 . Rational reduced rank1 suffices by their
instanton rank inequality; the mirror in the spectral sequence has the
same rank. Reduced tensor normalization and the zero-quantum-spread Lee
endpoint are detailed in work/research20/homology_scaffold.txt.
