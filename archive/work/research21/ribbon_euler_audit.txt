RIBBON-CONCORDANCE EULER FAITHFULNESS: FULL SCAFFOLD AND BAND TEST
Status: the inference chain is valid; its Euler-faithfulness premise is
unproved. A bounded source search found no counterexample, not a proof
that none is known.

1. PRECISE PROPOSED PREMISE

For every actual ribbon concordance C:K0->K1, the induced map on normalized
reduced Kh over Q is a bigraded split injection. Write its cokernel H_C.
Proposed Euler faithfulness says
  chi_q(H_C)=0 => H_C=0.
Equivalently, V_K0=V_K1 forces this induced map to be an isomorphism.
This is a condition on actual ribbon-concordance maps, not all abstract
bigraded complexes. It allows distinct knots to have isomorphic Kh.

Established split-injection source:
Levine--Zemke, Khovanov homology and ribbon concordances,
https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.12303 . The reduced based variant follows by
carrying a basepoint along the concordance and the same left-inverse
cobordism argument; it is also used explicitly in Wang's reduced results.

2. COMPLETE GENERIC INFERENCE

Given ANY Jones-one K, let R=K#mirror(K), with the conventional orientation
reversal if needed. This is ribbon and V_R=V_K(t)V_K(t^-1)=1. A ribbon
disk punctured near a minimum gives a ribbon concordance U->R. Euler
faithfulness makes reducedKh(R)=Q. Reduced connected-sum Kunneth gives
  rank reducedKh(R)=(rank reducedKh(K))^2,
so reducedKh(K) has rank1. Kronheimer--Mrowka implies K=U.

There is no separate generic-to-unknotting-number-one or special-braid
coverage assumption. Restricting Euler faithfulness merely to U as source
is equivalent to Jones detection via this construction; that restricted
version is not intrinsically easier. The all-source version is a stronger
testable geometric statement, and no evidence here proves it.

3. WANG'S SINGLE-BAND FAMILY IS AN EXACT TEST OF THE PREMISE

Let L=L1 disjoint-union L2 be a split two-component link; K_#=L1#L2.
Let K_b be a band sum along a nontrivial band. Miyazaki supplies a ribbon
concordance K_#->K_b. Wang proves, for the reduced theory over Q,

  Kh_red(K_{b+n}) = Kh_red(K_#) direct-sum h^(2n) q^(4n) H_b,
  H_b != 0.

Here n full twists are added to the band. The reduced Euler polynomial
obeys, in the same q convention,

  V_red(K_{b+n}) = V_red(K_#) + q^(4n) P_b,
  P_b = chi_q(H_b).

PRIMARY AND COEFFICIENT AUDIT:
Joshua Wang, The cosmetic crossing conjecture for split links,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.01070 .
Theorems1.7--1.8 on printed pp6--7 initially state the results for
unreduced F2 homology; Remark1.9 explicitly extends BOTH statements to
reduced F2 and reduced Q. Thus the needed H_b!=0 over Q is justified.
His polynomial V in the introduction is unreduced, V_U=q+q^-1; dividing
by this common factor translates equality to normalized Jones without
changing the question P_b=0.

On printed p5, immediately after the twist formula, Wang explicitly states
that he does not know if P_b is nonzero for every nontrivial band b.
Therefore his theorem is not already a Jones detection theorem for bands.
The published statement of that uncertainty is evidence of the exact
missing bridge, not a proof that the question remains open today.

4. CONSEQUENCES OF AN ACTUAL P_b=0 EXAMPLE

If a nontrivial band with P_b=0 were found, ALL K_{b+n} would have exactly
the same Jones polynomial. Since H_b!=0 and Kh_red(K_#)!=0, their quantum
diameters would grow as 4|n|+O(1): the fixed summand stays put while the
nonzero complement shifts arbitrarily far. That would falsify H-spread
from the global scaffold and Euler faithfulness for this ribbon map.

It would NOT falsify H-rank: Wang's decomposition makes the total reduced
rank independent of n. This distinction is essential.

Conversely, H-spread on exact Jones fibers would imply P_b!=0 for every
nontrivial band by this formula. This supplies a concrete geometric
consequence of that global compactness proposal, independent of the
unit-fiber formulation.

5. LIMITS OF THE BOUNDED CHECK

Searches for same-Jones ribbon concordances and Jones-invisible nontrivial
bands found Wang's explicit uncertainty and the split-injection results,
but no primary-source example with P_b=0. Standard Kanenobu/Watson examples
with identical Jones and Kh do not refute Euler faithfulness; their maps,
if induced by an applicable ribbon concordance, could simply be
isomorphisms. Nor may a diagram resembling a band be assumed to express
a SINGLE band sum of the claimed split components: Wang's theorem would
then force a nonzero homological complement.

The earlier formal Euler-invisible objects show that split injection and
Lee rank alone do not prove this premise. What is needed is a constraint
on cokernels of actual ribbon concordance maps, beyond their abstract
splitting and Euler characteristic. No such new constraint is proved here.

6. APPENDIX: THE TWO GLOBAL BOUNDS WOULD DETECT NUGATORY CROSSINGS

This is a CONDITIONAL consequence, not a proved Jones detection theorem.
Let H-rank assert a finite upper bound on rational reduced Kh rank in
each fixed normalized-Jones knot fiber. Let H-spread assert a finite
upper bound on its quantum diameter in each such fiber. Together they
imply: if a single crossing change preserves the Jones polynomial, that
crossing is nugatory. More generally, two distinct members of the same
oriented band-twist family cannot have equal Jones polynomials unless
the underlying link is split and the band is trivial.

6a. THE EXACT TWIST RECURRENCE

Start with oriented knots K_-, K_+ differing at one crossing. Their
oriented smoothing is a two-component link L. Wang's introduction,
printed p3 and Figure2, identifies K_- with band surgery K_b on L and
K_+ with K_{b+1}. Thus this is the oriented-band twist orbit, not an
arbitrary coherent two-strand braid-twist orbit. Its oriented skein
triple has the SAME smoothing L at every step.

Use Wang's variable and unreduced polynomial J, with J_U=q+q^-1:

  q^-2 J_{n+1} - q^2 J_n = (q^-1-q) J_L,
  J_{n+1} = q^4 J_n + (q-q^3) J_L.

Consequently D_n=J_{n+1}-J_n satisfies D_{n+1}=q^4 D_n for all
integers n. If J_1=J_0, all J_n coincide. More generally, for m>n,

  J_m-J_n = D_n (1+q^4+...+q^(4(m-n-1))).

The Laurent polynomial ring is an integral domain, so equality of any
two distinct members makes D_n=0 and hence the entire orbit constant.
Dividing all knot polynomials by q+q^-1 gives precisely the same
equality criterion for normalized Jones polynomials.

6b. BOUNDED RANK FORCES THE SMOOTHING TO SPLIT

Primary source: Joshua Wang, Split link detection for sl(P) link
homology in characteristic P, Journal of Topology 16 (2023), 806-821,
https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12297 ; arXiv text:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.09110 .

Despite the title's characteristic-P emphasis, Proposition1.7 is
explicitly stated for EVERY field F and every N>=2. It says that
bounded reduced sl(N) homology ranks along ONE band-twist orbit are
equivalent to an embedded sphere separating the two marked components
of L. Here N=2 and F=Q apply. The source's sl(2) convention agrees with
the reduced Kh of a mirror; mirroring does not alter total rank.
Since L has exactly two components, the separating sphere says it is
geometrically split. Remark1.8 strengthens the nonsplit alternative:
ranks diverge in both directions of twisting.

Under H-rank, the constant Jones orbit from 6a has bounded rational
reduced ranks, so Proposition1.7 makes L split.

6c. BOUNDED QUANTUM DIAMETER FORCES THE BAND TO BE TRIVIAL

Now Wang 2006.01070 Theorems1.7-1.8 and Remark1.9 apply in reduced
homology over Q. If b were nontrivial, then

  Kh_red(K_{b+n};Q) = Kh_red(K_#;Q) direct-sum h^(2n)q^(4n) H_b,
  H_b != 0.

The first summand is nonzero and fixed, while the second shifts in
quantum degree by 4n. Quantum diameter therefore grows as
4|n|+O(1). H-spread forbids this in the constant Jones fiber. Hence b
is trivial. Wang's introduction explicitly identifies split smoothing
plus trivial band with a nugatory crossing.

The two hypotheses play different roles. H-rank handles a potentially
nonsplit smoothing; H-spread handles a split smoothing with a
nontrivial band. This argument does not show either hypothesis alone
implies this strengthened crossing-detection statement.

6d. WHAT THIS TEST DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

A genuine non-nugatory classical crossing change preserving Jones
would falsify at least one of H-rank and H-spread. In fact the proof
classifies the possible failure: nonsplit smoothing forces unbounded
ranks in that Jones fiber; split smoothing with nontrivial band forces
unbounded quantum diameter. Such an example need not have Jones one
and would not by itself refute Jones unknot detection.

A bounded primary-source search did not locate a certified example of
such a crossing. This is not a literature-completeness claim. Wang's
explicit uncertainty about P_b on printed p5 concerns the split-band
case and should not be promoted into a claim about today's complete
status. Likewise, the usual cosmetic-crossing conjecture assumes the
two knots are isotopic; the conditional statement here assumes only
equality of their Jones polynomials and is correspondingly stronger.
