RIBBON PROJECTORS: WHAT EULER-FAITHFULNESS WOULD REQUIRE
1 November 2026. Independent architectural audit.

1. THE SPLIT INJECTION IS REAL, BUT IS NOT POSITIVITY

Levine--Zemke, Khovanov homology and ribbon concordances, Theorem1:
a ribbon concordance C:L0->L1 induces a bigrading-preserving injection,
with left inverse induced by the reverse movie (after fixing its sign).
https://arxiv.org/html/1903.01546v2
Bull. Lond. Math. Soc.51 (2019),1099--1103, DOI10.1112/blms.12303.
Use the corrected v2 source. Proposition7 and the proof of Theorem1 give
the sphere/dot relations underlying the left inverse.

Consequently e=i p on Kh(L1), where i=Kh(C), p=Kh(reverse C), is an
idempotent. Its image is Kh(L0). Thus the complement is a genuine bigraded
direct summand. Its graded Euler characteristic is the difference of the
two Jones-type Euler characteristics. This does not make that Euler
characteristic positive or faithful on nonzero summands.

The same based construction gives the reduced statement for knot
concordances; equivalently one can retain unreduced homology throughout
and its extra (q+q^-1) normalization factor. None of the obstructions below
depends on that choice.

Normalization used for the data below: reduced Kh(U)=Q in (h,q)=(0,0).
Writing Q for the quantum-grading variable and t for ordinary Jones,
chi_Q(Kh_red(K))=V_K(Q^2), so t=Q^2. A mirror changes (h,q) to (-h,-q).

2. MOVIE REVERSAL CANNOT BE A POSITIVE DAGGER

Already the local Khovanov Frobenius algebra rules this out. Over Q take
 A=Q[X]/(X^2), eta(1)=1, epsilon(1)=0, epsilon(X)=1.
The birth eta is nonzero, whereas its reversed death satisfies
 epsilon eta=0.
If reversal were the adjoint for a positive-definite Hilbert structure,
this would say ||eta(1)||^2=0, a contradiction. Equivalently the Frobenius
pairing epsilon(ab) has matrix [[0,1],[1,0]], which is indefinite.

Geometrically these are exactly the undotted sphere=0 and dotted sphere=1
relations in Levine--Zemke Proposition7. The ribbon inverse theorem uses
those cancellations; it does not furnish a positive inner product. A
positive structure on a different quantum representation cannot simply
be transported to the Khovanov movie maps without an additional theorem.

There is also an abstract obstruction to positivity of an additive Euler
trace on the entire triangulated category: [X[1]]=-[X]. A trace that is
both nonnegative on every identity and respects this relation must vanish
on every X. Thus nontrivial Euler additivity and such global positivity
are incompatible.

3. EVEN THE MOST FAVORABLE End(H) IDENTIFICATION DOES NOT CLOSE THE GAP

For H=Kh_red(K;Q), the usual mirror duality and connected-sum Kunneth
identification give, as bigraded vector spaces,
 Kh_red(K#mirror K)=H tensor H^*=End(H).
This vector-space statement does not by itself identify the particular
canonical ribbon map with a coevaluation movie. Grant even the favorable
identifications i(1)=I_H and p(f)=supertrace(f). They are algebraically
compatible because superdimension(H)=V_K(1)=1, so p i=1.

Then e(f)=supertrace(f) I_H and the complementary summand is ker(p).
Its graded Euler polynomial is
 V_K(Q^2) V_K(Q^-2)-1.
If V_K=1, that polynomial vanishes. It still need not imply ker(p)=0.

Concrete formal model: let Z have zero ordinary differential with
generators at (h,q)=(0,0),(1,4). Equip it with a Lee differential joining
these two generators. Put H=Q_(0,0) direct-sum Z direct-sum Z[1]. Then
dim H=5, its graded Euler polynomial is1, and its Lee homology is Q.
The supertrace projector above has one-dimensional image and a
24-dimensional complement of graded Euler polynomial0. This model has
duality, a split unit, and the indicated Lee structure. It is NOT asserted
to be a knot homology. It shows why those formal structures alone do not
give Euler-faithfulness.

4. ACTUAL SMALL KNOTS REFUTE A UNIVERSAL POSITIVE DEFECT TRACE

The exact elementary braid computations are
 V_T(t)=t+t^3-t^4,                       T=trefoil,
 V_F(t)=t^2-t+1-t^-1+t^-2,               F=figure-eight.
They are independently encoded by the already-validated TL calculation
in work/attack_search/search.py: closures sigma1^3 and
sigma1 sigma2^-1 sigma1 sigma2^-1, respectively; t=A^-4.

At t=exp(i theta), theta near0,
 V_(T#mirror T)-1 = +6 theta^2 + O(theta^4),
 V_(F#mirror F)-1 = -6 theta^2 + O(theta^4).
Both doubles have canonical ribbon concordances from U. This region is
compatible with the positive finite Temperley--Lieb arc near A=i.
Therefore their Jones defects cannot all be a positive norm or a faithful
positive trace times a universal nonzero scalar of fixed sign. The
complementary projectors are genuine, but their Euler traces change sign.
Taking an absolute value or squaring the defect would evade this sign
test but would not establish faithfulness; the formal zero-Euler model
would still survive.

5. EVEN UNIVERSAL EXTREME-GRADING PARITY PURITY FAILS ON ACTUAL DOUBLES

The previously verified KnotInfo rational data retained in
work/proof10/homology/polys.json, entry name='12*n_25', field
'khovanov_reduced_rational_vector', give for K=12n25:
 qmin=-4, with one generator each at h=-2,-1;
 qmax=10, with one generator at h=6.
Hence End(H_K)=Kh_red(K#mirror K) has
 qmax=14, with one generator each at h=7,8;
 qmin=-14, with one generator each at h=-7,-8.
At each extreme the Euler coefficient is zero. Any ribbon-concordance
image of reduced Kh(U) is supported at (0,0), so these extreme classes
remain in the complement, independently of the particular ribbon map.

The full reduced Euler check in that normalization is
 Q^-2-1+2Q^2-2Q^4+2Q^6-2Q^8+Q^10=V_K(Q^2).
Thus the extra q=-4 pair is an actual cancelling homology pair, not a
grading-normalization artifact. The earlier exact reconstruction script
is work/proof10/homology/composite_counterexample.py; its companion JSON
records the PD code and source-bar polynomial, not the whole reduced
polynomial, so consult polys.json for the raw reduced groups.

Thus a proposed geometric theorem that every such complementary summand
has parity-pure extreme quantum grading is false. This is an actual
symmetric-double example, not a formal-model objection. Its full Jones
polynomial is nontrivial, so it does not refute the special conditional
statement that a complement with ENTIRE Euler polynomial0 must vanish.
The raw data and provenance were audited in the earlier pass; no fresh
large-diagram homology computation is claimed here.

6. THE EXACT MISSING GEOMETRIC STATEMENT

For canonical ribbon concordances C_K:U->K#mirror K, the statement

  chi_q(im(1-e_K))=0  implies  im(1-e_K)=0

would close the original problem: the target would have reduced Kh rank1,
hence be the unknot, and then K would be the unknot. It is also equivalent
in strength to Jones unknot detection when restricted to this canonical
class. Indeed if Jones detects U, chi_q=0 gives
V_K(t)V_K(t^-1)=1; Laurent units plus V_K(1)=1,V_K'(1)=0 force V_K=1,
and detection gives K=U. Conversely a Jones1 counterexample would give a
nonzero zero-Euler complement by the ribbon reduction and Kh detection.

A stronger sufficient mechanism would be a faithful positive trace on
these PARTICULAR complementary summands whose vanishing is forced by
their full Euler polynomial. It must use a new relation specific to
actual knot ribbon geometry: ordinary Euler/supertrace, arbitrary positive
inner products, split injections, and the local cobordism relations do
not supply it. The local dagger obstruction and the actual sign/extreme
countertests eliminate the most immediate formulations.

VERDICT: the ribbon-projector scaffold is logically complete conditional
on a sharply stated Euler-faithfulness lemma, but the proposed categorical
positivity justification does not prove that lemma. No weaker geometric
relation establishing it has been identified in this audit.
