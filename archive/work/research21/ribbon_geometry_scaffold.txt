RIBBON-CONCORDANCE ROUTE: GLOBAL COVERAGE AND THE GEOMETRIC GAP

Status: an exact conditional route to the full Jones conjecture, with no
generic-to-unknotting-number-one assumption. Its central hypothesis is
unproved. This note complements ribbon_euler_audit.txt; it does not claim
a new theorem about Jones detection or a literature-wide absence of
counterexamples to the proposed hypothesis.

1. A PRECISE GLOBAL PREMISE

Work with normalized reduced Khovanov homology over Q, whose graded
Euler characteristic is normalized Jones after the usual variable change.
For an actual ribbon concordance C:K0 -> K1, the induced bigraded map is a
split injection. Let E_C be its bigraded cokernel. Proposed premise EF:

  chi_q(E_C)=0  implies  E_C=0.

Equivalently, equal Jones polynomials at the endpoints of a ribbon
concordance force its Khovanov map to be an isomorphism. This does not
claim that the knots are isotopic or that the concordance is a product.
The bigraded dimensions of E_C depend only on the endpoint homologies,
although its geometric interpretation uses the actual map.

The split-injection input is Levine--Zemke, Khovanov homology and ribbon
concordances, https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.12303. The reduced variant and
the rational coefficient issue are audited in ribbon_euler_audit.txt.

2. COMPLETE IMPLICATION EF => JONES DETECTS THE UNKNOT

Suppose V_K=1. Put R=K#(-K), where -K is the orientation-reversed mirror.
The standard doubled disk makes R a ribbon knot and

  V_R(t)=V_K(t)V_K(t^-1)=1.

Puncturing that disk near one minimum gives an actual ribbon concordance
U -> R. Its cokernel has Euler characteristic V_R-1=0. Under EF, reduced
Kh(R) is the one-dimensional reduced homology of U. Khovanov unknot
detection then gives R=U. Genus additivity gives 2g(K)=g(R)=0, so K=U.
One can instead use reduced connected-sum Kunneth and rank(Kh(R))=
rank(Kh(K))^2, but the genus argument avoids any unnecessary Kunneth
normalization issue.

Every step after EF uses the actual canonical ribbon double of the
original arbitrary knot. There is no assertion that K itself is ribbon,
strongly invertible, a braid of a particular kind, or an unknotting-number-
one knot. No surgery or finite-type approximation is used.

The version of EF restricted to source U is equivalent in strength to
Jones unknot detection: the preceding argument proves one direction;
Jones detection proves the other because a target with Jones 1 is U.
It is therefore a reformulation, not an independently established easier
lemma. The all-source version is a stronger statement with tests outside
the unit Jones fiber.

3. WHY AN ABSTRACT TREE OF BANDS DOES NOT GIVE WANG ITERATION

A ribbon annulus with m births has m saddles. In a band presentation,
start with K0 and m split newborn unknots and attach m fusion bands.
The abstract connection graph is a tree. A ribbon disk similarly starts
from an unlink and attaches a tree of fusion bands.

The graph does not encode how bands pass through the complementary
regions and interact with the other components. After some fusions, the
components needed for the next fusion need not be geometrically split.
Vanishing linking numbers would not supply a separating sphere. Nor is
one entitled to move every birth immediately before its paired saddle:
the band can pass through the birth disk or its ball, so the required
movie interchanges need a geometric proof.

This is a documented limitation, not merely a concern about a picture.
Zemke explicitly distinguishes simultaneous band sums of three or more
split knots from iterated band sums of pairs in the introduction to:
  Knot Floer homology obstructs ribbon concordance,
  Annals of Mathematics 190 (2019), 931--947, printed p934, before Thm1.6,
  https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v190-n3-p05-s.pdf
His all-summand genus theorem uses the ribbon-concordance argument rather
than assuming pairwise iteration.

Consequently the theorem for a single band joining a split two-component
link does not cover a general ribbon movie. Claiming such coverage would
insert a false unrestricted decomposition premise.

4. A SECOND, INDEPENDENT GAP: EULER CANCELLATION BETWEEN STEPS

Even in a special movie which genuinely factors through knot-to-knot
ribbon concordances C_i of the appropriate split-band kind, splitting
would only give, as bigraded vector spaces,

  E_total = direct-sum_i E_i,
  chi_q(E_total) = sum_i chi_q(E_i).

Knowing every nonzero E_i has a nonzero Euler polynomial does not imply
that the sum is nonzero. The individual polynomials have signed
coefficients. A separate geometric no-cancellation theorem is needed,
such as a common extremal quantum grading with controlled homological
parity, or another pointed cone containing all applicable contributions.
No such theorem has been established here. The rank inequalities and
split injections alone supply no such cone.

Wang's actual single-band results are an important exact test, but already
leave an Euler-level issue: for a nontrivial band b on a split pair,
the homological complement H_b is nonzero, while nonvanishing of its
Euler polynomial P_b is not supplied. See the detailed primary-source
audit in ribbon_euler_audit.txt and Wang's printed p5 discussion:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.01070

Thus a proposed proof using Wang needs THREE distinct inputs:
  (a) genuine applicability to the geometric movie;
  (b) Euler visibility of each nonzero band complement;
  (c) prevention of cancellation among different steps.
Input (a) fails for the unrestricted pairwise-reduction formulation;
(b) is already a substantial unanswered band question in Wang's source;
(c) does not follow from (b).

5. WHAT WOULD COUNT AS A REAL ADVANCE ON THIS ROUTE

The correct direct target is a geometric restriction on cokernels of
actual ribbon-concordance maps, rather than on arbitrary bigraded spaces.
For example, a proof that every nonzero such cokernel has an extremal
quantum grading supported in just one homological parity would imply EF.
That is a sufficient statement, not a verified claim; existing formal
Euler-invisible examples explain why an actual geometric constraint is
essential.

The canonical doubled construction does not by itself supply positivity.
Its homology is a tensor product with a grading-reversed dual, but the
Jones polynomial is an alternating trace, not the positive dimension or
ordinary Hilbert series of that tensor product. Passing from a split
injection or tensor square to positivity of its Euler polynomial would
be precisely the missing step.

For now the route is a complete conditional architecture. It avoids the
earlier global coverage gaps, but it has not reduced its central premise
to an established band theorem or proved the Jones conjecture.
