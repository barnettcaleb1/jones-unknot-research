SYMMETRIC RIBBON DOUBLING AND THE QUANTUM NORM BRIDGE
1 November 2026. Architecture audit, not a proposed proof.

1. THE GENERIC TOPOLOGICAL REDUCTION IS VALID

If a nontrivial knot K has V_K=1, then R=K#mirror(K) is a ribbon knot,
V_R=V_K V_mirror(K)=1, and R is nontrivial by unique prime decomposition
(or connected-sum genus additivity). Thus it would suffice to prove Jones
unknot detection for all symmetric connected-sum doubles, or for all
ribbon knots. This removes a coverage gap only if the asserted rigidity
lemma covers that entire class; it does not place R in the specific
doubled-Brunnian pure-braid family already studied.

2. WHAT THE DOUBLE MEASURES

At a generic unitary specialization on a positive Temperley--Lieb arc,
take a 2b-plat presentation. Let psi be the normalized cup vector, U the
unitary braid operator, and delta>1 the loop value. In the standard
unknot-normalized Jones convention,

 V_K = phase * delta^(b-1) <psi,U psi>.

Reflection conjugates the phase, so

 V_(K#mirror(K)) = delta^(2b-2) |<psi,U psi>|^2.

For V_K=1 and b>1 the squared normalized overlap is delta^(2-2b), strictly
between 0 and 1. It is not the equality case of Cauchy--Schwarz. The ribbon
property of the connected sum does not alter that normalization.

In operator language put x=U psi and E=|psi><psi|. The ordinary double
measures <x,E x>, a rank-one projection observable. Reflecting the whole
open tangle and gluing by the identity instead measures <x,x>; that is a
different closure. Confusing these two gluings supplies exactly the
unjustified promotion from one Jones scalar to a full state norm.

3. PRECISE LINEAR-ALGEBRA OBSTRUCTION

At any one positive specialization with b>1, write a=delta^(1-b). Choose
unit orthogonal vectors e1=psi,e2. The unit vectors

 x_theta=a e1+sqrt(1-a^2) exp(i theta) e2

all give the same ordinary overlap a and the same reflected connected-sum
value 1, while ||x_theta-x_0|| is nonzero for theta not divisible by 2pi.
Consequently no state-rigidity implication follows from unitarity,
reflection positivity, fixed state norm, and the ordinary-double scalar.

This is a countermodel to the proposed linear-algebra inference, not an
actual knot counterexample. It does not prove these vectors are realized
by one Laurent-polynomial braid family, nor refute a genuinely new
all-q geometric restriction on states of symmetric ribbon doubles.

A fixed single-channel boundary change S cannot repair the rank defect:
 E becomes S^* E S, whose rank is at most one. It remains a single linear
functional squared. Several independently probed boundary channels can
produce a higher-rank observable, but their values are additional skein
evaluations; no identity supplied here determines them from V_K alone.
Noninvertible boundary operations also require an independent norm or
success-probability calibration before an extremal inference is valid.

Likewise, ||x-x_ref||^2 requires Re<x_ref,x>. The scalar <psi,x> determines
that overlap only when x_ref is proportional to psi. A stabilized unknot
plat has overlap delta^(1-b), so its state is not proportional to psi.
One cannot silently replace the actual unknot reference by the cup state.

4. A FULL CONDITIONAL ROUTE, WITH THE MISSING INPUT EXPOSED

A valid new theorem of either form would close the original problem:

 (R) Every symmetric double K#mirror(K) with Jones1 is the unknot.

or a genuinely stronger structural lemma implying (R), for example an
actual diagrammatic positive decomposition of its normalized Jones defect
into norms whose simultaneous vanishing forces K trivial. Such a
decomposition must exploit the ribbon disk geometry to eliminate the
rank-one kernel; it cannot follow from the reflection gluing above alone.

Statement (R) is equivalent to ordinary Jones unknot detection, via the
unconditional doubling reduction. It is therefore a useful change of
geometric class, not a proved weakening of the main difficulty. The prior
Brunnian projected-vacuum norm identity uses extra deletion identities
that arbitrary symmetric doubles do not have. No replacement for those
identities has been derived here.

5. VERDICT

The ribbon reduction is exact and generic. The proposed automatic
calibrated-norm bridge does not follow: the ordinary symmetric double
retains a rank-one observable and an interior, rather than extremal,
overlap. A successful ribbon-disk argument would need an additional
geometric relation that changes this observable or controls its kernel.
No such relation is established in this pass.
