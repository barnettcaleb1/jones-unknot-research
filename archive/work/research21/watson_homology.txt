WATSON COMPENSATING TWISTS: A FIXED SUPPORT WINDOW
31 October 2026. Derived argument from Watson's diagrams and skein sequences.
No novelty claim; this is not a theorem about every fixed Jones fiber.

SUMMARY
For a fixed Watson template beta,T,U satisfying his connectivity assumptions,
the whole compensating-twist orbit has uniformly bounded Khovanov quantum
support, even when T and U are not simple. The two skein sequences confine
changes to a fixed finite set of bidegrees. They do NOT, by themselves, bound
the multiplicity of homology inside that set. They give a linear rank bound
in the twist parameter. No actual example with unbounded rank has been
established here.

1. EXACT FAMILY AND SOURCE

Fix beta in B3 and two 4-ended tangles T,U with two arc components and no
closed components. Use precisely the wiring of Watson Figure1, and his
horizontal or crossing endpoint connectivities (exclude the vertical
connectivity discussed on printed p4). Assume the resulting diagrams are
knots, as in the source. Set

  K_n = K_beta(T^{sigma^n}, U^{sigma^{-n}}), n in Z.

The sigma action adds a right-hand tangle crossing to T and its inverse to
U; it is not a simultaneous arbitrary twist at unrelated sites. The Jones
polynomial is independent of n without simplicity: Watson's remark before
Lemma3.1's proof cites his earlier Lemma5.2 for exactly this fact.

Primary source throughout:
Liam Watson, Knots with identical Khovanov homology,
Algebr.Geom.Topol.7(2007),1389--1407.
https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~liam/files/identical.pdf
https://msp.org/agt/2007/7-3/agt-v7-n3-p11-s.pdf
Use the author's PDF printed pp4--10, Figures1--3, equations(3)--(8).
Section7.3 asks whether nonsimple tangles can change Kh at all. The present
support argument does not answer that constancy question.

2. WHY THE CAP LINKS RANGE OVER A FINITE FIXED LIST

Compare K_{n+1} and K_n by distinguishing the two extra crossings, as in
Watson's proof. Resolving both in the through direction recovers K_n.
There are four one-cap resolutions in the two orders of smoothing.

Simplicity is used in the source only to identify those cap links with U2.
For a nonsimple tangle, adjoining the vertical cap leaves a fixed properly
embedded long knotted arc, together with the complementary trivial cap.
The allowed connectivities and absence of closed components are important
here. A long knot may be localized inside a small ball along its strand.
Slide that local knot out of the braid-cancellation region. The adjacent
beta and beta^{-1} then cancel just as in the source diagram. Any remaining
integer twist string adjacent to a cap is a sequence of Reidemeister-I
curls, and hence disappears from the normalized link invariant. The other
tangle is capped in the resulting picture as well.

Thus each cap link is built from the two fixed capped long knots by a fixed
connection determined by beta's endpoint permutation. It may distribute
the local knots among components differently in different resolutions; we
do NOT assume the different cap links have identical Kh or are isotopic.
Only their membership in a finite fixed list is needed. Twist parity can
interchange the connection type, but cannot introduce further types.

Orientations also cause no infinite list. A fixed link with c components
has at most2^c orientation choices. Include all choices induced by the
finite connectivity/permutation cases. Normalized Kh is unchanged by the
Reidemeister-I removal of arbitrarily many curls; do not use unnormalized
bracket grading here. The orientation changes in Watson's crossing-count
argument reverse BOTH strands of T or U, so all their internal crossing
signs, including all n added twists, are preserved. Consequently there is
no omitted quantum shift growing with n. The only removed distinguished
crossing gives c=-1 or c=0, as in Watson pp6--10.

This cap-isotopy argument and the absence of an n-dependent crossing-count
correction were independently checked by the braid subagent against the
actual Figure1 diagram. No assumption that a knotted arc is trivial is
made; its fixed local knot remains in the cap link.

3. PRECISE NORMALIZED SKEIN SEQUENCES

Write H^{i,j}(L)=Kh^{i,j}(L;Q), unreduced, in Watson's normalization. Let
A_n and B_n be the two cap links in one of the resolution orders, and let
Y_n be its common intermediate diagram with the induced orientation.
Equations(3),(4) have the form

 H^{i,j+1}(A_n) -> H^{i,j}(K_{n+1}) -> H^{i+1,j+2}(Y_n)
                       -> H^{i+1,j+1}(A_n),
 H^{i,j+1}(B_n) -> H^{i,j}(K_n)     -> H^{i+1,j+2}(Y_n)
                       -> H^{i+1,j+1}(B_n).

Simplicity was only used to replace A_n,B_n by U2. Without it, retain
their actual homology in these exact sequences.

The opposite order, equations(6),(7), instead has the form

 H^{i-1,j-1}(A'_n) -> H^{i-1,j-2}(Y'_n) -> H^{i,j}(K_n)
                       -> H^{i,j-1}(A'_n),
 H^{i-1,j-1}(B'_n) -> H^{i-1,j-2}(Y'_n) -> H^{i,j}(K_{n+1})
                       -> H^{i,j-1}(B'_n).

Watson printed pp9--10 checks the other allowable connectivities and braid
permutations by reordering/rotating these diagrams. The reverse sigma
move interchanges the sequences. This check depends on orientations and
connectivity, not on the cap links being trivial. We include both orders
in the exceptional set below, thereby covering both directions n and all
parity types at once.

4. FIXED-SUPPORT LEMMA AND PROOF

Let L be the finite oriented list of all cap links above. Put
  E = union_{L in L} supp H(L),
  S = E + {(0,-1),(-1,-1),(1,1),(0,1)},
where + denotes the finite Minkowski sum of subsets of Z^2.

If (i,j) is outside S, all four outside terms in either pair of sequences
vanish. Both middle knot groups are isomorphic to the same intermediate
group. Hence
  H^{i,j}(K_n) ~= H^{i,j}(K_{n+1}) for (i,j) outside S.

This is an isomorphism assertion about vector spaces at each bidegree;
no canonical compatible isomorphism through all n is required. Iterating
in either direction gives
  supp H(K_n) subset supp H(K_0) union S   for every n.

Thus the FULL unreduced bigrading support, and in particular its quantum
diameter, has a uniform finite bound depending on beta,T,U. Let [a,b] be
the quantum range of supp H(K_0) union S. Then all unreduced quantum
diameters are <=b-a.

For a knot, the based reduced/unreduced exact sequence has shifts +/-1.
At the maximal quantum grade it gives maxq H=maxq H_red+1, and similarly
minq H=minq H_red-1. Therefore the normalized reduced quantum diameter is
the unreduced diameter minus2. In particular it is uniformly bounded on
this Watson orbit. The same reduced skein sequences can alternatively be
obtained by placing a basepoint away from the two crossings and reducing
the degreewise split short exact sequence of complexes.

5. WHAT CAN BE SAID ABOUT RANK

For every exact triangle of finite-dimensional vector spaces, the ranks
of two terms differ by at most the rank of the third. Applying this twice
to the common intermediate group gives

  |r_red(K_{n+1})-r_red(K_n)|
       <= r_red(A_n)+r_red(B_n) <= C,

for a finite constant C obtained from the finite based cap-link list
(including the alternate resolution order if necessary). Hence
  r_red(K_n) <= r_red(K_0)+C|n|.

There is NO deduction here that the ranks are uniformly bounded. Repeated
addition of Euler-cancelling classes in the finite exceptional grading
set is not excluded by these exact sequences. A new argument controlling
the actual saddle maps or their iteration would be necessary. Finite
grading support does not bound multiplicities.

The parameter n here counts twisting within ONE fixed template. It is not
the number of connected-sum factors. This linear bound cannot be inserted
into the generic connected-sum amplification proof unless a separate
uniform-template realization theorem is supplied.

6. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE GLOBAL SCAFFOLD AND NEXT DECISION

This entire fixed-template Watson family cannot falsify H-spread, even
with nonsimple tangles. It remains a possible test of H-rank, because
growth confined to S is not ruled out. A meaningful next test should use
the finite fixed cap complexes and their saddle maps, not recompute the
full growing crossing diagram for a long list of n.

An attempted reduction to intersection growth of one multicurve under a
single Dehn twist needs a separate wiring theorem: the construction glues
two 4-ended tangles through beta and beta^{-1}, and twists both inputs in
opposite directions. Treating it as an arbitrary one-sided Dehn twist
would discard the exact cancellation visible in the two skein sequences.

Targeted searches for later answers to Watson's nonsimple-tangle question
found the original question and related tangle/mutation papers, but no
primary source establishing unbounded rational rank in this orbit. This
is a bounded search report, not a claim that the question is still open.

No uniform bound across ALL templates has been proved. The constants S,C
depend on the actual capped tangles and can be arbitrarily complicated.
Thus this result supports one broad test family but does not prove either
H-spread or H-rank on an entire Jones-polynomial fiber.
