INDEPENDENT WATSON SUPPORT / RANK AUDIT
1 November 2026. Internal research note; no novelty claim.

Scope: fixed beta,T,U in Watson's actual Figure 1, allowed two-arc
connectivities, with K_n=K_beta(T sigma^n,U sigma^(-n)) a knot. This is not

1. SUPPORT ARGUMENT: PASSES

I independently reread Watson's Figure 1 and equations (3),(4),(6),(7),
including the crossing-count calculations immediately preceding them.
Source: Liam Watson, Knots with identical Khovanov homology,
Algebr. Geom. Topol. 7 (2007), 1389--1407, DOI 10.2140/agt.2007.7.1389.
https://msp.org/agt/2007/7-3/agt-v7-n3-p11-s.pdf
Author pagination pp4--10; nonsimple question p16, Section7.3.

Simplicity turns each capped tangle into an unknotted arc. Without it the
same operation produces a fixed long knotted arc. The latter can be
localized in a ball along its strand and retained while the exterior
beta/beta^-1 cancellation is performed. The other tangle is then capped
as well. Twists next to a cap become RI curls. Thus the one-cap links are
drawn from a fixed finite oriented list (including endpoint parity cases),
not links whose knotting grows with |n|.

The normalized grading correction is the important check. Watson's
calculation reverses BOTH strands of each affected tangle, preserving
every internal crossing sign, regardless of simplicity and regardless of
the inserted twist count. Only the distinguished crossing is removed.
Consequently c=-1 or 0 remains valid. There is no suppressed linear
quantum or homological shift after RI normalization.

With E the union of the cap-link bigraded supports, the exceptional set
 E+{(0,-1),(-1,-1),(1,1),(0,1)}
in watson_homology.txt is correct. Outside it, the two knot groups map
isomorphically to the same intermediate group. Iterating proves a fixed
finite unreduced bigraded support window for all integer n. The ordinary
based reduced/unreduced exact sequence also bounds reduced quantum
support (indeed its extremes differ by +/-1). No actual constancy of Kh
has been proved when the tangles are nonsimple.

2. FIXED CAP COMPLEXES DO NOT ALONE BOUND RANK

There is a precise abstract countermodel, stronger than a dimension-count
warning. It satisfies genuine distinguished mapping-cone triangles, fixed
cap complexes, constant graded Euler characteristic, a fixed bigrading
window, and a Lee-like filtered differential with constant limiting rank.
It is NOT claimed to be realized by knot diagrams or saddle cobordisms.

Use cochain complexes and let [1] be the usual homological translation.
Let Z have zero ordinary differential and two generators a,b with
 (h,q)(a)=(0,0), (h,q)(b)=(1,4).
Give it a Lee differential a->b. This differential has degree (1,4), so
Z is Lee-acyclic. Put W=Z direct-sum Z[1]. Its graded Euler polynomial
vanishes because translation changes the Euler sign, and it is still
Lee-acyclic. Let B be a single reduced-unknot generator, and set
 X_n=B direct-sum W^n,       Y_n=X_n direct-sum Z[1]    (n>=0).

The zero map Z->X_n gives a distinguished triangle
 Z --0--> X_n --> Y_n --> Z[1].
The inclusion of the Z summand in
 X_(n+1)=X_n direct-sum Z direct-sum Z[1]
gives another distinguished triangle
 Z --inclusion--> X_(n+1) --> Y_n --> Z[1],
because the cone of that inclusion contracts to X_n direct-sum Z[1].

Thus two triangles have the SAME fixed cap Z and SAME intermediate Y_n,
exactly the structural feature used to compare consecutive Watson knots.
All maps commute with the indicated Lee differential. Nevertheless
 rank X_n=1+4n,
while its quantum support stays in {0,4}, its ordinary graded Euler
polynomial stays 1, and its Lee homology stays rank1. Direct-sum inclusions
also supply compatible maps between successive such diagrams. The model
can be extended to n in Z using |n| and reversing the two roles at zero.

This does not claim to model every grading placement or every additional
relation of the geometric Watson saddle maps. It proves that merely
promoting the LES to abstract natural mapping-cone triangles, or adding
the ordinary Lee spectral-sequence constraint, cannot establish a rank
bound. One must use an additional geometric relation not present in these
axioms. In particular a finite-dimensional domain for each saddle map
does not stop the common intermediate space from growing.

3. A SUFFICIENT, TESTABLE CHAIN-LEVEL REPLACEMENT

A genuinely fixed-size monodromy model would settle bounded rank. For
example, if all Kh complexes C(K_n) were homotopy equivalent, up to fixed
grading shifts, to Cone(f_n:C->D) for TWO FIXED finite complexes C,D, then
 rank Kh(K_n) <= dim C + dim D
uniformly. A more specific f_n=f M^n g model would be stronger than needed.
Likewise a decomposition into a fixed complex plus uniformly boundedly
many shifted fixed summands would suffice.

No such model follows just from the braid action being invertible. A
power of a tangle/Rouquier complex is an iterated tensor product whose
number of terms can grow; it is not automatically an endomorphism of one
fixed finite complex. Watson's proof identifies the cap objects, but does
not identify a fixed ambient complex containing all iterated saddle maps.

The next meaningful calculation is therefore the actual two-saddle map
relation in the Bar-Natan tangle category after the beta/beta^-1 cap
isotopies. If it yields a fixed finite cone or eventual cancellation, rank
boundedness follows. If its iteration creates a nonzero repeated block
like W, rank growth becomes a plausible target. Neither alternative has
been established here. No long list of full knot computations is needed
before that categorical relation is understood.

4. CENTRAL-POWER TOPOLOGY: PRECISE LIMITED AUDIT

The tangle agent's correction to the earlier annulus warning is valid:
in an actual S^2 x I six-strand shell, full twists about complementary
triples induce the same separating Dehn twist on the six-punctured sphere.
Their opposite powers cancel in its mapping class group. A possible
residual spherical-braid 2pi rotation is absorbed by rotating the entire
endcap ball. Knotted pieces inside that ball move with the ball and cause
no marking obstruction.

This audits closure-triviality of an adjacent j(z)^k s(z)^k factor with
the stated opposite signs and shell placement. It does not independently
establish the agent's separate SL2(Z) normal form or its connected-sum
identification. Those need their own checks.

5. VERDICT

The support theorem is supported by the source diagrams and the exact
grading calculations. Its constants depend on the fixed tangles and beta.
The rank bound supplied by the two skein triangles is only O(|n|).
Fixed cap complexes plus abstract naturality do not improve that bound.
Actual rank growth, actual uniform rank boundedness, and any generic
fixed-Jones-fiber conclusion remain unproved.
