INDEPENDENT AUDIT RECORD: DEGENERATE WATSON TEMPLATE
1 November 2026. Braid track's checks relayed to geometry track.

The independent braid track checked the three necessary pieces of the
connected-sum reduction in geometric_bridge.txt:

1. Complementary central twists.
   In the actual S2 x I six-strand shell, full twists on complementary
   triples give the same separating Dehn twist in Mod(S2,6). Opposite
   powers cancel. The residual spherical-braid pi1(SO(3)) class is killed
   on closure by rotating the entire endcap ball through 2pi. Arbitrary
   local knots in that ball move with it and cause no marking obstruction.
   Required qualifications: the factors must occur in that same shell,
   with the stated opposite signs, and be adjacent. Here s sends the
   left full twist to the right inverse full twist, while centrality
   permits adjacency.

2. Exact SL(2,Z) normal form.
   For U=[[1,1],[0,1]], L=[[1,0],[-1,1]],
      L^r U^epsilon L^s
       = [[1-epsilon*s, epsilon],
          [-r(1-epsilon*s)-s, 1-epsilon*r]].
   If M=[[a,epsilon],[c,d]] has determinant one and epsilon=+/-1,
   r=epsilon(1-d), s=epsilon(1-a) give M exactly because
   c=epsilon(ad-1). The B3 -> SL(2,Z) kernel is generated by z^2,
   z=(sigma1 sigma2)^3, whose image is -I.

3. Elementary marked cap trace.
   For alpha=sigma1^epsilon, beta=sigma1^epsilon sigma5^-epsilon.
   The outer top cap1--6 becomes the U cap2--5. The outer bottom cap1--6
   becomes the T cap2--5. Strands3 and4 are the two connecting arcs.
   This is D(T)#D(U), with the two opposite outer crossings removable
   in the separating-cap isotopy. No algebraic or endpoint objection
   was found in that independent trace.

The determinant argument and two-bridge interpretation are laid out
separately in geometric_bridge.txt. The finite PD checks are conventions
