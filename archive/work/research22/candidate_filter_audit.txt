CONVENTIONS

KnotInfo's Kh vectors are [modulus, rank, homological, quantum].
Modulus1 denotes Q, modulus2 denotes F2, and modulus0 denotes Z.
The audit asserts the field marker for each rational or mod2 record;
it does not confuse the first entry with the rank. Mirror duality negates
both gradings of reduced Kh. The unknot normalization has q-degree0.

Primary documentation:
https://knotinfo.org/descriptions/khovanov_reduced_rational_vector.html

As an independent convention check, all 2,977 records with separately
available Jones vectors agree exactly with the Euler polynomial after
substituting t=q^2. The remaining 9,988 records' Jones equality is derived
from their supplied Kh Euler characteristics, not separately recomputed
from diagrams. This is a data provenance limitation worth retaining.

KnotInfo HFK vectors are [rank, Alexander, Maslov], computed over F2.
Mirror duality negates Alexander and Maslov with no arbitrary shift.
The normalized symmetric Alexander polynomial is the Maslov-signed Euler
sum. Directional divisibility is Delta_source divides Delta_target.
The code removes Laurent-unit shifts and performs exact rational
polynomial long division. Since knot Alexander polynomials are primitive
integral polynomials, Gauss's lemma makes this equivalent to integral
divisibility; it does not use numerical roots or floating-point division.

Primary documentation:
https://knotinfo.org/descriptions/hfk_polynomial_vector.html

The HFK filter is explicitly an F2 filter, not a claim about rational HFK.
Zemke's Theorem1.2 supplies exactly the required grade-by-grade inequality:
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v190-n3-p05-s.pdf

The Kh injection direction is source<=target. Ribbon concordance induces
a bigraded split injection; the based reduced version uses the same
left-inverse cobordism. Over either Q or F2 the necessary inequality is
therefore valid. Equality of s, tau, signature, and smooth g4 is necessary
for any smooth concordance; mirroring changes signs of s,tau,signature
and leaves g4 fixed. Metadata keys remove the asterisk in the polynomial
table's 13*a/13*n names before lookup.

SCOPE AND UNSAFE CLAIMS TO AVOID

The result is 27 possible directed pairs up to simultaneous mirror that
pass this particular finite list of obstructions. They are NOT 27 ribbon
concordances, not 27 counterexamples to Euler faithfulness, and not a
complete enumeration of potential ribbon pairs. The initial strict-rank
condition intentionally excludes equal-Kh-rank pairs; it targets a
nonzero Euler-invisible cokernel. The input table covers the listed
prime knots only, not arbitrary connected sums or larger knots.

Passing graded dimension inequalities does not construct compatible
chain maps, prove integral direct summand conditions, or establish
concordance. The full CFK-infinity splitting, integral Kh torsion,
Levine-Tristram signatures away from -1, and other concordance invariants
have not been tested. No source-target isotopy, band, or ribbon movie is
asserted here. Smooth four-genus equality was correctly checked and does
not reduce the reported 54 survivors.

The correct mirror convention was checked explicitly before filtering:
  free summand at (h,q)       -> free summand at (-h,-q);
  torsion summand at (h,q)    -> torsion summand at (1-h,-q).
The latter is the Ext term in cohomological duality. It must not be
replaced by the free summand rule. With cohomological conventions,
an even torsion class in integral degree h contributes to mod2 Kh in
degrees h and h-1. The displayed mirror rules therefore give mod2
classes in degrees -h and 1-h, exactly the negation of the original
mod2 degrees. The checker confirmed the integral-to-mod2 UCT against
the independent mod2 vectors for EVERY original record AND its mirror:
25,930 checks, no mismatch. In the complete reduced integral dataset,
the only nonzero torsion modulus is 2 (14 cyclic summands in total);
none belongs to an endpoint of a surviving candidate.

For a split injection over Z, one must compare free ranks and the
multiplicities of each cyclic prime-power elementary divisor in each
bigrading. Merely comparing mod-p dimensions does not suffice; neither
does allowing a Z/p summand to embed nonsplit into Z/p^2. The checker
implements exact elementary-divisor multiplicity comparisons, with
coprime moduli separated into their prime-power factors.
