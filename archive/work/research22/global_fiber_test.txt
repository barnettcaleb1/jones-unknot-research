PASS 22: GLOBAL JONES-FIBER FALSIFICATION TEST
Date: 2026-11-01 UTC. Bounded primary-source check; no completeness claim.

1. RESULT AND BOUNDARY

No certified classical non-nugatory Jones-preserving crossing change,
or infinite exact-Jones knot fiber with unbounded rational reduced Kh
rank/quantum diameter, was located in this pass. This is a report of the
bounded search, not a claim that no such example is known.

The strongest useful outcome is an exact local replacement target:
single-coordinate vanishing in the classical two-tangle skein module
would have to detect a geometric separating disk. This is a more
specific geometric problem than whole-fiber compactness, but remains
unproved. Section4 gives a new scoped consequence using existing Wang
theorems: Jones-flat non-nugatory twist orbits have only finitely many
members whose rational reduced Kh has no Euler cancellation. In the
split-smoothing case, there are no such members at all.

2. WHY THE MUTATION SEARCH DID NOT GIVE A COUNTEREXAMPLE

Conway mutation: Kotelskiy--Watson--Zibrowius,
On mutation invariance in Khovanov homology, arXiv2602.23442v1,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.23442 , TheoremA and its knot corollary,
state bigraded reduced Kh invariance over every field. Thus, subject to
this 2026 preprint's theorem, an orbit of ordinary Conway mutations
preserves both quantities under examination. The theorem was read,
not independently reproved here. Older rational-coefficient mutant-LINK
counterexamples need not preserve components and do not contradict it.

Genus-two mutation: Dunfield--Garoufalidis--Shumakovitch--Thistlethwaite,
https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/mutation.pdf ,
give actual equal-colored-Jones pairs with different Kh. This refutes
determination, not boundedness of a whole exact-Jones fiber. The
Moore--Starkston infinite family examined earlier has equality within
each pair, not one fixed polynomial across its parameter; see the
prior scaffold for the explicit growing-support audit. Neither source
supplies the required unbounded fixed-polynomial family.

Rotants: Joseph MacColl, Rotors in Khovanov Homology,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.00851 , Theorem1.5, constructs infinitely
many pairs with equal Jones and distinct Kh. These are links, and the
polynomial is not fixed as the parameter varies. There is a short exact
check directly in his data: at quantum degree -76 for L_0, the rational
ranks in homological degrees -32,-31,-30 are 75,127,23. Their alternating
sum is -29. Proposition2.6 transports this entire quantum slice to

  Q(n)=-76+22 ceil(n/2)-26 floor(n/2).

Each two steps shift the homological grading by -2 and Q by -4.
Therefore the unreduced Jones coefficient at Q(2m)=-76-4m is always
-29. The odd subsequence similarly has a nonzero coefficient escaping
to negative infinity. Neither parity subsequence lies in a single
exact-Jones fiber. Equality of the two rotants at each n must not be
read as equality across n. This check uses published homology data,
not new diagram enumeration.

Crossings: targeted primary-source searches on Jones cosmetic crossings,
crossing changes among mutants, and skein-equivalent tangles did not
produce an actual example meeting the required one-crossing condition.
Wang's explicit split-band question remains the exact source boundary
we can certify: on printed p5 of https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.01070 he
does not establish that the Jones defect P_b is nonzero for every
nontrivial band. This historical statement is not a current exhaustive
status claim. Surgery papers on cosmetic Dehn fillings do not answer
the stronger question of merely equal Jones polynomials of the branch
knots.

3. A CONCRETE LOCAL GEOMETRIC REPLACEMENT TARGET

Let T be an actual classical two-arc tangle, with no circle components,
and choose the crossingless basis I,e of TL_2 over Z[A,A^-1]. Write

  [T]=a(A) I+b(A)e,    delta=-A^2-A^-2,
  R_+=A I+A^-1 e,     R_-=A^-1 I+A e.

Use the closure with tr(I)=delta, tr(e)=1 (unknot bracket1). Since
e^2=delta e, direct multiplication gives

  <T R_+>=-A^3 a-A^-3 b,
  <T R_->=-A^-3 a-A^3 b.

Assume these closures are knots and choose the orientation/basis so
the displayed R_+ crossing is positive. The writhes differ by2.
Equality of normalized Jones polynomials is therefore equivalent to

  <T R_+>=A^6<T R_->,
  (A^9-A^-3)b=0,
  b=0.

The last step is exact over the Laurent integral domain. Mirroring or
rotating the convention exchanges the relevant basis rays and changes
units, not the geometric question.

DECISIVE MISSING LEMMA (UNPROVED):
If an actual classical two-arc tangle lies on one crossingless skein
ray, then its arcs can be separated by a properly embedded disk in
the ball, in the corresponding endpoint configuration. Local knots on
the individual arcs are allowed.

The local-knot allowance is essential: cutting open any knot produces
a one-strand scalar, and adding such a local knot to I still gives a
scalar multiple of I. Requiring rationality rather than geometric
separation would be immediately false.

The separating-disk conclusion makes the displayed crossing nugatory.
Conversely, the usual nugatory-crossing decomposition gives such a
separated tangle. Sikora's geometric reformulation of the cosmetic
crossing conjecture uses exactly this 'unlinked tangle' notion:
Tangle equations, the Jones conjecture, slopes of surfaces in tangle
complements, and q-deformed rationals, Canadian J.Math.76(2024),
https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08162 . His formulation concerns isotopic
closures; replacing isotopy by Jones equality is an additional,
unproved strengthening. No theorem from that paper is being asserted
to prove this missing lemma.

This target provides a finite exact certificate for each candidate:
one skein coefficient is zero, and topology must either supply a
separating disk or certify that none exists. It avoids invoking rank
or quantum-support bounds for unrelated knots in the same Jones fiber.
It is still not a full generic unknot-detection scaffold: V_K=1 does
not supply a Jones-invisible non-nugatory crossing, and no such generic
reduction is assumed here.

4. PROVED SCOPED CONSEQUENCE: EULER-EFFICIENT FILLINGS CANNOT RECUR

Define r(K)=dim_Q reducedKh(K), and let P_K(q) be its graded Euler
polynomial (normalized Jones in the Kh variable). Call K
Euler-efficient if

  r(K)=sum_j |[q^j]P_K(q)|.

Equivalently, for every quantum grading j, all nonzero homological
groups have the same parity: there is no cancellation at that j.
An Euler-efficient knot has quantum support exactly supp(P_K).
In particular every knot whose reduced Kh_Q is supported on one
delta=j/2-i diagonal is Euler-efficient. This definition does not
require any unproved assertion about all knots.

THEOREM (direct synthesis of established band results).
Let K_n be an oriented band-twist family from a two-component link L,
and suppose all P_Kn=P. If the crossing is non-nugatory, then:

(a) If L is nonsplit, r(K_n) tends to infinity as n tends to either
    positive or negative infinity. Hence only finitely many K_n are
    Euler-efficient, and their Euler-cancellation excess
    r(K_n)-||P||_1 tends to infinity in both directions.

(b) If L is split but b is nontrivial, no K_n is Euler-efficient.
    The ranks are constant, while quantum diameter grows as
    4|n|+O(1).

PROOF OF (a): Wang, Split link detection for sl(P) link homology in
characteristic P, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.09110 , Proposition1.7
and Remark1.8, apply to N=2 and F=Q. The field restriction in the
paper's main title does not apply to these propositions. Rank grows
in both directions, while ||P||_1 is fixed.

PROOF OF (b): Wang, The cosmetic crossing conjecture for split links,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.01070 , Theorems1.7--1.8 and Remark1.9,
give reduced-Q decompositions

  reducedKh(K_n)=reducedKh(K_#) direct-sum h^(2n)q^(4n) H_b,
  H_b!=0.

Thus ranks are constant. If one member were Euler-efficient, every
member would have the same minimal rank ||P||_1, and hence every
member would have quantum support supp(P). This contradicts the
diameter divergence caused by the fixed nonzero summand and moving
nonzero H_b. This proves (b).

COROLLARY: If a Jones-flat band-twist family contains infinitely many
Euler-efficient members (in particular, infinitely many rational
reduced-Kh-thin members), the smoothing is split and the band is
trivial. In the split case, a single efficient member suffices.

This is an independently testable theorem about actual twist families,
not an assumption of global fiber boundedness. Its potential use is
to look for a genuine geometric source of efficient fillings. No
general existence of such fillings is proved or assumed: a nugatory
crossing on a thick knot already cautions against demanding thinness
for every flat orbit without accounting for fixed local knot factors.

5. STRATEGIC VERDICT

The searched mutation mechanisms do not presently disprove H-rank or
H-spread. The local scalar-ray problem is a sharper falsification
target than broad searches for arbitrary equal-Jones pairs. The exact
efficient-filling theorem is a useful restricted endpoint, but neither
it nor the scalar-ray target supplies the missing full-scope bridge
from an arbitrary hypothetical Jones-one knot. The bounded pass has
not proved Jones unknot detection and has found no counterexample.
