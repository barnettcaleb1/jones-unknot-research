ALL MARKOV PRESENTATIONS: GLOBAL QUANTUM-CONSTRAINT AUDIT
1 November 2026. Exact algebraic tests; no novelty claim.

QUESTION
Can the ordinary Jones-one hypothesis, applied to EVERY presentation of
the same knot, provide independent measurements that force positivity
saturation or a vanishing operator ideal, without adding satellite data?

VERDICT
The proposed measurement amplification does not occur. All scalar probes
obtained by transporting the ordinary closure through Markov moves are
the same linear functional. More strongly, a Markov stabilization has a
strict conditional-expectation variance, independent of the knot. The
unknot itself supplies an exact counterexample to operator rigidity.
These facts do not rule out a new geometric theorem about which
destabilizations exist; they rule out deriving it from the stated scalar
and channel identities alone.

1. FULL CONDITIONAL ROUTE, BEFORE TESTING THE MISSING PREMISE

Every knot has a closed-braid presentation. For the standard unitarized
Temperley--Lieb representation, the writhe-normalized n-strand operator
R_beta satisfies
 V_(closure beta)=delta^(n-1) tau_n(R_beta),  tau_n(I)=1.

A sufficient global bridge would be:
 (S) If V_K=1, some Markov representative of K has
     |tau_n(R_beta)|=1 at a positive specialization delta>1.
Indeed the displayed formula gives |tau_n(R_beta)|=delta^(1-n), so (S)
forces n=1. The closure of a one-strand braid is U. Thus this chain reaches
an arbitrary Jones-one knot and closes exactly.

But (S) is equivalent in strength to Jones unknot detection, since U has
a one-strand representative. It is a precise target, not a newly proved
weaker lemma. The proposed way of proving it was to turn all Markov
presentation measurements into tomography or an entropy equality. The
following tests defeat that mechanism as presently formulated.

2. CONVENTIONS AND THE ONE-DIMENSIONAL PROBE THEOREM

Work first over Q(A), with
 delta=-A^2-A^-2, e_i^2=delta e_i,
 rho(sigma_i)=A I+A^-1 e_i,
 rho(sigma_i^-1)=A^-1 I+A e_i.
Let w(beta) be the exponent sum and
 R_beta=(-A)^(-3w(beta)) rho(beta).
The normalized Markov traces obey
 tau_n(I)=1, tau_n(xy)=tau_n(yx),
 tau_(n+1)(iota(x))=tau_n(x),
 tau_(n+1)(iota(x)e_n)=delta^-1 tau_n(x).

Define linear normalized stabilization maps on the entire algebra by
 S_+(x)=(-A)^-3 iota(x) rho(sigma_n),
 S_-(x)=(-A)^3  iota(x) rho(sigma_n^-1).
Direct substitution gives, for BOTH signs,
 tau_(n+1)(S_+(x))=delta^-1 tau_n(x),
 tau_(n+1)(S_-(x))=delta^-1 tau_n(x).
Conjugation by any invertible braid image preserves tau_n.

Put L_n(x)=delta^(n-1) tau_n(x). Then
 L_(n+1) S_+=L_n=L_(n+1) S_-,
 L_n Ad(rho(gamma))=L_n.
Consequently every ordinary closure functional transported back through
a prescribed sequence of conjugations and stabilizations is exactly L_n,
as an identity of linear functionals. An inverse destabilization, when
geometrically defined, has the inverse equality on its domain. Therefore
all such transported scalar probes span one dimension, not a tomographic
family. This holds identically in A, including every derivative or level.

This does not say all information in a braid presentation is scalar.
The existence of a special geometric move sequence can carry additional
information. It says that evaluating the SAME ordinary invariant after
known closure-preserving moves supplies no new independent functional.

3. STABILIZATION IS A STRICT QUANTUM CHANNEL STEP

At a specialization where TL_n and TL_(n+1) have faithful positive Markov
forms and |A|=1, let E_n:TL_(n+1)->TL_n be the trace-preserving conditional
expectation. It is unital and completely positive and satisfies
 E_n(iota(x))=x, E_n(iota(x)e_n)=delta^-1 x.
Thus the stronger operator identity is
 E_n(S_+(x))=delta^-1 x=E_n(S_-(x)).

For a unitary normalized braid R, S_+(R) and S_-(R) are unitary too. Hence
their Kadison--Schwarz defects are EXACTLY

 E_n(S(R)^* S(R))-E_n(S(R))^* E_n(S(R))
                  =(1-delta^-2) I.

For delta>1 this is strictly positive, for every R, including R=I from
the one-strand unknot. No Jones-one condition changes it. Stabilization
therefore does not enter the multiplicative-domain equality case of this
channel. Multiplying E_n by delta restores the Jones normalization but
makes the map nonunital; one cannot then import the unital-channel
equality argument. This is a concrete obstruction to the proposed
recoverability/entropy bridge, not merely a warning about normalization.

4. ACTUAL UNKNOT COUNTERTEST TO OPERATOR RIGIDITY

In B_2 the braids sigma_1 and sigma_1^-1 both close to U, being the two
Markov stabilizations of the trivial one-strand braid. Their normalized
operators are
 R_+=-A^-2 I-A^-4 e_1,
 R_-=-A^2 I-A^4 e_1.
Therefore
 R_+-R_-=(A^4-A^-4)(e_1-delta^-1 I).
This is generically nonzero although both closures have Jones polynomial
1 in every Markov presentation and at every A.

Let D=e_1-delta^-1 I. Then
 tau_2(D)=0,
 tau_2(e_1 D)=tau_2(D^*D)=1-delta^-2>0
on the positive real-delta arc. In particular
 ||R_+-R_-||_tau^2=|A^4-A^-4|^2(1-delta^-2)>0
away from the isolated zeros of A^4-A^-4.

This sharply distinguishes ker(tau), which is only a linear hyperplane,
from the trace radical {D:tau(aD)=0 for all a}, which is zero for a
faithful positive trace. Markov invariance preserves trace-zero defects;
it does NOT turn them into a two-sided ideal or permit multiplication by
arbitrary test operators. The latter operations change the closure and
usually its knot or component type. They would be new data.

The earlier beta_m family in work/proof11/braid/markov_moment_obstruction.txt
shows more: actual unknot presentations can have unbounded second-moment
derivatives. The two-strand example above already suffices for the present
global-presentation inference, so no further computations are required.

5. AN ADDITIONAL INFINITE-TOWER LIMITATION

For each fixed finite n there is an open unitary arc near A=i, delta=2,
on which the Markov form is positive and faithful. There is no common
GENERIC open arc with this property simultaneously for every strand
number. Full-tower positivity for 0<delta<2 occurs only at the discrete
Jones values delta=2cos(pi/(k+2)); beyond a finite strand number one must
pass to the Jones quotient, which kills the relevant Jones--Wenzl ideal.
At delta=2 the generic tower remains positive, but the corresponding
ordinary Jones specialization is t=1 and is universal for knots.

Thus an argument using arbitrary stabilizations cannot silently retain
both a fixed generic unitary parameter and faithful positivity in every
unquotiented TL_n. Any finite move sequence can still be treated on a
sufficiently small arc, and root-of-unity quotients can treat all n; this
limitation does not invalidate those legitimate uses.

6. WHAT A MINIMIZATION REFORMULATION REALLY MEASURES

At a fixed unitary Jones level with delta>1, the simple positive defect
 tau_n((R_beta-I)^*(R_beta-I))=2-2 Re tau_n(R_beta)
for a Jones-one knot is
 2(1-delta^(1-n)).
Its infimum over all braid presentations is therefore
 2(1-delta^(1-b(K))),
where b(K) is braid index. It vanishes exactly for the unknot. This is a
valid Markov-invariant minimization, but it merely repackages braid index
on the Jones-one fiber: the scalar hypothesis does not show its minimum
is zero. A proof that it is zero would be the missing geometric theorem.

7. PRECISE BOUNDARY OF THIS NEGATIVE RESULT

Not excluded: a theorem that the Jones-one identity forces a special
exchange move, a geometrically recognizable destabilization, or some
new monotone presentation complexity to decrease. Such a theorem could
prove the sufficient bridge (S) without introducing satellite invariants.

Excluded by the exact calculations here: getting (S) solely by treating
all Markov-related closure values as independent probes, promoting a
trace kernel to an operator ideal, or asserting equality in the standard
conditional-expectation inequality. The simplest proposed premises fail
already on actual unknot presentations.

No new generic positive bridge has been established in this bounded pass.
