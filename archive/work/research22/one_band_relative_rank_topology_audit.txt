The key operator assertion is stronger than the theorem's displayed
isomorphism of images, but is explicitly present in its proof: the SAME
W induces, up to sign, 2X_K and 2 i X_J p when d=1. Therefore ker(p) is
killed by X. The split-injection statement holds in the lifted theory
by Sarkar's footnote(iv). Dot-action sign conventions can replace X by
-X in a module identification; this preserves all graded elementary
divisors and does not affect the argument.

The passage from graded homology splitting to Poincare polynomial
splitting does not require a chain-level splitting of the particular
cobordism maps. A bounded free complex over graded Q[X] decomposes into
the free resolutions of its graded homology modules and contractibles.
Each added Q[X]/(X) homology summand is represented by a two-term bar
with endpoints (h-1,q-2),(h,q), as also recorded explicitly in Sarkar
Section5. Specializing X=0 therefore adds a nonnegative multiple of
1+u q^2. The common reduced normalization is harmless.

Finally, normalized Jones has derivative zero at t=1 for each endpoint
knot. Differentiating the Euler-polynomial difference yields S(-1,1)=0,
so S(1,1) is even. Its added rank 2S(1,1) is a nonnegative multiple of4.

ADDENDUM: GENERAL d-SADDLE EXTENSION

The same operator proof passes for every d. Sarkar's two evaluations of
the same W are, up to overall signs,
  2^d X_K^d Id_K  and  2^d i X_J^d p.
Over Q, 2^d is invertible, so X_K^d ker(p)=0. Dot-motion sign choices
do not affect this annihilation. The graded splitting and PID argument
therefore add only torsion bars with lengths 1<=a<=d. Consequently

  P_K(u,q)-P_J(u,q) = sum_{a=1}^d (1+u q^(2a)) S_a(u,q),

with every S_a a finite Laurent polynomial with nonnegative integer
coefficients. For d=0 the difference is zero. Thus existence of a
positive-length bar matching of the relative bigraded rank data is a
necessary condition for ANY actual ribbon concordance, even when its
number of saddles is unspecified. Failure of every such matching rules
out ribbon concordance; it does not require a factorization through
one-band concordances between knots. No new sign or grading issue arises.

This audit checks the general implication, not the separate computation
of which concrete knot pairs fail its matching condition.
