PASS22: A TOPOLOGICAL ENDGAME FOR RIBBON DOUBLES, AND TWO EXACT GATES

Status: bounded research audit. The axis endgame below is a valid
conditional architecture using a recent primary preprint; the missing
Jones-to-axis implication is not proved. The cyclic-disk counterexample
is an established obstruction to a tempting substitute. No full Jones
detection theorem or new counterexample is claimed.

1. THE GLOBAL STARTING OBJECT

For arbitrary K, let R=K#(-K), with -K the reversed mirror, and use the
standard product ribbon disk D_K. Its boundary is R, its complement group
is pi_1(S^3 minus K), and a puncture near a minimum gives C_K:U -> R.
If V_K=1 then V_R=1. If K is nontrivial, genus additivity makes R nontrivial.

The precise product-disk group statement is recorded in Friedl--Teichner,
New topologically slice knots, Geometry & Topology 9 (2005), p2135:
https://math.berkeley.edu/~teichner/Papers/topslice.pdf
It matters that this is the PARTICULAR product disk, not an arbitrary
ribbon disk with the same boundary.

The previous Euler-faithfulness route is logically complete but leaves
the entire ordinary-Jones/categorified-cancellation issue in its premise.
The purpose here is to isolate a purely geometric endgame with an actual
finite movie certificate, then locate the exact missing bridge to it.

2. AXIS EXTENSION: A CONDITIONAL ROUTE WITH AN EXISTING TOPOLOGICAL ENDGAME

Call C:U -> R axis-extendible if there is a disjoint embedded annulus
A in S^3 x I such that:
  (i) A is itself a ribbon concordance from an unknot A_0 to an unknot A_1;
  (ii) R is a closed braid about A_1 in the terminal S^3.
No restriction is needed on the linking or geometry of U union A_0,
beyond its two individual components being unknotted.

Proposition. If C is axis-extendible, then R is the unknot.

Proof. C union A is a componentwise ribbon concordance
  U union A_0 -> R union A_1.
By Daniels' braid-axis minimality theorem, its terminal link is ribbon-
concordance minimal. Thus the two endpoint links are isotopic. Both
components of the initial link are unknots, so the component R is an
unknot, regardless of whether that isotopy preserves component labels.

Primary input: Benjamin Daniels, Braid closure union braid axis is ribbon
concordance minimal, arXiv:2607.14030 (2026), Theorem1.0.1 and Corollary1.0.2:
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14030
The source is a recent preprint. Its stated theorem was checked; this
pass does not independently reprove its full link-Floer argument.

Consequently the following candidate bridge would close the FULL Jones
problem, not merely a subclass:

  AX: If V_K=1, the canonical C_K can be chosen axis-extendible.

Indeed AX -> proposition -> R=U -> g(K)=0 -> K=U. There are no hidden
unknotting-number, strong-inversion, fusion-number, or braid-index bounds.

A finite geometric sufficient criterion for axis extension is explicit:
give a ribbon movie, choose a moving unknot disjoint from every birth
ball and saddle band throughout the movie, and verify that the terminal
knot is braided about the final unknot. The trace of the moving unknot
is an annulus with no critical points, disjoint from the ribbon annulus.
This is a checkable movie certificate rather than a homological assertion.

3. COVERAGE AND INFORMATION AUDIT OF AX

There is NO universal axis-extension theorem for ribbon concordances.
The standard U -> square-knot ribbon concordance already contradicts it,
by the proposition. Thus adding an axis is not a harmless decoration:
although every terminal knot can be braided about some unknot, that axis
need not extend disjointly over the particular ribbon concordance with
an unknotted initial component.

AX restricted to the unit Jones fiber is itself equivalent in strength
to Jones detection (the reverse direction uses the trivial concordance
when K=U). Its attraction is a concrete geometric target and a known
topological endgame; it is not an established reduction in difficulty.
It should not be advertised as a proof or as following from Markov's
theorem.

Knowing the Jones scalar for every Markov presentation adds no values:
Markov moves reproduce the same invariant. An elementary actual-unknot
example exhibits the annular information that is discarded. In the
Kauffman bracket skein module of the annulus R[z], put
  delta=-A^2-A^-2.
The identity one-braid has annular skein z. A positive stabilization is
the two-braid sigma_1, whose writhe-normalized annular skein is
  (-A^3)^-1 (A z^2 + A^-1 delta)
  = -A^-2 z^2 - A^-4 delta.
These are different annular skeins, although filling the annulus into S^3
sets z=delta and makes both equal delta. After the usual circle reduction,
both are the normalized Jones polynomial 1 of the unknot.

Thus a Jones-to-axis proof needs new geometry relating band moves to an
axis; it cannot silently recover annular skein state, link-Floer support,
or an axis-colored family from the ordinary scalar. Stabilization does
not repair that loss. This is the same scalar-versus-state gate in a
particularly concrete topological form.

4. A DECISIVE COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE ARBITRARY-DISK GROUP SHORTCUT

The statement
  'a knot bounding a ribbon disk with cyclic complement group is unknotted'
is FALSE.

Nakanishi--Nakagawa, On Ribbon Knots, Mathematics Seminar Notes 10 (1982),
423--430, proof of Theorem3b on printed p429, starts with a nontrivial
Kinoshita--Terasaka-type knot K and constructs a ribbon disk with boundary
K#rK whose complement group is infinite cyclic. Here r denotes reflection.
The boundary is nontrivial by connected-sum genus additivity.
https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/E0001171/E0001171.pdf

The exact text identifies the first lower half as the ribbon disk for
B union rB and then gives its complement group Z. This is sufficient for
the counterexample; no claim about the second lower half's ribbon status
is needed. This reading was independently checked by the homology agent.

Therefore merely producing SOME simple-complement disk cannot replace
Euler faithfulness. The standard product disk has group pi_1(K), but the
same boundary can have other disks with much simpler groups. A proof
must retain its specified disk or additional peripheral/embedding data.

For the specified D_K, proving pi_1(B^4 minus D_K)=Z WOULD finish the
problem, because this group is the original knot group. However deriving
that assertion from V_K=1 is another full-strength missing bridge. A
representation-theoretic version asking that all traceless SU(2)
representations be abelian encounters the same issue: ordinary Jones is
not the full skein module or the character variety of the disk complement.
AJ-type results concern the colored Jones sequence, not its single
two-dimensional color. Neither ribbonness nor the split Khovanov map
supplies the omitted colored sequence.

5. EXISTING INSTANTON RESULTS DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY COVER THE DOUBLE

A further recent primary check was Imori--Park--Taniguchi, Unknotting
number, ribbon concordance, and singular instantons, arXiv:2607.12768,
Theorem1.2 and the following discussion:
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12768
Their obstruction to same-sign unknotting of slice band sums requires a
summand with a nonzero specified instanton concordance invariant. An
arbitrary hypothetical Jones-one knot has no such established property.
The paper explicitly exhibits a double of the figure-eight knot where
the unrestricted same-sign conclusion fails. Consequently this theorem
cannot be applied universally just because R is a ribbon double.

6. RESULT OF THIS PASS

There is a clean topological endgame through braid-axis ribbon minimality,
with a precise finite movie certificate. The missing Jones-to-axis bridge
is still a genuine global mathematical problem. The disk-group shortcut
has an explicit established counterexample, so it should be removed from
the candidate proof architecture unless the canonical disk and its
peripheral information are preserved. No existing Floer or gauge theorem
checked here converts the ordinary Jones scalar into the needed state,
axis annulus, or complement representation restriction.
