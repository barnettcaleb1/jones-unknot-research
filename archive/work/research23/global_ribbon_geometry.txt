Status: bounded research deductions and exclusions; no counterexample to E or

E means: for an actual ribbon concordance C:J -> K, the reduced rational
Khovanov split complement E_C cannot be nonzero with graded Euler polynomial
zero. Thus equal Jones endpoints with a strict reduced-QKh rank increase
would falsify E. Matching polynomials and homology inequalities alone are
not a certificate of a ribbon concordance.

1. A global geometric filter removes 17 of the 24 remaining mirror classes.

Theorem (established inputs). If J -> K is ribbon, K is fibered, and
g(J)=g(K), then J and K are isotopic.

Precise primary locator: Ian Zemke, Knot Floer homology obstructs ribbon
concordance, Annals of Mathematics 190 (2019), section 1.5, printed p935:
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v190-n3-p05-s.pdf
This explicitly states the needed target-fibered version, not just a
both-endpoints-fibered version. An alternative proof uses his grading-
preserving HFK injection: at the common top Alexander grading the target
has rank one, so the source has rank one; Ni implies the source is fibered;
Gordon's Lemma 3.4 then gives isotopy.

Corollary. Equal-Alexander ribbon endpoints with fibered target are isotopic.
Indeed the Alexander breadth is 2g(K), so g(J)>=g(K); ribbon genus
monotonicity gives the reverse inequality. This uses equal Alexander, not
equal Jones. Equal Jones does not in general supply the hypothesis.

2. All seven require genuinely interacting bands: one-band searches cannot work.

Lemma. A one-birth/one-saddle ribbon concordance J -> K with g(J)=g(K)
has isotopic endpoints.

Proof. It presents K as a band sum of the split link J disjoint-union U.
The genus equality case of Gabai--Scharlemann gives an embedded minimal
Seifert surface S_J union b union D_U, with D_U a disk. A regular
neighborhood of D_U supplies a splitting sphere meeting b in a single
cocore. The band is trivial and K=J#U=J.

A precise primary source for both steps is Miyazaki, When is a
band-connected sum equal to the connected sum?, arXiv:1903.11266:
https://arxiv.org/html/1903.11266
Proof of Lemma 5 recalls the equality-case surface theorem; proof of
Theorem 1, Case 1, gives the disk-neighborhood argument. We only need
the case with one unknot summand. We do not rely on a stronger general
claim that genus equality trivializes every two-nontrivial-summand band.

Corollary (movie-width gate). Any ribbon concordance between distinct
equal-genus knots has at least two births. Every ribbon Morse movie for
it has a regular level with at least three link components.

Proof. In a connected genus-zero annulus with no maxima, all saddles merge
components: writing b for births, s_+ for splitting saddles, and s_- for
merging saddles, Euler characteristic gives s_++s_-=b, and the initial
and final component counts give b+s_+-s_-=0, hence s_+=0. If no level
has three components, each birth must be followed by a merging saddle
before another birth. Thus the movie factors through knot-to-knot
one-birth/one-saddle stages. Genus is nondecreasing at every stage; equal
endpoint genera force equality at every stage, and the lemma makes all
successive knots isotopic.

This is a geometric obstruction additional to the relative-Lee-bar test.
Bars of length one are a necessary algebraic condition for a one-band
movie, not a construction of such a movie. Six of the seven remaining
classes pass the length-one bar test while being excluded from one-band
geometry. The 13n5018 -> mirror(13n1003) class already requires bar length
at least two. All seven pass the arbitrary-positive-length bar test.

The bound is sharp for actual ribbon concordances. Let J be the trefoil
and let R=6_1 be the stevedore ribbon knot with its usual one-band disk.
There is a one-birth/one-saddle ribbon J -> J#R. Apply the standard
untwisted Whitehead pattern, which has geometric wrapping number two.
The parallel-copy construction gives a two-birth/two-saddle ribbon
Wh_0(J) -> Wh_0(J#R). Both endpoints are nontrivial genus-one knots, and
they are distinct (the companion decomposition distinguishes J from
J#R). The preceding lemma proves that the minimum birth count is exactly
two. This does NOT assert that their Jones polynomials coincide.
Satellite preservation and Whitehead nonisotopy references were audited
in ../research22/satellite_same_genus_appendix.txt, using Baker's A note
on the concordance of fibered knots, Remark 6, and its cited Kawauchi and
Kouno--Motegi results. This template confirms why a general ribbon movie
cannot always be decomposed into one-band knot-to-knot stages.

3. Concrete genus-two mutation attempt fails before any search.

Moore--Starkston construct the pair 14n22185,14n22589 and infinite
families (K_n,K_n^tau) by twisting their ribbon bands. Each knot is itself
a band sum of a two-component unlink, so each is ribbon. All colors of
Jones agree across each pair. However Corollary 4.2 gives rational
unreduced Kh dimension 26 for both, with distinct bigradings, for every
n>=0. A grading-preserving ribbon injection between finite-dimensional
spaces of equal total dimension would be an isomorphism, so neither
direction is ribbon. Their HFK data gives an additional version of the
same obstruction. Thus two individually ribbon mutant knots do not
supply a ribbon concordance between them.
Primary: https://msp.org/agt/2015/15-1/agt-v15-n1-p02-s.pdf
Theorem 2.1 (colored Jones), Figure 2 / p48 (base pair and bands),
Corollary 4.2 (different graded Kh, equal dimension).

The standard Kanenobu equal-polynomial family is also unusable for a
directional ribbon relation between distinct members: all members are
fibered genus-two knots, so the theorem in section 1 excludes the
relation. Their being ribbon knots individually is insufficient.
Primary account of these properties: Hedden--Watson, On the geography
and botany of knot Floer homology, section 6.5, Figure 6 and Theorem 6.12:
https://d-nb.info/114370181X/34

4. Two general construction traps, with exact hypotheses.

(a) Reversible-movie obstruction. Suppose an involutive operation M
exchanges J,K and carries a proposed ribbon movie J->K to an actual
ribbon movie M(J)->M(K), preserving the critical-point types. Then it
also gives K->J. Agol's ribbon partial-order theorem forces J=K.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.03626
This applies to a genuinely mutation-compatible movie, not to arbitrary
mutants: mutation need not extend over a given concordance at all.

(b) Connected-sum common-upper-bound circularity. The obvious common
ribbon successor of ribbon knots A,B is A#B, with V(A#B)=V(A)V(B).
If V(A)=V(B)=f, this has f^2, not f. Demanding equality f^2=f in the
Laurent domain (f is nonzero since f(1)=1) forces f=1. More generally,
adding a ribbon summand to preserve Jones requires that summand's Jones
to equal one. This cannot manufacture an E counterexample from known
non-unit equal-Jones mutants without reintroducing the original problem.

Fixed satellites preserve actual ribbon concordances, but cannot supply
a missing relation between two merely concordant/ribbon knots. A fixed
satellite operation that turned every ordinary concordance into ribbon
in the indicated direction would also do so to the reverse concordance;
Agol would then force its outputs constant on each concordance class.
Essential Whitehead patterns do not have that property, as the explicit
distinct ribbon Whitehead doubles above show. Thus no orientation-blind
satellite ribbonization argument is available.

5. Result for the full goal.

No falsifier of E was found. E remains an unproved sufficient statement
for Jones unknot detection via U -> K#mirror(K); nothing here proves E.
The concrete change of next action is substantial: the finite target
set drops from 24 to seven mirror classes, and every remaining pair
requires at least two interacting births/saddles. The natural infinite
equal-Jones mutant and Kanenobu templates are rigorously ruled out as
direct ribbon pairs. A new construction must provide an actual
directional multi-band movie, not merely individual ribbon disks,
mutation, or a common connected-sum successor.
