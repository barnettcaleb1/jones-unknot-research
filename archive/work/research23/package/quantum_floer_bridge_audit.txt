INDEPENDENT AUDIT: QUANTUM BRAID FLOER BRIDGE
1 November 2026. All three proposed deductions pass, with F2 coefficients.

Primary source checked: Anghel--Juhász, Quantum braid Floer homology,
arXiv:2601.08805v4 (7 September 2026).
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08805v4

Source facts used: Theorem 1.13 identifies the two specializations of Omega^q as the symmetrized Alexander polynomial and Jones polynomial of the mirror. Proposition 8.19 identifies its graded Euler characteristic; Theorem 8.22 supplies a spectral sequence to hat HFK. The complexes are over F2 (Section 5 and Definition 8.15). Section 9.2 explicitly gives Markov non-invariance and the quotient for Omega-prime. These source theorems were read, not independently reproved in this short audit.

1. The quotient is exactly a fiber product

Set S=Z[x^{+-1}], R=S[d^{+-1}], I=(d-1), J=(xd+1).
The map

  R -> S x S,    F -> (F(x,1),F(x,-x^-1))

has kernel I intersection J. R is a UFD, and d-1 and xd+1 are distinct nonassociate prime elements: each quotient is isomorphic to S. Therefore their principal ideals intersect in their product. This proves injectivity of the induced map

  R/((d-1)(xd+1)) -> S x S.

The ideals are NOT comaximal; I+J=(d-1,x+1), whose quotient is Z. Generalized CRT says the image is the fiber product

  {(A,Jones) in S x S : A-Jones is divisible by x+1}.

There is also a direct constructive proof of sufficiency. If H=(A-Jones)/(x+1) belongs to S, then

  F(x,d)=A(x)+x(d-1)H(x)

has the required two specializations. Thus this quotient invariant is precisely the ordered Alexander/Jones pair subject to agreement at x=-1. It cannot contain an additional independent mixed invariant. This is our algebraic deduction, not an extra assertion attributed to the source.

The source writes the quotient (d+1)(xd-1) for Omega-prime. Substituting d -> -d, as prescribed by Theorem 1.13, gives (d-1)(xd+1) for Omega^q. The signs therefore agree with the proposed ring.

2. Unknot test

Section 9.2 gives Omega-prime_(sigma1)=xd^2+xd-d. Consequently

  Omega^q_(sigma1)=xd^2-xd+d.

Its coefficient l1 norm is 3. The total dimension of a triply graded homology is at least the coefficient l1 norm of its bigraded Euler polynomial. Therefore

  dim_F2 qHFB(sigma1) >= 3,

although the braid closure is the unknot. Both specializations are exactly 1. Hence the claim that Jones-one forces one-dimensional qHFB for EVERY presentation is false even for U. No assertion about the minimum among all presentations follows from this one example.

3. The minimized-rank conditional proof is valid

Define

  bq(K)=min {dim_F2 qHFB(beta): closure(beta)=K}.

The set is nonempty and consists of positive finite integers, so its minimum exists. Taking all presentations, rather than a fixed braid or conjugacy class, makes this a knot invariant by definition even though qHFB itself is not Markov-invariant. The spectral sequence gives

  dim_F2 hat HFK(K) <= bq(K).

Proposed new hypothesis: for every whole Jones polynomial P there is a finite B(P) with bq(K)<=B(P) whenever V_K=P.

Assume this hypothesis and let V_K=1. For all m>=1, V_(#m K)=1, and the ordinary hat-HFK connected-sum theorem over F2 gives

  [dim_F2 hat HFK(K)]^m
      =dim_F2 hat HFK(#m K)
      <=bq(#m K)<=B(1).

Thus dim_F2 hat HFK(K)=1. Genus detection and Alexander-grading symmetry imply K is the unknot. No tensor-product theorem, connected-sum formula, or Markov invariance for qHFB is used. The finite constant B(1) need not be 1.

Scope: the minimized-rank boundedness hypothesis remains completely unproved. The quotient-ring observation does not imply it. A unit-fiber version alone would again be equivalent to Jones detection, rather than independent progress toward the conjecture.
