QUANTUM BRAID FLOER HOMOLOGY: A GLOBAL SCAFFOLD AND TWO EXACT OBSTRUCTIONS
1 November 2026 UTC. Derived analysis; no proof of Jones unknot detection.

New input examined
------------------
Anghel--Juhasz, Quantum braid Floer homology, arXiv:2601.08805v4,
7 September 2026:
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08805v4

Their braid invariant qHFB is over F_2, with a spectral sequence to
HFK-hat of the closure. Its two-variable Euler polynomial satisfies
  Omega_beta(x,1)=Delta_K(x),
  Omega_beta(x,-x^-1)=V_mirror(K)(x).
It is not invariant under conjugation or stabilization. These statements
are read from the preprint; its construction has not been independently
reproved here. The deductions below isolate what would still be needed.

1. A complete conditional proof using presentation minimization
--------------------------------------------------------------
For a knot K define
  m(K)=min{dim_F2 qHFB(beta): closure(beta)=K}.
Use the braid presentations covered by the paper, with at least two
strands. Every knot has such a presentation, so this minimum exists as
a positive integer. It is a knot invariant by its definition, even
though each individual qHFB(beta) depends on the braid presentation.

Proposed hypothesis Q (unproved):
For every whole Jones polynomial P there is a finite B(P) with
  V_K=P => m(K)<=B(P).

Let f(K)=dim_F2 HFK-hat(K). The spectral sequence gives f(K)<=m(K).
If V_K=1, then for every positive integer n, V_(K#...#K)=1. The HFK
connected-sum formula therefore gives
  f(K)^n=f(K#...#K)<=m(K#...#K)<=B(1).
It follows that f(K)=1, which forces K to be the unknot by knot Floer
genus detection (or its rank-one detection consequence).

No connected-sum formula for qHFB is used. The bound B(1) need not equal
one. Restricted to the Jones-one fiber, Q is equivalent to detection,
not an independently easier theorem. Its potential value would be a
geometric method for choosing braid representatives and cancelling
Floer generators. No such method has been obtained.

2. The invariant polynomial quotient has no extra mixed information
------------------------------------------------------------------
The paper's Section 9.2 gives a Markov-invariant quotient for Omega.
In its q convention put
  R=Z[x,x^-1,d,d^-1], I=((d-1)(xd+1)).
Consider
  R/I -> Z[x,x^-1] x Z[x,x^-1],
  F -> (F(x,1), F(x,-x^-1)).

This map is injective. Indeed, if both evaluations vanish, first
F=(d-1)G. Evaluating at d=-x^-1 gives
  -(x+1)x^-1 G(x,-x^-1)=0.
The Laurent polynomial ring is an integral domain, so G(x,-x^-1)=0,
and hence xd+1 divides G. Thus F belongs to I. The converse is immediate.

The image consists exactly of pairs (A,J) satisfying
  A(-1)=J(-1), equivalently A-J divisible by x+1.
Necessity follows because the two specializations meet at x=-1,d=1.
For sufficiency the explicit Laurent polynomial
  F=A+x(d-1)(A-J)/(x+1)
has the prescribed evaluations. Therefore the quotient carries exactly
the Alexander/Jones pair with its compatibility at -1. It supplies no
additional mixed polynomial datum that can automatically recover
Alexander or Floer information from Jones alone.

This is a direct commutative-algebra calculation, independently checked
by another agent. It does not assert the unquotiented braid invariant
or its homology has no additional information.

3. Actual unknot presentations already have extra quantum Floer classes
----------------------------------------------------------------------
All three braids below close to the unknot: sigma_1, its positive
stabilization sigma_1 sigma_2, and the conjugate
sigma_1^2 sigma_2 sigma_1^-1. Converting Section 9.2 to the q convention:

  A=Omega_(sigma_1)=xd^2-xd+d,
  B=Omega_(sigma_1 sigma_2)=x^2d^4-x^2d^3+xd^3-xd+d,
  C=Omega_(sigma_1^2 sigma_2 sigma_1^-1)
    =x^2d^2(1-d-x^-1)^2.

Both specializations of A,B,C are 1. Their coefficient l1 norms are
3,5,9, respectively. Since these are graded Euler polynomials, their
qHFB dimensions are at least 3,5,9. Thus V=1 does not force one quantum
Floer generator in every braid presentation. These are lower bounds;
we do not claim exact homology ranks from Euler polynomials.

Their differences factor as
  B-A=xd^2(d-1)(xd+1),
  C-B=-d(x-1)(d-1)(xd+1).
This exhibits precisely the mixed information lost in the invariant
quotient. The short verifier checks the identities and specializations.

The paper also gives a trefoil example where its distinguished class
Theta is nonzero on the qHFB page but dies in the spectral sequence.
Consequently its mere existence does not supply a permanent Floer
class or a rank-one theorem.

Scope
-----
The new construction provides genuine geometric objects on which one
could seek a cancellation theorem. It does not presently prove Q, and
its spectral sequence alone does not convert a Jones Euler identity
into a Floer rank bound. The polynomial quotient and the distinguished
class do not remove that missing step.
