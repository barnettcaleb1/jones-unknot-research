BRAID COUNTEREXAMPLE STRATEGY: COMPLETE CHAINS AND A STOP RULE
1 November 2026 UTC. Strategic audit of the full-proof scaffolds and
research24 notes. No new computation, novelty claim, or claim of proximity
to a solution.

Decision in one paragraph
-------------------------
The genuine generic Temperley--Lieb kernel route has a complete known
bridge from its algebraic target to nontrivial Jones-one knots. The
four-moment route has a cheaper certificate but currently TWO unresolved
construction requirements: an actual braid-image point on its exact
moment locus, and a nontrivial knot among its closures. Our spectral
lemma removes the only explicit formal diagonal family previously offered
as intuition for the first requirement. Prefer a structural kernel
construction if one is available; do not continue a word search merely
because four equations look smaller than a matrix identity.

A. Genuine kernel: a complete counterexample scaffold
----------------------------------------------------
Fix n and the FULL generic fundamental TL_n representation over Q(A),
with raw generator A I+A^{-1}e_i. The gates are:

1. Produce an actual braid word b with rho(b)=I as an exact Laurent
   identity on every TL block. A numerical or root-of-unity identity,
   one block alone, or an uncorrected projective scalar is insufficient.
2. Certify b != 1 as a braid, for example by a nonidentity image in the
   faithful Artin action on the free group. Identity of its permutation
   is not a nontriviality certificate.
3. Apply Ito's theorem to the nontrivial normal kernel and a specified
   unknot anchor. The raw kernel has exponent sum zero, so is not a
   nontrivial subgroup of the braid center. Its classical specialization
   makes its elements pure. Ito supplies kernel elements whose anchored
   closures are hyperbolic knots of arbitrarily large genus.
4. The exact kernel identity preserves the normalized Jones polynomial
   of the anchor. Purity preserves its one-component closure. Thus the
   knots supplied in step 3 are genuine counterexamples.

The only unknown existence premise is the nontrivial generic kernel in
steps 1--2. This premise is sufficient, not known necessary for Jones
failure. In B5, choosing b in the exact Burau kernel makes the 1D and
4D TL blocks identity; the remaining required identity is M=I in the
5D block. The cached Bigelow word fails this last identity.

If an explicit knot diagram is wanted, Ito's braid-order construction
can be executed and checked. Its existence theorem already closes the
mathematical implication; a second conjecture about closures is not
required. No statement about all colored Jones polynomials is used.

B. Four moments: the complete but narrower alternative
------------------------------------------------------
Take an actual B in the B5 Burau kernel, hence pure and exponent-zero.
Set C=sigma1 sigma2 sigma3 sigma4, M=rho5(B), Z=rho5(C). The exact TL
trace decomposition gives
  V_(closure(B^m C))(A^-4)-1 = A^-12 tr(Z M^m).
Here tr Z=0. The necessary and sufficient conditions for the ENTIRE
integer-power family to have Jones polynomial one are the four exact
identities tr(ZM^j)=0, j=1,2,3,4. Cayley--Hamilton supplies all other
powers, including negative ones.

The full counterexample scaffold therefore requires:
  (i) an actual braid B with the stated Burau identity;
 (ii) all four exact Laurent identities;
(iii) an independent certificate that closure(B^m C) is nontrivial for
      at least one integer m.
A sufficient certificate for (iii) is C B^m >_D Delta^2, giving positive
genus by Ito's bound. Other rigorous knot certificates can replace it.
Failure of this sufficient order test alone does not prove the closure
is the unknot.

The moment locus need not be a subgroup or normal. Consequently Ito's
normal-subgroup amplification cannot be imported at (iii). Taking
conjugates or products to increase braid order may destroy (ii), and
powers of one braid need not have unbounded Dehornoy floor.

This is a restricted counterexample search, so it needs no theorem
covering all knots. Conversely, excluding this family would not prove
generic unknot detection. Requiring all powers is stronger than asking
for one Jones-one closure; it is a certification device, not a necessary
condition on a counterexample.

C. What the spectral result changes
----------------------------------
An actual Burau-kernel braid lies in [P5,P5], so M=I+O((A-1)^2).
Every eigenvalue of M in Q(A) must equal 1. Complete splitting over Q(A)
therefore forces M=I, by fixed-level TL unitarity.

Thus a moment solution either already gives a genuine TL kernel, or
has a characteristic polynomial that does not split completely over
Q(A). It need not be irreducible. The formal diagonal monomial/clock
model cannot be realized by a nonidentity Burau-kernel residual image.
Density or arbitrary diagonalization does not repair this obstruction.

This is a necessary image constraint, not a construction method or a
proof that nonsplit moment solutions exist. The four equations remain
strictly weaker in unconstrained matrix algebra; we have not shown that
they are easier to solve in the actual braid image with condition (iii).
Calling them an easier reformulation of the original problem would be
unsupported. They describe a more structured sufficient subclass.

D. Minimal worthwhile next test, and when to stop
-------------------------------------------------
A useful kernel construction target is a CENTRALIZER MISMATCH. Start
with an actual nontrivial B in the Burau kernel and construct an actual
braid h such that
  [rho5(B),rho5(h)]=I, but [B,h] != 1 in B5.
Then [B,h] is in the Burau kernel and has identity residual block, hence
is a nontrivial full TL kernel element. Section A finishes the route.
The Artin-action and exact 5D matrix checks are finite, independently
checkable certificates. Linear algebra alone cannot lift a matrix in
the centralizer to an actual braid h.

This target is NOT a request to repeat the earlier short-conjugator and
commutator scans. Before any further expensive work, provide a new
geometric or representation-theoretic reason that predicts such a
mismatch and an actual word construction to test. If no such mechanism
is available, pause this branch. We currently have none certified.

For a supplied candidate, stop that candidate immediately if its exact
Artin commutator is trivial, or if a valid exact specialization makes
its residual commutator nonidentity. For a claimed parameterized family,
a proof that residual commutation implies actual braid commutation closes
that entire mechanism; a finite cutoff does not. An exact common-gcd-one
specialization can similarly close an entire proposed moment family, as
already done for all single-generator-power anchor conjugations of the
cached seed. Do not respond by enlarging the word cutoff without a new
structural idea. These are branch-specific stop conditions, not a proof
that no counterexample exists.

Relation to the global proof scaffolds
-------------------------------------
The homology and ribbon proposals in outputs/jones-full-proof-scaffolds.md
would prove detection if their global geometric premises were established.
The present route instead seeks one counterexample and therefore avoids
their coverage requirements. It does not remove the central difficulty:
exact generic representation nonfaithfulness, or an exact trace collision
with certified nontrivial topology. A restricted algebra lemma earns
further investment only when its conclusion feeds one of these complete
chains without an additional unnamed assumption.
