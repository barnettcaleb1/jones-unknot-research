GEOMETRIC INTERSECTIONS AT THE JONES SPECIALIZATION
1 November 2026 UTC. Bounded pass24; research deductions, no novelty claim.

Outcome
-------
The naive route “opposite polynomial contributions imply a removable bigon”
fails on actual geometric objects, and the smallest quantum-Floer unknot
already requires cancellations that an ordinary Floer differential cannot
perform. There is nevertheless a precise extremal-intersection certificate
for nontrivial Jones polynomial. Its geometric coverage, not its algebra,
is the missing theorem. None of the following proves unknot detection.

1. The cancellation has the wrong parity for a Floer bigon
---------------------------------------------------------
Use the conventions of Anghel--Juhasz, Quantum braid Floer homology,
arXiv:2601.08805v4, Sections 6 and 9:
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08805v4
Their graded Euler polynomial is Omega(x,d), and the Jones specialization
is d=-x^-1. A generator of degrees (A,Q,M) therefore contributes
  (-1)^(M+Q) x^(A-Q)
to Jones, rather than the usual Euler sign (-1)^M.

For the actual unknot braid sigma_1 the paper gives
  Omega = x d^2 - x d + d.
The cancelling Jones contributions are x d^2 and d: their ordinary Euler
signs agree, their A degrees differ by one, and their Q degrees differ by
one. They cannot be directly cancelled by a basepoint-free index-one HFK
disk, which preserves A and reverses Maslov parity. A quantum differential
also preserves Q. Here the displayed coefficients are net Euler blocks;
we are not inferring exact homology ranks from the coefficients.

There is a different possible HFK cancellation, between x d^2 and -x d,
which preserves A and changes Q. It does not cancel a pair of Jones terms.
Thus “run the HFK spectral sequence until only the Jones terms remain” is
not a valid operation. The trefoil example in Section 9.1 makes this actual:
qHFB has five generators, HFK has three, and the intervening disk changes Q.

For three actual unknot presentations the Euler coefficient norms are
3,5,9. Those are lower bounds for their qHFB dimensions, not proven equalities.
Any useful minimum-presentation result must allow changes of braid
presentation. Cancelling acyclic pairs inside one fixed quantum complex
cannot lower that complex's homology rank.

2. Actual essential arcs can have identically zero weighted intersection
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Bigelow, The Burau representation is not faithful for n=5,
Geometry & Topology 3 (1999), Theorem 1.4 and Section 3:
https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~bigelow/publications/02.pdf
constructs embedded arcs in a five-punctured disk that cannot be homotoped
apart relative to endpoints, but whose Laurent weighted intersection is
zero. Put them in minimal position: they still intersect, and have no
removable empty bigon. This is an actual geometric counterexample to
weighted-intersection cancellation implying geometric cancellation.

The example is the one-point/Burau model, not a Jones-one knot and not a
counterexample to a special theorem about Jones fork systems. Its lesson
is precise: equal abelian winding labels do not give a null-homotopic
Whitney boundary. Retaining nonabelian path or Nielsen-class labels could
test that obstruction, but these labels are not recovered from Jones.

3. The existing geometric positivity proof degenerates exactly at Jones
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Bigelow, Braid groups are linear, JAMS 14 (2001), Sections 2.3 and 3:
https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/bigelow2.pdf
proves that the unspecialized fork--noodle pairing detects intersection.
In minimal position its lexicographically maximal q^a t^b terms have a
common sign. Separately, passage from a proper fork surface to a closed
homology cycle multiplies it by
  (1-q)^2(1+qt).
The proof cancels this nonzero factor over Z[q^+-1,t^+-1].

Bigelow's actual Jones formula uses t=-q^-1, precisely where 1+qt vanishes:
A homological definition of the Jones polynomial, Theorem 2.8,
https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~bigelow/publications/06.pdf
Consequently the unspecialized argument cannot simply be base-changed:
its normalizing factor becomes zero, and lexicographic extremality need
not survive the projection (a,b) -> a-b. This does NOT prove that any
particular specialized representation is unfaithful. It identifies a
specific invalid step in trying to import the faithful-representation proof.

4. A valid geometric certificate: surviving extreme faces
---------------------------------------------------------
For a transverse Bigelow plat diagram let the actual intersection points p
have signs epsilon_p and winding labels (a_p,b_p). Put
  j_p=a_p-b_p,  s_p=epsilon_p (-1)^b_p.
Let j_minus and j_plus be the minimum and maximum of the j_p.

PROPOSITION. If the signs s_p are constant among the points attaining
j_minus, and also constant among the points attaining j_plus, then
  span V_K = j_plus-j_minus-1.
In particular j_plus-j_minus>=2 certifies V_K!=1.

Proof. Neither extreme coefficient can cancel after t=-q^-1, so the
specialized intersection polynomial has exactly that span. The Jones
formula multiplies this polynomial by a Laurent monomial and divides
by -(q^(1/2)+q^(-1/2)), of span one. Laurent polynomial spans add under
multiplication. This proves the assertion, including the normalization.

The hypothesis is a condition on winding numbers and intersection signs;
one need not compute any interior coefficient. A sufficient version is
that each extreme face consists of just one intersection. For the
two-variable fork--noodle model, Bigelow's positive maximal monomial also
survives if no other exponent pair projects to its a-b value. These are
checkable certificates, not generic coverage theorems.

Analogously, for Omega(x,d), extreme coherent faces of A-Q give span V
without the extra subtraction of one (Omega is already normalized).
For the paper's trefoil:
  Omega=x^-1 d^3-d^3+x d^3-x^2 d^3+x^2 d^4.
The projected extreme degrees -4 and -1 each have one contribution.
They survive, while the two terms at degree -2 cancel. Thus the extreme
certificate detects it despite an actual interior Jones cancellation.
For sigma_1 the lower extreme degree -1 contains opposite specialized
signs, so the hypothesis fails, as it must for this unknot example.

5. Exact complete conditional route, and where it remains open
------------------------------------------------------------
A proposed geometric-normal-form theorem would have to say more than
“Jones-one knots have bounded homology.” One concrete possible statement:

  Every plat can be changed by the actual plat-preserving moves to either
  an explicit one-bridge unknot, or a diagram satisfying the extreme-face
  condition above with projected width at least two.

If proved, this yields detection immediately: the second output has
nontrivial Jones by the proposition; the first has an actual unknot
isotopy. Its moves must be the full plat moves (handlebody braid actions
and stabilization), not arbitrary alterations of the fork surfaces.

This is an unproved strong geometric coverage statement, not a result
obtained here and not evidence that every knot has such a normal form.
An extremal-coherence assertion solely for minimal-position arcs is too
weakly motivated: the Burau example shows that absence of bigons does
not control abelian cancellation, and the quantum unknot already shows
that Jones cancellation is different from differential cancellation.

A falsifiable local research target is to retain the actual path classes
between extreme cancelling intersections and ask which of them can be
changed by a specified plat-preserving move to expose an extreme face.
Each proposed move must come with (i) an endpoint-marked isotopy, (ii) a
strictly decreasing well-founded diagram complexity, and (iii) a proof
that the path obstruction has actually disappeared. Equal winding number
alone establishes none of these. We have no descent theorem of this kind.

In particular, formal fork/skein relations are not substitutes for those
plat-preserving moves: a relation in an intersection module can change
the actual embedded tangle. This is the same realizability distinction
that blocked the earlier formal tangle candidates, now with a published
embedded-arc stress test available.

For the minimum-rank question, a normal form that explicitly identifies
the unknot would yield the finite bound m(K)<=dim qHFB(sigma_1) on the
Jones-one fiber. We do not need to assert that this fixed finite number
is three. Merely assuming such a fiber bound without the geometric
normal-form construction is the earlier unproved scaffold in new notation.

6. Why adding a transverse derivative is not free extra information
------------------------------------------------------------------
One might try to recover the lost factor 1+qt by a divided difference
normal to the Jones locus. The actual quantum-braid examples obstruct
assuming such data is automatically a knot invariant. With A,B denoting
the sigma_1 and sigma_1 sigma_2 polynomials, respectively,
  B-A = x d^2(d-1)(xd+1).
The polynomials have the same Jones specialization, while their first
derivatives normal to xd+1=0 differ by a nonzero Laurent polynomial.
The presentations are related by an actual Markov stabilization.
Therefore a repair via nearby-cycle/derived information would first
need its own presentation-invariance theorem and a bridge back to Jones.
Explicitly, setting z=xd+1 makes the derivative of B-A at z=0 equal to
  -x^-2-x^-1,
which is nonzero. The accompanying standard-library verifier checks this
factorization, both specializations of the three unknot examples, their
Euler norms, and the trefoil's surviving extreme coefficients.

Next-action conclusion
----------------------
Do not invest in a general empty-bigon cancellation claim: it has a
published geometric counterexample and a separate parity obstruction.
The viable geometric problem is presentation change at the resonant
specialization, with nonabelian path information retained until the move
is certified. The extremal-face proposition supplies a clean success
certificate for such a move, but generic coverage is still unproved.
