GLOBAL GEOMETRIC STRATEGY AUDIT
1 November 2026 UTC. Six-minute bird's-eye pass. No claim of novelty or proof.

Recommendation
--------------
Retain whole-Jones homology boundedness as the main conditional scaffold.
Do not promote fixed-diagram intersection positivity to a separate proof
route: the previous pass found exact obstructions to it. A genuinely
different geometric bridge would control the MINIMUM PLAT bridge number,
not closed-braid strand count. The latter proposal has a published
counterexample, found in this pass.

1. One complete alternative conditional proof
---------------------------------------------
Let b(K) be the classical bridge number, equivalently half the minimum
number of strands in a plat presentation of K.

Proposed hypothesis B-plat (UNPROVED): for each whole Jones polynomial P
there is a finite B(P) such that V_K=P implies b(K)<=B(P), for all knots K.

Conditional theorem. B-plat implies Jones unknot detection.
Proof. If V_K=1, multiplicativity gives V_(K#...#K)=1. Schubert's exact
connected-sum formula then gives
  n(b(K)-1)+1 = b(K^{#n}) <= B(1)
for all n. Therefore b(K)=1, and K is the unknot. This uses no promotion
to colored Jones data, no categorification, and no unknotting-one reduction.
Primary source for the additivity theorem and the bridge-one endpoint:
Schultens, Additivity of bridge numbers of knots (2003),
https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~jcs/pubs/addbri15.pdf

Logical status: restricting B-plat to P=1 is equivalent to the original
conjecture, so that restriction is not progress. The all-polynomial claim
is broader and independently falsifiable on known nonunit Jones fibers.
We have not established its literature status or truth. Bounds restricted
to prime knots would not justify the displayed connected-sum proof.

2. Physics inspiration, with the mathematical content separated
----------------------------------------------------------------
A plat expression is a vacuum-pair-creation / braid-evolution /
pair-annihilation matrix element. Its bridge number measures the least
number of simultaneous pairs needed in that preparation geometry. The
idea is to seek a bound on that geometric resource from the full response
polynomial, while allowing arbitrarily long evolution. This is an analogy
to state-preparation resources, not a physical law or positivity theorem.

Mathematically this means looking for legitimate isotopies and plat moves
which lower the number of cups, with the Jones polynomial constraining
when the reduction process must stop. The actual proposed theorem is
B-plat above. Neither a small matrix element nor a small Euler index
implies a small resource count in a general quantum system; knot geometry
would have to supply the missing restriction.

This would bypass the homology-rank question rather than prove it.
Bounded bridge number alone does not bound Floer/Khovanov rank, nor does
the present argument need such an implication.

3. Decisive published obstruction to the nearby braid-width proposal
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Replacing b(K) by minimum closed-braid strand count makes the proposed
whole-polynomial bound FALSE.

Takioka, On the Braid Index of Kanenobu Knots, Kyungpook Math. J. 55
(2015), 169--180, Theorem 1.1, gives knots k(n) with fixed HOMFLYPT
polynomial
  P(k(n);v,z)=(v^-2-1+v^2-z^2)^2,
and hence fixed Jones polynomial, but
  braid(k(n)) >= n+3  for n>=3.
Primary paper, DOI 10.5666/KMJ.2015.55.1.169:
https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/1cfd/410a7f28da8ead9f610c18d6bcce6b0886cf.pdf

Takioka's theorem concerns closed-braid index and does not by itself
determine the bridge numbers of these knots. We have not directly
verified a bridge-number claim, including parameter exceptions, in
Kanenobu's original primary paper: the AMS PDF was inaccessible during
this check. Accordingly this note makes no exact or uniform bridge-number
claim for Takioka's k(n). Its unbounded braid index alone neither proves
nor disproves B-plat.

This is a substantive restriction: no strategy may bound the minimum
particle count in the closed-braid/trace model from the whole Jones
polynomial. Minimizing over all Markov presentations does not repair that
statement. Bounded homology survives this test because geometric strand
count and homological rank are different quantities.

4. One decisive next test; no local calculation proposed now
-----------------------------------------------------------
Before attempting a geometric proof of B-plat, try to falsify it on a
known NONUNIT constant-Jones family with genuinely growing topology.
A family already equipped with plats of uniformly bounded strand count
cannot do this. Such a bound must be checked for the exact family and
parameters. Conversely, an unbounded closed-braid index or a schematic
twist diagram is not a certificate of unbounded bridge number.

A precise sufficient falsification certificate would be actual knots K_m
with one fixed nonunit Jones polynomial P_0, together with surjections
  pi_1(S^3 minus K_m) -> S_m
sending meridians to transpositions, for unbounded m. Then b(K_m)>=m-1:
the group of a b-bridge knot has b meridional generators; transpositions
generating S_m give a connected graph on m vertices and require at least
m-1 edges. This lower-bound argument does not assume the meridional-rank
conjecture. Any growing finite quotient with a comparable lower bound on
the number of meridional generators would also suffice.

This is a proposed kill test, not a claimed family or quotient. It asks
for a genuine geometric witness in a nonunit fiber and does not invoke a
hypothetical Jones counterexample. A positive result would terminate
B-plat while leaving H-rank open. A failure to find one would not support
B-plat, and should not trigger a prolonged search without a concrete
growing-template construction.

5. Why the earlier shortcuts do not fill the remaining gap
---------------------------------------------------------
The companion intersection_geometry.txt proves only a sufficient extreme-
face certificate for nontrivial Jones, not that every nontrivial knot has
such a presentation. Essential arcs can have zero abelian weighted
intersection; equal winding labels do not provide a removable bigon.
The Jones specialization also kills the factor used in Bigelow's generic
intersection-detection argument. Quantum Floer unknot presentations can
have Euler norms 3,5,9, so a presentation must be selected geometrically.

Nor can a replica/swap trick simply remove the Euler signs. For a graded
complex the chain-compatible transposition carries the Koszul sign.
The unsigned flip is not a chain map in general: take du=v with degrees
|u|=0, |v|=1. On u tensor v, unsigned-flip-after-d gives v tensor v,
whereas d-after-unsigned-flip gives minus v tensor v. Over Q these differ.
A positive ordinary trace of homology would require extra structure;
it is not produced by replacing the signed trace in a local diagram.
This elementary obstruction does not forbid a separately constructed
global operation, but such an operation would need its own invariance
and Jones-control theorem.

Priority assessment
-------------------
B-plat supplies a clean full conditional chain and a useful distinction
between two geometric notions of width. The new proved information is
the failure of the closed-braid-width version, not evidence for B-plat.
Keep B-plat as one falsifiable side hypothesis. Shelve a generic
bigon/positivity proof until a real plat-preserving reduction with a
well-founded complexity is exhibited; otherwise it only renames the
desired implication.
