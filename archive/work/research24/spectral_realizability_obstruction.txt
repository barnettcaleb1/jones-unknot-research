SPECTRAL REALIZABILITY OBSTRUCTION FOR THE FOUR-MOMENT ROUTE
1 November 2026 UTC. Derived lemma with two independent audits.
No novelty claim, no faithfulness theorem, no Jones-one knot construction.

Setup
-----
Let R=Z[A,A^-1], F=Q(A), and let rho be an actual integral Laurent
Temperley--Lieb module of a fixed braid group B_n. Both rho(beta) and
its inverse have entries in R. Use rho(sigma_i)=A I+A^-1 e_i,
e_i^2=(-A^2-A^-2)e_i. The application is the five-dimensional TL5 block
left over after the Burau and one-dimensional blocks have been removed.

THEOREM. If beta belongs to [P_n,P_n] and the characteristic polynomial
of M=rho(beta) splits completely over F, then M=I. More precisely, any
individual eigenvalue of M that belongs to F equals one. Thus a nonidentity
M must have at least one eigenvalue outside F. This does not assert that
its characteristic polynomial is irreducible.

Proof
-----
1. At A=1, the braid generators square to the identity:
   (I+e_i)^2=I, because e_i^2=-2e_i. Thus the representation factors
   through S_n, and every pure braid has value I at A=1.

   For matrices I+(A-1)X+O((A-1)^2), multiplication adds the first
   derivatives. Hence the first derivative on P_n is a homomorphism
   into an additive matrix space, and vanishes on [P_n,P_n]. Therefore
     M=I+(A-1)^2 N(A), with N regular at A=1.

2. If an eigenvalue lambda lies in F, the monic characteristic polynomial
   makes it integral over R. Its reciprocal is an eigenvalue of M^-1,
   so lambda^-1 is also integral over R. The Laurent UFD R is integrally
   closed; both therefore belong to R. Its units are exactly +/-A^k.
   Specialization at A=1 gives lambda(1)=1, so lambda=A^k.

3. Suppose k is nonzero and put h=A-1. Then
     lambda I-M = k h I+O(h^2).
   In dimension d its determinant has nonzero leading term k^d h^d.
   This contradicts lambda being an eigenvalue. Hence k=0 and lambda=1.
   No analytic eigenvector or simultaneous diagonalization was assumed.

4. If the characteristic polynomial splits, it is (z-1)^d; M is
   unipotent. At a fixed strand number, the TL modules have positive
   unitary specializations on an open unit-circle arc near A=1. To see
   the sign convention, put f_i=-e_i and d=A^2+A^-2=2cos(2theta).
   In the positive path model f_i is self-adjoint and f_i^2=d f_i.
   For A=e^(i theta),
     (A I-A^-1 f_i)^*(A I-A^-1 f_i)=I-d f_i+f_i^2=I.
   For n=5, the path weights sin(2j theta)/sin(2theta), through height
   six, are positive on the conservative arc |theta|<pi/12, with
   positive limiting weights at theta=0. This is a fixed-level arc;
   no positivity interval for all strand numbers at once is claimed.

   A unitary unipotent matrix is the identity. Thus M(A)=I on infinitely
   many A in this arc. Its Laurent entries then imply M=I identically.
   This proves the theorem.

Why it applies to the generic Burau kernel
-----------------------------------------
Use unreduced Burau matrices with sigma_i block [[1-t,t],[1,0]]. At t=1
they are adjacent permutation matrices, so a kernel braid is pure.
For a standard pure generator A_ij, the derivative at t=1 is
  L_ij=(e_i-e_j)(e_i-e_j)^T.
Indeed sigma_i^2 has derivative [[1,-1],[-1,1]], and conjugation at t=1
permutes its two coordinates. The matrices L_ij are linearly independent:
the (i,j) off-diagonal entry singles out its coefficient. Since the
standard A_ij freely generate the abelianization of P_n, a Burau-kernel
braid has all abelianization coordinates zero. It lies in [P_n,P_n].

Thus for the residual TL5 block of a Burau-kernel braid,
  characteristic polynomial completely split over Q(A)  <=>  M=I.
The reverse implication is immediate. M=I here would give a genuine
generic TL kernel because the other two blocks already act as the identity. Whether
a nontrivial braid satisfying this exists remains unresolved.

What this removes from the construction search
----------------------------------------------
The formal matrices
  M0=diag(A^4,A^-4,A^8,A^-8,1)
and a weighted five-cycle Z0 with Z0^5=A^-12 I satisfy
tr(Z0 M0^m)=0 for every integer m, have det(M0)=1, and are unitary on
the unit circle. Nevertheless M0 cannot be the residual matrix of a
generic Burau-kernel braid: already its first derivative at A=1 is
diag(4,-4,8,-8,0), not zero. The theorem rules out ALL completely split
rational-eigenvalue replacements, not just this example.

Nonconstant rational functions adjusted to have value one and derivative
zero do not evade the argument: a rational eigenvalue and its inverse
would have to be integral over R, hence Laurent units. Matrix integrality
is an essential realizability condition missing from a free GL5 model.
The surviving options are a genuine kernel or a nonsplit algebraic
spectrum satisfying the four moments plus an independent knot-genus
certificate. Neither has been constructed.

A second, purely geometric symmetry obstruction
-----------------------------------------------
Let C=sigma_1...sigma_4. Its braid centralizer in B5 is generated by C.
This is Gonzalez-Meneses--Wiest, Proposition 3.3 (the relatively prime
rotation case):
https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1016/j.ansens.2004.04.002.pdf

A Burau-kernel braid has exponent sum zero, since its Burau determinant
is (-t) to that exponent. But exponent(C)=4. Therefore the intersection
of the Burau kernel with the centralizer of any braid-conjugate of C is
trivial. Requiring a candidate to commute with an actual primitive
periodic braid cannot implement a nontrivial clock/shift symmetry model.
Commutation of representation matrices is weaker than braid commutation;
no faithfulness assumption is inserted here.
