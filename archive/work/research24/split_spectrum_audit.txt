INDEPENDENT AUDIT: SPLIT SPECTRUM OF A BURAU-KERNEL BRAID
2026-10-31. Scope: generic B_5 and its standard five-dimensional
Temperley--Lieb module. Verdict: the proposed lemma is valid, with the
scope qualifications below. This is an internal proof audit, not a
novelty claim or an unknot-detection result.

Statement.
Let B lie in the kernel of the unreduced Burau representation of B_5
over Z[t,t^{-1}]. Let M=rho_5(B), where rho_5 is the standard integral
five-dimensional Temperley--Lieb representation with
  rho(sigma_i)=A I+A^{-1}e_i,  e_i^2=delta e_i,
  delta=-A^2-A^{-2}.
Then every eigenvalue of M which belongs to Q(A) is 1. If the
characteristic polynomial of M splits completely over Q(A), then M=I.
Equivalently, M != I implies that its characteristic polynomial does
not split completely over Q(A).

1. The Burau kernel lies in [P_5,P_5].

Use the local generator matrix [[1-t,t],[1,0]], as in the exact code
work/pass2/braid_kernel.py. At t=1 it is an adjacent transposition.
Consequently a Burau-kernel braid is pure.

For a pure braid beta, the derivative D(beta)=Burau(beta)'(1) is an
additive homomorphism, because every pure braid evaluates to I. For a
standard pure generator A_ij, direct differentiation gives
  D(A_ij)=L_ij,
where L_ij has diagonal entries +1 at i,j, off-diagonal entries -1 at
(i,j),(j,i), and zero elsewhere. Indeed for sigma_i^2 the derivative
is [[1,-1],[-1,1]]; conjugating to A_ij conjugates this matrix by the
specialized permutation. The derivative contributions of conjugator
and inverse cancel. The ten L_ij are Z-linearly independent, as each
off-diagonal position detects its corresponding coefficient.

The abelianization of P_5 is Z^{10}, with A_ij as its standard basis
(the pairwise linking numbers). Hence D is injective on this
abelianization. A Burau-kernel braid has D(B)=0, so B is in [P_5,P_5].
This calculation fixes the possible factor-of-two issue: A_ij has
linking number one, and the derivative above is exactly L_ij.

2. The residual matrix has zero first jet at A=1.

All generator matrices and their inverses have entries in
R=Z[A,A^{-1}]. At A=1, delta=-2 and (I+e_i)^2=I, so the specialized
braid representation factors through the symmetric group. Thus every
pure braid maps to I. As in step 1, its first derivative is additive
on the pure subgroup and therefore vanishes on [P_5,P_5]. It follows
that
  M = I + (A-1)^2 N(A),
with N regular at A=1. This step does not require faithfulness of the
classical specialized representation.

3. A rational eigenvalue must be 1.

If lambda belongs to Q(A) and is an eigenvalue of M, the monic
characteristic polynomial makes lambda integral over R. The inverse
matrix also has entries in R, so lambda^{-1} is integral over R.
The Laurent polynomial ring R is integrally closed and has fraction
field Q(A). Therefore lambda and lambda^{-1} belong to R, making
lambda a unit of R. Its form is thus +/- A^k for an integer k.

Since M(1)=I, lambda(1)=1 and the sign is positive. If k were nonzero,
the determinant of
  M-A^k I = -k(A-1)I + O((A-1)^2)
would have nonzero leading term (-k)^5(A-1)^5. This contradicts that
lambda=A^k is an eigenvalue identically. Hence k=0 and lambda=1.

If all roots are rational, the characteristic polynomial is (X-1)^5.
Cayley--Hamilton then gives (M-I)^5=0.

4. Unitarity eliminates nonidentity unipotents.

Put E_i=-e_i and d=-delta=A^2+A^{-2}. For A on the unit circle near
1, d is real and near 2. The fixed finite-dimensional TL_5 algebra
has a positive faithful Markov trace form at d=2. For example, its
standard faithful action on (C^2)^{tensor 5}, with E_i twice the
antisymmetric projection on factors i,i+1, realizes this trace as a
positive scalar multiple of the ordinary matrix trace. All three
TL_5 simple modules occur. Equivalently one can use the positive
Jones--Wenzl/path basis at d=2.

The Gram matrix is finite and depends continuously on d. Its strict
positivity therefore persists on a neighborhood of d=2. This provides
positive inner products on the fixed TL_5 simple modules, in
particular the five-dimensional module, for a sufficiently small
open arc of A around 1. In that inner product E_i is self-adjoint and
  (A I-A^{-1}E_i)^*(A I-A^{-1}E_i)
    = I-(A^2+A^{-2})E_i+E_i^2 = I.
Thus M(A) is unitary on this arc. A unitary matrix whose sole
eigenvalue is 1 is the identity. Since every Laurent entry of M-I
vanishes on an open arc, every entry vanishes identically.

Source for the positivity framework: Aaserud--Evans, Realizing the
braided Temperley--Lieb--Jones C*-tensor categories as Hilbert
C*-modules, arXiv:1908.02674, sections 2.4.1 and 2.4.3.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.02674
Their full-tower positivity statement has discrete parameters below
2. The open interval used here is instead the elementary continuity
argument for the ONE FIXED finite algebra TL_5; no full-tower open
unitary interval is asserted.

Consequences and limits.

* “Does not split completely” is the precise conclusion. Irreducibility
  is not claimed: some roots could equal 1 while others are algebraic
  over Q(A).
* The formal diagonal monomial models for all-power trace cancellation
  cannot be the nonidentity residual image of a Burau-kernel braid.
  The first-order pure-braid constraint already rules their nontrivial
  monomial eigenvalues out.
* Nonsplit eigenvalues remain a genuine possibility. Nothing here rules
  out the four-moment trace locus with algebraic eigenvalues.
* M=I is not asserted to imply B=1. Since the other generic TL_5 blocks
  are already identity on a Burau-kernel braid, M=I would put B in the
  entire generic TL_5 representation kernel. Its braid nontriviality
  remains a separate topological/algebraic issue.
* The same proof works for any fixed standard integral TL_n module and
  B in the unreduced Burau kernel of B_n. The finite-n positive arc may
  shrink with n. No statement about every colored representation or
  every root of unity follows.
