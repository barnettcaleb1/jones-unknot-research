WHOLE-JONES BRIDGE BOUNDEDNESS: GLOBAL SCAFFOLD AND A NEW SURGERY TEST
1 November 2026. Bounded primary-source investigation; no novelty claim.

VERDICT

No counterexample to whole-Jones bridge boundedness was found. This is a
search outcome, not a theorem or a claim that the literature has none.
The useful new test is an explicit annulus-twist family with PROVEN
unbounded bridge number. Its ordinary Jones behavior remains uncomputed
here. The second Jones derivative is provably constant in that family,
so the first sensible derivative test is the third-order invariant.

1. COMPLETE CONDITIONAL PROOF ARCHITECTURE (PRIOR WORK, NOT NEW)

B-plat: for every Laurent polynomial P there is a finite B(P) such that
every classical knot K with V_K=P has bridge number b(K)<=B(P).

If V_K=1, multiplicativity gives V_(K#^r)=1 for every r. Schubert's exact
formula then gives

                 r(b(K)-1)+1 = b(K#^r) <= B(1).

Hence b(K)=1 and K is the unknot. This is an end-to-end implication,
with no categorification, colored-Jones promotion, or generic-bridge
assumption hidden in it. The restriction of B-plat to P=1 is equivalent
to Jones unknot detection, however. It is not a simpler established
lemma. The all-P version is stronger and independently falsifiable at a
nonunit polynomial. A prime-knot-only bound would not justify this proof.

Primary modern proof of Schubert's results:
J. Schultens, Additivity of Bridge Numbers of Knots,
https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~jcs/pubs/addbri15.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0111032

These observations, the finite-template lemma below, and Stoimenow's
historical Question8.1 were already recorded in
work/research21/fiber_bound_audit.txt. They are not pass25 discoveries.

2. THE FIXED-TEMPLATE BARRIER, NOW WITH A SHARP PRIMARY BOUND

For a fixed knot/tangle wiring containing finitely many braid boxes of
fixed strand counts, arbitrary words inserted in those boxes give a
uniform upper bound on bridge number. Put each box in its own fixed
height slab. The strands in the slab are monotone, so all maxima occur
in the fixed exterior wiring. Fixed local knots in the wiring cause a
fixed additional number of maxima. This is an elementary geometric
argument; it does not require a Jones identity.

For ordinary two-strand twist regions there is also a published linear
bound: Blair--Kjuchukova--Velazquez--Villanueva, Wirtinger systems of
generators of knot groups, CAG28(2020), Corollary1.4, gives

                         b(K) <= 2 t(K)

for nontrivial knots, with t the usual minimum diagram twist number.
Theorem1.3 identifies their diagrammatic Wirtinger number with bridge
number. This corroborates rather than replaces our earlier argument.
https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cag/2020/0028/0002/CAG-2020-0028-0002-a002.pdf

Consequences: fixed-input Kanenobu/Watson twist orbits cannot falsify
B-plat merely by increasing their twist exponents. We make NO exact
three-bridge assertion about Takioka's Kanenobu k(n): that assertion was
not independently verified from the original diagram/source in pass24.
Takioka's proven unbounded CLOSED BRAID INDEX at fixed HOMFLYPT does not
contradict bounded bridge number. Those are different minima.

Important scope correction: a fixed SURGERY diagram is not a fixed
braid-box presentation. Nor does bounded hyperbolic volume bound bridge
number. Section3 gives an actual family separating these notions.

3. A GENUINELY DIFFERENT GLOBAL TEST: ANNULUS TWISTING

Luecke--Osoinach, Infinitely many knots admitting the same integer
surgery, arXiv1407.1529v1, Definition0.1/Theorem0.2, provide an explicit
four-component link L=k union l1 union l2 union l3 in Figure1. Their
parameters are

              k_n^m = L(*, -1/m, 1/m, -1/n).

For each fixed n!=0, bridge number tends to infinity as m tends to
infinity, while the n-surgeries agree by orientation-preserving maps.
The n=0 row instead has bounded bridge number. The proof says the
twisting annulus meets k four times geometrically and zero algebraically;
after the l3 surgery its two boundary components have linking number n.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.1529

Figures1 and3 were visually inspected, and the parameter conventions
and the bounded n=0 exception were checked against the proof. The public
PDF and inspected page images are saved in this directory. No PD code
has been transcribed or verified. Do not infer one from the sketch.

Underlying primary bridge-growth mechanism:
Baker--Gordon--Luecke, Bridge number and integral Dehn surgery,
AGT16(2016)1--40, Theorem1.1 and Corollary1.4.
https://msp.org/agt/2016/16-1/agt-v16-n1-p01-s.pdf
Its catching-surface and annulus hypotheses matter; it is not a theorem
that every annulus twist makes bridge number grow.

Exact falsification target: find a fixed n!=0 and an infinite set of
positive m on which V_(k_n^m) is one polynomial. The bridge theorem then
immediately falsifies B-plat, with no new bridge computation needed.
An equality of surgery manifolds, Alexander data, or a few Jones values
does NOT establish this target. No Jones-constancy theorem or explicit
Jones formula for this family was located in the focused source search.

4. A DEFINITE EARLY KILL-TEST CORRECTION: v2 CANNOT VARY

For a fixed nonzero integer n, the Casson--Walker surgery formula has
the form

        lambda(S3_n K) = lambda(L(n,1)) + c_n a2(K),

where c_n is a fixed nonzero normalization-dependent rational number.
The orientation-preserving n-surgery identifications above therefore
force a2(k_n^m) to be independent of m. For V_U=1, the standard relation
V_K''(1)=-6 a2(K) shows that the SECOND Jones derivative is also constant.
This is a deduction from the surgery theorem, not a computation of the
full Alexander or Jones polynomial. No constancy of a4 or v3 follows.

An explicit primary use of the surgery formula, in doubled normalization,
is Ni--Zhang, Finite Dehn surgeries on knots in S3, equation(6.2):
https://www.math.buffalo.edu/~xinzhang/half-integral-surgery%209.pdf
The precise overall normalization cancels in the constancy deduction.

The next proposed finite test is therefore v3 (equivalently the third
Jones derivative after subtracting the known v2 term), obtained from a
verified diagram or a justified surgery/Gauss-diagram formula. A formula
which is a nonconstant polynomial in m would imply that EACH fixed Jones
polynomial occurs only finitely often in that row, ruling out not just
whole-row constancy but every infinite constant-Jones subsequence.

Remaining gates are concrete: transcribe and independently verify
Figure1; implement its rational surgeries with the stated ambient S3
identification; derive the all-m formula and a degree bound rather than
interpolate a guessed polynomial. None of these were completed here.
In particular, we have not asserted that v3 is polynomial in m merely
because this is called twisting. This is not an ordinary fixed-strand
disk-twist family, and importing that finite-difference proof unchecked
would recreate the geometric mistake noted in Section2.

5. A SECOND, EXACT SUFFICIENT COUNTEREXAMPLE MECHANISM

Suppose an actual winding-zero knot pattern P in a solid torus has
positive geometric wrapping number w, and its full Kauffman-bracket
skein class is a scalar
multiple of the empty diagram. Use zero-framed satellites throughout.
Then skein substitution gives one Jones polynomial for P(J), independent
of the companion J. The framing correction is companion-independent:
companion crossings occur in opposite signed pairs for winding-zero P;
equivalently the satellite framing change is winding(P)^2 times the
companion framing. Winding zero is included as an explicit hypothesis;
we need not deduce it from the scalar skein condition.

For nontrivial companions, Schubert gives b(P(J))>=w b(J). Taking J to
be connected powers of a trefoil yields an unbounded-bridge constant
Jones family. Thus an essential winding-zero SCALAR annular knot pattern
would refute B-plat even if P(U) is a nontrivial knot and its Jones
polynomial is not1. This does not require a Jones-one knot as input.

No actual such pattern was found. This is a sufficient construction
criterion, not an existence result. Ren's previously studied wrapping4,
annular-degree2 example does NOT satisfy it. Pairwise equal Jones
polynomials of satellites of mutants likewise do not suffice: varying
the pattern may vary their common polynomial.

Primary proof of the needed satellite bridge inequality is again
Schultens' paper linked in Section1. The scalar skein substitution is
the usual linear satellite evaluation, and the logical combination
above is our deduction rather than a priority claim.

6. WHAT SURVIVES THE AUDIT

The full conditional scaffold remains logically sound but unproved.
The already studied twist templates offer no generic coverage. The
annulus-twist row is a meaningful new test precisely because its bridge
growth is independently certified. The first informative low-degree
Jones invariant is v3, since surgery forces v2 to be constant. An exact
nonconstant v3 formula would eliminate that row as a B-plat falsifier;
an exact constant whole Jones polynomial on an infinite row would
disprove B-plat. Neither outcome was established in this pass.
