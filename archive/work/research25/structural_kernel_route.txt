PASS 25: STRUCTURAL KERNEL MECHANISMS AND TWO OBSTRUCTIONS
1 November 2026 UTC. Bounded conceptual pass, with no braid-word scan.
No nontrivial generic Jones kernel or Jones-one knot was constructed.

Complete endpoint retained throughout
-------------------------------------
For B in the generic B5 Burau kernel, a braid h satisfying
  [rho5(B),rho5(h)]=I,  [B,h] != 1
would give a nontrivial FULL generic TL5 kernel element. Its other blocks
are already identity. Ito's normal-subgroup theorem then supplies
nontrivial hyperbolic Jones-one knots. Matrix identity must be generic;
braid nontriviality must be certified independently. This remains the
complete counterexample scaffold, not an existence theorem for h.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.2028

Outcome
-------
No credible new geometric mechanism forcing this mismatch emerged.
There are, however, two exact restrictions worth retaining: the Burau
kernel contains no nontrivial virtually multitwist braids, and the
natural spherical-boundary kernel in B6 leaves precisely a 9-dimensional
block uncontrolled. The latter is a real structural construction of a
partial kernel, but it does not close the argument. Existing short-word
centralizer searches in work/pass3/braid_notes.md were not repeated.

1. A broad geometric no-go: virtually multitwist inputs
-----------------------------------------------------
PROPOSITION. Let beta belong to the generic unreduced Burau kernel of
B_n. If beta^r is a multitwist about a disjoint multicurve in the
punctured disk, for some positive integer r, then beta=1. Boundary
parallel full twists are allowed; peripheral twists about one puncture
are omitted since they are already trivial as punctured-disk mappings.

Proof. The Burau derivative at t=1 identifies pairwise pure-braid
linking coordinates: on A_ij it is the matrix
  (e_i-e_j)(e_i-e_j)^T.
These matrices are independent, so every kernel braid is pure and all
its pairwise linking numbers vanish. The same holds for beta^r.

Write beta^r=product_c T_c^{m_c}, where the distinct c are disjoint and
enclose subsets S_c of at least two punctures. The subsets form a
laminar family. For any pair i,j the linking coordinate is
  sum_{c: i,j in S_c} m_c.
For an outermost S_c, choose i,j in different maximal proper child
clusters, treating punctures outside the child curves as singleton
children. Such a pair exists: otherwise c would coincide with its sole
child or enclose at most one puncture. No proper child contains both.
Thus the displayed linking number determines m_c, which is zero.
Continue inward, subtracting the already zero ancestor exponents.
Every m_c is zero. Hence beta^r=1, and torsion-freeness of B_n gives
beta=1.

The proof uses actual geometric disjointness, not the unsupported claim
that arbitrary curves enclosing the same puncture set are isotopic.
Within a disjoint multicurve, equal enclosed subsets would bound a
puncture-free annulus and have already been identified as one curve.

Consequences. Simultaneously conjugated products of nested full twists,
or of the corresponding geometric Jucys--Murphy braids, cannot furnish
a nontrivial Burau-kernel input. Nor can a nontrivial periodic input:
a suitable positive power of a periodic braid is a boundary full twist.
This agrees with, but does not require, the stronger representation
spectral obstruction from pass24. It is valid for every n.

Scope. This does NOT exclude all reducible braids, since a reducible
braid may have pseudo-Anosov components. It also does NOT exclude a
multitwist helper h paired with a complicated Burau-kernel B. Its exact
use is to kill a tempting source of simultaneous diagonalization for B.
For context, Lee--Song prove a stronger exclusion of reducible kernel
elements for B4 modulo primes; their introduction explicitly explains
why this does not extend automatically to larger strand counts.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0403347

2. The spherical-boundary trick: an exact partial construction
-------------------------------------------------------------
Generic TL6 has blocks of dimensions 1,5,9,5, corresponding to through
labels k=6,4,2,0 and Young diagrams [6],[5,1],[4,2],[3,3]. Let D_m be
the full twist on the first m strands and put
  P=D_6 D_5^{-1}.
Here D_m denotes Delta_m^2, so the inverse is of the full twist, not
the half twist. P has exponent sum 10.

The raw full-twist scalar on a through-k TL_m block is
  A^{k(k+2)-3m}.
One way to verify the normalization is to divide the ribbon twist of
the fused label k by the m individual fundamental twists; the signs
cancel because k and m have the same parity. It gives A^20 on the
1D TL5 block and A^-12 on its residual 5D block, matching the existing
exact model.

The rectangular [3,3] module restricts irreducibly to the [3,2] B5
module. Thus rho_[3,3](P)=A^-6 I. Every conjugate H=gPg^-1 also acts
as this scalar on the rectangular block. Consequently [B,H] kills
that entire block for ANY B.

Now take an actual B5 Burau-kernel braid B, embedded in B6. Its Burau6
matrix is identity (the unreduced representation simply adds an
unused coordinate). The braid [B,H] therefore kills the 1D and Burau
blocks as well. This is an actual word construction, with three of
the four generic blocks automatically killed.

The remaining [4,2] block restricts to [3,2] plus [4,1], of dimensions
5 and 4. On these two subspaces P has eigenvalues
  A^2  (multiplicity 5),   A^-10 (multiplicity 4).
They are distinct generically. Killing the last block requires B's
9-dimensional image to preserve the conjugated 5+4 decomposition.
That is exactly an additional centralizer condition; it is not forced
by the Burau identity or the scalar rectangular block.

There is also a direct geometric tautology to avoid: if g lies in the
original B5 subgroup, then P commutes with that whole subgroup, since
D6 is central in B6 and D5 is central in B5. In this case [B,H]=1 as
an actual braid. A potentially nontrivial construction must move the
five-puncture boundary curve and then solve the remaining 9D condition.
No such solution is furnished here. This is not presently simpler than
the original B5 residual problem, and does not justify a new scan.

The generic branching rule and rectangular descent are recorded in
Kasahara, Propositions 2.4 and 2.6. His Theorem 3.1 also identifies the
faithfulness of the B5 [3,2] representation with the corresponding
six-punctured-sphere Jones representation (up to the standard transpose
and scalar conventions). The geometric reinterpretation therefore
connects to an established faithfulness problem; it is not an independent
proof of nonfaithfulness.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0304

3. A literature trap: a partial kernel is not the missing B6 block
----------------------------------------------------------------
The abstract of Piwocki--Traczyk, Representations of small braid groups
in Temperley--Lieb algebra, mentions a representation into TL_{9,2}.
That subscript is a quotient of a nine-strand diagram algebra, not the
nine-dimensional [4,2] block of B6. Thus their result cannot simply be
combined with section 2 to conclude that all B6 blocks have kernels.

Jackson--Kerler's explicit discussion, printed pages 1707--1708 (arXiv
PDF pages 19--20), makes the distinction clear: they describe a
380-crossing element in ker J_{9,2}, then a possible passage to J_{17,4}
and the [15,2] representation, with nontriviality verification still
specified. Neither is the missing B6 representation. I did not obtain
the complete Piwocki--Traczyk text and do not extrapolate its theorem
to an unverified iterative full-kernel construction.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0912.2114
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.topol.2008.07.016

4. What would justify continuation
----------------------------------
The next useful input would be a geometric reason a nontrivial B
preserves the conjugated quantum fusion decomposition while moving
the actual associated curve. Such a theorem or explicit construction
would predict a centralizer mismatch before matrix checking. An arbitrary
commuting matrix in End(V), a rational diagonal model, a root-of-unity
kernel, or separate partial kernels at different strand numbers provides
no such input.

For a proposed construction, the endpoint is still exact generic matrix
commutation plus nonidentity Artin action, followed by Ito. A nonzero
exact specialized commutator disproves that candidate. A proof that the
preserved decomposition forces the curve to be preserved would instead
close that mechanism. We currently have neither implication for the
remaining genuinely interacting class. Stop this bounded branch here;
larger word bounds would repeat the old computational approach without
a new structural reason to expect success.
