Audited satellite_mechanism.txt sections1–3 and the conditional

1. Use a fixed framing of P and insert its solid torus using the
companion's preferred zero longitude. The relative framing of P(J)
then depends only on the framed pattern: changing the companion
does not add a self-linking correction. Thus the normalized ordinary
satellite has a single companion-independent linear functional of
the annular pattern skein. Without the preferred-zero convention,
the claimed common coefficients would require extra framing factors.

2. A disk meeting the actual pattern at most2d times bounds its
annular skein degree by2d. Kauffman smoothing preserves its mod2
homology class; winding zero therefore allows only even degrees.
Changing from powers of the core to the Chebyshev/Jones-Wenzl basis
is triangular. Its label2i evaluates to the nonzero generic quantum
dimension times reduced H_(2i+1)(J). These dimensions and the common
framing/unit normalization may be absorbed in c_i in Q(A).
Consequently all c_i with i>0 vanish IF AND ONLY IF the original
annular class is scalar. No implication from a single closure is used.

3. The normalized reduced colored invariant is multiplicative under
connected sum. Thus for J_r=#^r T the finite expansion becomes
sum c_i lambda_i^r with lambda_i=H_(2i+1)(T) and lambda0=1.

For the displayed trefoil formula the a-th numerator term has
minimum exponent
  -3j(j+2)/2 +(3a^2+a)/2.
The unique minimum is a=0; already a=1 is two degrees higher.
The denominator [j+1] has valuation -j/2. Therefore
  val H_(j+1)(T)=-(3j^2+5j)/2,
  val lambda_i=-6i^2-5i.
As a normalization check j=1 gives V_T=q^-1+q^-3-q^-4,
whose lowest exponent is indeed -4. The lambda_i are distinct and
lambda_i!=1 for every i>0.

4. Subtracting the r=0 evaluation gives the square matrix
lambda_i^r-1, 1<=r,i<=d. Dividing column i by lambda_i-1 gives
monic polynomials of successive degrees0,...,d-1 evaluated at lambda_i.
Their evaluation determinant is the Vandermonde, so the stated
determinant is nonzero over Q(A). Exactly d+1 complete ordinary
satellite polynomials certify scalarity, GIVEN the geometric2d bound.

The valuation argument for eventual pairwise distinctness also passes:
the highest nonzero positive-color term has a strictly most negative
slope in r, so it is uniquely minimal for all sufficiently large r.
No cancellation of that term or infinite constant subsequence is possible.

5. COMPLETE CONDITIONAL COUNTEREXAMPLE ENDPOINT
Suppose P is an actual knot pattern with positive geometric wrapping w,
zero algebraic winding, scalar full annular bracket, and V_(P(U))=1.
Then V_(P(T))=1 for a trefoil T. The Schubert bridge inequality uses
GEOMETRIC wrapping (Schultens Definition2 explicitly calls it index/
wrapping), and gives b(P(T))>=w*b(T)=2w. Since w is positive and even,
this is greater than1; P(T) is a nontrivial knot. Thus these hypotheses
really do close an ordinary-Jones counterexample construction.
Using #^r T gives unbounded bridge numbers and infinitely many examples.

If the scalar value V_(P(U)) is not1, the same construction instead
falsifies broad bridge boundedness within one Jones fiber. It does not
settle unknot detection. No geometric essentiality may be inferred
from annular scalarity or from passing the finite polynomial test.
