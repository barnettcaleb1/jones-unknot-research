INDEPENDENT ADVERSARIAL AUDIT OF THE WATSON FIXED-INPUT RANK BOUND
1 November 2026 UTC; braid track.
Audited: homology_mechanism.txt and research21/geometric_bridge.txt.

Verdict: the stated theorem and the quantitative bound pass. The scope is
the explicitly marked mirrored-three-braid Watson template, with the
two fixed input tangles containing two arcs and no closed components.
This is not a bound over an arbitrary Jones fiber without an additional
bound on the input crossing parameter N.

A. Six-port geometry and the word-order issue
The rainbow coevaluation pairs i with 5-i. Moving a crossing through
its cups converts sigma_i into sigma_(6-i), so the reflected INVERSE
crossing cancels. Hence g_i=sigma_i sigma_(6-i)^(-1) fixes the rainbow
cap, as does g_i^(-1). Because the reflected block is a group
homomorphism with the SAME word order, a product of these paired
generators also fixes the cap. There is no unsupported reversal of
the word. The two disjoint braid blocks commute, allowing the actual
six-strand word to be regrouped into these generators.

After either input resolves to infinity, its variable integer twists
are curls and that cap is rainbow. The paired middle braid then cancels
geometrically. Pairing the other integer-tangle cap with rainbow gives
a denominator closure of an integer tangle plus an outside trivial
circle, namely U2; two rainbow caps give U3. Framing and orientation
changes do not affect normalized total Khovanov ranks.

B. Independent component-count audit
I independently constructed the six-port graph: top cap matching;
bottom cap matching pulled back through p on the first three ports and
reflection-p-reflection on the last three. For all six p and all nine
cap pairs, connected-component counts reproduce the submitted table.

If p fixes its first port (123 or132), no allowed A/X input pair yields
a knot. For each other p, AA,AX,XA,XX each give one component. Every
pair with exactly one B gives two components; BB gives three.
Thus every use of Watson's knot-only Lemma3.1 is legitimate:
the both-zero branch is AA for even n and XX for odd n, always a knot.
The other branches are LINKS, but their ranks come from the explicit
unlink isotopy rather than an unproved extension of Watson's theorem.

C. Resolution cube and state circles
Only the N fixed input crossings are resolved. Every state consists of
one of the two planar matchings in each input ball plus state circles.
A circle cannot enclose an arc reaching the boundary in this planar
state without meeting it. All circles therefore split off, including
nested planar circles, which still form an unlink.

There are at most 2^N states. Initially the two input tangles have four
arc components and no circles; finally there remain four arcs and ell
circles. Each smoothing changes component count by at most one.
Consequently ell<=N and sum_s 2^ell(s)<=4^N.
The exact-triangle rank inequality works irrespective of intermediate
orientations and grading shifts, and yields the claimed finite sum.

D. Determinant and characteristic-two reduction
For an allowed input connectivity, denominator closure joins its two
arcs into a knot. Therefore det D(T),det D(U) are positive odd integers.
The previously proved bracket matrix, evaluated at delta=0, gives
  d=det K_n=p^2 det D(T) det D(U),
where p=det J_beta and the both-zero base is J_beta#mirror(J_beta).
Thus p^2<=d. Integer twists leave denominator knot types unchanged.

A two-bridge knot is alternating and quasi-alternating. The primary
Manolescu–Ozsvath Theorem1 explicitly establishes reduced integral
thinness; universal coefficients therefore give reduced-F2 rank p.
The reduced connected-sum formula gives base rank p^2.
Shumakovitch Corollary3.2.C gives unreduced-F2 rank exactly twice
reduced-F2 rank. Watson's integral Kh constancy transfers to F2 by UCT.
The elementary branch ranks are consequently bounded by max(2d,8).

Combining the cube bound and applying mod2 splitting and UCT once more:
  dim_Q Kh_red(K_n) <= dim_F2 Kh_red(K_n)
                    <= 4^N max(d,4).
No characteristic-two splitting over Q is assumed. The alternative
Section4 Lee-bar argument is unnecessary to this proof and may remain
optional.

GLOBAL-SCAFFOLD CAUTION REQUESTED BY ROOT
A bounded-N covering theorem for an entire Jones fiber is not proved.
Moreover it would already bound bridge number: there are finitely many
marked four-ended planar tangle diagrams with at most N crossings, up
to combinatorial type and planar isotopy. Choose bounded Morse
complexity for each of these finitely many endcaps. The six middle
braid strands, however long beta is, are height-monotone and add no
local maxima. Closing through these endcaps therefore gives a bridge
bound depending only on N and the fixed wiring.

Consequently bounded-N coverage of the Jones-one fiber would itself
imply unknot detection using connected-sum powers and Schubert bridge
additivity, even without the Khovanov estimate. It is not an established
easier global gate. The unconditional new result remains the uniform
rank bound for the specified family with bounded input complexity.
