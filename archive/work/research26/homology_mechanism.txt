UNIFORM RATIONAL KH RANK FOR EACH FIXED WATSON ORBIT
31 October 2026. Derived argument, independently geometrically audited.
No novelty claim. This is a fixed-template result, not global Jones detection.

RESULT AND WHY IT IS DIFFERENT FROM THE EARLIER SUPPORT ARGUMENT

Fix a braid beta and tangles T,U in Watson's actual Figure 1 construction,
with his two-arc/no-closed-component hypotheses, allowed nonvertical
connectivities, and such that K_n=K_beta(T+sigma^n,U+sigma^(-n)) is a knot.
Then dim_Q Kh(K_n) and dim_Q Kh_red(K_n) are uniformly bounded for n in Z.
The constants depend on the fixed diagrams beta,T,U, not just their Jones
polynomial. No constancy of Kh for nonsimple tangles is asserted.

The mechanism is to resolve only the fixed crossings inside T and U.
It is NOT an iteration of the paired-twist long exact sequences. Every
resulting elementary closure is either a simple-tangle Watson knot, to
which Watson's published theorem applies, or an explicit unlink. The
finite resolution cube bounds rank before any middle-window maps need
to be identified. This additional geometric input was absent from the
abstract fixed-cap models in the previous pass.

Here is an exact six-port encoding of Figure 1, rotated into a plat.
Ports at each cap are numbered 0,...,5 from left to right. A four-ended
tangle occupies the middle four ports; an outside arc joins ports 0,5.
For the three endpoint connectivities the resulting endcaps are

 A (zero):     (0,5), (1,2), (3,4),
 X (crossed):  (0,5), (1,3), (2,4),
 B (infinity): (0,5), (1,4), (2,3).

In particular B is the full rainbow cap. If beta is given by a word in
sigma_1,sigma_2, the six-strand middle braid replaces each generator by

 g_i = sigma_i sigma_(6-i)^(-1),  i=1,2.

Left and right factors have disjoint support, so this is beta on the
first three strands followed by the reflected crossing-inverse word
on the last three strands, in the SAME word order. Do not replace the
second word by its reverse as well. This is the rotated beta/beta^-1
wiring in Watson's figure. The exact existing Spherogram construction is
work/pass2/watson_model.py: endcap reshapes T to four bottom or four top
ends, adds the outside strand, and closes the six-strand word
  beta + [-sign(i)*(6-abs(i)) for i in beta].
The construction is only an optional check; the proof below is geometric.

1. THE CAP ISOTOPY, FOR ARBITRARY BETA

Each g_i fixes B up to an isotopy relative to the six external endpoints.
Slide its first crossing through the matching pair of rainbow cups. It
meets the reflected inverse crossing and the pair cancels by RII.
Equivalently this is the rigid-tangle coevaluation identity
  (f tensor (f^(-1))^*) coev = coev.
It is an identity of actual tangles, not just an equality in their skein
module. It holds for g_i^-1 as well. Applying it successively removes
the entire middle braid whenever either endcap is B. The same argument
works from the bottom cap, by reversing the height coordinate.

Adding any integer twist to an infinity tangle preserves its underlying
unframed tangle: the added crossings become curls next to its cap. Thus
an infinity resolution of T or U is still a B cap after adding the n or
-n crossings, up to such curls. Once the middle braid is removed, an
A_n cap paired with B gives two trivial components; all integer twists
again become curls. B paired with B gives three trivial components.
The curls may change bracket framing, but never the normalized link
type or the TOTAL Khovanov rank. These cases therefore have unreduced
rational rank 4 and 8 respectively, for every integer n.

This geometric cap identity was independently checked by both the root
and the tangle agent. The earlier small-diagram simplifications were
sanity checks only, not the basis for this assertion.

2. THE BOTH-ZERO RESOLUTION REALLY IS A KNOT

Watson's Lemma 3.1 assumes knots, so it cannot simply be invoked for all
resolved links. The required knot condition is checked as follows.
Let p be beta's permutation on 0,1,2. The full permutation is
 p on the first three ports and reflection-p-reflection on the last
three. Join this permutation to the two cap matchings and count cycles.
The resulting table is:

 p (one-line)    AA XX AX XA    AB BA XB BX    BB
 123              3  3  2  2     2  2  2  2     3
 132              3  3  2  2     2  2  2  2     3
 213              1  1  1  1     2  2  2  2     3
 231              1  1  1  1     2  2  2  2     3
 312              1  1  1  1     2  2  2  2     3
 321              1  1  1  1     2  2  2  2     3

The original allowed knot hypothesis excludes the first two permutations
(id and (23)), exactly as Watson states. For each remaining permutation,
all four allowed cap pairs are knots. In particular when both fixed
tangles resolve to zero, the added twists give AA for even n and XX for
odd n, and every member is a knot. Both integer tangles are simple.
Watson Lemma 3.1 therefore gives a constant unreduced Kh group for this
entire both-zero orbit. Put R_beta=dim_Q Kh(K_beta(0,0)).

watson_connectivity_check.py is a portable, package-free exact verifier
of the component table; its JSON contains all nine cap-pair counts for
all six permutations. It does not try to verify link isotopy from a
permutation: that is the separate cup-isotopy proof in section 1.

3. FINITE FIXED-CROSSING RESOLUTION BOUND

Let c be the total number of crossings in the fixed diagrams T,U. Resolve
these c crossings, not the variable twists or the beta-shell crossings.
Every complete state replaces each fixed tangle by one of the two planar
matchings A,B, together with ell(s) disjoint planar state circles. Such
circles are split from the boundary-connected arcs: a planar circle
cannot enclose an arc reaching the boundary without intersecting it.
They may be nested with each other, which is still the trivial unlink.
There are finitely many states and ell(s) is independent of n.

For unreduced ordinary Kh over Q, the mapping-cone long exact sequence
at a crossing gives r(L)<=r(L_0)+r(L_1). Iterating over these c fixed
crossings gives

 r(K_n) <= sum_s 2^ell(s) r(E_(s,n))
         <= sum_s 2^ell(s) max{R_beta,8} =: C(beta,T,U).

Here E_(s,n) is the elementary closure without the state circles. The
factor 2^ell(s) is the disjoint-union tensor formula for Kh over Q.
All grading and writhe shifts are irrelevant to total rank. The proof
therefore does not assume a common orientation on resolved components,
or that a link version of Watson's theorem exists. Every simple-family
application is to a knot by section 2; all other links are explicit
unlinks by section 1. Negative n is treated by the same isotopy and
Watson's inverse-action clause, with no asymptotic restriction.

4. PASSING TO THE REDUCED RATIONAL RANK

For an actual knot let r_red and r_unred denote the rational ranks.
The based generalized Lee complex over Q[X] splits into one free block
and torsion blocks with differential X^a (a>=1). Setting X=0 gives
ordinary reduced Kh; setting X^2=0 gives ordinary unreduced Kh.
If m1 counts length-one blocks and m2 counts longer blocks, then

 r_red=1+2m1+2m2,
 r_unred=2+2m1+4m2 >= r_red+1.

This elementary specialization is the same previously audited block
calculation. Primary reference: Dirk Schuetz, arXiv:2412.05156,
Section 2.1, equation (1); Section 3 relates the reduced Bar--Natan
convention. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.05156
Consequently the uniform unreduced bound also bounds rational reduced
rank. We do not silently use the characteristic-two splitting over Q.

5. PRECISE ROLE IN THE FULL PROOF SCAFFOLD

This rules out unbounded rational Kh rank as a counterexample to H-rank
inside ANY ONE FIXED Watson template satisfying the hypotheses above.
It is stronger than the earlier fixed-support argument, whose exact
triangles alone permitted unbounded multiplicities.

It does not prove boundedness on an entire Jones-polynomial fiber.
The constants depend on c, state-circle counts and R_beta, and these
quantities can vary without bound as the template changes. No global
finite-template covering statement is available. In particular a fixed
template cannot be substituted for the connected-sum sequence
J#K^#m used in the full H-rank scaffold without a new coverage theorem.
Thus this is an actual structural theorem for the proposed adversarial
family, not a solution to generic Jones unknot detection.

6. SIMPLER CHARACTERISTIC-TWO ROUTE AND A UNIFORM-IN-BETA BOUND

Watson's theorem is integral and bigraded, so the universal coefficient
theorem makes the simple branch constant over F2 too. Run the same finite
fixed-crossing resolution argument over F2. Shumakovitch's Corollary
3.2.C gives
 Kh^{i,j}(L;F2)=Kh_red^{i,j-1}(L;F2) direct-sum
                Kh_red^{i,j+1}(L;F2).
Hence r_red(F2)=r_unred(F2)/2; moreover r_red(Q)<=r_red(F2) by UCT.
This provides an alternative reduced-rank argument needing no Lee bars.
Primary source: A.N. Shumakovitch, Torsion of the Khovanov homology,
Fund.Math.225(2014),343--364; arXiv:math/0405474v2, Corollary3.2.C,
PDFp12. https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0405474

There is a stronger quantitative consequence using the exact geometric
template and determinant factor already proved in research21:

 K_beta(0,0)=J_beta#mirror(J_beta), with J_beta a two-bridge knot;
 det K_n=(det J_beta)^2 det D(T) det D(U).

Here D is the denominator closure. Its topology is unchanged by adding
integer twists, since they become curls at the denominator cap. Because
T,U have allowed A or X connectivity and no closed components, D(T),D(U)
are knots, so their determinants are positive odd integers. If d=det K_n
and p=det J_beta, then p^2<=d. A two-bridge knot is alternating and has
reduced F2 Kh rank p. Thus the both-zero branch has unreduced F2 rank
2p^2<=2d, by the reduced connected-sum tensor formula and mod2 splitting.
One can see rank p directly from the alternating spanning-tree complex:
it has p generators, and the absolute Jones evaluation gives lower bound p.
Equivalently use quasi-alternating homological thinness; primary reference
Manolescu--Ozsvath, On the Khovanov and knot Floer homologies of
quasi-alternating links, arXiv:0708.3249.
https://web.stanford.edu/~cm5/quasi.pdf

If the two FIXED input diagrams have at most N crossings in total, then
there are at most 2^N complete states, and ell(s)<=N. The latter follows
by resolving crossings one at a time: each smoothing increases the number
of components by at most one, and the original input tangles have no
closed components. Therefore sum_s 2^ell(s)<=4^N. Combining the estimates:

 dim_F2 Kh(K_n;F2) <= 4^N max{2d,8},
 dim_Q Kh_red(K_n;Q) <= 4^N max{d,4}.

These bounds are uniform over beta AND n, subject to the stated knot
connectivity, determinant d, and input-crossing bound N. They require no
bound on beta's word length. In particular the same conclusion applies
to a whole Jones fiber if a separate theorem bounds the INPUT complexity
of Watson presentations throughout that fiber. No such theorem is proved
here. This is a concrete geometric candidate for a global bridge, with
an explicit complexity parameter, not an assumption that all equal-Jones
knots already lie in one fixed template.
