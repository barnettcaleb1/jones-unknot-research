GEOMETRIC INPUTS FOR THE PASS-26 WATSON RANK BOUND
1 November 2026 UTC. Extracted and restated from our pass-21 argument.
Only the two inputs used in the rank proof are included here.

Marking: ports 0,...,5 at either end; the outside cap is (0,5).
The zero and infinity input caps are respectively
 A=(05)(12)(34), B=(05)(14)(23).
For a three-braid beta, the six-strand middle word replaces sigma_i
by sigma_i sigma_(6-i)^(-1), i=1,2, in the same word order.

1. THE BASE IS A MIRRORED TWO-BRIDGE CONNECTED SUM

With both input tangles zero, a sphere between the two three-strand
halves meets the knot twice. It exhibits the connected sum of a knot
J_beta and its mirror. Each half is obtained by capping the inner two
strands at both ends and closing the outer strand. This is a four-plat,
so J_beta has bridge number at most two.

The component check in the companion verifiers proves J_beta is a knot
under the stated allowed-output hypothesis. A two-bridge knot is
alternating. Consequently its reduced Khovanov homology over F2 has
rank det(J_beta); the unknot case has determinant and rank one.

2. THE DETERMINANT FACTOR

Use the Kauffman bracket with the unknot equal to one and
delta=-A^2-A^-2. Write the input tangle skein vectors in the zero,
infinity basis as [T]=(a,b), [U]=(c,e). Their denominator closures are
  <D(T)>=a+delta*b, <D(U)>=c+delta*e.

The full rainbow cap B absorbs each paired middle generator by a
crossing slide followed by a Reidemeister-II cancellation. Thus the
four cap evaluations have matrix
  [[X,delta],[delta,delta^2]],
where X=<J_beta><mirror(J_beta)>.
The mirrored framing exponents cancel, so
  X=V_J(A^-4)V_J(A^4).

By bilinearity,
  <K_beta(T,U)>=X*a*c+delta*(a*e+b*c)+delta^2*b*e.

Set zeta=exp(pi*i/4). Then delta(zeta)=0 and zeta^-4=-1.
Taking absolute values (the writhe corrections have absolute value
one) gives
  det K=(det J_beta)^2 det D(T) det D(U).

For each allowed nonvertical two-arc input connectivity, denominator
closure is a knot. Its determinant is a positive odd integer, so
  (det J_beta)^2 <= det K.
Adding horizontal integer twists to either input makes only curls
in its denominator closure and does not change that knot type.

These are geometric and skein deductions, not conclusions inferred
from the numerical component table. They require the marked template;
an arbitrary placement of a braid and its inverse need not have the
same cap cancellation.
