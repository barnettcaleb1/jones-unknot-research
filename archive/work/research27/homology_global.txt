PASS 27: TESTING SMITH AND STEENROD MECHANISMS ON ACTUAL RIBBON GEOMETRY
1 November 2026. Bounded research audit. No generic Jones proof obtained.

STRATEGIC RESULT

The natural connected-sum symmetry does not provide an extra positivity
constraint: all quantum-graded Smith inequalities for its periodic lift
are automatic polynomial inequalities in the nonnegative Khovanov ranks.
Separately, actual hyperbolic ribbon knots support arbitrarily high
Steenrod squares. Thus neither periodicity nor ribbon/hyperbolic geometry
alone limits the cohomological operations in a ribbon complement.

These are concrete tests of proposed mechanisms, not new boundedness
conjectures and not counterexamples to Jones unknot detection. A theorem
coupling these operations to the ENTIRE Jones polynomial is still missing.

1. AN ACTUAL PERIODIC-CONNECTED-SUM TEST, INCLUDING QUANTUM GRADINGS

Let K be any actual classical knot, and form an annular knot A_K by tying
a local copy of K into the core of the solid torus. Its wrapping number
is one. The p-fold cyclic annular lift, with p=2^r, is the actual knot
K^{#p}, with a p-periodic rotation and an unknotted fixed axis disjoint
from the knot. Its quotient in the axis complement is A_K.

Put H=Kh_red(K;F2) and P(q)=sum_j dim H^{*,j} q^j, where the star sums
over homological degree. P has nonnegative integral coefficients.

Each resolution of a local diagram of A_K has exactly one essential
circle, the circle containing the basepoint of the cut-open K. The two
annular labels on that circle have weight k=+1 and k=-1. Taking the
annular associated graded separates these two labels. In either sector
the differential is exactly the based reduced Khovanov differential;
the only quantum shift is respectively +1 or -1. Consequently

 AKh(A_K;F2) = H{q+1,k+1} direct-sum H{q-1,k-1}.

In particular its total rank is 2 dim H. This is a local chain argument,
not a claim that arbitrary wrapping-one annular links have a trivial
annular filtration (the specific local-knot/core presentation is used).

Reduced connected-sum Kunneth and characteristic-two reduced/unreduced
splitting give the quantum rank polynomial of the covering knot:

   P_unred(K^{#p};q)=(q+q^-1)P(q)^p.

The fixed-point grading formula for a p-periodic knot sends quotient
quantum/annular grades (a,b) to pa-(p-1)b. A class of reduced grade j
in A_K has a=j+b and b=+/-1. Its corresponding covering quantum degree
is therefore pj+b. Thus the right side of the quantum-graded Smith
inequality has polynomial

   (q+q^-1) P(q^p).

The entire resulting family of inequalities is exactly the coefficientwise
inequality

       (q+q^-1)P(q)^p >= (q+q^-1)P(q^p).

This holds for EVERY nonnegative integral Laurent polynomial P. In the
multinomial expansion, the pure term at pj contributes a_j^p>=a_j, and
all mixed terms have nonnegative coefficients. The total-rank inequality
is just 2r^p>=2r. No Jones assumption was used anywhere.

Likewise the periodic Jones congruence in this example is simply
V_K(t)^p=V_(K^{#p})(t) congruent to V_K(t^p) modulo2, the Frobenius
identity. For a hypothetical V_K=1 knot it reduces to 1=1. Thus applying
Smith localization to these canonical connected-sum symmetries does not
repair the missing Euler-to-rank step, even if all quantum gradings are
retained. This does NOT assert that Smith theory for every other actual
symmetry, or additional equivariant maps, is powerless.

2. RIBBON/HYPERBOLIC GEOMETRY DOES NOT BOUND STEENROD DEGREE

For every n>=1 there exists an actual hyperbolic ribbon knot R_n with a
nonzero Sq^n on reduced Khovanov homology over F2. Here is a deduction
from established constructions; it is not a claim of a new publication.

Bodish's Lemma5.3 supplies an actual knot K_n with nonzero Sq^n. Let
D_n=K_n#mirror(K_n), an actual ribbon knot. Choose a nonzero class y in
the highest nonzero homological degree of Kh_red(mirror(K_n);F2).
Every Sq^i(y), i>0, vanishes by degree. If Sq^n(x) is nonzero in K_n,
the connected-sum stable splitting and Cartan formula give

   Sq^n(x tensor y)=Sq^n(x) tensor y !=0.

Thus D_n is ribbon and retains the operation. Bodish's Theorem7.1,
quoting Silver--Whitten, supplies a hyperbolic knot R_n and a ribbon
concordance D_n -> R_n. Compose with U -> D_n: R_n is ribbon.
Naturality of Sq^n and the split injection induced by that concordance
preserve the nonzero operation. Since the reduced Kh of U has no such
operation, it lies in the nontrivial ribbon complement for U -> R_n.

This defeats an attempted proof of E based only on uniformly bounded
operation degree for actual ribbon complements, including prime or
hyperbolic ones. It does not defeat a Jones-dependent bound, and these
knots are NOT claimed to share a Jones polynomial.

Primary source: Holt Bodish, Nontrivial Steenrod Squares on Prime,
Hyperbolic and Satellite Knots, arXiv:2006.10881v3: Theorem5.1 (stable
connected-sum splitting), Theorem5.2 (naturality), Lemma5.3 (nonzero
operations), Theorem7.1 (hyperbolic ribbon successor).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.10881

3. CRITICAL SOURCE CORRECTION: THERE IS NO GLOBAL NO-2-TORSION ENDPOINT

Gujral--Wang's theorem DOES NOT say that an actual knot whose unreduced
integral Khovanov homology has no 2-torsion is the unknot. That unrestricted
assertion is Shumakovitch's conjecture. Theorem1 instead says: if K has no
2-torsion, then its reduced rational rank is no greater than that of any
knot J obtainable by ONE proper rational tangle replacement. Corollary2
therefore supplies 2-torsion for every nontrivial unknotting-number-one
knot, and more generally knots obtainable from U or a trefoil in one
proper rational replacement. The proof cannot be iterated through an
arbitrary crossing-change sequence: its no-torsion hypothesis need not
hold for the intermediate knots. A search snippet that runs the theorem
into the corollary can misleadingly suggest the unrestricted endpoint.

Even within that restricted endpoint, torsion absence has not been
deduced from Euler polynomial1. Torsion has zero rational Euler contribution
by definition, and the ordinary Jones polynomial does not even determine
the number of Bockstein pairs.

An already available actual test is 5_1 versus mirror(10_132). Both have
  V=t^2+t^4-t^5+t^6-t^7.
The unreduced integral table has respectively 2 and 5 Z/2 summands, and
no other torsion. Thus their first integral Bocksteins have total ranks
2 and 5, respectively. The rational reduced ranks are5 and11, while
the unreduced rational ranks are6 and12. These values were extracted
only for this known pair from the saved KnotInfo data, not by a new scan.
Mirror changes torsion gradings but not their counts. This is not a
ribbon-related pair and does not falsify E.

Primary source: Gujral--Wang, A minimality property for knots without
Khovanov 2-torsion, AGT25(2025),4073--4075, Theorem1 and Corollary2.

4. STOP CONDITION AND THE REMAINING GEOMETRIC REQUIREMENT

There is no new full proof from these operations. The following shortcuts
have now been tested against actual constructions, not merely formal
complexes: canonical periodic connected-sum Smith inequalities add no
constraint; ribbon/hyperbolic geometry permits unbounded Steenrod degree;
the whole Jones polynomial need not determine even the first Bockstein
rank. One should not deepen those routes without an additional geometric
statement tying an operation to the Jones-unit fiber or controlling the
actual relative ribbon summand in a way absent from these examples.

The finite-resolution Watson argument from pass26 is different: it
supplies a concrete geometric mechanism and an explicit input-complexity
bound. No comparable finite decomposition of arbitrary ribbon complements
has been established here. Replacing its missing coverage theorem by
another unexplained rank/torsion budget would only rename the problem.

5. INDEPENDENT FINAL AUDIT OF THE BING-SATELLITE PRODUCT ENDPOINT

The tangle and braid tracks independently derived the annular formula
from Suzuki's Bing-doubling coefficients. This audit assumes their
coefficient calculation; it separately verifies its satellite consequence
and the nontriviality endpoint.

Let H_C be the reduced, zero-framed dimension-three colored Jones
polynomial, delta=-A^2-A^-2, and P_C one component of the untwisted Bing
double B(C), regarded as a pattern in the exterior of the other component.
Their formula is
  [P_C]=delta H_C +(1-H_C)z^2/delta.
On a zero-framed companion J, z^2 evaluates to
  1+(delta^2-1)H_J.
Divide the resulting zero-framed bracket by delta to get normalized V.
Direct substitution gives exactly
  V_(P_C(J))-1=-(delta^2-1)/delta^2 (H_C-1)(H_J-1).
Both C=U and J=U give V=1, as required geometrically. There is no
additional writhe monomial in these zero-framed conventions.

An integrality check follows from Habiro's expansion. With q=A^-4,
  P(3,1)=q^-3(q^2-1)^2(q^2+1),
and all nonconstant color-three terms contain P(3,1). Since
delta^2=(q+1)^2/q, H_C-1 is divisible by delta^2. Thus the displayed
rational-looking formulas have no actual Laurent-polynomial pole.

For nontrivial C, the Bing-link exterior is obtained by gluing E(C) to
the Borromean exterior along the infection torus. Both pieces are
irreducible with incompressible gluing boundary; their remaining boundary
tori stay incompressible. Thus B(C) is nonsplit. Its two components are
unknots because the corresponding proper sublinks of the Borromean link
are unlinks. Hence P_C is geometrically essential in the exterior solid
torus of the other component. For nontrivial J, the companion torus of
P_C(J) is incompressible on both sides, so the resulting knot is nontrivial.

Therefore for nontrivial C,J this is an actual nontrivial satellite with
ordinary Jones polynomial1 if and only if H_C=1 or H_J=1. The equivalence
uses that Q(A) is a field and delta^2-1 is nonzero, not numerical testing.
This is a concrete product construction with a precise remaining colored
condition; it does not show that either factor can vanish for a nontrivial
knot. See the tangle track's tangle_realization.txt for primary coefficient
and geometric sources.
