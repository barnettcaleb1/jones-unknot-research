STATUS AND FULL ENDPOINT

There is an explicit geometric construction, using the untwisted Bing
double of an arbitrary zero-framed knot C, whose scalar annular-skein
condition is EXACTLY H_3(C)=1. Here H_N is the reduced, dimension-N,
zero-framed colored Jones polynomial, H_N(U)=1. The construction needs
no formal linear-combination realization assumption. It does not supply
a nontrivial C with H_3(C)=1, and ordinary V_C=1 cannot be promoted to this
condition without another theorem.

For nontrivial C, the pattern is essential and its ordinary unknot
closure is an actual unknot. Consequently a nontrivial H_3-unit knot
would produce an actual essential scalar pattern, and its satellites on
nontrivial companions would be nontrivial Jones-one knots. This is the
same unresolved color-three gate already reached by the Whitehead-double
formula in outputs/jones-cabling-and-realizability.txt, now with the FULL

1. ACTUAL PATTERN AND GEOMETRIC NONTRIVIALITY

Let B(C)=B_1(C) union B_2(C) be the ordinary untwisted, zero-framed Bing
double. Both components are actual unknots, and their linking number is
zero. Let P_C be B_1(C) in the solid torus S^3 minus int N(B_2(C)). Use
the preferred zero framing on the axis and pattern. In particular
P_C(U)=U geometrically, not merely by a Jones calculation.

Cimasoni, Slicing Bing doubles, Section 1, gives an exact construction:
infect one component of the Borromean rings by C, exchanging meridian
and preferred longitude. Each remaining component is an unknot because
its pair with the infection component was a two-component unlink.

For nontrivial C, E(B(C)) is obtained by gluing the Borromean exterior to
E(C) along their indicated incompressible boundary tori. Both pieces
are irreducible and the gluing torus is incompressible on both sides.
The elementary innermost-circle argument shows their union irreducible.
Thus B(C) is nonsplit, so P_C is geometrically essential in the solid
torus. This uses no Jones or colored-Jones detection assumption.

Primary construction:
https://www.unige.ch/~cimasoni/Bing.pdf , Section 1, pp. 3--4 of PDF.
Published version: https://msp.org/agt/2006/6-5/agt-v6-n5-p13-s.pdf .
The essentiality conclusion is also explicitly used by Ren, Remark 1.3:

2. FULL ANNULAR FORMULA, WITH INDEXING AND FRAMING

Write delta=-A^2-A^-2 and z for the zero-framed core in the Kauffman
bracket skein of the solid torus; the empty diagram is 1, and a local
zero-framed unknot is delta. Put q=A^-4. Then

       [P_C] = delta H_3(C) + (1-H_3(C)) z^2 / delta.             (1)

Equation (1) is an equality over Q(A); its left side is an actual
integral annular skein. No positivity or division at a root of unity is
asserted. A different pattern framing multiplies the entire equation by
the corresponding framing unit; use zero framing for the displayed form.

Proof from Suzuki, Bing doubling and the colored Jones polynomial,
https://arxiv.org/html/1305.0602 , Section 2.1, Theorem 3.2, and
Proposition 4.3/Corollary 4.4.

CAUTION: Suzuki's V_m has dimension m+1, whereas our H_N is indexed by
dimension. Put a={1}=q^(1/2)-q^(-1/2), b={2},
s=q^(1/2)+q^(-1/2), r=q^(3/2)+q^(-3/2). Her reduced colors are

 P'_0=1, P'_1=(V_1-s)/a,
 P'_2=(V_1-s)(V_1-r)/(ab).

Her Bing coefficients satisfy x_ij^(l)=0 unless i=j. For i=j=1,
x_11^(1)=-{4}, x_11^(2)=-ab, and all other l vanish. Since
{4}/a=r+s and V_1^2=V_2+V_0, their combination is

 sum_l x_11^(l) J_C(P'_l)
   = -J_C((V_1-s)(V_1+s))
   = -J_C(V_2-(q+1+q^-1)V_0)
   = -[3](H_3(C)-1).

The pattern itself has the FUNDAMENTAL color V_1=sP'_0+aP'_1.
Therefore its pairing with axis color P'_j equals s when j=0,
equals -a[3](H_3(C)-1) when j=1, and equals zero for EVERY j>=2.
The extra factor a in the j=1 pairing must not be dropped.

These are full annular probes, not just ordinary two-component Jones
values. The axis is an actual unknot; coloring it computes the standard
Hopf pairing against the annular class of P_C. In the even annular
Jones-Wenzl basis E_(2k), its pairing with P'_j is

 [2k+1] P'_j(q^(k+1/2)+q^(-k-1/2)).

This is zero for k<j and nonzero for k=j. A finite even annular
polynomial whose probes vanish for all j>=2 thus has degree at most two:
apply the probe j=k to its highest nonzero coefficient, then descend.
This proves the required separation; no colored information is inferred
from an ordinary scalar closure.

In the positive quantum-dimension convention the degree-two basis is
E_2=z^2-1, with [3]=s^2-1. Its j=1 pairing is [3](r-s)/a. Consequently
the z^2 coefficient is

       (1-H_3(C)) a^2/(r-s) = (1-H_3(C))/s,

because r-s=s a^2. The j=0 pairing gives the constant coefficient
s H_3(C). Switching to the Kauffman convention changes the sign of the
single pattern component and sends s to -delta; the even z^2 term has
no further sign. This gives (1).

Thus, exactly:

          P_C has scalar annular skein  iff  H_3(C)=1.            (2)

In that case [P_C]=delta, not an arbitrary nonunit scalar. This family
therefore does NOT offer an easier nonunit fixed-Jones-fiber countertest.

3. ENTIRE TWO-INPUT SATELLITE FAMILY: NO COMPENSATING CANCELLATION

For any other zero-framed knot J, satellite evaluation sends
z^2 to the bracket of its zero-framed two-parallel:

             z^2 -> 1+(delta^2-1) H_3(J).

Normalize the output knot bracket by delta. Because the pattern has
winding zero, the zero-framing convention introduces no companion-
dependent writhe factor. Substituting into (1) gives

 V_{P_C(J)}-1
       = -(delta^2-1)/delta^2
                       (H_3(C)-1)(H_3(J)-1).                   (3)

The prefactor is nonzero in Q(A), an integral domain. Therefore this
actual two-input construction has Jones polynomial one if and only if
one of its two inputs has H_3=1. Two nontrivial color-three deviations
cannot compensate. This excludes a broad attempted cancellation
mechanism without an integer-parameter search.

It is consistent with the previous Whitehead result
V_Wh+(C)-1=((1-t^3)/(1+t))(H_3(C)-1). In particular the scalarity gate
is the same color-three gate, not a new proof of ordinary-to-colored
promotion. Ren's all-colored Bing observation does not itself state
this single-color formula; the derivation above is directly from
Suzuki's theorem, with no novelty claim.

4. A DIFFERENT PROPOSED LOCAL MOVE, AND ITS FIRST EXACT OBSTRUCTION

Suppose a crossing change in a local unknot pattern produces a pattern
with the same normalized full annular invariant 1. The oriented skein
relation forces the oriented two-component smoothing to have exactly
the normalized annular invariant of the two-component unlink. At
A=-1 its two component winding numbers must both vanish: they sum to
zero, and if they are k,-k with k!=0 the classical SL_2 trace evaluation
is a nonconstant square of the degree-|k| Chebyshev trace polynomial.

Thus a simple smoothing that winds nontrivially around the axis cannot
realize this move. A candidate would need a null-homologous interaction
with the axis in each smoothing component, in addition to exact skein
equality. This is only a necessary condition; it does not establish
locality or supply a candidate. Formal commutators, Kirby colors, and
clasper alternating sums do not replace an actual single diagram.

5. RESEARCH DECISION

The Bing construction settles a concrete realization question exactly:
its geometric pattern exists for every input, its essentiality is
topological, and its scalarity is one explicit whole colored polynomial
condition. It also proves that pairing two such inputs cannot cancel
their defects. The next useful step within this class would have to
construct a genuine H_3-unit knot or prove a substantive geometric
restriction on that fiber. Repeating satellite evaluations or testing
more Bing companions does not close the global problem. No full Jones
proof or counterexample was produced in this pass.
