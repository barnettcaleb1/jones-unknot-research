ACTUAL KNOTS, COMPLETED QUANTUM INVARIANTS, AND THE COLOR-PROMOTION GAP
Pass 28, 2026-11-01. Research deductions; no priority claim.

Main finding
------------
There are actual knots R_N, for every N >= 5, for which

  V_R_N(e^h) = 1 + O(h^(N+1)),
  H_3(R_N;e^h) = 1 + 320 h^5 + O(h^6).

Here H_m is the reduced, zero-framed m-dimensional colored Jones
polynomial; H_2=V and H_m(U)=1. Every V_R_N is unconditionally NOT 1.
The construction uses actual inverses modulo clasper equivalence, not
formal linear combinations of knots. Thus finite-order realizability,
group-likeness, and naturality cannot promote ordinary Jones data even
to the fifth coefficient of color three. The particular completed
ratio constructed below is nevertheless excluded from exact realization
by a simple full-polynomial obstruction: its color-three value has a
denominator that cannot cancel. This separates finite-order geometry
from an actual all-orders constraint.

1. Exact actual-knot input, including chirality
--------------------------------------------
Use the closures of these explicit braids:

  K: B_2 word (-1,-1,-1,-1,-1),
  L: B_4 word (1,1,1,-2,-1,-1,-2,-3,2,-3,-3).

These are chirality choices of 5_1 and 10_132. The words, rather than
unqualified table names, define the examples. Their whole polynomials
are equal:

  V_K(q)=V_L(q)=q^-2+q^-4-q^-5+q^-6-q^-7.

In the quantum-group parameter u, with q=u^-2, their unreduced color
three polynomials are

  U3(K)=u40-u32-u30-u28+u14+u12+u10+u8+u6,
  U3(L)=u44-u40-u30-u24+u16+u14+u10+u6+2-u^-4.

An exponent after u denotes a power. Divide each by u2+1+u^-2 to get
the reduced color. The difference at u=e^s has first nonzero term
-30720s^5. Division by dimension 3 at s=0 and s=-h/2 gives 320h^5.

Full exact braid calculations were rerun in this pass:
  work/proof12/braid/all_color_collision_check.py
  work/proof12/braid/all_color_collision_check.json
They use integer Laurent arithmetic, verify the local inverse and full
Yang--Baxter relation, check unknot stabilizations, and independently
compute the ordinary and color-three polynomials from the braids.
Primary table data: https://katlas.org/wiki/5_1 and
https://katlas.org/wiki/10_132 . R-matrix convention: Jackson--Kerler,
https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.2114 , equation 23, finite-dimensional s=u^2.

The all-color coefficient does NOT follow merely by interpolating these
two colors. Habiro's cyclotomic expansion gives

  H_m = sum_(k=0)^(m-1) a_k(q) P(m,k;q),
  P(m,k;q)=product_(j=1)^k(q^m+q^-m-q^j-q^-j).

Equality of the entire H_2 implies Delta a_1=0. The exact color-three
difference determines

  Delta a_2=q^-15(q^2-1)(q^3+1)(q^5+1),
  Delta a_2(e^h)=8h+O(h^2).

All k>=3 terms are O(h^6), by cyclotomic integrality. Therefore, for
EVERY fixed integer m>=2,

  H_m(K;e^h)-H_m(L;e^h)
    =8(m^2-1)(m^2-4)h^5+O(h^6).                     (1)

Source for the integral expansion and central universal invariant:
Habiro, On the quantum sl_2 invariants of knots and integral homology
spheres, GT Monographs 4 (2002), Sections 2.2--2.3 and Theorem 3.1:
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0211044 . The symmetric normalization above
is related to its kernel by Laurent units absorbed into a_k.

2. Geometric finite inverses give actual examples for every order
----------------------------------------------------------------
Habiro's Claspers and finite type invariants of links, GT 4 (2000),
Theorem 5.4(1),(4), says that string knots modulo C_l form an abelian
group. Set Sigma=D^2, n=1, k=1 and l=N+1. Every one-strand string link
in this ball is homotopically trivial, so this includes every knot.
Section 6.4 identifies string-knot product with ordinary connected sum.
Theorem 6.18 identifies C_(N+1)-equivalence with equality of finite-type
invariants through degree N. Source:
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0001185 .

Choose an ACTUAL knot L_N representing the inverse of [L] in that
group. Thus L#L_N is C_(N+1)-equivalent to U. Define R_N=K#L_N.
The h^j coefficient of any fixed-color Jones polynomial at q=e^h is
finite type of order at most j. This follows directly by resolving
singular crossings: each difference of the quantum crossing operators
is O(h), so N+1 singular crossings annihilate all coefficients <=N.
Knot-independent division by the quantum dimension preserves the
statement for reduced colors. Primary finite-type reference: Bar-Natan,
On the Vassiliev knot invariants, Topology 34 (1995), Theorem 5 and
Remark 2.2, citing Lin and Piunikhin:
https://www.math.toronto.edu/~drorbn/papers/OnVassiliev/OnVassiliev.pdf .

Consequently, for each fixed m,

  H_m(L_N) H_m(L)=1 mod h^(N+1),
  H_m(R_N)=H_m(K)/H_m(L) mod h^(N+1).

The denominator has constant coefficient 1. Taking m=2 gives the
ordinary unknot jet through order N. Taking m>=3 and using (1), for
N>=5, gives

  H_m(R_N;e^h)=1+8(m^2-1)(m^2-4)h^5+O(h^6).

This is an existence construction from a geometric clasper theorem;
we have not produced explicit PDs for L_N. The knots are nevertheless
actual individual knots, not rational combinations or virtual knots.

Moreover V_R_N=V_K V_L_N cannot equal 1: V_K has five nonzero terms and
is not a unit of Z[q,q^-1]. A factor of a unit in this integral ring
would itself be a unit. Thus none of these R_N is a counterexample.
They are deliberately composite, and are nontrivial independently of
the Jones detection conjecture.

3. Sparsity and global size consequences
---------------------------------------
If f(q)=sum_(i=1)^s b_i q^(e_i) is a nonzero Laurent polynomial with
distinct exponents and vanishes at q=1 to order at least N+1, then
s>=N+2. Otherwise its first s exponential moments give
sum b_i e_i^j=0, 0<=j<s; the Vandermonde matrix is invertible.
Apply this to f=V_R_N-1. Adding back 1 removes at most one monomial:

  support(V_R_N-1)>=N+2,
  support(V_R_N)>=N+1,
  rank_Q reducedKh(R_N)>=||V_R_N||_1>=N+1.

The exponent span of V_R_N-1 is also at least N+1. Thus these finite
inverses cannot be compressed into a uniformly bounded Jones support.
For each FIXED N, the actual knots R_N#...#R_N (r factors) still have
the same ordinary unknot jet through degree N. Schubert's connected-sum
formula gives bridge number r(b(R_N)-1)+1. The reduced-Q Khovanov
Kunneth formula gives rank (rank reducedKh(R_N))^r. Both are unbounded.
Therefore no bound depending only on ANY fixed finite ordinary-Jones
Taylor jet can replace the full-polynomial bound in our global scaffold,
even when the input jet is the unknot's. This is stronger than a purely
formal perturbation counterexample.

4. What group-likeness actually allows
-------------------------------------
Use a standard zero-framed, long-knot normalization of the Kontsevich
invariant Z, multiplicative for connected sum and group-like. The ratio

  G=Z(K) Z(L)^-1

is a legitimate element of the completed group, and log G is primitive.
Every finite truncation of G is realized by the actual R_N above. Its
entire fundamental sl_2 series is 1, whereas its color-three series has
fifth coefficient 320. These two conclusions hold in the completion;
they do not assert that G is Z of a single finite knot.

In the ordinary Jones h convention, the sl_2 weight of the degree-five
primitive part of log G is 8c(c-3), with Casimir eigenvalue c=m^2-1.
This follows from the ALL-color equation (1), not from color-three data
alone: equality of all lower coefficients makes the logarithmic leading
difference the same, and a polynomial identity follows from all m.
We identify this primitive's sl_2 image, not the primitive diagram itself.
It vanishes on both trivial and fundamental representations but not on
color three. Thus there is an actual-knot-derived primitive invisible
direction; connected diagrams need not be visible in the fundamental
representation. Group-like review: Ohtsuki, A Cabling Formula for the
2-Loop Polynomial of Knots, Publ. RIMS 40 (2004), Section 2, p.951:
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/40872 .

Do not equate two coproducts. Kontsevich group-likeness uses the diagram
coproduct splitting connected dashed components. Quantum cabling uses
the coproduct of U_h(sl_2), or distribution of legs onto parallel
skeleton strands. The latter does NOT say Delta z_K=z_K tensor z_K.
Parallel copies of the same long knot are not two independent locally
knotted strands. Centrality gives naturality on representations, not
monoidality of the resulting natural transformation.

This distinction is already visible before quantum deformation: a
single chord is primitive for the diagram coproduct but its Lie weight
is the quadratic Casimir c. In an orthonormal Lie algebra basis,
Delta(c)=c tensor 1+1 tensor c+2 sum_i X_i tensor X_i (up to the chosen
Casimir scale). It is not primitive for the enveloping-algebra
coproduct. Applying the weight system does not intertwine these two
coproducts, so an argument transferring group-likeness across them
fails at this elementary algebraic step.

Indeed z_K on V2 tensor V2 acts as 1 on its trivial summand and H3(K)
on its color-three summand. In contrast z_K tensor z_K acts as H2(K)^2
everywhere. For a nontrivial trefoil polynomial these already differ on
the trivial summand. If H2(K)=1 is assumed, imposing equality on this
first tensor square is EXACTLY H3(K)=1. It repackages the previously
identified missing color-promotion theorem; it does not prove it.

The completion is the crucial boundary. A single actual L_infinity
with Z(L_infinity)=Z(L)^-1 cannot exist: its ordinary Jones would be
the reciprocal of a nonunit Laurent polynomial. In fact the RATIO G
also cannot be realized by any single actual knot: its color-three
value is U3(K)/U3(L), because the common quantum dimension cancels.
The numerator has u-exponent span 40-6=34, while the denominator has
span 44-(-4)=48. A nonzero Laurent quotient would have span -14,
impossible because spans add under multiplication in a domain.

Thus G has an unavoidable color-three pole away from the formal
expansion point, although every finite truncation is realized by an
actual knot. Full Laurent polynomiality supplies a decisive exact
realizability obstruction for this particular construction. Finite
Taylor data cannot see it. This does not prove generic promotion:
the earlier formal cyclotomic example a0=1,a1=0,a2=q-1,ak=0 for k>=3
has H2=1 and H3!=1 while ALL colors are Laurent polynomials. Its
actual geometric realization remains unknown; we do NOT assert that
this second example has a group-like Jacobi-diagram lift, or actual
realizations of every finite truncation. These are two different
examples, not one object satisfying both sets of properties. Neither
finite-order geometric realization alone nor polynomiality alone
suffices.

5. Full conditional route, and why this pass does not close it
-----------------------------------------------------------
A logically complete quantum-to-classical route would be:

  (P) For every actual knot, H2=1 implies H3=1.
  Previously audited cabling/Habiro bootstrap then gives all H_m=1.
  (AJ) The AJ conjecture identifies the classical specialization of
       the colored-Jones recurrence with the A-polynomial.
  Dunfield--Garoufalidis' theorem says the A-polynomial detects U.
  Therefore K=U.

The classical endpoint is a theorem, and AJ is an independently
motivated quantum/classical conjecture. Dunfield--Garoufalidis state
this endpoint explicitly as Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.4:
https://msp.org/agt/2004/4-2/agt-v4-n2-p21-s.pdf .
But P remains a distinct unproved assumption; calling it monoidal
naturality, primitive faithfulness, or unit-fiber reconstruction does
not make it independently established. The examples above disprove
every finite-order substitute with a uniform order. This pass supplies
an obstruction to a proposed mechanism, not a proof of P or AJ.

Physics warning: nonabelian flat-connection/A-polynomial data are
associated with varying color and semiclassical scaling. At fixed
fundamental color every actual Jones polynomial is a finite sum of
exponentials in h, so it is analytic at h=0 for EVERY knot. One cannot
infer absence of nonabelian geometry from the absence of an essential
singularity or a nonperturbative sector in this fixed-color function.
Moving from fixed color to the double-scaling regime is the missing
information step, not an automatic consequence of analytic continuation.

Assessment
----------
Shelve finite-order/group-like positivity as a promotion proof. The
useful new constraint on any future full proof is quantitative: it must
use an all-orders property tied to a single finite embedded knot and
must distinguish actual-knot finite approximations from their completed
limit. Laurent polynomiality does this for the specific ratio G, but
is insufficient for arbitrary formal cyclotomic data. No condition
closing the generic promotion gap was established in this pass.
