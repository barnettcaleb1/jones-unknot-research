Independent braid/fusion audit of the fixed-Jones Watson family
1 November 2026 UTC. Scope: all-parameter fusion argument, normalization,
and the implication to unbounded colored span. I did not independently
regenerate the cabled diagrams in this pass.

Reviewed verify_colored_family.py, colored_family_certificate.json, and
the tangle agent's construction description. The ordinary family uses
alpha=sigma1^3 sigma2^-1 sigma1 and the mirrored six-strand Watson
template, with end tangles 2m and -2m. These are simple integer tangles;
Watson's invariance mechanism supplies the common whole ordinary Jones
polynomial. The stored diagrams have knot connectivity and writhe0.

1. All-integer fusion is valid, not an extrapolation from samples.

The dimension-three color has label2. Tensoring two copies gives labels
0,2,4. With theta_j=(-1)^j A^(j(j+2)), the full-twist eigenvalues are

  theta_0/theta_2^2=A^-16,
  theta_2/theta_2^2=A^-8,
  theta_4/theta_2^2=A^8.

For t=A^-4, their exponents are4,2,-2. Global inversion of all crossing
conventions gives -4,-2,2, without changing the difference set. Two
opposite 2m-crossing boxes therefore yield a finite sum

 H3(K_m)=sum_(k in {0,+/-2,+/-4,+/-6}) c_k(t)t^(km)

with rational coefficients independent of m. One can work over a fixed
square-root extension if a chosen fusion basis requires it; this does
not affect the span conclusion. Negative m is covered by inverse full
twists, whose eigenvalues remain nonzero generically.

2. Framing is controlled by the actual diagram.

Both variable tangles have even crossing count, so their endpoint
connectivity and induced strand orientations are independent of m.
Their oriented writhe contributions are linear in m. The fixed middle
braid has zero writhe, and the opposite variable contributions cancel.
The raw m0,m1 diagram checks are consistent with, and calibrate the signs
of, this all-m cancellation; merely observing many sampled writhes would
not otherwise be a general proof. With total writhe0 there is no extra
m-dependent correction from the colored zero-framing normalization.

The Seifert-framed two-parallel relation used in the script is

  bracket(K_zero_parallel^2)=1+(delta^2-1)H3(K).

Its raw bracket is delta times the normalized oriented Jones polynomial
of that zero-framed two-parallel. Thus

  H3(K)=(delta V_zero_parallel-1)/(delta^2-1),

with delta=-x-x^-1 and x=sqrt(t), is correct. The unknot test gives1.

3. Two unequal samples imply genuine span growth.

Suppose only one generic frequency survived, so H3(K_m)=c(t)t^(km).
The knot normalization H3(K_m)(1)=1 gives c(1)=1. The universal identity
H3(K_m)'(1)=0 then gives c'(1)+km=0 for every m, hence k=0. Such a
sequence would be constant in m. The two unequal stored full colored
polynomials therefore establish at least two surviving generic modes.

After multiplying by one fixed denominator, the coefficient functions
become Laurent polynomials. For sufficiently large positive or negative
m, the maximal and minimal exponents come from the unique extreme
surviving slopes, so their difference grows linearly in |m|. Dividing
back by the fixed denominator changes span by a fixed constant only.
This proves unbounded whole-polynomial H3 span in this one ordinary
Jones fiber.

At t=4 the fitted modes +/-4 are nonzero. Given the fusion theorem this
also proves that those two generic coefficients are nonzero, and yields
at least8|m|+O(1) span growth. It does NOT prove that generic coefficients
at +/-2 or +/-6 vanish just because they vanish at this specialization.
The eighth sample is an independent arithmetic consistency check; the
fusion theorem, not the finite recurrence fit, proves the all-m claim.

4. Scope.

This falsifies the proposed bound on dimension-three colored-Jones span
throughout every whole ordinary-Jones fiber. It does not falsify a bound
on ordinary reduced Khovanov rank or quantum diameter in such a fiber;
indeed Watson's simple family has a different known homological
invariance. It supplies neither a Jones-one knot nor a nontrivial H3-unit
knot, and it does not obstruct one-way statements restricted solely to
the unit fiber.
