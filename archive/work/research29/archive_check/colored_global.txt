WHOLE ORDINARY JONES DOES NOT BOUND COLOR-THREE SPAN
1 November 2026 UTC. Bounded research deduction, not a priority claim.

Outcome and complete proof scaffold

There is an explicit family of actual knots K_m, m in Z, whose entire
ordinary Jones polynomial is fixed, whereas the span of the reduced,
zero-framed dimension-three colored Jones polynomial H3(K_m;t) is
unbounded (indeed grows linearly in |m|). Its value at t=4 is also
unbounded. The finite exact diagram calculations below are witnesses;
local fusion proves the assertion for all integers, not extrapolation.

Why this answers a global proof-route question: suppose ONE specified
nonempty ordinary Jones fiber F had uniformly bounded H3 span. Choose
J in that fiber. For any hypothetical V_K=1, all J#K^{#r} remain in F.
Multiplicativity and Laurent span additivity force span H3(K)=0.
H3(K;1)=1 and H3'(K;1)=0 then force H3(K)=1. Thus such a fiber bound
would supply universal ordinary-to-color-three promotion. That promotion
is only an intermediate step: an independently justified promotion to
all colors and an all-colored unknot detector would still be required
for a full proof. Neither is supplied by this pass.

The universal version, 'every fixed ordinary Jones fiber has bounded
H3 span,' is FALSE by the family below. Existence of some specially
chosen bounded fiber is not disproved. Allowing the unspecified fiber
to be the unit fiber merely repackages the promotion issue. This example
does not falsify the separate proposed bounds on bridge number or
ordinary Khovanov rank in a Jones fiber: its twisting template is fixed.
No unbounded support-size or coefficient-norm assertion is made solely
from unbounded span. The ordinary polynomial here is nonunit; this is
neither a Jones-one counterexample nor an H3-one counterexample.

Actual geometry and ordinary Jones constancy

Use Watson's six-strand closure L_beta(T,U), Figure 7 of [W]. In braid
word notation take

 alpha = [1,1,1,-2,1],
 beta  = [1,1,1,-2,1,-5,-5,-5,4,-5],
 K_m   = L_beta(<2m>,<-2m>).

The fixed braid is i0(alpha)i3(s(alpha)), where s(sigma1)=sigma2^-1,
s(sigma2)=sigma1^-1. Top and bottom outside caps connect ports 1 and 6;
the four ports of each integer tangle occupy positions 2,3,4,5.
The exact executable endcap construction is in verify_colored_family.py.

[W] Section 8 gives this exact beta, identifies L_beta(0,0) with
5_2#mirror(5_2), and places its evaluation matrix in the form of
Proposition 5.1. That matrix is invariant under opposite twists;
Lemma 5.2 supplies Jones constancy when the writhe is unchanged.
Our even twists are pure, so every K_m has the same connectivity as
K_0 and is a knot. Endpoint orientations do not change with m. The
variable writhe contributions are opposite linear functions, while the
fixed mirrored braid has writhe zero. Thus total writhe is zero for
all integers, including negative m. The raw m=0,1 diagram checks
calibrate this orientation convention; the linearity proves all m.
Consequently

 V(K_m;t) = -t^5+2t^4-4t^3+6t^2-7t+9
            -7t^-1+6t^-2-4t^-3+2t^-4-t^-5.                 (1)

Exact color-three witness and framing

Set x=sqrt(t), delta=-(x+x^-1), Delta2=x^2+1+x^-2. For the actual
Seifert-framed two-parallel C_m of K_m, color decomposition gives

 H3(K_m;t) = (delta V(C_m;x)-1)/Delta2.                    (2)

The program explicitly calls Regina's Framing.Seifert, rather than
assuming a blackboard parallel is zero framed. Equation (2) follows
from V_fundamental tensor V_fundamental = V_trivial plus V_dimension3.
The unknot test gives (delta^2-1)/Delta2=1. Normalized fixed-color Jones
has H3(1)=1 and H3'(1)=0; the latter also follows from the absence of
nonconstant order-one finite-type knot invariants.

Eight stored actual PD diagrams, m=0,...,7, were regenerated and their
whole two-parallel Jones polynomials computed exactly. The minimal
witness consists of m=0 and m=1:

 H3(K_0;4) = 648962362809961849/1073741824,
 H3(K_1;4) = 27171282052396757/33554432.

The polynomials are unequal. Their t-spans for m=0,...,7 are
30,28,37,45,53,61,69,77. These numbers alone are not the growth proof.
Full Laurent coefficients are in colored_family_certificate.json and
watson_color3_samples.json.

Fusion gives the all-integer proof

The three-dimensional color has Jones-Wenzl label 2. Fusion of two
such strands has labels 0,2,4. Equations (2.7),(2.9) of [F] are universal
local skein identities. They do not require the global diagram to be
alternating. The twist scalars square to A^16,A^8,A^-8, or their
inverses under the opposite crossing convention. With t=A^-4, the
three full-twist frequencies are {-4,-2,2} or {4,2,-2}.

The boxes contain m and -m full twists. Their eigenvalue ratios have
frequency set

 E={-6,-4,-2,0,2,4,6}.

The total writhe cancellation above removes any m-dependent framing
shift. All remaining fusion coefficients and the division by the
quantum dimension are fixed rational functions, independent of m.
Therefore for EVERY integer m,

 H3(K_m;t) = sum_{k in E} c_k(t) t^(km).                   (3)

Coefficients can be taken in Q(t): solve the seven-by-seven Vandermonde
system using m=0,...,6, whose entries and outputs lie in Q(t). Inverse
full twists prove the same identity for negative m.

If only one c_k survived, (3) would be c(t)t^(km). At m=0, c(1)=1 and
c'(1)=0; at m=1, derivative zero forces k=0. This would make the whole
sequence constant, contradicting the exact two-diagram witness.
Hence at least two generic frequencies survive. Choose a fixed common
Laurent denominator D and put p_k=D c_k. For sufficiently large m,
the maximal exponent of sum p_k t^(km) comes from the unique greatest
surviving k, and the minimal exponent from the least. Thus

 span_t H3(K_m) = (k_max-k_min)|m| + O(1),                 (4)

with possibly different constants in the two directions. Dividing by
fixed D changes span by the fixed number span D. Since k_max>k_min,
this proves unbounded span.

A stronger exact specialization certificate

At t=4 the seven distinct numbers 4^k permit exact rational interpolation.
From m=0,...,6, with m=7 as an independent consistency check, (3) becomes

 H3(K_m;4) = a*4^(-4m) + b + c*4^(4m),
 a=-144137512715625/71303168,
 b=11054702264346151433/18253611008,
 c=14215924490625/17825792.                               (5)

The fusion theorem upgrades these exact initial values to all m.
Since a and c are nonzero, the generic modes -4 and +4 both survive;
(4) has slope at least 8. Formula (5) also proves divergence to +infinity
as m tends to +infinity, and to -infinity as m tends to -infinity.
It does NOT imply c_{+/-2}(t) or c_{+/-6}(t) vanish identically merely
because their values at t=4 vanish. No such claim is needed.

Reproduction and scope of verification

Portable, Python standard library only:
  python3 verify_colored_family.py
It reconstructs H3 by exact Laurent division, verifies normalization,
checks all eight whole ordinary polynomials agree, and checks (5) at
an eighth sample excluded from the seven-value interpolation.

Actual diagram regeneration (same directory files, Python 3.13):
  python3 verify_colored_family.py --diagrams --packages /absolute/path/to/packages
Alternatively set JONES_REPLAY_PACKAGES. Default local packages path is
../braid_classification/packages relative to this note's directory.
Verified runtime: Python 3.13.7, Regina 7.4, Spherogram 2.4.1. Local
interpreter /opt/homebrew/opt/python@3.13/bin/python3. Binary wheels in
the local packages directory are cp313; use a compatible interpreter.
Regeneration took about two seconds in this environment. It checks
raw component count and writhe, regenerates PDs, and computes actual
zero-framed two-parallel Jones polynomials. It passed on this pass.

The independent braid audit checked fusion, the all-m framing argument,
and span deduction. The independent homology audit checked the fixed
color normalization argument. These are mathematical/internal checks,
not external peer review or a claim of novelty.
