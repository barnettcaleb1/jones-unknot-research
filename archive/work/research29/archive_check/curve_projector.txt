0. Full endpoint before the local mechanism
--------------------------------------------
Let a,b be distinct curves in the five-punctured disk, each enclosing
three punctures. In the residual generic TL5 block [3,2], write

 rho(D_a)=A^-6 I+(A^6-A^-6)P_a,

where P_a has rank1. If P_a=P_b, D_a D_b^-1 is an actual nontrivial
exponent-zero braid in the residual kernel. The normal-intersection
argument of Long combines this kernel with the known Burau kernel at
the SAME strand count. The intersection is a nontrivial full TL5 kernel;
Ito then produces nontrivial hyperbolic Jones-one knots.

This is a complete conditional counterexample route. The missing step
is an actual distinct-curve equality P_a=P_b. Pass28 excluded different
enclosed subsets. This pass does not produce an equality for the
remaining same-subset case.

Ito: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.2028
The projector formula, classical S5 model, and exact verification are
in work/research28/braid_structure.txt and verify_rank_one_twists.py.

1. A geometric all-parameter restriction within the same-subset case
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix a curve a enclosing a set S of three punctures. Let h1,...,hk be
half twists on pairwise disjoint embedded arcs, each joining a puncture
of S to a puncture of its complement. Here k is1 or2. The arcs may wind
arbitrarily in the disk; their actual disjointness is required. Put

   g=h1^(2m1) ... hk^(2mk),       mi integers.

Because g is pure, g(a) encloses the SAME subset S.

THEOREM. If P_(g(a))=P_a, then every mi=0.

This rules out exact cancellation by compensating twists on two disjoint
cross-partition arcs, including opposite exponents and arbitrary winding.
It is a theorem about generic matrices, not just a geometric observation
or a finite computation. It does not cover overlapping arcs or a product
of several successive disjoint-arc layers.

Proof. The half-twist matrices commute and have distinct generic
eigenvalues A and -A^-3. Their spectral projectors are regular at A=1,
since these eigenvalues specialize to1 and-1. Thus the joint spectral
decomposition is regular there. Let v(A) be a regular nonzero vector
spanning im(P_a), chosen so that v(1) is the vector w_S from pass28.

At A=1, each hi becomes the transposition of its endpoints. On the
five-dimensional edge-function model for [3,2], use the ordinary
positive inner product and normalize w_S to norm1. For one cross-partition
transposition s, <w_S,s w_S>=-1/3. Therefore the + and - eigenspace
components have squared norms1/3 and2/3, both nonzero.

For two disjoint cross-partition transpositions s1,s2, swapping both
changes two members of S, so <w_S,s1 s2 w_S>=+1/3. The four joint
projectors are (1+epsilon1 s1)(1+epsilon2 s2)/4. The squared norms of
their components of w_S are

       (++):1/6,   (+-):1/6,   (-+):1/6,   (--):1/2.

Every joint channel therefore also has a nonzero component of v(A)
over Q(A). This conclusion uses only the regular specialization; it
does not assume coefficientwise positivity of the quantum matrices.

On a joint channel indexed by the subset E of negative eigenvalues,
rho(g) acts as

     A^(2 sum_i mi - 8 sum_(i in E) mi).

If P_(g(a))=P_a, its image line is invariant under rho(g), so v is an
eigenvector. Since v has nonzero component in EVERY joint channel,
all these monomials must agree. Comparing E=empty with E={i} forces
mi=0 for each i. This proves the theorem.

The regular specialization and branching inputs are Kasahara,
Propositions2.4 and2.5, not a conjectural faithfulness theorem:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0304
verify_joint_support.py independently checks the four rational weights
with standard-library exact arithmetic. There is no word enumeration.

2. The full surface skein algebra does distinguish the curves
------------------------------------------------------------
On a six-punctured sphere, the residual B5 space can be viewed as the
five-dimensional vacuum space with all six boundary colors equal to1.
Let C_a be the operator obtained by adding a fundamental-colored loop
parallel to a. The three punctures on each side admit only fusion labels
1 and3. The encircling eigenvalues in the Kauffman convention are

      mu_h=-A^(2h+2)-A^(-2h-2),       h=1,3.

Hence

      C_a=mu_1 I+(mu_3-mu_1)P_a,

and P_a=P_b is equivalent to C_a=C_b over Q(A).

In the UNIVERSAL surface skein algebra, distinct essential simple curves
are distinct elements of its multicurve basis. Embeddings into quantum
tori can detect these universal elements and their leading coordinates.
They do not say that a single fixed-color finite-dimensional quotient
distinguishes them. Detcherry--Santharoubane's construction explicitly
uses the sum over boundary colorings in its handlebody skein action:
Theorem1.1 and the paragraph following it; Section2.2 defines that sum.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.03291
Published version: Geometry & Topology29(2025),313–348.

Thus one cannot replace their varying colors by six fixed fundamental
colors without a new theorem. Increasing colors would supply additional
representations, rather than proving the required residual B5 identity.

3. Exactly what happens to positivity under the projection
----------------------------------------------------------
The finite representation has the exact polynomial relation

       (C_a-mu_1 I)(C_a-mu_3 I)=0.                 (2)

Its universal skein counterpart

       x=(a-mu_1 empty)(a-mu_3 empty)

is NONZERO: its leading multicurve term consists of two parallel copies
of a, with coefficient1. It is killed by the fixed-color representation.

There are two different notions of positivity to keep separate.

* Basis positivity concerns nonnegative multiplication coefficients in
  a chosen skein basis. It does not imply that the finite representation
  preserves a basis or keeps distinct positive basis elements distinct.
  In particular, it does not give an order relation between C_a and C_b.
  Thurston's original positive-basis theorem is at the classical
  specialization; even granting a quantum positive-basis statement does
  not fix this representation-theoretic issue.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4103368/

* Reflection positivity gives a genuine positive Hermitian form on the
  finite TL5 module on an appropriate open unitary arc. It is faithful
  on the represented matrix algebra. Pulled back to the UNIVERSAL skein
  algebra it is only semidefinite: x* x is nonzero but maps to zero by(2).
  Here reflection conjugates A to A^-1, fixes the curve, and fixes mu_h;
  x* x=x^2 has nonzero leading four-parallel-curve term. Thus this is an
  explicit positive-square element in the kernel, not dimension counting.

The last example does NOT assert that x*x has positive coefficients in
a bracelet basis. It is positivity in the *-algebra sense. It also does
NOT exhibit a difference of two simple curves in the kernel; that much
more restrictive question is precisely the remaining gate.

A norm computation of P_a-P_b can certify matrix equality, but zero norm
then yields equality of represented projectors only. To conclude a=b
requires the unproved injectivity statement for this particular set of
simple-curve operators. The ambient skein basis theorem and reflection
positivity do not supply it.

4. What could still justify a construction attempt
--------------------------------------------------
The disjoint-arc theorem says a proposed same-subset equality must use
genuinely overlapping operations, or a different geometric source of
stabilization of the quantum line. The generic projector must retain
both its image and kernel; fixing only its classical vector or one
matrix coefficient is insufficient.

A useful next input would predict equality in the finite skein quotient
for two actual embedded simple curves while separately proving they are
not isotopic. For example, one would need a geometric move whose entire
skein difference lies in the boundary-color annihilator ideal and whose
output is again a SINGLE embedded curve. Equation(2) alone cannot do
that: it is a linear combination of multicurves, not a geometric curve
move or a braid word.

No such move or relation emerged in this pass. Conversely, proving that
the annihilator ideal contains no nonzero difference of the relevant
simple curves would establish the desired projector injectivity, but
we have not reduced that statement to a known positivity theorem. It
should not be described as an easier solved gate or as evidence of
proximity to Jones unknot detection. The concrete new result here is the
all-integer disjoint-cross-partition-twist exclusion above, together with
an explicit explanation of why universal positivity does not survive
as a faithful geometric test in the five-dimensional quotient.
