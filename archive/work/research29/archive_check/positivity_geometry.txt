PASS 29: SURFACE QUANTUM-TRACE POSITIVITY DOES NOT SURVIVE S^3 CLOSURE
Status: precise obstruction to a proposed proof mechanism; no Jones counterexample or detection proof.

1. Full-proof target before the local test

A useful positivity mechanism would have to do BOTH of the following: construct a geometrically controlled positive surface-state presentation of an actual knot, and show that its S^3 closure controls the amount of categorified information surviving the gluing. For example, a constructive finite state bound depending on one entire Jones polynomial P would bound reduced rational Kh on that whole fiber. The already audited connected-sum amplification would then prove generic unknot detection. Surface Laurent positivity alone supplies neither the categorified gluing bound nor the closure bound.

The precise tempting shortcut tested here is: positive quantum traces admit a positive, coercive S^3 closure, so large positive state mass cannot disappear. This shortcut is FALSE, even for one fixed punctured torus, and even when restricted to actual zero-framed unknot curves for the coercivity assertion. Thus cluster positivity cannot simply replace the missing geometric cancellation theorem.

2. Exact primary input and conventions

Let S be a punctured surface, A=omega^-2, and Tr_T^omega the Bonahon–Wong quantum trace into the square-root quantum torus of an ideal triangulation T. Cho–Kim–Kim–Oh Theorem1.3 says that a nonperipheral essential simple closed curve, lifted with constant elevation and vertical framing, has Laurent-positive quantum trace in EVERY ideal triangulation: coefficients belong to Z_{≥0}[omega^{+/-1}]. Their proof is a geometric ordering theorem, not an Euler-characteristic positivity assumption. Source: Laurent positivity of quantized canonical bases for quantum cluster varieties from surfaces, arXiv1710.06217v2, Theorem1.3, PDFp5; precise parameter relation in §4.2, PDFp34: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.06217 .

Use the ordinary skein convention empty link=1 and a zero-framed trivial circle=delta=−A²−A^-2. Let E be the linear skein map induced by embedding S×I into S^3. It is NOT an algebra homomorphism in general: stacking in a surface collar need not become split union in S^3. Quantum-trace functoriality for cutting and gluing surfaces is not positivity for attaching compressing disks or handlebodies.

3. The positive cone meets the closure kernel

Take S to be a once-punctured standard Heegaard torus. Let mu be its meridian, essential and nonperipheral in S, but bounding a disk after embedding in S^3. Its vertical/surface framing is zero. Define

  X = mu + (A²+A^-2)·1.

This is a nonzero element of the surface skein module: mu and the empty multicurve are distinct simple-multicurve basis elements. Its quantum trace is universally Laurent-positive by Theorem1.3, linearity, and A=omega^-2. Nevertheless

  E(X)=delta+(A²+A^-2)=0.

The failure is unbounded. Put Y_n=mu+X^n. Quantum traces are algebra homomorphisms, and multiplication of positive quantum Laurent polynomials stays positive after reordering (the reordering coefficients are monomials). Thus every Y_n has universally positive quantum trace. On the subalgebra generated by mu, closure IS evaluation mu↦delta: parallel meridians become unlinks. Hence E(Y_n)=delta for every n. At omega=1 and all shear variables1, if Tr(mu)=M>0, the positive coefficient mass is M+(M+2)^n. No bound on positive mass can be extracted from the fixed unknot closure value on this cone.

The Y_n are positive skein combinations, NOT asserted to be single actual knots. The next example removes that realizability limitation for the failure of a presentation-wise bound.

4. Actual zero-framed unknots with unbounded positive quantum-trace mass

On the same standard punctured Heegaard torus, take the simple slope gamma_n=(1,n). Each gamma_n becomes the torus knot T(1,n), hence the unknot, in S^3. Its surface framing is n, since the torus push-off linking number for a (p,q) curve is pq. Restrict to n=2m. Correcting the framing to zero multiplies the skein by

  (−A³)^(-2m)=A^(-6m)

in the usual bracket convention (inverting A in the opposite convention changes nothing here). This is a POSITIVE Laurent monomial. Therefore the zero-framed skeins Z_m=A^(-6m) gamma_(2m) retain universal Laurent positivity, and

  E(Z_m)=delta,       V_{closure(Z_m)}=1

for every m. They are actual framed knots in the thickened surface; the scalar records their actual framing change.

Their positive trace mass in ONE fixed standard triangulation grows exponentially. At the classical specialization omega=1 with all shear coordinates1, the three adjacent primitive slope traces have values (3,3,3). Hikami's explicit positive coordinate formulas (3.4) give this directly. Successive twists in one primitive slope induce (x,y,z)↦(z,y,yz−x), Proposition3.4. Consequently the traces a_n for slopes(1,n) satisfy

  a_0=a_1=3,       a_(n+1)=3a_n−a_(n−1).

The first values are 3,3,6,15,39,102,267,699,1830. With lambda=(3+sqrt5)/2,

  a_n=(3/sqrt5)(lambda^(n−1/2)+lambda^(-n+1/2)),

so a_(2m) tends to infinity exponentially. Equivalently, this follows from the elementary SL2 matrices

  A0=[[2,1],[1,1]],       B0=[[2,−1],[−1,1]],

for which tr(A0)=tr(B0)=tr(A0 B0)=3 and Cayley–Hamilton gives the recurrence for tr(A0 B0^n). Because the quantum Laurent coefficients are nonnegative, evaluation of all their variables at1 is exactly their coefficient mass; the framing monomial becomes1. Primary coordinate source: K. Hikami, Note on Character Varieties and Cluster Algebras, SIGMA15(2019)003, Theorem3.1/equation(3.4) and Proposition3.4: https://sigma-journal.com/2019/003/sigma19-003.pdf .

This is an actual-knot countertest of any proposed bound on quantum-trace mass of arbitrary positive surface presentations in terms of the whole closed Jones polynomial. It is NOT a counterexample to H-rank: all these closures are unknots, with Kh rank1. A theorem minimizing over geometrically simplified surface presentations is not excluded; such a minimization/descent theorem would be additional geometric work, not a consequence of Laurent positivity.

5. The failure is not fixed by an overall sign or framing convention

A simple slope(2,3) curve on the punctured Heegaard torus has universally positive quantum trace, while its normalized closure is a trefoil. In one chirality convention

  V(t)=t+t³−t⁴.

Multiplication by a Laurent monomial or changing the overall sign cannot remove its mixed coefficient signs. Even the raw unframed bracket retains mixed signs, since

  delta·V(t)=−t^(1/2)−t^(3/2)−t^(5/2)+t^(9/2),

where delta=−t^(1/2)−t^(-1/2). A surface-framing change only adds a monomial unit. Thus a coefficientwise-positive evaluation on all simple-curve quantum traces cannot reproduce the actual closure map under any overall normalization.

There is also a direct multiplicativity check. For primitive meridian and longitude curves alpha,beta on a standard torus, the two stacking orders give an unlink and a Hopf link after closure. With consistent choice of ordering, their raw bracket values are delta² and −delta(A⁴+A^-4), respectively. This follows as well by applying the two smoothing relations to their single surface intersection. The two primitive smoothings are unknots with surface framings+1 and−1. Thus treating S^3 closure as substitution of positive numbers into quantum coordinates is incorrect even before asking about homology. The torus product-to-sum framework is Frohman–Gelca, Theorem1: https://homepage.divms.uiowa.edu/~frohman/torus.pdf .

6. Precise scope and stop condition

The established quantum positivity theorem survives changes of ideal triangulation; it does not survive arbitrary 3-dimensional filling. An ordered positive cone cannot simply be pushed through filling: it already contains a nonzero kernel element X, and the actual unknot family has unbounded positive trace mass at fixed closed value. The problem occurs at the geometric passage from a surface collar to S^3, not at the choice of triangulation or proof of cluster positivity.

No claim is made that every conceivable cone, categorification, or optimized presentation fails. To revive this route, one needs a concrete closure-sensitive simplification or matching construction that removes the entire unknot family above and controls analogous compression behavior for arbitrary knots. Proposing positivity only after the S^3 skein quotient supplies no extra information by itself: that quotient is already the one-variable bracket module. Without a new geometric construction of this kind, stop the cluster-positivity-to-H-rank route; additional verification of surface positivity cannot address the missing implication.

7. Independent audit of the peer's colored-twist frequency inference

Suppose an actual knot family has H_m(t)=sum_k c_k(t)t^(km), with fixed coefficients independent of the integer twist parameter m, and normalized color-three polynomials H_m(1)=1, H'_m(1)=0. If only one generic mode survives, H_m=c(t)t^(km). The m=0 case identifies c=H_0, so c is an actual Laurent polynomial regular at1, with c(1)=1 and c'(1)=0. Differentiating the m=1 case gives 0=c'(1)+k c(1)=k. Thus the only possible one-mode family is constant. Any independently certified H_0 != H_1 therefore forces at least two generic twist modes. This argument does not assume regularity of all individual coefficients in a multi-mode decomposition; it only uses regularity of the sole coefficient after the one-mode hypothesis. This audits the inference only, not the peer's separate diagram computations.
