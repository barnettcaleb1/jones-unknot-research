PASS 30 — THE CURVE COLLISION GATE IS EXACTLY THE RESIDUAL FAITHFULNESS GATE
1 November 2026 UTC. Bounded architecture audit; no braid-word scan.

Outcome
=======
No new generic Jones kernel was constructed. There is, however, an exact
architectural conclusion: finding two distinct three-puncture curves with the
same residual B5 projector is equivalent to finding a nontrivial kernel of
that residual representation. Fixing the first curve in advance and requiring
the same enclosed puncture subset does not weaken the equivalence. Thus this
geometric formulation should not be advertised as a simpler intermediate
hypothesis. The equivalence below is derived here; no novelty claim is made.

1. Definitions and the complete counterexample endpoint
======================================================
Work over Q(A), with raw Kauffman bracket braid generators
 rho(sigma_i) = A I + A^-1 e_i, delta = -A^2-A^-2.
Let rho denote the five-dimensional TL5 residual block [3,2], equivalently
through-label 1. For a simple closed curve a enclosing exactly three of the
five marked points in the disk, let D_a be its positive Dehn twist. The
branching calculation, already checked exactly in research28, gives
 rho(D_a) = A^-6 I + (A^6-A^-6) P_a,
where P_a is a rank-one idempotent. It is equivariant:
 P_(g(a)) = rho(g) P_a rho(g)^-1.

A collision P_a=P_b with a != b gives D_a D_b^-1 != 1 in ker rho and has
exponent sum zero. Its normal closure is nontrivial and noncentral. Intersect
it with the known nontrivial generic B5 Burau kernel, using Long's normal
intersection lemma, to obtain a nontrivial common kernel. The scalar TL5 block
is also killed because the exponent sum is zero. This is a FULL generic TL5
kernel, not merely a trace collision or a colored specialization.

Ito's Theorem 1 then gives, in this normal subgroup times a fixed unknot braid,
hyperbolic knot closures of arbitrarily large genus. The full Jones operator
and writhe correction agree with the unknot, so these would be genuine
Jones-one counterexamples. Nontriviality is supplied by the theorem; it is not
inferred from a nontrivial braid word. Primary endpoint and Long locator:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.2028
 Theorem 1, Corollaries 1–2, and the reference to Long Lemma 2.1 on p1.

2. Exact equivalence theorem
============================
Fix any three-puncture curve a0. The following are equivalent:
 (i) rho is not faithful;
 (ii) there exist distinct three-puncture curves a,b with P_a=P_b;
 (iii) there is b != a0, enclosing the same puncture subset as a0,
       with P_b=P_a0;
 (iv) the inverse image under rho of the matrix centralizer of P_a0
      strictly contains the geometric stabilizer of a0.
Here the stabilizer fixes the curve's isotopy class; the matrix centralizer
condition is literal commutation with P_a0.

Proof of the topology input.
The action of B5 on all three-puncture curve classes has kernel exactly the
center. Suppose g fixes all such classes. It fixes every three-element subset
of the punctures, so its permutation is trivial. For any two-puncture curve c,
choose two three-puncture curves a,b containing c together with two distinct
outside punctures, arranged with geometric intersection two. A regular
neighborhood of a union b is a four-holed sphere. Its complementary regions
are: the disk containing c's two punctures; two one-puncture disks; and the
outside region, which contains the last puncture and meets the disk boundary.
The two-puncture region is distinguished by these markings. The isotopy
classes of a,b determine this neighborhood up to isotopy (equivalently take
minimal representatives and the filled regular neighborhood). Thus a pure
g fixing a,b fixes c. Since c was arbitrary, g fixes every two-puncture curve.
The boundary of a regular neighborhood of a connecting arc and its two
endpoints determines that arc class. Hence g fixes each standard half-twist
arc; conjugation preserves the positive half twist, and g commutes with all
Artin generators. Therefore g is central. Conversely boundary twists fix
all these curves. The geometric part of this argument was independently
audited by the homology agent during this pass.

Proof (i) => (ii), including the same-subset condition.
At A=1, rho factors through a faithful five-dimensional irreducible S5
representation (shape [3,2], or its sign transpose under other conventions).
It is faithful because the only possible proper nontrivial normal kernel is
A5, and an irreducible representation of S5/A5 has dimension one. Therefore
any generic kernel braid k is pure. The central full twist Delta5^2 acts as
A^-12 I, so rho is injective on the center. A nontrivial kernel braid is
therefore noncentral. By the topology input, it moves some three-puncture
curve a. Equivariance gives P_(k(a))=P_a. Purity ensures identical puncture
subsets. Finally conjugate both curves by a braid carrying a to a0; this
produces (iii). Such a braid exists since all three-puncture curves form one
B5 orbit.

Proof (ii) => (i).
The displayed twist formula gives rho(D_a D_b^-1)=I. Distinct essential
curves have distinct positive Dehn twists, so the braid is nontrivial.

Equivalence with (iv).
P_(g(a0))=P_a0 is precisely rho(g) P_a0=P_a0 rho(g). Geometric stabilizers
always satisfy it, and an extra element gives a distinct-curve collision.
The reverse implication follows from (iii) and transitivity on curves with
the same enclosed subset by pure braids (or from any braid carrying a0 to b).

Framing convention check.
The raw generator has eigenvalues A (multiplicity three) and -A^-3
(multiplicity two), hence determinant A^-3. Every raw kernel braid has
exponent sum zero. Passing to Hecke normalization or transposing the Young
diagram changes parameter/scalar conventions but does not change this
exponent-zero kernel question. The center itself is not a usable kernel.

3. Known mapping-class reformulations do not remove the same gate
===============================================================
Kasahara, Remarks on the faithfulness of the Jones representations:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0304
 Proposition 2.4: branching; Proposition 2.5: symmetric-group specialization.
 Theorem 3.1: the sphere representation for a rectangle is faithful exactly
 when the associated smaller braid representation is faithful.
 Theorem 5.1: for the genus-two five-dimensional Jones representation,
 ker rho_genus2 = <hyperelliptic involution> direct-sum
                  (ker rho_genus2 intersect Torelli_2),
 and faithfulness on Torelli_2 is equivalent to the B5 [2,2,1] question.
This is the transpose convention for the residual block above. Consequently
moving to separating curves, genus-two Torelli, or its Johnson filtration
is a meaningful change of tools, but not a weaker sufficient hypothesis.

The relevant low-dimensional mapping-class classification does not force a
kernel here. Korkmaz's theorem in dimension at most 2g-1 reaches only dimension
three for genus two, not this dimension five:
 https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.4816
The later 3g-3 bounds likewise do not cover this five-dimensional genus-two
case; applying a higher-genus theorem would first require an actual extension
of this representation, which has not been constructed.

4. Why a skein annihilator does not yet yield an actual relation
==============================================================
On the six-fundamental-label sphere realization of this five-dimensional
space, a loop around three labels has two eigenvalues
 mu_1=-A^4-A^-4, mu_3=-A^8-A^-8.
Thus for every such curve a the skein element
 (a-mu_1 empty)(a-mu_3 empty)
is annihilated by the fixed-color representation, although it is nonzero in
the universal skein algebra: its leading multicurve is two parallel copies
of a with coefficient one. This is a concrete annihilator, not a dimension
argument. Its existence supplies no pair of simple curves a != b with the
same operator. That extra geometric realization statement is exactly the
faithfulness gate proved equivalent above.

Detcherry–Santharoubane's universal quantum-torus embedding is injective:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.03291 , Theorem 1.1 and Section 2.2.
It keeps the full skein action, including the direct sum over boundary colors.
It does not assert that restricting to six fixed fundamental labels remains
injective. Root-of-unity representation/classical-shadow results in the same
paper likewise concern a different representation-theoretic setting.

Freedman–Walker–Wang explicitly separate the issue:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0209150 , Theorem 5.1.
Every nontrivial braid is detected after SOME cabling. This does not give
faithfulness or nonfaithfulness of the fixed fundamental TL5 block. Likewise,
finite-order twist images at individual roots of unity do not give a single
nontrivial braid word invisible as a Laurent-polynomial identity.

5. A genuinely faithful geometric encoding, and its missing comparison
=====================================================================
Ng's noncommutative cord/arc encoding offers a rigorous independent way to
certify that a proposed geometric relation is nontrivial:
 https://sites.math.duke.edu/~ng/math/papers/knotII.pdf
 Proposition 2.7: polynomial degree recovers minimal arc height.
 Corollary 2.8: the arc encoding is injective.
 Proposition 2.9: the braid action on the cord algebra has exactly central
 kernel, and its extension with a distinguished boundary point is faithful.

This is a concrete model in which skein relations retain geometric information.
But its coefficients live in a noncommutative free algebra, not the fixed TL5
matrix quotient. No comparison theorem found in this pass carries its arc
height/noncancellation property through that quotient. It can certify the
nontriviality half of a future candidate; it does not produce Jones equality.

Decision and stop condition
===========================
The complete counterexample route remains valid. The proposed exact
projector collision is not an easier gate than residual nonfaithfulness.
No source-grounded construction producing such a collision was found.
Further local curve exclusions or unmotivated braid scans are not justified
by this pass. A worthwhile next kernel attempt needs an explicit mechanism
that realizes a fixed-color annihilator by a DIFFERENCE OF ACTUAL SIMPLE
CURVES (or another actual braid relation), with nontriviality independently
certified. Merely invoking skein positivity, quantum-torus injectivity,
point-pushing, finite roots, or low ambient dimension does not supply it.
