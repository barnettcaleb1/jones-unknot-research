BOUNDED STRATEGIC CHECK: FIXED WHOLE JONES POLYNOMIAL AND VOLUME
1 November 2026 UTC. No exhaustive literature claim.

No primary example was located of a sequence of hyperbolic KNOTS with one
exact, normalized Jones polynomial and unbounded hyperbolic volume.
This negative search result is not a proof of boundedness or a definitive
statement of the current literature.

Most directly relevant published primary source:
Champanerkar–Kofman, On links with cyclotomic Jones polynomials,
Algebraic & Geometric Topology 6 (2006), 1655–1668, introduction p1655:
 https://msp.org/agt/2006/6-4/agt-v6-n4-p03-s.pdf
 https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0605631
They explicitly describe all then-known infinite fixed-Jones hyperbolic
families, including Kanenobu and Watson, as finite-twist-site families and
therefore volume-bounded. Their own unbounded-volume result concerns
DISTINCT cyclotomic Jones polynomials, with increasing span, and does not
contradict boundedness on an exact Jones fiber.

Additional primary observations checked:
Stoimenow's 2004 conference research transcript, The second coefficient
of the Jones polynomial, p4, poses the fixed-polynomial/unbounded-volume
construction as the way to obstruct a volume upper bound and explains why
Kanenobu's series does not supply it:
 https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/papers/talk.pdf

The 2019 numerical paper Deep learning the hyperbolic volume of a knot
and 2025 Colored Jones Polynomials and the Volume Conjecture discuss
same-Jones knots with DIFFERENT volumes in finite censuses. Neither source
found here supplies unbounded volumes within one exact fiber:
 https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.05547
 https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18575

Consequences for the projective-beta proposal:
The finite ACTUAL twist-template conclusion supplies finitely many augmented
Dehn-filling parents, hence a uniform volume bound for hyperbolic members.
Thus a verified fixed-Jones/unbounded-volume family would indeed falsify
all-fiber beta boundedness. None was found in this bounded check. Even such
a family would not directly refute the weaker proposal that ONE specified
nonempty Jones fiber has bounded beta.
