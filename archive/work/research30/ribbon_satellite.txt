1 November 2026. Bounded audit, no counterexample or priority claim.

Full endpoint. To falsify hypothesis E, construct an ACTUAL ribbon
concordance J->K whose ordinary Jones polynomials agree but whose reduced
rational Khovanov ranks differ. A fixed satellite preserves an existing
ribbon concordance; it does not manufacture a missing one between two
knots with equal polynomials. For a full Jones proof, E would apply to
U->H#mirror(H); E itself remains unproved.

1. Exact obstruction to the most immediate use of the new Watson family.

For any fixed winding-zero pattern P with annular skein degree at most
two, its reduced satellite Jones polynomial has the form
  V(P(C))=a(t)+b(t) H3(C;t),
where a,b are companion-independent rational functions. This is the
even annular basis {1,S2}, with quantum dimensions and the fixed pattern
framing absorbed in a,b. Companion zero framing is essential. If b is
nonzero, the Laurent field has no zero divisors, so
  V(P(J))=V(P(K)) iff H3(J)=H3(K).
Thus a Whitehead-type pattern cannot erase the exact H3 difference
between the Watson m0 and m1 knots just computed. Nor can it turn that
entire fixed-Jones family into another fixed-Jones family. The b=0 case
is precisely scalar annular skein, an existing realizability gate, not
an automatic construction. This is a structural channel statement, not
a claim of new novelty.

Moreover the simple Watson family has constant ordinary Khovanov
homology. Even if a ribbon concordance between two members existed,
its ordinary reduced-Q complement would be zero. Applying a pattern
might change that fact, but it first requires BOTH an actual ribbon
movie and equal output Jones polynomials. Neither follows from the
ordinary collision or from each knot separately being ribbon.

2. A concrete published ribbon-satellite family fails before any search.

Cochran--Friedl--Teichner, New constructions of slice links,
arXiv:0709.2148, Section4.1, Figure4.3 and following Remark, supplies
infected knots I(K_i,C,eta_i) related by a ribbon movie to Wh(C). They
explicitly compute the Alexander polynomials independently of C.
After symmetric normalization they are
  Delta_I1=5-2(t+t^-1),
  Delta_I2=13-6(t+t^-1),
whereas Delta_Wh(C)=1. Hence their Conway a2 values are -2,-6,0.
The standard Jones identity V''(1)=-6a2 therefore gives 12,36,0.
Consequently neither family has equal Jones endpoints for ANY companion.
The direction convention of the ribbon movie does not affect this
polynomial exclusion. This is a full companion-family obstruction,
not a numerical knot-table check.
Primary full text: https://math.berkeley.edu/~teichner/Papers/Satellite.pdf
Satellite preservation primary reference: Baker, A note on the
concordance of fibered knots, Remark6,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.7646

3. What remains, and why this route was stopped.

No actual falsifier of E was obtained. Formal annular coefficients can
be chosen to cancel higher colors, but a formal skein combination is
not a single ribbon satellite pattern. The low-degree obstruction above
makes clear why ordinary collisions plus a Whitehead pattern do not
solve that realization problem. No new diagram scan was started.

At the parent's request this pass then pivoted to the more productive
RP2 graph route. The resulting complete bounded-beta-to-bridge theorem
is in projective_templates.txt. It advances a full conditional scaffold
without requiring a new ribbon realization: a beta bound on one whole
Jones fiber now implies Jones unknot detection via connected sums.
The Jones-to-beta bound remains unproved.
