Main result: for a classical knot K placed in a ball in RP3,
beta_Re(K)=c_S3(K). For any nullhomologous projective knot,
beta_Re(K) is at least the crossing number of either lifted component.
Only the integral Jones sector and knots are covered.

Consequently T(3,4) contradicts the local-knot specialization of
Conjecture 1.1(2) in Boyle--Spyropoulos, arXiv:2609.40122v1:
Jones span=5, Alexander span=6, hence beta_Re=c>=6>5.
This concerns an auxiliary conjecture, not Jones unknot detection.
There is no proof or counterexample to the main conjecture here.
These deductions received internal mathematical audits only; no external
review, publication acceptance, or novelty claim is made.
