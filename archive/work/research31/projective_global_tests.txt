PASS 31 — GLOBAL TESTS OF PROJECTIVE COMPRESSION
1 November 2026 UTC. Bounded research pass; no novelty claim.

Conclusion
==========
No known fixed-whole-Jones KNOT family found in this pass contradicts the
projective-beta bound. But finite actual twist templates impose two stronger,
concrete restrictions that are suitable falsification targets:

 (A) For every fixed color N, the Mahler measure M(H_N(K)) is uniformly bounded.
 (B) At every sufficiently high-order root of unity, H_N(K) takes only finitely
     many values across the family.

Here H_N is zero-framed, normalized by H_N(U)=1 and H_2=ordinary Jones.
Neither restriction says that colored-Jones degree span is bounded. Our
fixed-Jones Watson family with unbounded H_3 span therefore does NOT refute
projective compression. A fixed-Jones family violating (A) or (B) would.

Input from the independently audited projective geometry
=======================================================
Bounded minimized beta gives finitely many actual RP3 diagrams with variable
integer two-strand twist boxes. Each twist ball has two lifts to S3. For a
classical knot placed locally in RP3 the lift is K disjoint-union K, split.
After fixing the finitely many box parities, the component connectivity is
fixed. Selecting either component and deleting the other gives a member of
one of finitely many classical twist templates: a box is retained if both
of its strands lie in that component, and disappears if at most one does.
Thus the arguments below apply directly to K. Alternatively the unreduced
colored invariant of the split lift bounds the square of H_N(K).

1. Uniform denominator-cleared coefficient bound
================================================
Fix one template and one parity class; write its integer twists as epsilon_i
+2m_i. Put j=N-1. Generic SU(2) fusion gives
 V_j tensor V_j = direct-sum_{a=0}^j V_(2a).
A full twist on the a channel acts by
 A^[4a(a+1)-2j(j+2)].
This follows from theta_l=(-1)^l A^[l(l+2)] and the ratio
 theta_(2a)/theta_j^2. Projectors and the rest of the diagram are fixed over
Q(A). The zero-framing correction is another monomial whose exponent is
affine in the m_i within the chosen parity/connectivity class.

Consequently there are fixed rational functions c_nu(A) and integral affine
functions ell_nu(m) such that
 H_N(K_m; A^-4) = sum_nu c_nu(A) A^[ell_nu(m)].
The sum has finitely many terms independent of the twisting parameters.
Choose a fixed nonzero integral Laurent polynomial D clearing all denominators
and let p_nu=D c_nu be integral Laurent polynomials. Then
 ||D H_N(K_m; A^-4)||_1 <= sum_nu ||p_nu||_1 = C,
for ALL integer m, including simultaneous compensating twists and negative
parameters. Exponent collisions can only decrease this coefficient-norm
upper bound. A maximum/product over finitely many templates and parity
classes yields a common denominator and bound for the whole bounded-beta
class at that N.

This is stronger than merely deriving convergence along generic rays. It
covers rays on which some linear combinations of twist parameters stay
bounded, including exact Jones-constant compensation lattices.

2. Mahler and root-distribution consequences
===========================================
Mahler measure satisfies M(PQ)=M(P)M(Q), M(P)<=||P||_1, and monomial factors
have measure one. Therefore
 M(H_N(K_m)) = M(H_N(K_m; A^-4)) <= C/M(D).
The substitution A->A^-4 preserves Mahler measure. This proves (A).
For any epsilon>0, the number of nonzero roots, counted with multiplicity,
outside the annulus 1-epsilon<|z|<1+epsilon is uniformly bounded, by applying
the Mahler bound to the polynomial and its reciprocal. Large degree alone
can be accommodated by roots accumulating near the unit circle.

Primary background, with normalization caveat:
Champanerkar–Kofman, On the Mahler measure of Jones polynomials under twisting,
Algebraic & Geometric Topology 5 (2005), 1–22:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0404236
 Theorem 2.4 treats colored invariants and multiple twisting; Theorem 2.5
 treats roots; Corollary 3.2 treats fixed coefficient blocks. Their J_N is
 unreduced. Dividing by the fixed quantum dimension changes no Mahler measure,
 because that dimension is a monomial times a product of cyclotomic factors.
The uniform coefficient estimate above also follows directly from their
finite spectral-expansion method and does not require a generic-ray limit.

Silver–Stoimenow–Williams, Euclidean Mahler measure and twisted links,
AGT6(2006), 581–602, proves related bounded-twist Mahler statements:
 https://msp.org/agt/2006/6-2/agt-v6-n2-p04-s.pdf
Do not apply its alternating Alexander statement to arbitrary knots.

3. Finite values at roots of unity
==================================
Take A=zeta of order L>4N(N-1). This explicit bound is sufficient, not sharp.
The full-twist eigenvalue exponents above differ by
 4[a(a+1)-b(b+1)],
whose absolute value is at most 4j(j+1)=4N(N-1). Hence distinct channels have
distinct eigenvalues at zeta. The relevant quantum dimensions do not vanish:
the roots of the quantum integers through N have orders at most 4N, which
is below the chosen threshold (with the same strict bound also covering N=2).
The full-twist operator is therefore diagonalizable with eigenvalues that
are L-th roots of unity, and its L-th power is the identity. Framing factors
are roots of unity as well; using period 2L if necessary covers their signs.

Each variable box consequently has a finite period. Finitely many boxes,
parity classes, and templates give a FINITE image for H_N(-;zeta^-4).
Equivalently one can choose any root avoiding the finite set of local fusion
singularities; the large-order condition is an easy uniform sufficient choice.
This proves (B). It is a statement about exact complex values, not about
numerical closeness. A family with infinitely many such exact values at ONE
allowed root would refute all-fiber bounded-beta, regardless of its volumes.

4. Checks against actual fixed-Jones families
============================================
• The previously certified Watson example has two variable twist boxes.
  Its H_3 has a fixed finite exponential expansion and therefore automatically
  satisfies both (A) and (B). Its values at t=4 grow, but t=4 is off the unit
  circle and the twist eigenvalues need not have modulus one. Its linear
  colored degree growth is not a compression obstruction.

• A recent primary example also remains a finite-template family:
  Kanenobu, Infinitely many knots with the same polynomial invariant, II,
  version dated 14 October 2025, Theorem 1 and Section 2:
   https://www.mathsoc.jp/publication/JMSJ/pdf/JMSJ9541.pdf
  The knots share HOMFLYPT and Q polynomials, hence ordinary Jones, but are
  distinguished by Kauffman polynomial. Section 2 places the entire family
  in a single diagram K(k,l,m) with THREE integer two-strand boxes. Variation
  of the stronger polynomial therefore supplies no contradiction to (A)/(B).
  This pass did not independently recompute those published polynomials.

• Traczyk's often-cited fixed-Jones five-braid examples are LINKS:
  3-Braids with proportional Jones polynomials, Kobe J.Math.15(1998),187–190:
   https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/E0003672/E0003672.pdf
  The abstract specifies three components. The starting three-braids have
  proportional Jones polynomials; connected sums cancel monomial factors
  but give three-component links. They cannot be treated as a counterexample
  involving one whole Jones fiber of KNOTS.

No primary source located in this bounded search provides a fixed-Jones knot
family with unbounded bridge number, unbounded fixed-color Mahler measure,
or infinitely many fixed-color values at a single allowed root. This is
search uncertainty, not a theorem that all Jones fibers satisfy these bounds.

5. What not to use as a template obstruction
==========================================
A finite number of twist sites does NOT bound crossing number, Seifert genus,
signature, colored degree span, or determinant. The two-strand torus family
already disproves such unconditional implications. Even Alexander Mahler
measure can grow without bound in a fixed twist-knot template: its Alexander
polynomial has the form n(t+t^-1)-(2n+1), up to chirality/convention, whose
Mahler measure grows with n. Varying Alexander module structure alone does
not imply unbounded numbers of generators; the original Kanenobu family
has bridge number three.

Thus a proposed counterexample must force growth of a quantity actually
bounded by the template structure, not just a stronger invariant omitted
from the ordinary Jones polynomial.

6. Scope of the resulting strategy
=================================
These are necessary consequences of geometric compression and usable tests
of the ALL-FIBER version. They provide no Jones-to-beta inequality or global
normal form. The weaker target that ONE specified nonempty Jones fiber has
bounded beta survives a counterexample in another fiber.

A redundant but exact logical check: if one fiber were beta-bounded and
V_K=1, then H_N(J#K^{#m})=H_N(J)H_N(K)^m and (B) would force H_N(K)(zeta)
to have modulus one at a dense set of roots, apart from finitely many zeros.
Continuity gives constant unit-circle modulus, so H_N(K) is a Laurent
monomial; H_N(1)=1 and H_N'(1)=0 force H_N(K)=1. This is a CONSEQUENCE of
the geometric hypothesis, not promotion from ordinary Jones alone. It adds
no endpoint to the existing argument: the bridge-number theorem already
forces K=U, and detection by all colored Jones polynomials is not assumed.
