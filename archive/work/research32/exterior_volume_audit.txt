INDEPENDENT AUDIT: THE EXTERIOR-VOLUME ENDPOINT
1 November 2026 UTC. Audit of generic_tangle's geometric_mechanism.txt.
Status: the stated conditional endpoint passes. No bound on a whole Jones
fiber has been proved. No use of a colored volume conjecture is needed.

1. Definition and topology
==========================
For a knot K in S^3 set

 v(K)=sum of hyperbolic volumes of the JSJ pieces of E(K),

equivalently v_3 times the relative simplicial volume. This is defined for
composite and satellite knots, not only hyperbolic knots. Its zero set is
the graph knots. It is additive under connected sum.

I independently opened and checked these primary-source passages:

* Motegi–Takata, The slope conjecture for graph knots, v3, Introduction,
  paragraph after Theorem 1.2: graph knots are generated from unknots by
  cabling and connected sums, citing Gordon, Corollary 4.2; they are exactly
  knots of zero Gromov volume.
  https://arxiv.org/html/1501.01105v3
  The paper's earlier-version slope-proof gap does not concern these
  classical classification statements; v3 explicitly replaces that lemma.

* Detcherry–Kalfagianni–Yang, Turaev-Viro invariants, colored Jones
  polynomials and volume, Section 5, printed p18/PDF page index17:
  defines the JSJ hyperbolic sum and explicitly recalls Soma's additivity
  under connected sums and split unions.
  https://users.math.msu.edu/users/kalfagia/paper1-23.pdf
  Original: Soma, The Gromov invariant of links, Inventiones 64 (1981),
  445–454, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01389276 .

Thus it is legitimate to use v(J#K^#m)=v(J)+m v(K). One must not substitute
the volume of the branched double cover, nor count only knots that are
themselves hyperbolic. Those different quantities do not give this proof.

2. Independent algebra audit of the zero-volume escape
=====================================================
Use symmetric Delta_K(t)=Delta_K(t^-1), Delta_K(1)=1. Then Delta'_K(1)=0
and a2(K)=Delta''_K(1)/2 is the z^2 Conway coefficient. In exponential
coordinate h, Delta_K(e^h)=1+a2(K)h^2+O(h^4).

Define cable_(p,q)(K) to have LONGITUDINAL WINDING p, so its curve class is
p lambda+q mu. This definition matters: some authors interchange p and q.
Take p>=1 and gcd(p,q)=1. The satellite Alexander identity gives

 Delta_cable(t)=Delta_T(p,q)(t) Delta_K(t^p).

The symmetric torus polynomial satisfies, for nonzero p,q,

 Delta_T(p,q)(e^h)
   = sinh(pq h/2) sinh(h/2) / [sinh(p h/2) sinh(q h/2)].

Expanding sinh independently yields the h^2 coefficient
(p^2 q^2+1-p^2-q^2)/24. Therefore

 a2(cable_(p,q)(K))
    =p^2 a2(K)+(p^2-1)(q^2-1)/24.                       (A)

Also a2(K#L)=a2(K)+a2(L). All terms in (A) are nonnegative once a2(K)>=0.
Negative q does not change the formula. Mirroring preserves a2.

Degenerate operations are harmless but should be explicit:
* p=1 gives a curve isotopic to the core of the solid torus, hence K.
* q=0 with gcd(p,q)=1 forces p=1.
* For companion U, p>=2 and |q|=1 gives U, not a nontrivial torus knot.
* For p>=2 and |q|>=2, the torus contribution is strictly positive.
* Any cable of a nontrivial graph companion has positive first term.

Induction through the classification therefore proves

  graph knot K and a2(K)=0  ==>  K=U.                    (B)

This is stronger and more precise than saying graph knots have nontrivial
Alexander polynomial: it supplies the particular ordinary-Jones derivative
needed here. No cancellation between mirrored graph summands is possible.

3. Jones derivative normalization, derived directly
===================================================
Use V_U=1 and

 t^-1 V_+ - t V_- = (t^1/2-t^-1/2) V_0.

At t=1 an l-component link has value (-2)^(l-1). Expanding the skein
relation first to order h, t=e^h, shows V'_K(1) is invariant under crossing
changes of knots, hence zero. For a two-component link L, the linear
coefficient b(L) changes by -3 at a positive versus negative MIXED crossing,
and by zero at a self crossing. Since b(U_2)=0, b(L)=-3 lk(L).

Write V_K(e^h)=1+c2(K)h^2+O(h^3). At a knot crossing whose oriented
smoothing has linking number l, the h^2 skein coefficient gives

 c2(K_+)-c2(K_-)=-3l.

The Conway skein relation gives a2(K_+)-a2(K_-)=l. Crossing changes reduce
any knot to U, where both coefficients vanish, so c2=-3a2. Because
V'_K(1)=0, the ordinary second derivative is

                    V''_K(1)=-6a2(K).                   (C)

Thus V_K=1 implies a2(K)=0. Combining (B), (C), and the zero-volume
classification proves: every nontrivial Jones-one knot has v(K)>0.

4. Complete conditional theorem
===============================
Assume a realized polynomial P has a finite uniform upper bound B on v(L)
for ALL classical knots L satisfying V_L=P. Choose J in that fiber.
If a nontrivial K had V_K=1, multiplicativity and additivity would give

 V_(J#K^#m)=P,
 v(J#K^#m)=v(J)+m v(K) -> infinity,

contradicting B, since v(K)>0. Hence Jones detects the unknot.

No prime, hyperbolic, ribbon, or unknotting-number restriction is imposed
on K. No conjecture connecting colors to volume enters this implication.
A fiber bound only on prime/hyperbolic knots would miss the displayed
connected sums and is insufficient.

5. Logical scope
================
The implication is rigorous given the established classification and
additivity inputs. The global premise remains unproved. The existential
claim that SOME whole fiber has bounded exterior volume is equivalent to
detection, because detection itself makes the unit fiber the singleton U.
Its utility would come from an independently proved bound for a specified
nonunit fiber, or an actual polynomial-controlled geometric operation.
The proof does not itself make that premise easier.

In particular, this audit supports the endpoint in geometric_mechanism.txt,
not the as-yet-unconstructed Jones-to-hyperbolic-parent mechanism or any
reverse Turaev–Viro/volume inequality.
