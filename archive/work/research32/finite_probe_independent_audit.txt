INDEPENDENT AUDIT: FINITE JONES PROBES AND UNBOUNDED VOLUME
1 November 2026. Audit of finite_probe_volume_obstruction.txt; no edits to it.

Verdict: the ordinary-Jones theorem and its quantifiers pass. The direct
TL2 argument avoids the reduced/unreduced normalization trap entirely.

1. Exact TL2 periodicity.
With g=A I+A^-1 e and e^2=(-A^2-A^-2)e, direct multiplication gives
  g^2=A^2 I+(1-A^-4)e.
The minimal polynomial divides (X-A^2)(X-A^-6). For t=A^-4 not equal to
+1 or -1, these roots are distinct. If A^R=1, both eigenvalues have
R-th power1, and hence (g^2)^R=I in TL2 itself. This is an algebra
identity before any closure; no quantum dimension is divided out.
A twist of R full turns changes writhe by +2R or -2R depending on the
local orientation, and (-A^3)^(2R)=A^(6R)=1. Thus normalized Jones
values are preserved for both orientation choices and both twist signs.
Taking a common multiple of the finitely many A-orders is legitimate.

2. The exceptional root -1.
For a classical knot, V is an integer Laurent polynomial, V(1)=1 and
V'(1)=0. Reduction of sum_j j a_j=0 modulo2 gives sum_odd a_j even,
also when exponents are negative. Hence V(-1)=1 modulo4. Since
Delta=1 implies |V(-1)|=1, its value is +1. No assumption about an
orientation-dependent determinant sign is needed. At t=1 the usual
knot normalization gives1 directly.

3. Geometric quantifiers and adjacency.
Fix the probe data first. Then choose n>2 satisfying both the finite-type
order threshold and a strict cusp-volume margin above the desired B.
Kalfagianni's 2007 Lemma2.4 gives one hyperbolic (n+1)-component parent
with disjoint generalized-crossing supports and all proper partial
operations unknotted. After all n fillings, reversing any nonempty
subset is exactly a proper partial operation on the parent, so the
resulting knot is n-adjacent to U. The 2004 Theorem2.3, printed p1115,
then gives Delta=1 and agreement with U of finite-type invariants of
orders <2n-1. Thus 2n-1>d suffices for the Jones Taylor condition.

For each fixed n, the allowed positive multiples of R are unbounded
on every filling torus. Hyperbolic Dehn filling therefore provides
hyperbolicity and convergence of volume to that of the parent, which
has at least (n+1)v3 volume. Choose the fillings sufficiently long to
retain volume>B. One must vary n to obtain unbounded volume overall.
The source's displayed equality between filled and parent volumes in
its Equation(2) is not literally correct; the proof uses convergence,
and the audited note correctly uses convergence rather than copying
that equality.

4. Separate unreduced all-color strengthening in the source variable.
Let r_1,...,r_s>2 be fixed orders and R a common multiple. Use the same
construction with every filling order divisible by R. For each r_i,
a full twist of order m r_i can be realized as m successive order-r_i
generalized crossing changes. Apply Kalfagianni Corollary2.3 at each
successive step; equality of evaluations is transitive, so no extra
claim about a transitive version of its congruence definition is needed.
All moves have algebraic intersection zero and preserve the zero
framing. Thus, simultaneously for every positive color N,
  J_N(K,e_(r_i))=J_N(U,e_(r_i))
in Kalfagianni's UNREDUCED variable convention. Integer Laurent
coefficients extend this to Galois-conjugate primitive roots as well.
The same knots can have arbitrarily large volume, Delta1, and any
prescribed bounded finite-type agreement, by the preceding choice of n.

This equality is informative for normalized H_N only if the evaluated
unknot quantum dimension [N] is nonzero. At zeros it is vacuous and
cannot be divided. The source itself emphasizes this in concluding
Remark3(1), with N=r. No claim about all reduced colored values at
quantum-dimension zeros is justified. The direct TL2 proof and Delta
argument above are why the ORDINARY Jones statement nevertheless holds
at every prescribed ordinary root, including -1, without translating
between the source's colored variable and the usual t convention.
