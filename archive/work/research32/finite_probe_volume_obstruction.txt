FINITE JONES PROBES DO NOT CONTROL EXTERIOR VOLUME
1 November 2026. Research deduction from published constructions; no priority claim.

THEOREM.
Fix an integer d>=0, a finite set Omega of roots of unity in the ordinary
Jones variable t, and a real number B>0. There is a hyperbolic classical
knot K satisfying simultaneously
  Delta_K=1,
  V_K(e^h)=1+O(h^(d+1)),
  V_K(zeta)=1 for every zeta in Omega,
  vol(S3 minus K)>B.
All finite-type invariants of any prescribed bounded order can also be
made equal to those of U. This does NOT assert V_K(t)=1 as a polynomial.
The theorem is an existence construction, not an explicit knot diagram.

SOURCE INPUTS.
Kalfagianni, arXiv:0710.0076v2, Lemma2.4, supplies hyperbolic Brunnian links
L_n=U union C_1 union ... union C_n: the C_i bound disjoint crossing disks,
and twisting any proper subset leaves U unknotted. Their exteriors have
(n+1) cusps. The proof of Theorem1.2 uses the cusp-volume lower bound and
hyperbolic Dehn filling to obtain volumes approaching that of L_n.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.0076
Kalfagianni, arXiv:math/0411384, Theorem2.3, states that n-adjacency to U,
for n>2, forces Delta=1 and all finite-type invariants of order<2n-1 to
agree with U.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0411384

PROOF.
Choose n>2 with 2n-1>d and (n+1)v_3>B, with enough strict margin to permit
Dehn filling; v_3 is the volume of a regular ideal hyperbolic tetrahedron.
Fix the link L_n above. Apply twists of positive orders r_i at all n disks.
The result is a knot K. Undoing any nonempty subset of those twists leaves
an operation on a proper subset of the original disks, hence gives U.
Thus K is n-adjacent to U, independent of how large the r_i are. The second
source supplies Delta_K=1 and the finite-type assertion. Coefficients of
V_K(e^h) through degree d are finite-type invariants of order at most d.

We now impose the root conditions while leaving arbitrarily long filling
slopes available. The following direct two-strand computation avoids any
division by a quantum dimension at a root of unity.

At zeta not in {1,-1}, choose a root of unity A with A^(-4)=zeta. In TL_2
write e^2=delta e, delta=-A^2-A^(-2), and g=A I+A^(-1)e for a crossing.
Then
  g^2=A^2 I+(1-A^(-4))e.
The two eigenvalues of left multiplication by g^2 are A^2 and A^(-6).
They are distinct because zeta^2 is not 1. Therefore, for any positive
multiple R of the multiplicative order of A,
  (g^2)^R=I
in the specialized TL_2 algebra itself. This identity holds before closing
the tangle and is independent of the surrounding knot. The oriented writhe
change of 2R crossings also has normalization factor
  (-A^3)^(+/-2R)=1.
Consequently a crossing-disk twist whose order is a multiple of R preserves
the normalized ordinary Jones evaluation at zeta. Choose one common R for
all these finitely many A's and choose every r_i to be a multiple of R.
Applying the n twists successively preserves each such evaluation from U.

At zeta=1 every normalized knot Jones polynomial is 1. At zeta=-1 use
Delta_K=1: the absolute value of V_K(-1) is the determinant, hence it is 1.
The sign is also fixed. Write V=sum a_j t^j. The identities V(1)=1 and
V'(1)=0 imply sum_(j odd) a_j is even, so
  V(-1)=1-2 sum_(j odd) a_j = 1 mod4.
It therefore cannot be -1, and equals 1. This handles the exceptional
specialization where the two-strand algebra has a nilpotent direction.

Finally let all positive multiples r_i of R tend to infinity. The Dehn
filling slopes 1/r_i have unbounded length, so the resulting knots are
hyperbolic for sufficiently large orders and their exterior volumes
approach vol(S3 minus L_n)>B. This proves all assertions simultaneously.

The n parameter must be allowed to increase to make volume unbounded.
A single fixed augmented parent only yields a bounded volume sequence.
The framing is the ordinary zero-framed Jones normalization throughout.

STRATEGIC CONSEQUENCES.
Neither a fixed finite ordinary Jones Taylor jet, nor any fixed finite
list of exact root-of-unity evaluations, nor both together, can bound
exterior volume, even when the full Alexander polynomial is also given.
Thus a proof of volume boundedness in a whole Jones fiber must use the
full polynomial or further geometry. This theorem does not contradict
such whole-polynomial boundedness: the constructed knots are not shown
to lie in one exact Jones fiber.

The proof avoids reducing unreduced colored values by vanishing quantum
dimensions. A separate audit considers the corresponding unreduced
all-color statement directly in Kalfagianni's conventions.
