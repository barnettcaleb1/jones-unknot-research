PASS 32 — EXTERIOR VOLUME AS A GLOBAL JONES-DETECTION SCAFFOLD
1 November 2026 UTC. Research deductions, not a claim of novelty or a proof of
Jones unknot detection. No report files edited.

MAIN CONCLUSION

A uniform bound on the total hyperbolic JSJ volume of knot EXTERIORS in ONE
nonempty exact ordinary-Jones fiber would prove the full unknot-detection
conjecture. This is a genuinely different quantity from crossing number,
bridge number, braid index, colored-Jones span, and branched-cover volume.
The downstream proof is complete. The whole-fiber volume bound is not proved.

1. THE PRECISE CONDITIONAL THEOREM

Write E(K)=S^3 minus the interior of a tubular neighborhood of K, and let

  v(K) = v_3 ||E(K), boundary E(K)||
       = sum of volumes of its hyperbolic JSJ pieces.

Here v_3 is the volume of the regular ideal tetrahedron. This convention applies
to satellite and composite knots as well as hyperbolic knots. It gives zero
to graph knots, not to every non-hyperbolic knot.

THEOREM (deduction). Suppose there is a realized Laurent polynomial P and a
finite constant C(P) such that v(K)<=C(P) for EVERY knot with V_K=P. Then
V_H=1 implies H is the unknot.

Proof. We establish below that any nontrivial Jones-one H has v(H)>0. Choose
one knot J with V_J=P. Multiplicativity gives V_(J#H#...#H)=P for every number
m of H summands. Simplicial-volume additivity gives

  v(J#H^#m)=v(J)+m v(H),

contradicting the bound. This covers arbitrary H, without assuming H is prime,
hyperbolic, ribbon, strongly invertible, or of unknotting number one. QED.

The fiber must include composite knots. A bound only for hyperbolic knots
having polynomial P would not suffice without a further construction.

2. WHY A JONES-ONE COUNTEREXAMPLE HAS POSITIVE EXTERIOR VOLUME

Primary classification input: Motegi–Takata, The slope conjecture for graph
knots, arXiv:1501.01105v3, p2, states that zero Gromov volume characterizes
graph knots, and that these are generated from U by cabling and connected
sums. It cites C. McA. Gordon, Dehn surgery and satellite knots, Trans. Amer.
Math. Soc. 275 (1983), 687–708, Corollary 4.2, for that characterization.
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.01105
This includes mirrors and arbitrary signs of the cable parameter.

Let the symmetric normalized Alexander polynomial satisfy Delta(1)=1, and
write a2(K)=Delta_K''(1)/2, equivalently the z^2 Conway coefficient. Then

  a2(K#L)=a2(K)+a2(L),
  a2(cable_(p,q)(K))=p^2 a2(K)+(p^2-1)(q^2-1)/24.             (1)

The second formula follows by differentiating
Delta_cable(t)=Delta_T(p,q)(t) Delta_K(t^p), using Delta_K'(1)=0,
and the standard torus Alexander polynomial. Take p>=1, gcd(p,q)=1;
a genuine nontrivial cabling can be taken p>=2, q nonzero.

Consequently every nontrivial graph knot has a2>0. This follows inductively:
a connected sum of nonnegative contributions is zero only if every summand
is U. A cable of a nontrivial graph companion has the strictly positive first
term in (1). A cable of U is a torus knot; its second term is strictly positive
unless it is U (|q|=1, or the degenerate winding-one case). Chirality introduces
no negative contribution, because the formula contains only p^2 and q^2.
This is positivity specifically for graph knots, not for arbitrary knots.

For the convention V_U=1 and
 t^-1 V_+ - t V_- = (t^(1/2)-t^(-1/2)) V_0,

  V_K''(1)=-6 a2(K).                                         (2)

A short normalization check: put t=e^h. A knot has expansion
1+c2(K)h^2+O(h^3), while an oriented two-component link of linking number l
has expansion -2-3l h+O(h^2). The skein equation gives
c2(K_+)-c2(K_-)=-3l. The Conway skein equation gives the corresponding a2
difference l. Both invariants vanish on U, so c2=-3a2. Since V'_K(1)=0,
V''_K(1)=2c2(K), proving (2).

Thus V_H=1 implies a2(H)=0. A nontrivial H cannot be a graph knot, and hence
must have positive v(H). This resolves the zero-volume escape in the global
connected-sum argument.

Primary additivity reference: T. Soma, The Gromov invariant of links,
Invent. Math. 64 (1981), 445–454, DOI 10.1007/BF01389276.
 https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01389276
An accessible primary paper explicitly recalling the JSJ sum and connected-
sum additivity is Detcherry–Kalfagianni–Yang, Turaev-Viro invariants, colored
Jones polynomials and volume, Section 5, p18 (PDF index17):
 https://users.math.msu.edu/users/kalfagia/paper1-23.pdf

3. LOGICAL STRENGTH AND NON-CIRCULARITY AUDIT

The existential statement that SOME whole Jones fiber has bounded v is
logically equivalent to detection: detection makes the unit fiber {U}.
Accordingly, merely restating that existential claim is not a proof advance.
What is useful is an independently motivated bound or geometric certificate
for a SPECIFIED NONUNIT fiber, or a broadly valid all-fiber theorem. This
would close detection without first recognizing the unit fiber geometrically.

The all-fiber volume assertion is stronger than necessary and remains open
in this investigation. No claim is made that it is logically equivalent to
detection. Nor is bounded volume a finite-knot-type claim: infinitely many
Dehn fillings of a fixed cusped manifold can have uniformly bounded volume.

The assertion concerns exteriors. A branched double cover may have zero
simplicial volume for a nontrivial knot, including many examples relevant to
earlier notes. That does not affect this proof.

4. WHAT THE KNOWN EXACT-JONES FAMILIES DO AND DO NOT TEST

A bounded primary-source search found no established family of KNOTS with
one exact ordinary Jones polynomial and unbounded exterior Gromov volume.
This is a negative search result, not a theorem about all current literature.

Champanerkar–Kofman, On links with cyclotomic Jones polynomials,
AGT 6 (2006), 1655–1668, introduction, explicitly states that the then-known
fixed-Jones families, including Kanenobu and Watson, arise by twisting at
finitely many sites and therefore have bounded volume.
 https://msp.org/agt/2006/6-4/agt-v6-n4-p03-s.pdf
Their unbounded-volume construction has DISTINCT cyclotomic Jones polynomials
of growing span; it does not contradict this scaffold.

Stoimenow explicitly asks for fixed-polynomial/unbounded-volume knots in
Question 8.2(4) of the author version linked below. Remark 8.3 explains why
Kanenobu and the non-hyperbolic EKT links do not supply them.
 https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/progs/ntrivodd.pdf

Our actual Watson family from pass29 has fixed ordinary Jones and unbounded
color-three span, but still lives in a finite twist template. It therefore
kills uniform color-three degree bounds while leaving exterior-volume bounds
untouched. One must not infer unbounded volume from the growing cable data.

Any bound based only on a fixed finite ordinary-Jones Taylor jet is impossible.
Our earlier Habiro-inverse knots R_N are actual nontrivial knots with
V_R_N(e^h)=1+O(h^(N+1)). For N>=2 they have a2=0, hence v(R_N)>0 by section2.
Their connected-sum powers retain the same finite jet while their exterior
volumes grow linearly. Thus the WHOLE polynomial matters here.

5. CONCRETE GEOMETRIC CERTIFICATE TARGET

A sufficient certificate for a specified fiber P is the following:
for every K with V_K=P, obtain all hyperbolic JSJ pieces by Dehn filling a
collection of compact cusped parents with TOTAL ideal-triangulation complexity
at most B(P). Allow arbitrary Seifert-fibered JSJ pieces and arbitrary gluing
parameters. The total, rather than per-piece, budget is essential.

Each ideal tetrahedron contributes at most v_3 to simplicial volume, and
Dehn filling does not increase simplicial volume. Thus v(K)<=v_3 B(P).
This yields the full theorem by section1. Arbitrary graph pieces are free in
this certificate, so it does not ask for bounded crossing or bridge number,
or a finite list of actual knot diagrams/twist templates.

This is an EQUIVALENT STRUCTURAL REFORMULATION of volume boundedness,
not progress on its missing premise. For the converse direction, the
Jorgensen–Thurston drilling/triangulation theorem, as explicitly stated in
Detcherry–Kalfagianni Theorem2.4, gives a drilled parent with at most a universal
constant times ||M|| partially ideal tetrahedra for each hyperbolic piece M.
Summing over the pieces gives the stated total budget from a volume bound.
 https://users.math.msu.edu/users/detcherr/TVboundsREVISION4.pdf
Finite combinatorics of a bounded total number of tetrahedra then gives
finitely many possible parents (with bounded total multiplicity).
A Jones-based procedure producing these parents with a polynomial-controlled
budget is the missing theorem. A useful next result must provide that actual
reduction, not simply give the reformulation a new name.

An adequate-diagram starting point is known, but it does not extend by
changing crossings or smoothing states: those operations change the knot.
There is presently no justified operation taking an arbitrary Jones-one
knot to a bounded-parent adequate object while retaining nontriviality.

6. PHYSICS CONNECTION WITH ITS EXACT EXTRA GAP

The Detcherry–Kalfagianni–Yang Theorem1.1 expresses exterior Turaev–Viro at the
relevant odd roots as a positive sum of squared ALL-COLOR Jones values,
up to an explicit prefactor with subexponential growth. Hence a possible
physical version of the missing bound controls the LARGE-COLOR EXPONENTIAL
RATE, rather than the degree of any fixed color, on one ordinary Jones fiber.

Concretely, a bound on
 limsup_(r odd -> infinity) (2pi/r) log[sum_(1<=j<r/2)
                       |J_K,j(exp(4pi i/r))|^2]
by a constant depending only on P would be compatible with pass29's
unbounded fixed-color spans. To deduce exterior volume from this bound,
however, one also needs a general reverse comparison with volume (a form of
the Turaev–Viro volume conjecture). This is an ADDITIONAL unproved gate.

Do not reverse the established inequality: Detcherry–Kalfagianni,
Gromov norm and Turaev–Viro invariants of 3-manifolds, Theorem1.1, proves
LTV(M)<=C||M||, not ||M||<=C LTV(M).
 https://users.math.msu.edu/users/detcherr/TVboundsREVISION4.pdf
It therefore does not turn a quantum upper bound into a geometric upper bound.
Also do not use the polynomial growth at the different unitary root
exp(2pi i/r); the volume-sensitive expression above uses exp(4pi i/r).

This explains a viable interdisciplinary inspiration without pretending that
norm positivity recovers all colors from the ordinary polynomial. The direct
geometric-parent route has fewer unproved inputs than this quantum version.

7. A TESTED OBSTRUCTION TO A DIFFERENT GLOBAL ROUTE

An operation F sending all knots to adequate knots, with V_F(K) depending
only on V_K, cannot simultaneously have finite fibers on knot types.
Indeed, an adequate diagram with c crossings and state counts s_A,s_B has
 span V=(c+s_A+s_B-2)/2 >= c/2.
Thus a fixed Jones polynomial permits only finitely many adequate knot types.
Applying F to an actual infinite fixed-Jones family forces some output to
have infinitely many preimages. This rules out an injective/recoverable
adequate compiler driven only by the whole ordinary Jones polynomial.
The weaker request to preserve only nontriviality on Jones-one inputs is not
refuted by this argument; no construction for it was obtained.

RECOMMENDATION

Retain the exterior-volume theorem as a clean global endpoint. The decisive
next test is an actual fixed-Jones mechanism that increases the TOTAL
hyperbolic JSJ mass, or an explicit polynomial-controlled reduction removing
Seifert/twist complexity while bounding the remaining hyperbolic parents.
Do not treat another bounded-volume fixed twist family, the all-color volume
conjecture alone, or a finite-jet estimate as progress on that missing theorem.

8. CONNECTION TO THE EXISTING SCALAR-PATTERN REALIZATION TARGET

This is a useful new interpretation of an OLD construction target, not a
new pattern. If an actual essential winding-zero annular knot pattern P has
scalar full skein class, all preferred satellites P(C) have one ordinary
Jones polynomial. Choose C_m=#^m 4_1. The companion torus is incompressible
for such an essential winding-zero pattern and nontrivial C_m. Cutting there
and using simplicial-volume additivity gives

  v(P(C_m)) >= v(C_m)=m v(4_1).

Therefore such a pattern would disprove the ALL-fiber exterior-volume
proposal, even if its scalar polynomial were nonunit. It would not by itself
refute the theorem that a bound on ONE OTHER fiber proves detection. The
previous finite scalarity certificate is therefore an exact test of a
possible global obstruction here, not just a bridge-number construction.
No actual essential scalar pattern was constructed in this pass.
