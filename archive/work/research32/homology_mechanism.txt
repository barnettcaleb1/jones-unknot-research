PASS 32: A SPECTRAL-GEOMETRIC ROUTE AND ITS EXACT LIMIT
1 November 2026 UTC. Bounded research pass. No proof of Jones unknot
detection, no counterexample, and no novelty claim.

Outcome
=======
The new primary source Grlj–Lauda, Spectral geometry of Khovanov
Laplacians (arXiv:2608.24298v1, 25 August 2026), supplies a genuine
geometric object to test: a positive Laplacian of an actual enhanced-state
complex, with graph models in extreme cube degrees. It does not supply a
bound throughout a Jones fiber. Below is a precise obstruction to the
most immediate route from that object to such a bound. All-temperature
signed spectral data remain exactly the Jones polynomial. Actual knots
with identical Jones and different harmonic dimensions demonstrate the
loss; no hypothetical complex is needed.

The targeted literature check did not locate a certified exact-Jones
knot fiber with unbounded reduced rational Khovanov rank or bridge
number. This is a statement about this bounded search, not a claim that
no such result exists. The familiar fixed-template families do not close
the missing global coverage step. No new geometric bridge was obtained.

1. Complete endpoint, before the proposed mechanism
==================================================
Fix a realizable Jones polynomial P and an anchor knot J with V_J=P.
Suppose an actual geometric construction bounded reduced rational
Khovanov rank r throughout this WHOLE fiber, including composites.
If V_K=1, then

 V_(J#K^#m)=P,       r(J#K^#m)=r(J) r(K)^m.

A finite bound independent of m forces r(K)=1. Kronheimer–Mrowka then
implies K is the unknot. This is the already established global
scaffold, not new progress. The proposed spectral mechanism would have
to establish the uniform bound independently; its name is not a proof.

Primary endpoint: P. Kronheimer and T. Mrowka,
Khovanov homology is an unknot-detector,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.4346

2. Precisely what the new source offers
======================================
Grlj–Lauda use the enhanced-state inner product and the Laplacian
Delta=d*d+dd*. Its kernel computes complex Khovanov homology; its positive
spectrum depends on the diagram. In minimal/maximal cube degrees they
identify signless graph Laplacians; loop-free bipartite components count
harmonic classes. Proposition 4.4 assumes the relevant rational homology
vanishes and bounds the gap in fixed-width extreme quantum bands. It
does not prove vanishing, control the bulk, or bound zero multiplicity.
Their Section 4.1.5 leaves graph behavior under diagram moves open.
Theorem 7.3 transfers Lee's differential to the harmonic subspace using
the full Hodge contraction. Sections 5–6 recover integral torsion from a
degree-weighted spectral product with a regulator correction. None of
these statements bounds the unsigned harmonic dimension using Jones.

Primary source and exact version:
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24298v1
Relevant locations: Theorem 4.1, Proposition 4.4, Section 4.1.5,
Equation (1.6), Theorem 7.3. This is a preprint; we audited its relevant
scope, not every proof in the paper.

3. Exact functional-calculus obstruction
=======================================
This argument is our direct elementary deduction, valid for every finite
Hilbert cochain complex and hence for an actual reduced Khovanov complex.
It is not asserted to be new.

Fix a quantum grading j and sum all homological degrees. Let

 Q=d+d*,       Delta=Q^2,       Gamma|C^i=(-1)^i.

The operator Q is odd: Gamma Q=-Q Gamma. It commutes with Delta. On the
eigenspace E_lambda for any lambda>0, Q/sqrt(lambda) is an invertible
map exchanging even and odd parity. Consequently these two parities
have equal multiplicity at EACH positive eigenvalue. On E_0, their
dimension difference is the quantum coefficient chi_j of the Euler
polynomial. Therefore for every function f on the finite spectrum,

 Str f(Delta_j)=f(0) chi_j.                              (1)

In particular, for every temperature parameter t>=0 and shift s>0,

 sum_i (-1)^i Tr exp(-t Delta_(i,j)) = chi_j;             (2)
 sum_i (-1)^i Tr (Delta_(i,j)+sI)^(-1) = chi_j/s;         (3)
 product_i det(Delta_(i,j)+sI)^((-1)^i) = s^(chi_j).      (4)

The determinant equality follows by cancelling matching positive
eigenvalue factors; the remaining factors are s for each zero mode.
Weighting (2) by q^j gives exactly V_K(q^2) in the reduced normalization.
The complete family of these signed observables, at ALL temperatures
or spectral shifts, carries no extra information beyond Jones.

Important distinction: (4) is the ordinary superdeterminant with parity
exponent (-1)^i. It is NOT the analytic torsion product whose exponent
also contains i/2. The latter need not cancel and can contain additional
integral/lattice information. Jones does not control that extra datum;
we make no claim that Grlj–Lauda's torsion theorem is trivial.

This closes the naive statistical-mechanical hope that cooling a Jones
supertrace, or observing it at every energy, exposes the hidden states.
The cancellation is exact on every positive eigenspace and persists
among zero modes of opposite parity.

4. Actual-knot test, with chirality and coefficients fixed
========================================================
Take the chirality versions of 5_1 and 10_132 represented by

 5_1:    B_2 word [-1,-1,-1,-1,-1],
 10_132: B_4 word [1,1,1,-2,-1,-1,-2,-3,2,-3,-3].

Both have

 P(t)=t^-2+t^-4-t^-5+t^-6-t^-7.

The retained KnotInfo conventions require mirroring its 5_1 entry to
match this pair. The reduced rational ranks are 5 and 11, respectively;
the unreduced rational ranks are 6 and 12. For 10_132 the reduced groups
at (i,j)=(-1,-2),(0,-2) are each one dimensional, while the coefficient
of q^-2 in P(q^2) is zero. Thus an actual classical knot has the precise
opposite-parity zero-mode cancellation in (1).

The complete reduced term lists, their Euler sums, and both rank checks
are saved in actual_collision_check.json alongside this note. The check
only parses the two retained records; this was not a knot-table scan.
The ordinary Jones identity was previously recomputed from the explicit
braids in work/proof12/braid/all_color_collision_proof.txt. The current
pass independently checked the homology ranks and Euler agreement from
the retained primary database data.

Hence all signed heat traces (2) of the two knots coincide, at every
t, but their unsigned heat traces converge to different ranks. This
refutes exact recovery of rank from those observables. It does NOT
refute a finite upper bound on rank in their common Jones fiber: the
two ranks are both finite, and no unbounded family is claimed here.

5. Why a gap estimate is not the missing bridge
=============================================
Let M be total dimension of the finite reduced chain complex, r its
homology rank, and lambda its least positive Laplacian eigenvalue. Put
Z_D(t)=Tr exp(-t Delta), WITHOUT parity signs. Then

 r <= Z_D(t) <= r+(M-r) exp(-lambda t).                  (5)

This is a good computational separation estimate. It places no upper
bound on r. Knowing lambda is bounded below never controls how many
zero eigenvalues remain. With no positive eigenvalues, Z_D(t)=r and the
same point is even more immediate.

Moreover, the proposed condition

 'for every K with V_K=P there exist a diagram D_K and t_K>0
  with Z_(D_K)(t_K)<=B(P)'

is, as a mere finiteness assertion, equivalent to H-rank on that fiber.
One direction is (5). Conversely, given a rank bound, take any finite
diagram and cool long enough to make the positive contribution less
than one. The additive change from B to B+1 is immaterial. Allowing
arbitrary diagrams and arbitrary cooling times would therefore rename
the original missing premise rather than construct it.

6. Decision and exact next-test requirement
==========================================
Stop this spectral branch unless a new geometric operation supplies
independent control of the UNSIGNED harmonic space. Concretely, the
graph model would need diagram moves controlling the number of
bipartite components and their bulk-degree analogues across a complete
Jones fiber; source Proposition 4.4 is not such a theorem. A local gap
calculation, a better quantum algorithm, or an all-temperature signed
trace identity does not advance the global rank argument by itself.

No new bounded-fiber theorem or counterexample emerged in this pass.
The useful result is the exact scope audit of a genuinely new spectral
construction, plus a rigorous reason that its most immediate positivity
mechanism cannot recover the lost information. This does not exclude
all spectral or geometric approaches, and it does not establish that
the existing H-rank scaffold is false.
