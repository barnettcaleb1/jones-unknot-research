PASS 32: TESTING THE NEW DYADIC JONES FRAMEWORK
1 November 2026. Bounded architecture test; no kernel or Jones-one knot found.
No novelty claim. No edits to the living report.

Outcome
=======
A new October2026 construction genuinely has global link coverage and embeds
all braid groups in one finitely generated group. Its explicit definitions
nevertheless give two structural obstructions to the intended use here:
(1) its algebraic Jones kernel is exactly the direct limit of the ordinary
finite-strand Jones kernels; the extra Thompson-group directions cannot
create a kernel automatically;
(2) link coverage does not give an extremal vacuum normalization. A nontrivial
knot cannot attain that normalization in this framework, even after adding
arbitrarily many split unknots.
There is also an explicit failure of a uniform positive parameter arc on the
unbounded-strand algebra, separate from the earlier fixed-strand scalar gap.

A. Complete conditional counterexample path, then its exact reduction
===================================================================
Proposed path:
  construct a nonidentity dyadic braid b with J_F(b)=1;
  extract a nontrivial finite braid in the full generic TL kernel;
  apply Ito to produce nontrivial hyperbolic knots with Jones polynomial1.
The second arrow is valid by the following elementary deduction. It also
shows that the enlargement has not supplied an easier automatic relation.

Write a dyadic braid as [P,alpha,f], where P is a finite ordered set of dyadic
points, alpha is an ordinary |P|-braid, and f belongs to oriented Thompson F.
The source's concentration equivalence preserves f exactly. Its TL map is
  J_F[P,alpha,f]=[P,J_|P|(alpha),f].
The linearity relations in the enlarged algebra occur within a fixed f-tag.

There is an especially direct tag detector. Let aug:TL_n->Q(A) send every
turnback e_i to0 and 1 to1. It respects the TL relations and the source's
backside-strand insertions: their formula for an inserted e_i is a sum of
terms containing turnbacks, all of which aug kills. Thus
  [P,x,f] |-> aug(x) f
is a homomorphism to Q(A)[oriented Thompson F]. On a braid alpha it has value
A^exp(alpha) f. If J_F(b)=1, this forces f=id and exp(alpha)=0.

The finite-stage TL insertion maps are injective. Geometrically, moving a new
backside strand to the right edge identifies the insertion with a braid
conjugate of x |-> x tensor1. The latter is injective on planar bases; also,
closing the added strand is a left inverse after dividing by delta. Iterating
gives injectivity for every P subset Q. Consequently
  J_F[P,alpha,id]=1 iff J_|P|(alpha)=1.
The braid insertion maps are likewise injective (forget the inserted strands).
Therefore the algebraic kernel of J_F is exactly the direct limit of the
finite generic Jones kernels. In particular, J_F is faithful iff every
finite generic Jones representation is faithful.

The link-compatible embedding iota of oriented Thompson F has f-tag g on
input g. Hence J_F restricted to iota(F) is algebraically faithful, regardless
of the unresolved braid faithfulness problem. This does NOT imply that its
single vacuum coefficient distinguishes links, or that the analogous GNS
representation after quotienting a trace radical is algebraically faithful.

If an actual nonidentity kernel element were found, exp(alpha)=0 rules out a
nontrivial central full twist. Ito's theorem then supplies nontrivial
Jones-one knots, as in the previous full-kernel scaffold. No extra all-colored
Jones detector or closure nontriviality conjecture is required.
Primary endpoint: Tetsuya Ito, A kernel of a braid group representation yields
a knot with trivial knot polynomials, Math.Z.280(2015), arXiv:1402.2028.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.2028

B. Complete proposed proof path and a topological normalization obstruction
==========================================================================
A tempting different path is:
  represent arbitrary K by a Thompson element whose vacuum coefficient is V_K;
  use V_K=1 and positive unitarity to force vacuum stabilization;
  prove a geometric stabilizer theorem yielding K=U.
Even before the last (unproved) stabilizer theorem, the first calibration
fails for every nontrivial knot in the source's actual construction.

Let the source's n-strand braid representative close to K disjoint union r
split unknots. Its ordinary Jones polynomial is delta^r V_K, and its writhe is
zero for the link-compatible Thompson embedding. Equations2.2 and5.1 give
  coefficient = delta^(r+1-n) V_K.
The closure map only identifies links up to adding split unknots; forgetting
that distinction would incorrectly replace this by V_K.

Deleting the r other components from the n-strand braid leaves a braid for K.
If K uses n_K strands, then n_K>=b(K), its classical braid index. Each of the
other r components uses at least one strand. Hence
  n-r >= n_K >= b(K).
For a nontrivial knot, b(K)>=2. Therefore, at any meaningful positive unitary
specialization with delta>1, a Jones-one representative has coefficient
  delta^(r+1-n) <= delta^(1-b(K)) <= delta^(-1) <1.
This is unchanged by adding distant trivial braid strands: both r and n rise
by one. No choice among the permitted representatives can make r=n-1 unless
K already has braid index1 and is the unknot. Thus the global link-coverage
statement does not repair the interior-amplitude obstruction.
This deduction is topological and does not rely on an assumed Jones detector.

C. Why the infinite enlargement also has a distinct positivity problem
=====================================================================
The source's Theorem5.6 proves preservation of a Q(A)-valued sesquilinear
form. It does not state that this form is positive definite at every complex
specialization. In fact no open unitary parameter arc works faithfully for
all strand counts at once.

Use the ordinary real TL loop parameter delta=2cos(theta), with 0<theta<pi/2,
and quantum integers [k]=sin(k theta)/sin(theta). The Jones--Wenzl idempotent
f_m is self-adjoint wherever its denominators [1],...,[m] are nonzero, and
its unnormalized Markov trace is [m+1]. Hence its normalized squared norm is
  tau(f_m^* f_m)=tau(f_m)=[m+1]/delta^m.
For generic theta, choose the first integer k with k theta>pi. Then all [j]
for 1<=j<k are nonzero while [k]<0, so f_(k-1) exists and has negative squared
norm. The test occurs at a finite but theta-dependent strand count.
At theta=pi/r, f_(r-1) exists, is nonzero (identity coefficient1), and has
trace[r]=0. The unitary root-of-unity theory uses the corresponding quotient;
it does not make the full generic TL direct limit faithfully positive.
At theta=0, delta=2, positivity survives every finite stage, but the Jones
variable is q=1 and the ordinary knot value is universally1.

Thus a fixed finite-dimensional positive arc near delta2 cannot be promoted
to a common faithful positive arc for the infinite dyadic algebra. Taking
larger strand counts and retaining a fixed generic positive specialization
are noncommuting steps. This does not invalidate finite-stage positivity or
root-of-unity unitary quotients.

Decision
========
The new framework is relevant and has true global coverage, but neither a
new full kernel nor an extremal normalized scalar follows from its larger
group or its formal unitarity. Do not start a Thompson-word search based only
on this enlargement. A worthwhile next attempt must predict an actual finite
braid relation in the generic TL image, or introduce genuinely new geometric
information that supplies the missing stabilizer/normalization premise.
No such construction was obtained in this bounded pass. The conclusions
are structural exclusions, not evidence of proximity to a generic solution.
