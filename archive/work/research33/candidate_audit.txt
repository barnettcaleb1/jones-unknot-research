PASS 33: CONCRETE CANDIDATE AUDIT
1 November 2026 UTC. No knot with Jones polynomial one has been obtained.

RESULT
======
The existing two-component EKT band-fusion route remains excluded. A
concrete, previously untested distinction is the published THREE-component
family LLL(n). Its smallest orientation-independent member LLL(1) is an
actual 19-crossing link with Jones polynomial equal to the three-component
unlink. It survives both the elementary homology gate and the stronger
torsion-linking-form gate for a possible two-band fusion to a determinant-one
knot. This makes it a valid input for a bounded two-band experiment, not a
Jones-one knot candidate by itself. Parent is performing that experiment.

1. WHAT WAS RECHECKED, AND WHAT WAS NOT
=====================================
I read the existing full-family EKT obstruction and rotor-surgery notes.
The two-component LL1/LL2 families have H1(double cover)=Z plus nonzero
finite torsion, so NO single embedded band can fuse them to any
determinant-one knot. That exclusion is independent of how complicated the
band is. There is no reason to repeat a short-band search on those links.

That argument cannot be applied unchanged to a three-component link and
two fusion bands. Two integral surgery components can present an infinite
group with one free generator and nonzero torsion. Confusing its free rank
with components-minus-one would wrongly discard the concrete LLL family.

2. ACTUAL LLL DIAGRAMS AND EXACT CHECKS
====================================
Primary source: Eliahou–Kauffman–Thistlethwaite, Infinite families of links
with trivial Jones polynomial, Topology 42 (2003), Section 4.3/Theorem 4.4:
https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/ekt.pdf

Its transformed tangle is the LEFT-ASSOCIATED dot expression

 T=2 dot (1/2 dot 1)^n dot 1 dot 2,
 LLL(n)=H(T,-3),

where each repeated pair inserts the two separate factors 1/2,1, and
X dot Y means reciprocal reflection of X followed by horizontal sum Y.
The constructor reuses the already calibrated EKT clasp and dot operation.
It does not replace these nonrational sums by their numerical fractions.

The actual PD diagrams are in lll_candidates.json; construction is in
lll_candidates.py. Exact full-diagram results are:

 n   crossings   components   Jones in x=t^(1/2)       H1(Sigma2)
 0      16           3        x^-10+2x^-12+x^-14       Z + Z/36
 1      19           3        x^2+2+x^-2               Z + Z/144
 2      22           3        x^2+2+x^-2               Z + Z/576
 3      25           3        x^2+2+x^-2               Z + Z/2304

The n=0 orientation caveat agrees with the source; use n=1 as the clean
experiment. The n>=1 unlink-Jones identity is independently supplied by
the source theorem, not extrapolated from these four computations.

For n=1 I also recomputed the entire bracket using the independent frontier
state sum, not Regina's Jones method:

 <D>=-A^-5-2A^-9-A^-13,       writhe(D)=-3.

Multiplying by (-A^3)^(-w) gives A^4+2+A^-4 exactly. Certificate:
lll_independent_bracket.json. Its maximum frontier is eight arcs and
maximum number of pairing states is thirteen.

The nonzero torsion itself proves LLL(1) is not the three-component unlink.
It does not prove that any particular fusion of it is nontrivial.

3. STRONGER TWO-BAND HOMOLOGY GATE: ALSO PASSED
============================================
Suppose two band moves take LLL(1) to a determinant-one knot K. Reversing
the lifted cobordism describes Sigma2(LLL(1)) by integral surgery on a
two-component framed link in the integral homology sphere Sigma2(K).
The 2x2 symmetric linking matrix A then has rank one because b1=1.
Its kernel is primitive. An integral basis extending that kernel makes
A congruent to diag(epsilon*144,0), epsilon=+/-1. Consequently its cyclic
torsion linking form must be represented by +/-1/144, up to changing the
generator by a unit modulo144.

I computed the actual diagram's Goeritz matrix using the crossing-corner
convention of Spherogram's white_graph/goeritz_matrix implementation, then
performed exact rational linear algebra independently of Regina. Its
primitive one-dimensional kernel has a coordinate +/-1, so replacing that
standard basis vector by the kernel vector is an integral unimodular
change of basis. The resulting form splits as B direct-sum [0].

For LLL(1), det(B)=+144 and a torsion generator e has

                    e^T B^-1 e = 143/144 mod Z.

Thus the linking pairing is -1/144, up to the overall boundary-orientation
sign convention. It PASSES the required test. The sign of det(B), a
six-dimensional block, is not the sign of a one-dimensional presentation
of its torsion pairing.

The same bounded check for n=0,2,3 gives numerator/order
11/36, 479/576, and 1559/2304. Each numerator is plus or minus a square
unit modulo its order, and each also passes. No all-n linking-form theorem
is asserted from these samples.

Full exact matrices, primitive kernels, generator vectors and residues:
lll_linking_form.json. Portable verifier: lll_linking_form.py. It uses
integer/Fraction Gaussian elimination and an explicit finite unit-square
test; no floating point or symbolic-algebra package is involved.

The Goeritz intersection-form construction and resulting boundary linking
pairing are the Gordon–Litherland construction. An accessible primary
algorithmic account explicitly recalls the simply-connected branched
four-manifold interpretation:
Owens–Swenton, An Algorithm to Find Ribbon Disks for Alternating Knots,
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10586458.2022.2158968
The present graph need not be alternating; only the general Goeritz
intersection-form construction is used. After splitting off the primitive
radical, the torsion pairing is the inverse of B modulo integers, up to
orientation. The +/-square gate is unaffected by that global sign.

4. IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION CORRECTION FOR THE BAND EXPERIMENT
============================================================
The installed Spherogram banded_links() routine is a FISSION enumerator:
it chooses both ends on the same component and asserts that the number
of components increases by one. Calling it unchanged on LLL(1) does not
test fusion bands. Its min_len_bands() has the same endpoint restriction.

A 3 -> 2 -> 1 experiment must explicitly permit ends on DISTINCT components,
use add_one_band or an adapted generator, and check the actual component
count after each move. The parity/orientation condition must be verified
on the resulting link, not copied from the same-component case blindly.
This warning was sent to the parent before its bounded experiment.

5. WHAT WOULD CERTIFY A SUCCESS, CONCRETELY
=========================================
For an LLL input, retain the exact two embedded-band specifications and
the intermediate/final PDs. A final one-component diagram must have its
FULL normalized Jones polynomial equal to1; determinant1 alone is only a
cheap preliminary filter. Then certify nontriviality independently, e.g.
nontrivial Alexander polynomial, Khovanov rank>1, a nonabelian finite
quotient of the knot group, or a certified non-S3 double branched cover.
Failure of an unknot simplifier is not a nontriviality certificate.

The existing rotor-surgery data do not yet contain a symmetric augmented
unlink whose flip is nontrivial. The known one-circle rational-to-essential
tangle family supplies a correct locally nontrivial building block, but
does not supply the required simultaneous unlink disks for a C4 orbit.
Before surgery, a usable rotor candidate must give an actual augmented
unlink diagram with marked rotation/flip data and a simultaneous disk or
unlink-isotopy certificate. Its flipped augmented link must already be
nontrivial; otherwise every prescribed disk-framed 1/k surgery is harmless.
Zero linking numbers cannot certify this distinction and are automatic on
both sides in the standard C4 setup. No new rotor candidate was found here.
