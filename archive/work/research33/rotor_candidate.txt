WHY THIS ASSEMBLY SHOULD NOW BE STOPPED
The necklace's global flip equals four ordinary Conway mutations, one inside
each identical two-string sector. The central connections return to the same
connections under reflection. This statement holds for any stator S, not just
S0. Ordinary Conway mutation preserves the double branched cover, and a knot
with double branched cover S3 is U. Therefore ANY unknot completion of this
specific two-tangle necklace has an unknot rotant. Changing only its stator
cannot rescue it. One must change the sector-interaction geometry; that was
not attempted or assumed here.
