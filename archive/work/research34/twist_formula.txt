TWO DISJOINT ANTIPARALLEL FULL-TWIST SITES: EXACT ALL-SLOPE CLASSIFICATION
Pass 34. This is a skein calculation, not a claim of a new Jones-one knot.

HYPOTHESES AND ORIENTATION
Start with an actual oriented knot K_00, with two disjoint balls in which
it consists of untwisted antiparallel strands. Let K_mn insert m and n
full twists. Full twists preserve the endpoint matching, so every K_mn
is still a knot. Let S1, S2 denote the Jones polynomials obtained by
replacing respectively the first or second two-strand identity by the
cup-cap smoothing; let S12 denote the double-smoothed Jones polynomial.
Use the orientations induced from the original knot. For band fusions
of three components, these are the reverse band-surgery smoothings.

Assume exactly
  V(K_00)=1,                  S12=delta^2,
where delta=-A^2-A^-2, t=A^-4 and V(unknot)=1. The double smoothing may
be a nontrivial three-component link with the Jones polynomial of the
unlink. Its isotopy to the unlink is NOT used or assumed.

A positive local braid generator is g=A*I+A^-1*e, e^2=delta*e.
Since the two strands have opposite orientations, g^2 changes oriented
writhe by -2. Thus its normalized operator is
  N=A^6*g^2=A^8*I+(A^6-A^2)*e.
It has eigenvalues A^8 on I-e/delta and 1 on e/delta. Put z=A^8=t^-2.
For every integer m, positive, zero, or negative,
  N^m=z^m*I+(1-z^m)*e/delta.                         (1)
All calculations take place over Q(A); the resulting Jones polynomials
are Laurent polynomials. Reversing the chosen local twist generator at
one site replaces its integer parameter by its negative. Consequently
the word 'diagonal' below refers to the SAME normalized z-eigenvalue
at both sites, not to an uncalibrated visual choice of band handedness.

The outside diagram and all its crossing orientations are identical
in the four smoothings: cup-cap connects an incoming to an outgoing
endpoint precisely because the strands are antiparallel. Therefore a
common outside writhe normalization is legitimate. One must not insert
independently chosen orientations of the partial links into this formula
unless their Jones normalization is checked to agree.

EXACT TWO-VARIABLE FORMULA
Multilinearity and (1) give
 V(K_mn)=z^(m+n)*V(K_00)
   +z^n*(1-z^m)*S1/delta
   +z^m*(1-z^n)*S2/delta
   +(1-z^m)*(1-z^n)*S12/delta^2.

Under the two hypotheses, define u=S1/delta-1 and v=S2/delta-1. Then

 V(K_mn)-1 = u*z^n + v*z^m - (u+v)*z^(m+n)            (2)
           = u*z^n*(1-z^m)+v*z^m*(1-z^n).

Thus the double smoothing condition alone supplies no Jones-preserving
trajectory. The two single smoothings are the remaining exact inputs.

CLASSIFICATION OF EVERY INTEGER LINE THROUGH THE ANCHOR
Fix integers a,b and set (m,n)=(ak,bk). Equality V(K_ak,bk)=1 for every
integer k holds exactly in the following cases:

  a=b=0:                always;
  a nonzero, b=0:       S1=delta;
  a=0, b nonzero:       S2=delta;
  a=b nonzero:          S1+S2=2*delta;
  a,b nonzero, a!=b:    S1=S2=delta.

Proof. Substitute into (2). The three exponential frequencies are b,
a,a+b. They are distinct unless a=0, b=0, or a=b. Distinct powers of
the transcendental z have linearly independent integer-k sequences over
Q(A), so their coefficients vanish individually. The exceptional cases
follow by direct substitution. Negative integers require no new case.

Equivalently, for fixed partial polynomials:
 * u=v=0: all integer slopes work, indeed every K_mn has Jones 1;
 * u=0, v!=0: only the horizontal slopes b=0 work;
 * v=0, u!=0: only the vertical slopes a=0 work;
 * u=-v!=0: only the diagonal slopes a=b work;
 * otherwise: no nonconstant linear trajectory works.

The diagonal cancellation is a REAL algebraic possibility, and is not
excluded merely by S12=delta^2. If u=-v!=0, equation (2) is just
  V(K_mn)-1=u*(z^n-z^m).
In that case its ENTIRE integer Jones-one locus is exactly m=n. Whether
any knot on that locus is nontrivial is a separate geometric question.

FINITE EXACT CERTIFICATES
One exact diagonal check is sufficient:
  V(K_11)-1=z*(1-z)*(u+v).
Consequently V(K_11)=1 iff the entire diagonal consists of Jones-one
knots. This includes all negative k. Also
  V(K_10)-1=(1-z)*u,
  V(K_01)-1=(1-z)*v,
so the diagonal criterion is equivalently
  V(K_10)+V(K_01)=2.
These two actual knot polynomials avoid any partial-link orientation
ambiguity, once the local full-twist sign is calibrated.

For a fixed nonzero unequal slope (a,b), checking k=1 and k=2 suffices
for the entire trajectory: the determinant of the two equations in
u,v is
  z^(a+b)*(1-z^a)*(1-z^b)*(z^a-z^b),
which is nonzero. Hence two Jones-one points at those parameters force
u=v=0. In the diagonal and coordinate cases, one nonzero k suffices.
These are full polynomial checks, not tests at one numerical value of A.

APPLICATION TO THE LLL(1) TWO-BAND ANCHOR
If the saved band-fusion unknot supplies two disjoint antiparallel band
sites, and reversing both bands recovers LLL(1) with its Jones polynomial
delta^2 in the induced orientation, the preceding formulas apply exactly.
It remains to identify the two marked sites in that unsimplified diagram
and compute either S1,S2 or K_10,K_01. This note does not silently infer
those data from an unmarked simplified unknot diagram. No Jones-one
trajectory or nontrivial knot has been certified for that input here.

The calculation uses only the displayed Temperley-Lieb relation,
Kauffman bracket skein relation, writhe normalization, and Laurent
polynomial identities. It needs no representation-faithfulness,
positivity, colored-Jones, or topology conjecture.
