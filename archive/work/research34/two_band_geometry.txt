TWO DISJOINT BANDS ON THE ORIGINAL LLL(1) DIAGRAM
1 November 2026. Actual geometric family, not a formal skein expansion.

Original diagram: the19-crossing,3-component EKT LLL(1), whose exact PD is
embedded in two_band_family.py and recorded in two_band_family.json.
Its Jones polynomial is (x+x^-1)^2 with x=sqrt(t).

BandA joins original half-edges (17,1),(5,3), with core in original face2.
BandB joins original half-edges (9,3),(3,3), with core in original face10.
Crossing and strand indices are zero-based, as in Spherogram.
The two faces are distinct and the four attaching edges are distinct, so
narrow rectangles around the two core arcs are disjoint in the original
diagram. There are no crossings between the rectangles and the link away
from their attaching intervals.

K(m,n) is obtained by doing both band surgeries and inserting2m half twists
inA and2n half twists inB. Thus m,n count full twists. The supplied generator
changes the crossing graph at BOTH original sites before doing any orientation
rebuild. It performs no simplification or relabelling between bands.
Consequently the sites do not drift when m,n change.

The zero parameter diagram is EXACTLY the saved PD of prior hit5, before its
final simplification. Both bands have zero initial twists. The intervening
simplification in the old enumeration happened to remove no crossings for
this hit; the only subtlety was an orientation normalization rotating the two
old second-band crossing indices by180degrees. The new construction avoids
that normalization entirely. The reverse application order was prior hit7.

K(0,0) has19crossings and simplifies by Reidemeister moves to a single
crossing-free circle. The three cut diagrams have3,2,2components. All four
untwisted closures have writhe−3, so their Jones-to-bracket normalizations are
common. At either band a positive full twist decreases the knot diagram writhe
by2. Corner writhes are −3,−5,−5,−7 for(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1).

Computed actual tests:
 K(0,0): Jones1, actual unknot.
 K(1,0),K(0,1),K(1,1),K(1,−1): each Jones polynomial nonunit.
In particular neither equal-sign nor opposite-sign one-step compensation
preserves the unknot polynomial for this marked pair.

All original PDs, Jones coefficient dictionaries in x, blackboard writhes,
and fresh simplification results are saved. Nonzero simplified crossing counts
are not asserted minimal and can vary with the simplifier; nonunit Jones
already proves those tested knots nontrivial.
