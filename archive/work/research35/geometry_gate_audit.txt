GEOMETRIC AUDIT OF THE DISJOINT-BAND OBSTRUCTION
2026-11-01. Research deduction; no priority claim.

Verdict: the proposed obstruction passes, with a harmless common sign in the
Jones-at-minus-one phase. A cleaner simultaneous-surgery argument avoids that
phase entirely and extends to any number of bands. Neither argument is a
proof of general Jones unknot detection.

SETUP AND THE EMBEDDED SURFACE
Let U be an actual unknot and let b_1,...,b_r be disjoint embedded bands with
disjoint attaching intervals. For the general-r normalized-Jones statement,
assume the bands are orientable/coherent, so the local fusion strands are
antiparallel. Their simultaneous band surgery gives L. The
bands must be actual marked rectangles, not sites recovered after untracked
simplification. Start with the standard disk for U in an inner 4-ball and
place the band saddles in its outer S^3 x I collar. This gives an embedded
ribbon surface in B^4 with one zero-handle and r one-handles. A chosen disk
and the bands need not be disjoint in S^3: their different radial levels in
B^4 remove that nonexistent requirement.

For r=2 and three-component L, this connected surface has chi=-1 and three
boundary components. The classification of compact surfaces forces it to be
orientable of genus zero. Each one-band partial surface is an annulus. All
boundary orientations are induced from the same surface.

The double cover of the initial disk is B^4. A band saddle lifts to one
2-handle. Therefore the branched cover W is B^4 with r 2-handles and has an
integral symmetric framed-link matrix B. Its boundary first homology is
coker B. Keeping only band i keeps only its handle: the partial cover has
matrix [B_ii]. These assertions do not require that the bands be unknotted.
The local handle correspondence is the Montesinos trick; Akbulut--Kirby
also give the surface-handle construction (Sections 2 and 3).

CLEANER SURGERY FORMULA AND ITS SIGN CONDITION
Take the double cover of the complement of all the marked band balls in U.
This is the exterior of an r-component link in Sigma_2(U)=S^3. Let mu_i be
its meridian, and let lambda_i be the slope for the band-smoothing filling.
Since smoothing and the original U replacement have distance one, lambda_i
is an integral framed longitude. Relative to preferred longitudes its
coefficient is B_ii; off-diagonal entries are the linking numbers.

The local rational-tangle half-twist lifts to a Dehn twist on the boundary
of its solid-torus cover. Adding k full twists therefore changes the U slope
to mu_i + epsilon*2k*lambda_i. Here epsilon is one common sign if all local
full twists have the same handedness in orientation-preserving charts.
This is an actual local slope calculation, not an inference just from
unlabelled eigenvalues. In the bracket convention b=A*1+A^(-1)*e,
e^2=delta*e, the antiparallel normalized positive full twist has eigenvalues
1,A^8 (raw b^2 has eigenvalues A^2,A^(-6), and its normalization is A^6).
Thus the common z=A^8 convention used in the band recurrence has the same
handedness at every site. A reflected chart reverses both z and epsilon.

For simultaneous parameters k_i, the meridian homology relations have
matrix I + epsilon*2*diag(k_i)*B (or its transpose, with the same determinant).
For the equal-parameter diagonal k_i=k,

  det(K(k,...,k)) = |det(I + epsilon*2k*B)|.

This includes k=0 by continuity of the polynomial formula, where the
branched cover is S^3. If Jones(K(k,...,k))=1 for every integer k, this
integer polynomial has absolute value one at every integer and hence is
the constant one. Since B is a real symmetric matrix, all its eigenvalues
are real; det(I+tB)=1 forces every eigenvalue to be zero, hence B=0.

IMPORTANT SCOPE: if sites receive independently reversed twist parameters,
the matrix is I+2k*E*B for a diagonal sign matrix E. E*B need not be symmetric
and can be nonzero nilpotent. The conclusion B=0 is then unavailable. One
must retain the common-handed/common-z hypothesis, not silently apply this
to arbitrary compensating twists.

APPLICATION TO THE THREE-COMPONENT LINK UNDER STUDY
Here H_1(Sigma_2(L)) has free rank one. With r=2 this forces rank B=1, so
B is nonzero. Consequently the common-z diagonal cannot have Jones one
for all k. Nonzero torsion in this homology is not needed for the rank
argument. This rules out the entire proposed exact diagonal mechanism for
ANY disjoint marked band pair with this actual unlink anchor and this
branched-cover first Betti number, not just the one computed pair.

ORIGINAL SIGNATURE PROOF, INDEPENDENT CROSS-CHECK
For r=2, rank B=1 implies tr B != 0. The one-band annulus cover has signature
sign(B_ii), and its boundary link determinant is |B_ii| (zero if B_ii=0).
For an arbitrary properly embedded oriented surface F in B^4, the ordinary
signature of its boundary link equals the signature of its double branched
cover, up to the fixed global orientation convention. Gordon--Litherland--
Murasugi Section 2 proves the more general cyclic-cover signature formula;
in B^4 its ambient-signature and self-intersection corrections vanish.
It is not necessary to assume F is a pushed-in Seifert surface in S^3.

The classical determinant phase identity gives, in a common Jones
square-root convention,

  S_i(x=i) = kappa*B_ii,    kappa in {i,-i}, independent of i.

The choice x=i versus x=-i conjugates the phase for two-component links.
Thus a bare +i should not be claimed without aligning conventions. In either
convention, S_1(i)+S_2(i)=kappa*tr B !=0, whereas delta(i)=0. Consequently
S_1+S_2=2delta is impossible. This agrees with the surgery proof above.

This audit establishes the stated necessary obstruction, not a claim that
B=0 forces an unknot family or that all band presentations fall under the
common-handed diagonal.

STRONGER RANK-ONE/TORSION COROLLARY (LLL1)
Assume H_1(Sigma_2(L))=Z plus torsion of order N>1. An integral symmetric
rank-one matrix has the form B=d*v*v^T, where v is a primitive integral
column vector and d is a nonzero integer. For completeness, choose a
primitive generator v for its rational image; symmetry forces this form
over Q, and an integral row u with u*v=1 gives d=u*B*u^T in Z. The gcd of
the matrix entries is |d| because v is primitive, so the Smith normal form
shows N=|d|. In particular |tr B|=N*||v||^2 >= N.

For any nonzero integer coherent twist k,
  |det(I+epsilon*2kB)|=|1+epsilon*2k*tr B| != 1.
Indeed equality could only give k=0 or k*tr B=-epsilon; the latter is
impossible since |tr B|>=N>1. Thus EACH nonzero coherent diagonal member
has nonunit determinant, not merely that the entire diagonal fails to be
Jones constant. For the LLL1 cover, N=144. This conclusion holds for every
qualifying disjoint two-band unknot completion of that link, without
searching its band presentations. It does not cover opposite-handed twists.
