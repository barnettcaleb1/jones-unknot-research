1. WHAT THE EXISTING TREFOIL CONTRADICTION DOES AND DOES NOT REFUTE
Take the THREE-CROSSING, crossing-minimal trefoil diagram, using the singleton
anti-parallel twist crossing shown in the source's own twist1.png. Its Jones
polynomial in the paper's variable is

  V_1=x^2+x^6-x^8,      p=x-x^-1.

Switching that seed crossing gives the actual unknot. Its oriented smoothing is
fixed under further twists and has

  H=-x-x^5.

Replacing the seed by an odd positive block of length L gives a genuine twist-knot
family. Changing one crossing cancels an adjacent pair by RII, and oriented smoothing
removes the other block crossings as RI curls. Hence the exact oriented skein is

  x^-2 V_L - x^2 V_(L-2)=pH,
  V_L=x^4 V_(L-2)+(x^2-x^4+x^6-x^8).

For L=2m+1 this solves to

  V_L=x^(4m)V_1+(x^2-x^4+x^6-x^8) sum_(j=0)^(m-1) x^(4j).

The highest x-power is2L+6 with coefficient-1. In the unique JVP reduction
V=A(p)+xB(p), x^2=px+1, the coefficient of p^(2L+5) in B is therefore-1:
x^N has B-degree N-1 with leading coefficient1, and every other monomial here has
smaller positive degree. Thus JVP degree is exactly2L+5, unbounded with L.

For this minimal host Bhost=3*3=9, Bhat=20, and Lemma5.8's claimed cap is22.
At L=23 the genuine residual state instead has b_51=-1.

PRECISE LOGICAL CONCLUSION:
The universal joint package Proposition5.12 -> Theorem5.6 -> Lemma5.8 is false as
stated. This contradiction already has a minimal host; a missing requirement of
host minimality alone cannot repair that package.

It is NOT correct to describe this example as a knot/machine proved to satisfy
C1–C3 but violating the conditional Theorem5.6. We have not verified C1–C3 for the
actual planted machine; the contradiction shows that the claimed package cannot
supply all the required conditions and identifications simultaneously. In particular,
at least one geometric premise, associated algebraic lemma, or identification is
wrong. This bounded audit does not isolate a single false picture or declare a
specific C1/C2/C3 clause false independently of the rest.

Theorem5.8's displayed paired skein identity itself checks algebraically:

  V_-+ +V_+- =x^-4 V_++ +x^4 V_-- -p^2 V_00.

Multiplication by x^±4 increases JVP degree by at most4. Thus a cap Bhat on the four
signed states really would imply cap Bhat+2 on V_00. The contradiction is not an
obvious missing degree shift in this extraction.

2. WHY SIMPLY ADDING A JONES-ONE ANCHOR DOES NOT FIX COMPRESSION
Start instead with the same trefoil shadow after switching its unknotting crossing.
This is an ACTUAL unknot diagram; it is nonminimal. Add positive full twists at the
marked anti-parallel seed. Write the resulting family as K_m with K_0=U. It has the
same fixed smoothing H=-x-x^5 and satisfies

  V_(m+1)=x^4 V_m +p x^2 H.

In particular K_1 is the trefoil and V_(K1)=x^2+x^6-x^8 !=1. This is a geometrically
realized Jones-one anchor, not a formal complex or hypothetical knot. It satisfies
the ordinary anti-parallel twist relations and the residual anchor identification.
It cannot satisfy the entire claimed compression package, since its degrees grow.

The example refutes a repair which ONLY adds V(host)=1. It does NOT refute a new
claim simultaneously restricted to crossing-minimal diagrams of nontrivial Jones-one
knots; no such knot is known here. That narrower statement remains unproved.

3. EXACT NECESSARY AND SUFFICIENT CONDITION FOR AN ANCHORED UNIFORM CAP
The obstruction can be stated without choosing a particular knot. For any actual
anti-parallel full-twist family with V_0=1 and fixed oriented smoothing H, set

  D=(x^4-1)+p x^2 H =p x^2(H+x+x^-1).

The skein recurrence gives, for every integer m,

  V_m=1+D (x^(4m)-1)/(x^4-1).

The quotient is a Laurent polynomial even for negative m. Therefore the following
conditions are equivalent for this fixed marked family:

  (a) the JVP degrees are bounded independently of positive m;
  (b) D=0;
  (c) H=-(x+x^-1), the Jones polynomial of the two-component unlink;
  (d) V_m=1 for every m;
  (e) V_1=1.

Proof of the only potentially subtle implication: if D!=0, the top Laurent exponent
of D sum_(j=0)^(m-1)x^(4j) tends to infinity. A JVP degree cap B confines Laurent
support to [-B,B+1], so bounded JVP degree is impossible. Conversely D0 makes the
entire family constant. This is an integral-domain argument, not a numerical test.

Thus the host anchor fixes one closure value but leaves a second skein channel,
namely its oriented smoothing, undetermined. A length-independent cap would ALREADY
force that missing channel to be the unlink channel. It cannot be deduced from
anchor vanishing alone. The actual switched-trefoil unknot has H=-x-x^5, explicitly
showing the undetermined channel is geometrically real.

4. WHAT A MINIMAL-HOST REPAIR WOULD STILL HAVE TO DO
A valid repair would need to establish the smoothing conclusion H=-(x+x^-1) for
the specific marked crossings of a hypothetical minimal nontrivial Jones-one knot,
or supply a different geometric propagation argument with the same force. That is
a substantial theorem, not a small numerical adjustment to the overhead11.

There is also a second scope issue. Theorem5.11's second-machine application uses a
two-state host family obtained by flipping the other crossing in the original
minimal diagram. It has the same NUMBER of crossings, but the flipped diagram need
not be crossing-minimal for the flipped knot (and may be a nonminimal unknot).
The source correctly only claims a common shadow cap there. A proposed replacement
lemma valid solely on minimal host diagrams therefore cannot be substituted into
that step without a further argument. Minimality of the original diagram does not
supply minimality of every generated host.

The two-crossing low-order endpoint is not repaired by ignoring this issue: actual
trefoil crossing faces can have three unknot vertices and one trefoil vertex. A
Jones-one anchor cannot simply be copied to a whole crossing face.

ASSESSMENT
The earlier audit can be made precise without overstating it. There is an exact,
minimal-host contradiction to the unconditional compression package; a separate
actual unknot example defeats the unrestricted anchor-only repair. The conditional
Theorems5.6/5.10 should not be called refuted by examples whose C1–C3 premises have
not been established. The unresolved content is precisely the geometric mechanism
that would kill the smoothing channel, uniformly on the hosts needed by5.11.
No proof repair satisfying those requirements was obtained in this pass.
