2026-11-01. Bounded investigation; no claim about priority.

RESULT
The displayed Reidemeister movie is not refuted here. Its endpoint is
misidentified as a state with the SAME four clasp coordinates. Under one
fixed orientation of the machine shadow, the movie switches all four
clasp signs, reverses the drawn orientation of the closed twist component,
and changes one residual crossing sign. Ignoring this distinction loses a
nontrivial Jones normalization factor. Consequently the stated same-layer
transfer from (C1d) to (C2) is not justified by the supplied movie.

This is an explicit failure of the relative-boundary same-clasp isotopy
used in the proof, not a counterexample to the conditional algebraic
statement (C2) under its two higher-level vanishing hypotheses. We do not
claim those hypotheses hold in the small examples below.

THE FIXED-ORIENTATION ERROR
Fix both seed strands upward and the omega_1 orientation of the closed
component throughout the crossing cube. In the displayed s=- instance:
  omega_1: clasp signs (-,-,-,-), residual (-,-,-);
  omega_5 with that SAME orientation: clasp signs (+,+,+,+), residual (+,-,-).
The final source picture reverses all arrows on the closed component. This
restores its four oriented clasp signs to minus, but this reorientation is
not the operation of changing one coordinate in a fixed oriented cube.

The closed component's total linking number with the rest is 2s in the
initial picture and -2s in the fixed-orientation final picture. Reversing
it changes the writhe by 8s. With t=A^-4, x=t^(1/2), the normalized Jones
factor is A^(-24s)=t^(6s)=x^(12s). Thus the corrected relation furnished by
the movie is
  V(initial fixed state) = x^(12s) V(final with four flipped clasp signs),
not equality with the claimed same-clasp state.

Compatible vertical closure (a1--b3 and c1--d3) checks this factor exactly.
Its fixed-orientation values are
  initial: x^-2 + 2x^-6 + x^-10,
  literal final, fixed orientation: x^10 + 2x^6 + x^2.
Their ratio is x^-12, as s=- requires. The vertical closure does NOT
separate the initial picture from the claimed same-clasp state; we do not
use it as an isotopy counterexample.

AN ORIENTATION-INDEPENDENT RELATIVE-BOUNDARY COUNTERTEST
Close a1--c1 and b3--d3 by disjoint exterior caps. This horizontal closure
is NOT compatible with both displayed upward arrows. It is used only as
an UNORIENTED relative-boundary test: an oriented relative-boundary isotopy
would in particular be an unoriented one under every exterior closure.
For omega_1 the automatically oriented closure has Jones
  -x^3 + x - 2x^-1 + x^-3 - 2x^-5 + x^-7.
For the claimed fixed-clasp state (only z0 switched), its Jones is
  -x - x^-1.
The latter actually simplifies to the two-component unlink. These cannot
be related by any signed Laurent monomial, so no component-orientation
choice can rescue the alleged unoriented relative-boundary isotopy.
The same six-term versus two-term discrepancy occurs when comparing L=3
to L=1 with all four clasp crossings fixed. L=1 with all four clasps switched
recovers the six-term polynomial, exactly locating the error in the first
length-reduction claim as well.

The root independently computed raw Kauffman brackets by a frontier state
sum from these PDs, without Regina. Its file independent_source_bracket.json
records, for the horizontal closures:
  omega_1: A^-15 - A^-11 + 2A^-7 - A^-3 + 2A - A^5;
  literal omega_5: A^6 times the preceding bracket;
  claimed same-clasp state: A^-5 + A^-1.
Reidemeister-I changes multiply the bracket only by signed A^3 powers.
The first and third brackets are not related by ANY signed monomial.
Thus this certificate is independent of Jones orientation conventions.

WHY (C1d) DOES NOT IMMEDIATELY REPAIR THE ARGUMENT
(C1d) does address every clasp state, so the change Y -> -Y by itself need
not be fatal. The missing factor x^(12s) is the obstruction to the stated
same-r coefficient inference. In the paper's ring x^2=px+1, multiplication
by x^12 or x^-12 mixes levels r,r-1,...,r-12. Vanishing at level r of the
new nonexceptional state does not make level r of its monomial multiple
vanish. The assumptions G_(r+1),G_(r+2) supply no vanishing of those lower
levels. A new argument would be required; no such repair is established
here. In particular, merely increasing a numerical cap does not justify
this downward induction step.

movie's output with a fixed oriented cube state in Proposition 5.12(G2).

EXACT TL2 TRANSFER VECTORS
In the basis I (two vertical arcs), e (top/bottom caps), with e^2=delta e,
write the tangle bracket f I+g e. Vertical and horizontal closures evaluate
respectively to delta*f+g and f+delta*g. Exact Laurent division of these two
computed closure brackets gives:
  omega_1:
    f = A - 2A^-3 + 3A^-7 - A^-11 + A^-15,
    g = A^3 - 2A^-1 + A^-5;
  literal omega_5: A^6 times BOTH preceding coefficients;
  claimed same-clasp state:
    f = A^3 + A^-9,
    g = A - 2A^-3 + A^-7.
Thus the literal picture movie preserves the whole bracket up to its two
RI framing factors, while the claimed fixed-clasp state has a different
skein vector. transfer_matrix.py and transfer_vectors.json reproduce these
exact integer Laurent coefficients. This is a source-grounded TL2 test,
not an assumed realization of a formal skein combination.
