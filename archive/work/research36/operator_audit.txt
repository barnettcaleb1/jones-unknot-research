LITERAL CLASP-MACHINE OPERATOR AUDIT: AN UNKNOT HOST DOES NOT GIVE COMPRESSION

Definition5.4, (C1)--(C4), Theorem5.6, Lemma5.8 and Proposition5.12;

DECISIVE RESULT
Plant the literal machine at the switched unknotting crossing in the usual
three-crossing trefoil shadow. This host is an actual unknot, V_host=1.
For the paper's locked clasp state M_-+=[--;++], the Jones polynomial has
unbounded JVP degree as the odd central twist length L increases. More
precisely, in the conventions below its JVP degree is 2L+7 for every odd
L>=7. At L=7, its coefficient b_21 is +1. The claimed uniform threshold is
Bhat=3*3+11=20. Thus this literal state violates signed-cube compression.
The argument identifies a specific clasp state, rather than merely inferring
that at least one unspecified state must fail.

1. THE EXACT PAIRED SKEIN IDENTITY, WITH SIGNS FIXED
Use the paper's normalization
  x^-2 V_+ - x^2 V_- = p V_0,   p=x-x^-1.
Fix the primed clasp signs (y0',y1')=(-,+), and write V_st for the Jones
polynomial at unprimed signs s,t. Applying the skein relation twice gives
  V_-+ = x^-4 V_++ - p^2 V_00 - p*x^2 V_0-,
  V_+- = x^4 V_-- + p*x^2 V_0-.
Consequently
  V_-+ + V_+- = x^-4 V_++ + x^4 V_-- - p^2 V_00.       (1)
Every exponent and sign in (1) was independently rederived. The orientations
are held fixed throughout. No component reversal occurs in this identity.

The literal clasp labels in this slice are
  V_-+ : [--;++] = M_-+       (the locked state),
  V_++ : [+-;++]              (left clasp cancels),
  V_-- : [--; -+]             (right clasp cancels),
  V_+- : [+-; -+]             (both clasps cancel).
Thus three terms in (1) are exactly the directly reducible states in G1,
Step1. Their canceling clasp is removed by RII; the remaining residual twist
is a succession of RI kinks, as in the source figures. For fixed odd parity,
adding two central crossings changes none of these normalized Jones
polynomials. Denote their fixed combination by
  C=x^-4 V_++ + x^4 V_-- - V_+-.
By the paper's ordinary double smoothing C4/G4, V_00 is precisely the
ordinary residual twist R_m at the seed. Hence the LITERAL locked machine
polynomial T_m satisfies

                     T_m = C - p^2 R_m.                (2)

This uses only the direct cancelable-clasp reductions and the double-smoothing
identification, not the disputed exceptional reduction G2 or the descending
finite-type argument. If a different reading of the source drawing invalidates
one of these local identifications, it invalidates that stated geometric
package rather than salvaging its consequences simultaneously.

2. THE TWO-CHANNEL TL2 RECURRENCE
For fixed odd parity, adding one full twist has two normalized eigenvalues
1 and z=x^4 (reversing the local generator uses z^-1). This also follows
directly from the paper's oriented skein relation:
  R_(m+1)=x^4 R_m+p*x^2 H,
where H is the fixed oriented smoothing. Thus every fixed clasp state has
form A+B*z^m over Q(x), and satisfies
  T_(m+2)-(1+z)T_(m+1)+z*T_m=0.
Equation (2) identifies its actual growth coefficient:
  B_locked=-p^2 B_residual.
No assertion that the host scalar determines this coefficient is justified.

3. ACTUAL UNKNOT HOST AND EXPLICIT NONZERO COEFFICIENT
Take the standard three-crossing trefoil diagram and switch its unknotting
crossing; the result is U. Choose that crossing as the seed. The oriented
smoothing is a Hopf link with
  H=-x-x^5.
Index residual full twists so R_0=1, R_1=x^2+x^6-x^8. Define
  D=x^4-1+p*x^2 H=x^2+x^6-x^8-1
   =(x^2-1)*(1-x^6).
The exact solution, valid for every integer m, is
  R_m=1+D*(x^(4m)-1)/(x^4-1).
For m>=1 the quotient is the finite geometric sum. Equivalently,
  R_m=[x^2*(x^4+1)+(1-x^6)*x^(4m)]/(x^2+1).
Therefore
  B_residual=(1-x^6)/(x^2+1) != 0,
  B_locked=-p^2*(1-x^6)/(x^2+1) != 0,
and the literal clasped machine obeys the exact difference equation

                   T_(m+1)-T_m=-p^2 D*x^(4m).          (3)

This is an exact operator-response counterexample to the suggestion that
V_host=1 forces the growing channel to vanish. It does not rely on numerical
root samples, a formal non-geometric tangle, or a guessed crossing closure.
The original unknot host is fixed while the actual central twist length grows.

4. A FINITE VIOLATION OF THE PAPER'S STATED THRESHOLD
The all-positive residual block has L=2m-1 crossings. Its Laurent maximum
exponent is 4m+4 with coefficient -1, and its JVP degree is 4m+3=2L+5.
Multiplication by -p^2 therefore has JVP degree 4m+5=2L+7, with leading
b-coefficient +1.

For the three directly reducible clasp states, the actual reductions leave
at most n+1 crossings and ell+2 components. With n=3, ell=1 this gives their
safe JVP cap 3*4+3-1=14. Multiplication by x^4 or x^-4 raises this cap by at
most four, so C has degree at most18. This is just the elementary diagram
cap, not signed-cube compression.

For odd L>=7, 2L+7>18, so the top coefficient in -p^2 R_m cannot cancel
against C. Hence deg_p T_m=2L+7 exactly. For L=7 (m=4), b_21(T_m)=+1,
whereas the combined assertion Proposition5.12 + conditional Theorem5.6
would force all coefficients above Bhat=20 to vanish. This does not refute
Theorem5.6 under independently verified C1--C3 hypotheses; it refutes the
claim that the literal geometric package supplies those hypotheses here.
The standard-library verifier checks the Laurent recurrence, reconstruction,
and these JVP leading coefficients; it does not claim to have calculated the
entire fixed polynomial C or a fresh PD state sum of the drawn machine.

5. RELATION TO THE SEPARATE EXCEPTIONAL-FIGURE AUDIT
The geometry agent reports that omega_1 -> omega_5 changes crossing data
and reverses the central component's orientation. If the relevant total
linking is +/-2, such a reversal can multiply Jones by x^(+/-12). A factor
of that kind mixes JVP coefficient levels and cannot be discarded in a
same-level vanishing argument. That is a plausible specific failure in G2.
It is separate from (1)--(3): those hold at fixed orientations and identify
the growing locked state M_-+, rather than the exceptional M_++ picture.
This note does not treat the reported figure reinterpretation as an
independently computed isotopy or as a replacement for its geometric audit.

SCOPE
This refutes the unrestricted local compression package as stated for an
arbitrary fixed host shadow, including a host with Jones polynomial1. The
host is a NONMINIMAL diagram of U. A newly proposed restriction solely to
crossing-minimal diagrams of hypothetical nontrivial Jones-one knots is not
refuted by this example; it is also not supplied by the paper's local
relative-boundary argument. Nothing here solves Jones unknot detection.
