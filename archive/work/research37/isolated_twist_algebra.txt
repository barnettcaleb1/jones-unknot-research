TWO-BAND TWISTS: A FINITE-JET EXCLUSION AND AN EXACT ISOLATED-POINT CERTIFICATE
Pass37. This is an algebraic consequence of the previously audited marked
band geometry and the exact two-site Jones recurrence. No new Jones-one
knot or general unknot-detection result is asserted.

INPUT AND CONVENTIONS
An actual oriented unknot has two disjoint antiparallel twist sites. The
parameters m,n count full twists in calibrated local charts having the SAME
normalized eigenvalue z=A^8=x^-4, where x=t^(1/2). Reversing the local
convention at a single site reverses that parameter before applying this
note. Let delta=-x-x^-1. The single cup-cap replacements have Jones
polynomials S1,S2, with the orientations induced by the original knot;
the double replacement has Jones polynomial delta^2. All full-twist
closures remain knots.

Set D1=S1-delta, D2=S2-delta. The exact recurrence is
  V_mn-1 = [D1*z^n*(1-z^m)+D2*z^m*(1-z^n)]/delta.        (1)
The expression has a removable singularity at x=i and is a Laurent
polynomial. It is an identity of full polynomials, not of evaluations.

0. STRONGER FIRST TEST: UNEQUAL VANISHING ORDERS EXCLUDE ALL Z^2
This test requires NO surgery-matrix assumption. Suppose D1,D2 are both
nonzero, with unequal finite orders r1,r2 of vanishing at x=1.
For m,n both nonzero, the two terms in the numerator of (1) have orders
r1+1 and r2+1: each 1-z^m has a simple zero, and z^n is a unit. Terms
with different orders cannot cancel. If precisely one parameter is zero,
(1) has one nonzero term. Therefore

       r1 != r2, D1,D2 !=0  ==>  V_mn=1 iff m=n=0.       (0)

This is a complete all-integer exclusion, including opposite signs and
arbitrarily large twists, from a finite Taylor-jet certificate. It is not
merely a rejection of a one-parameter trajectory.
Explicitly, suppose r1>r2 and write
  D_j=c_j*(x-1)^r_j + higher terms, c_j !=0.
For arbitrary m,n the coefficient of (x-1)^(r2+1) in V_mn-1 is -2*n*c2.
Thus Jones1 forces n=0. On that axis, the coefficient of
(x-1)^(r1+1) is -2*m*c1, forcing m=0.
The proof uses delta(1)=-2 and 1-x^(-4m)=4m*(x-1)+higher terms.
The case r2>r1 is obtained by exchanging the sites.

For the experimentally occurring orders (r1,r2)=(3,2), the exact finite
conditions are
  D1(1)=D1'(1)=D1''(1)=0, D1'''(1)!=0;
  D2(1)=D2'(1)=0, D2''(1)!=0.
Then V_mn'''(1)=-6*n*D2''(1). Once n=0,
V_m0''''(1)=-8*m*D1'''(1). Consequently these third/fourth knot jets alone
certify the entire integer locus. Euler derivatives give the same first
nonzero coefficients, so they can be used for these input tests.
For the saved band pair D1'''(1)=-288 and D2''(1)=-96, giving
V_mn'''(1)=576*n and, when n=0, V_m0''''(1)=2304*m.

Equal vanishing orders do NOT establish any candidate or preservation.
The rank-one method below provides an additional, independent exact
certificate for families that survive this first test.

RANK-ONE INPUT FOR THE REMAINING SECTIONS
Assume the symmetric integral double-cover surgery matrix has rank one:
  B=d*v*v^T,  d nonzero integer, v=(v1,v2) primitive integral.
Put a=v2^2 and b=v1^2. The previously audited signature/determinant formula,
in one common global sign convention kappa in {+1,-1}, is
  S1(i)=kappa*i*d*b,    S2(i)=kappa*i*d*a.                 (2)
No independent reorientation of either partial link is allowed here.
The absolute surgery determinant is
  |V_mn(-1)|=|1+epsilon*2d*(b*m+a*n)|,
with one common epsilon. We do not need to identify epsilon with kappa.

1. THE EXACT NECESSARY PARAMETER LINE
The limit of (1-z^m)/delta at x=i is 2*m*i, for every integer m.
Using (2) directly in (1) therefore gives the signed identity
  V_mn(t=-1)=1-2*kappa*d*(b*m+a*n).                       (3)
Consequently Jones polynomial1 forces
                         b*m+a*n=0.                    (4)

Alternatively, the absolute determinant gives (4) immediately for |d|>1.
The apparent determinant-minus-one exception when |d|=1 is not an exception
to (3). It also cannot arise in the stated oriented fission-band geometry:
S_i has odd integral x-exponents and S_i(1)=-2, so S_i(i)/i is even. Thus
B_ii is even. Since v is primitive, at least one v_i is odd; hence d is
even. This supplies a separate exclusion of |d|=1 for these actual bands.
The signed limit argument remains useful without relying on that parity.

2. BOTH BAND ENTRIES NONZERO: AT MOST ONE NON-ANCHOR SOLUTION
Assume a,b>0. Primitivity implies gcd(a,b)=1, so (4) is precisely
                     (m,n)=(a*k,-b*k), k in Z.
Because D1(i),D2(i) are nonzero, the rational function R=D1/D2 is regular
and nonzero at x=i, with R(i)=b/a. For k!=0, (1) vanishes identically iff

       R(x)=z^(a*k)*(z^(b*k)-1)/(z^(a*k)-1).             (5)

This is a necessary AND sufficient full-polynomial test. The apparent
singularity of the right side at z=1 is removable, including for negative k.
Writing z=exp(s), its logarithmic derivative at s=0 is (a+b)*k/2.
Since x*d/dx=-4*z*d/dz, equation (5) forces

 k = k_star := - [x*R'(x)/R(x)]_(x=i) / [2*(a+b)]
             = -i*R'(i)/[2*(a+b)*R(i)].                 (6)

Thus the whole integer Jones-one locus consists of the anchor (0,0), and
AT MOST ONE additional point. It can be determined without any parameter scan:
  (i) Compute k_star exactly.
  (ii) If k_star is not a nonzero integer, only the anchor works.
  (iii) Otherwise set (m,n)=(a*k_star,-b*k_star), and check exactly
       D1*(z^(a*k_star)-1)
         -D2*z^(a*k_star)*(z^(b*k_star)-1) = 0.          (7)
       If it holds, that point and the anchor are the entire locus;
       if it fails, only the anchor works.
Equation (7) is a Laurent polynomial identity and involves no division.
If it holds, topology of that possible Jones-one knot remains a separate issue.

For completely rational arithmetic, define the integers
  c_j=D_j(i)/i,   e_j=[x*D_j'(x)]_(x=i)/i.
Odd Laurent exponents guarantee these are integers, even for negative powers.
Then
  k_star= -(e1/c1-e2/c2)/(2*(a+b)).                       (8)
The formula is independent of the common phase kappa and the sign of d.
If k_star=0, equation (5) was derived only for nonzero k: it means there is
NO possible non-anchor solution, not that its formal k=0 limit needs checking.

3. ZERO BAND ENTRIES
If v1=0, primitivity gives v2=+/-1, hence b=0,a=1. Equation (4) forces n=0.
On this axis, (1) is V_m0-1=(D1/delta)*(1-z^m). Therefore
  S1=delta: every (m,0) has Jones1;
  S1!=delta: only (0,0) has Jones1.
If v2=0, exchange the two sites. These are exact coordinate-family criteria.
The one-candidate theorem requires both v_i nonzero and must NOT be claimed
for these cases. Rank-zero B is outside this theorem.

4. CHEAP ADDITIONAL EXACT REJECTION TESTS
For every nonzero candidate, the right side of (5) has finite nonzero limit
b/a at each fourth root of unity in x (z=1). In particular, at x=1:
  ord_(x=1) D1 = ord_(x=1) D2,
and the ratio of their first nonzero Taylor coefficients must be b/a.
The same statement holds at x=-1. Unequal vanishing orders reject every
nonzero mixed-sign solution even before computing (6).
There is also a full rational-function symmetry requirement R(ix)=R(x),
because the right side of (5) is a function of x^4. These are necessary
prechecks; they do not replace the final exact identity when they pass.
No general topology statement forcing such a mismatch has been established.

5. SAVED LLL(1) BAND PAIR: AN INDEPENDENT FINITE EXCLUSION
Data: work/research34/two_band_family.json. S1 is the cut with active_bands
[false,true]; S2 is [true,false]. This matches (1), since S1 smooths site1.
Here a=b=1, and exact coefficient arithmetic gives
  c1=144, e1=-576;   c2=144, e2=-720.
Hence
  k_star=-((-4)-(-5))/4=-1/4,
which is not an integer. Thus V_mn=1 iff m=n=0 for this entire marked family.
The x=1 precheck independently rejects it: D1 has order3, with third Euler
derivative -288, while D2 has order2, with second Euler derivative -96.
At the first nonzero order, Euler and ordinary derivatives agree.
This agrees with the previous support-span exclusion, without using it.

SCOPE AND REPRODUCIBILITY
The statements are the unequal-order all-integer exclusion and the rank-one
finite exact certificate, not a guarantee that its
single candidate is geometrically realizable/nontrivial or always excluded.
It applies to ANY qualifying rank-one two-band presentation with both diagonal
entries nonzero, not merely the previously computed band pair. Inputs are the
actual induced-orientation partial Jones polynomials and the audited band matrix.
No claim is made for unrelated two-variable skein templates, independently
reoriented partial links, rank-two B, or arbitrary many-band mixed-sign twists.

verify_isolated_twist_algebra.py checks (6) on exact synthetic Laurent pairs
for several signed k and unequal a,b, and independently reproduces the saved
actual example's c_j,e_j,k_star and x=1 orders. These synthetic checks are
algebra checks, not claimed tangle realizations. The proof above covers all
integers without extrapolating from them.
