SIXTEEN CERTIFIED MARKED RECORDS: COMPLETE INTEGER-TWIST EXCLUSION
2026-11-01. Bounded follow-up of the 22 stored unknot band-fusion hits.

Outcome
Of the 22 previously stored two-stage constructions, 16 were reconstructed
as actual pairs of disjoint band rectangles on the original LLL1 diagram.
For every one of these 16 marked records, the entire family K(m,n), m,n in Z,
has Jones polynomial one if and only if (m,n)=(0,0). The zero member is an
actual unknot, verified by simplification to a zero-crossing one-component
diagram. This is not a claim of 16 distinct isotopy classes of templates.
No new initial-band search was performed.

The unmarked records are 0,2,4,17,18,21. They remain unresolved as two-site

They are not counterexamples or newly verified candidate families.

Geometry and orientation tracking
marked_hits.py reconstructs each first move from its saved band specification,
tracks every surviving original crossing and its cyclic-port rotations,
then reproduces exactly the saved intermediate PD. It pulls the second
band's ports back to the original diagram. Certification requires:
  * each band lies in an original face;
  * its attaching edges are disjoint from those of the other band;
  * if the bands share a face, their boundary endpoints do not alternate;
  * the simultaneous diagram is planar and its anchor simplifies to U.
Both bands are inserted before any orientation rebuild or simplification.
Each carries its stored initial half-twist count (0 or -1), and parameter
m or n adds 2m or 2n half twists. Thus the entire integer family has the
same component connectivity as its unknot anchor.

Partial smoothings inherit the orientations of the actual anchor, restricted
to every surviving original strand; they are not independently reoriented
by a default algorithm. All four cut states have saved PDs. Spherogram and
Regina writhe agree. The double cut has Jones delta^2 in all sixteen cases,
where x=sqrt(t) and delta=-(x+x^-1). The parent independently recomputed all
48 cut brackets without Regina and confirmed the results.

As a further normalization check, verify_corners.py constructs K(1,0) and
K(0,1) directly for every certified record. Each has writhe exactly two less
than the anchor, and the exact full Jones polynomials satisfy
  delta*V(1,0)=delta*z+(1-z)*S1,
  delta*V(0,1)=delta*z+(1-z)*S2,
with z=x^-4 and S_i the Jones polynomial of the anchor with band i cut.
Thus both sites use the same normalized full-twist convention. All 32
checks passed; complete PDs and polynomials are retained.

Exact all-integer argument
Put P1=S1-delta and P2=S2-delta. The two-site Temperley--Lieb identity,
with V(0,0)=1 and S12=delta^2, gives

 delta*(V(m,n)-1)
   = z^n*(1-z^m)*P1 + z^m*(1-z^n)*P2.

This is valid for negative as well as positive integers because the twist
operator is invertible and its two eigenvalues are 1 and z.

In each certified record, the vanishing orders of P1,P2 at x=1 are 3 and 2,
in one order or the other. The leading Euler moments are respectively
-288 and +96 or -96. These are exact integer derivatives, computed as
  (x*d/dx)^j P(1) = sum_e coefficient(e)*e^j.
Vanishing of all preceding Euler moments makes the first nonzero Euler
moment detect the ordinary vanishing order as well.

If m,n are both nonzero, 1-z^m and 1-z^n each have a simple zero at x=1.
The two terms on the right therefore have DIFFERENT vanishing orders, 4
and 3, so their sum cannot be zero. If just one of m,n is zero, the surviving
term is nonzero because its P_i and 1-z^k are nonzero Laurent polynomials.
Therefore V(m,n)=1 only when m=n=0. No finite twist bound or determinant
argument is needed for this conclusion.

Why six records are not certified
Their second bands lie in a face opened by the first fusion, rather than
in a face of the original diagram. Pulling only their endpoint labels back
does not preserve the complete marked geometry. In fact that naive
construction becomes nonplanar when the first full twist is inserted (and
one record fails even at its pulled-back anchor). We explicitly reject
those diagrams. A full marked isotopy or a different simultaneous band
presentation would be necessary before applying the two-channel formula.
No Jones polynomials of nonplanar reconstructions are used in this result.
