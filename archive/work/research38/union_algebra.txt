THE EXACT ONE-AXIS SKEIN CONSTRAINT
Let K_n be a one-region symmetric union with partial knot J, and use Tanaka's
signed half-twist parameter n. Put
  F=V_J(t)V_J(t^-1),   Q=V_(axis infinity smoothing)/(−t^.5−t^−.5).
The off-axis mirror geometry makes F,Q reciprocal. The normalized local
skein expansion (Tanaka2022 Theorem1.1, proof§3) gives
  V_(K_n)=(-t^-1)^n F + (1-(-t^-1)^n)Q.
Applying t -> t^-1 and eliminating Q gives

 t^n V_(K_n)(t)+(-1)^n V_(K_n)(t^-1)
               =(t^n+(-1)^n)V_J(t)V_J(t^-1).            (1)

The primary complete source is Tanaka, A formula for the Jones polynomial
of symmetric unions (2022), Theorem1.3 and its proof on printed pp1,4:
  https://www.ed.gifu-u.ac.jp/file/5c3c1905e8c24e832f61fa5f837be463.pdf
It is also explicitly restated correctly as Theorem1.1 in Kose,
On amphichirality of symmetric unions, arXiv2111.08765:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.08765
These full primary texts were read in this pass; (1) was independently
rederived by the displayed two-channel elimination.

SPECIALIZATION TO KT, WITH THE FINITE FILTER EXPLICIT
Use the fixed chirality whose exact cached Jones polynomial is
 P=-t^-4+2t^-3-2t^-2+2t^-1+t^2-2t^3+2t^4-2t^5+t^6.
The prior pass computed this from an actual braid, and independently from
a published KT bridge projection. Set m=-n, matching kt_partial_filter.py.
Equation(1) becomes
  (1+(-t)^m)F=P+(-t)^m P(t^-1).                         (2)
Zero m gives J#mirror(J), not the prime nontrivial KT knot.
The one-axis derivative congruence 8|m| divides P'(-1)=-48 restricts
m to ±1,±2,±3,±6. This is an exhaustive arithmetic reduction, not a search
cutoff. Exact Laurent division of (2) gives:
  ±6: not Laurent;
  -3,-2,-1: negative constant coefficient;
  +1: constant coefficient1 but nonconstant F;
  +3: F(primitive cube root)=7;
  +2: F=1.
For a Laurent integral norm V_J V_J*, its constant coefficient is the sum
of squares of all coefficients of V_J. The negative cases are impossible;
constant coefficient1 forces a monomial and hence F=1. At a primitive
cube root every knot Jones polynomial is1, so the +3 case is impossible.
Thus m=2 is the only choice in this chirality, and V_J V_J*=1. The latter
forces V_J=±t^r; V_J(1)=1 and V'_J(1)=0 imply V_J=1.
Mirroring reverses the signed twist. In particular every permitted one-axis
presentation has EVEN twist number. The verified finite certificate is

WHAT IS KNOWN GEOMETRICALLY, AND WHAT IS NOT
Moore's primary Figure3 explicitly supplies the already known KT example
with actual partial unknot. Definition4 uses ONE elementary tangle replacement,
so it is the same one-region notion, not a generalized multiple-region union.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.07996
An arbitrary new partial J is not proved unknotted merely because V_J=1.
Lamm's theorem says a trivial WHOLE symmetric union has trivial partial knot;
it cannot be applied with the nontrivial whole knot KT.

The earlier source-audited pass12 restrictions sharpen this remaining gap:
even-symmetric-union genus and group theorems give Delta_J=1, g(J)<=1,
and meridional rank mr(J)<=3. If J is nontrivial, then g(J)=1 and mr(J)=3.
The genus-one/three-bridge classification and exact pretzel Jones moments
exclude b(J)<=3, hence b(J)>=4. Such a partial would therefore simultaneously
violate Jones unknot detection AND the meridional-rank conjecture.
These restrictions and their exact primary locators are in findings.txt§§5,7;
this pass does not re-label those earlier audits as fresh results.
They do not prove mr(J)=b(J), nor do they classify every possible KT quotient.

CONCLUSION FOR THE RESEARCH DECISION
The one-region KT target is a known inverse-decomposition problem with a
known trivial partial solution and a strong algebraic constraint. Merely
varying the twist box of the known diagram preserves its partial knot.
The task would require a genuinely different off-axis decomposition and a
nontriviality witness for the new partial. No credible new construction or
unconditional proof that all such partials are trivial was obtained here.
The full 2025 joint article remains an access/proposition-verification gap;
its abstract's main consequence is independently supported by the complete
older primary formula and the already retained exact certificate.
