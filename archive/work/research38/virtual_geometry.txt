1. Explicit diagram and complete height-only test
Let sigma be the positive generator of the two-strand braid group and tau a virtual crossing. Virtualize one crossing of the closure of sigma^3 using Dye--Kauffman's switch-flanked-by-two-virtual-crossings convention. This gives the closure of
    tau sigma^(-1) tau sigma^2.
Its virtual Jones polynomial is 1, because the virtual crossings disappear from the virtual bracket calculation and the result has the Jones polynomial of closure(sigma^(-1)sigma^2)=U.

Replace the two virtual crossings by actual classical crossings sigma^a and sigma^b, a,b in {+1,-1}. This is an ACTUAL specified classical diagram, not a formal skein element. Since B_2 is infinite cyclic, its closure is
    closure(sigma^(1+a+b)).
Consequently all four outcomes are completely identified:
  a=b=+1: T(2,3), Jones t+t^3-t^4;
  (+1,-1), (-1,+1): closure(sigma)=U;
  (-1,-1): closure(sigma^(-1))=U.
The Reidemeister-II cancellations in B_2, followed by destabilization of sigma^+/-1, give geometric unknot certificates independent of Jones detection. Regina/Spherogram independently verify the polynomials and simplify all three unit-Jones cases to zero crossings and one component.

Primary source: H. A. Dye and L. H. Kauffman, Minimal surface representations of virtual knots and links, Algebraic & Geometric Topology 5 (2005), 509--535, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0401035 . For the two-crossing positive braid tangle in this pairing order,

This calculation is independent of a common reduced/unreduced factor. It is NOT being advertised as a theorem that the published figure is wrong. 3. General embedding warning
A surface bracket retains essential state curves. The virtual evaluator counts each such state circle as an ordinary loop. A Heegaard torus embedding instead evaluates a slope (p,q) as an actual torus knot/link with the surface framing. These evaluators need not agree. If all nonzero essential states were simultaneously meridian or longitude parallels in one marked unknotted-torus embedding, their evaluations would agree; that sufficient condition still requires exact slope and framing identification from the actual diagram.

No nontrivial classical Jones-one knot was produced. The actual finite experiment above is complete; the arbitrary-embedding problem is not claimed solved.
