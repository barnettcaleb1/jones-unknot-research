ALL SL(2,Z) MARKINGS OF THE EXPLICIT THREE-CROSSING TORUS KNOT
Pass 38: exact algebra and independent integer reduction

Input certified separately by the parent's explicit ribbon-surface state sum:

  F=C+(A-A^{-3}) E_2(u)+A E_2(v),
  C=A^{-7}-2A-A^5,

where E_2(w) denotes two parallel copies of a primitive torus slope,
u=(p,q), v=(r,s), and ps-qr=1. This note audits the resulting all-markings
algebra. It does not independently certify the source PD or the final
geometric unknot simplifications.

The result: the S^3 image can have Jones polynomial 1 ONLY when

  pq=0, p^2+q^2=1, and rs is either 0 or -1.                 (A)

Conversely every such marking has Jones polynomial 1, by the full exact
Laurent identity below. There are finitely many such signed markings.
The parent/geometry agent separately determines their actual knot type.

1. Exact torus-state evaluation and convention.

Write [n]_A=(A^{2n}-A^{-2n})/(A^2-A^{-2}). In the convention with
positive torus self-framing pq and positive kink factor -A^3,

  E_2(p,q)=2+A^{4q(p+1)}[2p+1]_A
             -A^{4q(p-1)}[2p-1]_A.                       (1)

This is the A -> A^{-1} version of Almeida--Gelca, Proposition 3.2,
https://www.math.ttu.edu/~rgelca/almeida_gelca.pdf . It must not be
quoted as the verbatim exponent convention of that source. The formula
extends to every integer p,q using [0]=0, [-n]=-[n]. It gives E_2(1,0)
=delta^2 and E_2(1,1)=1+A^4+A^8+A^12, as required by surface framing.
No multiplicativity of the Heegaard functional is used.

More explicitly, apply that proposition with (p,q) replaced by (2p,2q)
and j=1. The first-kind Chebyshev skein is T_2(c)=c^2-2.
Use S_{-2p}=-S_{2p-2} and the zero-framed unknot evaluation
S_k(unknot)=(-1)^k[k+1]. This gives
2+t^{-4pq-4q}[2p+1]_t-t^{-4pq+4q}[2p-1]_t;
substituting t=A^{-1} proves (1). The proposition and its convention
were independently opened and checked in the primary PDF.

Set
  X=pq, U=p^2+q^2, Y=rs, V=r^2+s^2, and A=exp(h).
Direct expansion of (1) gives

 E_2(p,q)=4+24X h+(80X^2+16U)h^2
          +(448X^3+320XU+64X)h^3/3+O(h^4).                (2)

For example [n]_{exp h}=n+(2/3)n(n^2-1)h^2+O(h^4);
substitution in (1) proves (2) as a polynomial identity in p,q.

2. Framing is determined without guessing a torus writhe correction.

The constant and linear terms of F are 2 and 6+24Y. For an actual
framed knot its unreduced bracket is

  F=delta*(-A^3)^f V_K(A^{-4}).

Since V_K(1)=1 and V_K'(1)=0, F(1)=2 forces f odd and
F'(1)=3 f F(1) forces f=1+4Y. Therefore Jones 1 is equivalent to

  F=-delta*A^{3+12Y}.                                    (3)

This also agrees with the separately supplied torus homology -2v
and local writhe 1. That geometric framing calculation is unnecessary
for the algebraic necessity of (3).

3. The first two nonconstant Jones coefficients give integer equations.

The h^2 coefficient of F+delta*A^{3+12Y} is

  16*(V-4Y^2-3Y+6X-1).

After imposing its vanishing, the h^3 coefficient is

  32*(Y^2+Y-2-6X+10X^2-20XY+2U).

Thus (3) requires

  V=4Y^2+3Y+1-6X,                                        (4)
  2U=-10X^2+20XY+6X-Y^2-Y+2.                             (5)

These exact equations were obtained independently by parent and this
agent. The intermediate un-substituted h^3 coefficient is

 (16/3)*(-80Y^3+20YV-23Y-66Y^2+3V
          +60X^2+12U-18X-15),

which reduces to the displayed expression by (4).

4. Complete Diophantine elimination, without a marking scan.

The determinant condition gives

  XY=(ps)(qr)=t(t+1)>=0,  t=qr,                           (6)
  (ps+qr)^2=1+4XY.                                       (7)

For nonzero integers a,b with ab=Z,

  2|Z| <= a^2+b^2 <= Z^2+1,                              (8)

where the upper bound follows from (a^2-1)(b^2-1)>=0.

First dispose of the zero cases; this is essential.
If X=0, primitivity of u gives U=1. Equation (5) gives
Y(Y+1)=0, hence Y=0 or -1. If Y=0, primitivity of v gives V=1,
and (4) gives X=0. These are exactly the possibilities in (A).

Now assume X,Y both nonzero. Equation (6) makes them have the same
sign. If X=-x and Y=-y with x,y>=1, equations (4) and (8) give

  4y^2-3y+1+6x=V <= y^2+1,

or 3y(y-1)+6x<=0, impossible.

It remains that X,Y are positive. Equation (4) and V<=Y^2+1 imply

  X >= Y(Y+1)/2.                                        (9)

If X>=2Y+1, equation (5) would imply

  2U=-10X(X-2Y)+6X-Y^2-Y+2
     <=-4X-Y^2-Y+2<0,

impossible. Therefore X<=2Y. Combining with (9) gives Y<=3.
The complete short remaining list is:

  Y=1: X=1 or 2. X=2 gives V=-4. X=1 gives U=8,
       but integer pq=1 forces U=2.
  Y=2: X=3 or 4. X=4 gives V=-1. X=3 gives U=22,
       but integer pq=3 forces U=10.
  Y=3: X=6. Then XY=18, and (7) requires an integer square
       equal to 73, impossible.

This proves (A) for ALL integral determinant-one markings.

5. Full-polynomial check of every survivor.

When X=0 and U=1, E_2(u)=delta^2.
When Y=0, E_2(v)=delta^2, and direct substitution gives

  F=-delta*A^3.

When Y=-1, V=2 and E_2(v)=1+A^{-4}+A^{-8}+A^{-12}; substitution gives

  F=-delta*A^{-9}.

These are exactly (3), with f=1 and f=-3 respectively. Thus the
low-order necessary conditions have identified all possible unit-Jones
markings, and an exact Laurent check proves their sufficiency.

Scope. This is a complete all-markings polynomial classification for
the specified actual torus diagram, conditional only on its separately
certified surface state sum and the standard framed torus evaluation.
It is not a generic theorem about all virtual Jones-one knots. If the
finitely many survivors simplify to the unknot, this particular entire
SL(2,Z) embedding strategy is exhausted, not merely a finite sample.
