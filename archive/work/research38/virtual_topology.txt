PASS38 REPLACEMENT: VIRTUAL JONES-ONE KNOTS ON A HEEGAARD TORUS
Independent topology/framing audit, 2026-11-01. No actual candidate knot is asserted.

1. WHAT IS PRESERVED, AND WHAT IS NOT
Let D be an oriented knot diagram on T^2, representing a knot in T^2 x I. Give
its link the surface/blackboard framing and put delta=-A^2-A^-2. Use the UNREDUCED
bracket convention: the empty link evaluates to 1 and a trivial circle to delta.
The ordinary virtual bracket forgets the isotopy type of every state circle and
assigns delta per circle. An embedding e:T^2 x I -> S^3 does not generally make
that forgetting operation valid. It sends essential surface curves to framed
torus links, whose brackets have to be evaluated honestly.

Collect the surface bracket into its actual isotopy basis before applying tests:
  [D]=c0(A) empty + sum_(s,r>=1) c_(s,r)(A) gamma_s^(r),
where s is an unoriented primitive slope, gamma_s^(r) consists of r parallel
essential circles, and all contractible-circle factors have been absorbed into
c0,c_(s,r). Distinct curves in one state are parallel on the torus. Coefficients
that cancel after grouping contribute nothing and must not be counted as support.
For a fixed Heegaard marking M, let E_M be S3 framed-bracket evaluation and let
E_v(gamma_s^(r))=delta^r. These are LINEAR evaluation rules here; no claim that
E_v is an algebra homomorphism under the torus skein product is needed or correct.
The complete framed discrepancy is exactly
  E_M([D])-E_v([D])
       = sum c_(s,r)(A) [ E_M(gamma_s^(r))-delta^r ].
It can vanish by cancellation between DIFFERENT slopes. A supportwise test is
therefore only a sufficient preservation mechanism, not a necessary condition
for a particular D to preserve its Jones polynomial.

2. EXACT STATEWISE FRAMING GATE
Choose oriented meridian/longitude coordinates (p,q) for the standard unknotted
Heegaard torus, with the convention that the surface push-off of slope (p,q) has
self-linking pq. Reversing the ambient convention changes this common sign.
A primitive slope is an underlying torus knot T(p,q). Its r parallel copies form
T(rp,rq), with each component's torus framing pq and mutual linking pq (when
oriented consistently). Thus the total component framing is r pq.

For any framed r-component link L with total self-framing f, its unreduced bracket
F_L obeys the first-order identity
  F_L(1)=(-1)^f(-2)^r,  F_L'(1)/F_L(1)=3f.
One elementary derivation uses the normalized Jones formula and
  V_L(1)=(-2)^(r-1),
  V_L'(1)/V_L(1)=(3/2) sum_(i<j) lk(L_i,L_j),
where the derivative is in t. In F=delta(-A^3)^w V(A^-4), w=f+2 sum lk;
the linking contributions cancel, and delta'(1)=0. Equivalently, the framed
bracket's first jet is unchanged by crossing changes between distinct components,
while self-crossing changes account exactly for the framing change. This also
provides a direct skein proof of the displayed identity.

Consequently E_M(gamma_s^(r))=delta^r implies r pq=0, hence pq=0. Conversely,
primitive pq=0 means a meridian or longitude. Its parallel copies are genuine
zero-framed unlinks, so their evaluation is delta^r. Therefore
  E_M(gamma_s^(r))=delta^r  IFF  s is meridional or longitudinal in M.
This is stronger than checking whether the underlying primitive curve is unknotted:
(p,1) and (1,q) are unknots, but their torus framings are nonzero unless pq=0.
Changing the framing independently in each state is not a legitimate modification
of the original knot or its bracket expansion.

3. INVARIANT SLOPE TEST FOR THE STATEWISE MECHANISM
An SL(2,Z) change of Heegaard marking preserves the geometric intersection number
  I((p,q),(r,s))=|ps-qr|.
If every nonzero essential basis term is to evaluate statewise as an unlink, the
set of its distinct slopes must fit inside a meridian-longitude pair. Thus:
  - one slope always admits such a marking;
  - two distinct slopes admit one exactly when their intersection is 1;
  - three distinct slopes cannot all qualify;
  - two slopes with intersection greater than 1 cannot qualify.
This statement applies to the grouped nonzero SUPPORT, not all raw smoothing
states. It blocks this particular statewise mechanism; it does not prove the full
sum discrepancy is nonzero, and does not prohibit Jones preservation by cancellation.

4. THE KNOT'S OWN FRAMING MUST ALSO BE NORMALIZED
Let w_T(D) be the signed crossing count intrinsic to the oriented torus diagram.
Let [D]=(p,q) in H1(T;Z). The surface framing of the embedded knot has self-linking
  f_e(D)=w_T(D)+pq
in the same chosen meridian-longitude convention. This can be seen by oriented
smoothing: the resulting disjoint curves have total framing plus twice their
pairwise linking equal to the quadratic form q(p,q)=pq; reintroducing each local
crossing adds its signed crossing contribution.
Therefore the two normalized Jones evaluations are
  V_virtual(D)=(-A^3)^(-w_T(D)) E_v([D])/delta,
  V_e(D)=(-A^3)^(-f_e(D)) E_M([D])/delta.
The precise target for normalized equality is
  E_M([D])=(-A^3)^(f_e(D)-w_T(D)) E_v([D]),
not necessarily equality of the two raw brackets. If the virtual polynomial is 1
and strict statewise preservation does give equality of raw brackets, the embedded
Jones polynomial is the monomial (-A^3)^(-pq). An actual classical knot cannot
have a nonconstant monomial Jones polynomial: normalization at t=1 fixes the sign,
and the derivative there vanishes. Thus in that special situation pq must be zero.
For computations it is safest to obtain the actual embedded PD and verify its
writhe/framing directly, using the quadratic correction as an independent check.

5. PRIMARY-SOURCE CONNECTION AND PRECISE NONTRIVIALITY LIMIT
Dye--Kauffman, Minimal surface representations of virtual knots and links,
Algebraic & Geometric Topology5 (2005),509--535, arXiv math/0401035v5:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0401035
Section2 defines the surface-state bracket and explicitly warns that equal state
classes may cancel after collecting coefficients. Section3 analyzes a single
virtualization using the complementary tangle coefficients alpha,beta. Figure11
and the paragraph on printed p523 explicitly give the one-VIRTUAL-CROSSING model
with essential slopes m and m+2l. Their intersection is 2. When both coefficients
are nonzero, Section3's model therefore fails the statewise-preservation test
under EVERY Heegaard marking. Do not confuse that one-virtual-crossing model with
single VIRTUALIZATION, which has two virtual crossings; the latter's Figure7
requires separate slope tracing.

Kauffman's virtualization construction gives nontrivial virtual knots with unit
Jones polynomial; the same Dye--Kauffman paper's introduction and Corollary3.5
explain the single-unknotting-crossing case. A direct foundational source is
Kauffman, Virtual knot theory:
https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/VKT.pdf

Virtual nonclassicality means no allowed stabilization/destabilization and
surface isotopy turns the virtual knot into a classical diagram. An S3 isotopy
of the embedded knot is allowed to pass through the two complementary solid tori;
it need not remain inside T^2 x I. Hence a virtual nonclassicality certificate
is NOT a certificate that e(D) is a nontrivial classical knot. This is a categorical
scope distinction, not a claim that every such embedding is trivial.

7. ALL-MARKINGS NORMALIZATION: INDEPENDENT FIRST AND SECOND JETS
Let the images of mu,lambda in a chosen Heegaard marking be (p,q),(r,s), with
|ps-qr|=1, and consistently take positive torus framing to be pq. Define E2(p,q)
as the unreduced framed bracket of two parallel curves of primitive slope(p,q).
Then the actual S3 framed bracket for this diagram is exactly
  F=c0+(A-A^-3) E2(p,q)+A E2(r,s).
Set h=log A, P=pq, R=rs. The first two jets are
  E2(p,q)=4+24P h+[80P^2+16(p^2+q^2)]h^2+O(h^3).
A direct formula providing the same jets is
  E2(p,q)=2+A^(4pq+4q)[2p+1]_A-A^(4pq-4q)[2p-1]_A,
  [n]_A=(A^(2n)-A^(-2n))/(A^2-A^-2), for signed integers n.
This follows from Almeida--Gelca, Kauffman bracket versus Jones polynomial skein
modules, Proposition3.2 and T2(gamma)=gamma^2-2:
https://www.math.ttu.edu/~rgelca/almeida_gelca.pdf
Their printed embedding convention gives the A-inverted expression; the formula
above uses the opposite torus-framing convention, calibrated by
E2(1,1)=1+A^4+A^8+A^12 (total self-framing2). Do not mix those conventions.

Expanding the ACTUAL candidate F gives F(1)=2 and F'(1)=6+24rs. Since it is a
single framed knot, F'(1)/F(1)=3f determines its framing exactly:
  f=1+4rs.
This verifies the global correction independently of guessing a planar writhe.
Therefore normalized Jones1 is equivalent to the full framed equality
  F=-delta A^(3+12rs).
The coefficient of h^2 in their difference is
  16[-1-3rs+6pq-4r^2s^2+r^2+s^2].
Thus every successful marking necessarily obeys
  6pq=4r^2s^2+3rs+1-r^2-s^2,  |ps-qr|=1.
This is a necessary finite-jet equation, not asserted sufficient. The parent and
braid agent are analyzing the remaining marking equations and the tangle agent
is checking actual S3 topology. No further slope search or classification is
claimed in this independent audit.

8. INDEPENDENT THIRD-JET AND COMPLETE INTEGER REDUCTION AUDIT
Let X=pq,Y=rs,U=p^2+q^2,V=r^2+s^2. Direct expansion of the same framed torus
formula gives
 E2(p,q)=4+24X h+(80X^2+16U)h^2
          +(64/3)X(7X^2+5U+1)h^3+O(h^4).
For example, use [n]_(exp h)=n+(2/3)n(n^2-1)h^2+O(h^4); the cubic term then
comes only from the exponential multipliers and their product with that h^2
term. After imposing Q2: 6X=4Y^2+3Y+1-V, the h^3 coefficient of
F-(-delta A^(3+12Y)) is exactly
 32[2U+10X^2-20XY+Y^2+Y-6X-2].
Hence Q3 is
 2U=-10X^2+20XY-Y^2-Y+6X+2.

Here is an independently checked complete integer reduction, not a finite search.
If X=0, primitive (p,q) gives U=1 and Q3 forces Y(Y+1)=0. If Y=0, V=1 and Q2
forces X=0. If both are nonzero, the determinant identity
 (ps+qr)^2=1+4XY
forces XY>0. Integer nonzero r,s imply V<=Y^2+1. When Y<0 and X<0, Q2 would
give 6X>=3Y(Y+1)>=0, a contradiction. Thus X,Y>0. Q2 now gives
 X>=Y(Y+1)/2.
For X>=2Y+1, the right side of Q3 is decreasing in X and at X=2Y+1 equals
 -Y^2-9Y-2<0, impossible because U>0. Therefore X<=2Y, so Y<=3.
The only remaining (Y,X) are (1,1),(1,2),(2,3),(2,4),(3,6).
The second and fourth give V<0. The first gives U=8 although product X=1
forces U=2. The third gives U=22 although product X=3 forces U=10.
The last gives XY=18, but the determinant identity would make (ps+qr)^2=73.
All fail. Consequently Jones1 requires pq=0 and rs=0 or -1. Checking the
full bracket on those surviving primitive markings is separate and was done
by the parent/braid exact Laurent verification. This argument is independent
of any numerical projection of the embedded knot.

9. DIRECT TOPOLOGICAL CERTIFICATE FOR ALL SURVIVING HEEGAARD MARKINGS
The exact rational coordinates in torus_embedding.json realize the actual Carter
ribbon surface, not just a surface with the same homology. Independently rebuilding
its 24 barycentric triangles with rational arithmetic gives all negative areas,
with distinct values -1/16,-13/240,-11/240,-1/40,-1/120, and total -1. Thus the
cellular map is orientation-consistent of degree -1; it is a homeomorphism of the
torus (no folds along edges, and a local branch point would force degree greater
than one). The prescribed crossing rotations agree with the PD. This supplies
an exact topological realization of the flat torus diagram.

Let v be its second flat coordinate, dual to lambda. Around the oriented knot,
the six successive (start, midpoint, end) v values are
 (0,-13/60,-11/30),
 (-11/30,-1/2,-19/30),
 (-19/30,-47/60,-1),
 (-1,-73/60,-41/30),
 (-41/30,-3/2,-49/30),
 (-49/30,-107/60,-2).
They are strictly decreasing on every straight segment. Resolve each crossing
by a small displacement in the normal collar coordinate, without changing v.
The resulting embedded curve remains strictly monotone in v and has v-degree -2.

For every marking allowed by the algebraic reduction, pq=0, so the primitive
curve mu is the meridian of one of the TWO complementary unknotted solid tori.
In that solid torus its angular longitude is either v or -v. Indeed any allowed
lambda shear changes only the meridional coordinate; if mu is the other axis,
switch to the other complementary solid torus. Push the collar curve slightly
into this solid torus, preserving its longitude coordinate. It is a closed
TWO-STRAND BRAID. Since B2 is generated by one crossing, it is T(2,k) for an odd
integer k. The determinant of T(2,k) is |k|. Jones1 therefore implies |k|=1,
which makes the knot an unknot by braid destabilization.

This proves that the specific three-crossing virtual candidate cannot give a
nontrivial classical Jones-one knot under ANY unknotted-Heegaard-torus marking
covered by the exact skein calculation. The eight orientation-preserving signed
markings are all unknots. Orientation-reversing Heegaard markings are covered by
ambient reflection and mirroring; mirroring preserves both Jones1 and unknotness.
One must mirror the crossing/framing conventions together, rather than blindly
apply the orientation-preserving F formula to a determinant-minus-one matrix.

The exact rational PL projections in exact_eight_markings.json independently
produce diagrams simplifying geometrically to zero crossings and one component.
They corroborate this argument, but no numerical projection assertion is needed
for the closed-two-braid proof. This is a decisive negative result for this ONE
virtual candidate and standard Heegaard embeddings, not a general exclusion of
virtual-to-classical constructions, knotted torus embeddings, or the Jones
unknot-detection problem.
