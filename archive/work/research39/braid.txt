PASS 39: WHY QUANTUM DETECTION OF BRAID DYNAMICS DOES NOT DETECT THE CLOSURE
Bounded global architecture audit. No claim of a generic Jones theorem.

Outcome. The asymptotic-faithfulness / AMU mechanism detects a braid WITH
its axis. It cannot suppress that dynamics from the unknot value of its
closure, even if the ENTIRE colored Jones sequence of the closure is
supplied. An actual unknot has a pseudo-Anosov three-strand presentation.
More strongly, a fixed-four-strand, fixed-writhe family of actual unknots
has unbounded braid entropy. The latter is a new exact observable of a
previously checked family, not a new word scan or new geometric candidate.

Duplicate check. Read the STOPPED_ROUTE files for passes35,37,38 and
the global report; searched all research notes for entropy, AMU,
asymptotic faithfulness, Nielsen, tensor-generator/group-like promotion.
Group-like/color promotion was already examined in pass28; geometric
intersection cancellation in pass24; fixed-color projector collision was
proved equivalent to residual faithfulness in pass30. None is presented
as a new route here. The family below appeared in proof11 as a countertest
to Markov moment rigidity. Here the point is the exact obstruction to
recovering Nielsen--Thurston dynamics from CLOSED knot data, including
all colors, rather than another claim about moment tomography.

1. The complete proposed chain, and its first false arrow.

The plausible global chain would be:

  V_K=1 -> quantum braid matrices cannot have dynamical expansion
        -> no pseudo-Anosov piece in the braid monodromy
        -> graph-manifold braid complement and graph-knot closure
        -> K=U, using the positive a_2 obstruction for graph knots.

The first arrow is false on actual unknots. Asymptotic faithfulness and
the AMU results do not fix it: they read the operator for the punctured
surface, not the knot obtained after the braid axis is filled in.
No claim about graph knots is needed to falsify this chain.

2. The smallest actual test already gives positive dynamics.

Let b=sigma_1 sigma_2^{-1} in B3. Its closure is U: negatively destabilize
the third strand, then positively destabilize sigma_1 in B2. Therefore
all zero-framed normalized colored Jones polynomials of this closure
equal 1, identically in the parameter.

Nevertheless its capped four-puncture sphere mapping class is
pseudo-Anosov. The usual homological map is

 sigma_1 -> [[1,1],[0,1]], sigma_2 -> [[1,0],[-1,1]],
 b -> [[2,1],[1,1]].

Its trace is 3 and its expanding eigenvalue is (3+sqrt(5))/2.
Andersen--Masbaum--Ueno, Theorem1.2, Corollary1.3, and Section3 give
the quantum/classical interpretation and the trace criterion:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0503414
The eigenvalue approximation in their theorem uses appropriate NONUNITARY
root choices. It must not be confused with expansion of a unitary matrix.

There is an especially transparent exact Jones-block calculation.
Put delta=-A^2-A^{-2}, and take

 e1=[[delta,1],[0,0]], e2=[[0,0],[1,delta]],
 rho(sigma_i)=A I+A^{-1}e_i.

These satisfy the TL3 relations. Direct multiplication gives

 rho(b)=[[1-A^{-4},-A^2],[A^2,-A^4]],
 det rho(b)=1, tr rho(b)=1-A^4-A^{-4}=3-delta^2.

For exponent sum zero the reduced bracket/Jones closure formula is

 V_(closure b)=tr rho(b)+(delta^2-2)=1.

At A=exp(pi i/4), delta=0, this matrix is conjugate by diag(1,i)
to [[2,1],[1,1]]. Thus the scalar unknot value is compatible with,
and in this normalized 3-braid block even requires, a hyperbolic trace3.
This is not a formal matrix unrelated to a knot.

3. No entropy bound from even the full colored closure sequence.

For every integer m, use the previously topology-certified family

 b_m=sigma_2^{2m}(sigma_1 sigma_2)sigma_2^{-2m}sigma_3^{-1} in B4.

Every closure is U by negative destabilization and conjugation, and
every exponent sum is 1. Hence all closed colored Jones polynomials
are independent of m and equal 1. This proof is independent of any
polynomial detection conjecture.

Use the unreduced Burau convention whose generator block is
[[1-t,t],[1,0]]. At t=-1 this is I+N, N=[[1,-1],[1,-1]], N^2=0,
so its kth power is I+kN for every signed integer k. Consequently
the complete specialized matrix is, identically in m,

 B_m=[ [2,       2m-2,        0, 1-2m],
       [2m+1,    4m^2-2m,     0, -4m^2],
       [2m,      (2m-1)^2,    0, -2m(2m-1)],
       [0,       0,          -1, 2] ].

Its characteristic polynomial is

 (lambda-1)^2*(lambda^2-T_m lambda+1),
 T_m=4m^2-2m+2.

The two nontrivial eigenvalues therefore give, for m!=0,

 h_top(b_m) >= log((T_m+sqrt(T_m^2-4))/2),

which tends to infinity as |m| tends to infinity. The inequality is the
Burau entropy estimate: Band--Boyland, Introduction and Theorem1.1,
https://msp.org/agt/2007/7-3/agt-v7-n3-p09-s.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0612716
Using the unreduced matrix adds an eigenvalue1 and does not change
its expanding spectral radius. No assertion that every b_m is globally
pseudo-Anosov is needed; positive-entropy pieces suffice.

The matrix word and its characteristic polynomial are checked as
identities over Z[m,lambda] in verify_braid_dynamics.py. No numerical
marking or braid search is used to prove the infinite-family statement.

4. Precise strategic implication.

Even a hypothetical perfect theorem extracting Nielsen--Thurston type
and stretch factors from quantum representations would leave the
Jones-unknot problem unchanged unless one separately recovers those
representations, or suitable axis-independent information, from the
closed knot polynomial. The actual example above shows that this
cannot mean suppressing or bounding dynamics of arbitrary braid
presentations, even at fixed strand number and writhe.

One could instead minimize an axis-dependent quantity over all braid
presentations. But no formula relating such a minimum to Jones was
found, and the existence of a zero-entropy presentation specifically
for Jones-one knots would be another unresolved geometric theorem,
not a consequence of AMU or asymptotic faithfulness. Merely declaring
that minimum to be the missing bridge does not make the problem easier.

Conclusion: stop this global AMU/entropy route in its present form.
The bounded search produced no new non-equivalent tractable bridge.
It established a precise actual-knot obstruction to the proposed input
to a dynamics theorem. This does not refute ordinary Jones detection
or its all-colored analogue, and does not assume the latter is known.
