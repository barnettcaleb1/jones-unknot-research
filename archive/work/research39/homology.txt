PASS 39 — BOUNDED GLOBAL CATEGORIFICATION CHECK
Date: 2026-11-01
Status: no new generic proof mechanism, no certified fixed-Jones unbounded-rank
family, and no certified equal-Jones directional ribbon concordance were found.
This is a report of a bounded targeted check, not an exhaustive literature claim.

1. WHAT WAS NOT REUSED
The living report and prior notes already contain the connected-sum H-rank /
H-spread endpoints, formal Lee and full-tangle countermodels, permutation and
supertrace obstructions, the Laplacian/heat-trace no-go, finite-jet amplification,
Watson uniform-rank theorem, and small-table necessary ribbon screens. None of
those is a new result of this pass. In particular Grlj–Lauda 2608.24298, surfaced
again by the searches, was already treated in research32 and was stopped on that
basis. No further diagram scan or formal-complex construction was performed.

2. A NEW PRIMARY GO/NO GATE FOR RIBBON-CABLING EXPERIMENTS
Hom–Park, Ribbon concordance and cabling, arXiv:2608.06625v1 (6 Aug 2026):
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06625v1
Theorem 1.2 states: if J is a cable of K, p>1, and J is ribbon concordant to
K_(p,q), then J is that very (p,q)-cable of K. The paragraph immediately after
the theorem explicitly includes J=K as the trivial cable. Therefore the
following tempting attempt to manufacture an equal-Jones directional ribbon
pair is invalid before a polynomial calculation: start with a ribbon/slice K,
then posit a ribbon concordance K -> K_(p,1), or between distinct (p,1)-cables
of the same K. Ordinary concordance of these slice knots is not enough.

This is a genuine geometric obstruction, proved using minimum heights of knot
Floer immersed curves. It does NOT prove that arbitrary equal-Jones ribbon
concordances are homology isomorphisms. The paper gives no such example or theorem.
Its Question 1.7 asks whether every nontrivial knot has a length-one F[U]-torsion
summand in HFK-minus. That question is not a Jones assertion, and even an
affirmative answer supplies extra homology rather than an upper bound from its
Euler characteristic. No Jones-to-rank inference was made from it.

3. LATEST COEFFICIENT-CHANGE RESULT DOES NOT CLOSE THE RANK GAP
Wells, L-space knots and sl_n-like knot Floer homology, arXiv:2610.38927v1
(28 Oct 2026): https://arxiv.org/html/2610.38927v1
Theorem 1.4 describes HFK_n by tensor and Tor change of coefficients from
HFK-minus for thin knots, and for L-space knots satisfying a stated Alexander-gap
condition. Corollary 1.6 gives an actual sharp failure when n is too small:
for T(3,4), HFK_2 has dimension 6, whereas the proposed unrestricted
coefficient-change expression has dimension 8. Thus it is unsafe to promote
ordinary sl_2 information to all coefficient truncations by a universal base-
change rule. The cited reduced-Kh-to-HFK spectral sequence only gives a lower
bound on Kh rank from HFK rank. No upper bound by the whole Jones polynomial
is proved, and the Euler cancellation problem remains untouched.

This is a concrete stop test for a particular promotion mechanism; it is not a
counterexample to Jones detection or to H-rank. The n=2 failure above is already
an ordinary explicit torus knot, so no hypothetical geometric realization is
being used.

4. TWO SEARCH PITFALLS CHECKED AGAINST PRIMARY SOURCES
(a) Kanenobu's 1986 theorem provides infinitely many knots with identical HOMFLY
(and hence Jones and Alexander polynomials) but different Alexander MODULES.
A secondary search excerpt incorrectly suggested different Alexander polynomials.
The primary statement is on p159:
https://www.ams.org/journals/proc/1986-097-01/S0002-9939-1986-0831406-7/S0002-9939-1986-0831406-7.pdf
It therefore cannot be used to infer unbounded Alexander coefficient norm,
unbounded HFK rank, or a counterexample to H-rank. The known family has bounded
homology as already analyzed in the report.

(b) Diaz–Manchon, New torsion patterns in Khovanov homology, arXiv:2508.00606v1:
https://arxiv.org/html/2508.00606v1
Theorems 8 and 12 concern explicit ladders with additional periphery/even-module
hypotheses and independence of certain resulting 2-torsion classes. They do not
say that arbitrarily long twist boxes automatically supply independent torsion,
nor do they link torsion multiplicity to the ordinary Jones polynomial. Thus
these results do not contradict the fixed-Jones Watson bounds or supply a
Jones-one rank-one criterion.

5. PRECISE OUTCOME AND STOP CONDITION
No actual pair J -> K with a certified directional ribbon movie, equal full
Jones polynomials, and strictly larger rational reduced Kh at K was found in
the sources checked. Mutants, slice concordance, and being independently ribbon
do not provide that movie. No claim of nonexistence or complete status is made.
Likewise no actual fixed-whole-Jones family with a proved unbounded reduced
rational Kh rank was identified. Infinite cardinality of a fiber, growing braid
index, changing Alexander modules, and varying polynomials with the same few
coefficients are not substitutes.

The pass supplies two useful rejection tests (ribbon cabling rigidity and the
explicit failure of unrestricted coefficient change), not a new full proof
scaffold. There is presently no justified reason to continue the generic
categorification branch by introducing another rank budget, purity assertion,
or diagram-selection hypothesis. A meaningful restart needs either an actual
geometric operation preserving the WHOLE Jones polynomial together with a
proved homology growth formula, or a geometric theorem controlling cancellation
that is not merely a restatement of Jones-one unknot detection.
