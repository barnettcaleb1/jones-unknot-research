PASS 40: ENDPOINT PERMUTATION OBSTRUCTS A DIRECT POSITIVE-BRAID PROOF
Bounded mechanism check; no new generic Jones theorem or counterexample.

Decision. Most proposed real-parameter positivity mechanisms duplicate
work/fanout16/physics.txt, research20/quantum_scaffold.txt, and
research22/markov_global_constraints.txt. Stop those. One short additional
structural observation is useful: a multi-strand KNOT braid cannot have
a positive-semidefinite full Jones operator at real parameters near 1,
because its endpoint permutation survives there. Taking a Gram operator
repairs positivity but changes the endpoint permutation to the identity.

1. Exact real model and its limit.

Use the already audited faithful positive TL tensor model, A=i r,
r>0, d=r^2+r^{-2}, q=r^{-4}. On two tensor factors put

 e(r)=[[0,0,0,0],
       [0,r^2,-1,0],
       [0,-1,r^{-2},0],
       [0,0,0,0]],
 B_i(r)=r I-r^{-1}e_i(r).

The B_i satisfy braid relations, are real symmetric and invertible,
and represent the raw Jones generators with the scalar i removed.
For an n-braid beta of exponent sum w, write B_beta for the word product.
The usual knot normalization multiplies by the positive scalar r^{-3w};
the closure then has the additional positive trace factor d^{n-1}.

At r=1, e is twice the projection onto the antisymmetric two-tensor
line. Thus B_i(1) is EXACTLY the tensor-factor swap P_i. Consequently

  B_beta(1)=P_(pi(beta)) on (R^2)^{tensor n}.               (1)

This is an operator identity, not an asymptotic heuristic.

2. Proposition: the actual knot operator cannot be positive near 1.

If the ordinary closure of beta is a knot and n>1, pi(beta) is an n-cycle.
On the invariant n-dimensional subspace with exactly one tensor factor
equal to 1, P_(pi(beta)) acts by an n-cycle permutation matrix. Its
eigenvalues include all nth roots of unity. In particular it has an
eigenvalue that is not a nonnegative real number.

It follows from continuity of eigenvalues that B_beta(r) cannot be
positive semidefinite for all r sufficiently close to 1. More precisely,
there is a neighborhood of 1 in which it is NEVER positive semidefinite:
for n=2 the negative eigenvalue persists; for n>=3 a nonreal eigenvalue
persists. This applies in any positive-definite inner product, since
such a positive self-adjoint operator has real nonnegative spectrum.

No continuous nonzero scalar gauge fixes the obstruction. Indeed the
all-zero tensor is an eigenvector of B_beta(r) with eigenvalue r^w>0.
If c(r) B_beta(r) is positive semidefinite, this forces c(r)>0. The bad
spectral channel therefore remains bad. The ordinary writhe scalar
already has this positive sign. Changing basis also does not change
the spectrum.

Thus a proposed proof using a direct positive normalized braid operator
for EVERY knot presentation fails before any trace inequality is used.
The actual unknot beta=sigma_1 in B2 is the smallest test: its eigenvalues
are r and -r^{-3}, despite its closure Jones polynomial being exactly 1.

This is not the earlier Perron--Frobenius/cone obstruction: positivity as
a self-adjoint operator is different from preserving a positive cone.
Here the obstruction is the endpoint permutation of every multi-strand
knot braid, with no assumption about inverse generators individually.

3. Why the natural Gram repair changes the geometric problem.

Because each B_i and B_i^{-1} is symmetric, transpose reverses the order
of letters without changing their signs. If rev(beta) denotes that word
reversal, then

  B_beta(r)^T B_beta(r)=B_(rev(beta) beta)(r).               (2)

The matrix on the left is positive definite. But at the level of endpoint
permutations, reversing a word of transpositions gives its inverse:

  pi(rev(beta) beta)=pi(beta)^{-1}pi(beta)=identity.

Therefore the geometric braid on the right is PURE, and its ordinary
closure has n components. Those components need not be unknotted or
unlinked. Its closure polynomial is not supplied by V_(closure beta).

To reconnect these endpoints into one component, multiply by an actual
braid gamma with an n-cycle permutation. The resulting operator at r=1
again acts by that n-cycle, so the same positivity obstruction returns.
This rules out the particular repair of taking a braid Gram operator and
then restoring an ordinary one-component braid closure with a fixed
permutation anchor. It does not rule out different boundary states or
nonlocal constructions, which require independent skein identities.

4. Scope and stop condition.

Products of positive operators may be useful in special constructions.
But the full ordinary Jones operator of a multi-strand knot is not such
a positive operator near the classical real parameter, and the canonical
Gram replacement computes additional pure-link data. Applying equality
conditions from trace/determinant inequalities without first identifying
that changed geometric closure would use information absent from Jones1.

No genuinely new global inequality with a justified equality case emerged.
Do not extend this pass into another scan or reprove the already established
cone, stabilization, or Brunnian-vector obstructions. A future viable input
would need an actual topological identity connecting a NEW positive
quantity to ordinary Jones, with the permutation/closure issue explicitly
resolved; merely assuming such an identity repackages the missing step.
