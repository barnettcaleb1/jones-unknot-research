PASS 40: A CANONICAL RIBBON FAMILY EXCLUDES REAL-PARAMETER POSITIVITY
Status: exact geometric obstruction, not a Jones detector or counterexample.

Duplicate check. Read creative6/statmech_findings.txt (signed tree measures,
failed determinant factorization and strong Rayleigh property),
fanout16/physics.txt (crossing/inverse cone obstruction), research29's
quantum-trace closure examples, research22--23's ribbon/fusion audits,
and research32's Thompson normalization audit. The torus polynomials
below are standard; related three-term polynomials appeared in research23.
The new application here is the uniform obstruction on EVERY nonzero
real parameter, even for canonical ribbon doubles of determinant one.

1. One explicit geometric family, covering both signs of the parameter.

Let T_n=T(3,6n-1), n>=1, and R_n=T_n#mirror(T_n). These are canonical
reflection doubles and ribbon knots. Their exact Jones polynomials are

  V_(T_n)(t)=t^(6n-2)(1+t^2-t^(6n)),
  V_(R_n)(t)=(1+t^2-t^(6n))(1+t^-2-t^(-6n)).                 (1)

All exponents are even. In particular R_n has determinant one, because
V_(R_n)(-1)=1. Also V_(R_n)(1)=1, its signature is zero, and the canonical
mirror-pair diagram has writhe zero. It is nontrivial: the displayed
Jones polynomial is nonconstant. No conjectural realization is involved.

Fix ANY real t with |t|>1. The first factor in (1) is negative for all
sufficiently large n, while the second is positive for every n, since
1+t^-2-t^(-6n)>0. Thus

  V_(R_n)(t) -> -infinity.

Replacing t by its reciprocal proves the same statement for 0<|t|<1.
Taking R_n#R_n gives positive values tending to plus infinity. At the two
remaining nonzero real parameters t=+/-1, all these nontrivial ribbon
knots have value one. Therefore there is no fixed nonzero real Jones
parameter on which the ordinary scalar is a universal nonnegative norm
with an unknot equality case, even restricted to these determinant-one
ribbon doubles. At t=0 a Laurent evaluation is not universally defined.

2. Independent exact TL3 derivation of (1).

Set delta=-A^2-A^-2, t=A^-4. In the two-dimensional TL3 block take

  e1=[[delta,1],[0,0]], e2=[[0,0],[1,delta]],
  rho(sigma_i)=A I+A^-1 e_i.

Then

  C=rho(sigma1 sigma2)=[[0,-A^-4],[1,-A^-2]],
  C^3=A^-6 I.

Since tr C=-A^-2 and tr C^2=-A^-4, for q not divisible by3,
tr C^q=-A^(-2q). The reduced closure functional on TL3 is

  cl(B)=tr_2(B)+(delta^2-2) rho_1(B),

where rho_1(sigma_i)=A. Its weights can be checked on I,e1,e1e2:
respectively delta^2, delta,1, the actual planar closures. Symmetry gives
e2 and cyclicity gives e2e1; these five elements form a TL3 basis.
Therefore the reduced bracket of closure((sigma1 sigma2)^q) is

  -A^(-2q)+(A^4+A^-4) A^(2q).

Its writhe is2q, so multiplying by (-A^3)^(-2q) gives

  V_(T(3,q))(t)=t^(q-1)+t^(q+1)-t^(2q)

for positive q coprime to3. Put q=6n-1 to obtain (1). The formula agrees
with the standard torus-knot formula

  V_(T(p,q))(t)=t^((p-1)(q-1)/2)
                 (1-t^(p+1)-t^(q+1)+t^(p+q))/(1-t^2).

Primary source for this formula: Wu--Wang, Zeroes of the Jones polynomial,
Physica A296 (2001), 483--494, Section4, equation7:
https://arxiv.org/html/cond-mat/0105013 .
Mirror inversion and the chosen trefoil normalization are also stated in
Vaughan Jones's survey, pp1--4:
https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/sites/doug-ravenel/otherpapers/Jones-poly.pdf .
The standard-library verifier checks the TL3 relations, closure weights,
C^3 identity, and exact closed-polynomial calculations at n=1,2,3.

3. What is excluded, and what is not.

This is a geometric, decomposition-independent rejection of formulas
that express the NORMALIZED ORDINARY JONES SCALAR as a sum or integral
of nonnegative real weights times a positive normalization. No change of
state basis or canonical decomposition can change its negative value.
A common sign correction from component number, zero framing, or
signature cannot help: the unknot shares those data and has value+1.
No claim is made about an arbitrary knot-dependent sign correction or
a different observable; connecting either to the full Jones polynomial
would require a separate theorem.

Reflection sends t to t^-1. On the unit circle this is complex
conjugation, so a reflection double has absolute-square Jones value.
For real t other than+/-1, inversion is not complex conjugation. Calling
reflection a positive adjoint there assumes a structure contradicted by
(1). Actual unitary TQFT positivity is not contradicted.

The old positive-real two-strand test is subsumed by (1): its square-knot
example gave V(2)=-27/8. The unified three-strand family additionally
works on the negative axis and already has determinant one. This remains
a no-go result, not progress on an ordinary-Jones equality detector.
No new conditional scaffold is proposed. Stop this positivity route
unless a different concrete geometric observable and a proved link to
the ENTIRE polynomial are supplied.
