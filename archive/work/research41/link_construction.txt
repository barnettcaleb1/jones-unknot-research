Primary sources actually located:

1. Thistlethwaite, Links with trivial Jones polynomial:
https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/tj.pdf
The stated examples are two- and three-component Hopf satellites, the
same source of the already studied constructions. The PDF's text layer
is garbled, so this pass does not newly claim to reconstruct its figures.

2. Eliahou--Kauffman--Thistlethwaite, Infinite families of links with
trivial Jones polynomial, Topology42(2003),155--169:
https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/ekt.pdf
The authors' explanatory page is
https://web.math.utk.edu/~mthistle/ekt.html .
The general theorem adds components while multiplying the polynomial
by the two-component-unlink factor. It does not supply an inverse
geometric operation dividing that factor while producing a knot. Its
split-link connected-sum observation starts with an actual split link;
it cannot be applied to the prime Jones-trivial examples merely because
their polynomials equal those of split unlinks.

3. Kauffman, Detecting Links With The Jones Polynomial:
https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/MLinks.pdf
This is the scalar-tangle construction already audited in research30.
The closed-component obstruction is retained; it is not a new seed.

4. The distinct constructive hit is Eliahou--Fromentin,
A remarkable 20-crossing tangle, JKTR26(2017),1750091:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05560 .
It produces actual prime knots K_r with V(K_r) congruent to1 modulo2^r,
using a tangle bracket pair congruent to the trivial pair modulo2.
Those are congruences for different knots as r grows. This is not an
exact Jones-unlink input, not one knot satisfying all moduli, and not an
operation known to preserve an exact unit polynomial. No limiting knot
or cancellation of the remaining integral defect is provided by the
abstracted construction. It therefore does not answer this pass's task.

Additional search hits for nontrivial knots with trivial Q-polynomial

Virtual Jones-one examples were also excluded from this search: the
previous pass already completed the chosen genus-one Heegaard test.
