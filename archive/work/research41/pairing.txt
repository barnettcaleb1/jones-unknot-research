EKT OPPOSITE-OMEGA: TWO PAIRINGS AND THE BOUNDARY-BRAID LIMIT
2026-11-01. Exact algebra plus a restricted geometric obstruction; no Jones-one
nontrivial knot is constructed.

1. Exact invariant-pairing space
Write Omega=[[a,b],[c,e]], where
a=-A^-1+A^3-A^7; b=A^-3;
c=-A^-11+2A^-7-2A^-3+2A-A^5;
e=A^-13-A^-9+A^-5.
The Hopf outside pairing is H=[[h,delta^2],[delta^2,delta]],
h=-A^-14-A^-6-2A^-2-2A^2-A^6-A^14.
The ordinary numerator-of-sum pairing is Q=[[delta,1],[1,delta]].
EXACTLY, Omega^t H=H Omega AND Omega^t Q=Q Omega.
Consequently both are preserved by (T,U)->(T^omega,U^(omega^-1)).
These are bracket statements. Jones normalization must separately account for
the actual orientations/writhes; EKT itself uses even iterations in general.

Over Q(A), every universally preserved bilinear form is in span{H,Q}.
Indeed Q is invertible generically; G is invariant iff Q^-1 G commutes with
the nonscalar 2x2 matrix Omega. Its centralizer is span{I,Omega}. The two
displayed invariant forms are independent, hence span the answer. In
particular every invariant form is symmetric. Over Z[A^+-1], write its
entries G=[[u,v],[v,w]]; the exact sole equation is
    b u+(e-a)v-c w=0.
Because b=A^-3 is a unit, u=A^3((a-e)v+c w), with arbitrary Laurent v,w.
Thus two preserved pairings do NOT force Omega to be scalar or identity.
This elementary loophole matters: one must classify realizable outside
tangles, rather than infer uniqueness from a two-dimensional vector space.

2. Which endpoint patterns survive an immediate topology check?
At A=1, Omega=[[-1,1],[0,1]], so G00=-2G01 and G01=G10.
For an actual fixed outside diagram the four bracket evaluations at A=1
are a common sign times (-2)^(mu_ij-1). Hence necessarily
    mu00=mu01+1,  mu01=mu10.
Enumerating ALL 105 perfect matchings of the eight ports (not braid words)
gives precisely these tables (00,01,10,11), and counts:
    (4,3,3,2):1; (3,2,2,1):8; (2,1,1,2):4; (2,1,1,1):4.
There may be additional closed outside components, adding a common integer
to all counts; these cannot occur for a knot-valued anchor. The tables do
not certify the exact invariant equation, nor diagram realizability with
any particular bracket. The third table includes Q; the first includes H.
The other two are NOT ruled out by connectivity. In particular a source
link pairing and a knot pairing can be compatible at this necessary level.
A single crossing with resolutions exactly H and Q is impossible: at the
(0,infinity) input their component counts differ by two, whereas the two
resolutions of one crossing differ by at most one component.

3. A complete restriction for boundary-braid outside wirings
Suppose the outside is just the ordinary gluing Q with a four-punctured
sphere mapping cylinder inserted, in the subgroup fixing one puncture.
Such an outside has G=Q rho(g), with g represented by a three-strand braid.
Universal Omega-invariance forces [rho(g),Omega]=0.

Specialize A=zeta=exp(pi*i/4). The elementary horizontal twist M_+,
normalized by zeta, and vertical twist M_*, normalized by zeta^-1,
become, after the diagonal gauge diag(1,-i),
    L=[[1,0],[1,1]], U=[[1,1],[0,1]].
The Artin generators are L and U^-1. This is the standard modular
B3 -> PSL2(Z) map, whose kernel is its center, directly from the
presentation B3/<(sigma1 sigma2)^3>=PSL2(Z).
Omega=M_+^2 M_* M_+^2 therefore specializes projectively to
    M=L^2 U L^2=[[3,1],[8,3]].
An integral matrix commuting with M is [[a,b],[8b,a]] with a^2-8b^2=1.
The Pell solutions show its projective centralizer is generated by M.
Equivalently M is primitive hyperbolic: a proper power of an integral
hyperbolic matrix has absolute trace at least 7, whereas tr(M)=6.
Therefore g is omega^k modulo the braid center. This conclusion uses the
topological modular quotient, NOT presumed faithfulness of a generic
Jones representation. Central powers only give the familiar framing
scalar in the two-dimensional skein action.

The remaining opposite omega moves now cancel as actual boundary
homeomorphisms across the gluing cylinder: omega^n on one input can be
transferred to the other, where it commutes with omega^k and cancels
omega^-n. Thus the closed link is unchanged up to isotopy (with framing
bookkeeping if framed tangles are retained). If its anchor was an actual
unknot, the entire opposite-omega orbit remains the actual unknot.
This covers boundary-braid wirings only. It does NOT cover general
essential outside tangles, nor show that every realizable form in
span{H,Q} is a boundary braid or the EKT Hopf pattern.

4. Verdict / exact remaining test
The simplest knot-producing invariant Q is harmless by actual boundary
cancellation; adding an arbitrary boundary braid gives no escape. The
unexcluded possibility is an essential outside tangle whose four closure
brackets satisfy the displayed exact linear equation and whose (0,infinity)
closure is an actual unknot. Endpoint connectivity alone leaves two extra
classes. No such essential tangle or nontrivial unit-Jones output has been
produced here. This is a constrained realizability question, not a proof
of existence and not a solved kernel problem.

Duplicate check: the known EKT double-cover homology obstruction already
rules out single-band fusion of the standard nonzero EKT link orbits to
determinant-one knots. The multi-band families checked in research34/37
are also already excluded. This note does not reopen either search.

5. Independent audit of root's component-removal obstruction
Although H/delta has Laurent-polynomial entries, it cannot be the pairing
of an actual outside tangle. Explicitly it is [[g,delta],[delta,1]], with
g=A^12-A^8+2A^4+2A^-4-A^-8+A^-12. At zeta=exp(pi*i/4), g=-8.
If realized, its 11 closure would be a knot of determinant1, so the
branched double cover Y would be an integral homology sphere. The two
disjoint local infinity-to-zero replacements lift to integral surgeries
on a two-component framed link in Y: the two filling slopes have distance
one in each local branched solid torus. Each single replacement has
determinant0, because its bracket is delta, so both diagonal entries of
the integral symmetric surgery matrix vanish. The simultaneous surgery
must therefore have homological determinant b^2 for an integer b. But
its bracket g gives determinant8, which is not an integer square.
This contradiction uses neither unknot detection nor an assumption that
Y is S^3. The Jones/bracket writhe factors at zeta have absolute value1,
so framing and orientation conventions do not affect it. It rules out
this particular formal invariant pairing, not every element of span{H,Q}.
