INDEPENDENT REALIZABILITY AUDIT: THE FORMAL EKT PAIRING H/delta
2026-11-01. The proposed obstruction is valid.

Statement
---------
Let an actual outside tangle P have two disjoint four-ended filling slots.
In each slot use the crossing-free rational fillings 0 and infinity.
Use the reduced Kauffman bracket, with one circle evaluating to1 and
delta=-A^2-A^(-2). No such P can have its four closure brackets equal to

                         [[g,delta],[delta,1]],

where

              g=A^12-A^8+2A^4+2A^(-4)-A^(-8)+A^(-12).

Multiplying ALL entries by a common framing monomial does not repair it.
In fact the obstruction only needs the four absolute values at a primitive
eighth root to be 8,0,0,1 in the displayed order.

Proof
-----
Put zeta=exp(pi i/4). Then delta(zeta)=0 and g(zeta)=-8. For an actual
nonempty link closure L, the absolute value of its reduced bracket at
zeta equals its determinant: the Jones writhe correction is a unit of
modulus1. The determinant is the order of H1(Sigma_2 L;Z) when finite,
and is zero when that homology is infinite.

Let Y_ij be the branched double cover of closure P_ij. The entry1 gives
det(P_11)=1, so Y_11 is an integral homology sphere. It is NOT necessary
to show P_11 is the unknot. In particular this argument assumes no form
of Jones unknot detection. If component counts are wanted, the values
at A=1 have absolute values4,2,2,1 respectively, so the ordinary
bracket specialization forces component counts3,2,2,1.

Each local infinity-to-zero replacement is a band move. Its lift changes
the meridional filling of a surgery knot to a slope at distance1 from
the meridian. In an integral homology sphere that is an integral surgery
coefficient. Because the two balls are disjoint, both changes lift to
simultaneous integral surgery on a two-component framed link in Y_11.
Write its symmetric integral linking matrix as

                              [[a,b],[b,c]].

Surgery on its first component alone gives Y_01 (or Y_10, depending on
the indexing convention); surgery on the second alone gives the other
single-changed cover. Their determinants are both0, so the one-by-one
surgery matrices must both be zero: a=c=0. This is independent of which
slot receives which index.

The simultaneous filling Y_00 consequently has presentation matrix

                              [[0,b],[b,0]].

If b=0 its first homology is infinite, contradicting det(P_00)=8. If
b is nonzero, its first homology has order |det|=b^2. No integer b has
b^2=8. This is the required contradiction.

Normalization and geometry caveats checked
------------------------------------------
1. Both slot replacements are exactly 0<->infinity, hence distance1.
   A crossing change (distance2) would be a different statement; none
   is substituted for a band move here.
2. The fixed outside must be one actual tangle with TWO DISJOINT slots.
   Four unrelated links with the displayed brackets need not share a
   surgery link, so their existence would not contradict this proof.
3. Writhe, component-orientation and framing monomials have absolute
   value1 at zeta. They cannot change zero determinants or change8 to
   a square. The proof does not assume a common transported orientation
   of all four closures.
4. A formal division of a skein pairing by delta can exist algebraically
   without being represented by an actual outside tangle. This argument
   distinguishes precisely those notions in the proposed example.
5. No conclusion is asserted here for every invariant matrix in the
   two-dimensional algebraic pairing space, nor for arbitrary combinations
   [[-8c,b],[b,c]] at zeta. The hypotheses giving an integral homology
   sphere and two zero-framed single surgeries were used explicitly.

General reusable form
---------------------
Whenever two disjoint band moves start from a determinant-one knot and
each single move gives a determinant-zero link, the link after BOTH moves
has determinant either zero or a perfect square. This follows from the
same matrix and does not require the starting knot to be the unknot.

Primary input
-------------
The band-move/distance-one surgery correspondence is the Montesinos trick;
a precise primary account is Moore–Vazquez, A note on band surgery and
the signature of a knot, Section4.1:
https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10182784

The integral surgery presentation and linking-matrix conventions agree
with Cochran–Melvin, The Milnor degree of a three-manifold, Section1:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0902.1731

The rest is the elementary two-by-two calculation above. The geometric
nonrealizability conclusion does not rely on the earlier finite band
searches, the auxiliary five-component input, or a generic Jones theorem.
