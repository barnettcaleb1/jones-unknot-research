1. General necessary gate, with exact surgery scope
--------------------------------------------------
Let Y=Sigma_2(L), with H1(Y;Z)=Z^b plus T. Suppose k embedded band moves
take L to a knot K of determinant one. Reverse the band cobordism. The
Montesinos correspondence lifts each saddle to an integral 2-handle, so
Y is obtained by k-component integral surgery in the integral homology
sphere Sigma_2(K). Let A be the k-by-k symmetric integral linking matrix.

Then coker(A)=H1(Y), and nullity(A)=b. The integral radical of A is a
primitive sublattice: Z^k/ker(A) embeds in the free group Z^k. Extending
a basis of the radical to an integral basis makes A congruent to

                         0_b direct-sum B,

where B is a nonsingular (k-b)-by-(k-b) integral symmetric matrix. The
torsion linking form of Y is presented by B^(-1) modulo Z, up to the
single overall orientation convention. Thus

  k >= b + m(lambda_Y) >= b + max_p dim_Fp(T tensor Fp),             (1)

where m(lambda) is the smallest size of a nonsingular integral symmetric
matrix presenting the linking form lambda. In particular:

* k=b: T must be zero.
* k=b+1: T must be cyclic. If |T|=N and a generator has self-linking a/N,
  then a must be plus or minus a unit square modulo N. Conversely that
  arithmetic condition gives a size-one presentation [N] or [-N].

These statements concern the full group, not just rational Betti numbers.
No assumption about orientations of the bands is needed for this necessary
integral surgery statement. No claim of an even matrix is being added.

2. The converse at the algebraic surgery level is an established theorem
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cochran–Melvin, Corollary 1.2, states that a symmetric integral matrix with
the correct nullity and linking form for a closed oriented 3-manifold Y
is realized as a surgery matrix for Y in some integral homology sphere.
Their Section 1 also gives the radical splitting and inverse-matrix rule.

Consequently the minimum number of surgery components, when the starting
integral homology sphere is allowed to vary, is exactly b+m(lambda_Y).
This is our deduction from their realization result and the preceding
lower bound. It is NOT a band-realization theorem: the source sphere need
not be a double branched cover with the required quotient, the surgery
link need not be equivariant, and the branch knot need not have Jones1.

In particular, for the actual LLL(1) cover, the matrix

                              diag(0,-144)

has precisely the required data (reverse the global sign if needed).
The realization theorem supplies a two-component surgery description in
some integral homology sphere. Thus a stronger obstruction based solely
on these same homological data cannot exclude that cover. Independently,
the stored actual two-band fusions to the unknot show directly that an
input-only theorem forbidding EVERY two-band determinant-one fusion of
LLL(1) would be false.

If a different three-component Jones-unlink input really had H1=Z^2,
its two-band algebraic gate would also pass: the matrix is simply 0_2.
The same realization result then applies. Torsion-free maximal nullity
is not a sufficient certificate of actual bands or a Jones-one output.

3. Concrete exclusion of padded and combined EKT inputs
-------------------------------------------------------
For a mu-component link, minimal fusion uses k=mu-1 bands. Call the
homology maximal-nullity here if b1(Sigma_2 L)=mu-1. If it also has any
nonzero torsion, equation (1) excludes EVERY such minimal fusion to any
determinant-one knot, regardless of band complexity.

For a split union L=L_1 split-union ... split-union L_r,

  Sigma_2(L) = (#_i Sigma_2(L_i)) # (r-1)(S^1 x S^2),
  b(L)=sum_i b(L_i)+(r-1),   T(L)=direct-sum_i T(L_i).

Thus if each input is maximal-nullity, their split union is again
maximal-nullity. Nonzero torsion cannot disappear. The same conclusion
holds under component-connected sum: component counts subtract one,
branched covers form ordinary connected sums, and nullities add.

Apply this to the already established EKT LL2(2m), m>=1: each has two
components, unlink Jones polynomial, and

  H1=Z plus Z/(1+12 q_(2m)^2),

with the displayed finite factor nonzero. Any split union or iterated
component-connected sum made from such inputs and unknots remains an
actual unlink-Jones link, yet no minimal fusion can yield a determinant-
one knot. For example LL2(2) has torsion order433; its split union with
one unknot is a THREE-component unlink-Jones link with H1=Z^2 plus Z/433.
Every pair of fusion bands on it is excluded, unlike LLL(1).

The cover formula follows by lifting a splitting sphere: its lift is two
spheres, producing the extra S^1 x S^2. A component-connected-sum sphere
meets the branch link twice and lifts to one separating sphere. Jones
multiplicativity supplies the stated unlink-Jones identities.

4. Why a metabolizer cannot be imposed on an arbitrary band trace
----------------------------------------------------------------
A band trace is not a rational homology ball. There is no general reason
its boundary linking form must be metabolic. A concrete countertest is
the Hopf link: cutting its standard annular Seifert surface along a
spanning arc gives a disk, so an inverse band fuses its boundary to the
unknot. Its double cover is L(2,1), with linking form (1/2) on Z/2. That
form has no metabolizer, since a metabolizer would have order sqrt(2).
The surgery trace has matrix [2], exactly as the general gate allows.

The Hopf link is not Jones-unlink; this example only blocks importing an
unjustified rational-ball condition from band topology alone. To obtain
a further obstruction one must use an additional proven property of the
specific Jones-unlink input and its geometric bands. None was obtained
in this bounded check.

---------------------------
For a proposed input, compute b, the full torsion group, and its linking
