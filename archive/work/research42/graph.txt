SIGNED PLANAR GRAPH ELIMINATION: THE MISSING BOUNDARY CHANNEL
2026-11-01. Bounded mechanism test. No generic detection theorem is claimed.

Verdict
The electrical-network analogy has an exact obstruction at its first
nontrivial elimination step. Decimating a degree-three vertex of a Jones
partition function generally creates a three-terminal interaction which
cannot be represented by any triangle of pair interactions. Among the eight
possible crossing-sign triples, precisely the six mixed triples avoid the
obstruction; these give the established Reidemeister III identities.
Moreover, an actual hard unknot falsifies the proposed monotone reduction
premise. This closes the specific local reduction mechanism, not every
possible signed-graph proof of unknot detection.

Duplicate check
The existing work already excludes raw spanning-tree-count bounds,
edge-factorization/strong-Rayleigh shortcuts, ferromagnetic positivity on
the Jones specialization, and universal positive braid operators. Those
arguments are not being repeated. Here the object checked is the FULL
three-terminal connectivity vector, not its one scalar closed evaluation.

1. Conventions and the global scalar
Let x=A^4, delta=-A^2-A^-2, and use the planar random-cluster sum
  Z_G(Q,{v_e})=sum_S Q^{k(S)} product_{e in S} v_e.
For Tait edge signs epsilon_e, the bracket unknot=1 is
  <D>=A^(-sum epsilon_e) delta^(-|V(G)|-1)
       Z_G(delta^2,{delta A^(2 epsilon_e)}).
Thus on the Jones curve
  Q=x+2+x^-1, u=-1-x, v=-1-x^-1,
  uv=Q, u+v=-Q.
An edge of either sign carries u or v. All calculations below are over
Q(x), with identities cleared of denominators when necessary.

2. Exact three-terminal elimination lemma
Fix three boundary vertices 1,2,3. Record separately the five noncrossing
boundary partitions 1|2|3, 12|3, 23|1, 13|2, 123. A central star with arm
weights a,b,c has connectivity coefficients
  (lambda, ab, bc, ac, abc),  lambda=Q+a+b+c.
The Q in lambda arises when the central vertex is an internal isolated
component; the three singleton arms give a+b+c.

A triangle with edge weights r12,r23,r13 has vector
  (1, r12, r23, r13,
   r12*r23+r12*r13+r23*r13+r12*r23*r13).
For lambda nonzero, proportional equality forces the multiplier lambda
and the three pair weights
  r12=ab/lambda, r23=bc/lambda, r13=ac/lambda.
The final coordinate agrees if and only if
  abc = Q(Q+a+b+c).                                      (*)
Equivalently the omitted 123 coefficient after matching the first four is
  kappa=(abc/lambda^2)*(Q*lambda-abc).
This is an exact local lemma, not a numerical response approximation.
For generic Q all five planar connectivity vectors are independent;
under the standard planar graph-to-bracket conversion they give the five
TL_3 basis matchings with invertible diagonal rescalings. Hence equality
of every surrounding closure requires this final coefficient too.

3. Complete crossing-sign classification of this move
If (a,b,c)=(u,u,v), then lambda=u and the triangle weights are (u,v,v).
The other five mixed triples follow by permutation and x->x^-1. Thus (*)
holds for all six mixed triples and the output weights again belong to
{u,v}. The graph-to-bracket prefactors agree: u=delta*A^2 compensates for
the removed vertex and the change in edge-sign sum. This is precisely
the usual graphical Reidemeister III transformation.

For all three arms u, the cleared obstruction is
  abc-Q*lambda = -(x+1)^3*(x-1)^2/x^2,
and for all three arms v it is
  abc-Q*lambda = -(x+1)^3*(x-1)^2/x^3.
Neither is zero as a rational function. Therefore not even arbitrary
rational pair weights on a triangle replace these stars universally.
Special zeros x=1 or -1 do not supply an all-polynomial identity; at
x=-1 the eliminated coefficients themselves degenerate.

This explains the precise failure of unrestricted electrical decimation:
its scalar/linear response formalism retains pair interactions, whereas
the Jones partition function generates the extra 123 interaction. Keeping
that interaction gives a correct state-sum computation, but the effective
object is no longer an ordinary signed Tait graph of individual crossings.
Discarding it changes some actual closures. This does NOT prove that a
particular closed graph cannot have further cancellations of such terms.

4. No universal positivity of the discarded channel after knot closure
Take the noncrossing matching associated to the 123 state and close it to
one circle. Tie a local trefoil into one exterior arc. This preserves the
number of components, and normalized bracket/Jones evaluation of each
local term is multiplied by the trefoil Jones polynomial. In conventions
V_trefoil(t)=t+t^3-t^4, one has V_trefoil(2)=-6. Consequently a nonzero
discarded-channel evaluation and its locally knotted version have opposite
signs at t=2. This is an actual one-component closure test, and the local
writhe correction is already included by using normalized Jones values.

Thus all-exterior sign-definiteness of the residuals is false. This does
not refute a proposed sign rule restricted to exteriors that are separately
proved to arise in a Jones-one host; such a restriction would require new
geometric input and is not supplied by the local calculation.

5. A precise diagram-reduction premise is already false
A tempting complete argument would be: every signed planar diagram with
monomial reduced bracket has a sequence of crossing-nonincreasing graph
Reidemeister moves ending at the empty diagram; therefore it is an unknot.
The premise fails even on actual unknots. Kauffman–Lambropoulou's Culprit,
Section 1, Figure 2, is a ten-crossing unknot with no simplifying type-I
or type-II move and no type-III move, including the exterior spherical
region. Its bracket is a framing monomial but this local reduction process
cannot even start. We use only that initial obstruction, not the paper's
stronger historical claim about the optimal number of extra crossings.

The conclusion remains valid if one renames these local operations as
loop/bridge removal, opposite-sign series/parallel cancellation, and
admissible signed star-triangle moves: they are the graphical versions
of the same Reidemeister operations. Allowing temporary enlargement can
evade this example, but no Jones-controlled enlargement or selection
rule has been derived. Allowing arbitrary electrical weights instead
encounters the independent-channel obstruction in Sections 2–3.

6. Scope of the surviving information
The exact Jones polynomial gives one closed evaluation, not all five
connectivity coefficients of every cut subgraph. A monomial closed answer
therefore cannot be promoted to vanishing of each local kappa by this
argument. Neither deletion-contraction nor electrical response supplies
that promotion. No new generic proof mechanism or counterexample emerged,
and there is no reason here to launch a larger graph enumeration.
