Outcome
-------
The explicit Roberts--Jaeger differential does not turn independent arc
weights or a nearly perfect support matching into an acyclic cancellation.
There is a small actual-knot countertest, T(3,5). This closes that proposed
mechanism; it does not refute a Jones-unit-fiber-specific geometric theorem.

Primary input and conventions
-----------------------------
Jaeger, A Remark on Roberts' Totally Twisted Khovanov Homology:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1109.1805
Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.2 (printed p.3) identify the reduced twisted
theory of a KNOT with reduced characteristic-two delta-graded Khovanov
homology. Moving every marking to the basepoint makes its added differential
vanish after reduction. Thus independent weights do not generically destroy
the knot's homology. Section 4 (p.4) gives the explicit tree differential:

  d(S,T) = 1 / w(C1) + 1 / w(C2),

when S is changed from zero to one at two crossings to give T. C1 and C2
are the unmarked circles in the two intermediate resolutions. Other
coefficients vanish. Independent arc variables make this coefficient
nonzero exactly when the two arc sets differ. This is over F_2(x_a).

Roberts, Totally twisted Khovanov homology, Geometry & Topology 19 (2015):
https://msp.org/gt/2015/19-1/gt-v19-n1-p01-p.pdf
Definition 1.11 (printed p.9) uses delta=2h-q, with differential of delta
degree +2. Section 1.11.2(2), printed p.18, reports T(5,3) homology of total
rank seven, written 4 delta^8 + 3 delta^6 in that paper's chirality convention.
Our rank argument is independent of chirality and global grading shifts.
The paper's diagram/tree count is not the diagram/tree count used below.

An integral analogue must not silently be identified with ordinary even
integral Khovanov homology: Manion's integral spanning-tree construction is
for reduced ODD Khovanov homology.
https://msp.org/agt/2014/14-2/agt-v14-n2-p04-p.pdf

Why the proposed global mechanism fails
---------------------------------------
The independent variables are assigned to arcs, not independently to every
matrix entry. The differential coefficients obey shared algebraic identities
and d^2=0. Unit entries can disappear or change when other pivots are
cancelled; a support/Hall matching does not control these effects.

T(3,5) has determinant one and Jones polynomial t^4+t^6-t^10 (or its
mirror under the opposite convention). The alternating sum of the five
chain multiplicities is 25-50+35-10+1=1. Thus this is a concrete obstruction
even when the collapsed Euler characteristic is one. It is NOT a Jones-one
knot. The explicit tree complex retains delta, not the full (h,q) grading;
its collapsed Euler information is a determinant specialization. A proof
using the whole Jones polynomial must supply additional, genuinely
geometric information to prevent these gradient cycles specifically on the
unit Jones fiber. Neither the weights nor their independence supply it.

A sufficient endpoint would be an actual acyclic unit matching leaving one
generator for every Jones-one knot diagram (or for a controlled diagram
chosen from it). This would give reduced F_2 rank one, hence reduced rational
rank one and the established Khovanov unknot detector. The present test
does not prove that premise, and calling ordinary graph matching a proof
of it is demonstrably incorrect.
