SIGNED-GRAPH CANCELLATION: BOUNDED STRUCTURAL AUDIT
1 November 2026. Pass 42. No proof or counterexample to Jones detection.

Outcome

No viable new global topological mechanism was obtained. The checked graph
methods either compute a scalar while leaving the category of actual knot
diagrams, or require a restricted geometric input that already supplies the
missing simplification. Continuing with unconstrained graph elimination would
repeat previously identified gaps. Recommend stopping this branch.

1. Scope of the recent determinant result

Asaner--Kumar--Molander--Pease--Poudel, arXiv:2408.13410v1, Introduction and
Section 7, treats closures of
  sigma_1^{m_1} sigma_2^{m_2} ... sigma_{n-1}^{m_{n-1}},
with all exponents positive or all negative. It does not give a determinant
formula for arbitrary homogeneous braids. Its dimer weights reproduce the
spanning-tree activity expansion on this specified class.
https://arxiv.org/html/2408.13410

Elementary geometric audit of this class (not a novelty claim): the closure
is a knot only if every m_i is odd. An even m_i removes the only permutation
edge crossing the cut {1,...,i}|{i+1,...,n}, so the closure cannot have a single
component. If all are odd, the adjacent transpositions form one n-cycle.
The standard braided connected-sum construction identifies the knot closure
with #_i T(2,m_i): each consecutive braid block shares just its joining strand
with the next block, and the standard closure exposes the separating spheres.
Consequently its determinant is product_i |m_i|. Jones polynomial one forces
every |m_i|=1, when all summands are unknots. This is a genuine geometric
certificate for that class; it gives no transformation of an arbitrary knot
into that class.

Cohen's original determinant construction is also explicitly restricted.
Remark 5.3 requires the indicated crossing ordering; Proposition 5.4 extends
selected replacements, while Remark 5.5 says the construction does not
automatically apply to arbitrary Montesinos columns.
https://arxiv.org/html/1011.3661

Neither result licenses interpreting arbitrary determinant row operations as
isotopies. The computational complexity issue is only conditional: a uniform
polynomial-time determinant recipe for arbitrary Jones polynomials would have
major complexity consequences, but #P-hardness alone is not an unconditional
nonexistence theorem.

2. A precise obstruction to a literal cluster/dimer lifting proposal

The urban-renewal/cluster exchange has the local form
  a_0 a'_0 = a_1 a_2 + a_3 a_4.
Kenyon--Pemantle discuss this exchange and its dimer interpretation in Section
2.3 and Proposition 2.2 of Double-dimers, the Ising model and the hexahedron
recurrence, arXiv:1308.2998 / JCTA 137 (2016).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.2998
https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~pemantle/papers/KP150619.pdf

Suppose one tries to make the exchange directly into a crossing replacement
by assigning every a_i a single evaluated Jones activity letter, hence a unit
of Z[A,A^{-1}], namely +/-A^k. Then a'_0 cannot be such a unit. Indeed its
numerator is the sum of two units: distinct exponents give two terms; equal
exponents give either zero or +/-2A^k. Dividing by the unit a_0 does not change
this obstruction. Equivalently, evaluation at A=1 gives 0 or +/-2, never +/-1.

This small algebraic observation is a rejection of that PARTICULAR literal
lifting rule. It is not a theorem that every dimer encoding fails: face
variables are not automatically the activity entries, gauge transformations
can change them, and a polynomial weight might be represented by an entire
tangle rather than one crossing. Such an enlarged construction would have to
exhibit the actual replacement tangle and prove that the complete marked
boundary tensor, not merely a closed scalar partition function, agrees.
No such construction was found here. Laurentness or cluster positivity alone
does not provide it.

3. Electrical graph reductions do not solve the missing topological problem

Chang--Erickson's Untangling Planar Curves proves a reduction theorem for
immersed curves and related unsigned electrical graph moves. The statement
concerns shadows, without over/under data.
https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SoCG.2016.29

Kauffman's signed-graph specialization is invariant under the particular
signed moves corresponding to Reidemeister II and III. This is not invariance
under an arbitrary unsigned electrical reduction sequence. The signs and
embedding of each move must be lifted to the actual crossing diagram.
https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kauffman/TuttePolySignGraphs.pdf

4. Exact stopping point

The missing operation would have to turn a cancellation of full Laurent
weights into a sequence of ACTUAL marked tangle replacements, preserve the
isotopy class (or a separately justified nontriviality obstruction), tolerate
temporary diagram growth, and remain valid when the cancelling homology is
not acyclic. None of the audited electrical, dimer, or cluster results implies
such an operation. Stating its existence only for Jones-one diagrams would
essentially restate the target problem; it is not proposed as a new scaffold.

No new knot candidate, claimed generic theorem, or unsupported positivity
assumption was produced in this pass.
