INDEPENDENT AUDIT: T(3,5) SUPPORT MATCHING
2026-11-01. Audit of torus_tree_support.json; no change to shared source data.

PASS, with characteristic-two scope retained.

1. Independently reconstructed connected components of all 1024 smoothings
using graph traversal on arc labels, rather than importing the supplied
verifier. Bit0 pairs (a,b),(c,d); bit1 pairs (b,c),(d,a). The list of121
one-circle states agrees exactly with the JSON.

2. For every inclusion of masks differing by two newly set bits, checked
both intermediate states have two circles. Their circles excluding arc0
are nonempty. With independent variables on arcs1,...,19, the coefficient
1/w(C1)+1/w(C2) is zero in characteristic2 iff C1=C2. All500 proposed edges
have unequal circle sets; all possible edges were reconstructed, and the
resulting support equals the stored500-edge list exactly.

3. All60 matching pairs are genuine support edges. Their120 endpoints are
globally distinct, not merely distinct within each degree or within separate
copies of a bipartite graph. The sole unmatched vertex is972.

4. Independently checked the directed cycle after reversing matched arrows:
15 ->159 ->135 ->207 ->195 ->231 ->39 ->63 ->15.
Each forward edge is an unmatched differential-support edge and each
backward edge is one of the supplied matching pairs. Thus this particular
matching is not an algebraic discrete-Morse acyclic matching.

5. Regenerated Spherogram Link(braid_closure=[1,2]*5). Its PD agrees exactly,
tuple for tuple, with the JSON; it has one component and writhe+10. This
certifies the positive T(3,5) diagram. Mirror conventions would not change
the total homology rank, but are not needed for this identification.

6. Read the primary sources. Jaeger arXiv1109.1805 Section4, printedp4,
specifically allows linearly independent edge weights except on the marked
edge and gives exactly the two inverse circle-weight sums used here.
Corollary3.2 identifies the resulting knot homology with reduced,
delta-graded characteristic-two Khovanov homology. Roberts, Geometry &
Topology19(2015), printedp18, Section1.11.2(2), gives T(5,3) Poincare
polynomial4*delta^8+3*delta^6, hence totalrank7. Roberts uses a different
diagram with27 generators; homology invariance makes this irrelevant to
the present121-generator diagram.

Consequences: a support matching covering all but one actual tree generator
does not imply that those60 pairs can be cancelled. In fact no acyclic
matching on the actual differential can cover all but one generator here,
because that would force total homology rank at most1 instead of7. The
coefficient functions satisfy genuine differential relations; they are not
independent generic variables attached to support edges.

Precise qualification: this is the reduced characteristic-two twisted/tree
complex over a rational FUNCTION field in independent arc variables, not a
calculation of Khovanov homology over the rational NUMBERS.
