APPROXIMATION, ARITHMETIC SEPARATION, AND EXACT JONES ONE
1 November 2026. Bounded pass 43; no counterexample or detection proof.

Outcome

There is an exact arithmetic certificate, but the known density/compilation
theorems do not produce a nontrivial knot satisfying it. The precise hurdle
is a simultaneous degree--height--precision inequality. Fixed-root exactness
and arbitrarily strong p-adic congruence are already achieved by actual
nontrivial knots, so neither is the missing construction on its own.

1. A self-contained degree and height bound

Let D be a diagram of a classical knot with c crossings, V its normalized
Jones polynomial in t=A^{-4}, and P=V-1. Set H=||P||_1. Then
  H <= 4^c+1,
and all exponents of P lie in [-3c/2,3c/2]. Thus multiplying P by its
lowest-power inverse gives an ordinary integer polynomial of degree <=3c.

Proof: there are 2^c bracket states. Each has at most c+1 circles, so its
factor delta^{s-1}, delta=-A^2-A^{-2}, has coefficient l1 norm <=2^c.
The sum has norm <=4^c. Its A-exponents have absolute value <=3c;
the writhe correction shifts by at most 3c more. Division of exponents
by -4 gives the claimed t interval. Subtracting 1 raises l1 norm by
at most one and does not enlarge this interval. These are deliberately
coarse bounds; an actual computed height or support may be much smaller.

2. The exact root-of-unity separation threshold

Let zeta be primitive of order r and d=phi(r). If P(zeta)!=0, then
  |P(zeta)| >= H^{-(d-1)}.                              (1)
Indeed P(zeta) is an algebraic integer, and its nonzero norm is an integer
of absolute value at least one. All its other d-1 conjugates have absolute
value at most H. Negative powers cause no problem because zeta is a unit.

If every coefficient of P is divisible by an integer M>=1, the stronger
bound is
  |P(zeta)| >= M^d/H^{d-1}.                            (2)
The same bound holds under the weaker condition P(zeta) in M Z[zeta].
Apply the preceding norm argument to P(zeta)/M.

Consequently, a RIGOROUS certified error interval proving
  |V(zeta)-1| < M^d/H^{d-1}
forces V(zeta)=1 exactly. It forces V=1 as a Laurent polynomial only if
the shifted degree is less than d. A sufficient diagram-only condition is
  phi(r)>3c.                                          (3)
Without (3), exactness only says Phi_r divides the shifted P.
For distinct root orders one can instead use the degree of the product
of their distinct cyclotomic polynomials. This is an exact verification
criterion, not a construction of a knot meeting it.

3. What braid compilation would actually have to beat

Write x=log(1/epsilon), where epsilon bounds the error in the NORMALIZED
Jones scalar itself. Any conversion from operator error to scalar error
must include its plat/trace normalization; it cannot be silently dropped.
With M=1 and only the diagram bound, (1) requires
  x > (phi(r)-1) log(4^c+1).                           (4)
Combining with (3), this sufficient threshold is of order c^2 when r
is chosen as a prime just larger than 3c. At a fixed root it is of order
c, but then cyclotomic equality is not whole-polynomial equality.

The original Freedman--Larsen--Wang density/compilation discussion gives
length polynomial in log(1/epsilon), not a method meeting (3)--(4).
The modern general Solovay--Kitaev bound is substantially better than
the often-quoted exponent near four: Kuperberg's October 2025 v2 gives
O(x^{1.44042...+delta}) word length for fixed generators. It still does
not give the crossing control c=O(sqrt(x)) that would close this coarse
adaptive-root certificate. Generator constants also change with r.

This is NOT a lower bound on every conceivable algorithm and NOT a claim
that every Jones polynomial has exponential height. It says the cited
upper bounds do not certify the required self-consistent inequality.
Even a fixed-root O(x) existence theorem would need appropriate constants
for (4), and would still leave (3) unresolved. Using a smaller actual H
could improve the test, but no topology-preserving construction controlling
H, r, epsilon, and nontriviality simultaneously was obtained.

4. All Galois embeddings: exact rigidity, and why density cannot supply it

If |P(zeta^a)|<1 for every a coprime to r, then its norm has absolute
value <1, so P(zeta)=0. More generally, if one conjugate is <=epsilon
and all the others are <=B, nonzero P requires
  epsilon B^{d-1} >=1.                                 (5)
Thus smallness in one embedding must be paid for elsewhere, unless the
evaluation is already exactly zero.

This is genuine arithmetic rigidity but not a free additional consequence
of unitary braid density. The full integral Temperley--Lieb representation
can be written using its diagram basis over Z[A,A^{-1}]. At a root A=eta,
its regular-representation matrices have algebraic-integer entries.
The diagonal collection of ALL Galois conjugates of each entry lies in
the discrete Minkowski lattice of Z[eta]. If every conjugate of every
entry of rho(beta)-I has absolute value <1, the norm argument makes
every entry zero. Therefore this full all-place image cannot be a dense
product around the identity. Density in a selected compact Jones sector
does not provide simultaneous approximation in all these places.

No assumption is made that a particular orthonormal quantum basis has
integral entries: the argument uses the integral diagram basis. Nor is
specialized equality of the full TL matrices asserted to be equality
over the generic Laurent ring. These are separate requirements.

At one fixed order, exact equality already automatically holds at every
Galois-conjugate primitive root. Those conjugates do not add independent
cyclotomic factors. At infinitely many distinct orders, equality would
force P=0, but changing the knot with the order is not that assertion.

5. The p-adic construction has the wrong height growth for exactness

Coefficientwise congruence P=0 mod M implies exact P=0 if M exceeds a
certified upper bound on every coefficient (H is one such bound).
The simple diagram-only criterion is M>4^c+1. Combining it with (2)
can help verify an already constructed example; it does not automatically
turn a congruence sequence into a single exact knot.

The actual Eliahou--Fromentin family has V(K_r)=1 mod 2^r and a displayed
diagram with c_r=20*2^{r-1}+1 crossings. More decisively, their Proposition
4.3 and Proposition 5.2 give the normalized-bracket leading term
  (2 A^{28})^{2^{r-1}}.
Its coefficient is 2^{2^{r-1}}, so these polynomials are explicitly nonunit;
their heights grow far faster than the guaranteed modulus 2^r. This is an
actual-knot obstruction to promoting that particular recursion to exactness,
not merely a failure to estimate it tightly enough. The source variable in
that displayed leading term is the bracket variable, renamed A here.
https://arxiv.org/html/1610.05560v2

Similarly, Kalfagianni's actual hyperbolic constructions realize prescribed
finite root data (and hence their Galois conjugates). The previously audited
ordinary-Jones version in work/research32/finite_probe_independent_audit.txt
handles normalization directly. This fact was already established earlier;
it is cited here only to rule out a fixed finite root set as the endpoint.
https://arxiv.org/html/0710.0076v2

6. Nontriviality remains an independent geometric condition

Density concerns represented operators or closed scalar values. It does not
certify that a resulting closure is a knot rather than a link, or that it is
nontrivial. Pseudo-Anosov braid dynamics is insufficient: an actual unknot
has such a presentation, as established in work/research39/braid.txt.
Even if those topological conditions were arranged separately, the arithmetic
conditions above would still have to be met for the SAME constructed word.

Conclusion: no approximation-to-exact counterexample mechanism was obtained.
The useful concrete output is the certificate (2)--(3), and the explicit
all-Galois tradeoff (5). Neither a density theorem, a growing sequence of
congruences, nor finite exact root agreement supplies its missing hypotheses.
