FINITE-QUOTIENT COLLISIONS VERSUS EXACT GENERIC JONES IDENTITIES
2026-11-01. Bounded structural audit. No generic Jones-one counterexample.

FINAL SIMPLIFICATION: Sections 8--9 are correct but unnecessary for the
finite-field knot conclusion. Ordinary T(2,2m+1), with m=lcm p_i(q_i-1),
already has V=1 at all nonzero values in the selected fields and integer
V(-1)=2m+1>1. See the final output note and verify_torus_finite_fields.py.
The commutator example is backup, not a substantive advance toward detection.

Verdict
Finite-field pigeonholing plus the usual universal degree/height certificate
does not close an exact-kernel construction. Its reconstruction threshold
requires quadratically many bits in word length, while the supply of words
has only linearly many bits. This conclusion concerns the specified
worst-case cardinality argument; it is not an impossibility theorem for
structured braid-specific arithmetic methods.

1. Full endpoint and the genuinely necessary gates
An actual nonidentity braid in the full generic Temperley--Lieb/Jones
kernel would enter the established Long/Ito route to a nontrivial knot
with Jones polynomial1. A finite specialized collision alone supplies
neither a generic kernel element nor its braid nontriviality. Comparing
two syntactically different words does not establish different braids.
Passing to zero-exponent-sum or pure braids does not change these issues.

2. Explicit universal Laurent bounds
Use TL_n over R=Z[A,A^-1] with diagram basis of size C_n (the nth Catalan
number), e_i^2=delta*e_i, delta=-A^2-A^-2, and
  rho(sigma_i^s)=A^s I+A^-s e_i,  s=+1 or-1.
Multiplication of a basis diagram by e_i creates at most one loop.
Thus one letter changes exponents by at most3 and multiplies the total
absolute coefficient sum by at most3. Induction gives, for word lengthL,
  support_A rho(w) subset[-3L,3L],
  sum_over_basis_and_exponents |coefficient| <=3^L.
These statements concern the entire TL element, so no matrix-denominator
or choice-of-irreducible-lattice issue occurs.

For two words u,v of length at mostL, every coefficient of rho(u)-rho(v)
has support in[-3L,3L] and absolute value at most H=2*3^L. Multiplying
by A^(3L) gives polynomials of degree at mostD=6L. If the words represent
different braids and their TL elements agree, uv^-1 is the desired generic
kernel element. Its length is at most2L; compare the two images directly
to retain the sharper bound above.

3. A valid exact certificate, and why the elementary count fails
Choose a prime p>H and alpha in F_(p^k) with minimal polynomial degree
k>D. If rho(u)(alpha)=rho(v)(alpha), each coefficient difference vanishes
identically modulo p: a nonzero polynomial of degree at mostD cannot
vanish at alpha. All integer coefficients have magnitude<p, so they
then vanish over Z. This IS a correct finite exact-equality certificate.

The specialized TL algebra has p^(k*C_n) elements. The usual pigeonhole
argument would require more distinct candidate braids than this bound.
But the number of words of length at mostL is at most
  (2n-2)^(L+1),
whereas the certificate above requires
  p^(k*C_n) > (2*3^L)^((6L+1)*C_n).
The latter is larger for every n>=2,L>=1. For a simple uniform proof,
C_n>=n and 2n-2<=3^n, while L(6L+1)>L+1. The log of the ambient
finite-algebra bound grows as Omega(C_n L^2), compared with O(L log n)
for the candidate-word bound.

Important logical qualification: this does NOT prove that the actual
specialized braid image has that many elements. Its cardinality could be
much smaller. It proves that the ambient-algebra counting bound alone
never forces a collision within its own safe reconstruction range.
A new structural upper bound on the ACTUAL image could change the issue;
none is supplied by residual finiteness or by the Catalan dimension.

4. General information bound for universal modular separators
Let F be an additive map from integer polynomials of degree at mostD
to a finite group, and suppose F detects every nonzero polynomial whose
coefficient height is at mostH. Apply F to all polynomials whose D+1
coefficients lie in{0,...,H}. Any two of these with equal image have a
nonzero difference in the forbidden height-H box. Consequently
  |target F| >= (H+1)^(D+1).
For C_n independent coefficient polynomials, the analogous lower bound is
  (H+1)^(C_n*(D+1)).
This applies to combined finite-field evaluations and congruence tests,
including Chinese-remainder collections, if they are supposed to work
universally for this whole box. It explains the quadratic bit scale
without committing to one extension field or one prime.

This is deliberately a bound for UNIVERSAL polynomial reconstruction.
Actual braid coefficients form a restricted subset; a certificate using
proved special constraints on that subset need not obey this ambient-box
bound. Such constraints, rather than additional unstructured congruence
tests, would be the missing construction mechanism.

5. Congruence-depth amplification does not automatically repair it
For a lengthL braid beta, rho(beta)-I has coefficient height at most3^L+1.
If it is nonzero and coefficientwise divisible by p^r, necessarily
  p^r <=3^L+1.
Thus exceeding this threshold certifies the identity, but just asserting
that a congruence can be made deep does not show it exceeds the threshold.
For example, the standard estimate for a commutator of two lengthL words
which are I modulo p^r gives length at most4L and congruence modulo p^(2r).
The guaranteed depth-to-length ratio is halved. Usual pth-power estimates
likewise incur multiplicative word growth for incremental depth growth.
Actual extra cancellation can improve these bounds, but proving that is
additional structure, not a consequence of the formal commutator estimate.

6. Root-of-unity and residual-finiteness distinctions
At a primitive Mth root of unity zeta in characteristic0, exact vanishing
of a Laurent polynomial of spanD implies generic vanishing if phi(M)>D.
Here there is no coefficient-height requirement. However, the specialized
Jones braid image is not thereby finite; root-of-unity generators can
generate an infinite or dense group. Thus the required finite pigeonhole
premise is absent. Freedman--Larsen--Wang explicitly study dense Jones
images, so finiteness cannot be inferred from finite-order generators.

For alpha in a finite field, high MULTIPLICATIVE order alone is not the
analog of cyclotomic degree: its minimal polynomial degree is what enters
the exact polynomial argument. Confusing these quantities defeats the
reconstruction step.

Finally, every fixed finite quotient of an infinite braid group has many
collisions, even in settings with a faithful generic representation.
For example the generic reduced Burau representation of B3 is faithful,
yet all its finite-field specializations have finite image. Thus existence
of arbitrarily many finite collisions cannot by itself imply a generic
relation. Residual finiteness separates a fixed nonidentity element in
some quotient; it does not produce a common nonidentity element in every
kernel. A growing sequence of different collision words can escape every
fixed length bound, precisely as the reconstruction inequalities allow.

7. Operational conclusion
Finite tests remain useful filters for a geometrically predicted word,
and an exact certificate with explicit degree/height bounds is possible.
They do not provide a kernel-construction theorem by the standard ambient
pigeonhole estimate. No larger word scan is justified by this audit.
The only substantive escape identified is a proved restriction on actual
braid images strong enough to beat the universal reconstruction count,
together with an independent certificate that the collision braid is
nontrivial. Neither has been established here.

8. Explicit full-operator false positives for ANY finite list of fields
This is an actual braid construction, not merely a counting analogy.
Let the selected fields have sizes q_j=p_j^(k_j), and choose positive N
divisible by every p_j(q_j-1). Then N is even. In B3 put
  beta_N=[sigma1^N,sigma2^N]
         =sigma1^N sigma2^N sigma1^-N sigma2^-N.
It is pure, has exponent sum0 and word length4N.

For every nonzero A in each selected field, a TL generator g satisfies
  (g-A I)(g+A^-3 I)=0.
Both roots belong to F_q^*. In characteristic p, X^N-1 has every nonzero
field element as a root of multiplicity at leastp>=2. Therefore the
displayed quadratic divides X^N-1, including when its two roots coincide.
It follows in the ENTIRE specialized TL algebra that g^N=I. Consequently
rho(beta_N)=I for EVERY nonzero parameter in EVERY chosen finite field.
This includes arbitrary finite lists of extension fields, not just one
parameter value or one characteristic.

Nevertheless beta_N has nonidentity generic TL image. At
A=exp(pi*i/4), use the standard two-dimensional TL3 block. After a diagonal
gauge and removing the common scalar A, the generators are
  L=[[1,0],[1,1]], U^-1=[[1,-1],[0,1]].
The scalar cancels in the commutator. Direct multiplication gives
  rho(beta_N)=[[1+N^2,N^3],[N^3,N^4-N^2+1]],
which is nonidentity for every positive N. This also independently proves
braid nontriviality. The block conventions are the same horizontal twist
and inverse vertical twist checked in research41/pairing.txt.

Thus exhaustive full-operator tests over any FIXED finite collection of
finite fields still admit explicit nontrivial false positives. There is
no contradiction with the exact certificate in Section3: N grows with
the field orders, and the resulting word exceeds the required degree
threshold. This construction supplies no Jones-one knot and no generic
kernel; it precisely demonstrates the failure of finite tests without
a compatible length bound.

9. Root's strengthening: explicit NONTRIVIAL KNOT false positives
The preceding braid gives a particularly clean knot family. For evenN>=2,
let K_N be the closure of
  [sigma1^N,sigma2^N] sigma1 sigma2.
The commutator is pure and the final permutation is a3-cycle, so K_N is a
knot. The writhe is2, the same as the unknot anchor sigma1 sigma2. At every
selected finite-field parameter from Section8, the commutator is the full
TL identity. Hence the normalized Jones evaluation of K_N equals1 there,
including degenerate delta=0 values: this argument uses no division by
delta or quantum dimension.

At zeta=exp(pi*i/4), set R=L U^-1=[[1,-1],[1,0]] and M equal to the
commutator matrix in Section8. Then tr(MR)=1+N^2. The reduced TL3 closure
trace decomposes as
  tr_2+(delta^2-2)*char_1.
Here char_1=A^2 and the two-dimensional matrix is A^2 M R. At delta=0,
the raw closure is A^2(tr(MR)-2). Writhe normalization multiplies by
A^-6, giving
  V_(K_N)(-1)=1-N^2,
  det(K_N)=N^2-1>=3.
Thus K_N is certified nontrivial. Independent full Laurent TL3 computations
at N=2 and N=4 returned V(-1)=-3 and-15, respectively.

Parameter caveat: tests for every nonzero A in F_q cover the ordinary
Jones parameters t=A^-4 in that field. To cover EVERY nonzero t in F_q,
one may instead include F_(q^4) among the operator-test fields. Every
nonzero F_q element has a fourth root in that extension. Therefore,
given any finite list of finite fields F_q, choosing N divisible by all
p(q^4-1) yields an explicit nontrivial K_N with
  V_(K_N)(t)=1 for every t in each F_q^*.
This is equality of finite-field functions, NOT equality of Laurent
polynomials over Z. The increasing word length is essential and agrees
with all reconstruction bounds above.
