PASS 44: INTEGRAL UNITS, LONG KNOTS, AND THE AMBIENT-MANIFOLD GAP

Bounded conceptual audit. No new Jones-unknot proof, counterexample, or
independently tractable missing lemma was obtained. This route should stop
in its present form. Its proposed unit-reflection premise is precisely
the original problem, and ordinary character-variety theorems apply to a
different ambient manifold.

Duplicate check: work/generic/tangle_notes.md sections1--3 already proves

1. Exact rank-one computation, with framing retained.

Let R=Z[A,A^-1], delta=-A^2-A^-2. The relative Kauffman bracket skein
module of a ball with two marked boundary endpoints is R*eta, where eta
is the straight framed arc. Indeed, resolving all crossings leaves one
planar arc and contractible circles; deleting the circles spans the
module by eta. The normalized closure bracket sends eta to1, showing
linear independence. Stacking makes this algebra exactly R.

A zero-framed long knot K therefore has the image

                    [K]=V_K(A^-4)*eta.

A framing change f multiplies this by (-A^3)^f. The only units of R are
signed monomials. For an actual normalized knot polynomial V(t), the
identities V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0 imply

                    V is a Laurent unit  <=>  V=1.

Proof: a unit has the form sign*t^k; evaluation at1 fixes the sign and
the derivative fixes k=0. If starting in the A-variable, the same proof
uses the derivative of V(A^-4). Thus retaining integral coefficients
does avoid the vacuous field statement, but gains no new condition.

Geometric units of the long-knot connected-sum monoid are just U: genus
is additive, nonnegative, and zero exactly for the unknot. With framings
allowed, these units are the framed straight arcs. Consequently:

  "the integral (1,1)-tangle skein functor reflects geometric units"

is equivalent to V_K=1 => K=U, with no remaining weaker subproblem.
The complete proposed chain closes, but only by naming its first step
as the theorem to be proved.

Sikora's stronger two-string version is independently established in
Theorem9.2, Section9 of the 2023 revision: Jones detection is equivalent
to the bracket vector (1,0) detecting the framed zero tangle. The proof
uses local knot insertion for one direction. Source:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08162
https://doi.org/10.4153/S0008414X23000755

2. Exact limitation of integral algebraic postprocessing.

For ANY R-module M and ANY R-linear map Phi:R*eta -> M,

                    Phi([K])=V_K(A^-4)*Phi(eta).

Thus every fixed one-hole skein context, arbitrary coefficient extension,
completion, localization, or specialization carries an equality [K]=eta
to the same equality. Algebraic unit tests, divisibility tests and zero
sets of this element can extract consequences of V, but do not recover
an omitted geometric coordinate. The assertion is about constructions
factoring through THIS rank-one module; it does not assert that all
possible geometric constructions factor through it.

In particular, independently inserted copies of a long knot produce
powers of V. A parallel cable does not arise by substituting independent
one-strand boxes; claiming otherwise would reintroduce the already
excluded colored-promotion assumption.

There is also an actual failure of generic skein injectivity, not just a
dimension heuristic: the Conway and Kinoshita--Terasaka knots have the
same Jones polynomial but are distinct. Cutting each at a regular point
and choosing zero framing gives distinct long knots with identical
elements of R*eta. Piccirillo's paper recalls mutation preservation of
Jones and proves Conway is not slice, whereas Kinoshita--Terasaka is
slice. This disproves full injectivity; it does NOT disprove injectivity
of the special fiber over eta, which remains the original conjecture.

3. Why the character-variety theorem does not repair this.

Bullock identifies the coordinate ring of the SL2(C) character variety
of a FIXED ambient manifold M with its A=-1 skein specialization modulo
the nilradical. For M=B^3 or S^3, that character variety is a point.
An embedded knot in that ball is not the ambient manifold whose group
the theorem uses. In particular every zero-framed long knot specializes
to eta at A=-1, since V_K(1)=1, including the trefoil.

To obtain the knot-group character variety one must instead use
M=E_K, the exterior of K. Drilling the particular K changes the ambient
manifold. No R-linear operation on the preceding relative ball module
implements that change: its entire input has already become a scalar.
Skein relations among links in a fixed ball do not give corresponding
linear relations among the coordinate rings of their different exteriors.

Source: Bullock, Comment. Math. Helv.72(1997),521--542, main theorem:
https://arxiv.org/abs/q-alg/9604014
https://ems.press/journals/cmh/articles/207

The established endpoint is attractive: the A-polynomial detects U.
Thus a claimed bridge V_K=1 => A_K is the unknot A-polynomial would
complete the argument. But, because the endpoint is an equivalence,
that unrestricted bridge is itself equivalent to ordinary Jones
detection. No map producing it was found here. The source's conditional
colored-Jones corollary requires its additional conjecture; it is not
an unconditional colored-detection theorem.

Source: Dunfield--Garoufalidis, AGT4(2004),1145--1153:
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405353

4. An actual countermodel to the purely ring-theoretic axioms.

The Alexander polynomial is an integral, connected-sum-multiplicative
invariant with Delta_U=1, Delta(1)=1, Delta'(1)=0 in symmetric
normalization, and the same signed-monomial Laurent units. Nevertheless
the nontrivial Conway knot has Delta=1. Its nontriviality is particularly
decisive because it is not slice. Therefore integrality, multiplicativity,
Laurent-unit classification and these normalizations, on the actual knot
monoid, do not by themselves imply geometric unit reflection.

This is NOT a counterexample to Jones detection, and no claim is made
that Alexander satisfies every Jones-specific skein identity. It is a
test showing that a proof using only the listed algebraic properties
has omitted a necessary Jones-specific geometric input.

Primary source: Piccirillo, Ann. Math.191(2020),581--591, introduction
pp581--582 and Theorem1.1:
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v191-n2-p05-s.pdf

Decision. Do not pursue more arithmetic on the already scalar (1,1)
image as an independent route. A useful new claim would need an explicit
geometric construction carrying V=1 to a nontrivial statement about the
EXTERIOR or its peripheral algebra, together with a proof that it does
not assume the desired unit-reflection property. No such construction
was obtained in this bounded pass; generic character-variety injectivity
and integral unit terminology alone do not supply one.
