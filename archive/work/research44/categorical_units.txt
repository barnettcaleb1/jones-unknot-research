PASS 44: CATEGORICAL UNIT REFLECTION FOR A CUT KNOT
2026-11-01

Verdict
-------
The proposed implication has a rigorous endpoint, but its unknown premise,
restricted to actual cut knots, is equivalent to the original Jones unknot
conjecture. Tensor language does not reduce the missing mathematical work.
The nearby versions that discard the quantum grading, invert every nonzero
decategorified polynomial, or use an invertible braid complex instead of a
cut-knot object fail on actual knots.

1. Precise setup and full conditional chain
-----------------------------------------
Cut a based oriented knot K open to a one-input, one-output tangle T_K.
Use the writhe-normalized Khovanov tangle complex X_K. Vertical stacking
of these tangles is connected sum. At the fundamental Temperley--Lieb
level, the endomorphism space of the one-strand object is the coefficient
ring, and the normalized scalar for T_K is V_K (after the usual variable
substitution). There is no extra unknot-circle factor in this cut scalar.

Bar-Natan's tangle construction respects planar composition, including
stacking, in the homotopy category. Apply reduced rational TQFT by setting
the basepoint dot to zero. This gives a bounded bigraded chain complex C_K
with reduced Kh_Q(K) as homology and C_U=Q. On a resolution, the distinguished
arc contributes Q and every free circle contributes the ordinary Frobenius
algebra; stacking joins the distinguished arcs and leaves the circle factors
separate. Thus this reduction is monoidal for the present operation:

          C_(K#L) ~= C_K tensor_Q C_L.

Now suppose X_K has a two-sided tensor inverse in this bounded tangle
homotopy category. Applying the monoidal reduction gives an inverse D for
C_K. Kunneth then gives

          dim H(C_K) * dim H(D) = dim H(Q) = 1.

Both dimensions are nonnegative integers, so reduced Kh_Q(K) has rank one.
Kronheimer--Mrowka's unknot detector implies K=U. Conversely, if K=U its
normalized cut complex is the identity object and is tensor-invertible.
Consequently, for actual cut knots,

          X_K tensor-invertible  <=>  K is the unknot.

The same endpoint already holds for invertibility of C_K in the derived
category of finite bigraded rational vector spaces. Every such complex
splits up to quasi-isomorphism into its homology; an invertible one is a
single one-dimensional grading shift. No classification theorem is needed
beyond the displayed Kunneth argument.

Primary construction: Bar-Natan, Khovanov's homology for tangles and
cobordisms, Theorems 2 and 3, especially printed pp.1466 and1469:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0410495
Primary endpoint: Kronheimer--Mrowka, Khovanov homology is an unknot-detector,
Corollary1.3 and Proposition1.4 (pp.2--3) give the rational-rank endpoint:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1005.4346

2. Why the proposed missing lemma is exactly the original problem
----------------------------------------------------------------
Use a quantum variable q with chi_q(C_K)=V_K(q^2), and normalize U to one.
The Grothendieck ring for finite graded rational complexes is Z[q,q^-1],
not Q(q). Its units are exactly +/-q^j. For knot polynomials V_K(1)=1 and
V'_K(1)=0. Therefore chi_q(C_K) is a Laurent-ring unit if and only if it
equals one: the value fixes the sign and the derivative forces j=0.

The desired assertion

  (*) For actual cut knots, a unit decategorified class implies that the
      Khovanov tangle complex is tensor-invertible

therefore implies Jones unknot detection by Section1. Conversely, Jones
unknot detection implies (*) immediately: a unit class is V_K=1, hence
K=U, hence X_K is the identity. This is an equivalence, not merely a claim
that (*) is difficult or resembles the original problem.

The checked primary sources supply composition and invertibility for
specified objects. They do not supply reflection of invertibility from
the Grothendieck class for arbitrary knot-realizable cut objects. An actual
proof of (*) would have to construct an inverse or prove that a candidate
unit/counit cone is contractible. Euler class zero for that cone is the
original cancellation problem, not a contractibility criterion.

3. Concrete tests of the closest stronger versions
--------------------------------------------------
(a) Forgetting quantum degrees is fatal. Every knot has ungraded reduced
Euler characteristic V_K(1)=1. The trefoil has reduced rational Kh rank
three (it is alternating with determinant three), so its reduced complex
is not invertible. Thus unit reflection in the ungraded Grothendieck ring
Z is false even for a three-crossing knot.

(b) Extending the DECATEGORIFIED ring to Q(q) is also fatal. Every nonzero
Jones polynomial becomes a unit there; all knot Jones polynomials are
nonzero because V_K(1)=1. This would again label the trefoil invertible.
The quantum grading variable is not an ordinary scalar in the original
chain complex, and coefficient extension of the base field preserves its
rank-three homology. A categorical localization designed to force these
objects invertible cannot then be assumed conservative on knots.

(c) Rouquier braid invertibility is the wrong operation. Rouquier's
Proposition3.4 assigns an invertible complex to EVERY braid, its inverse
coming from the inverse braid. In particular the complex of sigma1^3 is
invertible, although its closure is a trefoil. Closing a braid (a trace
operation involving caps/cups) does not preserve the relevant tensor
inverse. A one-strand cut knot with maxima/minima is not a one-strand
braid. The braided construction therefore cannot be substituted into
the cut-knot inference.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409593

(d) A stronger 'equal Euler classes differ only by an invertible factor'
principle is already false on actual knots. The previously checked pair
5_1 and the appropriate chirality of10_132 has the same whole Jones
polynomial but reduced rational Kh ranks5 and11. Tensoring with an
invertible finite rational complex preserves total rank, so their reduced
complexes cannot be related this way. This reuses established data; no
new scan or claimed ribbon relation is involved. The primary original
calculation already distinguishes their unreduced homologies (ranks6,12):
https://www.math.toronto.edu/~drorbn/papers/Categorification/Categorification.pdf
See work/research20/homology_scaffold.txt and the retained reduced KnotInfo
records in work/research27/homology_global.txt for the reduced conventions.

4. Stop condition
-----------------
This route currently has no independent categorical mechanism for the
unit Jones fiber. The precise complete chain is valid, and several tempting
shortcuts are genuinely ruled out by actual knots. Further work would be
useful only if it produces a knot-geometric theorem about the needed
unit/counit maps, beyond their Euler classes. Naming that assertion 'unit
reflection', invoking braid invertibility, or passing to a fraction field
does not provide such a theorem.
