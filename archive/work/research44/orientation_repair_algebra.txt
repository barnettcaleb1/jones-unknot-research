PASS44: ORIENTATION-INDEPENDENT REPAIR OF THE CLASP-MACHINE ARGUMENT

Outcome. The normalization F_L=x^{-3 ell(L)}V_L, ell=sum of pairwise
linking numbers, genuinely removes the component-reorientation factor.
Squaring H=F^2 also has a plausible and independently audited finite-type
packaging (homology agent). Neither change removes a nonzero growing
Laurent channel. Thus they cannot repair an L-independent machine
compression theorem that is already obstructed by that channel.

This is a statement about this proposed repair. It is not a theorem that
all nonlinear finite-type arguments, or all possible corrected machines,
are impossible.

1. Normalization.

Use x=A^-2, p=x-x^-1, delta=-x-x^-1, and the source convention

                 x^-2 V_+ - x^2 V_- = p V_0.

For an oriented diagram w=w_self+2 ell, hence

 F_L=(-A^3)^(-w_self)<L>=A^(6 ell)V_L=x^(-3 ell)V_L.

Reversing a component changes w and 2ell by the same amount, leaving
w_self invariant. Thus F is independent of the orientations chosen for
components. It is invariant under Reidemeister moves. On knots ell=0,
so F=V. On knots H=F^2 has H=1 iff V=1: the Laurent ring is a domain,
and V(1)=1 excludes V=-1. Neither normalization weakens the desired
unknot-detection conclusion on the original knot.

2. Exact skein relations for F.

(a) Mixed crossing. Suppose the crossing is between distinct components,
and their linking number in D_+ is m. Their linking in D_- is m-1.
Oriented smoothing merges them. If ell_0 denotes total linking of D_0,
then ell_+=ell_0+m and ell_-=ell_0+m-1. Substitution gives

                  F_+ - x F_- = p x^(2-3m) F_0.           (M)

(b) Self crossing. Smoothing splits one component into two. Let m be
the linking number of those two new components in D_0. The original
total linking is the same in D_+ and D_-, while ell_0=ell_++m. Therefore

                  x^-2 F_+ - x^2 F_- = p x^(3m) F_0.      (S)

These linking numbers are not determined by the local crossing picture.
They can depend on the entire residual twist block. Replacing V by F in
the paper's original local skein formulas, leaving coefficients unchanged,
is therefore incorrect.

Actual small checks, using the positive trefoil convention:
 V_trefoil=x^2+x^6-x^8, V_U=1,
 V_positiveHopf=-x-x^5, ell(Hopf)=1,
 F_positiveHopf=-x^-2-x^2, F_U2=-x-x^-1.
The Hopf-to-unlink mixed skein has m=1 and satisfies
 F_Hopf-x F_U2=1-x^-2=p*x^-1.
The trefoil unknotting self skein has smoothing Hopf with m=1 and obeys
 x^-2 F_trefoil-x^2 F_U=p*x^3*F_Hopf.
Thus even these actual links distinguish (M),(S) from the old recurrence.

For the source's two-coordinate paired identity, write ell_st for total
linking of each oriented state. The exact replacement is

 x^(3ell_-+)F_-+ + x^(3ell_+-)F_+-
   = x^(3ell_++-4)F_++ + x^(3ell_--+4)F_--
     - p^2*x^(3ell_00)F_00.                               (P)

If a,b in{0,1} specify whether the first and second crossings are mixed
in the unsmoothed diagram, then ell_++=ell_--+a+b,
ell_-+=ell_--+b, ell_+-=ell_--+a. Divide (P) by x^(3ell_--) to get

 x^(3b)F_-+ + x^(3a)F_+-
   = x^(3(a+b)-4)F_++ + x^4 F_--
     - p^2*x^(3(ell_00-ell_--))F_00.

The double-smoothing normalization still carries a global linking term.

3. Squaring: what is repaired and what is changed.

The homology agent independently checked that F itself fails the paper's
finite-type claim at level0 because linking parity enters F(-1), whereas
H=F^2 is even in x and its finite JVP coefficients are finite-type of the
claimed bounded order. Also H(1)=4^(number_of_components-1), retaining
the component-count distinction. This note does not claim a finite-type
failure for H.

But write either exact relation above as F_+=a F_-+b F_0. Then

             H_+ - a^2 H_- - b^2 H_0 = 2ab F_- F_0.      (Q)

For (M), a=x and b=p*x^(2-3m). For (S), a=x^4 and
b=p*x^(3m+2). Equation(Q) is an exact polynomial identity. It identifies
the additional product channel that is absent from the paper's linear
three-state coefficient recursion. In characteristic zero the cross term
does not disappear. Introducing tensor-product states may encode it, but
then one must prove new geometric collapse and escape relations for them;
the existing linear proof cannot simply be reused.

4. General no-repair lemma for normalization and positive powers.

Let T_m in Q(x) be Laurent polynomials for all m, and suppose

                    T_m=A(x)+B(x)x^(d m),

where A,B are NONZERO rational functions and d>0. Choose a common
Laurent denominator q with a=qA,b=qB nonzero Laurent polynomials. For
sufficiently large m their supports are disjoint, giving

 span(T_m)=span(a+b*x^(d m))-span(q)=d m+O(1).

Here span is largest minus smallest Laurent exponent; its additivity
under multiplication uses the nonzero extreme coefficients in a domain.
For any integer shifts e_m and any fixed positive integer s,

                 span((x^(e_m)T_m)^s)=s*d*m+O(1).        (R)

Thus arbitrary monomial reorientation and squaring cannot turn a genuine
two-channel growing family into a bounded-span family. A JVP bound D in
the basis{1,x}, p=x-x^-1, implies Laurent support in[-D,D+1], so(R)
also excludes any uniform JVP-degree bound.

Both channels matter: a single monomial-shifted channel could have fixed
span and would not support this argument. The next calculation checks
the two-channel premise rather than assuming it.

5. Application to the previously audited source-machine identity.

work/research36/operator_audit.txt derives, from the paper's DIRECT
canceling-clasp reductions and double-smoothing identification,

 T_m=C-p^2 R_m,
 R_m=[x^2(x^4+1)+(1-x^6)x^(4m)]/(x^2+1),

for the switched-trefoil unknot host. C is a fixed Laurent polynomial
from the three directly reducible signed states. This derivation does
not use the disputed G2 exceptional movie. It is NOT an independent full
PD computation of this entire host-machine family; the stated direct
clasp/double-smoothing geometric identifications are its explicit input.

The two channels of T_m are

 A=C-p^2*x^2(x^4+1)/(x^2+1),
 B=-p^2*(1-x^6)/(x^2+1).

B is nonzero. A cannot vanish for ANY Laurent C: at x=i the numerator
p^2*x^2(x^4+1) is8, so the rational term has a genuine pole, while a
Laurent polynomial has none there. Equivalently qA evaluated at i is-8
and qB at i is8 for q=x^2+1. Thus both channels are rigorously nonzero.

Consequently span(T_m)=4m+O(1). Whatever the actual integer linking
number ell_m of this state, F_m=x^(-3ell_m)T_m has the same span and
H_m=F_m^2 has span8m+O(1). The corrected reorientation invariant and its
finite-type square therefore still violate a uniform degree-compression
conclusion, conditional on exactly those source geometric identities.

Scope of the verdict: fixing the G2 orientation error with F or H is a
legitimate local change, but it leaves the full growing-channel problem
untouched. Any successful repaired proof must alter or independently
restrict the compression claim, not merely adjust normalization, square
the invariant, or enlarge its numerical cap by a fixed amount. The host
used here is a nonminimal unknot diagram; no new theorem specifically
restricted to minimal diagrams of hypothetical nontrivial Jones-one knots
is disproved by this example.
