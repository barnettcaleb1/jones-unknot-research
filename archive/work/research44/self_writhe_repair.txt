SELF-WRITHE NORMALIZATION REPAIR OF THE CARMI--COHEN DESCENT
Independent audit, 2026-11-01

Result: F itself fails the finite-type prerequisite. H=F^2 genuinely repairs
that prerequisite, retains component counting, and agrees with V^2 on knots.
However the proposed repair still cannot retain a twist-length-independent
signed-cube compression theorem: the previously audited literal machine has
unbounded Laurent span, which neither a framing monomial nor squaring removes.
This does not exclude every conceivable different nonlinear descent argument.

1. Conventions and orientation independence
-------------------------------------------
Let ell(L)=sum_{i<j}lk(L_i,L_j), x=A^-2, and p=x-x^-1. With the paper's
normalization V=(-A^3)^(-w_total)<L>, define

  F_L=(-A^3)^(-w_self)<L>=A^(6ell)V_L=x^(-3ell)V_L,
  H_L=F_L^2=x^(-6ell)V_L^2.

Here w_total=w_self+2ell. Reversing a component preserves all self-crossing
signs and leaves the unoriented bracket unchanged, so F is genuinely
independent of the component orientations. For a knot, ell=0 and F=V.

2. F fails already in coefficient level zero
---------------------------------------------
For a two-component link, V(1)=-2 and V(-1)=2, because its Laurent x
exponents are odd. Therefore

  F(1)=-2,                 F(-1)=2(-1)^ell,
  a_0(F)=-1+(-1)^ell,     b_0(F)=-1-(-1)^ell.

At an intercomponent crossing change, ell changes by one. On a cube of n
such crossing changes the alternating derivative of (-1)^ell is nonzero
(magnitude 2^n). Such cubes exist for arbitrarily large n on two-component
link shadows. Hence a_0 and b_0 are not finite-type invariants of ANY finite
order. The original level-zero finite-type setup cannot be used for F.

3. Squaring DOES restore finite-type layers
------------------------------------------
H(x) is even in x, since V has a fixed exponent parity and the exponent
-6ell is even. Write its unique finite JVP expansion

                  H(x)=a(p)+x b(p).

First, every coefficient of h^n in

                  H(e^h)=exp(-6ell h) V(e^h)^2

is finite type of order at most n. The coefficients of exp(-6ell h) are
powers of the order-one invariant ell; Jones exponential coefficients have
order at most their degree; products add finite-type orders. The latter
Jones fact also follows directly on links from

 V_+-V_-=(x^4-1)V_- + p x^2 V_0,

since both scalar factors vanish to first order at x=e^h=1, and repeated
crossing differences add orders of vanishing. The classical primary source
is Birman--Lin, Knot polynomials and Vassiliev's invariants:
https://www.math.columbia.edu/~jb/v-inv.-knot-poly.pdf

Set s(p)=sqrt(p^2+4), x_+(p)=(p+s(p))/2, and C(p)=H(x_+(p)). The change
h=arcsinh(p/2) has no constant term, so [p^n]C(p) still has order at most n.
The other root is x_-(p)=-x_+(-p). Evenness of H gives
H(x_-(p))=C(-p), and hence the exact formal identities

  b(p)=(C(p)-C(-p))/s(p),
  a(p)=(C(p)+C(-p))/2 - p b(p)/2.

The coefficient at p^n on either right side is a rational linear combination
of coefficients of C of orders at most n. Thus each actual integral
coefficient a_n(H),b_n(H) is finite type of order at most n. Moreover

                c_0(H)=H(1)=4^(number_of_components-1),

which still distinguishes component counts. On knots, V=1 gives H=1, and
H=1 would give V=1 since V(1)=1. These parts of a squared-invariant repair
are mathematically sound. It should not be rejected merely for using F^2.

4. The literal uniform-compression obstruction survives
--------------------------------------------------------
Use exactly the hypotheses/scope of work/research36/operator_audit.txt:
the source's direct cancelable-clasp reductions (G1) and ordinary double
smoothing (C4), in a fixed switched-trefoil unknot host. For one actual
locked clasp state the fixed-orientation Jones polynomial is

  T_m=C(x)-p^2 R_m,
  R_m=[x^2(x^4+1)+(1-x^6)x^(4m)]/(x^2+1),

where C is a fixed Laurent polynomial independent of m. No disputed
exceptional G2 move was used in that identity. Equivalently

  T_m=U(x)+W(x)x^(4m),
  U=C-p^2 x^2(x^4+1)/(x^2+1),
  W=-p^2(1-x^6)/(x^2+1).

Both channels are nonzero. W is visibly nonzero. U cannot vanish for a
Laurent polynomial C: the subtracted rational function has numerator
(x^2-1)^2(x^4+1), which equals8 at x=i, and hence a genuine pole at i.
A Laurent polynomial can have poles only at zero or infinity.

As m tends to positive infinity, the valuation at x=0 of T_m eventually
equals the fixed finite valuation of U. At infinity, deg W=6, so for all
sufficiently large m,

                  maxdeg_x T_m=4m+6.

Therefore span_x(T_m)=4m+O(1). If ell_m is its total linking number, then
the modified invariant is F_m=x^(-3ell_m)T_m. A monomial changes neither
Laurent span nor its growth. In the integral Laurent domain,

                  span_x(F_m^2)=2 span_x(T_m).

Finally a polynomial written as sum_{j<=D}(a_j+b_j x)p^j has x-exponents
contained in [-D,D+1], so its Laurent span is at most2D+1. Thus the JVP
degree of H_m=F_m^2 is unbounded as well. No fixed overhead or larger
constant cap can turn this family into a length-independent compression.

This supplies an orientation-independent obstruction to the same proposed
global prerequisite. Even granting that F repairs the exceptional picture's
component-orientation mismatch, and granting the successful finite-type
repair for F^2, the original uniform-compression/descent architecture still
does not follow. A different argument would have to abandon or materially
restrict that prerequisite.

Scope caveat retained: the exhibited host is a nonminimal unknot diagram.
This refutes a local package asserted for arbitrary fixed host shadows.
It does not refute a newly restricted theorem solely about minimal diagrams
of hypothetical nontrivial Jones-one knots; that new geometric theorem is
not supplied by the paper's existing relative-boundary proof.
