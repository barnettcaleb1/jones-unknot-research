PASS 44: SURGERY, LASAGNA CLASSES, AND ACTUAL HANDLE CANCELLATION
2026-11-01. Bounded primary-source audit; no priority claim.

Outcome
-------
No noncircular Jones-to-surgery bridge emerged. Recent four-dimensional
skein results provide useful invariants, but two exact geometric tests rule
out the proposed shortcuts. A simplest possible surface skein class need
not have a simple complement. Even the entire graded, filtered Lee lasagna
module of every integer trace can agree with the unknot's while its zero
surgery is different. These tests use actual surfaces and actual knots.

1. The complete topological endpoint, and the remaining gap
---------------------------------------------------------
Write X_0(K)=B^4 with a zero-framed two-handle attached along K, and
Y_0(K)=boundary X_0(K)=S^3_0(K). If Y_0(K) contains a nonseparating embedded
two-sphere, it has an S^1 x S^2 connected-sum factor. For a knot in S^3,
Property R in its summand form implies K=U. Conversely Y_0(U)=S^1 x S^2.

One primary source sufficient for this endpoint is Yi Ni, Null-homotopic
knots have Property R (2022), abstract and main theorem:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.10613
Its hypotheses hold here: K is null-homotopic in S^3, and a knot exterior
in S^3 has no S^1 x S^2 summand.

Thus the chain

 V_K=1 -> an actual nonseparating S^2 in Y_0(K) -> K=U

is valid only conditionally. The first arrow, without an independently
checkable intermediate criterion and a new realization theorem, is exactly
the original detection assertion in surgery language. It is not a weaker
research target merely because the conclusion describes a sphere.

2. Exact countertest: the simplest lasagna class does not simplify a
   surface complement
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Manolescu--Walker--Wedrich, Skein lasagna modules and handle decompositions,
Corollary 4.2 and Remark 4.3, prove over a field that the empty-boundary
module of W=S^1 x B^3 is one-dimensional, generated by the empty filling.
For an actual framed link K in B^3, the actual surface T_K=S^1 x K maps to
the scalar chi_(q=1)(KhR_N(K))=+/-N^(number of components). The trace includes
Koszul signs. At N=2, every knot therefore gives a nonzero scalar of
magnitude two over Q. This is explicitly specialization at q=1, not the
whole Jones polynomial.
https://arxiv.org/html/2206.04616#S4.SS1

Here is the geometric countertest, deduced directly from that computation.
The complement of a tubular neighborhood of T_K is

 W \ nu(T_K) = S^1 x (B^3 \ nu(K)),
 pi_1(W \ nu(T_K)) = Z x G_K.

For U this group is Z^2. For the trefoil it is nonabelian: its knot group
<a,b | a^2=b^3> surjects onto the nonabelian group S_3. Hence the two
surface complements, and therefore the ambient surface pairs, are not
diffeomorphic. Both surfaces are genuine embedded, normally framed tori;
no formal linear combination is used in their construction.

Consequently, "the actual surface class is a nonzero scalar multiple of
the empty filling" cannot force a simple complement or geometric handle
cancellation. The module is being used as a vector space here, not silently
equipped with a multiplication and a notion of units. The overall sign
convention has no bearing on this countertest.

3. Stronger countertest: equality of full filtered Lee trace modules
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Ren--Willis, Khovanov homology and exotic 4-manifolds, Proposition 1.12
(general form Proposition 5.3), state that framed-concordant attaching
links yield isomorphic tri-graded, quantum-filtered Lee skein lasagna
modules of their two-handlebodies. Their generator classes are identified
by the concordance. Section 1.2 also defines shake genus as the minimum
genus of a generator in an integer trace.
https://arxiv.org/html/2402.10452#S1.SS3
https://arxiv.org/html/2402.10452#S1.SS2

Apply this to the square knot R=trefoil # mirror(trefoil). It is ribbon,
so concordant to U. It is nontrivial, for example because its determinant
is 3*3=9. A concordance identifies the zero framings and extends to equal
integer framings n. Therefore, for every integer n,

 S_0^Lee(X_n(R)) ~= S_0^Lee(X_n(U))

with the homological Z-grading, quantum Z/4-grading, H_2-class grading,
and full quantum filtration retained, not merely equal Euler
characteristics or equal ranks. Nonetheless Y_0(R) has no nonseparating
sphere by Ni's stronger summand theorem in section 1. There is also a
direct verification for this particular R: the square knot is fibered
with genus-two fiber; zero surgery caps that fiber and extends its
monodromy, giving a closed genus-two surface mapping torus. Its universal
cover is the product of the contractible universal cover of the fiber
with R, so it is aspherical and cannot contain a nonseparating sphere.

There is even an actual generator sphere INSIDE X_0(R): cap a slice disk
for R with the two-handle core. Its self-intersection is zero. This does
not supply a sphere in the BOUNDARY to which a three-handle can attach.
Thus replacing the required boundary sphere by zero shake genus, or by
the corresponding minimal-genus interior representative, is invalid.

This does not assert that R has Jones polynomial one, that its Khovanov
lasagna module agrees with U's, or that a Lee module determines a
Khovanov module. It isolates an actual failure of the proposed intermediate
rigidity condition before making any Jones-to-module inference.

4. Why the available handle formulas do not repair the chain
----------------------------------------------------------
The handle formula of Manolescu--Walker--Wedrich, Theorem 3.10, uses cabled
homology and cobordism maps. Three-handle relations are imposed along
specified, already-existing attaching spheres. The formula is not a
converse producing a sphere when a linear relation happens to hold.
An equality in a skein quotient is not an isotopy or handle cancellation.

Ren--Willis obtain genuine smooth-topological information from lasagna
filtrations, but do not extract these filtrations from the ordinary Jones
polynomial. They expressly distinguish their rank comparison from a
presently constructed Khovanov-lasagna-to-Lee-lasagna spectral sequence.

The newer Montague--Sullivan handle-decomposition preprint likewise uses
cabled homology and an additional relation, rather than only the ordinary
Jones polynomial; its abstract supplies no scalar-to-handle theorem:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29452

Decision
--------
Stop this route in its present form. The required Jones-to-geometric-sphere
bridge remains unproved and equivalent to detection when stated directly.
The natural weaker substitutes checked here are actually false. A viable
new version would have to retain extra relative or boundary data, prove
that the ordinary Jones scalar determines that data for actual knots, and
then prove a geometric realization statement. No such independent bridge
was located in this bounded pass.
