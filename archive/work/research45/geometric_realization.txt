PASS 45: CAN AN ACTUAL SURGERY GADGET REALIZE A FORMAL CANCELLATION?
2026-11-01. Bounded research deduction; no priority claim.

Outcome
-------
No Jones-one knot candidate was constructed. A specific proposed route
through Kirby coloring fails at a small, exact realization test: no actual
finite annular link, even after normalizing by its own nonzero evaluation,
can act as the positive or negative surgery twist on just the two even
channels 0 and 2. The required formal coefficient has a pole at A=1.
There is also a quantitative obstruction for rational formal gadgets:
matching N+1 consecutive channels requires annular degree at least N.

Scope is crucial. This is an obstruction to replacing a surgery color by
a fixed finite link inserted around arbitrary external strands. It does
not prohibit actual Dehn surgery, a full-twist braid drawn on a specified
number of strands, or a cancellation tailored to one particular exterior.

1. The proposed complete route and the first gate
------------------------------------------------
The construction under examination would convert a formal quantum
identity into surgery circles or ribbon handles, use handle slides to
absorb its signed summands, and then remove those circles to leave one
ordinary knot. A successful construction would have to verify all of:

 (a) one actual framed diagram, not a sum of diagrams, realizes the needed
     operation in the contexts where the quantum identity is used;
 (b) the surgeries return S^3 and the surviving diagram has one component;
 (c) the exact ordinary Jones polynomial is one;
 (d) a separate geometric invariant certifies nontriviality.

The universal Kirby-color implementation of (a) fails below. Therefore
there is no reason to pursue (b)--(d) for this implementation. This is not
a claim that every possible contextual realization must fail.

Duplicate check: research27/tangle_realization.txt already warns that
Kirby colors and clasper sums are not single diagrams. The finite-channel
integrality obstruction proved here makes that warning precise for the
surgery-twist color. It is separate from the prior finite-type/clasper
approximation results and from the braid agent's additive-PI versus
multiplicative-braid-word obstruction.

2. Primary setup and exact formulas
----------------------------------
Use the empty-link-normalized Kauffman bracket. The annular skein module
is Z[A,A^-1][z]. Let e_0=1, e_1=z and e_i=z e_(i-1)-e_(i-2). Set

 lambda_i = -A^(2i+2)-A^(-2i-2),
 theta_i  = (-1)^i A^(i(i+2)).

For the Hopf pairing, any polynomial f satisfies

 <f(z),e_i> = f(lambda_i)<e_i>.

A full positive twist has eigenvalue theta_i on e_i. Masbaum's paper
states these as equations (2)--(4), printed p.539, and gives Habiro's
infinite surgery-twist expansion in Theorem 1.1, p.540. Its introduction
explicitly distinguishes this completed series from a finite skein.

Primary source: G. Masbaum, Skein-theoretical derivation of some formulas
of Habiro, Algebraic & Geometric Topology 3 (2003), 537--556:
https://msp.org/agt/2003/3-1/agt-v3-n1-p17-p.pdf
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0306345

The even-only sector suffices for the obstruction. No promotion of
ordinary Jones data to colored data is being assumed: a universal
annular operation on all ordinary parallel-strand contexts would, by
linearity, also act on the e_i combinations of those contexts.

3. An actual finite annular link fails already on channels 0 and 2
----------------------------------------------------------------
Let F(A,z) be the skein polynomial of any fixed finite framed annular
link. Polynomial subtraction gives

 lambda_2-lambda_0 divides F(A,lambda_2)-F(A,lambda_0)

in Z[A,A^-1]. Direct factorization gives

 lambda_2-lambda_0 = -A^-6 (A^4-1)^2(A^4+1),
 theta_2-theta_0   = A^8-1 = (A^4-1)(A^4+1).

Consequently, no F can satisfy F(lambda_0)=1 and
F(lambda_2)=A^8. More generally, suppose one normalizes by a factor
g(A), regular and nonzero at A=1, and asks

 F(lambda_0)=g(A),       F(lambda_2)=g(A) A^8.

The first difference has a zero of order at least two at A=1, while the
required difference g(A)(A^8-1) has order one. This is impossible.
The same argument works for A^-8 and for any nonzero integer power
of the twist, since A^(8r)-1 has a simple zero at A=1.

Equivalently, there is a direct differential proof. For every i,
lambda_i(1)=-2 and lambda_i'(1)=0. Therefore the derivative at A=1 of
F(A,lambda_i(A)) is F_A(1,-2), independent of i, whenever the coefficients
of F are regular there. The twist derivatives in channels 0 and 2 are
0 and +/-8. Multiplication by the same regular nonzero normalization
cannot remove that difference. Meridian channels separate only at
second order, whereas these twist channels already separate at first
order. This is a local explanation of the required pole.

For an actual annular link, the natural g=F(lambda_0) is its ordinary
closure bracket. At A=1 this equals a signed power of two determined
by the number of components, so it is nonzero. Thus normalization by
the gadget's own evaluation does not escape the obstruction.

An explicit small check uses the actual meridian unknot F=z. Its
normalized channel-2 eigenvalue is

 lambda_2/lambda_0 = A^4-1+A^-4.

Its derivative at A=1 is zero, whereas the required A^8 has derivative
eight. The general argument proves that replacing this circle by any
more complicated finite annular link cannot repair the discrepancy.

The formal degree-one interpolant instead has coefficient

 (A^8-1)/(lambda_2-lambda_0) = -A^6/(A^4-1).

This is exactly the first nonconstant coefficient in Habiro's expansion,
written in Masbaum's A convention. The pole is the obstruction, not a
missing clever isotopy. Clearing the denominator changes the required
vacuum normalization into something vanishing at A=1; such a scalar is
not the closure evaluation of an actual uncolored finite link.

4. Even with rational coefficients the required annular degree grows
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Allow F in Q(A)[z], so the preceding pole is permitted. Suppose F matches
theta_i (or theta_i^-1) at lambda_i for i=0,...,N. Its Nth divided
difference is

 sum_(i=0)^N theta_i^(+/-1) /
                    product_(j!=i)(lambda_i-lambda_j).

At A tending to infinity, the denominator in summand i has degree

 i^2-i+N^2+3N.

For positive twist the summand degrees are 3i-N^2-3N, with a unique
maximum at i=N. For negative twist they are -2i^2-i-N^2-3N, with a
unique maximum at i=0. Thus the divided difference is nonzero, and
deg_z F >= N. This is an exact degree comparison, not a numerical limit.

Restricting to even channels 0,2,...,2N gives the same lower bound.
The corresponding summand degrees are

  2i^2+6i-2N^2-4N          (positive twist),
 -6i^2-2i-2N^2-4N          (negative twist).

Again each has a unique maximum. Consequently no finite rational
annular skein implements either twist on every even channel. This also
explains why the completed surgery element cannot be replaced by a
single finite polynomial. Truncations can act on a bounded sector but
do not become actual uncolored links merely because their action is
well-defined there.

5. What is and is not ruled out
-------------------------------
Actual surgery is not contradicted: it changes the ambient gluing, rather
than replacing its effect by the bracket of one finite uncolored link in
the original solid torus. Drawing a full twist directly is also allowed:
that is an open braid operation on a specified strand set, not the fixed
closed annular insertion treated here. Root-of-unity quotients can truncate
the theory; that does not supply an identity of ordinary Laurent polynomials.

No new actual unknot-preserving knot operation, no exact signed-sum
realization, and no nontriviality witness emerged. A viable alternative
would have to realize the desired identity in its particular marked
exterior without invoking a universal finite surgery color. That
context-specific task remains open in this pass.
