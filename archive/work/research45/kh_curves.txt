PASS 45: IMMERSED CURVES, FULL JONES CONTRIBUTIONS, AND REALIZABILITY
2026-11-01. Bounded audit; no generic unknot-detection theorem is claimed.

1. Exact full chain and conventions
-----------------------------------
Use a pointed Conway tangle (two arcs in B^3, four boundary ends, a distinguished
end). Over F_2, its reduced Khovanov invariant is a bigraded multicurve in the
four-punctured sphere. Its components are rational curves r_n(s) or special
curves s_(2n)(s), with slopes in Q union infinity, and the specified rank-one
local systems. This component classification is NOT a classification of which
collections with which relative grading shifts arise from actual tangles.

For a rational filling Q, use its reduced BN arc a(Q), reflected by the gluing
map. The gluing theorem identifies

       Kh_red(T union Q; F_2) = HF(m(a(Q)), Kh_tilde(T)).

This is the full bigraded pairing, not merely an ungraded intersection count.
Write h for homological degree and j for the ordinary integral quantum degree.
Its graded Euler sum is sum(-1)^h Q^j dim, giving the normalized Jones polynomial
after the usual variable/sign convention. The delta convention in KWZ2022 is
delta=j/2-h; this differs by a sign and factor from some other papers.

If the relevant pairing has total dimension one, then rational reduced Kh also
has rank one and Kronheimer--Mrowka implies that the knot is U. A theorem saying
that a Jones-one rational closure of ANY actual Conway tangle has only one Floer
generator would therefore prove the conjecture. Conversely every knot admits
such a decomposition (take a crossing ball), so that assertion is equivalent to
the conjecture when stated without further geometric restrictions. The useful
question is what additional, proved realizability condition supplies it.

2. A special component is NOT invisible to every full Jones pairing
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The published KWZ2022 Lemma2.5, p.231, supplies an exact counterexample to
that tempting claim. For the special curve s_(4n)(infinity), n>=1, its pairing
with a(0) has graded Euler polynomial, up to one signed quantum monomial,

 E_n(Q)=Q^(-4n-1)(1+Q^4)(1-Q^6) sum_{i=0}^{n-1} Q^(8i).

This is nonzero. For n=1 the four bigrades are
  (h,j)=(-2,-5),(0,-1),(1,1),(3,5),
so E_1=Q^-5+Q^-1-Q-Q^5. Every class has delta=-1/2.

Three different operations must be distinguished:
  * Full quantum Euler polynomial: E_n(Q) is nonzero.
  * Forget the quantum grading: E_n(1)=0.
  * Determinant specialization: E_n(i)=-4ni, so its magnitude is4n.
Thus even the delta-thin determinant contribution is nonzero. It is incorrect
to describe the ordinary Euler cancellation at Q=1 as vanishing of the
delta-Euler characteristic. Pairing a rational arc of the SAME slope with the
special curve can instead be geometrically disjoint and have zero Floer
homology; this does not say that other rational fillings are invisible.

The formula covers the published length4n convention. Do not silently replace
it by a theorem that every actual special curve has length divisible by four:
that divisibility was conjectural in the cited 2022 source. The general
geography theorem has s_(2n), not automatically s_(4n).

3. What actual geometry already refutes, and what remains unrealized
-------------------------------------------------------------------
An unknot infinity closure does NOT force quantum-parity purity of the special
sector. Watson's actual strong-inversion quotient of the figure-eight knot has
two quantum grades containing both homological parities. Its special-sector
Euler polynomial is nevertheless nonzero. This was checked directly from his
Section7, printed p.27, in work/proof12/homology/multicurve_findings.txt:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.1085

The prior one-special obstruction is more substantial: with one r_1 component
and exactly one standard s_(4n)(infinity), an actual unknot anchor plus a
Jones-unlink longitude filling is excluded by the reduced BN spectral sequence.
Its proof used the precise sector supports and an unavoidable top class, not
positivity of all intersections. Scope and independent audits are recorded in
work/proof12/homology/one_special_bn_obstruction.txt. It is not a published
generic theorem and does not cover arbitrary special components or arbitrary
knots.

The general obstruction is still missing. Four shifted special copies have
Euler multiplier 1-Q^2-1+Q^2=0 under EVERY rational pairing. Our prior formal
object was lifted through the BN cone, the full specified A-infinity extension,
integral signs, and all rational pairing functors. That is recorded in
work/proof13/homology/algebraic_realization.txt. It is NOT a classical tangle.
Realizing its exact grading data by a classical diagram would produce a
Jones-one nontrivial knot: one rational closure has Jones polynomial one but
pairing rank17. Its infinity closure has rank one and would be the actual
unknot. Hence calling this model 'realizable by geography' would assume the
very missing geometric step. The checked primary sources provide neither
this realization nor an exclusion theorem for it.

4. A separate ACTUAL all-rational-filling test of stronger rigidity
------------------------------------------------------------------
There is a simple construction showing that even the WHOLE skein pair of an
actual tangle cannot generally recover its graded curves.

Take the rational zero tangle and tie a zero-framed local knot J into one of
its arcs. Call the resulting actual tangle T_J. In the normalized skein module,

                  [T_J]=V_J(A^-4)[0].

For every rational closure, the resulting link is the corresponding rational
closure of[0], with J locally connected-summed into its designated component.
Its Jones polynomial is the corresponding rational-link polynomial times V_J.
Thus whenever V_J=V_K, T_J and T_K have the SAME entire bracket pair and the
same Jones polynomial for EVERY rational filling.

Use the established actual pair5_1 and the appropriate chirality of10_132.
Their reduced rational Kh ranks are5 and11. A rational cap closing[0] to one
circle closes T_J and T_K to precisely J and K, so their graded multicurve
invariants cannot agree in a theory recovering those homologies. The same
distinction holds overF_2 for this pair (unreduced rational ranks6,12 and
respectively2,5 integral Z/2 summands give reducedF_2 ranks5,11).
This is an actual geometric test, not a formal direct-sum object or a search.
It rules out 'same all-filling Jones data implies same curves.'

The local-knot construction does NOT have an unknot anchor: the indicated
cap closes to J or K. It therefore does not refute the narrower unit-fiber
or anchored-realizability conjecture. The ordinary Jones coincidence and
unreduced Kh distinction are in Bar-Natan's original Examples21--23:
https://www.math.toronto.edu/~drorbn/papers/Categorification/Categorification.pdf
The retained reduced/integral data and conventions were previously checked in
work/research27/homology_global.txt; no new table scan is used here.

5. Assessment and stop condition
--------------------------------
There is real geometric information here: per-component shapes, exact graded
pairings, and restricted realizability obstructions. There is no justified
step from a unit Jones pairing to a single Floer generator in general. The
classification does not control the relative shifts of distinct components,
and the algebraic extension conditions already tested do not eliminate the
four-special cancellation. Further work on this route should require a new
classical-realization theorem, rather than another objectwise algebra check.
