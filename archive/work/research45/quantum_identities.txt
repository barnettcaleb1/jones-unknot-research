PASS45: EXACT QUANTUM IDENTITIES VERSUS ACTUAL GEOMETRIC OPERATIONS

Outcome. No Jones-one knot or generic braid kernel was constructed.
There are two exact obstructions to the most direct realization recipes:
  (1) no single ordinary tangle represents a nonzero scalar multiple of
      a Jones--Wenzl projector of width at least two;
  (2) even the actual generic TL3 braid image contains a free group while
      its ambient algebra satisfies the degree-four standard identity.
Thus additive polynomial identities do not supply multiplicative braid
relations. These statements do NOT exclude a special difference of two
actual tangles, or a genuinely geometric surgery construction.

1. Complete counterexample endpoints, before proposing a realization.

A sufficient direct construction would give ONE actual framed tangle T,
an actual closing context C, and all three of:
  - the closure C(identity) is a certified unknot;
  - its normalized whole Laurent Jones value equals that of C(T);
  - C(T) is a knot and has an independent nontriviality certificate.
A signed sum of diagrams is not T. A nontrivial braid word is not an
independent nontriviality certificate for its knot closure.

Alternatively, an actual noncentral braid in the FULL generic TL kernel
does suffice: Ito's normal-subgroup theorem supplies nontrivial knot
closures with the same Jones polynomial as an unknot anchor. If only
one irreducible block is killed, the missing blocks still have to be
killed; Long's intersection lemma can help only with independently
nontrivial normal kernels. None is manufactured just by an additive PI.
Primary endpoint: Ito, arXiv1402.2028, Theorem1 and Corollaries1--2.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.2028

2. A geometric nonvanishing lemma.

Over R=Z[A,A^-1], no actual uncolored framed tangle in a ball has
identically zero Kauffman bracket skein vector. Close all its endpoints
by actual exterior arcs. This produces an actual nonempty link L, whose
Jones value at t=1 is (-2)^(components(L)-1), hence nonzero. Its raw
bracket differs by a framing unit and, if using empty-link normalization,
one extra delta=-A^2-A^-2. Neither vanishes at A=1. A zero vector would
make every such closure zero, a contradiction.

The same statement applies after composing with any actual cap/cup
tangle: the result remains an actual tangle or link. Closed components
do not provide an escape.

COROLLARY. For n>=2, let f_n be the generic Jones--Wenzl projector.
There is no actual uncolored n-to-n tangle T and nonzero rational scalar
c(A) for which [T]=c(A) f_n in TL_n(Q(A)).

Proof: f_n e_i=0 for each adjacent cup-cap diagram e_i. Thus the actual
composition T e_i would have zero skein vector, contradicting the lemma.
The argument is an identity over Q(A); clearing denominators still gives
a zero Laurent closure, so poles or a zero of c at A=1 do not help.

In particular, denominator clearing is insufficient. For n=2,
f_2=I-delta^-1 e and delta*f_2=delta I-e is integral, yet
(delta I-e)e=0, so this integral element is not a single actual tangle.

An exact quantum antisymmetrizer that is zero on the chosen tensor power
likewise cannot be installed as a single actual uncolored tangle. A
colored edge or a formal projector box is additional categorical data,
not a geometric replacement by an ordinary collection of strands.

3. An exact polynomial identity in an algebra with faithful braid gates.

The Amitsur--Levitzki identity for 2x2 matrices is

 S_4(X1,X2,X3,X4)=sum_(pi in S4) sign(pi) X_pi1 X_pi2 X_pi3 X_pi4=0.

It equates two sums of twelve products, not two individual products.
The fundamental U_q(sl2) color being two-dimensional does not mean an
n-strand braid is a 2x2 operator: it acts on V_2^tensor n, with growing
Temperley--Lieb multiplicity blocks. Applying S_4 directly to an
arbitrary larger braid block would already be an error.

Even at width three, where S_4 really DOES hold on the entire generic
TL algebra, it gives no automatic braid relation. Here is an explicit
actual-braid test with a proof, not a word search.

Use delta=-A^2-A^-2 and
 e1=[[delta,1],[0,0]], e2=[[0,0],[1,delta]],
 rho(sigma_i)=A I+A^-1 e_i.
Together with chi(e_i)=0, chi(I)=1 this realizes

                 TL3(Q(A)) = Q(A) direct-sum M2(Q(A)).

To check injectivity, e1,e2,e1e2,e2e1 are linearly independent matrices
because delta^2-1 is nonzero; chi then separates the remaining identity.
Both summands satisfy S_4, so TL3 does too.

Nevertheless the two actual braid images

                       rho(sigma_1^3), rho(sigma_2^3)

freely generate a group. Suppose a word in these images is generically
the identity. The character chi gives A^(3s)=1, where s is the sum of
the exponents of the two abstract generators, so s=0.
At zeta=exp(pi*i/4), divide each Artin generator by zeta and conjugate
by D=diag(1,i). Their two-dimensional matrices become respectively

 U=[[1,1],[0,1]],  L^-1=[[1,0],[-1,1]].

Thus the hypothesized word specializes to a relation in U^3 and L^-3;
the scalar zeta^(3s) is exactly1, not an ignored projective factor.

These matrices freely generate by elementary ping-pong on P1(R):
take X={|z|>1}, Y={|z|<1}. Every nonzero power of U^3 maps Y into X,
and every nonzero power of L^-3 maps X into Y. Hence no nonempty reduced
word is the identity. This proves the claimed generic freeness.

Therefore an EXACT dimension identity already coexists with completely
faithful actual geometric gates inside the SAME small TL algebra. The
failure to obtain a braid relation is substantive, not a missing choice
of coefficient normalization.

More generally GL_d(Q), d>=2, has no nontrivial group law because it
contains these free matrices (and their block-diagonal embeddings).
Consequently there is no universal composition/inversion recipe that
converts a matrix polynomial identity into a nontrivial word relation
for arbitrary invertible substitutions. This leaves specially constrained
substitutions open, and does not assert generic TL_n braid faithfulness.

4. What a geometric realization would still have to supply.

An alternating sum over surgery sublinks is an element of the free
abelian group on actual links. Even an exact vanishing of its quantum
image only equates the two signed collections. To isolate a single
nontrivial knot from that equation, every other term must be identified
geometrically and their complete normalized invariants combined exactly.
An associated-graded clasper/IHX identity by itself controls only the
specified finite-type order, not every higher correction of one fixed
surgery. The exact extra realization theorem is the missing input.

Habiro's central invariant is constrained to an integral completion;
membership in its center, or having a factor killed by the fundamental
color, is not a surjectivity theorem onto invariants of single knots.
The prior pass28 examples distinguish these issues explicitly, including
actual finite-order realizations whose proposed exact limit is ruled out
by a non-Laurent higher-color quotient. This note does not repeat them
as a new construction.

Stopping verdict. The direct projector-gadget and universal PI-to-word
recipes are rigorously excluded. A specific surgery cube whose other
vertices are certified unknots, together with an exact all-orders quantum
identity for that ENTIRE cube, would evade these objections and would be
worth testing. Such a cube was not obtained here. Neither the projector
identity, the matrix PI, nor a central invisible term alone supplies it.
