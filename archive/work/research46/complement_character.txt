PASS 46 — AN ACTUAL TEST OF JONES-TO-PERIPHERAL TRANSFER

Outcome: no new proof bridge. A concrete same-Jones countertest rules out
reconstructing even the peripheral SL2 character equation from the whole
ordinary Jones polynomial. The newer bottom-tangle construction makes
precise which operation loses that information. This is an audit of a
proposed mechanism, not an unknot counterexample or a novelty claim.

Duplicate boundary: research44/algebraic_units.txt already explains that

1. Full conditional endpoint and its exact missing input.

Let A_K(L,M) be the peripheral A-polynomial, with the ubiquitous abelian
factor removed. Dunfield--Garoufalidis prove that A_K is nontrivial for
every nontrivial knot in S^3. Thus the proposed chain would be

 V_K=1 -> the peripheral character curve has only the unknot component
       -> A_K=1 -> K=U.

The first arrow is unproved. Restricted to the unit Jones fiber it has
exactly the strength of Jones unknot detection, since an actual unknot
has that peripheral curve. A claim that the entire peripheral equation
is determined by V would be genuinely stronger and independently
testable. It is false by the following exact example.

Primary endpoint: Dunfield--Garoufalidis, Non-triviality of the A-polynomial
for knots in S^3, AGT4 (2004),1145--1153:
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405353
Its colored-Jones corollary is explicitly conditional; it does not
supply the missing ordinary-Jones arrow.

2. An actual peripheral countertest, with normalization-safe certificate.

Use the chirality choices specified by the actual braids
 K = closure of [-1,-1,-1,-1,-1] in B2,
 L = closure of [1,1,1,-2,-1,-1,-2,-3,2,-3,-3] in B4.
These are 5_1 and 10_132 and the earlier exact TL calculation gives

 V_K(q)=V_L(q)=q^-2+q^-4-q^-5+q^-6-q^-7.

They also have the SAME Alexander polynomial, so this example is not an
Alexander-polynomial shortcut. Their peripheral equations differ:
Knot Atlas records A_5_1=L+M^10, while its A_10_132 entry has L-width8.
The latter has nonzero constant term -M^20 and leading term L^8 M^6.
The widths 1 versus8 cannot be repaired by a Laurent unit, mirror
chirality, or simultaneous inversion of meridian/longitude eigenvalues.
Restoring the common abelian factor increases both widths by one and
still distinguishes them.

The accompanying small exact-integer checker verifies two more facts
about the full transcribed table entry:
 A_10_132(L,1)=(L-1)^3(L+1)^5,
 A_10_132(-1024,2)=-867153500544475948769280000 !=0,
whereas A_5_1(-1024,2)=0.
This verifies transcription/algebra, not an independent computation of
the character variety. No numerical hyperbolic certification is needed
for this table-based test. The source's peripheral meridian/longitude
marking is retained; arbitrary SL2(Z) changes are not allowed in a knot
exterior comparison.

3. What the newer bottom-tangle construction actually retains.

Murakami--van der Veen construct a quantum representation object from
all bottom-tangle relations of a plat presentation. Their Theorem5
identifies its quantum-character part with the exterior skein module.
In Section4.6, the longitude operator x_l followed by the puncture-
removing counit produces the ordinary polynomial; powers x_l^n give
zero-framed parallel-link evaluations. Chebyshev combinations produce
colored polynomials. Section4.8 forms the peripheral ideal as the
kernel of the boundary quantum-torus action on the empty skein. Such
relations yield recurrences after counit evaluation. The construction
therefore goes from exterior relations to closure evaluations. It does
not reconstruct the relations from one evaluation.

Primary source (version checked):
https://arxiv.org/html/2402.06891v2
Sections4.2,4.6--4.8, especially Theorem5 and Propositions4.3--4.4.
No full independent audit of that paper's proofs is claimed here.

The precise attempted reversal would be: equality of the fundamental
longitude counit as a Laurent polynomial forces equality of the radical
peripheral ideals after classical specialization. The actual pair in
section2 falsifies that universal reversal. Adding arbitrary meridional
scalars to the same one-color evaluation does not fix it. Introducing
longitude powers changes the supplied data to parallel/colored values,
whose independence from the ordinary polynomial has already been
verified for this same pair in pass12.

4. Stop condition.

This source provides a concrete model of the missing transfer, but no
new evidence that it holds on V=1. A viable future claim would have to
prove a special geometric condition on the ideal BEFORE the counit is
applied, and derive that condition from the ordinary polynomial without
using longitude powers or complete exterior relations as extra input.
No such condition emerged in this bounded pass. Do not extend this into
another generic AJ or scalar-to-module reconstruction program merely
because its terminal unknot detector is established.
