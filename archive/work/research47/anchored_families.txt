PASS 47 — ACTUAL COUPLED-TWIST FAMILIES WITH AN UNKNOT ANCHOR
Bounded primary-source test. No proof or counterexample to Jones detection.

Target and decision.
The complete constructive endpoint is elementary: an actual family K_q
with K_0=U, exact V_(K_q)=V_(K_0) for all q, and a geometric certificate
K_q!=U for one q supplies a counterexample. Both identities and topology
must apply to the SAME marked construction. This pass found a new-to-our-
notes 2026 coupled-twist family, but its determinant excludes the anchor.
A new annulus theorem provides topology but not polynomial invariance.
Neither is a candidate. No assertion is made that this bounded search
classifies all Jones-preserving constructions.

1. A fresh three-axis family is excluded before any diagram search.
Yuki Matsushima's 2026 conference extended abstract, On infinite families
of knots with the same Jones polynomial (Japanese), Theorem2.1, presents
M(1,q,-q), q positive, as an infinite equal-Jones family, distinguished
using the Kauffman polynomial. This is a different stated three-axis
construction from the earlier mirrored-three-braid Watson calculation.
Remark2.2 identifies its partial knot as 4_1: the diagrams are symmetric
unions 4_1 union mirror(4_1)(infinity,q,1,-q). 

Lamm's determinant theorem for symmetric unions gives

 det M(1,q,-q) = det(4_1)^2 = 25.

Thus every member of that reported family has nonunit
Jones polynomial; no exceptional parameter can create an unknot anchor.
This exclusion does not depend on accepting the extended abstract's
full Jones or Kauffman calculation. Its stated symmetric-union presentation
and partial-knot identification suffice. In particular, taking q=0 or
letting a pair of twist numbers cancel does not remove the partial knot.

2. Exact scope of the tempting one-axis symmetric-union route.
Tanaka's formula makes a GEOMETRICALLY AMPHICHIRAL one-axis symmetric
union of U have Jones1. Köse proves that every such knot is U; more
generally an amphichiral one-axis symmetric union with partial J is
J#mirror(J). This closes that proposed construction by actual topology,
using cosmetic surgery/branched-cover arguments, not by assuming Jones
detection. It was already cited in our pass38 and is not fresh progress.

Important boundary: Jones reciprocity V(t)=V(t^-1) does not imply
geometric amphichirality. Nor does constancy along a twist family make
K_q isotopic to K_-q. Therefore Köse's theorem does NOT automatically
exclude an arbitrary hypothetical Jones-constant one-axis family with
partial unknot. We found no legitimate replacement for that geometric
hypothesis. The known KT partial-knot inverse problem is also already
covered by passes12 and38 and is not reopened here.

Primary: Köse, On amphichirality of symmetric unions, TheoremsA,B:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.08765

3. What the genuinely newer annulus theorem would certify.
Scott Taylor, Closures of 1-tangles and annulus twists, arXiv2506.11226v1,
Theorem1.3, assumes K_0 is U and A is an UNKNOTTED, SUFFICIENTLY
INCOMPRESSIBLE twisting annulus with |A intersection K_0|=1. Then at most
one nonzero twist q gives U, and any such q is +1 or -1. Consequently
all |q|>=2 members are certified nontrivial under these hypotheses.

Definition5.1 is essential: A lies in a sphere P, neither boundary
component bounds a disk disjoint from K, and every compressing disk for
P minus K has boundary meeting partial A more than twice. The proof
translates the annulus operation into changing an arc in a solid torus.
This is a real nontriviality certificate for an explicitly marked
construction, not just a numerical invariant obstruction.

Primary full text, Definition5.1 and proof of Theorem1.3:
https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11226v1
The paper contains no Jones-preservation theorem. No source found in this
bounded check supplied an exact Jones identity for its U-anchored class.
Thus it does not presently complete the constructive endpoint above.
It becomes actionable only with an actual annulus diagram meeting those
hypotheses and an independent exact V_(K_2)=1 calculation (or a genuine
whole-family identity). No such diagram/certificate was produced here.

4. Why surgery-preserving annulus examples do not fill that gap.
The familiar Osoinach-type operation preserves a specified surgery
manifold under its additional hypotheses; it does not generally preserve
Jones. Indeed Abe--Tagami's explicit annulus-twist pair starting at6_3
is distinguished by Jones while its0-surgeries agree:
https://www.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/mrl/2016/0023/0002/MRL-2016-0023-0002-a001.pdf
proof of Theorem1.6 and AppendixB.

Moreover, any actual orbit preserving0-surgery and containing U is
entirely U: every member has0-surgery S1xS2, and Gabai's PropertyR
characterizes U by precisely that surgery. This observation does not
apply to arbitrary annulus twists, including Taylor's general setup;
one must establish surgery preservation, not infer it from the word
'annulus'. The known surgery-preserving families therefore cannot supply
an unknot-anchored escape, and the broader families still lack the Jones
identity. The prior unbounded-bridge annulus examples in research25 are
not a solution to this mismatch.

Stop. We have a decisive determinant rejection for a newly located
actual coupled-twist family, and a precise scope for a newer topology
certificate. There is no justified new U-anchored Jones-preserving family
in this pass. Do not turn these source checks into another generic
conditional program or launch an unmarked annulus scan.
