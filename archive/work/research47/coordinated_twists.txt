PASS 47: TWO OPPOSITE DISK TWISTS — AN ACTUAL CHANNEL-CANCELLATION TEST
2026-11-01. Research deduction and countertest; no novelty claim.

Result
------
The single-disk integral argument does NOT extend by multiplying quantum
dimensions for two separated disks. An actual unknot supplies a countertest:
both disks have coherent winding two, both labels 0 and 2 survive, and all
opposite twists give the unknot. More precisely, the actual knot has a
nonintegral coefficient in the generic tensor Chebyshev basis of the
complement of the two unlink axes. The denominator is a quantum dimension.

This is not a counterexample to Jones detection. The two twists cancel
geometrically. Its value is to rule out a proposed algebraic exclusion of
higher channels and identify the exact integral lattice that it overlooks.
No nontrivial Jones-one knot is produced by this pass.

1. Actual marked construction and topology
------------------------------------------
Take the standard closure of the two-strand braid sigma_1. It is an unknot.
Place two transverse disks across its coherent braid tube at two separate
heights, one before and one after the crossing. The disk interiors are
disjoint, and their boundary circles C_1,C_2 are an unlink disjoint from the
knot. Each disk meets the knot twice, with the same intersection sign, so
w_1=w_2=2. Keep these disks marked.

Insert m and n full twists at the two disks. The resulting actual knot is

 K_(m,n) = closure(sigma_1^(2m) sigma_1 sigma_1^(2n))
         = T(2,2(m+n)+1).

The odd exponent guarantees one component for every integer m,n. On the
opposite-rate line (m,n)=(k,-k), the word equals sigma_1 in B_2, so every
K_(k,-k) is geometrically the unknot. This is a literal braid cancellation,
not an inference from its Jones polynomial. On either coordinate axis the
family is generally nontrivial; the joint cancellation cannot be assigned
to vanishing of all higher channels at either disk separately.

Equivalently, let y be the unknot running once around each component of the
two-component unlink, the connected sum of its two meridian cores. The
initial knot is the (2,1) cable of y. This gives the same marked picture.

2. Exact TL_2 calculation, with framings
----------------------------------------
Set R=Z[A,A^-1], delta=-A^2-A^-2 and Delta_2=A^4+1+A^-4.
In TL_2 use e^2=delta e and b=rho(sigma_1)=A I+A^-1 e.
Its inverse is A^-1 I+A e. Over Q(A), the two projectors are

 E_0=e/delta,                 E_2=I-e/delta,
 b E_0=-A^-3 E_0,            b E_2=A E_2.

Their unreduced ordinary closure values are 1 and Delta_2. Therefore, for
p=2(m+n)+1 (which is odd), writhe normalization gives

 V_(m,n)(A^-4)
   = delta^-1 [A^(-6p) - A^(-2p) Delta_2]
   = c_0 A^(-12(m+n)) + c_2 A^(-4(m+n)),                 (1)

 c_0=A^-6/delta,          c_2=-A^-2 Delta_2/delta.

Both coefficients are nonzero, c_0+c_2=1, and at A=1 they are -1/2
and 3/2. The two individual normalized full-twist frequencies are -12
and -4, exactly j(j+2)-3w_i^2 for j=0,2 and w_i=2.
Along n=-m both frequencies become zero. Their sum, not either term
individually, gives the unknot value.

The initial one-crossing braid has framing +1. Multiplication by -A^-3
makes its initial framing zero. A meridional annulus twist changes the
transported framing by 4 per twist, while the braid word writhe changes
by 2. Removing transported framing thus supplies A^-12(m+n), as in (1).
Keeping this distinction prevents an erroneous r-dependent correction.

3. Why even the unnormalized joint coefficients are not integral
----------------------------------------------------------------
The complement M of C_1 union C_2 is the connected sum of two solid tori.
Over Q(A), its skein is the tensor product of their annular skeins. Let
x_1,x_2 be their cores and e_j the monic Chebyshev basis, e_0=1,e_1=z.
The integral module, however, is not the tensor polynomial module over R.

A directly applicable primary source is Bakshi–Le–Przytycki, Kauffman
bracket skein module of the connected sum of two solid tori,
arXiv:2604.09971v1, Theorem 1 and equation (1):
https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09971v1
Their variable q is A here. With {n}=A^(2n)-A^(-2n), their n=2 relation is

             {3} e_2(y) - {1} e_2(x_1)e_2(x_2) = 0.

Consequently, after passing to Q(A),

                 e_2(y) = e_2(x_1)e_2(x_2)/Delta_2.       (2)

Here y is exactly the connected sum core described in section 1. The
blackboard-framed (2,1) cable has annular bracket A e_2(y)-A^-3. After
removing its +1 framing, its ACTUAL zero-framed skein class is

 [K_(0,0)] = A^-6(e_0 tensor e_0)
              - A^-2/Delta_2 (e_2 tensor e_2).             (3)

Thus the denominator is present before normalizing the Jones polynomial
or evaluating the two quantum dimensions. It cannot be dismissed as
only the usual division by delta. Evaluating (3) after m,n twists gives
(1), since theta_0=1, theta_2=A^8, and the two closure dimensions in the
second term are Delta_2^2.

Przytycki's generic connected-sum theorem is Theorem 1.2 / Corollary 1.3
in Kauffman bracket skein module of a connected sum of 3-manifolds:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9911120
It explicitly assumes the requisite A^k-1 are invertible; the generic
field tensor-product conclusion is not an integral tensor-basis claim.
The newer source supplies the precise integral relation (2), avoiding
any extrapolation from generic linear algebra.

4. The exact surviving two-disk gate
------------------------------------
For an arbitrary actual knot P in the complement of two disjoint-disk
axes, write its zero-framed generic skein class as

 [P]=sum_(j,l) a_(j,l)(A) e_j tensor e_l,   a_(j,l) in Q(A).

This is a finite sum. Parity gives j=w_1 mod 2 and l=w_2 mod 2. The two
independent twists have normalized Jones expression

 V_(m,n)=sum_(j,l) b_(j,l)(A)
       A^{m[j(j+2)-3w_1^2]+n[l(l+2)-3w_2^2]},
 b_(j,l)=a_(j,l) Delta_j Delta_l/delta.                    (4)

Group terms by the common opposite-rate frequency

 d_(j,l)=j(j+2)-l(l+2)-3(w_1^2-w_2^2).

Then infinitely many identities V_(k,-k)=1 hold if and only if the
coefficient sum in frequency zero is 1 and every other grouped
coefficient sum is zero. This follows from the same finite generalized
Vandermonde argument used for the one-disk case; equal frequencies
must be grouped BEFORE applying it.

When |w_1|=|w_2|, all diagonal labels j=l have frequency zero. Formula
(3) shows that simultaneous nontrivial diagonal labels and the rational
coefficients needed to combine their dimensions are geometrically
realizable, even for an actual unknot. Fusion multiplicity is not needed
to expose the problem: the two elementary TL_2 channels already suffice.

5. What this closes and what it leaves open
-------------------------------------------
It is false that an unknot-anchored, two-disk, opposite-twist Jones-one
family must use only the single-disk allowed labels/windings. It is also
false that the two-solid-torus tensor coordinates of every actual knot
must be integral Laurent polynomials. Neither statement can be used to
exclude the remaining coordinated-twist route.

This test has an evident geometric annulus/tube cancellation, so it gives
no evidence that a nontrivial Jones-one knot exists. To obtain a genuine
candidate one would need an actual two-axis exterior satisfying the
frequency-group equations (4), and an independent topological argument
that some opposite-twist output is nontrivial. The equations alone do
not supply that topology. For example, a hyperbolic three-component
exterior with such equalities would supply hyperbolic, hence nontrivial,
outputs for sufficiently long simultaneous fillings; no such exterior
is constructed here.

This is separate from the previously settled Watson mirrored-three-braid
unknot orbits, and from the same-sign band-surgery symmetric-matrix
obstruction. It addresses exactly why the new one-annulus integrality
proof does not combine across two opposite twist disks.

6. Bounded check of the source's torsion endpoint (section 3.5)
--------------------------------------------------------------
There is a potentially stronger exact mechanism: if two ACTUAL framed
knots P and U in M have a torsion difference, every embedding/filling
M -> S^3 kills that difference, since the skein of S^3 is the torsion-free
ring R. If U is an appropriate unknot reference with matching transported
framings, their filling Jones polynomials agree. One would still need an
actual P and a filling with independently certified nontrivial topology.
A single actual link cannot itself be nonzero torsion here: its S^3
bracket specializes at A=1 to a nonzero signed power of two. A difference
of actual links can be torsion, so this observation does not kill the
proposed pair mechanism.

The 2026 preprint's Corollary 3.11(4) and Corollary 3.12 display

 J_n=[n+1]e_n(y)+(-1)^(n+1)e_n(x_1)e_n(x_2),
                 (A^2-A^-2) J_n=0.

The first example is more concrete than a formal colored sum:

 J_1=[2]y+x_1x_2 = [x_1 disjoint-union x_2] - [y disjoint-union U].

These ARE two actual two-component links, with distinct component
homology classes in M: {(1,0),(0,1)} versus {(1,1),(0,0)}. But under
every reciprocal-integer filling of the two unlink axes, both are
geometrically the same UNFRAMED two-component unlink. The first consists
of the two core unknots; in the second, y becomes their connected-sum
unknot. The individual framing pairs are (m,n) and (m+n,0), respectively,
so they need not be isotopic as framed links. Their total framings agree,
and hence their brackets agree; their normalized Jones polynomials are
both those of the two-component unlink.
The elementary band joining the first pair produces y, and the trivial
band joining y to the small unknot produces y as well. Thus this explicit
torsion witness supplies no nontrivial knot.

For n>=2 the displayed J_n involve Chebyshev combinations and multiple
parallel components; the source does not exhibit them as a knot-minus-
unknot pair. Nor does one obtain such a pair merely by attaching bands
to the terms: band attachment is not an operation on an UNMARKED absolute
skein class. An appropriate torsion relation would first have to lift to
a relative skein module retaining the attachment points and band data.
Only then could a common closing/banding map be applied to the relation.
That relative actual-pair realization, together with nontriviality of a
filling, is the precise missing construction. It has not been provided.

Source caution: this is a 2026 preprint, and its concluding Question 1
asks about torsion coprime to {1} immediately after Corollary 3.12 claims
that all torsion is annihilated by {1}. We do not treat the entire torsion
classification as independently audited. The explicit low-degree
relations used here agree with the older generic connected-sum theorem
and with our independently verified two-variable fillings. In particular,
formula (3) is also uniquely determined by those fillings via successive
Vandermonde arguments, independently of the broader 2026 classification.
