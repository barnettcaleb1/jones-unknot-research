PASS 47 — WHAT COMPOSING REN GADGETS ACTUALLY DOES
2026-11-01

Result and limit
================
The main extension is that stacking any positive number of copies of the
SAME Ren-core-and-braid block has annular degree TWO, for every attached
four-strand braid. A second observation is that satellite composition of
two standard Ren-family patterns has annular degree EIGHT. Neither
operation removes the surviving annular channel. These are exact all-
parameter obstructions, not more braid-word searches. They do not cover
arbitrary distinct interacting blocks or all multi-axis surgeries.

The ordinary closure may still be an actual unknot: nonzero annular degree
does not imply nontrivial ordinary Jones after filling the axis.

1. Fixed conventions and already certified input
================================================
Let R=Z[A,A^-1], delta=-A^2-A^-2, and let z denote the standard framed
core of the solid torus. Positive crossings are A*1+A^-1*e.

Take the six-strand word

  b=sigma_2^-1 sigma_1^3 sigma_2^-1 sigma_4 sigma_5^-3 sigma_4.

Cap positions 3,4 at both ends, leaving a four-strand tangle T. Write
K(p,q) for its annular closure after the two top twists sigma_1^p and
sigma_3^q on the remaining four strands (sigma_1^p sigma_5^q before
capping). For p,q odd this is a knot pattern. Ren's example is K(1,1).

The old exact certificate proves

  [K(p,q)] = b_(p,q)(A)+a_(p,q)(A) z^2,
  a_(p,q) != 0,
  [z^8][K(p,q)^(2)] = A^(4(p+q)) h(A),

  h(A)=-A^-24 (A^4-1)^4 (A^4+1)^2
               *(1+A^4+A^8+A^12+A^16+A^20+A^24) != 0.       (1)

Here K^(2) is the actual two-parallel BLACKBOARD cable, not the square of
the uncolored polynomial. The coefficient was computed in the 54-
dimensional 12-strand/eight-through cell module and independently checked:

  work/pass4/braid_ren_cable.py
  work/pass4/braid_ren_cable.json
  work/pass4/braid_notes.md

Ren's primary paper already reports degree eight for the original
two-cable (Remark1.3); our old certificate supplies the explicit factor
and all top-twist parameters. No priority claim is made.
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11084v2

2. Satellite composition raises the detected degree
====================================================
For a framed knot Q inside a solid torus, its tubular-neighborhood
embedding induces an R-LINEAR skein map Phi_Q. It sends

  1 -> 1,           z^j -> [Q^(j)].

It need not be an algebra homomorphism for the usual annular product.
In particular Phi_Q(z^2) is the two-parallel cable of Q, not [Q]^2.

Suppose P is any Ren-core annular closure with [P]=b_P+a_P z^2 and
a_P != 0; let Q=K(p,q) be one of the knot patterns above. Then the actual
satellite composition has

  [P(Q)] = b_P + a_P [Q^(2)],
  [z^8][P(Q)] = a_P A^(4(p+q)) h(A) != 0.                 (2)

All coefficients are in the integral domain R. Since [Q^(2)] has degree
at most eight, the degree in (2) is EXACTLY eight. This excludes all
choices of the two top twists in each copy, including opposite twists,
as a way to obtain a scalar annular pattern by ordinary satellite
composition. Mirroring either gadget replaces A by A^-1 and does not
change this nonvanishing conclusion.

The formula is stated with the inherited blackboard tubular framing.
Changing the companion framing by s meridional twists acts on the color-
two channel z^2-1 by A^(8s), while fixing the empty channel. Thus the top
coefficient in (2) is merely multiplied by this nonzero monomial.
Changing the framing of the satellite itself also only multiplies the
whole bracket by a framing unit. Neither adjustment creates cancellation.

3. New all-powers extension for actual tangle stacking
======================================================
For EVERY beta in B_4 and EVERY positive integer k,

  degree_z [annular closure of (T beta)^k] = 2.           (3)

This is a different geometric operation from satellite composition.
It includes every component count, so no knot-connectivity assumption
enters the proof. It extends the previously proved k=1 braid-independent
Ren-core obstruction; it does not assert anything about products with
different intervening braids beta_1,...,beta_k.

Proof. The four-through coefficient of T is zero, and this property is
a two-sided ideal condition in TL_4. Therefore (T beta)^k has no four-
through term. On this ideal, the z^2 coefficient of annular closure is
the ordinary trace in the three-dimensional two-through cell module.

Use the previously verified gauge

  x=-A^-4, S=diag(1,A^2,A^4),
  M(x)=A^-2 S^-1 rho(T)S,
  U(x)=A^-e(beta) S^-1 rho(beta)S.

Both M and U have entries in Z[x,x^-1]. Set u=(1,0,1)^t,
v=(1,0,-1), e_2=(0,1,0)^t, and w=-15u-25e_2. The exact core jets are

  M(1)=w v,
  M(1)=0 mod5,
  M'(1)=2I mod5.                                       (4)

Every U_0=U(1) lies in SL_3(Z) and fixes u. These facts, including the
integral gauge and trace interpretation, are in the existing proof:

  work/ren-braid-obstruction-note/main.tex
  outputs/jones-ren-braid-obstruction.pdf

Up to the nonzero scalar A^(k(e(beta)+2)), the desired z^2 coefficient is

  f_k(x)=tr((M(x)U(x))^k).

Suppose f_k is the zero Laurent polynomial. Since M(1)U_0 is rank one,

  0=f_k(1)=(v U_0 w)^k,
  v U_0 w=-25 v U_0 e_2.

Thus v U_0 e_2=0. The flag <u> contained in ker(v)=<u,e_2> is invariant
under U_0. The quotient Z^3/Z u has basis [e_1],[e_2] and determinant
one. Its upper-right entry is zero, so its two integer diagonal entries
are both epsilon, epsilon=+1 or -1. Consequently

                   tr(U_0^k)=1+2 epsilon^k.

Now reduce the ENTIRE Laurent-polynomial identity f_k=0 modulo5 and
expand at x=1. Equation(4) gives

  M(x)U(x)=2(x-1)U_0+O((x-1)^2) mod5.

Every one of the k factors contributes at least one x-1, hence

  [ (x-1)^k ] f_k = 2^k tr(U_0^k)
                 = 2^k(1+2 epsilon^k) != 0 mod5.

The last parenthesis is 3 or -1, neither zero modulo5. This contradicts
f_k=0 and proves (3). There is no division by k or a derivative factorial,
so the proof includes k divisible by five.

The same argument gives the quantitative local statement

                  ord_(x=1) f_k <= k.

Indeed, if the constant coefficient is nonzero the order is zero; if it
vanishes, the integer coefficient of (x-1)^k is nonzero modulo five.

4. What remains outside these obstructions
==========================================
Opposite twists located at two different sites of a genuinely interacting
multi-axis diagram can have joint spectral collisions. Neither (2) nor
(3) excludes that situation. A construction there must specify the actual
outside tangle, all surviving simultaneous fusion channels, their closure
weights, and an actual unknot anchor. Replacing this information by a
formal subtraction of two nonzero annular coefficients is not a geometric
construction. The tangle agent is independently analyzing that joint
spectrum; no such candidate was produced by this Ren-composition pass.

5. Independent audit of the coordinated-twist warning
=====================================================
The other agent supplied an actual elementary example showing why the
single-axis integral-dimension argument cannot simply be copied here.
Take closure of sigma_1^(2m) sigma_1 sigma_1^(2n); its two disjoint twist
sites each have oriented winding two. Put p=2(m+n)+1 and
Delta_2=A^4+1+A^-4. Direct TL_2 decomposition gives

  V_(m,n)=delta^-1 ( A^(-6p) - A^(-2p) Delta_2 ).

Thus m+n=0 gives the actual unknot for all integers m, even though both
sites have winding two. In a generic tensor Chebyshev description of the
two-unlink-axis exterior, the zero-framed initial pattern has coefficients

  a_(0,0)=A^-6,          a_(2,2)=-A^-2/Delta_2.

Filling with twist parameters m,n multiplies channel (j,l) by
A^(m*j(j+2)+n*l(l+2)); the oriented normalization contributes
A^(-12(m+n)). These coefficients reproduce the exact formula above.
Two successive Vandermonde arguments show uniqueness among finite
generic tensor expansions, because the individual twist eigenvalues are
distinct. This audit is algebraic, conditional on the usual localized
connected-sum skein identification for the two-axis exterior; the tangle
agent is supplying that source and the geometric axis model.

The denominator 1/Delta_2 is essential. It is not permissible to treat
this localized tensor basis as an integral R-basis and conclude that a
product of quantum dimensions must divide delta in R. Opposite-site
diagonal channels can therefore survive; this example is an unknot,
not a counterexample or a proposed nontrivial Ren construction.
