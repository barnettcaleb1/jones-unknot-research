PASS 48: THE RELATIVE-TORSION GATE AND AN UNLINKABLE-TANGLE ENDPOINT
1 November 2026. Bounded research audit; no proof or counterexample.

Result
------
No usable relative lift of the connected-sum torsion relation was found.
The source's relative module does not itself supply one: its marked arcs
index relations after closing them, so the relevant band marks have already
been forgotten. However, a four-endpoint lift would have a much stronger
and more concrete consequence than an equality of two closed brackets.
It would give an actual unlinkable two-string tangle with exactly the full
bracket vector of a trivial tangle. Certifying that this tangle is not
rational would then finish a counterexample, using a known topological
theorem. The standard band choices cannot do this: their filled tangles
are rational, and in fixed numerator/denominator coordinates are trivial.

1. What the connected-sum source actually proves about relative skeins
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Let R=Z[A,A^-1], delta=-A^2-A^-2, and M=H_1 # H_1. In the notation of
Bakshi--Le--Przytycki, the closed relation is

 (A^2-A^-2)(x_1 x_2-delta y)=0.

Here x_1 x_2 and delta y represent two actual two-component links,
respectively x_1 disjoint union x_2 and y disjoint union a small unknot.

Their Section 2.2 and Lemma 2.1 use TWO marked points u,v on the attaching
curve gamma of a 2-handle. A relative arc alpha is closed by either of
the two boundary arcs gamma_1,gamma_2. The differences of these closures
span the kernel of the map from the handlebody's CLOSED skein module to
the CLOSED skein module after attaching the handle. Lemma 3.3 then gives
four generators for that relative module as a module over the closed
handlebody skein algebra. None of this asserts that the displayed torsion
relation persists after removing a band neighborhood with FOUR fixed
endpoints. Such a statement requires a new relation in that new module.

Source, checked in full text at Sections 2.2 and 3.2:
https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09971v1

In particular, cutting open selected components is not an operation on
an unmarked closed skein module: even the contractible-circle relation
removes a component and hence removes any proposed cutting location on it.
This is a structural issue, not missing notation in the existing proof.

2. A relative lift forces the FULL two-tangle bracket vector
----------------------------------------------------------
Suppose B is an embedded band ball in M, meeting each of two link
representatives in two trivial arcs, with the same four endpoint marks.
Set N=M minus int(B); write T_0,T_1 for their remaining tangles. Assume

              f(A)([T_1]-[T_0])=0 in S(N; four points),       (1)

for nonzero f in R. This is the missing RELATIVE assumption.

Under any specified filling of the two axis tori giving S^3, N fills to
S^3 minus int(B), a ball. Its four-point relative bracket module is the
free R-module of rank two on the two crossingless pairings, denoted
h and v. Therefore (1) implies

                    [T_1]=[T_0] in R h plus R v             (2)

after that filling. This uses the actual induced relative embedding map
and torsion-freeness; it does not assume closure is injective.

For completeness the two ordinary planar closure evaluations are

 N(alpha h+beta v)=delta^2 alpha+delta beta,
 D(alpha h+beta v)=delta alpha+delta^2 beta.

Their matrix has determinant delta^2(delta^2-1), nonzero in R. Thus the
two evaluations together determine the full vector over Q(A). A single
closed torsion relation only supplies the first evaluation; banding asks
for the other, which is independent. This explicitly locates the lost
information.

Freeness follows by resolving crossings in the ball and using the two
crossingless diagrams; independence follows from the same closure matrix.
This is an elementary proof, rather than an appeal to an unspecified
relative connected-sum theorem.

3. Exact counterexample scaffold and its topological endpoint
------------------------------------------------------------
Choose the band coordinates so that N is the closure restoring the
original two components, and D is the band-sum closure. For a filling in
which the original torsion witness links are the two-component unlink,
both N(T_i) are geometrically U_2. Let the reference band's D(T_0) be U.

If a relative lift (1) exists, equation (2) implies D(T_1) has the same
framed bracket as D(T_0). With compatible zero framing this says
V(D(T_1))=1. The D closure joins the two components into a single knot.

There is a convenient independent NONTRIVIALITY criterion: prove that
T_1 is not a rational tangle. A theorem of Scharlemann says that a
two-string tangle admitting both an unknot closure and a split-link
closure must be rational. Since N(T_1)=U_2 is already a split closure,
nonrationality implies that D(T_1) cannot be the unknot. Thus the entire
endpoint would be proved by an actual relative relation plus one
nonrationality certificate. No exhaustive knot identification is needed.

Exact accessible statement: Nogueira--Salgueiro, Theorem 4.8, page 14,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.15645
They attribute it to Scharlemann, Smooth spheres in R^4 with four critical
points are standard, Inventiones Mathematicae 79 (1985), 125-141.
This audit checked the precise tangle theorem in the Nogueira--Salgueiro
paper; it did not independently reprove Scharlemann's theorem.

Equivalent direct research target:

 Find an actual NONRATIONAL two-string tangle T with N(T)=U_2
 geometrically, and [T]=[h] with matching framing.

Then D(T) is a nontrivial Jones-one knot by the preceding argument.
The target belongs to the one-band fusion/ribbon subclass. No reduction
of the general Jones conjecture to that subclass is asserted.

4. The canonical band choices have a complete geometric no-go
------------------------------------------------------------
For each canonical band in the closed x_1 x_2 versus y U witness, after
filling both N closures are U_2 and both D closures are U. Consequently
both filled tangles are rational by the same theorem. In the fixed N,D
coordinates, a rational tangle of fraction p/q has numerator a two-bridge
link with determinant |p|. The numerator U_2 has determinant zero, forcing
p=0 and hence fraction 0. Therefore both tangles are the SAME trivial
unframed tangle relative to their four boundary points. Any common
replacement in the deleted band ball produces isotopic unframed links.

This is stronger than checking that the two original simple bandings
give y: it rules out obtaining different unframed outputs by arbitrary
common two-tangle replacements after these canonical cuts and fillings.
It does not rule out different cuts/bands whose candidate D closure is
not already geometrically known to be U.

The rational-tangle fraction classification is recalled with references
in Sikora, Section 2.1:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.08162
That paper also relates rational-solution uniqueness for systems of DNA
tangle equations to Jones unknot detection. Its introduction explicitly
warns that passing from unframed to framed equations matters. Here (1)
and (2) are framed throughout; the final isotopy conclusion is unframed.

5. Endpoint-parity caution, not an extra theorem used above
--------------------------------------------------------
Literature on relative connected sums includes localized vanishing
phenomena when markings lie in different summands. Such vanishing is
not automatically useful for knots in S^3. If an odd number of endpoints
lies on each of the two boundary tori of M, the complementary two solid
tori cannot separately pair those endpoints into arcs: each component
of a compact one-manifold has an even total number of boundary points.
Thus this marking choice has no closure using the disconnected S^3
complement. The four endpoints on the NEW band-ball boundary avoid this
elementary parity obstruction and are the correct setting for (1).
No generic relative connected-sum classification is invoked or claimed.

Conclusion
----------
The current evidence supplies neither the required relative lift nor an
actual nonrational tangle of trivial bracket vector. The canonical lift
idea, even if established algebraically, cannot yield a counterexample
through common local replacements, because its filled tangles are
geometrically trivial. A further search must specify the noncanonical
band/cut and the nonrational tangle FIRST; absolute torsion alone is not
an algebraic shortcut to their existence.
