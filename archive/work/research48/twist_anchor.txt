PASS48 — THE ONE-PUNCTURED ANNULUS IS NOT THE DIAGONAL-CANCELLATION CASE
2026-11-01. Bounded research deduction; no Jones detection proof/counterexample.

Task and endpoint
-----------------
Taylor supplies nontriviality for most twists of an unknot along a sufficiently
incompressible unknotted annulus meeting the knot once. The missing condition
is constant Jones along that SAME marked family. This pass shows why the
previous two-disk diagonal cancellation does not supply it, and derives a
stronger necessary condition. It does not prove that the condition forces
geometric triviality.

Primary sources read
--------------------
Taylor, Closures of 1-tangles and annulus twists, arXiv2506.11226v1, §5:
https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11226v1
The neighborhood W of the annulus is an unknotted solid torus; the knot meets
W in one trivial arc. Its complement V is a solid torus containing a relative
1-tangle G. Twisting changes only the complementary trivial arc, winding it
longitudinally. The sufficient incompressibility hypothesis implies G is
nontrivial; Taylor's main theorem limits unknot closures.

Abernathy–Gilmer, Even and odd Kauffman bracket ideals for genus-1 tangles,
NYJM22(2016),1039–1053, §4 and Proposition5.2:
https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2016/22-48v.pdf
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.7914
Over Q(A), the relative solid-torus skein has a graph basis g_(i,epsilon)
where epsilon=i+1 or i-1. The standard untwisted closure evaluates g to
Theta(1,i,epsilon). For adjacent colors this is Delta_max(i,epsilon), where
Delta_n=(-1)^n[n+1]. Their §3 uses the integral crossingless-diagram basis;
§4 carefully localizes for the graph basis. No integral-basis assertion is
being attributed to their graph basis.

1. Fusion eliminates the diagonal cancellation mechanism
--------------------------------------------------------
Identification of the actual twist operator matters here. In Taylor's
V=closure(S3 minus (A times I)), the two components D1,D2=P minus int(A)
are meridian disks of V. The endpoints of the arc G lie in the two boundary
regions between their boundaries. The two edges of the graph basis cross
these respective disks. Opposite surgery twists are equivalently the local
disk twists in V, leaving the initial normal arc phi0 in W fixed. On the
graph basis this is multiplication by theta_j^q theta_l^(-q), with
theta_n=(-1)^n A^(n(n+2)). Transported knot framing changes by
q(w1^2-w2^2). Therefore the normalized eigenvalue is A^(q*d_(j,l));
the signs cancel because j-l and w1^2-w2^2 are both odd. The sphere P
supplies the standard initial framing, and closing with phi0 is the planar
theta closure. This identifies the geometric Taylor family with the
spectral calculation, rather than presuming that any relative closure
family has this operator.

Take the annulus A in a sphere P, and complementary disks D1,D2 in P.
Orient their boundary circles compatibly along the annulus. Since K meets
A once, oriented linking numbers satisfy w1-w2=+1 or -1. Reverse choices
so w1=w2+1=w+1. In particular |w1| cannot equal |w2| for integer w.

Project the strands meeting D1,D2 onto colors j,l. The remainder of P is
one-punctured, so the three colors j,l,1 must form an admissible triple.
This is precisely the relative-solid-torus graph basis. Therefore

                  |j-l|=1.

For opposite full twists the zero-framing-normalized frequency is

        d_(j,l)=j(j+2)-l(l+2)-3(w1^2-w2^2).

The first difference equals +(2l+3) if j=l+1 and -(2j+3) if l=j+1.
These are distinct across all allowed ordered adjacent pairs. Hence there
are no coincident frequencies to group. A finite Vandermonde argument
implies an identically Jones-one family has a single nonzero graph channel,
and its frequency is zero. The zero-frequency pair is uniquely

 w>=0:  (j,l)=(3w+1,3w),
 w<=-1: (j,l)=(-3w-3,-3w-2).

This is fundamentally different from the earlier actual two-braid example,
whose annulus has zero punctures and which permits every diagonal j=l.
It cannot be imported into Taylor's one-punctured setting.

2. Leading-term integrality removes all large surviving labels
---------------------------------------------------------------
Here is the required lattice argument, rather than assuming localized
graph coefficients are integral in general.

Realize the relative solid torus as an annulus times an interval, with two
marked points on one boundary. Its integral skein basis consists of
crossingless essential arc-and-circle diagrams. Cut the annulus by the two
meridional arcs separated by the marked points. A graph g_(j,l), |j-l|=1,
has a unique leading crossingless diagram of bidegree (j,l), with coefficient
1: take the identity term from each Jones–Wenzl projector. Every other
projector term contains a turnback and lowers at least one of the two cut
intersection counts. Thus, if an integral relative skein equals c*g_(j,l)
with no other graph channels, its leading crossingless coefficient is c,
and c lies in Z[A,A^-1]. It would already suffice that c(1) is an integer;
all projector denominators are quantum integers nonzero at A=1.

Closing by the initial standard complementary arc gives

                    c*Delta_max(j,l)=u(A)*delta,

where delta=-A^2-A^-2 and u is a framing monomial with u(1)=+1 or -1.
Consequently (max(j,l)+1) divides 2. Adjacency gives max>=1, so max=1.
Only the pairs (1,0) and (0,1) remain. Their graphs are the two elementary
boundary-parallel arcs. The zero-frequency equation also now gives

                    {|w1|,|w2|}={0,1}.

This leading-term argument needs the relative graph basis tied to the two
actual disk cuts; one must not replace it by the false tensor-integrality
statement for the two-axis absolute skein from pass47.

3. Exact remaining obstacle
----------------------------
An all-parameter Jones-one Taylor family, if it exists, must have its
actual relative 1-tangle G skein-equal (up to framing) to an elementary
arc. The fact that the graph has small degree does not show that the
actual embedded arc is boundary-parallel. That is a new instance of the
same geometry-versus-skein faithfulness difficulty; no such implication
has been proved here.

Accordingly, Taylor does not automatically rule out the surviving
possibility: its nontriviality concerns the embedded arc, whereas the
calculation constrains its skein class. Conversely no nontrivial actual
arc with this elementary skein class and the necessary unknot closure
has been constructed. The apparent solution is not obtained by silently
identifying those two notions of triviality.

The useful action change is precise: for one-punctured annulus candidates,
stop exploring equal-winding diagonal channels and higher adjacent colors.
A genuine candidate must have boundary linking magnitudes 0 and1, and
an actual relative 1-tangle with exactly elementary-arc skein. A formal
graph, absolute torsion relation, or arbitrary band attachment does not
supply such a tangle.

4. Complete conditional counterexample endpoint
-----------------------------------------------
It would suffice to exhibit an actual unknot K0 and one-punctured unknotted
annulus A satisfying Taylor's sufficient incompressibility, with the
relative arc G in the complementary V skein-equal (with the compatible
framing) to the elementary graph of zero normalized frequency. The spectral
argument then gives V_(Kq)=1 for every q. Taylor gives Kq nontrivial for
|q|>=2. Thus K2 would be a counterexample with both polynomial and topology
established for the same diagram. No such G or diagram is constructed.

Conversely, any all-parameter Jones-one family in this one-punctured setup
must have exactly this elementary relative skein class. This reduces the
construction goal to an actual geometrically nontrivial relative arc with
that class and unknot anchor, not a search among arbitrary adjacent colors.
Taylor's theorem is only used in the final nontriviality implication; the
skein deductions do not assume its sufficient incompressibility.

Independent parent audit: the parent inspected the actual graph diagram
in the source and confirmed the leading terms. The short arc plus i core
circles leads g_(i,i+1); the long arc plus (i-1) core circles leads
 g_(i,i-1). These have distinct pairs of cut counts. Nonidentity projector
terms reduce the count at their disk and do not increase the other count.
This is the required monic triangular argument, not an assertion that the
entire localized graph basis is integral.
