INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE PURE-CHANNEL ARGUMENT
1 November 2026. Bounded audit of work/research48/twist_anchor.txt.

Verdict: no normalization counterexample or algebraic proof flaw found.
The lattice assertion is narrower than integrality of the graph basis and
is justified by a monic leading diagram. It must be retained in that form.

I inspected the actual g_(i,epsilon) diagram on printed page 1044 of
Abernathy--Gilmer, https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2016/22-48v.pdf . The two
projector boxes can be placed across two distinct meridional disks. Each
nonidentity Temperley--Lieb summand of f_n has through-degree at most n-2.
Caps and cups can be pushed to the appropriate sides of its disk entirely
within that box. The resulting diagram has a representative with at most
n-2 intersections at that disk and does not acquire new intersections at
the OTHER disk. Thus it cannot isotopically become the crossingless
identity-resolution diagram whose minimal cut counts are (i,epsilon).
Choosing identity in every projector is the unique term of that bidegree,
and all these identity coefficients are 1. Consequently c*g integral
with only one graph channel implies c integral, despite lower coefficients
of g being rational functions.

Low-label checks, with h the short arc, v the long arc, and z a core:
 g_(0,1)=h; g_(1,0)=v;
 g_(1,2)=h z-delta^(-1)v;
 g_(2,1)=v z-delta^(-1)h.
The latter two close by the standard complementary short arc to
delta^2-1=Delta_2. This matches the claimed theta normalization and makes
the distinction from false global tensor-lattice integrality explicit.

The standard closure normalization is also directly present in
Proposition 5.2(i) at n=0: the pairing with x_0^even is theta(1,i,epsilon).
The usual quantum-factorial theta formula gives
theta(1,i,i+1)=Delta_(i+1) and theta(1,i,i-1)=Delta_i.
At A=1 these are signed integers of absolute value max(i,epsilon)+1.
The closing unknot bracket is delta, not 1; using the reduced bracket
prematurely would lose precisely the divisibility needed by the argument.

For framed disk twists the eigenvalue is theta_j/theta_l, with
theta_n=(-1)^n A^(n(n+2)). The two disk twists have opposite signs; after
removing transported framing q(w1^2-w2^2), the signs cancel because
j-l and w1^2-w2^2 are both odd. A simultaneous reversal of disk or q
conventions only reverses all frequencies, leaving distinctness and the
zero-channel conclusions unchanged. The physical operator identification
requires the two graph cuts to be Taylor's actual complementary sphere
disks and the initial closing arc to be the normal arc in Nb(A); the note
states these data explicitly. It should not be generalized to arbitrary
closure families without that identification.

No inference from a skein-equal elementary arc to a geometrically trivial
embedded arc is justified by this audit. That remains the stated gap.
