PASS49 — BRUNNIAN THETA-CURVE TEST FOR THE ELEMENTARY-ARC CONSTRUCTION
2026-11-01. No proof of Jones detection, no counterexample construction.

Scope and change of direction
-----------------------------
Two unknot closures do give a large, genuinely geometric source of genus-one
arcs: Taylor's Section4 identifies these with Brunnian theta-curves. But
neither the two closures nor the Brunnian property supplies elementary
relative skein. This pass finds a useful whole-family obstruction through
an actual edge unzip, avoiding computation of every relative coefficient.
It also records an exceptional parameter in the cited source argument.

Primary sources
---------------
Taylor, Closures of 1-tangles and annulus twists, Section4:
https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11226v1
Jang, Kronaeuer, Luitel, Medici, Taylor, Zupan, New examples of Brunnian
theta graphs, Sections4–5, especially Figure15 and Section5.3:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1508.03014
No claim is made that this is a new literature theorem.

1. Why the two-closure hypothesis does not reduce to rational tangles
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Starting with a Brunnian theta graph, remove a neighborhood of a constituent
unknot and retain the third edge as a relative arc in the complementary
solid torus. The other two constituent unknots give two inequivalent
closures, with wrapping index1. Taylor gives the converse construction.

There is a tempting but invalid shortcut: drill an edge to convert the
relative arc into a two-string tangle and then claim that both original
unknots are distinct rational closures of that tangle. One original
constituent instead becomes an edge-deletion condition. Only the other
is the corresponding rational closure. Thus Bleiler–Scharlemann's
uniqueness theorem for unknot closures of prime two-tangles cannot be
invoked twice. This diagnoses the exact failure of that short proof.

2. A general linear edge-unzip obstruction
-----------------------------------------
Suppose the marked Brunnian theta graph is assembled from a relative arc G
and the two edges inside the complementary solid torus W. Fix the annulus
coordinates in which replacing G by the proposed elementary arc gives the
planar theta graph. This is the comparison required by the pass48 candidate.
Unzip one of the TWO W-edges, retaining the edge G only once. This operation
is an actual complementary closure of G, so it is R-linear on its relative
skein. If [G] equals the elementary arc up to framing, then the resulting
knot has the Jones polynomial of the same unzip of the planar theta graph.

By Jang et al. Lemma5.5, every knot obtained by unzipping an edge of a planar
theta graph is a T(2,k), k odd (including the unknot). Thus a single such
unzip whose Jones is not any two-strand torus-knot Jones excludes the marked
arc. One must NOT unzip the retained G-edge itself: that doubles G and is
not an R-linear operation on its fundamental relative skein.

Simple exact sufficient rejection test: if an actual such unzip J has

                    det(J)>span(V_J) and det(J)>1,

its Jones is not that of any T(2,k). Indeed |k|=det(T(2,k)); for |k|>=3,
span(V_T(2,k))=|k|. The |k|=1 cases have determinant1. A displayed diagram
with c crossings gives span(V_J)<=c, so det(J)>c and det(J)>1 already
suffice without computing Jones.

The hypothesis that the elementary comparison produces a planar theta
graph is explicit. Arbitrarily marked elementary classes and arbitrary
complementary closures must not be silently identified with this comparison.

3. Application to an actual infinite Brunnian family
---------------------------------------------------
Jang et al. construct graphs Gamma(n,m)=G(A_n,-n,m), with
A_n=P_23^(-n)P_13, from their fixed four-braid template. Their Section5.3
performs an explicit RED-edge unzip and obtains

             J_(n,m) = 3_1 # K(-3/(6(n+m)+5)),

where K(p/q) is the DENOMINATOR closure of the rational tangle p/q.
The continued fraction displayed for its second factor is

                  (0, -2(n+m)-1, -1, -1, -1).

Set s=n+m. The determinant is 3*|6s+5|. The displayed diagram has at most
3 + |2s+1| +3 = |2s+1|+6 crossings, so

                    span(V_J)<=|2s+1|+6.

For s>=0, 3|6s+5|=18s+15 > 2s+7.
For s<=-2, 3|6s+5|=-18s-15 > -2s+5.
Therefore every parameter pair with s!=-1 passes the rejection test.

Consequence: for choices retaining the BLUE or GREEN edge as G, so that
the red-edge unzip lies entirely in the complementary closure, these graphs
cannot have the elementary relative class in any proposed planar-comparison
marking. This excludes the whole stated parameter range using one geometric
closure and one inequality. It does not exclude the marking retaining the
red edge, since the red unzip would then double G. No scan was performed.

4. The exceptional line and source caution
------------------------------------------
At s=-1, the rational fraction is -3/(-1)=3, whose denominator closure is
the unknot. The displayed unzip is then just a trefoil. Thus this particular
closure does not distinguish the graph from a planar theta graph's unzip.

The source text claims its second factor could be trivial only if 6s+5=1,
which is impossible. Its stated Schubert convention also allows denominator
-1 (normalize the fraction to denominator+1). The displayed formula therefore
leaves a gap at s=-1 in THAT argument. This is not a proof that the graph is
trivial, nor that the paper's theorem is false; another unzip or another
argument may handle the exception. No such repair was established in this
bounded pass. Do not cite the all-parameter theorem as independently audited
on the strength of this calculation.

5. Endpoint status
------------------
No actual nontrivial elementary-skein relative arc was obtained. The new
concrete filter is that its linear complementary edge unzips must all have
two-strand torus-knot Jones polynomials. The explicit newly checked Brunnian
family fails that filter for all s!=-1 and for the two stated retained-edge
choices. The remaining line or third marking is not claimed promising;
it merely escapes this one certificate. No further parameter scan is
warranted without a new invariant identity.
