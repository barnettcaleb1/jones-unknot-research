2. Theorem: an unknot product prevents the desired framed ambiguity
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Let X be a two-string tangle and R a rational tangle, with the same marked
boundary. Let P and Q be rational closing tangles with DISTINCT SLOPES
(a mere difference of framing is not sufficient). Suppose the
two corresponding closures agree as framed links:

                 C_P(X)=C_P(R),    C_Q(X)=C_Q(R).

If either common underlying link is the unknot, then X is rational.
Consequently such a system cannot produce a rational/nonrational pair.

Proof.
(a) Sikora's Theorem 21 says that two distinct rational probes determine
the full framed bracket vector. Therefore [X]=[R] in the rank-two relative
ball skein module. The theorem applies to balanced framed isotopy, the
setting used by the existing DNA criterion.

(b) Without loss, C_P(X)=C_P(R)=U geometrically. Let M_X and M_R be the
oriented double branched covers of the tangle balls. Filling them along
the slope alpha determined by P gives S^3. Thus M_X is the exterior of
a knot J in S^3. M_R is a solid torus, the exterior of the unknot U.
The common boundary marking identifies the meridian slope alpha.

(c) Choose a THIRD rational closing tangle H so that C_H(R) is the
two-component unlink. Such a probe always exists for a rational tangle.
Write lambda for its filling slope on the common boundary torus. It is
the preferred longitude of the unknot exterior M_R: its filling is
S^1 x S^2. Since [X]=[R], the H-closures have equal brackets and hence
equal determinant zero. This does NOT require Jones detection of the
unlink. Determinant zero means H_1(M_X(lambda);Z) is infinite. In a knot
exterior in S^3, the UNIQUE slope with this property is the preferred
longitude. Thus the common boundary marking also identifies longitudes.

(d) The second probe Q now has the SAME rational surgery coefficient r
in both meridian/longitude systems. Since Q differs from P, this is a
nonmeridional slope. The common link C_Q(X)=C_Q(R) is a rational link
(possibly an unknot or unlink in degenerate cases). Its oriented double
branched cover gives an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism

                       S^3_r(J) = S^3_r(U).

Kronheimer--Mrowka--Ozsvath--Szabo, Theorem 1.1 of Monopoles and lens
space surgeries, says this implies J=U for EVERY rational r. The case
r=0 is included through Property R. Hence M_X is a solid torus. The
standard branched-cover characterization of rational tangles implies
X is rational. End proof.

Orientation detail: the identifications come from the same oriented
marked tangle ball and orientation-preserving equivalences of its closed
branch links. The common meridian and longitude slopes, with boundary
intersection orientation, determine the rational coefficient up to
simultaneous sign, which does not change r. Comparing merely unoriented
lens-space homeomorphism types would be insufficient.

Framing detail: the theorem does not infer a peripheral longitude from
blackboard writhe. The full bracket equality provides the auxiliary
determinant-zero closure, which determines the longitude homologically.
This is exactly what the old UNFRAMED ambiguity lacks.

The equality of closed brackets in (c) is first divided symbolically by
delta to obtain equality of reduced brackets, and only then specialized
at the determinant root. Direct specialization of the unreduced bracket
would give zero for every nonempty link and would prove nothing.

COROLLARY (unavoidable variable framing defect). Suppose an UNFRAMED
two-probe ambiguity has rational R, nonrational X, and an unknot as one
common product. In Sikora's balanced-framing convention, its two total
framing defects cannot be equal. If both defects were the same integer,
adding that many framing twists to X on one fixed arc would correct both
closures simultaneously, contradicting the theorem. This covers every
such ambiguity, including parameters and geometric constructions not
covered by the old fixed-X calculation. The former four-versus-zero
defect is one instance of this general obstruction.

3. What this does NOT exclude
-----------------------------
It does not prohibit a Jones-one knot with a rational unknotting move:
there the new closure has only the POLYNOMIAL of the unknot, not an
established geometric rational-link type. Step (d) would be unavailable.
It does not exclude the existing preferred-quotient/Brunnian target.
It does not settle framed two-probe systems whose two common geometric
products are both nontrivial rational links. Their covers are knots in
lens spaces, and the cited S^3 theorem does not simply extend to them.

4. A concrete untested source, and a cheap exact obstruction
-----------------------------------------------------------
Baker--Doleshal--Hoffman give actual strongly inverted tangle families
A'_(m,n) and B'_(p/q) with THREE rational-link fillings, in Figures 3 and
4 of https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.5002 . These are quotients of fillings of
the magic manifold. The three specified filling slopes are infinity, 1,
and 2. This source supplies actual boundary markings and branch diagrams,
unlike an abstract list of lens-space homeomorphism types. It was not
among the concrete families in the three checked prior deliverables.
No member was established here to share TWO FRAMED closures with a
rational comparator. Merely listing their lens fillings is not a result
about the Jones problem.

There is an immediate filter before any framing or bracket work. For a
rational comparator, determinants of closures by two probes at distance
d satisfy

                gcd(det L_1,det L_2) divides d.

Indeed the determinant is the absolute value of a primitive integral
linear form on the slope lattice, namely intersection with the solid-
torus meridian. For slope vectors v_1,v_2, a common divisor of their
linear-form values divides det(v_1,v_2). In particular adjacent probes
force coprime determinants. This is a necessary condition only.

This already eliminates the tempting Figure-Eight-Sister triple as an
unframed comparator source: the three fillings are mutually distance
one and have lens-space orders 5,5,10. Every pair has determinant gcd5,
so no rational tangle can give the same two products at those marked
slopes. The exact lens orders and slopes are stated in the source's
Corollary 39. No census calculation is required.

For any remaining A' or B' family, a viable endpoint would require:
two non-unknot products with a rational comparator at the SAME marked
slopes; geometric equivalence of the branch links (not just their
covers); and equal total framing defects for the two comparisons.
Only then would a common framing adjustment yield the framed ambiguity
and Sikora's implication give a Jones counterexample. None of those
combined requirements has been achieved for a new member here.

Action consequence
------------------
Stop trying to repair an UNKNOT-ANCHORED rational/nonrational DNA pair:
the obstruction above excludes the whole framed target, not only the
previous trefoil example. The remaining lens-to-lens construction must
use two non-unknot products and keep its branch involution, peripheral
marking, and total framing data. The explicit A'/B' source is a genuine
new input to that construction, but its required framed coincidence
remains unproved; it is not presented as a promising solved subproblem.
