THEOREM A: UNIVERSAL POLYNOMIAL ORDINARY-JONES SATELLITES ARE AFFINE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Let P be a fixed framed knot pattern in a solid torus, inserted using the
zero framing of its companion. Suppose there is a polynomial
 F_A(s) in Q(A)[s]
such that for EVERY knot K,
 V_{P(K)}(A^-4)=F_A(V_K(A^-4)).
Then F_A has degree at most1. Moreover:

 * if the algebraic winding of P is even, F_A is constant;
 * if that winding is odd, F_A(s)=b(A)*s.

Equivalently, the appropriately normalized annular skein of P has only
Chebyshev labels0,1; parity permits only the label agreeing with winding.
There is no assumption about the geometric wrapping number.

Proof
-----
Write e_j for the monic Chebyshev annular basis, Delta_j for its unknot
value, and J_j(K) for normalized color j+1, so J_0=1 and J_1=V_K.
The finite annular expansion gives
  V_{P(K)}=sum_{j=0}^N a_j(A) J_j(K),
where the fixed scalar normalization and nonzero Delta_j are absorbed into
a_j. This is valid for zero-framed satellites, with the pattern framing
correction fixed independently of K. The skein respects mod2 homology in
the solid torus, so a_j=0 unless j equals the winding modulo2.

Let T be the trefoil with V_T=t+t^3-t^4, t=A^-4, and set c_j=J_j(T).
Normalized colors multiply under connected sum. Evaluate the asserted law
on K=T^{#r}, r=0,1,2,..., where r=0 denotes U. Then

  sum_j a_j c_j^r = sum_l b_l (c_1^l)^r.                    (1)

All elements here lie in the field Q(A). The c_j have exact degree intervals

  min_degree_t c_j = j,
  max_degree_t c_j = (3j^2+5j)/2.                           (2)

Thus the c_j are pairwise distinct. Also c_1^l has interval [l,4l]. Equality
c_j=c_1^l first forces j=l by minimum degree; comparison of maximum degrees
then gives 3j(j-1)=0. The only coincidences are (j,l)=(0,0),(1,1).

Collect equal bases in (1). For finitely many distinct bases d_1,...,d_m,
the equations sum_i z_i d_i^r=0, r=0,...,m-1, have only the zero solution:
the Vandermonde determinant is product_{i<k}(d_k-d_i), nonzero in Q(A).
Therefore a_j=0 for j>=2 and b_l=0 for l>=2. Parity proves the two listed
forms. This is a finite exact algebraic argument, not a numerical inference.

Degree check for (2)
--------------------
A convenient standard two-strand fusion expression is

 c_j = Delta_j^-1 * sum_{k=0,2,...,2j}
       (-1)^(k/2) Delta_k A^((3/2)k(k+2)-6j(j+2)),
 Delta_j=(-1)^j sum_{h=0}^j A^(2j-4h).

The fusion channel k of two j-colored strands has braid eigenvalue
 (-1)^(j-k/2) A^((k(k+2)-2j(j+2))/2).
Cube this and remove the three colored framing twists
 ((-1)^j A^(j(j+2)))^3; taking the quantum trace gives the displayed formula.
This is the elementary SU(2) case of the torus-knot formula; primary reference
for the general calculation is Morton,
https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/~su14/papers/colouredjones.pdf .

In its numerator the minimum A exponent comes uniquely from k=0 and is
-6j(j+2). The maximum comes uniquely from k=2j and is -2j. Dividing by
Delta_j, whose exponent interval is [-2j,2j], gives the c_j interval
[-6j^2-10j,-4j] in A, equivalent to (2). No highest/lowest cancellation is
possible because those channels are unique. At j=1 the formula gives
A^-4+A^-12-A^-16 exactly. The small exact-arithmetic verifier
check_satellite_independence.py reproduces labels0 through6 and their degree
intervals; the all-label proof is the preceding unique-extremum argument.

THEOREM B: CONSEQUENCE FOR AN UNKNOTTING-NUMBER-ONE PROMOTER
----------------------------------------------------------
Assume P is geometrically essential, its outputs under nontrivial companions
are genuine satellites, and P(U)=U. Suppose it has a universal rational
Jones law as in the earlier satellite-rigidity report (in particular, the
polynomial law of Theorem A suffices), with the law defined at 1. Suppose
P(K) has unknotting number1 for at least one
nontrivial companion K. Then

  V_{P(J)}=1 for EVERY companion J.

Indeed Scharlemann--Thompson prove that a genuine satellite with nonzero
algebraic winding has unknotting number at least2. Hence winding(P)=0.
The earlier rational rigidity theorem now makes F constant; evaluating
at U makes that constant1. For polynomial F, Theorem A alone suffices.

Precise primary locator for the established topological input:
Hom--Lidman--Park, Unknotting number and cabling, Introduction, immediately
after Theorem1.1, explicitly states the general satellite conclusion and
cites Scharlemann--Thompson [ST88, Corollary3.3]:
https://preprint.press.jhu.edu/ajm/sites/default/files/AJM-hom-lidman-park.pdf
Original: Unknotting number, genus, and companion tori, Math. Ann.280 (1988),
191--205, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01456051 .

Geometric essentiality with nontrivial companion gives an incompressible
companion torus here (the winding-zero pattern is not a core). Thus choosing
J to be a trefoil would already give an explicit nontrivial Jones-one knot.
The proposed device would not need a hypothetical Jones-one input at all.

Interpretation for the generic scaffold
---------------------------------------
This does not prove that such a scalar essential pattern cannot exist. Its
existence is itself an unconditional counterexample construction. It does
prove that a fixed satellite with a universal polynomial law in ordinary
Jones cannot provide a genuinely input-dependent reduction to u=1.

An odd-winding pattern can pass ordinary Jones through, but the companion
torus obstructs u=1. A winding-zero pattern can support u=1, but its colors
are even, and any universal polynomial dependence on the ordinary fundamental
color collapses to a constant. This combines a sutured-manifold obstruction
with a representation-theoretic parity obstruction.

Scope qualifications are essential. The result does NOT exclude:
 * operations depending on the particular diagram/companion;
 * several independent infection boxes, including separate mirror inputs;
 * an identity assumed only on the unknown Jones-one locus;
 * arbitrary non-polynomial functions of the ordinary Jones polynomial;
 * a construction using additional information from higher colors.
The third escape still needs a proof that Jones1 controls the higher-color
combination, precisely the missing bridge. Nothing here assumes ordinary
Jones determines higher colors or that all-colored detection is known.

This bounded pass combines an already-proved algebraic rigidity theorem
with an established u=1 topology theorem. It produces an obstruction to a broad tempting global
reduction, not a complete generic proof scaffold. It changes the next action:
do not pursue a fixed one-companion satellite whose success is justified by
an ordinary-Jones polynomial substitution. A useful generic-to-u1 reduction
must add genuinely different geometric input or escape that single-companion
universal-law framework.
