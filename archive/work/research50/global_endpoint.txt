1. Prime extension is not prescribed-fiber compression
------------------------------------------------------
A potentially useful new bridge would replace J#K^m by a prime knot in the
SAME prescribed Jones fiber, while retaining a growing topological or
homological obstruction. That would make prime-fiber information usable.

The primary source checked was Liam Watson, Any tangle extends to non-mutant
knots with the same Jones polynomial, 2005, main theorem and Section7:
https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~liam/files/preprint.pdf
It says that a prime tangle T extends to a PAIR of prime knots with equal
Jones polynomials. Their common value is obtained from the whole bracket
vector of T and its mirrored partner through the construction's evaluation
matrix. The statement does not say that this value equals the polynomial
of a specified initial closure of T, nor that it depends only on that one
closure polynomial.

Moreover, a tangle containing a nontrivial local knot is not prime under
its definition (Section3). Inserting J#K^m as local knots in a strand cannot
satisfy the prime-tangle hypothesis. Thus this source supplies no reduction
of the composite amplification family to one prime fiber. Its paired-braid
construction also overlaps the already completed Watson audits, so no new
Watson parameter investigation was started.

2. Exact topological limitation of independent long-knot boxes
-------------------------------------------------------------
Take any fixed actual tangle construction having disjoint input balls, each
meeting the final knot in one properly embedded arc. In those balls insert
copies of a long knot K (or its mirror/orientation variants). Assume all
subsequent changes are supported outside the balls. Each boundary sphere
continues to meet the final knot in exactly two points. Capping the inside
and outside arcs exhibits the inserted knot as a connected-sum factor.
Applying this to all the disjoint balls shows that the output still has
all inserted nontrivial factors in its prime decomposition.

Thus independent one-strand substitutions cannot turn J#K^m into a prime
knot, even though their Jones values are exactly controlled as powers of V_K.
To evade the statement, the construction must interact through a ball
boundary or insert K through more than one strand. Ordinary Jones equality
then does not automatically supply the needed multistrand bracket identity.

This observation is an elementary geometric form of the already recorded
rank-one limitation, not an independent new obstacle to unknot detection.
The relative skein of a ball with two endpoints is R*eta and [K]=V_K*eta;
all R-linear contexts preserve that scalar equality. Parallel cabling is
not substitution into independent one-strand balls. The previous actual
5_1/10_132 colored mismatch already blocks general scalar-to-cable promotion.

3. Other tempting variants are duplicates or insufficient
---------------------------------------------------------
Unique factorization of Laurent polynomials bounds nonunit polynomial
factors of a fixed Jones value, but says nothing about the number of
Jones-one prime knot factors. The scaffold already makes this exact.
Choosing an irreducible or cyclotomic anchor does not remove those factors.

A scalar-long-knot inverse is another statement that V_K=1. Reflecting its
algebraic invertibility into geometric invertibility is equivalent to
unknot detection, as pass44 already proved; no independent construction of
an inverse emerged.

Replacing the full polynomial by Mahler measure or a dynamical entropy
built from it was also considered. The figure-eight has cyclotomic Jones
polynomial, so its connected sums have Mahler measure1 despite growing
prime-factor count, bridge number and total exterior volume. Stronger
prime/cyclotomic-volume countertests already occur in passes30 and32.
This is therefore not new progress and cannot provide a selected-fiber
bound. The whole polynomial changes along these examples.

4. Decision
-----------
The source audit closes a specific tempting citation shortcut: Watson's
prime extension theorem is not a prime replacement theorem for a prescribed
Jones polynomial. All other candidates returned to earlier equivalents.
There is no new independent missing lemma or actual fixed-fiber mechanism
in this pass. Do not count a reformulated prime-factor bound, an entropy
name, or the rank-one argument as evidence toward a proof.
