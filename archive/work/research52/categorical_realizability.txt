PASS 52: UNIVERSAL DEFORMATION COMPATIBILITY DOES NOT REMOVE HIDDEN HOMOLOGY
1 November 2026. Bounded global-mechanism test; no Jones theorem or
counterexample. No claim of literature novelty for the calculation.

PRIOR WORK CHECKED

Read the Khovanov and relative Lee-bar sections of
outputs/jones-full-proof-scaffolds.md, work/research20/homology_scaffold.txt,
and work/research27/homology_global.txt. Their formal Euler-one/rank17
model, Lee compatibility, Steenrod growth on actual ribbon knots, and
2-torsion obstruction are not presented as new findings below.

The specific new test is stronger than separately imposing Lee rank,
integral torsion, or a formal Euler condition: impose ONE integral
universal Frobenius complex, built from genuine cobordism morphisms,
whose every semisimple specialization has the unknot's homology. An
explicit calculation shows that even these simultaneous conditions
allow nonzero Euler-invisible homology.

1. The putative independent deformations share one source.

Khovanov's universal rank-two system has

 R=Z[h,t],    A=R[X]/(X^2-hX-t),
 epsilon(1)=0, epsilon(X)=1,
 Delta(1)=1 tensor X+X tensor 1-h*(1 tensor 1),
 Delta(X)=X tensor X+t*(1 tensor 1).

His Proposition 5 produces rank-two Frobenius systems by base changes
and twists from this system. When 2 is invertible, Y=X-h/2 gives

                    Y^2=t+h^2/4.

Thus treating rational Lee and Bar-Natan parameters as unrelated
constraints overcounts the available structure. The integral prime-2
behavior is genuinely additional, and is retained in the construction
below. Naot's universal geometric theory also reduces link complexes
to one-variable module complexes, though this does NOT characterize
which such complexes arise from actual link diagrams.

Use quantum degrees deg(X)=deg(h)=-2 and deg(t)=-4; the circle module
has the usual extra shift giving its generators quantum degrees +1,-1.

2. A block built from actual surfaces, not arbitrary scalar differentials.

Let O be a circle object in the additive graded Bar-Natan cobordism
category. Let H:O->O be the connected genus-one cylinder. It has quantum
degree -2. Under the universal Frobenius functor, H=m Delta acts by

                         H=2X-h.

Consequently

               H^2=(2X-h)^2=h^2+4t=:D.

Define the two-term formal cobordism complex E in homological degrees
zero and one by

 E^0 = O direct-sum O{-2},
 E^1 = O direct-sum O{+2},

                       [ 2 Id   H ]
             d_E   =   [  H     0 ].

Every matrix entry is a genuine cobordism or integer linear combination
of cobordisms. The grading shifts make each entry degree zero. There is
only one differential, so d^2=0 imposes no additional relation. This is
an honest object of the formal cobordism-complex category. It is NOT
claimed to be the cube of resolutions of any knot diagram.

After applying the universal Frobenius functor, if D is invertible then

                   [ 2  H ] [ 0   H ]
                   [ H  0 ] [ H  -2 ]  = D*I.

The reverse product is also D*I. Thus d_E is invertible and E contractible
over A[D^-1]. In particular:

 * Bar-Natan specialization h=1,t=0: D=1, contractible integrally.
 * Rational Lee specialization h=0,t=1: D=4, contractible over Q.
 * Every field specialization with nonzero discriminant: contractible.

These specializations come from the same universal differential, with
all base-change maps automatically compatible. This is stronger than
choosing independent differentials after specializing.

3. The Khovanov special fiber retains free homology and 2-torsion.

Set h=t=0. Then A_0=Z[X]/(X^2), H=2X, and

                        [ 2   2X ]
                d_E  =  [ 2X   0 ].

Apply the integral, grading-preserving row operation row2-=X*row1 and
then column2-=X*column1. The result is diag(2,0), since X^2=0. Hence

 H^0(E)=A_0{-2},
 H^1(E)=A_0{+2} direct-sum (A_0/2A_0).

The free classes at the two different quantum shifts form the familiar
deformation bar, but the same block also contains genuine integral
2-torsion. Nothing has been discarded by passing first to Q.

Now put

                  C_N=O direct-sum (E direct-sum E[1])^N,

where [1] shifts homological degrees up by one. All complexes and maps
remain inside the same formal cobordism category. Each pair E+E[1] has
zero full graded Euler characteristic, so C_N has exactly the unknot's
unreduced Euler polynomial q+q^-1.

At the ordinary Khovanov specialization, reduce at the chosen circle by
X=0 with the usual normalization. The resulting homology has

  rational reduced rank = 1+4N,
  integral reduced 2-torsion summands = 2N,
  mod-2 reduced rank = 1+8N,
  normalized reduced Euler polynomial = 1.

For example N=4 gives rational reduced rank17 and mod-2 rank33. Arbitrary
N shows that this is not merely an accidental small-rank loophole.
Meanwhile every semisimple specialization of C_N is chain equivalent to
the circle alone. In particular its rational Lee homology has rank two,
and its integral h=1,t=0 homology is the corresponding rank-two unknot
module, with all extra blocks contractible.

Thus adding coherent universal deformations and integral Bar-Natan
acyclicity does not force the Khovanov special fiber to have minimal rank.

4. What cohomology operations do and do not add to this test.

The ordinary integral special fiber just computed is a direct sum of
free one-generator complexes and two-term multiplication-by-2 complexes.
Quantum grade by quantum grade, this chain data is realized by wedges
of suspended spheres and mod-2 Moore spectra. The Moore pieces have
the nonzero first Bockstein/Sq^1 dictated by their integral 2-torsion;
the usual Steenrod relations are consistent, with higher squares zero.
So the construction does not evade integral constraints merely by
declaring all cohomology operations zero on torsion classes.

This does NOT construct the Lipshitz-Sarkar stable homotopy type of an
actual knot, nor prove compatibility with every knot-cobordism operation
or an odd/even unified theory. Those are additional geometric realization
conditions. Ordinary Steenrod-module axioms and integral universal
coefficients alone do not exclude this special fiber.

5. Precise obstruction and stopping point.

The proposed mechanism was: a Jones-unit complex cannot contain hidden
homology because all Lee/Bar-Natan deformations must be simultaneously
realizable by one integral universal Frobenius differential.

The construction above disproves that implication for formal geometric
complexes. Its excess is supported on the discriminant D=0, becomes
contractible on the entire semisimple locus, and cancels in the full
graded Euler characteristic. Universal specialization compatibility
cannot see it disappear by an algebraic contradiction.

The remaining realizability condition is specifically that the complex
must be produced by the signed planar cube of saddle maps of ONE knot
diagram, up to the established Reidemeister homotopies. Membership in
the ambient cobordism category is insufficient. A valid new proof must
extract a restriction from that cube origin which rules out these
blocks or their substitutes. No such restriction was proved in this
bounded pass. Asking simply that every Jones-unit knot complex be
indecomposable or have no such excess would restate the detection problem.

This supplies a reusable exact falsification test for proposed universal
deformation arguments. It does not supply a candidate knot, and should
not trigger enumeration for a diagram realizing C_N: such a realization
would already be a Jones counterexample.
