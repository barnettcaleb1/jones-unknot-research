Pass 52: root-of-unity Galois bootstrap audit
2026-11-01. Bounded test of one specific mechanism. No new promotion theorem.

Prior work read: outputs/jones-cabling-and-realizability.txt and the finite-order
realization/group-like discussion in outputs/jones-full-proof-scaffolds.md.
The question tested here is narrower than those earlier arguments: can the
Galois permutation of SU(2) modular labels turn ordinary Jones evaluations
into higher-color evaluations at infinitely many roots of unity?

VERDICT. No. The genuine modular Galois theorem does not give that scalar
identity, and the proposed identity fails on the actual trefoil. The existing
formal cyclotomic countermodel also obeys the root-reflection and arithmetic
Galois identities that genuinely apply to scalar colored Jones values.

1. Exact scalar reflection constraints.

For zero framing and reduced normalization H_N(U)=1, write the cyclotomic
kernel as

 P(N,k)=product_(j=1)^k(q^N+q^(-N)-q^j-q^(-j)).

For a root zeta of order r, dependence on N is through zeta^N+zeta^(-N).
The integral expansion therefore gives

 H_(N+r)(zeta)=H_N(zeta),       H_(r-N)(zeta)=H_N(zeta).

One may compare finite sums because the j=r factor kills all terms with
k>=r; factors with smaller indices also kill the extra terms when reflecting
into the integrable range 1,...,r-1. This includes H_(r-1)(zeta)=H_1(zeta)=1.

Primary expansion source: Garoufalidis--Kashaev, 'The descendant colored Jones
polynomials', equation (2), with the color dependence stated explicitly just
afterwards. Multiplying each pair of its factors shows that its kernel is P.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.07553

The old formal example F_N=1+(q-1)P(N,2) passes EVERY one of these reflection
and periodicity identities, at every root. It also has the legitimate
arithmetic covariance sigma_a(F_N(zeta))=F_N(zeta^a). Nevertheless

 F_3-1=q^(-6)(q-1)^2(q^2-1)(q^4-1)(q^5-1),

which is nonzero at every primitive root of order r>5. Hence the new scalar
tests do not remove the previously identified countermodel. This observation
does not assert that it is realized by a knot or a modular-functor state, nor
that it satisfies every categorical constraint.

2. What modular Galois symmetry actually states.

Dong--Lin--Ng, 'Congruence Property in Conformal Field Theory', Theorem II(iii)
and section 4.1, equations (4.2)--(4.5), give

 sigma(S)=G_sigma S=S G_sigma^(-1),
 sigma^2(rho(gamma))=G_sigma rho(gamma) G_sigma^(-1),

where G_sigma is a signed permutation matrix. They also explicitly give
sigma(S_ij/S_0j)=S_i,pi(j)/S_0,pi(j). These statements concern modular data
and modular-group representations. They are not a theorem equating Galois
conjugation of a normalized knot scalar with a same-knot color substitution.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1201.6644

The vacuum need not be fixed by pi. In SU(2) at level r-2, use dimension labels
n=1,...,r-1, with vacuum n=1 and S_nm=sqrt(2/r) sin(pi*n*m/r).
The label permutation associated to an odd cyclotomic automorphism a folds
an modulo 2r into 1,...,r-1, with a sign. It moves the vacuum as well as the
external color. Thus applying the full conjugation identity changes boundary
states on both sides of a matrix element. Dropping the changed vacuum is the
specific invalid step in this proposed bootstrap.

3. Actual-knot falsification, with all colors inside the integrable range.

Take r=7, zeta=exp(2*pi*i/7), and the SU(2) level-five theory. The automorphism
a=3 acts on its cyclotomic field (one may use the usual conductor 56). The
S-matrix label permutation sends 2 to 6 and the vacuum 1 to 3.

Choose the trefoil chirality whose ordinary reduced Jones polynomial is

                         H_2(q)=q+q^3-q^4.

Its difference from one factors as

                    H_2(q)-1=-(q-1)^2(q^2+q+1).

This is nonzero at every primitive seventh root. Consequently both
sigma_3(H_2(zeta)) and sigma_3^2(H_2(zeta)) differ from 1. But the exact
root-reflection identity gives H_6(zeta)=H_1(zeta)=1 for this actual knot.
Thus neither candidate scalar identity

 H_pi(2)(K;zeta)=sigma_3(H_2(K;zeta)), nor its sigma_3^2 variant,

is true in general. Unknot normalization cannot repair it: all reduced
unknot colors equal one, so any knot-independent scalar correcting such an
identity would itself have to be one. This is a geometric example, unlike
the formal sequence in part 1.

4. Full endpoint and whether its missing claim is new.

A possible repair would restrict a vacuum-replacement rule to knots with
H_2 identically one. For example, choose infinitely many odd primes r and
a=r+2; the SU(2) label permutation sends color 2 to color 4. A rule strong
enough to identify the changed-vacuum matrix element with the original
normalized knot scalar would then imply H_4(K;zeta_r)=1 for infinitely many r.
Laurent polynomiality would give H_4(K)=1 exactly.

Universally over the Jones-one class, this is not a demonstrably weaker new
target. The winding-three, meridian-one cable has ordinary Jones polynomial
a nonzero weighted sum of H_2 and H_4, with weights summing to one. Thus
universal H_2=1 => H_4=1 is equivalent to preservation of Jones-one by that
cable. The already proved connected-sum moment argument in the cabling report
then yields universal all-color promotion. The proposed special vacuum rule
has not been proved; at the required scalar level it merely reformulates
this existing promotion problem.

Even successful all-color promotion would not by itself finish the generic
unknot problem using presently established results. A complete conditional
chain is: special vacuum rule => H_4 promotion => cable preservation => all
colors trivial =>, ASSUMING the AJ conjecture for the knot, trivial A-polynomial
=> unknot by Dunfield--Garoufalidis. The unproved AJ step is separate. Their
paper expressly distinguishes its unconditional A-polynomial detector from
the colored-Jones consequence conditional on AJ:
https://msp.org/agt/2004/4-2/agt-v4-n2-p21-s.pdf

Conclusion: the overlooked Galois idea is ruled out in its unconditional
form by an actual knot; its Jones-one-specific repair reintroduces the same
unproved color-promotion step. This pass supplies no new route around that
gap, and no claim about general failure of quantum or categorical approaches.

Verification: the displayed factorizations and reflection assertions have
algebraic proofs above. Small numerical evaluations through root order 40
were used only as sanity checks, not as proofs of any universal statement.

5. Additional audit: the NEW 2026 arithmetic lift does not remove the model.

Garoufalidis--Wheeler, 'A lift of the colored Jones polynomial of a knot',
arXiv:2603.01619 (27 February 2026 manuscript), Theorem 1.3, constructs a
lift in H_(Z[t^±1,Delta(t)^(-1)] / Z[t^±1]), with evaluation t=q^(N-1).
Its root expansions obey Frobenius-twisted gluing, a stronger requirement
than ordinary cyclotomic integrality. This is genuinely additional to the
tests in the previous report and was checked against its precise definition.
https://guests.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/lift-colored-jones-knot.pdf

For Delta=1 the old model has an explicit lift. Define the Laurent polynomial

 f(t,q)=1+(q-1) product_(j=1)^2
                    (qt+q^(-1)t^(-1)-q^j-q^(-j)).

For each m>=1 define its component at the root zeta_m by

 f_m(t,u)=f(t^(1/m),zeta_m+u),      u=q-zeta_m.

This is a power series in u with coefficients in
Z[t^±(1/m),zeta_m], exactly the coefficient ring required at Delta=1.
Negative q powers are harmless because zeta_m is a unit, so their Taylor
coefficients remain integral in Z[zeta_m].

Definition 1.1, equation (7), requires

 f_m(t,q-zeta_pm+zeta_pm-zeta_m)=phi_p f_pm(t,q-zeta_pm).

The left side is f(t^(1/m),q). On the right, phi_p fixes q and roots of unity
and sends t^(1/(pm)) to t^(1/m). Thus the right side is literally the same
Laurent expression. Gluing therefore holds exactly for EVERY m and prime p,
not merely modulo p or to a finite Taylor order.

This construction is also the polynomial case of their Proposition 3.4 and
equations (99)--(102): the inverse of the untwisting isomorphism takes an
ordinary polynomial to the family with m-th component obtained by t->t^(1/m).
The same construction applies to Laurent polynomials because t and q are
units. It is essential to include this root of t: repeating f(t,q) unchanged
at every root would in general fail the Frobenius gluing condition.

At q=1 the lift has f(t,1)=1, precisely the loop constant term 1/Delta(t)
for Delta=1 in equation (14). At color N, its q=1 expansion evaluates to

                         f(q^(N-1),q)=F_N(q).

The evaluation map of Theorem 1.3/equation (12) gives this same Laurent
polynomial as an element of the usual Habiro ring: equality can be checked
in its injective Taylor expansion at q=1. Thus F_1=F_2=1 and F_3!=1 persist
inside the new Frobenius-compatible lift. Moreover f is q-holonomic under
t->qt: product_(j=-2)^2(E-q^j) annihilates it, since it is a Laurent
polynomial in t of degree between -2 and 2.

This decisively excludes deriving color promotion from the new arithmetic
lift property alone, even when combined with integral cyclotomic expansion,
all-color Laurent polynomiality, the Delta=1 loop constant term, the exact
root-reflection identities, and q-holonomicity already checked above.

Scope: the construction is a FORMAL element of the target ring, not an element
known to lie in the image of the knot map. It supplies neither a group-like
Kontsevich lift nor a classical-knot realization. The paper's geometric
compatibilities with actual skein modules and actual Dehn fillings have not
been imposed on this example. Thus it is not a counterexample to its theorem
or to Jones unknot detection; it is a counterexample to the proposed
arithmetic-only implication H_2=1 => all colors=1.

6. Stronger formal obstruction: erase any finite color prefix while retaining
   the new arithmetic lift AND proven positive-volume asymptotics.

Fix J to be the untwisted Whitehead double of the trefoil, with the convention
in Zheng's theorem below. Its normalized Alexander polynomial is 1. This
standard property of untwisted Whitehead doubling is stated in Garoufalidis,
'Whitehead doubling persists': https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0003189 .
Use the reduced, zero-framed, N-dimensional colored Jones polynomial H_N(J;q),
with H_N(unknot)=1 and H_2 the ordinary Jones polynomial. Write its integral
Habiro expansion in the kernel used throughout this note:

 H_N(J;q) = 1 + sum_(k>=1) a_k(q) P(N,k),
 P(N,k) = product_(j=1)^k (q^N+q^(-N)-q^j-q^(-j)),
 a_k(q) in Z[q^±1].

The sum truncates at k=N-1. This kernel/normalization is the one in
Garoufalidis--Kashaev, 'The descendant colored Jones polynomials', equation
(2), after multiplying its displayed elementary factors:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.07553 .

For every FIXED integer M>=1, define

 G_N^(M)(q) = H_N(J;q) - sum_(k=1)^M a_k(q) P(N,k).

Then G_N^(M)=1 identically for every 1<=N<=M+1. Its cyclotomic coefficients
are simply a_0=1, then M zero coefficients, then the original a_(M+1),....
Thus all-color Laurent polynomiality and integral cyclotomic expansion are
immediate. The root-reflection and arithmetic Galois properties already
discussed in this note follow by the same kernel argument.

Why ALL a_k(1)=0 for k>=1, not merely a_1(1)=0:
the MMR diagonal, equivalently the loop constant term (14) in the 2026 paper,
gives the identity in formal power series at t=1

 1/Delta_J(t) = sum_(k>=0) a_k(1)(t+t^(-1)-2)^k = 1.

To justify this use of the infinite sum, put q=e^h and take the diagonal
with x=Nh fixed formally. Each kernel factor has leading diagonal
e^x+e^(-x)-2, of x-adic order 2; hence only finitely many k contribute to
each x-degree. The shift t=q^(N-1) in the 2026 convention does not affect
this leading diagonal. The formal substitution y=t+t^(-1)-2 has order 2
and nonzero leading coefficient, so the map Q[[y]] -> Q[[t-1]] is injective.
This proves a_k(1)=0 for every k>=1. In particular
a_1(1)=-Delta_J''(1)/2=0: compare the coefficient of (t-1)^2, using the
symmetric normalization Delta(1)=1, Delta'(1)=0.

Let J_lift(t,q) be the actual lift supplied by Garoufalidis--Wheeler Theorem
1.3 in the Delta=1 ring. The finite Laurent polynomial

 C_M(t,q) = sum_(k=1)^M a_k(q)
             product_(j=1)^k(qt+q^(-1)t^(-1)-q^j-q^(-j))

has a lift by the explicit root-of-t construction in section 5 above.
Therefore G_lift^(M)=J_lift-C_M belongs to the SAME Frobenius-compatible
Habiro ring and evaluates to G_N^(M) at t=q^(N-1). All prescribed p-adic
root/Frobenius gluing identities survive exactly. Since C_M(t,1)=0,
G_lift^(M)(t,1)=1, and every higher loop coefficient remains a Laurent
polynomial in t. No convergence of the infinite Habiro series away from
the relevant formal topology is being assumed here; the subtraction is finite.

The sequence remains q-holonomic: H_N(J) is q-holonomic, and the correction
is a finite linear combination of q^(jN), with -M<=j<=M, over Q(q).
Addition closure is Theorem 5.2(a) of Garoufalidis--Le,
'A survey of q-holonomic functions': https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.07487 .

Now put zeta_N=exp(2*pi*i/N) and V=Vol(S^3 minus Whitehead link)>0.
Zheng, 'Proof of the volume conjecture for Whitehead doubles of a family
of torus knots', Theorem 1.4 and equation (1.6), proves for this J

 2*pi*log|H_N(J;zeta_N)| = V*N + 4*pi*log N + O(1).

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0508138 , printed page 3.
The theorem includes the untwisted double of T(3,2). It uses the normalized
N-dimensional Jones polynomial at exp(2*pi*i/N); its quantum-group parameter
elsewhere in section 2 is squared to obtain this Jones variable. Thus the
root in the theorem already matches ours, with no further square change.

For every fixed k, a_k(zeta_N)=O(N^(-1)) and

 P(N,k)|_(q=zeta_N) = product_(j=1)^k(2-2*cos(2*pi*j/N))
                    = O(N^(-2k)).

The total removed finite sum D_N is therefore O(N^(-3)). Zheng's theorem
also gives |H_N(J;zeta_N)|=N^2*exp(V*N/(2*pi))*exp(O(1)), so

 G_N^(M)(zeta_N)/H_N(J;zeta_N)
       = 1 + O(N^(-5)*exp(-V*N/(2*pi))).

Consequently the SAME two-term theorem holds for the formal model:

 2*pi*log|G_N^(M)(zeta_N)| = V*N + 4*pi*log N + O(1).

In particular its growth rate is V>0 despite every color through M+1
being identically 1. Constants in these estimates may depend on fixed M;
there is no claim for a prefix length increasing with N.

Conclusion and exact scope: any fixed finite set of trivial initial colors
is compatible with integral cyclotomic coefficients, the new 2026 arithmetic
lift and its Frobenius/root compatibility, q-holonomicity, Delta=1 loop
rationality, and this genuine positive exponential semiclassical growth.
The construction is an explicit FORMAL sequence, not a knot and not a
group-like Kontsevich series. No actual-knot realization, exact TQFT gluing,
peripheral skein-module representation, character-variety compatibility,
or full state-integral/saddle interpretation has been established. Thus
these listed necessary properties alone cannot force color promotion or
zero volume; this is not a counterexample to Jones unknot detection.

For comparison, the earlier proposed multiplicative modification
1+(q-1)P(N,2)(H_N(J)-1) also preserves the leading positive exponential
rate; its multiplier at zeta_N is 128*pi^5*i*N^(-5)(1+O(N^(-1))). It changes
Zheng's logarithmic coefficient from 4*pi to -6*pi. The finite coefficient
erasure above is therefore both simpler and stronger, and is the preferred
model for any report. The preserved two-term asymptotics do not assert that
Zheng proved a full expansion to all orders.
