PASS 52: BRUNNIANIZING THE BURAU KERNEL DOES NOT REMOVE THE RESIDUAL BLOCK
1 November 2026. Bounded structural pass; no braid-word search.

RESULT
No nontrivial full generic Jones kernel was obtained. There is a new precise
obstruction to the proposed higher-commutator mechanism: on the residual
five-dimensional B5 block, the intersection of the Burau kernel with the
Brunnian subgroup has full projective Zariski image. Every finite term of
its lower central series and its derived series still has full image.
Nevertheless the intersection of its lower central series is the identity.
Thus imposing Brunnian deletion constraints and arbitrarily increasing
commutator depth does not automatically convert the known partial kernel
into a full kernel. An isolated extra exact relation remains possible.

A separate cabling check shows that every uniform ODD parallel cable retains
the original full Jones operator as a subrepresentation; it cannot turn an
already detected braid into a full-kernel braid. Even cabling introduces new
colored conditions, not conditions established by Burau nonfaithfulness.

1. SETTING AND PUBLISHED INPUTS
Let rho be the generic five-dimensional TL5 block [3,2], over Q(A), with
rho(sigma_i)=A I+A^-1 e_i and delta=-A^2-A^-2. Let N be the generic reduced
Burau kernel in B5. It is contained in P5 and has exponent sum zero. Let
H=Brun_5 be the subgroup of pure braids becoming trivial after deletion of
ANY one strand. Both N and H are normal in P5 (indeed in B5).

Kuperberg Theorem 1.1 gives full projective Zariski closure PGL5 for rho(B5)
at a transcendental specialization of A. P5 has the same closure since it
has finite index and PGL5 is connected. A nontrivial projective image of a
normal subgroup of P5 therefore has closure PGL5: the closure is a nontrivial
closed normal subgroup of the simple adjoint algebraic group PGL5.
https://msp.org/gt/2011/15-1/gt-v15-n1-p02-s.pdf

The prior exact Bigelow-seed certificate supplies a nonidentity rho image
of an element of N. See
work/second-pass-evidence/braid_certificate/README.md and its verifier.
This is not a new scan. The seed has trivial scalar and Burau blocks but a
closure Jones polynomial different from its unknot anchor. Hence its residual
matrix is nonidentity. It cannot be a nonidentity scalar: its determinant
is one, its entries are rational functions, and its specialization at A=1
is I, so a scalar image would be identically I. Therefore rho(N) is
projectively Zariski dense. One can also state the following argument as a
dichotomy: if rho(N) were projectively trivial, the required full kernel
would already exist; the cached certificate establishes the other branch.

2. AN EXPLICIT BRUNNIAN WITNESS FOR THE RESIDUAL BLOCK
Use [x,y]=xyx^-1y^-1 and the standard pure generators A_(i,5). Put

       b=[A_(1,5),[A_(2,5),[A_(3,5),A_(4,5)]]].

Deleting strand 5 kills all four inputs. Deleting any other strand kills
one leaf of the commutator, forcing the full nested word to be the identity.
Thus b belongs to H. It is not a formal Lie element: it is an actual pure
braid on five strands.

Set h=A-1. At A=1, the representation specializes to the S5 representation
[3,2], with s_(i,5) denoting the transposition matrix. The generator relation

 g^2=(A-A^-3)g+A^-2 I

implies (g^2)'(1)=4g(1)-2I. Conjugating a squared generator to A_(i,5) gives

 rho(A_(i,5))=I+h(4s_(i,5)-2I)+O(h^2).

Hence
 rho(b)=I+4^4 h^4 C+O(h^5),
 C=[s_(1,5),[s_(2,5),[s_(3,5),s_(4,5)]]].

Here C is nonzero on [3,2]. The following exact finite verification avoids
choosing a Specht basis. The 10-dimensional permutation module on unordered
2-subsets of {1,...,5} decomposes as [5] direct-sum [4,1] direct-sum [3,2].
The 5-dimensional permutation module on vertices is [5] direct-sum [4,1].
All transposition matrices are orthogonal, so the squared Frobenius norm of
C on the residual summand is the difference of its norms on these two
permutation modules. Exact integer matrix multiplication gives

           ||C||^2_(2-subsets)=88,
           ||C||^2_(vertices)=8,
           ||C||^2_[3,2]=80 > 0.

The decomposition can also be seen directly: map each vertex to the sum of
2-subsets containing it; this is an injective equivariant map, and its
orthogonal complement is the five-dimensional [3,2] summand. The scalar
summand contributes zero to the commutator. The certificate and all matrices
are saved as work/research52/brunnian_leading.py and brunnian_leading.json.
This calculation checks a predicted leading term, not a word search.

Consequently rho(b) is generically nonidentity, and non-scalar (the leading
commutator has trace zero). Normality plus Kuperberg now proves that rho(H)
has projective Zariski closure PGL5. The symmetric-group specialization and
Young-diagram labeling agree with Kasahara Proposition 2.5:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0304

3. THE INTERSECTION AND ALL ITS FINITE COMMUTATOR STAGES REMAIN DENSE
Let J=N intersect H. Since both groups are normal,

                      [N,H] is contained in J.

The product of two Zariski-dense subsets is dense in PGL5 x PGL5. Apply the
regular commutator map. The algebraic subgroup generated by its values is
the derived group of PGL5, namely PGL5 itself. It follows that rho([N,H]),
and hence rho(J), have full projective Zariski closure.

The same argument inductively proves

 closure(rho(gamma_k(J)))=PGL5 for every finite k>=1,
 closure(rho(J^(d)))=PGL5 for every finite derived-series stage d>=0.

In particular none of these subgroups is contained in ker rho. Neither
Brunnian deletion constraints nor taking an entire sufficiently deep
commutator subgroup kills the unresolved Jones block. There is no automatic
triangularization, nilpotent image, or scalar image after those operations.
A fixed nontrivial group word cannot be a universal kernel recipe for
arbitrary inputs from J: density would turn it into a group law on PGL5,
which contains a nonabelian free subgroup and satisfies no such law.
This last statement does not exclude a specially chosen tuple satisfying
an accidental exact matrix relation.

Pure braid groups are residually nilpotent. Since gamma_k(J) is contained
in gamma_k(P5), we also have

                       intersection_k gamma_k(J)={1}.

See Cohen--Prassidis, proof of Corollary 1.4, for the residual-nilpotence
input: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0404278 . Thus every finite stage here can
have a dense image even though their infinite intersection contains only
the trivial braid. Passing to an infinite-depth limit is not a construction
of a finite nontrivial braid. Long's finite normal-intersection lemma does
not justify an infinite intersection or a completed formal braid.

4. DEGREE AND STRAND COST
The witness above uses five strands throughout. With the standard words,
|A_(i,5)|=2(5-i), so its displayed nested-commutator word has length at most
88. It is detected at exactly order h^4. Deletion of all proper sub-braids
therefore does not mean a whole generic Laurent identity.

In a link-state TL module, a generator or inverse has Laurent entries with
exponents between -3 and 3. A word of length L consequently has every entry
of rho(beta)-I supported in [-3L,3L]. Vanishing at A=1 through more than
6L orders would certify the polynomial identity, but formal commutator depth
does not reach that threshold automatically. Balanced commutators of inputs
with length L0 and vanishing order r0 yield the generic estimates

          length <=4^d L0,     vanishing order >=2^d r0.

The guaranteed order-to-length ratio decreases. Actual cancellations may
improve these estimates; proving them is extra information, not a consequence
of being Brunnian or lying deep in the lower central series. No new strand
count or word-length scan is warranted by this mechanism.

5. CABLING CHECK
Use the generic semisimple Uq(sl2) category and let V1 be its fundamental
2-dimensional object. Uniform r-parallel blackboard cabling of an n-braid
is the braid action on (V1^tensor r)^tensor n. For odd r, V1 occurs as a
direct summand of V1^tensor r. Choose one split inclusion i:V1->V1^tensor r
and projection p with p i=id. Naturality of the braiding gives, for the
uniform cable c_r(beta),

 p^tensor n rho_(rn)(c_r(beta)) i^tensor n = rho_n(beta).

Therefore a full identity for the odd cable forces a full identity for the
original braid. In particular an odd cable of the cached Bigelow seed
cannot repair its nonzero residual block. This is an exact operator result,
not just a comparison of closure traces. The raw framed ribbon convention
must be consistent on both sides; natural block cabling supplies that.

Even r has only even labels in the decomposition of V1^tensor r. It contains
V0,V2,...,Vr, and the cabled operator tests their mixed-color sectors. These
are NEW colored braid representations. Burau-kernel membership supplies no
identity on the V2 sector, let alone on all mixed sectors. No claim that
an even cable can never help is proved here; it requires a genuinely new
colored-kernel input. It is not a consequence of the known Burau kernel.

Uniform cabling sends n to rn and a length-L diagram to one of length r^2 L.
The target has floor(rn/2)+1 TL blocks. This growth buys new constraints;
it does not inherit full coverage from the old first two blocks.
Freedman--Walker--Wang Theorem 5.1 runs in the detecting direction: every
nontrivial braid is detected after SOME cabling. It does not say a known
partial kernel becomes a full kernel under cabling.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0209150

6. FULL ENDPOINT AND DECISION
At a fixed strand count, independently established noncentral kernels for
ALL generic Jones blocks can be intersected by Long; Ito then produces
nontrivial hyperbolic Jones-one knot closures. That endpoint remains exact.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.2028 , Theorem 1 and the discussion of Long's lemma.

The new calculation does not furnish the missing block kernel. It proves
that the large subgroup obtained by simultaneously imposing the known
Burau identity and every strand-deletion identity STILL retains all the
projective algebraic complexity of that block, even after every finite
commutator-depth restriction. Odd cabling retains the obstruction; even
cabling asks for additional unproved colored identities. No complete generic
kernel mechanism survives from these automatic operations alone. Stop this
branch unless an actual exact residual relation is independently motivated.
No claim of literature novelty or of a solution to Jones detection.
