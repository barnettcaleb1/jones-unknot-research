# Independent audit: a braid cannot implement a nontrivial two-cap rank-one phase

Status: proof audit of the proposed theorem, with no braid scan. The argument is valid with the coefficient-field and normalization hypotheses stated below. No fatal gap found.

## Precise theorem

Let `rho: B_6 -> GL_9(Q(s))` be the generic normalized Temperley–Lieb representation W(6,2), with

```
rho(sigma_i)=I+s e_i,
delta=-s-s^-1,
P0=e_1 e_3/delta^2.
```

For any braid w, put `P_C=rho(w)P0 rho(w)^-1`. If a braid b satisfies

```
rho(b)=alpha I + beta P_C
```

over any coefficient extension of Q(s), then beta=0. Equivalently, no braid acts by one scalar on the rank-one cap line and a different scalar on its eight-dimensional complementary channel.

This does **not** assert that every rank-one operator or every arbitrary rank-one projector has this property. It concerns the braid-conjugacy orbit of the two-cap projector. It also does not assert that a sum or product of arbitrary non-braid operators cannot isolate that channel.

Conjugate b by w to reduce to P0. Multiplying the whole braid representation by its usual writhe-dependent scalar does not affect the statement, because that scalar is absorbed into alpha and beta.

## 1. Eigenvalues really are Laurent units

Write lambda=alpha+beta. Since P0 has trace and rank one,

```
lambda=Tr(rho(b) P0),
alpha=(Tr(rho(b))-lambda)/8.
```

Thus alpha and lambda belong to Q(s), even if the initially proposed coefficients were allowed in a larger field. In the link-state basis, rho(b) and its inverse have Laurent-polynomial entries. Alpha and lambda are roots of its monic characteristic polynomial, hence integral over Q[s,s^-1]. This ring is integrally closed in Q(s), so they lie in the Laurent ring. Since

```
det rho(b)=alpha^8 lambda
```

is a Laurent unit, both alpha and lambda are units. Consequently

```
alpha=c s^a, lambda=d s^b,
```

with c,d nonzero rationals and a,b integers. Working over Z[s,s^-1] would further restrict c,d to signs, but that stronger assertion is unnecessary.

## 2. Classical specialization forces a pure braid and constants +1

At s=1 the normalized generators become the transpositions T_i in the S_6 representation Specht(4,2). The projector specializes to

```
P=(I-T_12)(I-T_34)/4.
```

The two factors are commuting self-adjoint involution projections. Its rank is one; alternatively its trace is `(9-3-3+1)/4=1`.

The character of this representation is

```
chi(pi)=binom(f,2)+m2-f,
```

where f is the number of fixed points of pi and m2 its number of 2-cycles. One direct model is the kernel of the signless vertex-edge incidence map from the 15-dimensional permutation space on unordered pairs to the six-dimensional permutation space on vertices. Its character is therefore fixed unordered pairs minus fixed vertices, giving the formula. This is the standard polytabloid model for Specht(4,2).

The possible character values are `9,3,1,0,-1`; value 9 occurs only for the identity. In particular neither 7 nor -7 nor -9 occurs.

Because rho(b)(1) belongs to a finite real representation, its rational eigenvalues c,d are ±1. If c and d differ, the character is ±7, impossible. If both are -1, the character is -9, impossible. Thus c=d=1. The character is then 9, so the permutation of b is the identity. Therefore b is pure and

```
alpha=s^a, lambda=s^b.
```

This avoids an unstated appeal to faithfulness of the S_6 representation.

## 3. The pure-braid tangent space is small

The Hecke relation in this normalization gives

```
R_i(s)^2=(1-s^2)R_i(s)+s^2 I,
R_i(1)=T_i.
```

Differentiating at s=1 yields

```
(R_i^2)'(1)=2(I-T_i).
```

The pure braid group is generated by conjugates of the squares of standard generators. The derivative of each such conjugate at s=1 is a conjugate of `2(I-T_i)` by a permutation matrix in this representation: derivatives of the conjugating braid cancel because the square specializes to I. Derivatives add under multiplication of pure braids. Hence

```
rho(b)'(1) belongs to L=span_Q{I,T_ab : 1<=a<b<=6}.
```

No assumption about arbitrary braid derivatives is being made.

## 4. Exact certificate that the two-cap projector is not in L

Use the positive trace inner product on real self-adjoint endomorphisms. The 15 transpositions have Gram matrix

```
G_ef=Tr(T_e T_f)
     =9 if e=f,
      0 if e,f share one endpoint,
      1 if e,f are disjoint.
```

The transposition sum acts as 5I (centrality and trace), so adding I does not enlarge their span. Their Gram matrix is `9I + A`, where A is the disjointness matrix on the 15 edges of K_6. Its eigenvalues are 15,6,10 on subspaces of dimensions 1,5,9, respectively. To derive this directly, let B be the signless 6-by-15 incidence matrix. Then `A=J-B^T B+I` and `B B^T=4I+J`; the three subspaces are constants, B^T applied to vertex vectors of sum zero, and ker(B).

Set `v_e=Tr(P T_e)`. Expanding P and applying the elementary character formula gives

```
v_12=v_34=-1,
v_56=+1,
v_e=1/2 for all other e.
```

The squared norms of the projections of v onto the preceding three eigenspaces are respectively `5/3,4/3,3`. These can be obtained without enumeration: sum(v)=5 and ||v||^2=6; its vertex-incidence sums are `(1,1,1,1,3,3)`, whose mean is 5/3. The centered vertex-incidence map has squared singular value four, giving the middle norm 4/3. The remaining norm is three.

Therefore the orthogonal projection of P onto L has squared norm

```
v^T G^-1 v = (5/3)/15 + (4/3)/6 + 3/10 = 19/30.
```

But `||P||^2=Tr(P^2)=1`. The residual squared norm is 11/30, strictly positive, so P is outside L.

This independently confirms the supplied exact matrix-span certificate. It is a representation-theoretic calculation, not a search through braids.

## 5. Completion

Differentiate the hypothesized rank-one phase formula at s=1. Since alpha(1)=lambda(1)=1, the derivative of P0(s) is multiplied by zero and drops out:

```
rho(b)'(1)=a I + (b-a)P.
```

The left side lies in L, as does I, whereas P does not. Hence b-a=0. The Laurent-unit eigenvalues are therefore identical as functions of s, not merely to first order. Thus beta=lambda-alpha=0.

The Laurent-unit step is essential: without it, equal value and derivative at s=1 would not force equality of two arbitrary rational eigenvalue functions. The proof contains that step and is complete under the stated generic representation hypothesis.

## Scope for the research program

This gives a rigorous obstruction to copying the width-four rank-one commutator argument by finding a single width-six braid that is scalar plus the two-cap projector. It does not rule out a different braid word identity, a categorical projector, several operators together, or a nonlinear geometric rigidity theorem. It proves no unknot-detection theorem by itself.
