STRATEGIC RESET: EXACT QUANTUM SYMMETRY, TESTED BY 3-MANIFOLD GLUING
2026-10-31. Strategic memo, not a new theorem or a claimed counterexample.

Recommendation
--------------
Pursue one concrete mechanism: use a published, all-q Jones-preserving
operation on ACTUAL tangles, and make the missing work entirely geometric.
Rotants and related commuting-transfer-matrix surgeries supply this.
They are an old research direction, not a newly discovered solution. They
are nevertheless substantially different from our exhausted attempts to
promote one scalar into colored data or to realize arbitrary formal modules.

The physics connection is exact integrability: a protected partition
function survives a rearrangement of interactions even when the geometric
configuration changes. The useful question is whether that protected
rearrangement can act nontrivially on an unknot completion. It is not whether
more measurements can reconstruct the knot from the original scalar.

Published starting evidence
--------------------------
Anstee-Przytycki-Rolfsen prove Jones preservation for rotants of order n<=5,
with the required orientation/writhe conditions. Their Question 3.5 already
asks whether an unknot can have a knotted rotant; novelty is not claimed.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0405382

MacColl constructs an infinite family of ORDER-4 rotants with different
Khovanov homology and the same Jones polynomial. This is useful evidence:
the operation is not automatically invisible to an unknot-detecting
homology theory. It is not evidence that the published family contains an
unknot anchor, and does not justify scanning its twist parameter blindly.
https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/D57214ADA03771549477CCEFE30A8219/S0008439500019718a.pdf/rotors_in_khovanov_homology.pdf

Jones' transfer-matrix mechanism and its tangle interpretation are described
by Przytycki in Section 3 of this primary account:
https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/bcp/bcp34/bcp34111.pdf

Precise construction bridge
----------------------------
Let U=R union S be a CERTIFIED unknot diagram with an n-rotor R, n=3,4,5.
Let f be the allowed flip, with normalized Jones invariance verified by the
published rotor hypotheses. Let M_R and M_S be the double covers of the two
tangle balls, branched over their strands. The boundary is a surface Sigma
of genus n-1, by Riemann-Hurwitz. Since the starting knot is U,
  M_R union_id M_S = S^3.
The flipped knot K has double branched cover
  Y_f = M_R union_(f-tilde) M_S,
where f-tilde is a lift of the marked-sphere flip (convention may replace it
by its inverse; this does not affect the proposed test).

A single certified example with pi_1(Y_f) nontrivial proves K nontrivial,
while Jones preservation already proves V_K=1. This is an exact sufficient
bridge, using no colored-Jones promotion and no finite-level approximation.
The two lifts differ by the deck map, which extends over each piece, so the
homeomorphism type of Y_f is independent of that choice.

This changes the inverse-design problem: build an equivariant S^3 splitting
whose allowed rotor flip produces a nontrivial homology sphere. A nontrivial
finite quotient of the resulting fundamental-group presentation is a compact
positive certificate. Candidate finite perfect quotients such as A5 are
reasonable targets, but no such quotient or candidate is claimed here.

Why the first test must be nonabelian
------------------------------------
Jones invariance and the unknot anchor imply det(K)=|V_K(-1)|=1. Consequently
H_1(Y_f;Z)=0. Every successful candidate is already an integral homology
sphere. Checking a determinant or first homology can expose a convention or
construction error, but can never certify success. For handlebody pieces,
the homology quotient
  H_1(Sigma)/(L_R + f-tilde(L_S))
is therefore forced to vanish. The new information must live in nonabelian
meridian words, disk sets, or a genuine 3-manifold invariant.

What handlebodies and Waldhausen do -- and do not -- give
--------------------------------------------------------
If both tangles are trivial n-string tangles, both branched covers are
handlebodies. The unknot completion then gives a genus n-1 Heegaard splitting
of S^3. Fix its common-boundary marking, and let G_R and G_S denote the
subgroups of mapping classes extending across the respective handlebodies.
With a consistent gluing convention, the flipped cover is S^3 exactly when
  f-tilde belongs to G_S G_R.
Sufficiency follows by absorbing the two extending maps into the pieces.
Necessity follows from uniqueness of the genus-g Heegaard splitting of S^3
(Waldhausen), allowing an ordered-piece equivalence.

The Goeritz group is the INTERSECTION G_R intersect G_S. It is not the
product in the preceding criterion. Failure to extend over either piece,
or failure to lie in the Goeritz group, does NOT by itself prove that the
new manifold differs from S^3. Conversely, finding an extension over either
piece is an excellent immediate exclusion certificate. For nonhandlebody
pieces, extension/product membership remains sufficient for exclusion;
Waldhausen no longer supplies its necessity.

A primary new proof of the Heegaard uniqueness theorem:
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607332

Smallest sector not eliminated by these arguments
------------------------------------------------
The familiar two-string Conway mutation cannot unknot a nontrivial knot or
knot the unknot. Order 3 already lifts to genus 2, but that fact does NOT
eliminate it. The hyperelliptic involution is the DECK transformation: it
projects to identity on the six-marked sphere. An allowed rotor flip has a
nontrivial permutation of the marked points, so its lift is not that
hyperelliptic mapping class. The Birman-Hilden quotient distinguishes them.
Thus the theorem that hyperelliptic genus-2 mutation of S^3 is S^3 cannot be
applied merely by counting the genus.

Primary references for these distinctions:
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0010310
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0607258 (Section 2.6)

Accordingly, n=3 is not excluded by the present cover argument. Order 4 is
the more justified practical starting sector because the published
Khovanov-changing examples show extra freedom explicitly. I am not claiming
a new classification or that all trivial-tangle rotor constructions survive;
the specific equivariant disk systems must still be checked.

Proof branch versus counterexample branch
----------------------------------------
PROOF OF A FAMILY OBSTRUCTION: show that cyclic symmetry plus the particular
unknot-completion geometry forces f-tilde into G_S G_R (or forces extension
over one actual piece). This would eliminate the whole chosen construction,
not establish general Jones detection.

COUNTEREXAMPLE: maintain the geometric rotor symmetry and the certified
unknot completion, and exhibit a nontrivial quotient of pi_1(Y_f). For
handlebodies this is a balanced presentation obtained by expressing one
side's flipped disk boundaries as words in the other side's free group.
An arbitrary mapping class with an interesting quotient is insufficient:
it must descend to the permitted rotor flip with the required cyclic action.

Decision gates before computation
---------------------------------
1. Specify an actual rotor and an actual unknot completion, with a geometric
   unknot certificate. Formal skein equality is insufficient.
2. Identify the lifted flip and disk systems. If the flip visibly extends
   over either piece, stop that family before polynomial work.
3. If both pieces are handlebodies, ask for product-of-handlebody-groups
   membership, not only individual extension or a homological test.
4. Only then seek a nonabelian quotient or other non-S^3 certificate.
5. If the entire chosen family always yields a standard disk system, record
   that obstruction and abandon it; do not respond by simply increasing
   crossing bounds.

No knot candidate or generic proof has emerged in this strategic pass. The
concrete gain is selecting a route with its Jones identity already proved,
so the next unit of work must address the actual topological bottleneck.
