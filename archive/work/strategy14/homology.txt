STRATEGIC RESET: GEOMETRY AND EULER CANCELLATION
31 October 2026. Status: assessment, not a proof of Jones unknot detection.

Decision

Park the formal four-special-component object D. It is a useful stress test and
a conditional counterexample specification, but it is presently a poor route
to an answer. No classical diagram realizes it. Even proving that this one D
is unrealizable would not settle arbitrary knots; no generic reduction to the
unknot-anchored tangle setting has been established. More checks of necessary
objectwise conditions do not supply either missing step.

Two genuinely different geometric mechanisms were considered below. Neither
currently supplies an independently easier decisive lemma. This is an explicit
negative strategic result: we should not disguise a restatement of the Jones
conjecture as a new geometric conjecture.

1. Geometric image rigidity, rather than more algebraic object conditions

Known bridge. Abouzaid--Smith realize Khovanov homology as Lagrangian Floer
cohomology in characteristic zero. Their symplectic arc algebra and cup/cap
bimodules are formal. A link is made by a constrained sequence of crossings,
cups and caps; an arbitrary direct sum of shifted objects need not be in the
image of that construction.

Sources:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.5535
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.01230

Potential mechanism. A geometric condition on this specific image, absent from
the ambient additive/derived category, could exclude an Euler-invisible
summand. Examples of the kind of input required are an actual stability
condition respected by the diagrammatic construction, or a geometric
obstruction to realizing a particular extension by embedded classical
tangles. An arbitrary heart, positive cone, or sheaf simplicity condition is
not enough unless its preservation under BOTH crossing signs and cups/caps is
proved.

Decisive missing lemma for the current counterexample route: characterize the
image sufficiently to decide whether D comes from a classical tangle, or give
a crossing/cup/cap construction realizing D. This is a real realization
problem, not a consequence of the A-infinity Maurer--Cartan equation.

Decisive missing lemma for a generic proof would be much stronger: Jones-unit
objects in the actual knot image have reduced homology rank one. Without an
independent geometric formulation and proof method this is exactly the
original conjecture transported into another category, not progress.

Earliest kill tests. (a) Formality is already present for all knots and does not
imply parity purity of their Floer groups. (b) Actual tangle invariants can split
into rational and special components: the strongly inverted trefoil example is
r_1(4) plus s_4. Thus 'geometric objects are indecomposable' is false in the
relevant tangle category. (c) Our actual Watson example and ribbon examples
already exhibit opposite homological parities at a fixed quantum grading.
Any proposed positivity condition must first survive those examples.

Published source for the actual trefoil and special-sector framework:
  https://msp.org/obs/2022/5-1/obs-v5-n1-p10-p.pdf
  Example 2.2 and Lemmas 2.5, 3.6.

Go/no-go gate. Resume this route only with an explicit geometric invariant or
an actual synthesis move that is not automatic in the formal category. Adding
microlocal-sheaf, Hodge, or BPS terminology without such an invariant changes
neither the evidence nor the missing step.

2. Transfer a genus-detecting gauge-theory class into an uncancelled Jones term

Known bridge. Kronheimer--Mrowka's spectral sequence transfers reduced Kh to
singular instanton homology and proves rank-one unknot detection. A geometric
proof of Jones detection would need more than this rank transfer: it must make
some nontrivial-knot contribution visible in the signed quantum Euler sum.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.4346

Potential mechanism. A Seifert-surface-compatible refinement could constrain
which quantum degrees and parities can support the extremal instanton
eigenspace. A usable decisive lemma would identify, for every positive-genus
knot, a nonzero quantum Euler coefficient outside the unknot contribution,
using geometric surface data. Such a lemma would solve the problem. Merely
showing survival or nonvanishing of an extremal class does not establish it.

Earliest kill test, which already rules out the naive version: extremal
instanton homology itself need not have nonzero Euler characteristic. For a
nontrivial Alexander-polynomial-one knot, e.g. Kinoshita--Terasaka, let g>0 be
its genus. Genus detection gives KHI(K,g) != 0, whereas the Alexander Euler
identity gives chi(KHI(K,g)) = 0. Consequently its even and odd ranks are equal
and positive. Thus geometric genus detection and top-eigenspace nonvanishing
are demonstrably compatible with complete signed cancellation in that same
extremal eigenspace. This is an actual knot, not our artificial object.

Primary sources for the two facts:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.4891  (Knots, sutures and excision)
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.4639  (Theorem 1.1, Alexander Euler identity)
For the Alexander-one Kinoshita--Terasaka family and knot Floer calculations:
  https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/UW/gt/ftp/main/m7/m7l7.pdf

This does not disprove a new Jones-specific refinement; it establishes exactly
why generic 'gauge-theory positivity' or 'the top class cannot disappear'
arguments are insufficient. Any proposed refinement must explain why the
Alexander cancellation example does not carry over. If the repair inserts
additional colors or satellite probes, it must separately prove those probes
are controlled by the ordinary Jones-unit hypothesis. That is the same missing
information bridge as the global cabling approach, not a free consequence of
instanton detection.

Recommended allocation

Keep D as a recorded conditional target and stop extending its certificates.
Do not pursue purity/formality or extremal nonvanishing as generic proof
mechanisms: their naive decisive lemmas already fail. Prefer an exact global
bridge with a clearly identified hypothesis over further local necessary
conditions. No credible geometric theorem found in this bounded assessment
currently supplies the needed bridge from V_K=1 to homological rank one.
