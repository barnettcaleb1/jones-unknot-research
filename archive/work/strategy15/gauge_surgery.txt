JONES UNIT, BRANCHED COVERS, AND GAUGE THEORY: STRATEGIC AUDIT
31 October 2026. No resolution of the Jones unknot conjecture is claimed.

Outcome

The double-cover route has a rigorous recognition endpoint, but the familiar
Jones-to-Casson bridge stops at a signed count. There is an actual, infinite
family showing that even the whole Jones polynomial plus signature does not
recover the full Floer information of the cover. LMO/WRT constructions require
additional input; evaluating the same fundamental Jones polynomial at more
roots does not by itself supply that input.

1. Exact consequence of V_K(t)=1

Put Y=Sigma_2(K). Then |H_1(Y;Z)|=det(K)=|V_K(-1)|=1. Mullins' formula,
with lambda normalized as twice ordinary Casson on integral homology spheres,
is

  lambda(Y) = -V'_K(-1)/(6 V_K(-1)) + sigma(K)/4.

Hence

  V_K=1  =>  lambda_C(Y)=sigma(K)/8.

Casson therefore vanishes if signature zero is independently known, as for a
ribbon candidate. Do not assume signature zero from V_K=1 generically.

Mullins, Topology 32 (1993), 419--438, Theorem 5.1:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-9383(93)90029-U
Exact formula and normalization checked in Greene--Watson, Theorem 13:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1106.5559

2. The recognition endpoint is already a theorem

Cornwell--Ng--Sivek, Theorem 3.1, prove that pi_1(Sigma_2(K)) admits a
nontrivial SO(3) representation if and only if K is nontrivial. Their proof
uses the traceless-meridian SU(2) representation supplied by
Kronheimer--Mrowka. For determinant-one K, H^2(Y;Z/2)=0 removes the obstruction
to lifting the SO(3) representation to SU(2). Since H_1(Y;Z)=0, a nontrivial
SU(2) representation cannot be abelian, and is therefore irreducible.

Thus the following would suffice for generic Jones detection:

  V_K=1 implies that Sigma_2(K) has no irreducible SU(2) representation.

There is no need to assume the general conjecture that every nontrivial
integral homology sphere has an irreducible SU(2) representation. But the
displayed implication is not proved by the Jones information we possess.

Primary source, Theorem 3.1:
  https://sites.math.duke.edu/~ng/math/papers/concordances.pdf
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.1364

3. A decisive kill test for the Casson shortcut

Choose any nontrivial Alexander-polynomial-one knot J and take Y=S^3_{+1}(J).
The Casson surgery formula gives

  lambda_C(Y) = (1/2) Delta_J''(1) = 0.

Nevertheless Kronheimer--Mrowka's Property P theorem gives a nontrivial SO(3)
representation of pi_1(Y), which lifts to an irreducible SU(2) representation
because Y is an integral homology sphere. Therefore 'Casson zero implies no
irreducible representations' is false for actual surgery manifolds.

This failure also has an irreducible, toroidal example: +1 surgery on the
untwisted Whitehead double of the right-handed trefoil is the splice
of two right-handed trefoil exteriors. Hedden--Levine identify this manifold
and compute HF-hat rank 7. Its Alexander polynomial input is one, so Casson is
zero, while the splice is nontrivial. This example is not asserted to have a
Jones-one branch knot; its role is precisely to refute the proposed inference
from the extracted 3-manifold constraints.

Primary sources:
  https://people.math.harvard.edu/~kronheim/wittenp.pdf  (Theorem 2)
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.7055  (introduction and explicit splice example)
  Jim Hoste, A formula for Casson's invariant, Trans. AMS 297 (1986), 547--562.

4. Stronger no-go: the entire Jones polynomial still leaves cover data free

Greene--Watson's ribbon Kanenobu knots K_n=K_{-10n,10n+3} have fixed Jones,
signature zero and determinant 25. Their covers' minimum correction terms tend
to minus infinity, although Casson--Walker stays constant. Thus full Jones
plus signature does not determine these Floer invariants. This is outside the
Jones-unit fiber, and does not establish that these covers have different
LMO or WRT invariants; that stronger claim was not verified here.

Primary source: Greene--Watson, Sections 3--4, especially Corollary 8,
Proposition 11, Theorem 13 and Proposition 14:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.5559

5. What the all-q and perturbative routes actually require

Garoufalidis--Kricker provide the genuine all-orders branched-cover bridge:

  Z(Sigma_p(K)) = exp(sigma_p(K) Theta/16)
                 Lift_p [tau^rat_{alpha_p}(Z^rat(K))].

Their Theorem 1 uses the rational Kontsevich invariant Z^rat, followed by
twisting and lifting of diagrams, as well as the total signature. It does not
take the ordinary Jones polynomial as input. The hypothesis V_K=1 kills the
fundamental sl_2 evaluation of the knot's perturbative invariant; it does not
assert that the universal diagram-valued input equals that of the unknot.
To exploit the theorem one must prove that the needed lifting/weight-system
functional factors through precisely that fundamental evaluation, at least
on the unit fiber. No such factorization follows from their theorem.

Primary source, Theorem 1:
  https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/CBC.pdf
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0107220

At a root of unity, surgery quantum invariants similarly insert a Kirby color
(a sum of allowed irreducible colors) on the surgery link. This is not the
single fundamental Wilson-loop insertion supplied by ordinary Jones. The
distinction remains even when V_K is known at every root of unity.

Habiro proves a useful continuation theorem once the MANIFOLD invariant is
known: its entire Ohtsuki series determines its unified invariant and all
SU(2) WRT values. This does not identify the Jones Taylor series of the BRANCH
KNOT with the Ohtsuki series of its COVER. Moreover, equality of unified
invariants is equality of quantum invariants, not a homeomorphism theorem by
definition; that would be another recognition step to justify.

Primary source:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0605314
  Theorems 1.2, 1.4 and equation (1.6).

Strategic recommendation

Do not spend a further pass computing Casson zero for candidates already known
to have V=1 and sigma=0: it is automatic and supplies no representation-space
vanishing. Retain the Cornwell--Ng--Sivek theorem as a clean recognition
endpoint. The only substantive entry point for this route would be a new,
Jones-specific theorem controlling UNSIGNED representations, or a proven
factorization of the branched-cover perturbative invariant through ordinary
Jones. Neither has been obtained. The existing surgery and quantum formulas
identify the missing input sharply; they do not bypass it.
