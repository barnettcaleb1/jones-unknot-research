THE AUGMENTED-UNLINK GATE FOR THE EQUIVARIANT SURGERY CONSTRUCTION

Status: conceptual audit, not a counterexample construction or a proof of Jones
unknot detection. Two elementary necessary conditions are proved below. Neither
supplies the required geometric surgery link.

SETUP AND THE FIRST GATE

Let B contain a standard boundary-parallel C4 rotor R0, completed by a stator S
to the unknot U. Let eta_0,...,eta_3 be a free C4 orbit of disjoint surgery circles
in B minus R0. The required global hypothesis is that these four circles bound
SIMULTANEOUSLY DISJOINT embedded disks in S3 minus U. Framings are the disk
framings. This is stronger than zero linking and stronger than four separate
disk certificates with intersections among their disks.

The augmented link L=U union eta_0 union ... union eta_3 is therefore the
five-component unlink. Indeed, a regular neighborhood of each disk is a ball
disjoint from U and from the other disk neighborhoods, separating its boundary
component. The remaining component U is an unknot.

Include the eta circles as closed components of the rotor. After the prescribed
flip mu, the augmented link L'=U' union mu(eta_0) union ... union mu(eta_3) is a
C4 rotant of L. This use of closed rotor components is explicitly permitted in
Section 1 of Anstee--Przytycki--Rolfsen. The original trivial-rotor result gives
U'=U up to isotopy; the four-circle sublink is also an unlink, because it is the
image of an unlink in B under a homeomorphism of B. These assertions do not imply
that their UNION is an unlink.

NECESSARY CONDITION. If any equal 1/k surgeries on the flipped circle orbit
produce a nontrivial knot, L' must already be a nontrivial five-component link.

Proof. If L' is the unlink, its surgery circles have disjoint disk neighborhoods
disjoint from U'. Any 1/k surgery on such a disk-framed unknot replaces its ball
by a ball, and leaves U' unchanged. Thus all simultaneous equal surgeries leave
the unknot. Contraposition proves the condition.

Consequently, a useful seed already constructs a nontrivial C4 rotant of an
unlink, before the surgery step. This is precisely a special case of the unlink
rotant question posed as APR Question 3.5. It is a necessary gate, not a claim
that nontrivial unlink rotants suffice: one must additionally prove that some
specified equal surgery filling of L' has a nontrivial remaining knot. No such
L' has been constructed here. This observation does not assert a current
classification or a current open-status theorem about unlink rotants.

THE LINKING-NUMBER TEST IS AUTOMATIC IN THE STANDARD C4 MODEL

Use the standard cap model for R0, or an equivariant identification with that
model. Orient its four arcs alpha_i by transporting one chosen orientation under
the quarter-turn rho. Let e_i be their positive meridians; they form the basis
of H1(B minus R0;Z)=Z^4. The rotation acts by P e_i=e_(i+1), indices modulo 4.
The orientation of the completed unknot gives a sign vector a in {+1,-1}^4:
the inclusion into H1(S3 minus U;Z)=Z sends e_i to a_i.

Choose an orientation of eta_0 and let v=[eta_0]. Orient eta_i by rotation.
Then the four global linking numbers are

    ell_i = a^T P^i v.

In this standardization the flip induces epsilon Q_c on meridians, where
Q_c e_i=e_(c-i), and epsilon=+1 or -1 is a common orientation sign. The common
sign follows from mu rho mu=rho^(-1): the orientation effect on every rotated
arc is transported from that on alpha_0. This formula explicitly includes the
possible reversal of arc orientations. It does not assume the stator or the
global orientation vector a is rotation-invariant.

For every a in {+1,-1}^4 and every dihedral reflection Q_c, there is a j such that

    Q_c a=P^j a.

Proof. A binary cyclic word of length four has zero, one, two, three, or four
plus signs. The zero/four cases are constant. The one/three cases have a unique
exception, which a rotation can relocate. The two-plus cases are either an
adjacent pair or an opposite pair, and all reflected words of either type are
rotations. These exhaust all possibilities.

Since Q_c is symmetric and P is orthogonal, the post-flip linking numbers are

    ell'_i = epsilon a^T Q_c P^i v
           = epsilon (P^j a)^T P^i v
           = epsilon a^T P^(i-j) v
           = epsilon ell_(i-j).

Thus the orbit's four linking numbers are only cyclically permuted, up to a
common sign. In particular, all four being zero before the flip forces all four
to be zero after it. This is an integral calculation, not just a mod-two fact.

An alternative weaker proof works for any real orientation-weight vector:
the cyclic span of a decomposes into the constant line, the alternating line,
and the two-dimensional rotation plane. A reflection preserves each summand,
so orthogonality to every P^i a is preserved by reflection. The binary argument
above gives the stronger multiset equality actually needed here.

Scope: the formula uses the standard C4 cap model with its dihedral meridian
action, or a specified equivariant conjugate. For a separately proposed marked
initial rotor, that action must be checked under its chosen identification; one
cannot silently replace its marking by an arbitrary trivial-tangle marking.

COVER / HEEGAARD AUDIT

The double cover of S3 branched over U is S3. The double cover H0 of B branched
over its four trivial arcs is a genus-three handlebody. Each eta_i has two lifts:
its disk in S3 minus U lifts to two disjoint disks. The entire lifted surgery
link is therefore a globally trivial eight-component unlink in S3, contained
in H0, with disks that need not lie in H0. The disk framings lift unchanged.

After surgery, the whole covered manifold is still S3, but the surgically
modified piece H_k need not be a handlebody. The fact that a compact piece sits
inside S3 is not a handlebody criterion. Thus the original splitting does not
automatically remain a Heegaard splitting, and Waldhausen's uniqueness theorem
does not supply an isotopy preserving the rotor boundary or its disk set.
Even if H_k were separately shown to be a handlebody, one would still need to
track the marked boundary, deck involution, and order-four rotation; an
unmarked ambient uniqueness statement does not identify that marked data.

Generalized Property R concerns zero-framed surgery yielding a connected sum
of S1 x S2. Our global surgeries are already certified surgeries on an unlink,
with slopes 1/k, yielding S3. Rewriting the presentation by Kirby moves would
not by itself ensure that the moves preserve the rotor boundary, the stator,
or the two commuting symmetries. No relative or equivariant conclusion follows
solely from the ordinary global surgery conclusion.

The exact missing datum is the simultaneous unlink disks' position relative
to the rotor sphere (equivalently, the lifted disks' position relative to the
marked genus-three boundary). If the disks all lie in B minus R0, the flip
carries them to equally harmless disks and the construction fails. If they
leave B, zero linking after the flip is automatic, but disjoint embedded disks
after the flip are not supplied. That geometric distinction is where all the
remaining content lies.

REJECTED SEED

The proposed two-string family R_k=-1+1/2+1/(2k+1) has closure determinant
|1-2k|. Its k=0 and k=1 unknot closures therefore do NOT certify a globally
split disk-framed surgery circle: if such a circle generated this whole twist
family, every k would leave the unknot, contradicting determinant 3 at k=2.
No global nugatoriness conclusion from that two-unknot observation is used.

STRATEGIC VERDICT

The cover formulation does not currently kill the construction, but neither
does it bypass its difficult geometric premise. Before implementing a surgery
search, demand an explicit augmented unlink whose C4 flip is nontrivial, with
the exact marked rotor and simultaneous pre-flip disk certificate. Ordinary
pairwise linking calculations cannot certify the required asymmetry: all of
them vanish automatically on both sides. Only after this gate is passed is a
nontrivial-filling theorem or an explicit filling computation warranted.
