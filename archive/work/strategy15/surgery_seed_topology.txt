SURGERY-SEED AUDIT: GLOBALLY NUGATORY CAN BE LOCALLY ESSENTIAL
31 October 2026. Internal research note; no generic Jones conclusion or novelty claim.

1. The exact question and verdict

Let R_0 be a boundary-parallel tangle in a ball B, completed by a stator S to
the unknot U. A surgery circle eta lies in int(B), disjoint from R_0, and
bounds a disk in S^3 minus U; that disk need not lie in B. Does 1/k surgery
on eta necessarily keep the tangle boundary-parallel because the underlying
surgered ball is again a ball?

NO. The explicit single-circle family below refutes that inference. A
homeomorphism between the old and surgered underlying balls need not carry
the old embedded tangle to the new one.

2. Explicit diagram convention and the two twisting directions

Use the usual rational-tangle notation <p/q>, horizontal sum '+', and
reciprocal operation I, with fraction I(x)=1/x. The reciprocal is the usual
rotation/reflection convention for rational tangles; simultaneous mirroring
changes none of the conclusions. Only integers are added inside a rational
box. We never assume a horizontal sum of arbitrary rational tangles is itself
rational.

Consider the marked middle crossing in

  R(x) = <-1> + <1/2> + I(<1> + <x> + <1>).

At x=-1, the three consecutive crossings +,-,+ inside I cancel by RII to a
single positive crossing. Thus the final box is <1>, and R(-1) is a rational
tangle of fraction 1/2 (integer twists can slide across a rational box).
Its numerator closure is U. At x=+1 the final box is <1/3>, and

  R(+1) = <-1> + <1/2> + <1/3>.

There are TWO local twisting-circle choices which realize this same crossing
change. Their repeated twists differ, and their linking numbers with U differ.

REJECTED CHOICE: additive horizontal twisting, x=-1+2k. It gives
  R_bad(k) = <-1> + <1/2> + <1/(2k+1)>.
The numerator determinant is |1-2k|. Although k=0 and k=1 both give U,
k=2 gives determinant3. This circle is not globally nugatory. Its disk has
coherently oriented strands, hence linking number +/-2. The zero-algebraic-
intersection hypothesis of the cosmetic-crossing theorem must not be omitted.

CORRECT CHOICE: twist in the perpendicular direction. On the middle rational
box this is x -> x/(1+2kx); at x=-1 it becomes -1/(1-2k). The resulting family is

  R_k = <-1> + <1/2> + <(2k-1)/(4k-1)>,   k in Z.

The k=0 box is <1>; the k=1 box is <1/3>, as required. The circle and its
crossing disk are contained in a small ball around the marked middle crossing,
so in particular the circle lies in int(B).

3. Orientation audit for the correct circle

Temporarily replace the middle crossing x by the horizontal zero tangle.
The closure becomes N(<-1>+<1/2>+<1/2>), which has two components by its
endpoint pairing (it is also the two-component unlink). Therefore the exterior
of the small x-box pairs its two upper endpoints together, and its two lower
endpoints together. At x=-1, orienting the resulting single component gives
oppositely directed strands through a disk transverse to the VERTICAL twist
direction. The perpendicular surgery disk therefore meets U twice with
algebraic intersection zero. By contrast, the disk transverse to the additive
horizontal direction sees coherently directed strands.

Consequently the correct eta is a crossing circle in the precise convention
used by the cosmetic-crossing theorem. Its +/-1 surgery changes x=-1 to x=+1.
Both closed knots are U. The known unknot case of the cosmetic-crossing theorem
therefore implies that eta bounds an embedded disk in S^3 minus U. A regular
neighborhood of this disk gives a ball disjoint from U containing eta, so
eta union U is a split two-component unlink.

This conclusion is NOT inferred merely from the equality of two determinants:
the closed links are explicitly two-bridge links with numerator +/-1, and the
zero-linking hypothesis was checked separately.

4. All closures are U, all nonzero tangles are essential

The numerator closure of R_k is a Montesinos link with two nonintegral rational
summands. Such a link is two-bridge. Its signed numerator is

  -2(4k-1) + (4k-1) + 2(2k-1) = -1.

Thus N(R_k)=U for every integer k. This independently checks the behavior
expected from global nugatoriness; it is not a substitute for the orientation
audit in Section3.

The double cover of the tangle ball branched over R_k is Seifert fibered over
a disk with exceptional orders 2 and |4k-1|. The fraction is reduced because
gcd(2k-1,4k-1)=1. For k!=0, |4k-1|>=3. Its fundamental group surjects onto
C_2 * C_|4k-1|, a nonabelian group; it is therefore not the solid torus.
The cover of any boundary-parallel two-string tangle is a solid torus.
Hence R_k is not boundary-parallel for every nonzero k. In particular k=1
has the trefoil-exterior cover D^2(2,3).

So a globally nugatory circle contained in the rotor ball can be locally
essential relative to the marked tangle. The disk certifying nugatoriness
necessarily leaves that ball.

5. What this does and does not say about a C4 rotor

The seed does not provide a fourfold-symmetric rotor, nor four mutually
disjoint splitting disks in the global unknot complement. Those are additional
simultaneous geometric requirements. Local C4 symmetry of the rotor does not
extend to a general asymmetric stator, so one cannot rotate a global splitting
disk to obtain the required disk orbit.

A safe exclusion for naive replication is the following conditional statement.
Suppose a proposed rotor consists of four disjoint four-ended seed balls in a
connector tangle, and the connector itself is preserved by the rotor flip,
after reindexing the cyclic seed balls. Suppose also that on each seed boundary
the remaining flip is one of the ordinary Conway half-turns. Then flipping
the whole rotor is a sequence of ordinary Conway mutations of the closed knot.
If its initial completion is U, its flip is U as well. This includes the naive
planar necklace of four identical two-string seed boxes with a dihedrally
symmetric connector.

This statement does NOT exclude arbitrary sector decompositions. Higher-valence
interfaces, or a connector not returned to itself by the flip, are outside its
hypotheses. A viable fourfold replication would need such genuine inter-sector
interaction, as well as simultaneous globally split surgery-circle data.
No such construction has been supplied here.

6. Primary sources and scope

Kalfagianni, Crossing changes of fibered knots: defines a crossing disk to have
two intersections of zero algebraic number and proves the cosmetic-crossing
statement for fibered knots, including U.
  https://users.math.msu.edu/users/kalfagia/mynugatory15.pdf

Kalfagianni--Lin, Knot adjacency, genus and essential tori, Pacific J. Math.
228 (2006), Section5, p272: records the unknot case due to
Scharlemann--Thompson (1989), and Corollary5.2 gives the all-twists criterion.
  https://msp.org/pjm/2006/228-2/pjm-v228-n2-p.pdf

Scharlemann--Thompson, Link genus and the Conway moves, Comment. Math. Helv.
64 (1989), 527--535 (original unknot result).
  https://www.digizeitschriften.de/download/pdf/358147735_0064/log36.pdf

MacColl, Rotors in Khovanov Homology, Canad. Math. Bull.59 (2016), 159--169,
Introduction pp159--160: Conway mutation cannot turn U into a nontrivial knot;
also gives an argument using mutation invariance of mod2 Khovanov homology and
unknot detection. The general unknot-rotant question remains separate.
  https://doi.org/10.4153/CMB-2015-034-6

Standard Montesinos/rational-tangle conventions and examples may also be
compared with Nogueira--Salgueiro, On embeddings of tangles, Theorem4.5.
  https://www.mat.uc.pt/preprints/ps/p2033.pdf

The counterexample here is to a proposed LOCAL-TRIVIALITY INFERENCE, not to
Jones unknot detection. It is a construction with all closed knots equal to U.
