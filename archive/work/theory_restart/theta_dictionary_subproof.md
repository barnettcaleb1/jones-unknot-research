# The quotient coefficient and the colored theta evaluation

17 November 2026. Subproof for the dictionary requested in Claude050833.
No knot computations or enumeration. This note fixes the marking explicitly
and proves an identity of skein functionals, so its scope includes arbitrary
nonrational bodies. The separate root-sign lemma is not proved here.

## 1. Marking and the coefficient to be extracted

Let Theta=A union k be the marked quotient theta graph of a strongly
invertible knot: A is the unknotted axis circle and k is the single arc joining
the two vertices. Choose a ribbon neighborhood of k, with no unnamed twist
relative to its two end discs. Removing a regular neighborhood N(k) produces
the quotient two-string tangle T=(S^3-int N(k), A-int N(k)). The exterior of
N(k) is a ball. The four boundary ends inherit their cyclic marking from
the chosen ribbon.

Use the absolute bracket: the empty diagram is1 and a circle is
delta=-A^2-A^-2. In the marking used by `flat_search.bracket_vector`, the
four ends are 0,1,2,3 and the two matchings are

    b=(03)(12),         c=(01)(23),
    [T]=y b+x c.

For a TL2 rectangle put 0,1 on its left side and3,2 on its right side, in
top-to-bottom order. Then b is the identity I and c is the cup-cap e, with
e^2=delta e. This rotation is part of the dictionary; interchanging b and c
would extract the wrong coefficient.

The filling which restores the axis A has reduced bracket

    u=y+delta x.

Indeed its absolute bracket is delta y+delta^2 x. Since A is an actual
unknot, u is its blackboard-framing unit, a signed power of A. The axis-
unknot hypothesis is used at precisely this normalization step; the local
coefficient-extraction identity below does not require it.

## 2. Exact extraction by one color-two projector

Color both axis edges by V1 and the single arc k by V2. The planar
Jones-Wenzl convention means that k is replaced by its ribbon's two
boundary strands carrying

    f2=I-delta^-1 e.

At each vertex the two boundary strands of the ribbon attach to the two
incident axis edges without crossings. These are the ordinary admissible
(1,1,2) vertices, with no independently inserted vertex scalar.

In this marking the resulting closed colored graph is exactly the TL trace
of f2 T. This can also be seen without categorical notation: the identity
term of f2 joins the two long ribbon edges through N(k); the e-term joins
them by the short turnbacks at the two ends. These are respectively the
two closures of the same marked tangle T. Their absolute brackets are

    tr(T)  =delta^2 y+delta x,
    tr(eT) =delta y+delta^2 x.

Consequently the standard colored evaluation satisfies

    <Theta;1,1,2>
      =tr(f2 T)
      =(delta^2 y+delta x)-delta^-1(delta y+delta^2 x)
      =(delta^2-1)y
      =Delta2 y.                                      (1)

Equivalently f2 e=0 and f2 I=f2, while tr(f2)=Delta2. For the planar trivial
theta, T=b and y=1, so its evaluation is Delta2. Thus

    <Theta;1,1,2>/<Theta0;1,1,2>=y                    (2)

exactly in the specified common ribbon framing.

This is an equality of linear functionals on the whole two-tangle skein
module. No assumption that T is rational, that its diagram has small width,
or that it is generated by a particular two-bridge word is involved. Knots
or arbitrarily complicated tangles in the complementary ball change x and
y but do not change either functional in (1).

Changing the chosen ribbon by an integer framing twist multiplies the
color-two edge by its known ribbon unit and changes the corresponding
boundary marking/framing simultaneously. Formula (2) should not be quoted
with two independently chosen framings. With such independent choices the
safe statement is equality up to the determined common framing unit.

## 3. Relation to the preferred invariant and the regular axis model

The raw quotient invariant in the saved LP convention is

    j_raw=y/u,

where the common A-exponent class has been removed so that j_raw belongs to
Z[t,t^-1], t=A^4. The preferred invariant is the unique signed monomial
multiple J of j_raw satisfying J=1 modulo (t+1)^2. This is the already banked
preferred-longitude normalization. One must therefore write

    J=Norm(y/u)=Norm(<Theta>/<Theta0>/u),              (3)

not assert that every raw blackboard theta evaluates literally to preferred
J. Multiplying by a common ribbon unit does not change Norm or the unit
locus. In particular a skein computation up to one common ribbon unit is
sufficient for the nonflatness assertions.

The reduced-axis tensor model used in FT agrees with (2), rather than
introducing an additional nonunit factor. Generically cut the color-one
axis. The standard vertex convention gives a scalar endomorphism of V1
whose quantum trace is the left side of (1). Dividing by dim_q(V1)=delta
gives the scalar Delta2*y/delta. For the trivial theta that scalar is
Delta2/delta. Normalizing the paired vertices i,p by p i=Id multiplies the
scalar by delta/Delta2 and therefore gives y. Since the relevant generic
trivalent Hom spaces are one-dimensional, any pair of intertwiners with
this same product normalization has the same reduced-axis scalar.

This comparison is made over the generic field only. At delta=0 one uses
the explicit regular i,p of `ft_integral_referee.md` and reads a diagonal
entry of the endomorphism; no specialized division by delta is taken.
Equality of their generic rational functions establishes the dictionary,
while their separate integral-lattice proof establishes regularity.

## 4. Why arbitrary half-diagram bodies give the same theta object

A valid body begins with one half-strand, ends with one, touches no external
strand, and closes no component. In the quotient slab it has one axis arc
and one arc strand, with no theta vertex inside. Its state is consequently
an endomorphism of V1 tensor V2 regardless of its internal complexity.

The local quotient construction is the boundary of a ribbon neighborhood
of the single quotient arc k. Its elementary pieces are fixed geometrically:

* An off-axis half-diagram crossing becomes a crossing of two ribbon bands;
  its fundamental cabling consists of the four crossings of the two pairs.
* An axis crossing becomes the full monodromy of the single axis strand
  with the two boundary strands of the band, again four fundamental
  crossings. It is a full axis-band twist, not an uncolored half crossing.
* A maximum or minimum becomes the two boundary arcs of a band cap or cup.
* At each end of k the two band boundaries meet the two axis edges in the
  planar (1,1,2) vertex of Section2.

With x=A I+A^-1 e, the literal cabled words are x_(2i) x_(2i-1)
x_(2i+1) x_(2i) off the axis and x0 x1 x1 x0 at the axis, with inverse
letters for y. These are also the local words recorded in the quotient
implementation, but their justification is the ribbon boundary, not a
comparison on examples.

The single projector on k can be slid through every colored crossing by
naturality, through cups/caps by self-duality, and duplicated along k by
idempotence. Hence one may place f2 on every exposed band leg. This is a
global identity on one colored edge. It does not assume that an arbitrary
internal turnback has a fixed final connectivity.

An exterior strand omitted from a body remains untouched by all its moves.
Thus the same local replacements and the same framing convention work in
any larger necklace or nested construction. This proves that inserting an
arbitrary body into the colored box calculus computes the coefficient
extracted in (1), not a different invariant agreeing only on rational boxes.

## 5. The two-dimensional basis and its local operators

Work generically over K=Q(A). The fusion rule gives

    H=Hom(V1,V1 tensor V2 tensor V2),       dim H=2,
    End(V1 tensor V2)=K P1 direct-sum K P3.

Bending the second arc leg gives the isomorphism

    B -> (B tensor id_V2) E1,

where E1 is the band coevaluation with the axis straight. Let phi be the
outside two-vertex cap, normalized by phi(E1)=1. It kills the P3 box channel
because the adjacent vertex fuses axis and band to color1. For arbitrary
B=a P1+b P3 this gives phi((B tensor id)E1)=a. Again this is a linear
identity on the whole End space, independent of realizability or rationality.

Here use the saved LP variable t=A^4, with x=A I+A^-1 e. The raw arc-band
exchange x has eigenvalues A^-8,-A^-4 on the two channels0,2 allowed in H.
Normalize it by A^8 to obtain X, with eigenvalues1,-t. The raw y full twist
of axis and band has eigenvalues A^8,A^-4 on channels1,3. Normalize it by
A^-8 to obtain Y, with eigenvalues1,t^-3. These are ribbon-unit
normalizations; no independently chosen closure units appear.

In a basis (E1,F), F in ker phi, the operators have forms

    X=[[1,xi],[0,-t]],       Y=[[1,0],[eta,t^-3]].

The mixed central word (Y X^-1)^2 is scalar on fixed total color1.
YX^-1 is not scalar, since otherwise X and Y would be proportional, which
their different generic eigenvalue ratios forbid. A nonscalar two-by-two
matrix with scalar square has trace zero. Hence

    xi eta=t^-3(1-t^4).

Rescaling F uniquely to set xi=1-t^4 gives

    X=[[1,1-t^4],[0,-t]],
    Y=[[1,0],[t^-3,t^-3]],
    E1=(1,0),      phi=(1,0).                         (4)

Thus (4) follows from local colored operators and the marked cap/cup. It is
not limited to boxes whose enclosing diagram is two-bridge. Their internal
contents produce arbitrary elements of the same skein space; the operators
on that space remain (4).

Solving for v(P1),v(P3) from their Y eigenvalues and phi values gives

    v(P1)=(1,1/(t^3-1)),
    v(P3)=(0,1/(1-t^3)).

Therefore every box has the single common state

    v(B)=(a,(b-a)/(1-t^3)),
    a=v1,       b=v1+(1-t^3)v2.

Composition multiplies a,b, yielding the star product in Theorem N. Generic
projectors may be singular at roots; this formula is not a specialization
argument. The separate integral-body lemma and regular root model must be
used for the root-sign assertion and preferred outer-map unit.

## 6. Inputs, dependencies and scope

The exact extraction (1) uses only the displayed two-strand bracket algebra.
The generic fusion/ribbon facts in Section5 are the same standard identities
used throughout the banked TH-2BR and necklace notes; a primary account is
Masbaum--Vogel, *3-valent graphs and the Kauffman bracket*, Pacific Journal of
Mathematics164(1994),361--381, Sections1--2 and Theorem3.

The main argument closes the claimed quotient-coefficient/colored-theta
dictionary and its independence of rational body presentations. The appendix
below derives the marked vertical prism formula in the same normalization.
Neither argument proves a geometric classification of covering knots or any
novelty claim, or makes a scalar skein state a topology certificate. The root
eps-dichotomy is a separate proposition and must not be inferred from generic
fusion at delta=0.

## 7. Appendix: the vertical coefficients without interpolation of units

Use the marked vertical diagram from NEST Section4,

    s.l1.B.(x2)^a.u1,

with box state beta=A P1+B P3. Here A,B in the formulas are channel
amplitudes, not the bracket variable. To avoid that overloading during the
proof call them alpha,beta3. Fix the global theta normalization of Section2
and replace each raw vertical color-two crossing by its normalization
X=A^8 X_raw, whose eigenvalues on pair colors c=0,2,4 are

    lambda0=1,       lambda2=-t,       lambda4=t^3.

This defines a single raw linear functional F_a for every integer a. It
differs from the physical raw theta coefficient by the known common factor
A^(8a); preferred normalization comes only after F_a is computed. In
particular F_1,F_0,F_-1 must not be independently normalized before comparison.

Insert the three pair projectors at the vertical twist. Write

    F_a=sum_(c=0,2,4) lambda_c^a K_c(alpha,beta3).

Inserting a box projector P_k as well gives the marked triangular prism
network of NEST Section4. Its two triangles have outside labels (1,k,c).
The generic fusion Hom space is zero unless that triple is admissible.
Consequently

    K_0=k0 alpha,       K_4=k4 beta3.                (5)

These are exact zeros, with no nonzero coefficient evaluation needed.

There are two elementary closures that fix the remaining scalars in this
same normalization.

**Zero twist.** At a=0 the newly created l1 cup is closed by u1, leaving a
color-two circle passing through the box. The original arc from the start
vertex to the final vertex gives the trivial normalized vertex pair on
the axis. In the reduced-axis model it is p i=Id. Thus the remaining
endomorphism is the partial quantum trace of the box over V2. The quantum
trace of alpha P1+beta3 P3 is alpha Delta1+beta3 Delta3; division by the
dimension Delta1 of the remaining axis gives

    F_0=alpha+(Delta3/Delta1) beta3
       =alpha+(t+t^-1) beta3.                     (6)

This is a generic scalar identity. It makes no singular-root division.
The a=0 diagram need not have a connected quotient arc; it is used only
as a well-defined colored skein calibration of the common functional.

**Identity box.** When the box is the identity, the two color-one axis
edges bound the empty disk of the swap picture. The bubble removal
coefficient is theta(1,1,2)/Delta2=Delta2/Delta2=1 in the standard vertex
convention of Section2. Removing it leaves the ordinary quantum trace of
the color-two two-strand braid. The same global normalization divides by
Delta2, so

    F_a(1,1)=Tr_q(X^a)/Delta2,
    K_c(1,1)=Delta_c/Delta2.                       (7)

Both (6) and (7) concern the same global quotient theta / Delta2 and the
same local crossing normalization. They are not two separately normalized
closure formulas. At a=0 the identity calibration gives
(Delta0+Delta2+Delta4)/Delta2=Delta2, which equals
1+(Delta3/Delta1), as required. At a=1 it gives t^4 and at a=-1 it gives
t^-4; those framing monomials are expected and should not be erased.

Now Delta2=t^-1 Phi3 and Delta4=t^-2 Phi5, where
Phi3=1+t+t^2 and Phi5=1+t+t^2+t^3+t^4. Equations (5) and (7) give

    k0=t/Phi3,       k4=t^-1 Phi5/Phi3.

Subtracting K0 and K4 from (6) determines the middle coefficient:

    K2=((1+t^2) alpha+t beta3)/Phi3.

Restoring the channel-amplitude notation A,B gives exactly

    Phi3 F_a
      =t A+(-t)^a((1+t^2)A+t B)+t^(3a-1) Phi5 B.  (8)

No +/-1 interpolation, fitted relative unit, or rational-body assumption
was used. Formula (8) proves the generic marked skein statement for arbitrary
boxes. For the actual odd-a knot closures, J is its single preferred
normalization. It does not extend a nonflatness argument to arbitrary
special-region markings or certify the topology of an abstract input state.
