31 October 2026. Status: proved conditional reductions and exact skein
calculation. No generic Jones unknot-detection theorem or novelty claim.

1. WHAT THE ANCHORED TANGLE PROBLEM IS EXACTLY EQUIVALENT TO

The following two existence statements are equivalent:

(A) A nontrivial knot K has unknotting number one and V_K=1.
(B) A nonrational actual four-ended tangle T has an actual meridional
    unknot closure and lies on the preferred rational-zero skein line.

Here 'preferred' refers to the meridian-longitude marking on the knot
exterior that double-covers T. The tangle contains no closed components.

(A) implies (B). Remove a ball around an unknotting crossing. The positive
and negative crossing fillings give K and U. The oriented Jones skein
relation then gives the two-component unlink's Jones polynomial for their
oriented smoothing. The double cover of the outside tangle has an S3
filling, so it is a knot exterior E(J). The U filling is its meridian.
The smoothing has determinant zero and is at distance one from that
meridian, hence is its preferred longitude. The exact two-closure/common-
writhe lemma of quotient17 puts the entire tangle skein vector on Q0.
If the outside tangle were rational, K would be a determinant-one
two-bridge knot and hence U, a contradiction.

(B) implies (A). Fill T along mu+2lambda, i.e. surgery slope 1/2. This
filling and the meridional one differ by one crossing change: the matrix
[[1,0],[1,1]] sends the standard rational crossing slopes -1,+1 to
infinity,1/2. Equivalently two same-sign crossings become a cancelling
pair after changing one of them. The Q0 reference gives U in both
fillings. Proportional bracket vectors give a monomial Jones polynomial
for the new knot; V(1)=1 and V'(1)=0 force this monomial to be exactly one.
Its double cover is S3_(1/2)(J), which is not S3 for nontrivial J, by the
no-nonmeridional-S3-surgery theorem. Thus this knot is nontrivial and its
unknotting number is exactly one.

This is a restricted equivalence. A construction taking an ARBITRARY
Jones-one knot into (B) would reduce the full Jones conjecture to its
unknotting-number-one case. None of the earlier symmetric-sum or
Whitehead arguments establishes that missing construction.

Primary sources/background:
Gordon--Luecke's nonmeridional S3 surgery consequence is explicitly stated
in Kronheimer--Mrowka, Witten's conjecture and Property P, Introduction,
which cites the original theorem:
https://people.math.harvard.edu/~kronheim/wittenp.pdf
Sikora's general bracket-tangle formulation of Jones detection is Section9:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08162
It does not provide an actual unknot anchor from an arbitrary scalar
Jones-one knot. Tying such a knot locally into Q0 gives the scalar bracket
but retains that knot in the meridian closure.

2. EXACT NORMALIZED JONES FORMULA FOR THE 1/2 FILLING

Use delta=-A^2-A^-2, t=A^-4, and

  f_infinity(Q0)=1, f_infinity(Q_infinity)=delta,
  f0(Q0)=delta,     f0(Q_infinity)=1.

For an actual preferred exterior quotient T, normalize its bracket by
the framed bracket of its meridional unknot. Then

            T_hat=Q0+x(T)(Q_infinity-delta Q0),
            f_infinity(T_hat)=1,
            f0(T_hat)=delta+(1-delta^2)x(T).

Choose the crossing convention in which the first rational cap is

                         C1=A f0+A^-1 f_infinity.

The two-crossing cap is obtained from the same Temperley--Lieb operation
(A^-1 I+A e)^2, where e^2=delta e. Its exact closure functional is

                    C2=A^-2 f_infinity+(1-A^4)f0.

Indeed the coefficient of e is 2+A^2 delta=1-A^4. This cap has filling
slope 1/2 in the corresponding marking. For the reference tangle,

                              C2(Q0)=A^6.

The two crossings add writhe +2, in the convention just chosen. This is
the SAME writhe addition for T and Q0: after two half-twists the cap has
the same endpoint connectivity as the meridional cap, so the orientation
on the two outside tangle strands is unchanged. All crossings inside T
retain their original signs. Consequently the additional oriented Jones
normalization is precisely A^-6, independently of T. There is no
uncontrolled companion-dependent monomial. Therefore the actual branch
knot K_+(T) satisfies

 V_(K_+)(A^-4)-1
       =A^-6(1-A^4)(1-delta^2)x(T)
       =A^-10(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1)x(T).                    (1)

The mirrored cap gives the other half-integral slope and

 V_(K_-)(A^-4)-1
       =A^6(1-A^-4)(1-delta^2)x(T)
       =-A^-2(A^4-1)(A^8+A^4+1)x(T).                   (2)

Interchanging crossing/mirror conventions swaps these two formulas. Both
factors have exactly one zero at A=1. The result is independent of that
choice. The map A -> t=A^-4 has nonzero derivative at A=1.

3. ACTUAL UNKNOTTING-NUMBER-ONE KNOTS WITH ARBITRARILY DEEP JONES JETS

Root's separately checked preferred trefoil quotient has

            x_0=A^2(A^4-1)(1+A^8)(1+A^12),

up to the fixed chirality/framing convention. In particular it has a
simple zero at A=1. The separately computed normalized Whitehead shell
factor has a simple zero there too; changing the clasp sign only changes
a nonvanishing Laurent unit or mirrors the variable, and does not alter
the order.

Apply r compatible inherited untwisted Whitehead layers. The exact shell
law gives x_r=(product of r shell factors)x_0. Therefore x_r has exact
order r+1 at A=1 and is not the zero polynomial. For either half-integral
filling (1)--(2),

                   ord_(t=1)(V_(K_{r,+/-})(t)-1)=r+2.

These are actual branch knots. Each becomes the actual meridional unknot
by one crossing change, and its Jones polynomial is nonconstant by the
displayed exact nonzero product. Thus its unknotting number is exactly
one. No separate nontriviality oracle is needed for this family.

The polynomials agree with the unknot in derivatives of orders zero
through r+1 and differ at order r+2. These are only the finite-type
invariants extracted from ordinary Jones; no claim is made that all
finite-type invariants of those orders vanish. Different r give different
Jones polynomials by their different first nonzero Taylor orders.

The trefoil quotient and Whitehead scalar formulas used in this section
are independently being certified by the root/tangle tracks. Sections1
and2 do not depend on those explicit formulas.

4. BLIND AUDIT OF THE SHELL CONSTRUCTION

No overlooked linearity or orientation failure was found. The componentwise
strong inversion of P union A gives a shell with exactly ONE four-ended
input ball. Skein insertion is therefore linear, not the nonlinear
operation of making multiple copies of a tangle. Filling the outer
meridian restores E(A); its quotient cap has compressible slope lambda_A,
identified with the input meridian. This is the reason the meridional
functional is preserved up to one FIXED framing unit. It cannot be replaced
by an input-dependent arbitrary scalar. Preferred outer longitude is
unchanged because the winding number is zero. With P(U)=U the rational-
zero line is preserved geometrically. These hypotheses are all used.

Two important forbidden replacements remain: the input is not the scalar
V_J, and an arbitrary strong inversion of a satellite is not automatically
the compatible inherited inversion described by the shell.

5. FACTORED POLYNOMIAL AND EXACT ORDERS AT BOTH t=1 AND t=-1

Using the root/tangle tracks' explicit trefoil quotient and Whitehead
factor, write D(A)=(1-A^4)(1+A^8)(1+A^12). Each standard inherited layer
contributes D up to a signed Laurent unit and possibly A -> A^-1. The
mirror causes no change in its non-unit factors because

                              D(A^-1)=-A^-24 D(A).

Multiplication with x_0 and with either half-integral closure factor in
(1)--(2) gives, up to a signed Laurent monomial,

 V_(K_r)(A^-4)-1
   =(A^4-1)^(r+2)(A^8+A^4+1)(A^8+1)^(r+1)(A^12+1)^(r+1).

Equivalently, for an integer k depending on the coherent conventions,

 V_(K_r)(t)-1
   =+/-t^k (t-1)^(r+2)(t^2+t+1)(t^2+1)^(r+1)(t^3+1)^(r+1).       (3)

The exponent is integral because both V_(K_r)-1 and the displayed
non-unit factor are Laurent polynomials in t (all A exponents in the
non-unit factor are multiples of four). In particular

 ord_(t=1)(V_(K_r)-1)=r+2,
 ord_(t=-1)(V_(K_r)-1)=r+1,
 |[(t-1)^(r+2)](V_(K_r)-1)|=3*2^(2r+2)=12*4^r.

The last expression denotes the first nonzero Taylor coefficient at 1;
the corresponding derivative magnitude is (r+2)! times that number.
Different r cannot give the same Jones polynomial.

Mirror/clasp-sign changes are algebraically covered by the reciprocal
identity for D. Covering BOTH inherited clasp placements additionally
uses the audited geometric identification of their preferred trefoil
probe quotient: uniqueness up to meridian-longitude-preserving markings
and basis-preserving Conway half-turns. That is a geometric input, not
a conclusion of multiplying the displayed factors. A different,
non-inherited strong inversion is still outside the assertion.

6. CONDITIONAL CONVERSE TO A ZERO-TRANSMISSION SHELL: ALGEBRA PASSES,
   THE INHERITED CLASP INVOLUTION DOES NOT SUPPLY THE REQUIRED GEOMETRY

Suppose an actual nontrivial J has a preferred flat quotient T_J. The
inherited Whitehead quotient is then flat by transmission. The ordinary
Whitehead clasp crossing change makes Wh(J) into U. If its crossing circle
C were componentwise strongly inverted by the SAME inherited involution,
with lk(C,Wh(J))=0, the following argument would construct an essential
zero-transmission shell.

Undo the clasp by unit surgery on C. The dual circle C' remains an unknot,
and the resulting link P_0 union C' has P_0=U and linking number zero.
Doing the opposite unit surgery on C' restores Wh(J), with the inherited
strong inversion and preferred longitude. The componentwise quotient
would be a four-ended shell F. Its two rational probes Q0 and Q_(+/-1)
would have preferred flat outputs. Since FQ0=Q0 after normalization,
transmission gives 0=d*x(Q_(+/-1)); the latter input defect is a nonzero
Laurent monomial (in these conventions x(Q1)=-A^2). Thus d=0. The pattern
would be nonsplit: surgery on a split unknot cannot change the isotopy
type of P_0=U into nontrivial Wh(J).

There is no algebraic, framing, or peripheral gap in this CONDITIONAL
argument. However, the hypothesis about the crossing circle is not
provided by the usual inherited Whitehead inversion. Inspection of
Dai--Mallick--Taniguchi, arXiv:2606.05819, Example2.22/Figure4, shows the
axis running along the standard clasp twist direction. The standard
clasp crossing circle is transverse to that direction and is freely
rotated by the involution. A free circle is not componentwise strongly
inverted and does not produce the required four-ended input shell.

Replacing that circle by the perpendicular crossing-circle choice is
not an automatic repair. Locally, a strongly inverted circle bounds a
disk containing the fixed axis. If the two intersections of that disk
with the strongly inverted knot are exchanged, their oriented signs
agree (both disk and knot orientations reverse), producing linking
number +/-2 rather than zero. To get zero linking in this local model,
the intersections must be separately fixed points. A different global
involution or an additional explicit equivariant construction would
therefore be needed; neither has been established here.

Accordingly the proved implication remains essential zero shell =>
nontrivial u=1 Jones-one knot. Its proposed converse has not been proved.
The equivalence of Section1 concerns anchored flat tangles, not zero
shells, and is unaffected by this failed shell-converse construction.
