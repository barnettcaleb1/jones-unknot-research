31 October 2026. Bounded research pass; deductions, not novelty claims.

SETUP

Use the exact geometry and normalizations of

The two-component link P union C has both components unknotted, linking

1. A ZERO FACTOR WOULD GIVE A CERTIFIED COUNTEREXAMPLE, WITH NO
   SECOND GEOMETRIC SEARCH

The strongest useful topological input is Gabai's Corollary 2.5 in
Foliations and the topology of 3-manifolds II, J. Differential Geometry
26 (1987), 461--478. It is stated on p.462 and proved on p.471:
a winding-zero knot in a solid torus, not contained in a ball, has no
nontrivial surgery yielding a solid torus; the boundary of every such
filled manifold remains incompressible.

Apply this with C as a knot in E(P). This is a solid torus because P is
an unknot. Its winding number is lk(P,C)=0, and it is not contained in
a ball because the link is nonsplit. For each n != 0, 1/n surgery on C
preserves the ambient S^3, since C is an unknot, and produces P_n.
The resulting manifold with the P boundary unfilled is E(P_n).
Gabai's theorem shows E(P_n) is not a solid torus. Therefore

       P union C nonsplit  ==>  P_n is nontrivial for EVERY n != 0.

This is stronger than a finiteness statement about exceptional twist
parameters, and it does not need hyperbolicity or a bridge-number bound.

Now suppose d_P is identically zero. Insert the rational input Q_1.
The actual output T_1 is the preferred exterior quotient of P_1,
and its entire bracket is on the Q_0 line. Its meridian branch closure
is the actual unknot. Fill instead at slope 1/2, i.e. mu+2 lambda.
The resulting branch link B is a knot: its double cover is the integer
homology sphere S^3_(1/2)(P_1). Its bracket is a framing monomial times
the bracket of the same filling on Q_0, which is an unknot. Thus V_B
is a monomial. The knot identities V_B(1)=1 and V'_B(1)=0 force V_B=1.

Nevertheless B is nontrivial. P_1 is nontrivial by Gabai, so its
nonmeridional 1/2 surgery is not S^3 by Gordon--Luecke's no-S^3-surgery
corollary. The double branched cover of an unknot is S^3, ruling out B=U.

Moreover B has unknotting number exactly one. The rational-tangle
boundary transformation [[1,0],[1,1]] sends the standard crossing
slopes -1 and +1 to infinity and 1/2. Therefore these precise two
fillings differ by one crossing change. The infinity filling is U and
B is nontrivial. This slope argument was independently supplied by
the braid agent; it avoids asserting that arbitrary distance-two
replacements automatically come with a specified crossing diagram.

Consequently an essential zero shell is a DIRECT counterexample
generator. Once actual geometry and d_P=0 are certified, no additional
search for a nontrivial closure is necessary: Q_1 followed by the 1/2
closure supplies a nontrivial Jones-one knot of unknotting number one.
The same works with every Q_n, n != 0; distinctness of the resulting
branch knots is NOT claimed here.

Primary Gabai article (actual PDF inspected, including proof on p.471):
https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214441487
https://scispace.com/pdf/foliations-and-the-topology-of-3-manifolds-ii-4bywlfmfgl.pdf
The mirror hosts the original article, not a secondary summary.
Gordon--Luecke consequence is explicitly recalled in the introduction of
Kronheimer--Mrowka, Witten's conjecture and Property P:
https://people.math.harvard.edu/~kronheim/wittenp.pdf

2. SPLIT PATTERNS DO HAVE ZERO FACTOR; NO EQUIVARIANT SPLITTING
   ASSUMPTION IS NEEDED

If P union C is split, then every 1/n surgery on C leaves P an unknot.
Hence F(Q_0) and F(Q_1) are actual unknot exterior quotients, and with
their preferred output markings both lie on the rational Q_0 skein
line. Q_0 and Q_1 are linearly independent over Q(A). Therefore F has
rank one, and d_P=0. Its meridian evaluation shows that F is nonzero.

This argument does not require choosing an invariant splitting sphere
or assuming that the splitness is visibly compatible with the axis.

Thus the precise unresolved statement within this pattern class is:
  d_P=0  implies  P union C is split.
The reverse implication is proved above. A failure gives a Jones
counterexample by Section 1. Proving the implication only settles this
construction class; we have NOT reduced arbitrary Jones-one knots to
such shells. In particular, do not call it equivalent to the full
Jones unknot-detection conjecture.

3. A WHOLE PRESERVATION CONSTRUCTION IS UNAVAILABLE: CONWAY MUTATION

Starting with a split zero shell and performing ordinary two-string
Conway mutations inside the shell cannot produce an essential example.
The lift of a Conway mutation to the torus double-covering its four-
punctured sphere is isotopic to the identity (choose the translation
lift). The usual proof that mutant links have homeomorphic double
branched covers is local; using a collar at the mutation torus gives
a homeomorphism of shell double covers relative to their external
boundary tori and peripheral markings.

Therefore such mutations preserve the homeomorphism type of the
pattern-link exterior. In particular they preserve compressibility
of its boundary tori. A split link of two unknots has compressible
boundary tori; a nonsplit link has incompressible boundary. Hence any
mutated shell that again has the required two-component pattern-link
interpretation still represents a split link.

This excludes a natural exact-Jones-preserving construction without
using the Jones conjecture. It concerns ordinary Conway mutations of
the quotient shell, not arbitrary rotations with more branch points,
nor mutations performed on an unrelated diagram of the covering link.

Primary exposition/proof of the local lifting statement:
Morton and collaborators' mutation chapter, Theorem 3, p.6:
https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/~su14/papers/mutantchapter.pdf
It identifies the torus lift as isotopic to the identity.

4. INDEPENDENT SATELLITE ALTERNATIVE

Even without Gabai's theorem, an essential zero shell gives a
counterexample by inserting a strongly inverted trefoil. The gluing
torus of P(trefoil) is incompressible on the trefoil side. It is also
incompressible on the pattern side: a compressing meridian disk in
E(C) disjoint from P would cut the solid torus into a ball containing
P, contradicting essentiality. Thus P(trefoil) is nontrivial. Its
flat quotient gives the same nontrivial 1/2 branch construction.
Gabai's theorem is stronger because the simpler rational input Q_1
already suffices.

5. WHAT THE CURRENT PASS DOES NOT SUPPLY

No essential zero shell or new Jones-one knot was found. The results
make the exact construction target easier to certify and remove
ordinary Conway mutation of a split seed as a possible route. They do
not replace the entire-polynomial condition d_P=0 by beta=0, finite
derivatives, or vanishing of an Alexander polynomial. Iterated
Whitehead patterns remain concrete counterexamples to those shortcuts.

An additional possible route would have to construct an actual
non-compositional shell with full cancellation. Serial compositions
of already nonzero shell factors cannot do this, because their
factors multiply in the integral domain Z[A,A^-1]. No topological
operation implementing the required additive cancellation was
established in this pass.
