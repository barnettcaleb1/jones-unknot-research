PASS 18: WHAT THE SATELLITE MULTIPLIER CANNOT BE
31 October 2026. Research deductions; no claim of novelty.

Conclusion. The exact satellite multiplier d(A) is not determined by the
entire Kojima--Yamasaki eta-functions, either complete Cochran sequence,
or all Milnor invariants. Even the full two-loop rational Kontsevich
function of every rational-input covering knot can vanish while d is
nonzero. Moreover no fixed-degree collection of rational-surgery LMO
invariants, even at every slope and every rational input, detects whether
d vanishes. These claims use actual iterated Whitehead patterns, not
formal modules. The distinction between finite order and all orders is
essential throughout.

SETUP AND PREVIOUSLY ESTABLISHED INPUT

Use the compatible strong inversion of the untwisted Whitehead pattern W.
The pattern and its axis are unknots and their linking number is zero.
The normalized quotient shell map on the two-dimensional bracket module
has the form diag(1,d_W(A)) in the basis Q_0,Q_infinity-delta Q_0, where
delta=-A^2-A^-2. Previous pass established d_W != 0 and

                 ord_(A=zeta) d_W = 1,
                 zeta=exp(pi i/4).

For P_r=W composed with itself r times, compatibility of the inherited
inversions and preferred longitude gives

                 d_(P_r)=d_W^r != 0,
                 ord_zeta d_(P_r)=r.                         (1)

This is the exact composition law in
outputs/jones-satellite-transmission-and-hidden-derivatives.txt.
It concerns quotient tangles of satellite exteriors, not the ordinary
Jones polynomial of the companion knot.

1. A COMPLETE ETA/COCHRAN/MILNOR COUNTERTEST

Let L_r be P_r together with its axis. It can be obtained from the Hopf
link by untwisted Whitehead doubling the same component r times.
Meilhan--Yasuhara Theorem 4.1(i) and Corollary 4.2 imply that L_2 is a
boundary link; their theorem also shows the same for every r>=2.
Their convention calls the ordinary positive or negative Whitehead
double the Whitehead (+1) or (-1) double; this is distinct from the
longitudinal twisting parameter of a twisted Whitehead knot.

For a boundary link, choose disjoint Seifert surfaces. Its two complete
Cochran sequences vanish, as Jin states just after the definition on
p.294. The eta-function is the generating function of that sequence,
under the variable x=(1-t)(1-t^-1), so both orderings of eta vanish too.
All Milnor invariants of a boundary link vanish. Thus for every r>=2,

        eta_1(L_r)=eta_2(L_r)=0,
        beta_i(L_r)=beta_i(reverse-order L_r)=0  for all i,
        every Milnor invariant of L_r is zero.

The components themselves are unknots. Nevertheless (1) gives a nonzero
Jones transmission multiplier with arbitrarily high exact order at zeta.
These patterns are not split: a split two-unknot pattern link has every
axis-twist knot unknotted; all corresponding quotient outputs are on the
Q_0 line, forcing d=0 because two rational input vectors span the module.

Therefore no formula can recover d, or merely its vanishing, solely from
the data listed above. In particular the higher zeta-jets of d are not
simply the successive Cochran derived Sato--Levine invariants. The first
jet's relation to the ordinary Sato--Levine invariant does not extend in
that straightforward way.

2. EVEN THE ENTIRE TWO-LOOP FUNCTION DOES NOT SUFFICE

Insert the rational input Q_n into the P_r shell. Its covering knot is

       K_(r,n)=Wh_0^(r-1)(Wh_n(U)),                          (2)

where Wh_n(U) is a twist knot and subsequent Whitehead operations are
untwisted. The marking follows from zero winding: the outer longitude
remains preferred after the innermost axis surgery.

Garoufalidis Corollary 1.1 calculates the entire rational two-loop
Kontsevich contribution Q_1 of Wh_epsilon(K) as a fixed nonzero diagram
multiplied by epsilon*a(K), where a(K) is a fixed multiple of the second
derivative of Delta_K(exp(h)) at h=0. Consequently if Delta_K=1, the
entire Q_1 of its untwisted Whitehead double is zero.

For r=3 the companion of the outermost Whitehead operation in (2) is
Wh_0(Wh_n(U)), whose Alexander polynomial is 1. Thus simultaneously for
every n,

            Delta_(K_(3,n))=1,
            Q_1(K_(3,n))=Q_1(U)=0,

but d_(P_3)=d_W^3 !=0. This defeats a proposed criterion using all
coefficients in the two-loop rational function, not merely a finite
Taylor expansion of it. It does not say that every loop contribution
vanishes for this fixed family.

3. NO FIXED-DEGREE SURGERY-LMO TEST CAN DECIDE d=0

Lin's iterated-Whitehead result, stated by Birman on p.283, says that the
m-fold untwisted Whitehead double of any knot has the same finite-type
invariants as the unknot through degree m+1. Constants are understood
to be subtracted. Applying this to (2), for r>=2 the knot K_(r,n) agrees
with U through finite-type degree r, for every integer n.

Ito Proposition 3.1 says that the degree-N part of the LMO invariant of
S^3_(p/q)(K), p/q nonzero, depends only on the slope and the pieces of the
Kontsevich invariant with bidegree (e,k) satisfying

                    e+k/2 <= N.

Here k is the number of legs, e is minus Euler characteristic, and the
ordinary Vassiliev degree is e+k. For the zero-framed, one-colored knot
invariant, no nonzero tree component occurs: a non-strut tree with only
one leg color vanishes by antisymmetry, and the framing strut is absent.
Hence each nonzero component has e>=0. This gives

                    e+k <= 2(e+k/2) <= 2N.

Consequently finite-type equality through degree 2N suffices for equality
of the surgery LMO invariant through degree N, uniformly in the slope.
Together with (1)--(2), this proves the following bounded-order statement:

For every integer N>=1, choose r>=max(2,2N). Then, for EVERY integer n
and EVERY nonzero rational slope p/q,

  Z_LMO,<=N(S^3_(p/q)(K_(r,n))) = Z_LMO,<=N(L(p,q)),

while d_(P_r)=d_W^r is nonzero.

Thus even access to all surgery slopes and the entire rational-input
family does not rescue a test restricted to a fixed LMO degree. The
same construction also retains all the vanishing eta/Milnor data in
section 1. This argument does NOT assume that ordinary Jones determines
LMO, and does NOT produce one fixed nontrivial pattern invisible to LMO
at every degree. The iteration depth depends on N.

4. A PRECISE GEOMETRIC TARGET, AND AN ACTUAL COUNTEREXAMPLE TRIGGER

Consider a compatible strongly invertible two-component link P union A
with both components unknotted and lk(P,A)=0. Assume it is nonsplit.
An exact, meaningful target is:

   Can the normalized quotient shell multiplier d_P vanish identically?

If such a pattern exists, it gives a genuine Jones-unknot counterexample,
not just an algebraic model. Here is the conditional construction.

(a) Perform +1 surgery on A and let P_1 be the image of P. Then P_1 is
nontrivial. Otherwise A, regarded as a knot in the solid torus E(P),
would admit a nonmeridional solid-torus surgery. Gabai's classification
forces it into a ball or into a braid of nonzero winding. Zero linking
rules out the braid case, and nonsplitting rules out the ball case.

(b) If d_P=0, the actual quotient tangle of E(P_1), with preferred
marking, has bracket on the Q_0 line. Its meridian closure is U. Thus its
filling along +1 has Jones polynomial equal to that of the corresponding
Q_0 filling, up to a framing monomial. The latter filling is U.

(c) The actual +1 branch set C is a knot because its cover is the
integral homology sphere S^3_(+1)(P_1). A Jones monomial of a knot equals
1: V_C(1)=1 fixes the sign and V'_C(1)=0 fixes its exponent.

(d) C is nontrivial. Otherwise its cover would be S^3, whereas a
nontrivial knot P_1 cannot have a nonmeridional S^3 surgery. Equivalently
one may invoke Property P for the +1 slope.

Therefore an essential rank-collapse shell is an explicit geometric
counterexample trigger. Proving d_P !=0 for every such essential shell
would settle this restricted realization problem. There is still no
proved reduction of an arbitrary Jones-one knot to this shell class.
Accordingly this is a sharp target, not a generic unknot-detection proof.

The special application of Gabai in (a) is also written out in the proof

STRATEGIC CONSEQUENCE

Eta and derived Sato--Levine data are exhausted as a proposed exact
description of d: actual boundary patterns disprove that bridge. Fixed
perturbative order is also insufficient, even with every surgery slope.
The remaining useful target is nonperturbative geometric nonvanishing
of the shell determinant, or construction of a genuine essential shell
whose determinant is exactly zero. The present results locate that gap;
they do not solve it or imply that all-order LMO information is useless.
