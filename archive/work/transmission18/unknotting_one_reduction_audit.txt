DOES THE GENERAL JONES CONJECTURE REDUCE TO UNKNOTTING NUMBER ONE?
Focused literature audit, 31 October 2026.

Finding: no such reduction was established by the primary papers checked.
This is a bounded search result, not a claim that the entire literature
contains no reduction. The distinction between arbitrary bracket-flat
tangles and actual unknot-anchored bracket-flat tangles is explicit in
the strongest relevant equivalence found.

1. SIKORA'S ACTUAL EQUIVALENCE

Adam Sikora, Tangle equations, the Jones conjecture, slopes of surfaces in
tangle complements, and q-deformed rationals, Canadian J. Math. 76 (2024),
707--727. Section 9; published Theorem 9.2, arXiv Theorem 24.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.08162

The full Jones conjecture is equivalent to the claim that a framed
four-ended tangle with bracket vector (1,0) is the trivial rational
tangle. It is also equivalent to the corresponding rational-tangle
bracket-detection statement.

The converse proof on arXiv p.18 starts with a knot K whose Jones
polynomial is one, normalizes its framing, and ties K locally into one
strand of the zero tangle. This produces bracket vector (1,0).

This construction DOES NOT produce an actual unknot anchor. Closing the
strand that carries K retains K as a connected-sum factor. In particular
the meridional knot closure is K, not a geometrically certified unknot.
Sikora's proof in the other direction invokes the full Jones conjecture
precisely to conclude that the two monomial-bracket closures are actual
unknots, and then uses the double-cover surgery argument. That step
cannot be imported into a proof of the Jones conjecture.

2. WHAT OUR ANCHORED REDUCTION DOES PROVE

The independently audited result in generic.txt is an existence
equivalence between:
  (i) a nontrivial Jones-one knot of unknotting number one; and
  (ii) a nonrational actual four-ended tangle with a genuine unknot
       meridian closure and bracket on its preferred rational-zero line.

The reverse construction uses the 1/2 filling, whose replacement by the
meridian is explicitly one crossing change. Nontriviality follows from
the no-nonmeridional-S3-surgery theorem for the covering knot.

The proposed Whitehead/clasp-surgery construction, if its equivariant
geometry is verified, may identify this anchored problem with essential
zero shell multipliers. It still starts with an actual unknot anchor.
It does not turn Sikora's arbitrary local-knot tangle into an anchored
tangle. Thus it does not, by itself, bridge the full conjecture to u=1.

3. THE OTHER TARGETED PAPERS DO NOT PROVIDE THE MISSING STEP

Garoufalidis, Whitehead doubling persists, concerns the loop expansion
and finite-type information of Whitehead doubles. It does not show that
V_K=1 implies V_Wh(K)=1. Our exact formula explains the issue:

 V_Wh+(K)(t)-1 = ((1-t^3)/(1+t)) (H_3(K;t)-1),

where H_3 is reduced, zero-framed, dimension-three colored Jones. The
premise needed is H_3=1, whereas the ordinary Jones premise is H_2=1.
The equality of these unit loci has not been proved here.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0003189

Y. Miyazawa, The Jones polynomial of an unknotting number one knot,
Topology and its Applications 83 (1998), 161--167, derives relations
among Jones, signature, Conway, and the branched-cover Casson invariant.
Its abstract and the subsequent literature describe obstructions to
unknotting number one, not a reduction of arbitrary Jones-one knots.
https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-8641(97)00107-7

A. Stoimenow, The Jones and Q polynomial of an unknotting number one knot,
1999 preprint, likewise derives unknotting-number obstructions; no such
generic reduction appears in the paper inspected.
https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/papers/unkn1.pdf

4. WHY SIMPLY ENLARGING THE ANCHOR DOES NOT REPAIR THIS

For a 2b-ended tangle, the bracket module has Catalan-number dimension.
One ordinary Jones equality fixes one closure pairing, not the entire
skein vector. Passing to a larger boundary therefore requires additional
independent closure information. Conversely, tying a hypothetical
Jones-one knot locally into a strand produces a fully invisible local
factor in every bracket pairing, but every actual closure containing
that strand retains the knot geometrically.

This dichotomy is an exact warning for a higher-genus strategy: a new
construction must both remove local knot factors from a certified
unknot filling and preserve the required full skein information. Neither
condition follows from the single original Jones equality. No general
construction accomplishing both was found in this pass.

Scope: these remarks do not disprove the possibility of a generic
u=1 reduction, and should not be phrased as a theorem that no such
reduction exists. They identify the missing implication in the specific
published equivalence and in the proposed Whitehead route.
