Independent audit of the all-integer processive classification
2026-10-31, generic_braid

VERDICT: sound, for the fixed tangle X=<-1>+<1/2>+<1/3> and
integer second probe <j>, j nonzero. No novelty claim is assessed.

1. Exhaustion of rational comparators.
Write a reduced rational tangle as <p/q>. Its numerator closure has
determinant |p|, so an unknot closure forces p=+/-1. This gives precisely
<1/k>, k an arbitrary integer. k=0 represents infinity and is included.
The zero tangle is not missing: its numerator is a two-component unlink.

2. Determinant filter.
For X+<j>, the Montesinos determinant is
 |2*3*(j-1+1/2+1/3)| = |6j-1|.
For <1/k>+<j>, it is |1+jk|, including k=0. Equality gives either
 j(k-6)=-2 or k=-6. Since j is a nonzero integer, the former gives
exactly (j,k)=(1,4),(2,5),(-1,8),(-2,7). No sign branch is omitted.
All survivors close to knots, since the common determinant is odd
and the numerator of the rational comparator is odd.

3. Infinite branch k=-6.
In the stated inverse-A convention, the two-strand crossing b has
eigenvalues A^-1 and -A^3. For v=(f,g),
 delta*A^j*N(v*b^j) = (delta^2-1)f
                         + (-1)^j*A^(4j)*(f+delta*g).
This formula holds for negative as well as positive j, since b is
invertible. After inserting the writhe correction, define v=X-A^h R,
where h=30 for odd j and h=-18 for even j. The certificate computes
C0=(delta^2-1)f and C1=f+delta*g exactly.
Even j: C1=-C0 and C0 is nonzero, so vanishing forces j=0.
Odd j: C0 has support endpoints [-14,50], C1 endpoints [-14,46].
If C0-A^(4j)C1 vanished, equality of lowest exponents would force j=0
and equality of highest exponents would force j=1. This is impossible.
Multiplication by delta or a Laurent monomial introduces no issue,
since the Laurent polynomial ring is an integral domain.

4. Finite exceptions and orientation.
Ran the supplied exact certificate, then independently generated the
four exceptional diagrams in the original tangle addition order and
computed their Jones polynomials using Regina. Only (j,k)=(1,4)
matches. Also checked k=-6 at all j in [-8,8] except zero, confirming
the independently assigned writhe formulas and polynomial mismatch.
These finite tests validate conventions, not the infinite proof.
The independent data are in processive_regina_audit.json.

No Schubert alternative is needed after the determinant step:
different normalized Jones polynomials already exclude every actual
unoriented knot isotopy, including any alternative two-bridge slope
presentation. The one positive case is separately established by
Reidemeister simplification to identical planar diagrams.

5. Framed systems.
For the unique surviving unframed pair the two closure ratios are
A^12 and 1 in the usual A convention (A^-12 and 1 in the certificate's
inverse-A convention). Framing adjustments of either tangle multiply
both its closures by the same unit. Framing adjustments of the fixed
probes are shared across the two candidate solutions and cancel in
the comparison. Consequently the two ratios cannot both be corrected.
Thus no rational comparator solves the corresponding two framed
equations, for any nonzero integer j.
