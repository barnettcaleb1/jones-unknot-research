Independent audit, 2026-10-31, generic_braid

Let X=<-1>+<1/2>+<1/3> and R=<1/4>, using Spherogram's
RationalTangle conventions, with probes <0> and <1>.

VERIFIED GEOMETRIC CLOSURES.
Both N(X) and N(R) simplify by Reidemeister moves to one crossing-free
unknot. Both N(X+<1>) and N(R+<1>) simplify to the same five-crossing
planar diagram, crossing set
 {(1,6,2,7),(7,2,8,3),(3,8,4,9),(9,4,0,5),(5,0,6,1)}.
This is exactly the diagram returned for closure of the two-strand braid
sigma_1^(-5). Thus there is no hidden mirror mismatch between the
second closures. The accompanying script independently reconstructs
all four links and asserts these conclusions.

NONRATIONALITY.
The double branched cover of <1/2>+<1/3> is a Seifert manifold over the
disk with exceptional multiplicities 2 and 3. Its fundamental group
surjects onto the nonabelian orbifold group C_2*C_3 after killing the
regular fiber. Therefore it is not a solid torus. Adding the integral
tangle <-1> alters the boundary marking but not this homeomorphism
type. Since a rational tangle has solid-torus double branched cover,
X is nonrational. The precise chirality of its trefoil-exterior cover
is unnecessary for this argument.
Primary support for the Montesinos-cover fact: M. Teragaito, Cyclic
Surgery Between Toroidal Surgeries, Canadian Mathematical Bulletin
54 (2011), proof of Lemma 2.2, doi:10.4153/CMB-2010-108-6.

CONJECTURE CHECK.
Sikora arXiv:2005.08162v2 (2023), Conjecture 4 literally states that
if an unframed system with two distinct rational probes has a rational
solution, every solution is rational. The example fits and contradicts
that statement as printed. This says nothing against the framed
Conjecture 2 or Theorem 3 relating it to Jones detection.
https://arxiv.org/html/2005.08162#S1.SS1

FRAMING OBSTRUCTION.
Initial diagram writhes are (0,-5) for X's two closures and (4,-5) for
R's. With V=(-A^3)^(-w)<D>, the first brackets are 1 and A^12, whereas
the second brackets agree. In the inverse-A convention the first
values are 1 and A^(-12); this does not affect the mismatch. A fixed
framing correction to a tangle multiplies both closure brackets by
the same unit, so it cannot simultaneously reconcile these two ratios.

PRECEDENCE.
Independently checked Ernst--Sumners (1999), Example 3, printed pp.33--34,
in the full-text reproduction of their paper. For K_0=<1> and
K_1=<1,3,1>, solutions (i) and (ii) are O=(1/2,2/3,-1), R=-1 and
O=(0/1,3/4,-1)=-1/4, R=-1. The text explicitly notes coexistence of
rational and nonrational solutions. Simultaneous mirroring, followed
by the standard Montesinos integer-shift normalization, gives the
example checked here. The example is therefore old and must not be
described as a new construction.
