COMPLETE OBSTRUCTION FOR THE FIXED SEVEN-NODE INTERACTION FAMILY
2026-10-31. Exact finite computation, independently checked. This is an
obstruction to abstract colored-Jones candidates; it is not a theorem
about all knot quivers or a proof of Jones unknot detection.

STATEMENT
Let C be a symmetric nonnegative integer 7x7 matrix with diagonal
(0,0,0,0,1,1,1). On the nine pairs of equal-sign nodes, assign the multiset
  {0,0,0,0,2,5,6,9,11};
on the twelve pairs of opposite-sign nodes, assign the multiset
  {0,0,0,0,0,0,1,1,4,7,10,10}.
Use the specialized quiver series
 Z_C=sum_d (-q)^(d^T C d) product_i(q^(-C_ii)x)^d_i
                       /product_i(q^2;q^2)_(d_i),
and define H_N(y)=(y;y)_(N-1)[x^(N-1)]Z_C, y=q^2.
NO such assignment satisfies the necessary Habiro compatibility condition
for colors2,3,4 of a normalized zero-framed knot colored Jones sequence.

WHY THESE WERE NONTRIVIAL CANDIDATES
Every assignment has H_2=1 and
 H_3=1+F,  F=(y-1)(y^2-1)(y^4-1)(y^5-1).
Also H_N(1)=1 for allN. With
 A_(N,k)=product_(j=1)^k(1-y^(N+j))(1-y^(j-N)),
these first two colors require cyclotomic coefficients c_1=0,c_2=y^3.
Therefore color4 must satisfy
 H_4=1+y^3 A_(4,2)+c_3(y)A_(4,3),  c_3 in Z[y,y^-1].
Since A_(4,3) vanishes to order6 at1, the first six derivatives (orders0
through5) must be
                    (1,0,0,0,4320,86400).
The obstruction occurs before any full polynomial division is needed.

COMPLETE ENUMERATION, WITH PROVED SYMMETRY REDUCTION
There are 9!/4!=15,120 same-sign placements, and
12!/(6!2!2!)=166,320 opposite-sign placements. The full unquotiented
finite family thus has 2,514,758,400 assignments.
Permuting the four positive nodes and the three negative nodes leaves Z_C
unchanged. prepare.py constructs all 144 actions of S4xS3 on the nine
same-sign edges, and explicitly partitions all 15,120 placements into
175 orbits. One representative per orbit suffices: any node permutation
carries the opposite-sign edge multiset to another allowed placement.
For each of these 175 representatives, search.cpp visits ALL166,320
unique opposite placements with std::next_permutation. This gives
29,106,000 checked assignments, with harmless residual duplicates.
No heuristic pruning or numerical tolerance occurs.

EXACT FILTERS AND COUNTS
For s_i=(-1)^(C_ii), let z_i=sum_(j!=i) C_ij s_j. The already-fixed color3
moments imply H_4''(1)=6sum_i s_i z_i^2. Successive counts are:
  all reduced placements                      29,106,000
  H_4''(1)=0                                     141,944
  H_4'''(1)=0                                        455
  H_4''''(1)=4320                                      2
  H_4'''''(1)=86400                                    0
The two fourth-order survivors have fifth derivatives79200 and144000.
Thus neither can satisfy the color4 Habiro condition, and no assignment
in the whole specified family can be a knot colored-Jones sequence.

The direct color4 formula used in the enumerator is
 H_4 = sum_i s_i y^(3C_ii)
     +(1+y+y^2) sum_(i!=j) s_j y^(C_ii+2C_ij)
     +(1+2y+2y^2+y^3)
              sum_(i<j<k) s_i s_j s_k y^(C_ij+C_ik+C_jk).
It follows by separating occupancy patterns3,2+1,1+1+1 in the defining
q-multinomial sum. All derivatives and comparisons use signed integers.

INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION
The original general q-multinomial engine in work/bio7/quiver_candidate.py
was used by audit.py to recompute H3 and H4 of both last survivors. It
confirms their complete polynomials, derivatives, and nonzero exact Habiro
division remainders. The direct H4 formula was additionally compared
against that engine on20 independent matrices. These comparisons verify
the formula implementation; completeness comes from the explicit orbit
partition and exhaustive unique multiset permutations.

WHAT SHOULD CHANGE NEXT
This rules out one concrete proposed interaction design completely. Merely
rearranging its edges cannot fix it. New designs need different interaction
multisets, additional node charges, or new nodes, and must still satisfy
all higher-color compatibility and geometric realization conditions.
The proved arborescent tangle-to-quiver gluing grammar is a principled
source of realizable interactions; arbitrary symmetric-quiver positivity
alone supplies no such guarantee. No claim is made that these constraints
characterize knot invariants or that a surviving formal quiver would be a
counterexample to Jones unknot detection.
