ALL INTEGER REACTION POWERS FOR ONE ACTUAL NONRATIONAL DNA TANGLE

Status: exact deduction and reproducible calculation, not a generic Jones
unknot theorem and not a claim that the underlying near-miss is new.

Let X=<-1>+<1/2>+<1/3>, with the rational-tangle convention implemented by
Spherogram. Its double branched cover is Seifert over D^2 with exceptional
orders2,3. Its group has nonabelian quotient C2*C3, so the cover is not a
solid torus and X is nonrational. The numerator closure N(X) is an actual
unknot. For j=1, N(X+<1>) is the negative (2,5) torus knot. A rational
comparator R=<1/4> has exactly the same two unframed products. The two
five-crossing reduced PD diagrams have identical crossing sets, and both
zero-probe closures reduce to the crossing-free unknot.

Ernst--Sumners, "Solving tangle equations arising in a DNA recombination
model", Math. Proc. Camb. Phil. Soc.126 (1999), Example3 pp33--34,
DOI10.1017/S0305004198002989, already contains the equivalent ambiguity.
The parent investigator located that precedent. This reconstruction adds
no novelty claim concerning that example.

CLAIM: For this fixed X, any nonzero integer j and rational R with
N(R)=U and V(N(R+<j>))=V(N(X+<j>)) must have j=1 and R=<1/4>.
Furthermore this sole match never lifts to a common framed system.

1. Every rational tangle with unknot numerator is R=<1/k>, k in Z;
k=0 includes infinity. The Montesinos determinant formula gives
 det N(X+<j>)=|6j-1|, det N(<1/k>+<j>)=|1+jk|.
Consequently a Jones match requires either k=-6 or j(k-6)=-2.
The latter gives exactly four integer pairs:
 (j,k)=(1,4),(2,5),(-1,8),(-2,7).
All four have knot closures. The script checks their full Jones polynomials;
only (1,4) agrees. No bound on j or k was used.

2. Exclude k=-6 for every j!=0. Use delta=-A^2-A^-2 and algebra e^2=delta e,
with b=A^-1+Ae, the convention of our earlier TL2 script. This A is inverse
to the usual Spherogram Jones A. For T=f+ge and F_j=N(T b^j),
 delta A^j F_j=(delta^2-1)f+(-1)^j A^(4j)(f+delta g).
This identity holds for every integer j, because the two eigenvalues of b
are A^-1 and -A^3 and b is invertible.

The actual diagram writhes, in Spherogram convention, are
 w_X(j)=-j-4 if j odd, and -j if j even;
 w_R(j)=6-j if j odd, and -6-j if j even, for R=<-1/6>.
These follow by orienting the two parity diagrams: adding two signed twists
returns the endpoint connectivity and reduces writhe by2. The script also
checks every integer -10<=j<=10 independently from actual diagrams.

Since our formal A is inverted, normalized Jones equality is
 F_X,j=A^30 F_R,j for odd j, and F_X,j=A^-18 F_R,j for even j.
Apply the preceding two-eigenvalue formula to each difference. Write its
right side as C0+(-1)^j A^(4j) C1. Exact Laurent arithmetic gives:
 EVEN: C0=A^6+A^-2-A^-18-A^-26, C1=-C0.
 Thus equality forces A^(4j)=1, hence j=0.
 ODD: minimum exponent of both C0,C1 is -14, but their maximum exponents
 are50 and46 respectively, with nonzero extreme coefficients.
 Equality C0=A^(4j)C1 would force j=0 from the minima and j=1 from the maxima,
 a contradiction. The full coefficient dictionaries are in the JSON.
This excludes the entire k=-6 branch.

3. Framing at the sole remaining pair. Standard Spherogram writhes for the
first outputs are0 (X) and4 (R); for the second outputs they are both-5.
Thus in the standard A convention the first brackets are1 andA^12, while
the second brackets agree exactly. Changing the framing of X or R multiplies
both its output brackets by the SAME power of -A^3. No single such adjustment
can simultaneously erase a four-twist defect in the first product and a
zero-twist defect in the second. The probes can also be reframed, but that
changes both competing outputs equally and does not repair the mismatch.
The argument is valid in Sikora's balanced framed category as well.

This is a complete processive classification for the fixed X, including
negative reaction powers and infinity comparator. It does not classify
other tangles or more general rational probe pairs.

A CONCRETE MIRROR REPAIR AND WHY IT FAILS
Vertically stack X with its mirror and do the same with R. The numerator
closure of the stack is the connected sum of the two numerator closures,
so both zero-probe outputs are actual unknots. The algebraic framing defects
indeed cancel there. Add a single crossing probe: the rational stack still
closes to an unknot, but the X stack closes to the actual knot10_153.
The script mirror_geometry.py verifies this by reducing the diagram and
matching its complete PD crossing set with Link('10_153'), not merely by
matching an invariant. Its Jones polynomial is
 -t^4+t^3-t^2+t+1+t^-2-t^-3+t^-4-t^-5,
so the proposed repair fails at the second measured channel.

An exact obstruction extends this failed repair beyond the tested mirror:
under vertical composition the infinity coefficient g multiplies. For X,
 g_X=A^4+A^-8-A^-12
in the formal inverse-A convention. This is not a Laurent-ring unit. Therefore
no finite vertical stack containing X, with any additional actual tangles
and arbitrary framing twists, can have bracket (0,1): it would require g_X
times another Laurent polynomial to equal1. This applies the previously
proved rank-two unit obstruction to the present construction. Its scope is
the exact rational-infinity bracket target, not every possible Jones-one
closure of every possible stack.
