Outcome
-------
No supplied braid lies in all TL kernels. The blanket prediction that every
other summand is nonidentity has a published exception: the Long--Paton
braid acts EXACTLY as identity in W(6,0), shape (3,3), dimension5. Its W(6,2)
block is nonidentity, so this exception supplies no Jones counterexample.

Bigelow B5 (length118)          non-I      --       --       --
Bigelow B6 / Ren (length44)        --       I      non-I    non-I
Long--Paton B6 (length106)         --       I      non-I       I

W(n,k) has k through strands and partition ((n+k)/2,(n-k)/2).
Dimensions: W(5,1)=5; W(6,4)=5; W(6,2)=9; W(6,0)=5.
All input words have exponent sum0. All unreduced Burau matrices were also
checked to be identity exactly over Z[t,t^-1]. Thus no normalization scalar
is hidden in the TL comparisons.

Interpretation
--------------
These are finite explicit evaluations, not a faithfulness result for W(6,2)
and not a general obstruction to scalar matrix-coefficient cancellation.
Long--Paton's example isolates the remaining nontrivial TL behavior in
the nine-dimensional W(6,2) block. It is the particularly relevant seed
for the assigned width-six scalar-coefficient probe.

Bounded structural follow-up (no new braid-family scan)
------------------------------------------------------
Both B6 seeds have rank(rho-I)=6 over Q(A), hence a three-dimensional fixed
space in W(6,2). This is exact, not a genericity guess:

For a=A*I+A^-1*E, with E a conjugate of e_i, rank(E)=dim W(4,2)=3.
For either seed write M=[a,b]. Then
  M-I=(ab-ba)a^-1b^-1,
so rank(M-I)=rank(Eb-bE)<=2 rank(E)=6.
Nonzero six-by-six minors at A=2 mod1000003 give the reverse inequality.
Their explicit row/column indices and determinants are stored in
kernel_structure.json and reproduced by kernel_structure.py.

Consequently, for any fixed cap/cup vectors ell,v, the scalar sequence
ell*M^r*v satisfies a recurrence of order at most7 over Q(A):
Im(M-I) is invariant, has dimension6, and the induced map on the quotient
is identity. This provides a finite-dimensional way to study all powers
of either particular seed. It does NOT determine whether a scalar zero
means a split tangle, nor supply a uniform nonvanishing theorem.

For Long--Paton specifically, the full twist b=(s2 s3 s4 s5)^5 acts on
W(6,2)|B5 = W(5,1) direct_sum W(5,3), dimensions5+4, by A^-12 and1.
The other factor is scalar plus a rank3 idempotent (after dividing E by
delta). Thus it is a commutator of two scalar-plus-idempotent operators.
On W(6,0)|B5=W(5,1), the same b is scalar A^-12, directly explaining the
identity block. No mechanism forcing the requested cap/cup coefficient
nonzero emerged from this reduction; stop here rather than extrapolate.
