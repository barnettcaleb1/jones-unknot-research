BIGELOW6 POWERS ARE TRIVIAL IN THE TWO TESTED CAP/CUP CONFIGURATIONS
Status: TRUSTED. Finished family result; novelty unestablished.

Conventions and statement
------------------------
Use one-based braid generators and strands, first braid letter at the top.
For words, inv reverses the order and changes every sign. Set
  psi1 = [4,-5,-2,1],
  psi2 = [-4,5,5,2,-1,-1],
  a = inv(psi1) [3] psi1,
  b = inv(psi2) [3] psi2,
  beta = a b inv(a) inv(b).
The freely reduced beta is
 [-1,2,5,-4,3,4,-5,-2,1,1,1,-2,-5,-5,4,3,-4,5,5,2,-1,-1,
  -1,2,5,-4,-3,4,-5,-2,1,1,1,-2,-5,-5,4,-3,-4,5,5,2,-1,-1].

Let C be the cap state with caps (2,5),(3,4) and free endpoints 1,6.
Let D be the cap state with caps (1,2),(5,6) and free endpoints 3,4.
These are probe.STATES[4] and probe.STATES[5], respectively.

Theorem. For every integer n, cap_C beta^n cup_D and cap_D beta^n cup_C
are split two-string tangles. Together with the independently supplied
exact all-integer-powers bracket identity [X_n]=e for both configurations,
they are the trivial split tangle, up to unframed boundary-relative isotopy.
In particular this Bigelow6 power family produces no Jones-unknot
counterexample in either configuration.

Proof
-----
1. Actual braid identity. Use the Artin action B6 -> Aut(F6):
     sigma_i(x_i) = x_i x_(i+1) x_i^-1,
     sigma_i(x_(i+1)) = x_i,
   fixing the other free generators. Exact free reduction of the images
   under beta sigma_3 beta^-1 sigma_3^-1 gives
                     [x1,x2,x3,x4,x5,x6].
   This is a finite group-word certificate, recorded in central_arc_artin.json
   and reproduced by central_arc_artin.py. Controls checked sigma_i and
   sigma_i^-1 cancellation, sigma1 sigma2 sigma1 = sigma2 sigma1 sigma2,
   and sigma1 sigma3 = sigma3 sigma1. Faithfulness of the Artin action
   therefore gives [beta,sigma_3]=1 in the ACTUAL braid group.

2. Central arc preservation. Conjugation of a half twist carries its
   supporting arc to the image arc. Equality of the resulting half twists
   identifies their arc isotopy classes. Thus beta preserves the standard
   arc gamma from puncture 3 to puncture 4. So does beta^n for every
   integer n, including negative n and zero.

3. Cap slide and splitting. In cap_C beta^n cup_D, the cap on gamma can
   be slid through the braid using this preserved arc isotopy class.
   It reaches the two free D endpoints 3,4. A regular neighborhood of
   the transported cap gives a boundary-parallel arc and a disk separating
   it from the remaining arc. Hence the tangle is split. No coefficient
   or homology inference is used in this step. Reflection proves the
   same statement for cap_D beta^n cup_C, since it replaces beta^n by
   beta^-n and exchanges the cap/cup roles.

4. The possible local knot is two-bridge. Forget the two central strands.
   What remains is a four-strand braid. The other top cap and closure of
   its free endpoints give the nested matching; the bottom gives the
   adjacent matching. Each is a trivial two-string tangle. Closing the
   remaining arc therefore gives a four-plat, hence a two-bridge knot J_n.
   This is the only possible local knot after the boundary-parallel arc
   from step 3 has split off.

5. Determinant one removes the local knot. The separately supplied exact
   bracket identity [X_n]=e says the scalar multiplying the split tangle
   is 1, in the chosen framing. Changing framing only multiplies by a
   bracket unit. Thus the knot J_n has determinant 1: evaluate the
   normalized Jones polynomial at -1 and take its absolute value.
   A two-bridge knot K(p,q) has determinant |p|; |p|=1 is the unknot.
   Hence J_n is unknotted, proving the claimed triviality. This determinant
   argument also avoids any ambiguity from cap-slide framing units.

Computational calibration, separate from the uniform argument
-----------------------------------------------------------
For n=2,3,4, both configurations were additionally checked directly:
all six split by boundary-relative Reidemeister moves, their actual
numerator closures simplify to the two-component unlink, and their actual
denominator closures simplify to the unknot. The resulting Property R
certificates agree with the uniform argument. These six cases are saved
in powers_topology.json and reproduced by powers_topology.py.

Scope
-----
This disposes of this explicit family. It proves neither a general
width-six zero-implies-split statement nor Jones unknot detection. The
mechanism is beta's preserved actual central arc, which need not occur
for other exact coefficient-zero braids. Novelty has not been assessed.
