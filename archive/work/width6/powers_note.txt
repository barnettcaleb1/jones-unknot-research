ALL-POWERS FRAMED BRACKET IDENTITY

Work over R=Z[A,A^-1], with delta=-A^2-A^-2 and
rho(sigma_i)=A*1+A^-1*e_i. Products of braid letters are drawn
top-to-bottom from left to right, and act on columns rightmost first.

In B_6 set
  psi_1 = sigma_4 sigma_5^-1 sigma_2^-1 sigma_1,
  psi_2 = sigma_4^-1 sigma_5^2 sigma_2 sigma_1^-2,
  a = psi_1^-1 sigma_3 psi_1,
  b = psi_2^-1 sigma_3 psi_2,

Use 1-based strand positions, ordered left-to-right at each boundary.

Let D have cap/cup pairs (1,2),(5,6), leaving positions 3,4 free.
The caps in C are nested. In probe.py, C and D are state indices 4 and 5.

For every integer n, both 2,2 tangles
  X_n = cap_C beta^n cup_D,
  Y_n = cap_D beta^n cup_C
have framed Kauffman bracket EXACTLY e in TL_2, where e joins the two
top boundary points and joins the two bottom boundary points. There is
no unrecorded Laurent monomial or framing factor in this identity.

FINITE CERTIFICATE AND PROOF

Write [X_n]=u_n*1+v_n*e (and the same argument for Y_n).
The identity coefficient u_n is a matrix coefficient of rho(beta)^n
on W(6,2), dimension 9. Exact Laurent arithmetic gives u_n=0 for
n=0,...,9. Cayley-Hamilton gives an order-9 recurrence; the constant
term is a unit because rho(beta) is invertible over R. The first nine
zeros therefore imply u_n=0 for every integer n, in both directions.

For the numerator closure, join the two free points of each cap/cup
state. This is a matrix coefficient on W(6,0), dimension 5. Use the
unnormalized closure convention in which every circle contributes
delta, including the first circle. The computed value is delta^2 for
n=0,...,6. Subtracting delta^2 gives a matrix coefficient of the
invertible 6x6 block matrix diag(rho_W(6,0)(beta),1). Cayley-Hamilton
and the first six zeros show that the numerator value is delta^2 for
every integer n. But N(X_n)=u_n*delta+v_n*delta^2. Thus u_n=0 and
v_n=1, since R is an integral domain and delta^2 is nonzero.

TOPOLOGICAL LIMIT

This proves a framed skein identity, not that all X_n,Y_n are trivial
tangles. At n=1 both actual numerator closures simplify to the
two-component unlink and both denominator closures simplify to U;
Property R then supplies a separate rational/split-tangle certificate.
Additional finite exponents are being checked separately by the
topology agent. No uniform topological result is asserted here.

A short possible geometric target is a uniform cup/cap slide: a swaps
positions 1,6 and b swaps positions 2,5, so their endpoint permutations
are disjoint and beta is pure. Thus all powers have the same bare
two-arc connectivity. In W(6,2), rho(a) fixes the C basis line up to
the scalar A, consistent with pushing a boundary twist past C. That
cell-module observation alone does NOT establish the required isotopy;
the remaining cup/cap geometry must be controlled independently.
