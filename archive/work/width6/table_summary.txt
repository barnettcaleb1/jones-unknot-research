METHOD: Spherogram 2.4.1 census PD diagrams, HT-style names (e.g. 11a1,
12n1), converted to Regina 7.4.1 PD input by shifting labels by one.
Regina's exact Jones polynomial was computed for the original diagram
and for a fresh copy after each individual crossing change. Equality was
tested as exact Laurent polynomial equality. Crossing counts and the
one-component condition were checked for every original input.
HT names avoid the historical Perko duplicate in Rolfsen-style labels.

CONTROLS: The three crossings of a minimum trefoil diagram each change
its Jones polynomial to 1. The four-crossing Markov-stabilized trefoil
given by the braid word [1,1,1,2] has exactly one Jones-preserving crossing
change, at zero-based index 3, as expected: changing the stabilization
crossing preserves the knot. Thus the comparison procedure detects a
known preserving change when one is present.

SCOPE: One census diagram per prime knot type, not every minimum-crossing
diagram. The result also covers the corresponding mirrored diagrams,
since variable inversion preserves polynomial equality and inequality.
It says nothing about arbitrary nonminimal diagrams, additional minimal
diagrams, composite knots, or knots above 12 crossings. In particular,
the stabilized-trefoil control demonstrates why nonminimal diagrams cannot
be included in a blanket claim that all preserving changes are absent.
There are no hit identifications or plat widths to report.
