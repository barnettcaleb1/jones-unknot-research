U5-W6 test 3: topology of the supplied exact-zero cases
Status: TRUSTED, finite computational classification.

Input: 1,314 exact coefficient-zero cases supplied in probe_candidates.json.
These are CASES (braid word, top cap state, bottom cup state), not a claim
of 1,314 distinct isotopy types.

440 cases have internal closed components and are excluded.
All remaining 874 two-arc tangles have a split certificate:
  562: boundary-relative Reidemeister I/II, then disjoint arc projections.
  288: actual N closure simplifies to the two-component unlink, and actual
       D closure simplifies to the unknot. In the branched double cover,
       Property R implies a solid-torus cover; the tangle is rational and
       its unlink numerator fixes the split slope.
   20: boundary-relative Reidemeister III plus I/II, then disjoint projections.
    4: additional relative moves, then all mixed crossings have the same
       arc above the other; separating their heights gives a splitting
       disk. One of these four already has no mixed crossings at the end.
    0: unresolved cases.

The final uniform-over cases have input indices 922, 924, 985, 1151.
Indices 924,985,1151 have respectively 4,2,2 mixed crossings, all with
arc 1 above arc 0. Their endpoint pairs are noninterleaving, as required
for this boundary-relative height-separation certificate. Index 922 has
zero mixed crossings.

The two nonclosed Bigelow6 cases (i,j)=(4,5),(5,4) are both certified by
actual closures and Property R. The two initially difficult unit-Jones
cases of word lengths 108 and 110 are directly split after relative RIII.

SPLIT DOES NOT MEAN TRIVIAL: Some cases contain local knots on an arc.
434 cases were already crossing-free after I/II. The test requested
splitness, so local knots are retained and are not called the trivial
tangle merely from a zero coefficient. No negative inference was made
from a simplifier getting stuck. No Jones equality was used as an unknot
or unlink recognition algorithm.

The direct diagram procedure fuses crossing-free strands, excludes closed
components, performs R1/R2 only away from the tangle boundary, and uses
R3 only at internal triangular faces. R3 rewiring follows Spherogram's
local formula, with boundary-safe endpoint connections. Saved move lists
and deterministic seeds permit replay. The Property R route is the
theorem-level certificate supplied by the root agent, separate from the
direct Reidemeister certificates.
