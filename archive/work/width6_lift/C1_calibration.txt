C1: THE CORRECT LAWRENCE / TEMPERLEY–LIEB CALIBRATION
2026-11-03. Status TRUSTED (exact algebra and published input).

RESULT
------
The requested direct specialization of a 15-dimensional Hecke module and
unit multiple of the Budney pairing does not exist as stated. There is a
precise repair: the TL module is a nine-dimensional submodule at
t=-q^-1, and its Gram form is a derivative of a degenerating pairing.
The derivative is (1+q) times the TL Gram form, not a Laurent unit times it.
Every identity below was checked generically by exact Laurent arithmetic,
apart from the separately labelled rational calibration at q=4.

PRIMARY SOURCE AND CONVENTIONS
-----------------------------
Bigelow, The Lawrence-Krammer Representation (2003), Theorem6.1:
https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~bigelow/publications/08.pdf
At t=-q^-1, the IMAGE of absolute homology in the specified relative
homology module is the TL representation (n-2,2). In the absolute basis
v_ij, the map kills the n classes j=i+1 and (i,j)=(1,3); the other
n(n-3)/2 image classes give its basis. Section5.2 warns that the absolute
Lawrence lattice and the usual Krammer fork lattice are not isomorphic
over the Laurent ring, although they are over Q(q,t). Specialization
therefore must keep track of the lattice.

We use the Krammer fork matrices F_ij displayed in Bigelow section5.2,
not his absolute v_ij lattice. Generator eigenvalues are
                 1, -q, -t q^2.
At the correct TL parameter t=-q^-1 these are 1,-q,+q. This is NOT an
eigenvalue collision. The full module cannot be a direct sum of the
three stated Hecke modules 9+5+1: it still has the +q eigenvalue.
At q=4 each quadratic Hecke defect has rank1, hence is explicitly nonzero.
More explicitly its characteristic polynomial at the TL line is
                   (z-1)^10 (z+q)^4 (z-q),
whereas W(6,2) has characteristic polynomial (z-1)^6 (z+q)^3.
The +q eigenvalue alone rules out the proposed three-Hecke-summand model.

For our bracket convention sigma_i=A I+A^-1 e_i, set
               s=A^-2, q=s^2=A^-4, t=-s^-2=-A^4.
The Krammer/TL generator identification is with the normalized braid
generator A^-1 rho_TL(sigma_i)=I+s e_i. Here delta=-s-s^-1.

GENERIC NINE-DIMENSIONAL SUBMODULE
--------------------------------
Start with the fork vector
                  v=F13-F14-F23+F24.
For every q,t, sigma1 v=sigma3 v=-q v and sigma5 v=v. At t=-q^-1,
generate the nine columns by applying e_i=(sigma_i-I)/s in the same
orders that generate our nine TL link states from C0=(12)(34), free56.
The resulting 15-by-9 matrix B(s) satisfies exactly
               K_i(s^2,-s^-2) B(s) = B(s) [I+s e_i]
for all five generators. The columns are generically independent: their
q=4 specialization has rank9. Thus this explicitly embeds W(6,2) into
the specialized fork module over Q(s), and gives an integral Laurent
map over Z[s,s^-1]. No six-dimensional complement is asserted.

For clarity, the 15-by-9 B(s) is NOT the six-by-15 incidence matrix
appearing below. Its complete entries are in generic_intertwiner.json.

THE FORM: TWO DEGENERACIES AND A DERIVATIVE
-----------------------------------------
Use Budney's form as implemented by the pairing agent in budney_gram.py.
It is linear in its first argument: <x,y>=x^T G bar(y). The bar inverts
q and t. Its published common factor is
        c=-(1-t)(1+qt)(q-1)^2 t^-2 q^-3.
Write Graw=c Gnorm and epsilon=1+qt.

First, c vanishes at epsilon=0, so EVERY raw Budney entry specializes to
zero. Multiplication by a Laurent unit cannot fix this.

Second, after dividing by c, the specialized matrix is
 Gnorm(q,-q^-1)=-(q-1) Inc(q^-1)^T Inc(q),
where the (i,j) column of Inc(q) is e_i+q e_j.
Its rank is6 and its left radical has dimension9. Our B(s) lies in this
left radical, equivalently Inc(q^-1)B(s)=0. The right radical uses the
barred columns. Confusing the two radicals would give a false basis
mismatch.

Let G1=(dGnorm/d epsilon)|epsilon=0. The exact generic 9-by-9 identity is
                 B(s)^T G1 B(s^-1)=(1+s^2) Gram_TL.
All81 entries were checked, including zero entries and delta powers.
Thus division by the additional NONUNIT 1+q gives the TL Gram form.

This derivative is independent of first-order choices of lifts: if a
lift changes by epsilon*u, the new first-order contribution pairs u
with the zeroth-order radical, so vanishes. Equivalently, the raw form
has a second-order zero on such lifted cap classes and
       [epsilon^-2 B^T Graw bar(B)]_(epsilon=0)
                     = -(q-q^-1)^2 Gram_TL.
This last scalar again is not a Laurent unit.

WHAT THIS ENABLES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
-------------------------------------
Given CHOSEN geometric cap lifts u_C(q,t), u_C'(q,t) whose specializations
lie in this radical, the normalized pairing is divisible by epsilon.
Its quotient specializes to (1+q) times the TL pairing after matching
the cap normalizations. This defines a repaired two-variable object
for further tests; it is not the raw Budney form proposed in the message.
It does not establish invariance under three-dimensional cap slides,
which is a separate geometric descent question.

Short actual braid transports for all nine standard cap states have
been recorded in cap_braid_lifts.json. Each is a product of elementary
two-neighbor-generator cap slides and satisfies
                   rho_TL_normalized(w_j) v_C0=s^k v_j,
with k=length(w_j)/2. Therefore a chosen geometric lift is
                   s^-k K(w_j) v.
Its specialized image equals the corresponding B(s) column. The
generic lift can differ from an algebraic e-descendant by epsilon terms;
the derivative pairing is unaffected, but the full two-variable
polynomial need not be unaffected. This distinction is material for C3.
