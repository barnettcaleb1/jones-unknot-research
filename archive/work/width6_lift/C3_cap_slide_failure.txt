Use C0=(1,2),(3,4), free endpoints5,6, and its generic fork lift
v=F13-F14-F23+F24. Let

 beta0=[4,3,2,1,5,4,3,2,3,4],

 w=beta0*r1^-1=[4,3,2,1,5,4,3,2,3,4,2,3,-1,-2].

r1 is the elementary Tawn/Hilden cap slide of the first two caps.
It fixes the actual cap tangle C0 and its TL vector, but not the generic
two-variable cap-product vector. Its specialized action fixes v exactly.

Let P(w)=Gnorm(v, K(mirror(w))v)/(1+qt), with linear-first Budney
pairing and conjugation in the second argument as implemented in
lift_pairing.py. This is the repaired two-variable pairing from C1;
P|_(t=-q^-1)=(1+q)J in the matching normalized conventions.

The exact calculations are
                    P(beta0)=0,
          P(w)=q^4(1-q)(1-t)(1+qt) !=0,
          J(beta0)=J(w)=0.

The expanded nonzero P(w) has only seven monomials:
 q^4 - q^4 t - q^5 +2q^5 t -q^5 t^2 -q^6 t +q^6 t^2.
It vanishes on the Jones specialization. The lexicographically largest
(t-degree,q-degree) monomial q^6 t^2 dies: along collapsed weight a-b=4,
q^4 +2q^5 t +q^6 t^2 contributes 1-2+1=0 after t=-q^-1.

This is NOT a Jones counterexample. The cap slide preserves the actual
tangle. Moreover, for w itself, boundary-relative R1/R2 simplification
reduces X_w to the crossing-free split tangle, and its actual closures
are U_2 and U. Thus the witness lies on the actual-unknot locus and is
geometrically trivial. The generic lift remembers an arc-system choice
that the three-dimensional cap tangle forgets.

For an explicit action check, write q=s^2. The generic action r1(v) is

 F12: (q-1)t^-1 +q(q-1),
 F13: -q^-1 t^-1 +t^-1 -q^2 t,

with all other coordinates zero. At t=-q^-1 this becomes exactly v;
generically the extra F12 coordinate is (q-1)t^-1(1+qt), so it is not
even a scalar multiple of v.

Consequences
------------
1. The raw Budney form cannot satisfy the proposed unit specialization;
   C1 already repairs it with a derivative. Even after that repair, NJC
   is FALSE for these explicit geometric lifts.
2. Restricting to D(X)=U does not repair NJC: the witness already lies
   there and X is actually trivial.
3. An invariant of cap tangle types must quotient or otherwise eliminate
   this cap-slide dependence before a new extremal-monomial strategy can
   imply KL(6,2). Generic LKB faithfulness sees precisely more data than
   the cap tangle retains.
4. This does not refute KL(6,2), LemmaQ, or Jones unknot detection.
