SHORT WITNESS: EXACT FACTORS, ORDERS AND PAIR GROUPINGS
Algebra-only clarification of Claude 20261103-030900.

Use q (not s=sqrt(q)), h=1+qt, normal coordinate t=-q^-1(1+u), h=-u.
Let
  H=q^4(1-q)(1-t),
  P=H h                       [saved regularized polynomial],
  F=h P=H h^2                 [pairing for the divided Budney matrix],
  c=-(1-t)h(q-1)^2 t^-2 q^-3,
  L_raw=c F=q(q-1)^3(1-t)^2 t^-2 h^3.

Thus over Q(q), the exact orders along the TL line are respectively
  ord(P)=1, ord(F)=2, ord(L_raw)=3.
In particular the saved P does NOT vanish to second order.

The normal-coordinate expressions are
  P = q^3(q-1)(q+1+u) u,
  F = -q^3(q-1)(q+1+u) u^2,
  L_raw = -q(q-1)^3 (q+1+u)^2/(1+u)^2 * u^3.
Therefore
  P'(0)=q^3(q^2-1) != 0,
  F''(0)=-2q^3(q^2-1) != 0,
  L_raw''(0)=0,
  L_raw'''(0)=-6q(q-1)^3(q+1)^2 != 0.
These are generic identities; q=+-1 are exceptional specializations.

P has seven aggregated monomials (a,b,coefficient), meaning q^a t^b:
  (4,0,+1), (4,1,-1), (5,0,-1), (5,1,+2),
  (5,2,-1), (6,1,-1), (6,2,+1).
This is eight signed monomial copies if the +2 term is split into two copies.
An explicit h-pair grouping is
  +(q^4     + q^5 t)
  -(q^5     + q^6 t)
  -(q^4 t   + q^5 t^2)
  +(q^5 t   + q^6 t^2).
Each pair has equal polynomial coefficient signs. After specialization,
(-1)^b reverses the sign between its members, so its value is zero.
Thus a geometric sign-reversing involution must distinguish intersection
sign from the extra (-1)^b factor; these are not the same sign convention.

The second grouping belongs to F=hP, not to P itself.
For each seed epsilon*q^a*t^b in
  H=q^4-q^5-q^4*t+q^5*t,
F contains the four signed copies
  epsilon * [q^a*t^b
             +q^(a+1)*t^(b+1)
             +q^(a+1)*t^(b+1)
             +q^(a+2)*t^(b+2)].
At t=-q^-1 these have common q-degree a-b and relative weights
  1,-1,-1,1.
Their zeroth sum is 0; their first normal-derivative sum is
  b-(b+1)-(b+1)+(b+2)=0.
Their second-derivative sum is
  b(b-1)-2b(b+1)+(b+2)(b+1)=2,
so the second derivative of F is NOT zero. Multiplication by c supplies
one further h, giving the zero second derivative of the raw pairing.
The raw h^3 expansion has binomial weights 1,-3,3,-1: moments of degrees
0,1,2 vanish; the third falling-factorial moment does not.

These groupings are exact polynomial identities, not geometric intersection
pairings. The archived matrix calculation combines contributions before
saving them. Polynomial divisibility does not identify an involution on the
original geometric intersection set, and in general quotient coefficients
can require cancellation between formal copies absent in the original set.
The explicit trivial-tangle isotopy certifies a separating disk independently;
no intersection-point involution realizing that disk was reconstructed.
