1. THREE DISTINCT PAIRINGS, NOT ONE
Bigelow's original Key Lemma pairs ONE fork (with a parallel copy of its tine)
and ONE noodle, in the two-point configuration space. It does not immediately
assert a Key Lemma for products of TWO independent cap arcs and dual products.

Budney's form is an absolute-homology sesquilinear form, linear in its FIRST
argument. The TL cell form is ordinarily presented as a bilinear form with an
anti-involution. Conjugating A (or s) to its inverse is necessary to turn the
braid action into an isometry. For example a cap vector is an eigenvector of
sigma1 with eigenvalue -A^-3, so the raw bilinear norm scales by A^-6.

2. SPECIALIZATION AND DERIVATIVE REPAIR
Use Budney/Bigelow-2003 variables q,t, and q=s^2. The Jones line is
    h=1+qt=0, i.e. t=-q^-1=-s^-2.
The correct collapsed exponent of s^a t^b is a-2b, with sign (-1)^b.
For q^a t^b it is a-b, also with sign (-1)^b. Thus the earlier proposed
q-exponent collapse a-2b used the wrong variable/line combination.

Budney's Theorem2 writes his Gram matrix as c(q,t) G(q,t), where
    c=-(1-t)(1+qt)(q-1)^2 t^-2 q^-3.
The entire RAW Gram specializes to zero. Dividing c is not multiplication by
a Laurent unit. Even after that division,
    G(q,-q^-1)=-(q-1) B(q^-1)^T B(q),
where column(i,j) of the 6x15 incidence matrix B(q) is e_i+q e_j.
This factorization gives rank6 and a nine-dimensional radical over Q(q).
Remember that the form is linear first: the relevant LEFT radical is
ker B(q^-1). Numerical exact Fraction checks at q=2,3,4 agree; the displayed
factorization supplies the generic rank statement.

Let G1 be the h coefficient of G(q,(h-1)/q). The form induced by G1 on the
radical is independent of first-order changes of lifts: cross terms contain
G0 applied to a radical vector and vanish. It is a derivative/residue form.
Exact rank9 checks at q=2,3,4 were followed by the C1 agent's generic identity
    E(s)^T G1 E(s^-1)=(1+q) Gamma_TL,
for the explicit 15x9 intertwiner E. All81 entries were checked. There is no
unit relating the naive raw specialization to the TL form; the correct
construction includes this derivative and the nonunit scalar 1+q.

splitting mechanism. A viable geometric Key Lemma must track complete
intersection matchings / cap slides / splitting disks, not demand that every
individual arc intersection vanish.

7. CLAUDE'S 03:00 REVISION: THE JET CALIBRATION IS VALID, NOT YET A PROOF
The new message 20261103-030009 replaces the old assertion by: raw fork L has
order exactly2 along the TL line at every NONSPLIT pair. Our trivial14letter
example does NOT refute this weaker formulation. Keep these claims separate.

With epsilon=1+qt (not the s used above for sqrt(q)), the exact calibration is
    [L_raw / epsilon^2]_(epsilon=0)=-(q-q^-1)^2 J_norm.
For the normal coordinate u with t=-q^-1(1+u), epsilon=-u, so
    (d^2/du^2 L_raw)|_0=-2(q-q^-1)^2 J_norm.
The scalar is nonzero over Q(q), but it is NOT a Laurent unit. The order is
second for the raw fork lattice; the actual absolute cap-torus pairing instead
has zeroth-order specialization (q-1)^2/q J_norm. Lattice/regularization choice
must be stated before assigning an intrinsic jet order.

After fixing those conventions, 'nonsplit implies exact order2' is equivalent
to nonzero J on nonsplit pairs, namely the original KL zero-implies-split
statement. It is a useful translation, but currently supplies no independent
geometric mechanism. The14letter trivial example shows that order3 genuinely
occurs on the same actual-unknot locus, where it must be allowed.

Individual exponents b=0 or1 do not have an intrinsic 'less than a full turn'
meaning in this argument: changing the chosen cover lift multiplies the pairing
by t^k and shifts all b by k. Since the lower jets vanish, the complete second
jet still changes only by a unit, but the contribution assigned to each term
by b(b-1) changes. A monomial proof must respect that gauge dependence.
