TOTAL ONE-POINT INTERSECTION COMPLEXITY DOES NOT DETECT SPLITNESS
Scoped test of Conjecture T after replacing one boundary square by the
correct figure-eight-product dual. This is not an audit of a different
boundary-noodle functional, whose Jones calibration failed separately.

GEOMETRY
Place the punctures at 1,...,6 on the real axis. Source caps are straight
arcs D=(23)(56). The target cap type is C=(12)(34). Its torus dual is the
product of two figure-eight loops gamma12 and gamma34. Each loop consists
of a radius 0.26 counterclockwise circle around its first puncture, a
clockwise circle around its second, and connecting paths below the real
axis. The two loops have disjoint supports.

The source arc23 meets gamma12 once at (2.26,0), and gamma34 once at
(2.74,0). Both ordinary oriented intersections are -1 for the explicit
orientations in geometric_intersections.py. Arc56 misses both loops.
Thus the geometric and signed one-point matrices are respectively

    [[1,1],[0,0]],       [[-1,-1],[0,0]].

There is no complete matching: one of the two configuration points would
have to lie on arc56 and a target loop. Therefore there are ZERO actual
configuration-space intersection pairs. The mixed pairing and its Jones
specialization vanish identically, without cancellation.

TIGHTNESS FOR THIS TOTAL-INTERSECTION COMPLEXITY
The figure-eight homology classes are
    [gamma12]=[meridian1]-[meridian2],
    [gamma34]=[meridian3]-[meridian4].
These classes are fixed by changing a representative of the cap tangle
type while preserving its endpoint pairs (including cap slides). The
algebraic intersection of a path from puncture2 to puncture3 with a closed
curve is, up to the orientation convention, the difference of the curve's
winding indices at those endpoints. Each of the displayed figure-eights
therefore has intersection of absolute value one with EVERY such arc23.
The total geometric intersection count is consequently at least two for
every allowed representative of these two cap types. The displayed
representative attains two, so it is a GLOBAL minimizer for this corrected
total one-point complexity, not merely a diagram with no visible bigons.

TANGLE AND JONES CHECK
In the archived W(6,2) basis the target C is state7 and source D state2.
Direct independent TL computation gives coefficient([],7,2)={}; bare
incidence gives no closed components and boundary pairing (01)(23).
With no braid crossings, the resulting two-arc tangle is the trivial
e-tangle; its separating disk is visible in the planar cap/cup diagram.

Hence the statement
    "at every total-intersection minimizer with c>0, J is nonzero"
is FALSE for the corrected figure-eight-product dual: here c=2 and J=0,
with an actual split two-arc tangle. This is not a counterexample to
KL(6,2). It falsifies the proposed assertion that split pairs always have
zero TOTAL one-point complexity in this corrected dual model.

The obstruction is elementary: total intersection counts isolated edges,
whereas the configuration pairing requires a complete matching. A viable
replacement would have to handle matching support or use a complexity
whose vanishing is actually equivalent to the desired split geometry.
No such replacement is proved here.

ARTIFACTS AND CONTROLS
geometric_intersections.py builds actual PL curves and enumerates their
intersection points and configuration pairs; it does not expand matrices.
geometric_tight_counter.json contains the two explicit point positions,
their signs and curve parameters, and the empty configuration-pair list.
The simple counterexample also has the direct exact circle description
above, so its count does not depend on a floating-point recognition claim.

The same code has baseline identity and sigma2 / sigma2^-1 controls saved
in geometric_controls.json. Their counts are stable under doubling curve
resolution. The identity has four configuration intersections with
(a,b,sign)=(0,0,+1),(1,0,+1),(1,0,+1),(2,0,+1), hence (1+q)^2.
Multiplication by q^-2 gives the required mixed self value
q^-1*delta^2=(1+q)^2/q^2. Generator controls have six configuration
intersections each and specialize to 1+q and -(1+q^-1), compatible up to
per-sample deck units with q^-1 times the calibrated Jones entries.
Those controls do not establish minimum position, and the reference lift
is chosen independently at the first intersection for each sample.
