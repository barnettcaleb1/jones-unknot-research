1. A CONCRETE ESSENTIAL BURau-ZERO ARC/NOODLE PAIR

Let beta be the 106-letter Long--Paton braid from kernel_words.py.
Let c1=beta(arc34), and let N1 be a vertical boundary-to-boundary noodle
between punctures p2 and p3 (punctures ordered along the real diameter).
The braid beta is pure and its W(6,4)/Burau matrix is exactly the identity.
Thus <N1,c1> equals <N1,arc34>=0 over the generic field: the standard arc34
lies entirely on the right of N1. The same statement follows for the
puncture-relative arc class from the absolute Burau class after inverting
the nonzero factor 1-q.

Nevertheless c1 cannot be isotoped off N1. Its half-twist is
  a=beta sigma3 beta^-1.
If c1 lay in the right subdisk, a would lie in the standard braid subgroup
on punctures3,...,6 and its Artin automorphism would fix x1,x2.
The following finite-group evaluation proves it does not fix x1:
map the free generator xi to [[1,i],[i,1+i^2]] in SL2(F101), i=1,...,6.
Applying the Artin action of a sends x1 from
  [[1,1],[1,2]]
to
  [[37,3],[18,67]].
These matrices differ, so the free-group images differ. This is an exact
nonidentity witness, not a probabilistic inference or a faithfulness claim
for the finite quotient. Full evaluated images are retained in JSON.

3. THE DUALITY DISTINCTION IS MATERIAL

M_noodle above is NOT the one-cup TL/Burau Gram matrix of the top cap arcs
against the lower cap arcs. In the same row/column order, that latter matrix
is (off-diagonal adjacent arcs have pairing1; disjoint ones0):
  M_cap(A)=[[1,0],[1,1]], det=1;
  M_cap(B)=[[1,1],[0,0]], det=0;
  M_cap(C)=[[1,1],[0,1]], det=1.

We computed the generic regularized pairing P and absolute torus-torus
pairing H=(q-1)^2 q^-2 P/(1-t) for all three cases. Exact division and
specialization give
  H(q,1)=((q-1)^2/q) det M_cap,
  H(q,-q^-1)=((q-1)^2/q) J_hat.
The regularized P has 687,837,878 aggregated terms. The absolute H has
687,838,880. Thus in A and C the determinant of the genuine vertical-noodle
matrix is zero while H(q,1) is nonzero. This is not a contradiction: H is
the torus-torus pairing, not the pairing against those two literal vertical
noodles. Identifying the two would be a false dictionary.

This computation therefore supplies three real Long--Paton arc/noodle
completions and their cap Jones coefficients, but it does NOT establish
that those J values are the exchange-twisted determinants of the specified
M_noodle. A correct boundary dual to a TL cap is additional data.

5. STRONGER FINAL SET: THREE e-TYPE COMPLETIONS

  tau = 1, sigma4^2, sigma4^-2.

6. ESSENTIAL ZERO PAIR FOR THE PROPER ONE-CUP CAP GRAM

The original e-type case B also supplies an essential zero pair directly
for the cap Gram, independent of the literal-noodle dictionary:
  top cap arc56 versus beta(arc34).
Its one-cup pairing is zero because beta is a Burau kernel and arc56,
arc34 are disjoint in the untwisted configuration. But the actual image
arc beta(arc34) cannot be made disjoint from arc56. Half-twists about
disjoint arcs with distinct endpoints commute, whereas
  a=beta sigma3 beta^-1
does NOT commute with sigma5. In the same exact SL2(F101) evaluation as
above, a sigma5 sends x1 to [[37,3],[18,67]], while sigma5 a sends it to
[[5,19],[26,99]]. Full data: lp_arc56_certificate.json.

Thus the zero row for M_cap in original case B contains a geometrically
essential arc/cap pair, and its two-cup J is nonzero. This is a valid
specific instance where the two-cup pairing detects data missed by the
one-cup pairing. The sigma4 transports in Section5 change this top cap,
so their nonzero determinants must not be presented as further completions
of the SAME proper cap-Gram zero pair. They preserve the literal-noodle
zero pair of Section1 instead.
