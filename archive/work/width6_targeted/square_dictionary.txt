The relevant extended pairing is
 H2(C~,eta~) x H2(C~,boundary C~) -> Z[q^+-1,t^+-1].
A pair of disjoint puncture-to-puncture arcs c1,c2 represents the first
square. A pair of disjoint boundary-to-boundary arcs N1,N2 represents
the second square. Its intersections are the two complete matchings:
 (c1 intersect N1) x (c2 intersect N2), and
 (c1 intersect N2) x (c2 intersect N1).
Bigelow section4 describes individual monomials using loops in configuration
space; their sign contains the matching-permutation sign.

1. t=1 FACTORIZATION: VALID FOR ACTUAL SQUARES, WITH CONVENTIONS FIXED
At t=1 the local system is the symmetric product of the one-point q local
systems. With ordinary product orientations, the pairing is det(M), up
to one common deck unit, where Mij is the one-point arc/noodle pairing.
At q=1,t=-1, the (-1)^b exchange factor cancels the permutation sign,
so the pairing is, up to one overall sign, the permanent of the ordinary
algebraic-intersection matrix I. This latter statement only concerns the
literal two embedded squares, not an arbitrary homological dual class.

One zero M entry does not force a zero row or determinant. If M11=0 then
det(M)=-M12*M21, which can be nonzero. A Long-Paton zero pair must be
completed so that the OTHER entry of its row is zero if that is required.

2. EXPLICIT FAILURE OF THE OBVIOUS DUAL-NOODLE IDENTIFICATION
Bigelow's puncture square v'14 has arcs(12)(34), our C0. His boundary
square x14 has vertical noodles justrightp1 and justleftp4.
Lemma5.3 gives
 <v'14,x14>'=1,    <v'15,x14>'=0,
where v'15 has arcs(12)(45).
The TL Gram functional of the cap C0 instead gives
 Gamma(C0,C0)=delta^2,    Gamma((12)(45),C0)=delta,
with delta=-s-s^-1 and q=s^2.
Thus x14 is NOT the TL dual of C0 even up to a common scalar. Source-unit
choices do not alter the zero versus nonzero discrepancy. The finite check
is recorded in square_dual_calibration.json.

The 81-entry derivative calibration identifies the TL form with a torus/
absolute pairing after the specified factors. It does not itself supply
a map taking each cap system to ONE embedded boundary-noodle square whose
extended square pairing realizes that same functional. Such a dual map
must be constructed and calibrated. A general linear combination of the
xij can represent the functional over the fraction field, but that is not
automatically a pair of embedded noodles and changes the intersection model.

3. A STRONGER OBSTRUCTION TO SINGLE-SQUARE, UNIT-NORMALIZED JONES PAIRING
Suppose that, for C0=(12)(34), some pair of disjoint embedded boundary
noodles N1,N2 represented its TL Gram dual, with the square-square pairing
equal to Gamma(-,C0) up to a Laurent unit (as a generic polynomial identity).
Specialize q=1, t=-1, s=1. An embedded boundary noodle separates the disk.
Hence the ordinary algebraic intersection Iij of any puncture arc ci with
Nj is0,+1,or-1: it records whether its endpoints lie on opposite sides.
By the matching-sign calculation in section1,
 |square_pairing(C0,N)| = |I11*I22+I12*I21| <= 2.
But Gamma(C0,C0)=(-2)^2=4. Every Laurent unit specializes to+1 or-1.
This is a contradiction.

This REFUTES the literal claim that the ordinary TL Gram form is a
single-embedded-square/single-embedded-square pairing up to only a Laurent
unit, with the source cap represented by its ordinary puncture square.
The self-pair C0/C0 has extra closed components in its cap/cup tangle;
therefore this is an obstruction to the proposed FULL GRAM-FORM dictionary,
not a counterexample restricted to the two-arc/no-closed-component locus.
It does NOT refute a nonunit-normalized realization, a linear combination
of dual squares, another lattice, or the established derivative calibration.
Bigelow6.1 excludes q^2=1 for its module identification, but that does not
evade this test of a proposed GENERIC LAURENT-POLYNOMIAL equality up to unit:
such an equality would still specialize. A rational factor with a pole or
nonunit value at q=1 is a different claim and can evade the argument.

4. A POSITIVE LIMITED t=1 CALIBRATION FOR THE TORUS FORM
For adjacent cap arcs, use the one-cup TL/Burau Gram with delta on the
diagonal,1 between neighboring adjacent arcs,0 otherwise. Let M be its
2x2 cap-pair matrix. Four fixed identity-braid controls give
 P/(1-t)|t=1 = q det(M),
 H(q,1) = (q-1)^2/q * det(M).
Controls against C0: C0 itself(det delta²), (12)(45)(det delta),
(23)(45)(det1), (12)(56)(det0).
This matches the expected determinant after the actual torus normalization.
It is not a construction of the missing single-boundary-square dual map.

5. WHAT AN ACTUAL INTERSECTION CERTIFICATE WOULD NEED
To extract geometric (a,b) and partner maps for the14-letter witness,
one must first choose actual source arcs, actual dual relative cycle(s),
orientations, and cover lift paths matching the calibrated form. Then keep
the individual configuration-space intersections before combining monomials.
Matrix multiplication currently records only their homology aggregate.
The algebraic h=1+qt packets can be checked exactly, but they do not by
themselves recover an involution on the original intersection set.

Bigelow3.3 does provide a RELATED local geometric mechanism: a genus-two
surface is built from two punctured-torus copies joined by an annulus;
the half-rotation around the shared puncture changes the lift by qt, and
the two copies have the same orientation, producing a factor1+qt in its
relative image. This is a candidate local model for a slide defect, not
an established identification of the witness's individual partners.

6. TIGHTNESS SCOPE
Minimizing intersections over cap slides requires the missing dual-cycle
dictionary and an invariant definition of the representatives being varied.
The assertion 'split implies c=0 because a boundary-parallel arc slides off'
needs qualification: a split two-string tangle may carry a local knot on
each arc, so splitting alone does not make either arc boundary-parallel.
Without a precise dual map and model of c, this is a gap rather than a
verified counterexample to Conjecture T. No sample here is claimed globally
tight, and no geometric intersection involution has been certified.
