1. THE CANONICAL DUAL OBJECT EXISTS, BUT IS A TORUS, NOT A NOODLE SQUARE

Let z_C be the absolute product torus of two disjoint figure-eight loops
around the arcs of C. Let S_C be the corresponding puncture-relative
square. Bigelow2003 section3.2 gives, with consistently chosen lifts,

    i_eta(z_C) = (1-q)^2 S_C.

Let j_boundary send absolute homology to boundary-relative homology.
The extended intersection pairing of section4 is natural for these maps.
Therefore, writing H(C,C') for the absolute torus intersection pairing,

    H(C,C')
      = <i_eta(z_C), j_boundary(z_C')>'
      = (1-q)^2 <S_C, j_boundary(z_C')>'.

Thus the canonical mixed pairing is

    Phi(C,C') = <S_C, j_boundary(z_C')>'
              = H(C,C')/(1-q)^2.

The second argument is represented geometrically by the SAME compact
product of two figure-eight loops, now viewed in boundary-relative homology.
It is not a pair of embedded boundary-to-boundary noodles. No arbitrary
choice of an abstract homological dual is required for this repair.

Combining with the fixed existing fork/torus calibration,

    H = (q-1)^2 q^-2 P/(1-t),

gives the exact normalized formula

    Phi = q^-2 P/(1-t),
    Phi|_(t=-q^-1) = q^-1 J_hat.

The last scalar IS a Laurent unit. This is the appropriate direct
geometric specialization model. At the square/torus level the intersection
set is still the set of complete matchings between two source arcs and two
target loops; those loops are immersed figure-eights, not Jordan arcs.
It therefore evades the earlier single-noodle-square q=1 obstruction.
For example, an arc joining the endpoints of a figure-eight may have
ordinary algebraic intersection magnitude2 with that loop. The identity
self-pair can accordingly give4, as required by the TL Gram form.

At t=1, the mixed pairing is the determinant of the appropriate one-point
arc/figure-eight-loop pairing matrix, up to the fixed lift normalization.
The earlier four torus controls become Phi(q,1)=q^-1 det(M_capGram).
Literal arc/noodle matrices and these arc/loop matrices must not be conflated.

For the14-letter witness the mixed polynomial is particularly small:

    Phi = q^2(1-q)(1+qt)
        = (q^2+q^3t) - (q^3+q^4t).

This gives two formal h-partner packets. Identifying these four aggregate
terms with particular original geometric intersection points still needs
an explicit representative and lift-path computation; naturality alone
does not recover those labels.

2. TOTAL ONE-POINT INTERSECTION MINIMUM DOES NOT DETECT SPLITNESS

This section concerns transplanting the proposed complexity to the CORRECT
square-torus model. It does not refute a differently defined noodle model.

Take target cap C0=(12)(34), with the corresponding two disjoint standard
figure-eight loops gamma12 and gamma34. Take source caps D=(23)(56), with
standard straight arcs. Use the identity braid.

The cap/cup tangle has exactly two arcs and no closed components. It is
the crossing-free split e-tangle; its exact TL id coefficient is zero.
In local state indices this is probe.coefficient([],7,2)=0.

Consider total geometric one-point intersections

    c = #(D1 intersect gamma12) + #(D1 intersect gamma34)
        + #(D2 intersect gamma12) + #(D2 intersect gamma34),

minimized over cap-tangle representatives and their associated figure-eights.
Ordinary first homology gives a global lower bound. Up to overall signs,

    [gamma12] = x1-x2,
    [gamma34] = x3-x4.

The signed intersection of the arc from p2 to p3 with each loop is the
difference of that loop's winding numbers at the two endpoints. Each has
magnitude1. These homology classes are unchanged by cap slides preserving
the endpoint pairings (cap labels/orientations may only be exchanged/reversed).
Hence every allowed representative has c>=2.

The standard geometry realizes exactly those two crossings: arc23 crosses
each figure-eight once, while arc56 is remote from both. Thus the GLOBAL
minimum is c=2. No algorithmic/local-minimum inference is being used.

Nevertheless there is no complete two-point matching: arc56 meets neither
loop. Thus the configuration-space intersection set is empty and Phi=0,
consistent with J_hat=0 and actual splitness.

Consequences:
- The transplanted assertion 'split pairs have c=0' is FALSE.
- The version of Conjecture T asserting nonzero pairing at every global
  minimizer with total one-point c>0 is FALSE in the square-torus model.
- This is NOT a counterexample to the nonsplit Key Lemma. It is a split
  configuration, and it invalidates this total-intersection criterion.
- A repaired complexity must at least distinguish incomplete matchings
  from actual configuration-space intersections. Merely requiring fewer
  arc/loop intersections does not do this.
