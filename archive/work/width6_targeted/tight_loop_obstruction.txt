TIGHTNESS OBSTRUCTION FOR TOTAL ARC / FIGURE-EIGHT-LOOP INTERSECTION
Conditional on adopting the repaired source-square / target-torus model.

Take beta=identity, target caps C=(12)(34), source caps D=(23)(56), with
punctures1,...,6 on a horizontal diameter. Let ell12 and ell34 be the
standard figure-eight loops associated to the respective target caps.
Each has winding numbers +1,-1 at its two endpoints and zero at every
other puncture (up to reversing the whole orientation).

Define c to be the total geometric intersection of the two source arcs
with the two target figure-eight loops, minimized over cap-slide-equivalent
representatives preserving the marked endpoint pairings. Then c_min=2.

LOWER BOUND. Every source arc from p2 to p3 has algebraic intersection
of absolute value1 with ell12, and also absolute value1 with ell34, by the
difference of the loops' winding numbers at its endpoints. Hence it has
at least two geometric intersections in total. Cap slides change paths
but not the endpoint winding charges of the associated figure-eight
cycles, so this lower bound survives the entire proposed minimization.

ACHIEVABILITY. Use the straight source arc23 and small standard loops.
Arc23 exits the lobe around p2 once and enters the lobe around p3 once.
The other source arc56 misses both target loops. Thus total intersection2
is achieved, and these representatives are global minimizers for this c.

VANISHING. The two-point intersection set is empty: arc56 has no point
on either target loop, so no complete two-point matching exists. Therefore
the square/torus pairing vanishes for all q,t. The torus-torus pairing also
vanishes (the source56 torus factor is disjoint). The TL coefficient is
zero as well: in saved state labels, J_identity(top7,bottom2)=0.

TOPOLOGY. The cap-cup tangle has two arcs and no closed component. Its
standard diagram has no crossings and is the trivial e-type tangle:
source23 connects through target12 and34 to the remaining source endpoints
1,4, while source56 joins the two remaining target endpoints5,6.

CONSEQUENCE. For this repaired definition of c, both 'split implies c=0'
and 'every minimizer with c>0 has nonzero pairing' are false. The failure
is total intersections versus COMPLETE intersection matchings. This does
not refute a conjecture explicitly restricted to nonsplit pairs. It also
does not supply a counterexample to the Jones unknot conjecture.

This argument concerns figure-eight loops, not an unspecified choice of
two boundary noodles. Those choices must not be conflated: the corrected
duality object determines which complexity is actually being minimized.
