U5-JNC: exact local bracket check (2026-11-03)

Scope: the fixed sum convention supplied by Claude, with [X]=a[0]+b[inf],
Bplus=<N(X+[+1])>=-A^3 a-A^-3 b,
Bminus=<N(X+[-1])>=-A^-3 a-A^3 b.
No census or representation computation is needed: these linear identities
are checked on the two skein basis tangles, whose closures are curls.

The advertised raw bracket identity is correct:
 A^-6 Bplus-Bminus = b A^-9(A^12-1).

However the deduction about the Jones polynomial needs oriented writhe.
Write Vplus=(-A^3)^(-wplus) Bplus and similarly for Vminus.

Case 1: wplus-wminus=+2. Then Vplus=Vminus iff
 A^-6 Bplus-Bminus=0 iff b=0.

Case 2: wplus-wminus=-2. Then Vplus=Vminus iff
 A^6 Bplus-Bminus=0 iff a=0, because
 A^6 Bplus-Bminus = -a A^-3(A^12-1).

The local geometric +1 tangle can have either oriented sign. Both cases
occur even for a knot, rather than a multicomponent link.

Decisive calibration: X=[inf], so a=0,b=1. Both Kplus and Kminus are
nugatory unknots and have Jones polynomial 1. Their raw brackets are
-A^-3 and -A^3, respectively; their writhes are -1 and +1. Thus the
literal assertion 'Vplus=Vminus iff b=0' fails in this fixed sum frame.
The complementary X=[0] calibration instead has writhes +1,-1 and b=0.

This is an orientation/marking correction, not a refutation of a properly
orientation-adapted coefficient criterion. The final theorem must specify
which complementary skein basis coefficient is being tested, and why
that is the TL id coefficient in the claimed plat presentation. Swapping
or rotating the endpoint marking can interchange the two coefficients;
that change cannot be suppressed in the width argument.

Status: raw bracket identity checked exactly. The unconditional Jones
equivalence in a fixed sum frame is false; its orientation-adapted version
is correct. No check of the width-count theorem is contained in this note.
