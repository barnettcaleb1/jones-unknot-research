U5-JNC WIDTH CLAIM: EXPLICIT FOUR-PLAT COUNTEREXAMPLE
Status: TRUSTED cheap falsification; no broad scan.

Take standard caps/cups (12)(34) and braid
  beta = sigma2^2 sigma1 sigma2^3 = [2,2,1,2,2,2].
Mark the middle sigma1 crossing.

The upper prefix sigma2^2 is pure. Therefore the crossing's two strands
come from the same upper cap (12). Nevertheless this crossing is NOT
nugatory: changing it changes the Jones polynomial.

Spherogram/Regina exact checks (x is the Regina Jones variable):
  plat([2,2,1,2,2,2]): Jones=1; actual simplification gives 0 crossings,
  1 component.
  plat([2,2,-1,2,2,2]):
  Jones=x^10-2*x^8+2*x^6-2*x^4+2*x^2-1+x^-2.
  Thus the first is actually U, and the crossing is not a kink.

More strongly, its complement X is not rational and cannot have width2.
Replace the marked crossing by e1 (horizontal smoothing):
  E = ZeroTangle() | IdentityBraid(2)
  L = (BraidTangle([2,2],4)*E*BraidTangle([2,2,2],4)).numerator_closure()
Spherogram's diagrammatic deconnect_sum() splits L into the component
connected sum of a Hopf link and a trefoil:
  Hopf PD [(1,3,0,2),(3,1,2,0)], Jones=-x^5-x;
  trefoil PD [(1,5,2,4),(5,3,0,2),(3,1,4,0)],
  Jones=-x^8+x^6+x^2.
The full smoothing PD is
  [(9,5,8,4),(5,9,6,8),(1,7,2,6),(7,3,0,2),(3,1,4,0)].
Thus Sigma_2(L) is a connected sum of nontrivial lens spaces of orders
2 and3, with pi1=Z/2 * Z/3, hence not a lens space or S1 x S2.
Every rational closure of a rational tangle has cyclic cover fundamental
group; this closure does not. Therefore X is nonrational.
A width2 tangle cap_empty beta cup_empty is a two-strand braid, hence
rational. So the claim that every 4-plat crossing complement has width2
is false. This already refutes the general width formula in Proposition C.

Geometric failure: triviality of the complete upper bridge tangle does
not imply that an arc whose ends both meet the crossing ball splits off
relative to the remaining tangle. Its bridge disk can run across the
other strands on the punctured level sphere. Here sigma2^2 records that
interaction. Counting the remaining level punctures is insufficient to
put the complement in the claimed (2,2) cap/braid/cup normal form.

kink clause. It does not itself disprove the proposed conditional JNC
theorem for six-strand plats; that theorem loses the supplied argument.
