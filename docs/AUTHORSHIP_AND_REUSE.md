# Authorship, attribution, and reuse

Caleb Barnett directed this investigation and authorized publication of this ledger. Codex and Claude contributed generated arguments, calculations, code, criticism, and exposition. Historical authorship and model labels remain in the source records. They do not establish priority or external peer review.

The two manuscript drafts carry Caleb Barnett's author line and their stated AI-use disclosures. Their presence here does not claim that unfinished manuscript referee rounds have been completed. Criticism of other human work is scoped in [Human work and corrections](HUMAN_WORK_AND_CORRECTIONS.md), with exact citations and limitations.

## Licenses chosen by the maintainer

- Original notes, exposition, correspondence, figures, and data: [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt).
- Original software and verification code: [MIT](../LICENSE-MIT.txt).
- Third-party quotations, papers, software, and datasets: their own terms. They are not relicensed by this repository. Downloaded full papers and vendor trees are excluded rather than silently republished.

When reusing a project result, cite this ledger at a specific commit or release and the particular proof note. Also cite the underlying human sources at the point where their mathematics is used. The [reference map](REFERENCES.md) credits the main inputs; archived notes contain additional citations.

For a modified proof or program, explain your changes and preserve its provenance. Do not describe an internal AI referee as an external specialist, or attribute a project-derived deduction to a paper that only supplied its input.

The artifact manifest records the distribution decision for each available file. Some historical software depends on third-party code not included here; consult its source note and upstream license before reuse. The excluded Taketo Sano Yui source snippets are an example: their original MIT attribution is preserved in the archived license note, and they are not presented as original project code.
