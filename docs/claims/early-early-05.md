EARLY-05 — No fixed Taylor cutoff, even among three-braid knots
Status: INTERNAL-PROOF; explicit infinite nontrivial family.
In B3 set a=sigma1^2,b=sigma2^2,c1=a,c_(r+1)=[c_r,b],
K_r=closure(c_r sigma1 sigma2). For every m>=1,
 V_(K_2m)=1-3*4^(m-1)(t-1)^(2m)+O((t-1)^(2m+1)).
The TL specialization delta=-2 gives C2=16[e1,e2],
C_(r+1)=4[C_r,e2], C_(r+2)=64C_r, and nonzero closure coefficient.
A separate exact Burau recurrence proves ALL K_r nontrivial, odd r too:
b_(r+1)=2b_r^2,d_(r+1)=1+2b_rd_r, b_r/2<=d_r<b_r, forcing determinant>1.
Thus no fixed finite derivative order distinguishes U in this family.
Does not exclude complexity-dependent bounds or use of the full polynomial.
