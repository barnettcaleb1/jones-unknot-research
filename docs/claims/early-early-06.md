## Statement and recorded scope

```text
EARLY-06 — Complete TL2 skein-unit classification and cap obstruction
Status: INTERNAL-PROOF, algebra; topology deliberately not inferred.
R=Z[A,A^-1], S=R[e]/(e^2-delta e), delta=-A^2-A^-2,
b=A^-1+Ae. Every unit of S is eta A^s b^k, eta=+/-1,s,k integers.
Proof: the two characters f and f+delta g must both be Laurent units;
divisibility of their difference by A^4+1 imposes the stated powers/signs.
Hence each factor of a unit bracket is itself an integer-tangle bracket
up to a monomial. Geometric rationality DOES NOT follow; that would
invoke a Jones-equivalent bracket-detection assertion.
Actual nonrational X=<-1>+<1/2>+<1/3> has bracket f+ge with
 f=A^2-2A^-2+2A^-6-A^-10, g=A^4+A^-8-A^-12, delta f+g=1.
For rotated Q, e[Q]=e: caps can absorb a nonunit, so the factor argument
cannot be applied through arbitrary cups/caps or changing strand number.
Factors of units are units in TL_n by finite-free left-multiplication
determinants. But TL3 has N=delta e1e2-e1, N^2=0 and unit1+N;
at A=i it is not unitary, hence is not a scalar braid image. No actual
tangle realization of that formal unit is asserted.
Proof: work/generic-jones-note/main.tex, work/generic/tangle_notes.md.
```
