## Statement and recorded scope

```text
EARLY-07 — Crossing-cover homology supplies automatic, insufficient tests
Status: INTERNAL-PROOF + exact actual-knot countertests.
For any determinant-one knot, at any crossing the two resolutions have
branched-cover H1=Z/n and Z/(n+1), suitably ordered, while crossing change
gives Z/(2n+1); Z/0 means Z. This follows from distance-one surgery slopes
in an integral homology sphere. Cyclicity/consecutive orders are automatic.
All44 resolutions in standard KT and Conway diagrams check this. Both
have an unknotting crossing whose oriented smoothing has H1=Z but Jones
 x^9-x^7+x^5-x^3-x-x^-1-x^-3+x^-5-x^-7+x^-9,
not the unlink polynomial. Even bandability to ACTUAL U plus this homology
condition does not force the smoothing to be the unlink.
Source: work/generic/homology_notes.md and generic-jones-note/main.tex.
```
