## Statement and recorded scope

```text
EARLY-10 — Exact twist-parameter locus and finite polynomial certificates
Status: INTERNAL-PROOF, elementary effective method; no novelty asserted.
For any fixed finite diagram with k two-strand twist sites, on a fixed
parity class, V=1 locus is an effectively computable finite union of
affine integer lattices. TL2 diagonalization and denominator clearing give
 F_n(A)=sum_i c_i A^(a_i+b_i dot n).
F=0 iff terms admit a partition into equal-exponent blocks of coefficient
sum0. Each partition imposes integer affine linear equations. Auxiliary
monomial exponents and affine coupled parameters are allowed. The method
is finite but can be exponentially expensive. It does not classify knot
types or provide finitely many templates covering all knots.
Also a Laurent polynomial with <=M terms is zero if its first M logarithmic
moments at1 vanish: Vandermonde after collecting distinct exponents.
Sources: second-pass-note/main.tex; outputs/jones-proof-mechanisms.txt.
```
