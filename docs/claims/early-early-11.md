EARLY-11 — Finite geometric unknot certificates for fixed twist grids
Status: INTERNAL-PROOF, classical-surgery consequence; count sharp.
In ANY fixed one-site two-strand full-twist family, three distinct actual-U
members imply every member is U. Two S3 fillings make the complementary
cover a solid torus (Gordon–Luecke); rational closures have determinants
|an+b|, and three values1 force a=0. Two arbitrary samples are insufficient:
T(2,2n+1) has two U parameters and other nontrivial members.
For d independent sites, actual-U certificates on {0,1,2}^d imply all
integer tuples U by coordinatewise induction. Coupled affine lines such
as (n,-n) are outside this criterion. The stronger two-sample version in
an antiparallel crossing-circle setting requires its orientation premises.
