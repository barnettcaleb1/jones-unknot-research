EARLY-14 — Ren-pattern classification for all twists AND all companions
Status: INTERNAL-PROOF, specific diagram family; novelty unverified.
P_pq is Ren's annular pattern with its two outer crossings replaced by
odd powers p,q; n meridional twists, preferred-zero-framed companion C.
 V_(P_pqn(C))=1 iff (p,q,n)=(-1,1,0) AND H3(C)=1.
For the original pattern p=q=1, determinant=|100n-11|>1 for every C,n.
Determinant + second/cubic moments first restrict nonzero S=p+q solutions
to p=320r^2+32r-1,q=-320r^2+32r+1,n=800r^3-7r. They would require
H3's second logarithmic derivative to be
(13+12344r^2-883200r^4+15360000r^6)/6, nonintegral. S=0 leaves(-1,1,0).
At the exceptional pattern V-1=a*Delta2/delta*(H3-1), a!=0, and P(U)=U.
Every P_pq has wrapping4: two-cable z^8 coefficient nonzero; the actual
diagram gives upper bound4. Hence nontrivial C with H3=1 would give a
nontrivial satellite counterexample. No such C is known here.
Proof/diagrams: work/fourth-pass-note/main.tex, work/pass4 and reproduction.
