```text
EARLY-27 — Low derivatives detect U in three-box and all-odd pretzel classes
Status: INTERNAL-PROOF; known detection context, precise novelty unverified.
Every three-strand pretzel K=P(p,q,r) satisfies
V''(1)=V'''(1)=0 => K=U. Complete surviving triples are permutations of
(k,1,-1),(0,1,1),(0,-1,-1),(1,-2,-3),(-1,2,3), restricted to knot
parities. Elementary integer ellipse and rational closure geometry prove it.
Strong all-length extension: every (2g+1)-box all-odd pretzel with these
two derivatives0 is U. Let S=sum p_i. Moment conditions become
sum p_i²=S²+2g, sum p_i³=S³. Mirror S>0; |p_i|<=S and
sum(S-p_i)(p_i²-1)=0 is a sum of nonnegative integers. Thus parameters
are one S plus balanced +/-1, yielding U. No arbitrary permutation of
general pretzel boxes is needed.
Stipsicz–Szabo2020 has exact five-odd moment equations; Sleptsov has
low-order formulas. Jones detection for pretzels already known (Nicholson).
Sources: work/proof9/tangle/pretzel_moment_audit.txt,
work/proof10/tangle/findings.txt, outputs/jones-proof-mechanisms.txt.
```
