EARLY-43 — Rotor-cover symmetry excludes standard Suzuki graph exteriors
Status: INTERNAL-PROOF, necessary condition + published group computation.
For actual n-rotor n>=3, double branched cover M has C_n x C2 subgroup
in MCG(M). Lift rotation fixing a chosen unbranched boundary-pole lift;
its order n commutes with independent deck C2. Faithful finite boundary
action on genus n-1>=2 injects in mapping classes.
Ushijima's M_n,k with gcd(n,2-k)=1 have FULL MCG dihedral order2n,
not containing abelian order2n. Thus standard Suzuki theta_n exteriors,
including Kinoshita theta3 and theta4, cannot be required rotor covers.
Does not exclude arbitrary Brunnian spatial graphs or all handlebody exteriors.
