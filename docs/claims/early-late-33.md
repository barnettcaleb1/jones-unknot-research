LATE-33 [IP/FC, TABLE-DEPENDENT] ORDINARY JONES DOES NOT DETERMINE A-POLYNOMIAL
Specified chiralities of 5_1 and 10_132 have the same Jones polynomial
q^-2+q^-4-q^-5+q^-6-q^-7 and the same Alexander polynomial, but their table
A-polynomials have L-width 1 and 8 (abelian factor removed). Width survives
Laurent units, mirroring and inversion. The checker verifies
A_10_132(L,1)=(L-1)^3(L+1)^5 and a point disagreement. This is verified
transcription/algebra, not an independent character-variety computation.
The bottom-tangle exterior construction maps relations to counit evaluation;
it does not invert that map. A unit-fiber assertion V=1 => A=1 remains
an equivalent formulation of detection, without an additional mechanism.
Files: research46/complement_character.txt, verify_peripheral_collision.py,
peripheral_collision.json; Jones certificate in
proof12/braid/all_color_collision_proof.txt.
