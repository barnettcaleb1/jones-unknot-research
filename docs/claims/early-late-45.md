```text
LATE-45 [IP/LI] FLOER ENDPOINT LEAVES A CLOSED HOMOLOGY-SPHERE SUMMAND
Suppose H1(M)=Z, M is a knot exterior in an integral homology sphere, M is
Floer simple, and symmetric Delta''(1)=0. Binary torsion alternation gives
Delta=1; RR norm then forces boundary compression. Hence M=ST#Z where Z
is an integral homology-sphere L-space. Conversely such Z makes M Floer
simple. If M is irreducible, it is ST. An ACTUAL lens filling removes Z in
LATE-41; merely L-space fillings do not.
Concrete OLD model Z=Poincare#(-Poincare) is a nontrivial L-space homology
sphere with Casson0 and d0. ST#Z has all nonlongitudinal fillings L(p,q)#Z
with the lens-space HF ranks, Casson--Walker invariants and correction terms.
This is not asserted to satisfy rational bracket equality, is not a Jones
counterexample, and is explicitly reducible.
Local insertion of J into R gives [X_J]=V_J[R] and cover ST#Sigma2(J).
For hypothetical V_J=1, the auxiliary filling core is already a BALL UNKNOT
in Sigma2(J), so its Delta=1 is automatic. Floer simplicity of that cover
is equivalent to Sigma2(J) being an L-space, which does not imply S3.
Assuming irreducibility discards this generic local-insertion construction.
Kh-to-HF spectral sequence only bounds HF rank by TOTAL Kh rank; an Euler
polynomial1 does not bound that rank. TWO missing bridges remain: actual
Floer information from bracket data, and removal of the closed summand.
Files: research51/floer_endpoint.txt, adversarial_audit.txt.
```
