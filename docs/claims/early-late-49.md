LATE-49 [IP/FC/LI] BRUNNIAN/BURAU CONSTRAINTS KEEP RESIDUAL B5 BLOCK DENSE

and rho the residual five-dimensional TL block [3,2]. The cached Bigelow seed
has a nonidentity residual image. Explicit Brunnian braid

satisfies rho(b)=I+4^4(A-1)^4 C+O((A-1)^5), with C nonzero on [3,2].
Exact permutation-module squared Frobenius norms are88 on two-subsets,8 on
vertices, hence80 on the residual summand. This is a predicted-term check,
not a word search.
Kuperberg projective Zariski density plus normality gives closures rho(N)
and rho(H) equal PGL5. Since [N,H] is contained in J, rho(J) is dense, and
every finite lower-central or derived-series stage remains dense. Yet the
intersection of all lower-central stages is {1} by residual nilpotence of
pure braid groups. Thus an entire deep commutator subgroup is not a kernel;
infinite depth does not produce a finite nontrivial word. A specially chosen
exact relation remains possible.
Odd uniform parallel cabling retains the original fundamental operator as a
direct summand, so cannot repair a braid already detected there. Even cabling
requires NEW mixed-color identities, not consequences of Burau-kernel status.
