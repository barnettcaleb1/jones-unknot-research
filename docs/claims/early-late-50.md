LATE-50 [FM/IP] UNIVERSAL FROBENIUS COMPLEX HIDES ARBITRARY KH HOMOLOGY
R=Z[h,t], A=R[X]/(X^2-hX-t). Genus-one cylinder H=2X-h has
H^2=D=h^2+4t. Define a formal Bar--Natan two-term cobordism complex
 E0=O direct-sum O{-2}, E1=O direct-sum O{+2},
 d=[[2,H],[H,0]].
Entries are genuine cobordisms or integer linear combinations. Multiplying
by [[0,H],[H,-2]] gives D*I, so E is contractible at every semisimple D!=0
specialization, integrally at Bar--Natan h=1,t=0, and rationally at Lee h=0,t=1.
The ordinary Kh fiber h=t=0 reduces by graded row/column operations to
 diag(2,0), giving H0=A0{-2}, H1=A0{+2} direct-sum A0/2A0.
C_N=O direct-sum (E direct-sum E[1])^N has the unknot's full Euler polynomial,
reduced rational rank1+4N, integral 2-torsion summands2N, mod2 rank1+8N,
yet all semisimple homologies are the unknot's.
The special fiber can be modeled by wedges of spheres and Moore spectra,
with required nonzero Sq1/Bockstein. This is NOT an actual Lipshitz--Sarkar
knot stable type. Coherent universal deformations, integrality and ordinary
Steenrod axioms do not force Jones-unit rank1. The missing constraint is
origin as the signed planar cube of ONE actual knot diagram, not membership
in the ambient cobordism-complex category. No diagram realization is claimed.
File: research52/categorical_realizability.txt.
