```text
MID-03  D  Preferred longitude derivative detects a_2 of the covering knot
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let T be a four-ended tangle with actual unknot meridional closure. Its cover
is E(J) for J in S^3. A determinant-zero slope is the preferred longitude.
For the longitude branch link L_J,
 |V'_L_J(-1)|=|12 a_2(J)-1|,
where Delta_J is symmetric, Delta_J(1)=1 and a_2=Delta_J''(1)/2.
Its root at -1 is always simple. A longitude Jones polynomial equal to the
two-component unlink's, even up to a signed Laurent monomial, forces a_2=0.
Proof uses Garoufalidis's branched-cover formula and its OWN surgery
normalization: lambda_G(S^3_0(J))=2a_2(J)-1/6. Integrality excludes a_2=1/6.
Do not mix the rational-homology-sphere orientation law with b1=1 conventions.
This excludes nontrivial L-space cores and their mirrors: alternating symmetric
Alexander coefficients give a_2>0. It does not force Delta=1 or J=U.
Earlier weaker lemma: equal rational branch Jones polynomials at unbounded odd
numerators p force p|8a_2(J), hence a_2=0. The two-filling lemma supersedes it.
Proofs: work/fanout16/realizability_zero_filling.txt,
realizability_casson_audit.txt. Later general-homology-sphere extension belongs
to pass51, outside this ledger. Do not add an ambient Casson term there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
```
