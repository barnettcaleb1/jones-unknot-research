MID-05  D  Exact shell root order and Whitehead no-first-flatness
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For a winding-zero shell with P(U)=U, A^4+1 divides d_P. At zeta=e^(pi*i/4),
 |d'_P(zeta)|=48|beta(P union C)|.
Thus beta!=0 gives a simple zero; beta=0 means a second factor A^4+1,
including the possible identically-zero factor. Nonzero beta is sufficient,
not necessary, for injectivity: two Whitehead shells have beta=0 and nonzero
product factor. Iterated inherited untwisted Whitehead quotients are flat iff
their input quotient was flat. They cannot create a first invisible quotient.
For a core with a_2!=0 and r such layers, the preferred longitude link obeys
 ord_(t=-1)(V_Lr-V_U2)=r+1 exactly. These are actual two-component links,
not formal polynomials, and prove finite derivative tests insufficient here.
Proofs: same output as MID-04; work/quotient17/sato_levine_first_jet.txt,
pattern_root_order_corollary.txt, defect_simple_root_audit.txt.
