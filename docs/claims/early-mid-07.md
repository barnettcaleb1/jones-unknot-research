```text
MID-07  C/D/N  Zero shell endpoint, and two insufficient invariant packages
------------------------------------------------------------------------
An actual nonsplit two-component link of unknots with lk=0, compatible strong
inversion, and d_P identically zero yields a nontrivial Jones-one u=1 knot.
Gabai's solid-torus surgery obstruction makes every nonzero rational twist
input essential/nontrivial; half-integral branch filling is the endpoint.
No such pattern was found. Split patterns have d=0, so d=0=>split is the
unproved rigidity assertion. Serial products of nonzero factors cannot help.
Twice Whitehead-doubling one Hopf component gives a boundary-link example
with vanishing eta/derived/Milnor data but nonzero d_Wh^2: those classical
sequences do not recover the full shell factor. Arbitrarily many Whitehead
layers also hide any fixed finite LMO surgery order without killing d.
The proposed Whitehead-clasp converse FAILED: the inherited inversion acts
freely on the clasp circle, so it is not the required four-ended quotient
boundary. MID-08 repairs the converse by another operation, not this one.
Sources: output in MID-06; work/transmission18/geometry.txt, homology.txt,
unknotting_one_reduction_audit.txt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
```
