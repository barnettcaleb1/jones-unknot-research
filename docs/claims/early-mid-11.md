```text
MID-11  D/C  Genuine positive norm identity, but only in one clasp family
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For an actual Brunnian pure n-braid beta in the specified doubled-strand
Stoimenow clasp construction, let U=rho(beta-doubled) on 2n+2-endpoint TL
vacuum space; P projects all n internal pairs to color3; w=Pv. Then
 U-I=P(U-I)P and
 V_Kbeta-1=C_n(A)<w,(U-I)w>, C_n=A^(2n)(A^4-1)^n/delta.
On a fixed-level positive unitary arc,
 Re((V-1)/C_n)=-1/2 ||(U-I)w||^2.
Thus V=1 iff U fixes this ONE vector generically. It does not imply U=I.
Full proof would additionally require a nontriviality-preserving generic
reduction to this family and a geometric vector-stabilizer classification.
Neither is established; even faithful representations can have stabilizers.
Proof/check: work/research20/quantum_scaffold.txt, projected_clasp.py,
check_norm_identity.py. Omitting the two outer endpoints gives the wrong
one-dimensional state space and would create false commutator invisibility.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
```
