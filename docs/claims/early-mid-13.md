MID-13  D/N  Watson degeneracy, support window, and uniform rank theorem
------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the precisely mirrored-three-braid Watson shell, determinant one forces
the two-bridge base J_beta to be U. The marked geometry reduces the output
to the connected sum of its input denominator closures. Thus this shell
cannot manufacture a first u=1 Jones counterexample or enlarge an unknot
orbit into nontrivial knots. No assertion of generic Watson coverage.
A fixed compensating-twist orbit initially gave a fixed Kh bigrading support
window; the two exact triangles ALONE do not bound multiplicities (formal
mapping-cone countermodel, not a realized tangle).
Pass26 supplies extra geometry: resolve only the N fixed crossings in T,U;
every state is either a simple Watson knot with constant integral Kh or an
explicit unlink after rainbow-cap cancellation. Hence for ANY fixed valid
Watson template, including nonsimple input tangles, both rational reduced and
unreduced Kh ranks are uniformly bounded over all positive/negative twists.
With d=det(K), the strengthened uniform estimates are
 dim_F2 Kh(K)<=4^N max{2d,8},
 dim_Q Kh_red(K)<=4^N max{d,4},
uniform in the middle braid and twist parameters. Input complexity N remains
uncontrolled on a whole Jones fiber. This is boundedness, not the open stronger
claim of Kh constancy for nonsimple tangles.
Proofs: work/research21/geometric_bridge.txt, watson_rank_audit.txt;
work/research26/homology_mechanism.txt, watson_independent_audit.txt.
Exact 54-case cap/permutation checks: watson_connectivity_check.py and
verify_watson_independent.py. Do not reverse the reflected middle word twice.
