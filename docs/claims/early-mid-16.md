MID-16  D  Relative lifted-Lee bars constrain ribbon concordances
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For a ribbon concordance C:J->K with d births and d saddles, over Q the
split relative lifted-Lee summand T=ker(reverse(C)_*) is killed by X^d.
Sarkar's two evaluations of one cobordism give this RELATIVE statement;
do not replace it with an unsupported assertion about all endpoint torsion.
Graded PID decomposition and specialization X=0 imply
 P_K(u,q)-P_J(u,q)=sum_(a=1)^d (1+u q^(2a)) S_a(u,q), S_a>=0.
For d=1, differentiation at q=1 using V'(1)=0 implies total reduced
rational rank difference lies in 4 Z_{≥0}. For general d the weighted
equation sum a S_a(-1,1)=0 does NOT imply mod4 rank invariance.
With unknown d this is still a finite necessary positive-bar matching test.
It is not sufficient for existence of a ribbon concordance.
Proof: work/research22/one_band_relative_rank.txt;
one_band_relative_rank_topology_audit.txt; relative_bar_filter_audit.txt.
