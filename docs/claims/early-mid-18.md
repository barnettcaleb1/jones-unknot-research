MID-18  D/N  Three-term Jones polynomials can have unbounded homology rank
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Actual T(3,3k+1) family has three ordinary Jones terms of magnitudes1,1,1
but reduced rational Kh rank4k+1. Hence coefficient amplitude/l1 norm or
number of terms alone cannot bound Kh rank. Full polynomials vary; this does
not disprove a bound on a fixed WHOLE Jones fiber.
At T(3,4), reduced P=q^6+u^2 q^10+u^3 q^12+u^4 q^12+u^5 q^16, so the
zero-Euler q^12 column is actual nonzero homology, not cancellable chains.
For a chirality of 10_132, Kh reaches q=-2 while its Euler maximum is -4;
connected m-fold sums give spill2m beyond the Jones exponent convex hull.
No fixed universal margin around that hull can contain all Kh support.
Proof: Schuetz 3-braid formula as used in work/research23/global_homology.txt;
work/research24/spanning_tree_mechanism.txt and verify_homology_examples.py.
