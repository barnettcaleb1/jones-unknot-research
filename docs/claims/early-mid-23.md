## Statement and recorded scope

```text
MID-23  D/N  Spectral and geometric restrictions on Burau-kernel braids
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For an actual integral Laurent TL module and beta in [P_n,P_n], every
eigenvalue of rho(beta) lying in Q(A) equals1. If its characteristic polynomial
splits completely, rho(beta)=I. Proof: rho(beta)=I+O((A-1)^2); rational
eigenvalue and inverse are integral over Laurent UFD, hence +/-A^k; jets force
k=0; a unitary unipotent matrix is identity on an open fixed-level arc and
therefore generically. Burau-kernel braids lie in [P_n,P_n]: their pure
abelianization is recovered by independent derivative Laplacians L_ij.
Thus diagonal rational clock/shift models cannot represent a nonidentity
residual image. Nonidentity spectra must have a nonrational eigenvalue.
Also a generic Burau-kernel braid that is virtually a multitwist is trivial:
laminar enclosed subsets and zero pairwise linking kill all twist exponents;
torsion-freeness kills the root. This includes periodic inputs, not all
reducible braids. A Burau-kernel braid commuting geometrically with a primitive
Coxeter braid is also trivial by its cyclic centralizer and writhe zero.
Proofs: work/research24/spectral_realizability_obstruction.txt and two audits;
work/research25/structural_kernel_route.txt. verify_spectral_jets.py.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
```
