MID-25  D/N/C  Finite scalar-annular certificate and companion-power dichotomy
------------------------------------------------------------------------

at most2d times, the following are equivalent: full annular bracket scalar;
V_P(U)=V_P(T)=...=V_P(#^d T) for one fixed trefoil; companion-independence.
Proof expands sum_(i=0)^d c_i H_(2i+1)(J); on trefoil powers lambda_i^r,
val(lambda_i)=-6i^2-5i are distinct. The finite Vandermonde determinant is
product(lambda_i-1) product_(i<j)(lambda_j-lambda_i), nonzero.
If nonscalar, V_P(#^r T) is eventually pairwise distinct, with valuation
val(c_k)-r(6k^2+5k) for greatest surviving k>0. No sparse constant subsequence.
An essential scalar pattern would give unbounded bridge in a nonunit Jones
fiber; it need not disprove detection unless V_P(U)=1. No such pattern found.
Signed serial lasso/clasp chains have top annular coefficient product of
nonzero geometric-series factors; they cannot be scalar except local reductions.
Conway mutation or CLOSED genus2 mutation of a local pattern cannot make it
essential: the splitting sphere survives (uses valid DGST Prop2.1, NOT their
withdrawn Prop2.7). This is distinct from zero quotient-shell factor d=0.
Proof: work/research26/satellite_mechanism.txt and its audit;
verify_satellite_certificate.py.
