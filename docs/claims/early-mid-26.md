MID-26  D/C  Actual Bing pattern product formula
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the actual Bing construction P_C and zero-framed companions,
 [P_C]=delta H3(C)+(1-H3(C))z^2/delta in the annular skein, and
 V_P_C(J)-1=-(delta^2-1)/delta^2 (H3(C)-1)(H3(J)-1).
Hence two nonzero deviations cannot cancel. If an actual nontrivial C with
H3(C)=1 exists, P_C gives nontrivial Jones-one knots on every nontrivial J.
Topology and formula are supplied; the existence premise is NOT supplied.
Do not infer wrapping number or evaluations at poles from rational formulas.
