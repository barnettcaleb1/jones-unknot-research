## Statement and recorded scope

```text
The annular report gives a negative argument for packages determined by additive, grading-shift-equivariant functors of the tangle type-D structure, with conditions inherited by summands or additive numerical identities. Such a package cannot by itself exclude the formal opposite-parity blocks or their permitted shifted sums. This does not exclude additional geometric structure, maps not determined by that additive object, or non-additive compatibility conditions.
```
