## Statement and recorded scope

```text
Over F2, the report’s unit D-arrow and P2-run hypotheses, together with the KWZ extension input, force a component containing a bullet generator to have the stated square or staircase shape. Components with no bullet generators must be excluded or separately allowed. The staircase-plus-squares corollary additionally requires one noncompact component and an odd number of bullet generators. Tail cases are supplied by the referee. This is not the refuted unrestricted shape law and does not prove that every undeformed summand lifts separately.
```
