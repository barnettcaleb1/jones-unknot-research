## Statement and recorded scope

```text
For the marked V3 construction s.l1.(Y^N B_1...B_r).(x2)^a.u1, N is integral, all B_i are non-integral rational boxes, a is odd, and r>=1 for |a|>=3 or r>=2 for a=+-1. Put L_n=T(1/n). If a2(K)=0, V(L_n)!=1 for every nonzero integer n. Without a2(K)=0, every nonzero even n is excluded and at most one odd n can escape; in the fixed orientation convention it must satisfy j=n a2(K). Non-flatness alone does not remove that odd exception. This is a theorem for the marked construction, not a classification of all inversions of K.
```
